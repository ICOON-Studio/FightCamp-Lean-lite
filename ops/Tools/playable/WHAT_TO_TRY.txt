=====================================================================

START: double-click FightCamp.exe in this folder.
(FightCamp_console.exe is the same game with a console window - use it if the game
crashes, the console shows why. Keep the "assets" folder and FightCamp.pck beside the exe.)

WHAT TO TRY - quick start to fight night (about 20 minutes at 3x)
 1. QUICK START on the title board: a built gym in London, straight onto the floor.
 2. A fighter walks in: APPLICANT in the top bar -> the interview -> pick 3 cards -> SIGN.
 3. Her goal sits on the floor with its countdown.
 4. OFFICE > Staff > HIRE A COACH.
 5. Let the days run (3x). At the week's end WEEK in the top bar opens the week review:
    each stat's bar fills toward the NEXT WHOLE POINT and turns gold when it ticks over.
 6. WORLD > Schedule > FIND A BOUT -> pick a legal offer -> ENTER THE CARD.
 7. Back on the floor, SET CAMP waits in the top bar -> START A CAMP -> pick a focus and a
    protocol -> START CAMP.
 8. Run the weeks: fight night calls itself -> fight week -> weigh-in -> BEGIN FIGHT (watch it,
    or SIM TO RESULT).
 9. The result says why. Back home, NEW GOAL waits in the top bar -> it walks you into the
    office where she picks her next goal.
