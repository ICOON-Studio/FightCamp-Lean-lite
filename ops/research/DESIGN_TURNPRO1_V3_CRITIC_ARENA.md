# Turning pro v3 — independent design critique

SCORE depth=5 general=4

**Play: 5/10.** A first pro call, the boxer's wishes, and a choice between a gate gamble and a guarantee could make a memorable career moment. Waiting for a title or building a crowd gives the weekly training and fight loop a purpose. But a progress bar that promises money without a functioning saving rule is not a reward, and the wrong win odds or break-even date teach the wrong lesson. These are design scores, not evidence that v3 has been built or measured. Neither depth nor general design reaches the 7/10 bar.

**Depth: 5/10.** Timing, risk, cash and the boxer's ambition are good axes, and one ask makes the choice matter. Yet deferral transfers most of the bill to the boxer under the current code, so it can dominate for reasons the offer conceals. The price ask and safe-opponent arithmetic do not reliably deliver their stated effects. The map asserts unique uses rather than showing them. Longer-term promoter lean, ambition effects and funding rules need actual outcome paths, not labels.

**General design: 4/10.** The proposed forecast, cash timing, gap signs, decline exceptions and statistics contradict one another or current rules. The map calls estimates probe-verified and checks items as finished while also saying the new probe is outstanding. The UI can use existing surfaces, but new rules and persistence are required. Names are allowed in headers only; the map's example “She's eager” also breaks the sandbox's gender-neutral text rule: use “Your boxer is eager.”

## What the code actually says

Code read: `scripts/state/ProOfferWire.gd`, `scripts/rules/ProCall.gd`, `scripts/rules/ProDebut.gd`, and the turning-pro readiness and booking sections of `scripts/state/Roster.gd`. Also read the probe result, the brief and `ops/research/CRITIC_ACCESS.md`, plus map frame and note files. The notes file is empty; the referenced Windows real-screen directory and turning-pro preview are not present here, so I could not inspect those images. This is a mechanic review, not a visual approval. No Godot was run.

* First-time full costs are $230 + $875 + $140 = $1,245, but `ProDebut.start_costs` can omit medicals or trainer licence. Do not charge every boxer the full amount. At 60 tickets, the $1,500 offer gives the gym $375 at the bout and costs $1,245 at signing: net −$870, not a −$870 cash movement on signing day. The guaranteed $1,800 offer gives $450, so net −$795. `ProCall.money` calls that combined forecast `gym_at_signing`; it is not a dated cash ledger. With +$175/week, $795/175 rounds up to 5 weeks, not 6; neither calculation correctly places the purse at week 4 or 6. With −$75/week there is no break-even without additional income; week 17 or 10 needs an explicit, scheduled earnings model. The $75 is an estimate, not a paid weekly ledger item.
* Under current `ProOfferWire.money` and `night`, $1,345 is deducted from the **gross purse before the 25% split**, not from the gym's share. At 70 tickets with a $1,800 guarantee, no ticket surplus and the $100 fee, pay-now gives gym −$795 total and boxer $1,350; deferral gives gym about $114 and boxer about $341. The gym's total improves about $909 while the boxer loses about $1,009. This is not simply “gym pays $100 more”; at signing cash improves $1,245, but repayment incidence must be stated. A floor at zero also leaves the unpaid balance undefined if the purse is too small. The existing low-runway counter is $200 below 8 weeks, not v3's $100 below 4 weeks.
* The actual price ask adds 15 tickets and stores $40, but `ProCall.money` calculates the surplus using the fixed $50 `ProDebut.TICKET_PRICE`; it does not use the stored price. V3 instead says −10 tickets and improved retention without an actionable retention rule. Neither version proves the advertised price trade-off.
* `Roster` books the opponent at boxer power **minus** `opp_gap`; `ProCall._apply_softer` adds 0.5 to that positive gap. Thus −1.0 to −0.5 makes the opponent harder, not softer. The guarantee offer already uses 1.5, not −1.2; changing the same gap to 1.5 is not a v3 improvement. A displayed 60% versus 72% conflicts with the table's 75% versus 72% and does not follow from gap alone.
* The current call band is strictly inside 45–75 for both; 45 and 75 are single-house boundaries. First calls stop at 32 in code; this is a game rule estimate, not a verified boxing regulation. The next call after a decline is locked to the previous house, and terms compound 0.9 per decline until a title restores the factor. At two declines `pro_ready` checks rank 1 directly; at three it stops, with no reopen. An expired offer is not a decline but needs two more wins. A title resets terms in the offer calculation, not necessarily decline count or the closed door.
* A win's forecast adds two tickets, but the brief elsewhere says +8 members per win, not the map's +4; $450 per amateur show and +$300/month cannot be assumed net or unconditional. Fixed $150/week savings takes at least nine weeks to reach $1,245, only if that surplus is actually available. Reaching the first house's break-even does not itself cause a new call, and saving for costs is not necessary if the costs really are deferred.

## Options: effects, missing and useless

| Option | Intended change and judgement |
|---|---|
| Sign the ticket house | Four-week debut, $1,500 purse subject to $25 per missing ticket and $500 floor, 50% surplus share. More gate exposure and earlier camp; show expected gym and boxer proceeds, not a guaranteed return. |
| Sign the guarantee house | Six-week debut, $1,800 guaranteed, 30% over 70 tickets; less downside and later camp. The gap suggests an easier opponent, but the actual result must be measured. Only display houses that actually call. |
| Stay amateur | Keeps amateur eligibility, creates the next-call conditions and can affect morale and terms. Show exact next threshold and expiry/decline distinction, including the three-decline consequence. |
| Later four weeks | More time for a booked amateur bout or camp, $300 lower gross purse and later pro income. Specify how a booked fight survives signing: current booking marks amateur offers answered and installs a pro bout. Otherwise the headline use is unavailable. The two-week choice was removed; restore it only if it earns its place. |
| Softer opponent | Intended lower risk for two tune-ups afterwards; reverse the gap sign and enforce the restriction on subsequent offers. State whether two **bouts** or two **offer rounds** are constrained. Currently v3's numeric effect is backwards. |
| Better split | +8 percentage points above break-even for a harder opponent, or a +4-point counter at unchanged opponent. At 10 tickets over, +4 points earns the gym only $5 at $50 and 25% share. Define the threshold above 19 tickets, whether the counter is a choice, and how the opponent changes. With no surplus the ask is useless. |
| Own ticket price | Intended $40 price and more loyal members at a cost of 10 sales. Define demand, gate, break-even and retained members with bounds; lowering price while also selling fewer has no established benefit. Until wired to money and retention it is an ineffective or misleading ask. |
| Defer costs | Intended zero signing cash, later repayment plus fee, guarantee house only. Useful for liquidity but not automatically best: disclose who pays, the fee, shortfall and missed-bout handling. The v3 low-runway counter is ambiguous about whether the $100 is additional to the first $100. |
| No ask / return to plain | Retains original deal; current code permits return after an ask but spends it. Needed when an ask worsens outcomes or the player changes their mind; spell out whether one ask is per house or across both. |
| Delay until nationals | Missing a binding, buildable commitment: reserve an offer or explicitly decline and request a later call? Nationals can take multiple bouts, an injury or loss can end the run, and the offer expires in 14 days. Show exact consequences rather than imply an open-ended hold. |
| Fund / choose finances | Missing a real earmark or adjustable saving amount, and a cash-short alternative if the guarantee house does not call. An automatic $150 transfer without disposable cash can starve the gym. Show spendable cash, upcoming bills, funding progress and a cancel/release action if a reserve is implemented. |

Informational chips, a crowd animation and a corner reminder are not choices; keep only if they lead to a current action or put details in hover. “Keep stipend running longer” is not an action after signing, when the stipend stops. A goal to save is useless unless it changes an actual reserve or points to an available money action.

## What the probe can and cannot establish

The existing result is 30 careers per bot per seed, two seed sets, not a v3 run. The v3 percentages for ask use, offer closure and wins are hypotheses. “Best” needs a stated objective and tie policy; cash-only best is not the same as safer, faster or aligned with the boxer. Re-run paired seeds, report counts and uncertainty by stipend status and promoter, and keep the original definitions as well as any new ones.

1. **Dominance:** a new path may diversify results, but a guaranteed liquidity subsidy could instead strengthen one bot. Measure the share of paired week-104 wins and ties, not claim ≤55% from design alone.
2. **PREP vs YES:** saving or a timeline does not add $1,500 by itself; specify extra ticket, income and opportunity costs. The v2 paired median was $0, including many identical day-job paths.
3. **Stipend timing:** displaying a forecast cannot reverse cash outcomes. Model actual lost amateur events, stipend dates and later pro receipts; verify both groups at weeks 52 and 104.
4. **Debut loss:** 10/15 and 6/15 stipend debuts lost, already 67% and 40%; 27% was the reported average across four cells including one day-job debut and an empty cell. Report numerators and denominators and preserve the original aggregate alongside any stipend-only target. Zero cash outlay is not zero total loss.
5. **Door:** three declines are already a hard stop, but the RANK bot closed 0/60. Reopening makes closure less, not more, likely without altered decisions or offer cadence. Specify whether closure is measured ever or at week 104; keep the old 10–30% criterion alongside the proposed 10–20%.
6. **Win rates:** v2's 33/41 and 30/50 are small samples. Fix signs, hold opponent generation and camp assumptions constant, then test both gaps; probabilities are not interchangeable with a point estimate on one offer.
7. **Asks:** the stated 15/12/18/11/44 percentages are unsupported, and 44% “always best” is not proof that every other ask has a real niche. Fork the same offers, record eligibility, objectives, ties and samples; include no ask.
8. **Promoters:** v2 passed at 50/91 picks and 130/300 both calls. Recheck after changing deferral, eligibility and decline handling; “unchanged” is not proof of continued success.
9. **Boxer's say:** v2 measured AMB morale Steady+ at signing (1/30), not preference alignment. Record that original outcome separately from a newly defined preference-alignment rate, including neutral/unknown preferences and overrides. A morale modifier does not force players or bots to agree.

## To reach 10/10

1. **Make one dated, auditable money model.** At signing debit actual applicable licences; at each scheduled date record stipend, actual amateur opportunity costs, purse, gate and gym/boxer shares. Forecast ranges and assumptions, not guaranteed week numbers. Decide and implement who owes deferred principal and fee, what happens if the bout is cancelled or the purse is insufficient, and show both parties' before/after cash. This needs new debt/settlement state and tests.
2. **Make waiting a real decision.** Define a reserve from genuine free cash, release it on cancellation, and model dates for the next amateur show, national entry, medicals and future calls. Provide a non-deferral path when only the ticket house calls. Do not promise a call merely because a funding or ticket threshold is reached; show the exact trigger and uncertainty.
3. **Repair and test each ask end to end.** Correct the opponent-gap direction; keep bout dates and amateur eligibility consistent; use the selected ticket price in gate calculations and define its demand/retention effect; explicitly encode split counters, fee thresholds and the one-ask scope. Show the plain and changed terms before accepting and make the plain deal recoverable. These require rule and UI work, not just new labels.
4. **Specify the boxer's voice and door state.** Connect actual ambition values to a visible, gender-neutral preference, morale and any goal effect; define magnitude and duration of performance effects. Decide with the owner whether a regional or national title reopens a closed door, how many times, what resets and how expiry differs from declining. Do not present the age cutoff as real-world law without evidence.
5. **Run a prospective probe before claiming success.** Use 200 careers per bot per seed as proposed, paired seeds and fixed bot policies, preserve all nine v2 metrics, and add the proposed new metrics separately. Publish counts, denominators, confidence intervals, tie handling, money event traces and outliers; calibrate gaps and terms only after testing. Add targeted cases for both band boundaries, 32nd birthday, no disposable cash, low purse, title after closure, booked amateur fight, and a missed debut.
6. **Make the payoff visible in play.** At the weekly review show only changes the player can act on: next required win or title, forecast against actual cash and a link to training or the money page. After the debut, connect the result and the plan's Why to the next pro offer, without promising unbuilt promoter behaviour.
7. **1920×1080 page description:** Use the approved warm gym-whiteboard style on the existing offer modal, not a new unapproved screen. A single header identifies your boxer; beneath it put the deadline and an action to open the fighter's week. Two contract columns show only available houses, dates, opponent chance as a range, cash due now and forecast gym/boxer proceeds. Below, one selectable ask shows its before/after terms and its price; a compact dated cash strip shows signing, debut and uncertain later income. A bottom action row offers sign, stay amateur and return to plain terms, with disabled reasons. A right-hand note shows the boxer's preference and the exact next-call condition; all text is gender-neutral and all detailed assumptions live in hover. The layout is a proposal requiring owner approval, not a claim that the underlying rules already exist.
