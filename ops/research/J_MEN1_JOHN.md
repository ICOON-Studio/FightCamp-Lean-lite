# J_MEN1 - John - England Boxing senior men's classes, bout format, height/reach, first bouts (S371 collected inline; the J_MEN1.md attachment stays on the bot box)

**J_MEN1 is done, and two of our four women's limits need changing.** 61.2 kg doesn't appear in any rulebook, and 66.0 kg was dropped on 1 Jan 2025. 57 and 75 are still right. I'd switch our four to **57, 60, 65 and 75 kg**, which are valid under both England Boxing and World Boxing (the international federation).

**1. Weight classes, upper limits in kg** (OFFICIAL: England Boxing rulebook, valid from 1 Jan 2025; World Boxing rules, Nov 2024)
| | Classes |
|---|---|
| England Boxing senior men (13) | 50, 52.5*, 55, 57.5*, 60, 62.5*, 65, 70, 75, 80, 85, 90, 90+ |
| England Boxing senior women (12) | 48, 51, 54, 57, 60, 62.5*, 65, 67.5*, 70, 75, 80, 80+ |
| World Boxing since 2025 | Same lists without the * classes |
| Old classes, used until the end of 2024 | Men 51–92+; women 48, 50, 52, 54, 57, 60, 63, 66, 70, 75, 81, 81+ |

The * classes are England Boxing only. Its 2025 Senior Development Championships still used the old classes, so the change isn't fully in place yet.

**Bout format** (OFFICIAL)
| Bout | Men | Women |
|---|---|---|
| Development (beginner) club bout | 3×2 or 3×3 min, no headguard | Same rounds, headguard |
| Elite (open) bout | 3×3, no headguard | 3×3, headguard |
| Senior Development Championships | 3×2, no headguard | 3×2, headguard |
| National Amateur Championships | 3×3, no headguard | 3×3, headguard |

There's no separate "novice" tier. Senior boxers are either Development (never entered the national championships) or Elite.

**3. How a man gets to his first bouts.** It's mostly the same as for women: register on England Boxing's online system with a digital record book, pass a GP medical first and then the yearly and pre-bout medicals, and get matched on experience and weight.

What's different for men:
- no headguard
- a groin guard is compulsory
- no non-pregnancy declaration
- a separate weigh-in
- entry to the Senior Development Championships needs 5–20 bouts (women 2–14)
