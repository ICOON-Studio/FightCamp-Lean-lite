# Designer critic, 14 pages (the Miro frame held no owner stickies or comments; judged from the pack and the images)

## Home: 7/10
Missing: what the clock will do before the next stop (a plain preview); a way to settle the Lucy/Aisha pad-night clash from the note itself; notes sorted by deadline.
Useless: "Reputation 41" and "Members 68/70" rows with no action attached; the pinned break-even tooltip.
To reach 10/10:
1. Sort the notes by deadline and print the date on every one, so red notes read as a queue.
2. Give Reputation and Members a real action (link to dues band on Money) or remove them.
3. Make the Today column one verb button per row; several rows show a button plus a warning triangle.
4. Show weight and freshness only when they matter (colour flag), so eight fighters fit without scrolling.
5. Add a hover reason to every triangle note that says what is not built.
6. Show the club week as In and Out numbers, not only a net.

## Fighter Week (Lucy): 8/10
Missing: a preview of the effect of a change before the commit; injury-risk hint on Hard sessions near overreach; Undo.
Useless: the "Coach runs it / I run it" toggle (agreed, not built) sits beside working buttons; "Copy from a fighter" has no stated effect.
To reach 10/10:
1. Mark Coach runs it as planned with a triangle and say what it hands over; the game cannot do it yet.
2. Show the fatigue and stat forecast change before the red commit action.
3. Make the pad-night split (Lucy 2, Aisha 1) clickable in the strip, with the cost on both names.
4. Show the coach's default for the twist card as pre-selected, with both prices in one line.
5. Add a risk number to Hard cards when the load ratio nears 1.5.
6. Show on the eight-week footer how many fighters still need an answer.

## Fighter Week (Aisha, grappler): 8/10
Missing: mat partners free each night; a grappling-specific load hint; the no-gi coach hour limit.
Useless: Lucy's night greyed out three times with the same text.
To reach 10/10:
1. Show how many mat partners are free per night; grappling needs a body to drill with.
2. Collapse other-fighter greyed nights into a thin strip with one hover reason.
3. Draw the weight cut as a line to the weigh-in; 2.6 kg over 40 days is only a note.
4. Warn when no coach can teach a grappling session (needs a hired no-gi coach).
5. Show a Cage work session's effect on standing skills, not only Clinch.

## Profile (Tomas): 7/10
Missing: where each skill is trained (the agreed "one thing to work on and where"); a link from each bar to the session that raises it; what refusing the offer costs.
Useless: Standing grappling and Ground bars drawn live though not in the game; 24 numbers is more than a player can act on; the empty bottom third.
To reach 10/10:
1. Fold the unbuilt skill groups into one closed row, not seven live-looking bars.
2. Hover each bar with the sessions that raise it and the weekly gain at the current plan.
3. Move the one-thing-to-work-on from the bottom sticky to the header.
4. Say the 10 attributes are new numbers; the game still has Hand speed and Chin (not built yet).
5. State on the offer card what refusing costs (morale, loyalty); only three buttons now.
6. Use the empty lower third for the history strip or the goal.

## Profile (Aisha, BJJ specialist): 7/10
Missing: the belt and stripe road (for BJJ the belt is the ladder); what her ground skills do in the fight; how to cross-train toward striking.
Useless: five locked perk tiles she cannot reach.

1. Add the belt road strip: belt, stripes, time to next belt.
2. Show the sport switch only once both are trained, and say so.
3. Shrink the perk road to the next perk and its need.
4. Add the weight-cut line to the fight banner beside Open her camp week.
5. Make the Rosa sticky give a second answer (stand-up defence), not only takedowns.

## Profile (Kwame, veteran): 7/10
Missing: what each answer to the retirement talk costs; what the decline changes in the plan; a legacy option (a coaching job).
Useless: the "about 3 fights left" curve has no action; every falling bar carries an arrow and a number.

## Playbook, game plan standing: 7/10
Missing: side-by-side compare of two plans; mastery number per line; an energy cost per line.
Useless: the "Rate against" row repeats the Beats it column; Find a coach buttons in the move book on a plan page.
To reach 10/10:
1. Make Beats it / Works come from the real fight rules; they are hatched guesses until scouted.
2. Say "unscouted" once at the top, not on every row.
3. Move Find a coach to Club or keep one hint.
4. Show drilled as a 0-100 number so the player sees why a line is weak.
5. Add Duplicate plan to try a variant.
6. Say which part is "not built in the fight yet"; the label sits beside built features.

## Playbook, cage and ground: 7/10

## Fight night (corner minute and result): 7/10
