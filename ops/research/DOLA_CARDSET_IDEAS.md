# Dola - ideas: the card set for a card-game version of FightCamp (boxing first) (S536)

FightCamp is a boxing gym management game: the player is the club's head coach. He trains a boxer through camp weeks
(Technique, Power, Conditioning, Defence, Speed), writes a fight plan, shouts one call every 30 seconds in a round, and changes
the plan in the corner between rounds. Boxers have Power, Speed, Stamina, Chin, Technique, Defence, a style (Out-boxer,
Pressure, Counter-puncher, Volume puncher) and habits an opponent can read on film. Amateur bouts 3 x 3 min.

The owner wants a CARD-GAME version as a prototype, boxing only, other martial arts later. A coder is building v1 now: camp
weeks add cards to the boxer's fight deck; each exchange both corners play one card face down and reveal together (punches,
defence, footwork); scouting reveals the opponent's habits so reading him pays; a corner card between rounds.

Please give, in your own words:
1. A first card set of about 30 cards for boxing: name, kind, cost, effect in one line, and what beats it / what it beats.
   Every card must be a TRADE whose value depends on the opponent, the round or the read - no card that is always good.
2. 6 camp cards (what training adds to the deck) and 6 corner cards (between rounds), each with its cost.
3. How a boxer's STYLE and 2 HABITS should change which cards he favours, so a player who read the film can predict him.
4. What would make this fun to replay for 20 bouts, and the 3 things most likely to make it boring or solved.
5. How the same frame could take kickboxing / Muay Thai / MMA later (which card kinds are added, which change).
Plain words, a table for the card list. Under about 1,500 words.
