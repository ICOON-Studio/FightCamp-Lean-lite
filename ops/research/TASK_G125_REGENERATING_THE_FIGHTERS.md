## What would a human do?

A-pose, real proportions for the weight class), keeps one lighting and material setup across the roster, and checks
the rig on a few extreme poses before signing it off. A promotion's photographer shoots every roster headshot the
same way - same lens, light, crop and backdrop - so the cards read as a set. Find out how they actually do both.
