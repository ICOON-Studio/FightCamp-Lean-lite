# Injury badges per body part - the owner's pick from a reference game (S570)

Owner S570, with a screenshot of a reference card game's fighter panel (two red round badges under the bars: a leg with 1,
a torso with 2; the torso's tooltip: an injury that makes body attacks cost him more energy, going down 1 on rest):
"nice debuff ideas".

## The idea, in our words (nothing of theirs copied)
A hurt body part carries a small counter you can read at a glance - a badge on the fighter's panel, never on the fighter
(S562 rule: nothing near the fighters). Each point makes one kind of thing dearer, and the counter heals 1 at the round
break. It turns "his body shots add up" into something both players SEE and can plan around.

## How it could map onto Puncher's chance (to measure, not built)
- Body: each point = his stamina bar one shorter for the beat after a body shot lands (today a landed body shot shortens
  the bar for the bout, never below 4) - the badge would show what is already there and let it heal a little.
- Legs (kicks page): the limp counter we already keep (4 landed low kicks = no back off) shown as a badge 1..4.
- Head: each point = 1 less health back after a knockdown (N.kd), heals 1 at the break.
- Ties to BUILD1 (the body as the ship): a badge greys or weakens the cards that part holds - the "hurt part" the BUILD1
  design already plans to measure (a landed body shot greys one Core card for the round).

## Before it is built
MECHANICS NEED 8: probe on the balance tool first (one tweak: badges + their heal), the nine bars + the comeback share
(a badge must not turn one landed body shot into a lost bout), then two fresh critics. Feedback loop to cap: badge ->
weaker defence -> more badges; the cap is the heal at the break and a ceiling of 3 a part.
