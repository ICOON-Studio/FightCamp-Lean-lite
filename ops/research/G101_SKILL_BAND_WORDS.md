## What would a human do?

A real coach, scout, or matchmaker does not say "sixty." They say the fighter's place on a card and state that week.

Out loud, level is two sentences mixed together:
- Grade / circuit: novice, development, club, open, regional, national, international amateur, then pro small-hall → regional → national → world.
- Role on the card: opponent / tomato can, journeyman, gatekeeper, prospect, fringe contender, contender, challenger, champion.

Amateur federations grade by age class + bout cap, not a 0–100: England Boxing Development vs Elite (NAC entry locks you out of Dev); USA Boxing gym talk still uses novice (~0–10) then open/elite; Germany C/B/A by wins; IMMAF pathway is novice → beginner → intermediate → elite amateur → pro.

Condition is a different sentence: fresh, sharp, peaked, camp-ready, flat, stale, rusty, overtrained, not cleared, plus green/amber/red on the cut.

Games almost never print "60 = national." FM names 1–20 familiarity bands and relative stars. UFC 5 hides 0–100 and shows 3.5–5★. Fight Night prints a raw overall (~74 club-ish to 96 all-time). EA FC colours cards bronze/silver/gold.

That is the human mapping S78 needs: number → the word the corner would actually use.

Spoken first: club fighter, open class, national standard, he's a prospect, that's a gatekeeper, he's gone stale, he's sharp.

## 1. Real-world ladder of level words

Tag: SOURCED (federation rules + established gym/press lexicon) with PROXY where bout counts vary by country.

Two ladders run in parallel and get mixed in speech: amateur grade (bout-count / class) and career role (what the fighter is for on a card).

### Amateur grade — boxing

England Boxing (2025 rule book + club matrix):

| Spoken class | Meaning | Bout / entry notes |
|---|---|---|
| Skills / minors | Teaching bout, half-credit | Skills = ½ contest on the card |
| Development / Class A–B | First real contests | Junior Dev often 2–10 bouts; Youth Dev male 2–14 |
| Senior Development | Limited senior experience | Male: min ~5 EB-equivalent contests, max 20 bouts; 3×2 min |
| Elite / Open / NAC | National championship class | Entering EBNAC locks the boxer out of Development; 3×3 min |

USA Boxing (current national events + older local class still used in gyms):

| Spoken class | Meaning | Bout notes |
|---|---|---|
| Novice | First competitive window | Historic local rule: 0–10 bouts; at 5 some could elect Open |
| Open / Senior | Beyond novice | Historic: 11+ bouts forced Senior |
| Elite | Olympic-style national pool | 19–40; National Open elite male often min 10–15 bouts + win% floor |
| High Performance / Team USA | International amateur | Selection camps after nationals — not a bout-count class |

Germany (DBV Leistungsklassen, still cited): C <7 wins; B ≤14 wins; A >14 wins; Elite if 18+ and >25 wins or international vest.

WKO-style amateur (kick/MMA-adjacent, useful proxy): C novice → B intermediate → A elite amateur.

Coach speech for amateurs: novice, development, open, elite, national squad, international amateur. "Club amateur" is gym talk for a regular show fighter who is not national class.

### Amateur grade — MMA

IMMAF / national federations (Ireland IMMAA is the cleanest published split):

| Spoken class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Novice / Yellow | First contact, often restricted (no head shots) |
| Beginner / Orange | Full amateur rules, not yet regional-card ready |
| Intermediate / Green | Several amateur bouts, not IMMAF team |
| Elite amateur / Blue+ | National team / IMMAF pathway |
| Pro | Paid / licensed / contracted — ineligible for IMMAF |

IMMAF itself is amateur-only; "regional pro" and "local show" sit under national commissions, not IMMAF grades.

### Pro / card role — boxing and MMA (same words)

Tag: SOURCED (Wikipedia journeyman; Fighters Only eight types; Bad Left Hook typology; Boxing Unboxed gatekeeper).

Lowest → highest, as said out loud:

| Word | What it means | Typical signal |
|---|---|---|
| Gym / recreational | Trains, does not compete | 0 bouts |
| Novice / debut / rookie | First cards; nerves, holes | 0–few bouts |
| Club fighter | Local shows, club championships | Regular amateur or small-hall pro |
| Regional | Area / state / regional title or circuit | Area belt, regular regional cards |
| National | National amateur title, or domestic pro rank | ABA/EB NAC, Golden Gloves, national ranking |
| International amateur | Vests, multi-nations, worlds/Olympics path | National team |
| Tomato can / opponent | Built to lose; not a real test | Inflated prospect records |
| Journeyman | Competent pro, not contender/gatekeeper class; short-notice body | Often 10+ years in; wins vs lesser, loses vs better |
| Gatekeeper | Measuring stick below title class; beating him is a ticket | Ranked-ish, expected to test prospects |
| Prospect | Young, winning, believed to reach contender | Protected record, rising opposition |
| Fringe contender | On the rankings, not yet a real title threat | Top 15-ish, inconsistent |
| Contender | Believed to have a night against the champion | Top 10, title-eliminator type |
| Challenger | Has the shot (or has earned it) | Booked for the belt |
| World class / ranked | Consistently at world level | World ranking, world-level wins |
| Champion / elite | Holds or recently held major title; division standard | Belt, or p4p aura |

MMA press uses the same role words. Extra MMA-only: prelim fighter, gate, ranked, title shot, pound-for-pound.

Do not collapse role into number. A 60-proficiency veteran can be a journeyman; a 60-proficiency 19-year-old can be a prospect. Tooltip band = current standard, not destiny. Potential carries destiny.

## 2. Condition / readiness words (day-of / camp)

Tag: SOURCED (Steward overtraining talk; Boxing Science taper; H&G Boxing overtraining; camp vernacular).

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Broken / not cleared | Injury, medical hold, cannot spar |
| Overtrained | Volume past repair; speed gone, mood flat, weight falling too easy |
| Stale | Taper missed or camp too long; no snap |
| Rusty | Time off; timing late, wind untested |
| Flat | Grey-zone work; no pop, no spark |
| Heavy / sluggish | Legs gone, hands slow (often cut or fatigue) |
| Serviceable / fit enough | Can do the rounds, not peaking |
| Camp-ready | Engine built, not yet sharpened |
| Sharp | Timing, speed, reactions back |
| Peaked | Taper landed; this is the window |
| Fresh / fight-night ready | Recovered, weight done, snap on |
| Green (weight) | Comfortably on course for the cut |
| Amber / red (weight) | Cut getting dangerous |

Steward's tell: early camp crisp, late camp the same sparring partners start tagging him → overtrained, not "tougher."

## 3. How three games map numbers to words

### Football Manager (1–20 attributes + 0.5–5 stars)

Tag: SOURCED (SI manual positional bands; FM Scout star hover text; community 13/15/17 tiers are PROXY).

Attributes 1–20. Official positional familiarity bands:

| 1–20 | Word |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | Makeshift |
| 5–8 | Awkward |
| 9–11 | Unconvincing |
| 12–14 | Competent |
| 15–17 | Accomplished |
| 18–20 | Natural |

Star hover (role ability, relative to your squad): 1 Below Average → 1.5 Average → 2 Decent → 2.5 Fairly Good → 3 Good → 3.5 Impressive → 4 Excellent → 4.5 Superb → 5 First-class.

Community playing-level tiers (not official, widely used): 13 usable at this level / 15 good / 17 elite at this level. Thresholds slide with league.

Current Ability is 1–200 behind the stars. Stars are relative; 1–20 is absolute-ish.

### EA Sports UFC 5 (0–100 stats, 0.5–5 stars OVR)

Tag: SOURCED (producer Raman Bassi; published division star lists).

Hidden 0–100 per striking / grappling / health stat. Shown OVR is stars, not a 0–100 overall. 5★ reserved for all-time / peak alter-egos (Jones, Khabib, GSP legacy, Volk, Nunes…). Typical roster: 3.5–4.5★. 100 on a single stat is "best of the best forever" (e.g. Anderson Silva head movement). Community complaint: compression — most names live 90+ on the hidden 100s, so the word layer is the star, not the integer.

No public official table of "72 = national." Stars are the band words.

### Fight Night Champion + EA FC card tiers (third)

Tag: SOURCED (published FNC overalls; FIFA/FC bronze–gold card bands).

Fight Night Champion: overall ~70–96 on licensed boxers. Butterbean 74, solid contenders mid-80s, all-time 90–96 (Robinson 96, Ali 93). No published word legend; the number is the prestige band. World-class starts ~88–90.

EA FC / FIFA Ultimate Team card colours (community + Chinese official companion copy):

| OVR | Card word |
|---|---|
| <60 | Bronze |
| 60–69 | Silver |
| 70–79 | Gold |
| 80–89 | Elite / high gold |
| 90–99 | Master / icon territory |

FC 26 squad-star map (team rating → stars): 60–62 = 1★, 75–78 = 4★, 83+ = 5★. Individual 80+ is "elite player," 90+ is "world class / face of the game."

Takeaway for FightCamp: games either (a) keep a tight 1–20 with named familiarity bands (FM), (b) hide 0–100 and show stars (UFC 5), or (c) colour-band 0–99 (FC). None publish "60 = national amateur." That sentence is ours to invent from the real ladder.

## 4. Proposed FightCamp band tables

Tag: JUDGEMENT built on the sourced ladders above.

Design rules: 6 bands each (fits tooltip); short coach words; 60 lands on national / solid open for proficiency, as the brief asked; condition is state-of-day not career; potential is ceiling, not now; morale is dressing-room, not skill.

### PROFICIENCY (0–100) — what he is now

| Range | Band word | Tag | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–19 | Gym novice | JUDGEMENT | Recreational / uncarded |
| 20–39 | Club amateur | PROXY | Local shows, development class |
| 40–54 | Regional | PROXY | Area circuit, open but not national |
| 55–69 | National | JUDGEMENT | Open/elite amateur or domestic pro standard. 60 lives here. |
| 70–84 | International | PROXY | Vest / ranked / prospect–gatekeeper |
| 85–100 | World class | SOURCED | Contender–champion language |

Tooltip example: Now: 60 → National

### CONDITION (0–100 %) — camp / day state

| Range | Band word | Tag | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–19 | Not cleared | SOURCED | Medical hold / broken |
| 20–39 | Overtrained | SOURCED | Steward / H&G signs |
| 40–54 | Flat | SOURCED | No snap, grey-zone |
| 55–69 | Camp-ready | PROXY | Fit enough, not peaked. 61% lives here. |
| 70–84 | Sharp | SOURCED | Timing back |
| 85–100 | Fight-night | SOURCED | Taper landed, fresh |

### FIGHT IQ (0–100) — ring brain

| Range | Band word | Tag | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–19 | Lost in there | JUDGEMENT | Panic, no plan |
| 20–39 | Raw | PROXY | Athletic, late reads |
| 40–54 | Learning | PROXY | Needs the corner. 45 lives here. |
| 55–69 | Ring-wise | JUDGEMENT | Makes the right fight |
| 70–84 | High IQ | PROXY | Sets traps, adjusts mid-round |
| 85–100 | General | JUDGEMENT | Coach-in-the-ring |

### POTENTIAL (0–100) — ceiling a scout would say

| Range | Band word | Tag | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–19 | Limited | JUDGEMENT | Club ceiling |
| 20–39 | Club ceiling | PROXY | Honest local |
| 40–54 | Regional ceiling | PROXY | Area title type |
| 55–69 | National ceiling | JUDGEMENT | Domestic standard |
| 70–84 | World prospect | SOURCED | 72 lives here. Scout word: prospect |
| 85–100 | Generational | PROXY | Title-or-bust ceiling |

### MORALE (0–100)

| Range | Band word | Tag | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–19 | Checked out | PROXY | Quit in the eyes |
| 20–39 | Sour | PROXY | Camp friction |
| 40–54 | Flat | PROXY | Showing up, not hunting |
| 55–69 | Professional | JUDGEMENT | Does the work |
| 70–84 | Hungry | SOURCED | Standard fight-week word |
| 85–100 | Locked in | PROXY | Nothing else in the head |

### Worked tooltip (the brief's example)

Fighter shows PROFICIENCY 60, CONDITION 61%, FIGHT IQ 45, POTENTIAL 72.
- PROFICIENCY — current standard. Now: 60 → National. Why: open/elite amateur or honest domestic pro; not yet international.
- CONDITION — today's body. Now: 61% → Camp-ready. Why: fit enough to take a date; not sharp, not stale.
- FIGHT IQ — reads and choices. Now: 45 → Learning. Why: still needs the corner to call the fight.
- POTENTIAL — scout ceiling. Now: 72 → World prospect. Why: the number says the ceiling is above the present.

Role word (prospect / journeyman / gatekeeper) is not a function of one scale. Pair PROFICIENCY with age and record if S78 ever needs a role line.
