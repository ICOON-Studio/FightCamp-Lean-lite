# Loop drawings, S442: the three cheapest "visually engaging" steps, drawn on today's game

The owner liked the table "traditional menus vs visually engaging" for the three loop phases and said yes to drawings of the
three cheapest steps. Each drawing shows a change that can be built on today's game, from the real bout the result-page
work dumped (`docs/mockups/s442/result/bout.json`: Lucy Jackson v Priya Nair, Sat 7 Mar, lost on a split 28-29, 28-29, 29-28).

| Drawing | Picture | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Preparation: the corner minute's line change as two cards | `docs/mockups/s442/loop/corner_cards.png` | `corner_cards.html` |
| 2. Execution: reaction bubbles in the 3D fight | `docs/mockups/s442/loop/fight_bubbles.png` | `fight_bubbles.html` |
| 3. Resolution: a headline card before the result page (a loss and a milestone win) | `docs/mockups/s442/loop/headline_card.png` | `headline_card.html` |

The HTML files are self-contained (Barlow, Bebas Neue and IBM Plex Mono embedded; the portraits are the game's faces, cropped as
`docs/mockups/s442/result/build_result.py` does). They were generated by a builder kept outside the repo (as briefed:
`C:\Users\user\AppData\Local\Temp\claude\17518d645d82a59e\4b0bbd1c-7ccf-42d4-8e65-2f49ca84d744\scratchpad\build_loop.py`). It re-reads every number from bout.json
and asserts it (for example, its line C options must equal the edges the game itself wrote into `bout.json plan[2].options`).
The builder also blocks text under 16 px on screen and the words he/she/her/him/engine. Rendered with `tools/render_mockup.py`. Nothing committed.

Rules kept in all three: one gold primary that breathes (Pulse.gd); green = good / Beats it, amber = fair / Works / untrained,
red = poor / Walks into it / Risky (CallButtons.GRADE_COLOR, PlanLines.FIT_WORDS); red and blue = the two corners; purple =
an achievement, never a button; cream outline = hover. Staff by role ("Coach"), "Cardio" never "Engine". A dashed blue strip
at the bottom of each picture is a note, outside the game frame.

## 1. Corner cards (PREPARATION)

**What it shows.** The 3D corner card after round 2 (the minute before round 3), the way CornerOverlay draws it today, with
the PLAN row rebuilt. Today that row is one dropdown ("Keep the plan" plus every other move, `CornerJobsRow._plan_row`,

**What is new (the PLAN row only):**
- A header strip: the line that lost most, with its letter and tally, "C · LOST MOST · One-two-hook 1 of 2", and what the
  opponent showed, "Opponent waited to counter".
- Two swap cards: Double jab ("Beats it", +8 over improvising, C was -6, "Untrained: lands 80%") and Step out ("Works",
  +4, "Ends at: Outside"). Each card has a worth bar on the plan's own scale (PlanFight.EDGE, -7..+9), with a red tick where line C stands.
  The whole card is one click ("Swap into C").
- "Keep the plan" (selected by default, as today) and "Other changes" (today's dropdown, kept as the fallback for any other change).
- The ring strip ("Where the lines fire"): StrikeMap's eight places with the plan letters on their place (A and C at Long

  Long range, and the tile opens a second line, "C → Double jab". This is the table's "synergy line".

  Note: the S442 UX audit chose NOT to pulse corner calls (UX_AUDIT_S442.md, Pulse table). This drawing proposes the starred

**Stays as today:** the top bar (clock, energy), the stats, the coach's line, Recover, Set-up, Talk, One change, the eight calls.

## What could not be grounded in the game
- The show's name: the game has none. The line names the night, the date, the weight and the rounds.
- The talk effects in the corner: drawn at the default personality (50 / 50 / 50).
- Legs and guard numbers, "lasts to R3", punches thrown, the worst spot: not in the bout record, left out.
- The rank position on entering, and the opponent's rank after the milestone replay.
- "Heard" in the storyboard assumes Lucy's mood is not Low (CornerTrust.lands), which the dump does not record.
- The milestone variant's cause line is not FightWhy's; FightWhy was not run on the replay.
- **LineSwap as coded would draw a misleading card here** (rated against "Your fighter was hurt"). The drawing shows the fix,
  and the fix is part of drawing 1's build.
