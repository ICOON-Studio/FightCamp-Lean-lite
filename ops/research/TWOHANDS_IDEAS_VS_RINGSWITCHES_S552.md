# Kalli's and John's defence ideas, read against Ring Switches (S552)

Both were asked BEFORE Ring Switches existed (SQ326 Kalli, SQ328 John: five no-timing defence mechanics each;
`TWOHANDS_DEFENCE_IDEAS_BRIEF_KALLI.md`, `_JOHN.md`). The question now is only: what would ADD to the owner's game, not
replace it. Nothing below is built; the toy's rules are his.

## What they confirm
Both, independently, picked the same thing to build first: **the hand that does not punch is your guard** (Kalli 1 "Glove
Home", John 1 "The spare hand"). That is Ring Switches' own core rule ("a hand left off defends"). Two seats reached the
owner's idea without seeing it.

## Three things that would add (most worth first)
1. **A landed jab keeps him out** (K1 "Range", J2 "Range"). Today a jab is 1 health and a point. Rule to try: a jab that
   lands this beat stops his step in next beat (he pays for it and stays where he is). It gives the jab its real job and
   gives the Jabber a way to hold Long without backing into the ropes. One line in `resolve`.
2. **The player's own read** (K5, J2). Today only the machine reads habits. Rule to try: when HE repeats (the page already
   prints "Last beat he did"), the hold that stops it is marked on the board, and blocking a read punch makes the counter
   +2 instead of +1. It rewards watching him, with no new control.
3. **The ring as a track you can see** (K3, J5). Today "the ropes" is a tag that appears. A five-square strip under the
   stage (centre to each man's ropes) would show what backing off costs before it is paid. Picture work more than rules.

## Not taken
- K2 "how you finish is how you stand" and J3 "one guard call per combination": both need turn-taking leads; Ring Switches
  has both men act at once.
- K4 two-faced cards, J1's defence CARDS: decks are parked (METHOD: no decks).
