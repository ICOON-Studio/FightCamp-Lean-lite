RAIL RECOMMENDATION

ONE rail item: GYM — replaces MY GYM + FIGHTERS. Hub tabs ROSTER / FLOOR; STORE is a drawer/tab off FLOOR. Keeps the rail shorter, matches “home you return to every day,” and puts the profile one click from the squad row. Keeping both rail items would duplicate entry points into the same hub and fight the kit’s single-active-rail pattern.
