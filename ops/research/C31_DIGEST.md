# C31 DIGEST — the corner

Research only. No `.gd`, `.tscn`, data, test or `HANDOFF.md` file was touched.

**Finding:** a corner is a commission-licensed event assignment, not merely an
employed coach, and there is no universal corner count or towel rule. ABC makes
the referee the official stopper while recognizing corner stoppage as a result;
California permits a manager/chief second to toss a towel, but New York MMA
treats towel throwing as interference and New Jersey MMA reserves stoppage to
the referee/physician.

**Recommendation:** do not interrupt R46. Before adding another live corner
command, ship a small event-rule/assignment read for eligible chief second,
optional cut capability, allowed count/access and stop-signal policy. Keep
`coach_rating` for instruction quality; never treat it as a licence or every
specialty.

**Money:** no primary source retrieved publishes a standard trainer, cornerman
or cutman purse percentage. J6 A4/A6/A10 stands; corner fee and gym corner
income remain UNKNOWN/absent.
