# TASK D66 - A strict review of FightCamp by a management/simulation-game critic (Dola, S131 brief -> launch S132)

Self-contained brief for a browser agent. No repo. The screenshots listed below are ATTACHED at launch.

## Your role
You are a strict, experienced reviewer of MANAGEMENT and SIMULATION games (Football Manager, Motorsport Manager, Mad Games
Tycoon 2, Game Dev Tycoon, Punch Club, Out of the Park Baseball, Boxing Club Manager, Two Point Hospital, Frostpunk).
Judge FightCamp as it is today and tell us, bluntly, where it is weak and what would make it FUN and POLISHED.

## The game (as of 2026-09-30)
FightCamp: you own a combat-sports GYM (boxing first; MMA and kickboxing later). You sign fighters, train them on a 3D
training floor (stations, mastery pips, breakthroughs, coach bond), hire staff, scout opponents, take fight offers from
real promotions (fictional fighters), run a fight camp and fight week (weigh-in), then WATCH the bout live in 3D (a
real-time engine: judges, stoppages, knockdowns, a corner between rounds with jobs and dilemmas) and read the result.
Money: memberships, sponsors (gym deals by reputation tier), fight purses. Gym grows through 4 tiers; rooms behind the
floor: office, locker room, lobby, mat room, staff room, physio. The FIGHT PLAN today: five sliders set before the bout,
the opponent's coach plans too, and the result screen shows "THE PLANS" and what they changed. Built but switched off or
designed: scouting file + film room, kitchen/recovery/dorms, street and rival gyms. Demo target: 6 October 2026.
