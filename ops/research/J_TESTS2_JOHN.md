# J_TESTS2 - John - women's force, speed, bleep and bag-round values by level (S371 collected inline; the J_TESTS2.md attachment stays on the bot box)

**J_TESTS2 has filled part of the women's data, but four gaps are still open.** We now have measured punch force for youth club women and elite women. I also found club boxers' bleep scores, a measured rate of bleep gains, and the standard bag-round protocol. I found no published numbers for women beginners' punch force, beginner or intermediate punches in 10 s and 30 s, elite men's bleep level, or how many punches boxers throw per round when they pace themselves on a bag. One correction to J_TESTS1: the Korean national team women reach about level 8–9 on the bleep test, not "level 9".
