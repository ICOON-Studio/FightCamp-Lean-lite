## END JUDGEMENT

**No MEASURED (N, window) pair exists that a `FightStoppageRead` consumer can ship and TEST against as a standing-stoppage rule.**  
Rulebooks give a **qualitative** gate (“not / cannot intelligently defend”); studies give **Hutchison clocks** on different objects (post-KO delay; pre-repetitive-TKO 30 s). Standing-TKO rate remains **NOT FOUND**; doctor floor stays **K64 1.09%**; KD path stays **J20 63.5% / 3.9%**.

### Two INFERENCE candidates for the operator (not constants — candidates)

1. **Hutchison-envelope candidate (MMA, mixed stance/ground):** treat sustained failed defence as **≥ ~18 poorly defended / unanswered head-directed strikes inside a 30 s window** (source: Hutchison 2014 mean **18.5** strikes in **30 s** before repetitive-strike TKO; range around the mean unknown; **not standing-only**). Testable against video-coded UFC TKO-repetitive cases; **wrong** if used as pure standing-only truth.  
2. **Residual-share candidate (MMA bout-level, from K64 arithmetic):** expect on the order of **~9–11% of UFC bouts** to end in the non-KD / non-doctor KO/TKO residual bucket (standing referee + corner + injury + KD=0 coding) after **J20** KD-path and **K64** doctor are removed — use as a **calibration ceiling**, not a per-exchange strike rule. Standing-only slice inside that residual: **NOT FOUND**.

**Boxing judgement:** same qualitative defend/outclassed gate; **no** MEASURED N or standing-TKO share found; do not import Hutchison UFC seconds into boxing without a boxing study.

**One-liner:** *Smallest honest rule = ABC “not intelligently defending” with **no** certified N; only testable INFERENCE envelopes are Hutchison **~18 head strikes / 30 s** (mixed) or K64 residual **~9–11% of bouts** — standing-pure number **does not exist** in sources opened 2026-09-22.*
