## 1. Logic check
Things that hold: 12 days to Sat 28 Feb from Mon 16 Feb; PEAK phase two weeks out with two sparring nights is the rule; Hold 70.5 kg vs 70.0 limit makes the bout off, and Cut 69.6 has 0.4 spare; 51 + 1.5..4 = 52.5..55, shown as 53-55; Tue film "0 to +2.5" matches the range and the strip "55 -> 56.5" is consistent; Check hook 12 -> 21 (Mon), 24 (Wed), 31 (Fri) is one chain and Usable at 30 on Fri matches "6 by Fri, 4 now"; the 0.09 per morning matches a fifth of a focused week times coach and headroom. Names appear only in the header and breadcrumb.

Defects:
1. **Fixed pronouns.** Text says "her last hidden tell", "opens her weakness" (opponent) and the coach note says "I'd rest him" (the fighter). The sandbox rule (pack, last line) forbids a fixed he/she; both must read "the opponent" / "your boxer".
2. **Pad gain is not proportional.** Monday full night gives Check hook +9; Friday a full night +7; Wednesday a half night +3 on each move (a half should be about 4 to 4.5). The pad rule says the cap is 72, far away, so nothing explains the drop. Either the numbers come from the real PadWeek maths (then say "nights have different value", e.g. tired) or they are wrong.
3. **The film card names the tell's type while the film is blind.** The card says "opens her weakness", the note says the +2.5 counts only if a Usable move answers it, but the screen never says which move answers it, so the player cannot act on the one fact that decides +2.5. Not actionable (rule 7: only actionable information).
4. **Tiredness vs sparring numbers.** Sat 63 -> 68 for the dragged sparring is "+5" (flame 5) correct, but the plan's Sunday 55 "Rest 63 -> 55" is drawn from the old Sat 63; after the drop Sun should read about 59 (the picture shows 59 amber only as a stub). The solid curve and the card text still say 55.
5. **"Peak 70" chip vs the goal "55% peak 70"** are the same number; fine. But the two week goals Build 52% / Recover 49% sit beside a "coach's plan 51%" that is neither: the player cannot tell what the coach's plan is a case of.
6. **Weight row.** "Makes weight Fri 27, 69.6 kg 0.4 spare" appears with a Hold/Cut switch whose current state is not marked; the player cannot see whether Cut is on. The Cut costs something (energy / tiredness) that is not shown on the tiredness curve.
7. **Small:** the gain per morning (+0.09) is shown to two decimals while the goal shortfall is 0.1; the whole week is a fight over tenths of a point of Stamina that the player cannot feel.

## 2. As a game (6)
Good: one board, seven days, picture tiles, a tiredness line, drag a card and see the price before letting go (the best idea on the screen), green and red tags only on the player's own changes. The look matches the camp plan (dark panel, ringed icons).
Not 7 because:
- Five-second test fails. I count about 90 numbers and tags on the first view. The eye has no first place to go: the WIN dial, the goal bar, 14 morning/evening cards each with 3 to 4 marks, a curve, a strip, a right column, a note card. Text is 15 to 18 px.
- The icons are small (about 44 px) and sit beside cut-in photos that are lighter than them. Both are art, but the figure on the photo is the icon again. The ink icons match the Playbook only at a glance: the Playbook figures are big on a plain ground with a gold arrow; here they are thumbnails with a ring.
- The reward is decimals: +0.09 Stamina, +0.06 Power. No move feels like growing a boxer. Football Manager shows a bar that moves; Punch Club shows a stat jump; Motorsport Manager shows the tyre and the fuel as two big gauges. Here the only gauge that reads at distance is the WIN dial.
- Tiredness is a drawn curve under the days, far from the cards that cause it. The cards show flames and a number; the curve is read separately. A tank on each day (as my drawing) joins cause and effect.
- Intensity is a tiny L N H strip on mornings only; evenings have no intensity, so Hard evening is not a choice.
Would I enjoy planning a week here? Yes for the drag-and-see strip and the film/sparring/hook trade. No for the first minute, which reads as a form.

## 3. Depth (6)
A real decision exists: film (gives a hidden-tell point only if the right move is Usable) vs sparring (live rounds, Fight IQ, +1.5 win but injury 1 -> 3) vs rest (tiredness), the pad night split between two moves, Hold vs Cut, and the taper next week. These pull against each other and the strip shows it.
But:
- **One visible best plan.** Green tags and the WIN number make the right plan a hill to climb: put every green on, drop every red. Nothing changes between players or weeks, so it is solved after a minute. The coach's pick (51%) is a trap for the lazy and everything else is "add greens until 55".
- The pad cap rule, the hidden tell and the 0.1 Stamina shortfall are the only true gambles, and none shows risk, only the average. No range, no "if the opponent is X".
- Tiredness stays under 72 in every good plan, so it is a wall, not a trade-off. The only time it bites is the dragged sparring (68 under 72).
- Nothing the player can learn week to week: the numbers are the same whatever the fighter's habits, mood or the opponent's style. The opponent's habits sit top right but do not change a card.

## 4. Options: missing, useless, what each changes
Missing:
- **Evening intensity** (Light / Normal / Hard) and a Hard pad night that costs tiredness and risks an overreached morning.
- **Push / Hold-back on the week** as one big switch (the race-manager tyre/fuel idea in memory S442): "Push now, pay on Fri".
- **Show the risk of the film.** A film card that says which of your moves would answer the tell once found (e.g. "needs a Usable check hook") so the 0 to +2.5 is a choice, not a lottery.
- **Opponent-fit tag on each session**, e.g. "answers her habit" (the habit is on screen, no card is linked to it).
- **Two-fighter view**: scope says four fighters; the screen is one boxer with no tab for the others and no shared coach nights (agreed, not built).
- **Next week preview** is only a line of text; the taper is the biggest choice and has no drawing.
- A **what-if / compare** button: coach's plan vs yours as two columns of the final numbers.
Useless or weak:
- The three week goals Peak / Build / Recover: Build and Recover are never the right choice 12 days out; they are dead options on this screen.
- The goal bar and the Stamina medal repeat the same number three times (header, right column, week).
- "Weekly card unanswered" flag in the top bar competes with RUN THE WEEK; it belongs on the day it affects.
- The colour key at the bottom: the rings already show it; show a stat word on hover only.
- The "Neck" and "Mobility" cards: no number says what they change.

## 5. To reach 10/10 (most important first)
1. **One hero, one first look.** Put a large fighter figure with six stat rings (Stamina, Power, Speed, Defence, Technique, Fight IQ) in the left or middle: each ring shows now, forecast by the bout and the gap, and fills live as cards drop. The five-second answer to "what does this week improve" is the rings moving.
2. **Cards, not thumbnails.** The sessions are a hand of big playing cards (about 120 px, the ink figure large on a plain ground with a gold arrow, as the Playbook). Placed on a day they shrink to a big icon plus ONE number (the stat gain in its colour). Cut every second number off the day tile: tiredness belongs to the tank, not the card.
3. **A tank under every day, with the day's tiredness.** Replace the single curve with seven vertical tanks (fill = tiredness, a line at 72, red above) so cause and effect sit on one column; the dragged card shows a ghost fill on the day. Keep the coach's tank as a grey ghost.
4. **Make the choices real.** Add evening intensity, a one-switch Push / Hold back on the week with its price in the tanks, and make the film show which move answers the tell (or a plain "unknown, 1 in 3"). Show risk as a range, never only an average.
5. **Break the single best plan.** Link each session to the opponent's habits, to the fighter's habit and to his mood so that the best card differs by week; show two or three "goals this week" (a stat to reach, a move to make Usable, a tiredness ceiling) as medals above the board, each tickable. Green = done. The player then chooses which medal to give up, which is a trade-off, not a hill.
6. **Drag-and-see stays, and gets bigger.** The strip that costs and buys before the drop is the strongest piece; make it one line under the cursor (win, tank, injury) and tint the receiving day.
7. **Fix the logic.** Pronouns neutral; one explanation for pad gains (full night about +7 to +9, half a night about half, tired nights less); mark the Hold/Cut state and show what Cut costs; Sunday after the drop reads 59.
8. **Cut text to a third.** One line of coach's words (handwritten, who is sure and how much); hover shows the rest. No colour key line. One place for the goal.
9. **Next week as a mini row** (seven tiny tanks, the taper) so the player sees the week after.
10. Needs the game to do more: a record of how the last similar week went (for example "last time Hard Friday cost 4 tiredness"), and a four-fighter tab with shared coach nights. Neither exists yet.
