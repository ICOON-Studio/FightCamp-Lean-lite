## pro_left_jab

**coach: 3/10, power 2/10.** A slow, careful reach: the lead hand drifts out over about 6 tiles, stays parked out for 5 tiles, then floats home, with no step and no snap.
To reach 10:
1. Cut the start: the glove must leave the chin on tile 1 and be fully out by tile 3 (about 130 ms); today tiles 1 to 6 (about 400 ms) are a slow lift that tells the opponent it is coming.
2. Cut the time out at full reach from 5 tiles (tiles 7 to 11, about 330 ms) to 1 tile, then snap back faster than it went out: home in 2 to 3 tiles.
3. Add a short lead-foot step forward (a few inches) landing on the same frame the arm locks out, so the body weight travels with the hand.
4. Stop the whole upper body turning its back to the camera: a jab turns the lead shoulder in a quarter turn only; keep the chest more open to the camera.
5. Keep the rear glove glued to the chin the whole time (it drifts forward on tiles 7 to 11).
6. Turn the fist over (palm down) in the last tile before full reach and let the elbow lock fully; today the arm reads soft.
7. Trim the settle: tiles 15 to 22 are 8 tiles of nearly identical guard; end the clip about 4 tiles after the hand is home.

**animator: 4/10, power 3/10.** She turns her whole back to the target, waits almost half a second, then pushes a long straight arm out, leaves it hanging there a third of a second, and both gloves drift forward on the way home.
To reach 10:
1. Cut the wind-up from 6 tiles (400 ms) to 1 or 2 tiles: the jab must leave the guard with no visible preparation.
2. Arm out in 2 tiles, fully straight for 1 tile at most, home in 2 to 3 tiles. Now it stays straight for 5 tiles (tiles 7-11).
3. Cut the shoulder turn roughly in half: the lead shoulder rolls up to cover the chin, but her back must never square to the target as it does in tiles 7-11. That much turn belongs to a cross.
4. Pin the rear glove to the right cheek for the whole punch. In tiles 11-13 it floats forward beside the jabbing hand.
5. Add a short slide of the lead foot (about half a boot length) as the glove leaves, landing on the frame the arm is straightest, with the rear foot following after.
6. Turn the fist over in the last tile so the knuckles are flat at the end of the punch, and let the head move slightly off the centre line.

**player: 3/10, power 2/10.** A slow, polite reach: she spends about 6 tiles (400 ms) turning her whole back to us before the arm is out, holds it straight for 5 tiles (330 ms), and the rear glove drifts forward with it.
To reach 10:
1. Cut the build-up from 6 tiles to 1-2: the lead glove should leave the chin on tile 2 and be fully out by tile 4 (about 130 ms of travel).
