# Knowledge stakes v5 — what does the coach do differently?

## 1. What the game does today

- **CODE — `scripts/rules/FighterProfile.gd:65 make`, `scripts/rules/CoachNotes.gd:26 reveal, :117 _build_pool, :131 _pick_unseen`.** The boxer gets two habit IDs. Knowing one is separate from having it: the notebook appends `habit:<id>` to `notes_seen`. A fight note takes the first unseen item (habits before notable traits); a film note draws only from habits. A sparring *reveal call* has a 35% gate, not a guaranteed note per scheduled spar. `scripts/state/Sim.gd:293 end_week` calls it when the club has a coach.
- **CODE — `scripts/rules/HabitFix.gd:42 punished, :62 fixable, :67 offers`.** First matching habit wins, not the sum. Seventeen match situations, including switching stance under pressure (press), talking when ahead (counter), and stopping after one shot (press). Two match time: slow start on indices 0–1 in round one; late fade on indices 3 onward in the last round (fourth exchange onward). Going to the body when hurt is the one with no debit. Late fade is eligible for the corner; slow start and the body habit are not. Offers require ownership and a seen note.
- **CODE — `scripts/state/LiveRound.gd:155 exchange` (193–211, 228, 278); `scripts/rules/HabitFix.gd:55 cost`; `scripts/rules/Exchanges.gd:21 HABIT_BOUT, :249 uptake`.** The unfixed habit takes 6 × 1.75 = 10.5 off the exchange margin and blocks the live read. A matching named fix sets the debit to **zero** before settlement. Do not quote a partial saving: uptake is 0.7 + 0.3 × clamped coachability/100, halved after two shouts for a shouted call, then scaled by trust in the live call. The fallback cost expression passes zero prior shouts and a half factor if hot; on a mismatch `cost` ignores hold. Neither modifier restores a debit on a match.
- **CODE — `scripts/state/LiveRound.gd:392 close`; `scripts/rules/HabitFix.gd:91 result`.** The bell counts paid habits, adds missing notes, and returns a separate match count and `fix_hold` based on uptake and trust. The result text uses that hold percentage, not the margin actually saved. Knowing alone never pays back an exchange.
- **CODE — `scripts/rules/Recruit.gd:130 apply_focus`; `scripts/state/Sim.gd:293 end_week`; `scripts/ui/FightAnalysisOwn.gd:161 fix_on_pads`.** Signing can schedule removal of the *first* habit after four weeks instead of +10 signature mastery (cap 100) or +5 potential (cap 99). The weekly settlement erases the due ID. Signing is **not** the last chance: own-film analysis can schedule a named habit on the same timer if none is in progress. Neither timer counts completed drills.
- **CODE — `scripts/rules/KnowReads.gd:24 price, :56 apply, :105 _apply_trial, :114 _apply_test_day, :126 risk`.** Look closer ($120) checks an attribute group or hype; test day ($60) adds body exposure, doubled under five bouts; trial ($500) adds body and skills exposure and some under-fire exposure if open. Recheck ($40) addresses stale knowledge. Club/outside hard spar ($0/$160) study performance under fire; their risk record says double injury risk and +15 tiredness. These reads update attribute knowledge, not habit notes.
- **CODE — `scripts/rules/RoundStyle.gd:101 opponent_habits`; `scripts/rules/OppFile.gd:71 tells, :135 tape, :147 half_tape, :174 remember`; `scripts/rules/ModeTells.gd:53 order`.** The other corner has a file, not our notebook. Weakness/threat words supply up to two distinct habits. Full tape opens the next hidden tell: weapon, leading non-free mode if shown, habits, remaining mode if shown, weakness. Two halves open one tell; the file can be remembered for a rematch.
- **CODE — `scripts/rules/PlanLines.gd:159 tell_sit, :132 strength`.** Seven opponent habits have situation lookups: fade, slow start and admiring work → tiring; dropped right → range; lunging jab → press; digging body and loading up when hurt → hurt. This rates an *answer to their habit*, not the situation that taxes ours. A trained line contributes its value minus the place's average to plan edge; merely opening a tell adds no such points.

## 3. Decisions worth paying for (all ESTIMATE)

**Before signing — buy evidence or save the stipend.** “Call the old gym” costs $120 once to check one report. Generate a plausible rumour independently of verified ownership so the call can disprove it; never read out the second habit. Store the answer on the prospect file, not as something our coach saw. Buy it if it could switch our focus from signature mastery to removing a recurring debit; pass if cash is tight or both outcomes give the same signing decision. Keep the six attribute reads in attribute shopping, label the actual group (test day is body, not skills); suppress them in the habit question. CUT a second paid watch-spar button: same pre-signing decision, and a risky hard spar is not habit evidence.

**Before accepting — take this purse now or wait for a better fit.** Compare only confirmed, still-owned habits with the opponent style's situation shares. At share ≥0.45 flag amber, ≥0.50 red: “The dropped right costs power when the opponent waits to counter.” Use the highest overlapping share, not a sum of overlapping habits. Never call that share a win probability. Show clock risks separately even without a style match; state risks say *if* our boxer tires/is hurt or *if the opponent* is hurt. Unknown means no warning, not safe. Green “No habit tax” belongs on the body habit, not on the whole offer. CUT purse or morale bonuses for knowing a fact: a different booking and a different exchange margin are the stakes.

**During the week — protect margin or sharpen a move.** Put a confirmed costly habit in an evening pad slot instead of a move or combination. Charge normal pad fatigue; award no displaced move mastery. Each completed weekly drill cuts that habit's open debit by 25% of its initial value; after four completed weeks retire it. No credit on rest or missed weeks. Reuse the own-film route to assign the drill; keep the prepaid signing focus as the alternative. Corner match still zeros the *remaining* cost, never pays twice. If the bout is tomorrow and the habit rarely matches, sharpening a near-ready move beats buying slow protection.

**During camp — watch our spar or spend that coach night on their film.** Watch a *situation* such as pressure, not a hidden name. On a coached spar, test for an unseen matching habit at 60% instead of the broad 35% roll; on a miss no consolation reveal. Normal spar fatigue, no extra evening. For the same coach night, targeted film after the weapon can test a suspected situation on their file instead: open one matching tell, otherwise record “not seen on this film”; forgo spar and its training gain. Alone, keep half-tape progress. Spar when a recurring own debit needs a fix; choose film when an opponent answer would change the trained line and hence plan edge. If neither would change the plan, keep the recovery night.

**At the bell — protect the next round, not the last one.** If pressure is easing but the final round is next, late fade can matter more than the rope habit that just cost power. Offer late fade only before the final round, even in a short bout. Offer a known slow start in *pre-bout* instructions only, consuming the opening fix slot. CUT two-round carry and a post-round-one slow-start fix: neither creates a second opening minute. For each exchange store the unfixed debit after drills and actual debit. Sum their difference: “Came up twice; saved 21 of 21 power” for two full-cost matches; zero on a miss. Do not translate points directly to rounds won.

## 5. Check before tuning (ESTIMATE; not results)

Use paired seeds in the exchange balance bot: 1,000 bouts per cell, equal stats, press-heavy and counter-heavy opponents, four separate habits (dropped right, ropes, slow start, late fade) versus no habit. Hold calls and plans fixed; compare blind, known but unused, named fix, then trained plan plus fix. Log trigger opportunities, debits, saved power, blocked reads and wins. Blind and unused knowledge should settle identically. Targets per 100: about four fewer wins unprotected, two with a realistically timed fix, within half a win with both answers. An always-matching fix should show zero debit, not the partial target. If the open loss exceeds six across paired repeats, trial 5 power instead of 10.5 and rerun; don't retune a clock gate to hide a counting bug.
