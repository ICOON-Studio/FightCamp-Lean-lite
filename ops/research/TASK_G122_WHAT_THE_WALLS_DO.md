## Why
The game's gym grows by wings now (tiers 3-4, S88). The design notes (Pinterest boards of real gyms) found that the walls
carry a gym's character - murals, slogans, fight posters, neon, a trophy wall with the belts it won - and the game has
none of it. A later stage makes DECOR a buildable layer. Before anyone designs it, we need what real owners do and what
it actually buys them.

## What would a human do?
An owner with a few thousand spare dollars decides between another bag and a wall: he hangs his champions' posters and
belts where walk-ins see them from the door, paints a slogan behind the ring, puts the photos of every fighter who won a
title on the way to the lockers. He does it because it sells memberships and keeps fighters hungry - or so he says. Find
out what they do and whether anything shows it works.
