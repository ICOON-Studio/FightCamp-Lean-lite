Rendering the actual 3D club inside the SubViewport creates instant continuity: the gym you configure on this screen is the exact gym you walk into on Day 1.

Dragging, Swapping, and Grid Snapping
Grid Resolution: Snaps strictly to $1.0\text{ m}$ increments (toggleable to $0.5\text{ m}$ in advanced mode).
Direct Drag: Clicking and holding inside any zone polygon enters DRAG_STATE. The camera smooths slightly, and a semi-transparent ghost of the zone tracks the cursor across the floor plane.
Swap Mechanic: If the player drags a zone directly over an existing valid zone of compatible dimensions (or drops within $\ge 60%$ bounding box overlap), the UI indicates SWAP via a cyan perimeter highlight (#38767A). Releasing swaps the two zones' origins cleanly.
Rotation: Pressing R or clicking the rotation handle rotates the zone $90^\circ$ around its local center. If the rotation causes an out-of-bounds or collision error, it snaps back with an audible error buzz.
Fixed Elements vs. Moveable Zones
STRICTLY FIXED (Cannot be moved or deleted):
Exterior Hall Walls: North, South, East, West structural perimeter ($30\text{ m} \times 18\text{ m}$).
Front Entrance & Fire Exits: South wall double doors ($X: 13.5\text{--}16.5, Z: 18$).
Primary Thoroughfare Aisles:
Main East-West Transit Spine: $Z: 10.4\text{ m}$ to $11.6\text{ m}$ (must remain completely clear of apparatus).
Entrance Reception Vestibule: $Z: 16.4\text{ m}$ to $18.0\text{ m}$.
The Boxing Ring ($X: 12.0\text{--}21.0, Z: 3.5\text{--}10.4$): In the demo, the raised ring is structurally bolted to the concrete foundations in the center bay. It is immovable in Story Mode (serves as the architectural anchor of Calder House). In Open Sandbox, it can be relocated between 3 designated pre-reinforced floor bays.
FREELY MOVEABLE (8 Zones):
Bag Line West, Bag Line East, Boxing Floor, Cardio Zone, Timing Bag Run, Footwork/Agility Grid, Stretch Area, Strength/Weights Zone.
Collision, Overlap, and Boundary Rules
Clearance Envelopes: Every zone has a hard boundary that includes athlete operating clearance ($0.5\text{ m}$ perimeter padding around heavy bags; $1.0\text{ m}$ around weight barbells).
Invalid Drop Behavior: If dropped in collision with another zone, overlapping an aisle, or clipping exterior brick walls:
The boundary strokeline flashes high-contrast crimson (#D15341) at $4\text{ Hz}$.
An on-screen floating error tag appears at the cursor: BLOCKED: AISLE CLEARANCE REQUIRED or BLOCKED: ZONE OVERLAP.
Upon mouse release, the zone plays a smooth spring-damper tween (0.2s duration) returning to its last valid coordinates (revert_to_origin()).
Undo / Redo / Reset Stack:
Full command pattern (MoveZoneCommand, SwapZoneCommand, ResizeZoneCommand).
Ctrl+Z (Undo), Ctrl+Y (Redo).
RESET TO DEFAULT button instantly restores the canonical hall configuration.
Granularity: Zones vs. Individual Props (Demo Scope)
Demo Rule: ZONES ONLY.
The player manipulates functional zones as atomic units (e.g., moving the entire Strength Zone with its rack, bench, and platforms together).
Why: Allowing free-form placement of individual dumbbells, barbells, and speed bag swivels introduces collision bugs, athlete pathfinding mesh invalidations, and immense cognitive load before the game has even begun.
One Editor or Two? ONE UNIFIED SYSTEM.
This screen is simply an instance of the game's core Gym Renovation / Build Mode Engine (GymLayoutManager.tscn) instantiated with initial-setup flags.
The exact same viewport, gizmos, and zone data are used when the player opens the "Renovate Gym" tab later during active career management.

5. FINANCIAL & EDITING FEEDBACK RULES
A. Pre-Opening Editing (Step 3 Creation Screen)
Zero Cost: Rearranging, rotating, swapping, and reconfiguring zones prior to clicking OPEN GYM is 100% FREE.
Why: Penalizing a manager for planning their initial floor layout before day one creates friction and punishes experimentation.
Instant Telemetry Reactivity: If the player in Open Sandbox adds or removes zones, the four metric cards update on that frame: $$\text{Runway (Weeks)} = \frac{\text{Current Cash}}{\text{Base Hall Lease} + \sum \text{Installed Zone Upkeep} + \text{Staff Base}}$$
B. Post-Opening Editing (Live Career Build Mode)
Once the gym is operational, moving heavy gym machinery carries logistical realism:
Relocation Fee: Moving a zone costs $$150.00$ (contractor moving fee). Relocating the Boxing Ring costs $$1,200.00$ (structural rigging & canvas re-tensioning).
Operational Downtime: The moved zone is marked with a yellow caution barrier and is unavailable for training for 24 game hours (1 day) while bolts are set.
If an athlete is scheduled to train on that zone during that day, their training session cancels with an alert: STATION RELOCATING.

The 3 Riskiest Implementation Hurdles in Godot 4.7.2
========================================================================================================================
TECHNICAL RISK AUDIT (GODOT 4.7.2 ENGINE IMPLEMENTATION)
========================================================================================================================
#  RISK DESCRIPTION                              SEVERITY   EXACT ENGINE MITIGATION STRATEGY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1  SubViewport Input Event & Raycast Parallax   HIGH       In Godot 4, forwarding GUI mouse events from a parent 
                                                           Control through a SubViewportContainer to a 3D Camera3D 
                                                           often suffers scaling and coordinate offset bugs when 
                                                           window scaling is active.
                                                           --> MITIGATION: Implement an unhandled_input raycaster on 
                                                           the Camera3D using `project_ray_origin` and intersect with 
                                                           an invisible 3D StaticBody3D floor plane (`CollisionLayer 9`).

2  Dynamic AStar2D / NavigationMesh Invalidation MEDIUM     Moving zones on the floor alters walkability bounds for 
                                                           background athletes and coaches.
                                                           --> MITIGATION: Do not bake full 3D NavMeshes at runtime. 
                                                           Use a 2D discrete grid (`AStarGrid2D`) with 1m cell size 
                                                           representing the hall floor. Mark zone rectangles as solid 
                                                           obstacles; keep aisle cells strictly walkable.

3  Visual Overdraw: 2D CanvasLayer over 3D Depth LOW-MED    Rendering crisp 2D zone boundary outlines, drag handles, 
                                                           and selection pills over perspective 3D geometry causes 
                                                           Z-fighting or sorting flicker.
                                                           --> MITIGATION: Render zone outlines using 3D ImmediateMesh 
                                                           or Decals projected onto the floor geometry rather than 
                                                           floating 2D Control nodes, guaranteeing perfect perspective.
