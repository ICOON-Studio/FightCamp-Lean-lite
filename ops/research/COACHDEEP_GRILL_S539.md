# Deep coaches - the owner's answers (grilling, S539)

The owner asked for coaches "like football manager ... to tighten and improve the core loop" and "more deep , what he know how
he mix with fighters and different personalities , his playbook , needs proper attributes and personality". Claude grilled the
design (mattpocock-skills:grilling); every answer below is his. Design work (TASK_COACHDEEP1, DESIGN_COACHDEEP1.md) follows this
page; where they differ, this page wins.

## Round 1 (owner: "q1 c q2 4 ok q3 a q4 a q5 b q6 b q7 a")
- Q1 You are the owner AND head coach, with NO ratings; the coaches you hire carry the ratings.
- Q2 Up to 4 coaches (start with 1, room grows with the club).
- Q3 A weekly wage (Football Manager style).
- Q4 Exact ratings 1-20.
- Q5 Coaches improve slowly: coaching courses (England Boxing levels: money + evenings away) and years with good boxers.
- Q6 Every club has a coach; you see only what you have learned (the card starts mostly unknown, fills from film, fights, reputation).
- Q7 Build order: your own coaches first, then the opposing coach, then coach careers - the opposing card planned on the same model from day one.

## Round 2 (owner: "yes to all")
- Q8 Eight ratings 1-20, each wired to a rule that exists: pads/technique (moves and combos learned), conditioning (fitness,
  recovery), tactics (plan grades, calls between rounds), cutman (guard and cuts between rounds), weight management (weigh-in
  miss chance, the cut's toll), film/scouting (the opponent's file opens), motivation (morale, trust in the corner), teaching
  beginners (novices, classes).
- Q9 What he knows: 2-3 specialties (a style he reads best, move families he teaches well); a move outside them drills slower;
  shown on his card with a hover.
- Q10 His playbook: one of the six styles; a boxer whose style fits learns faster and trusts the plan; pulled against his style,
  a visible cost.
- Q11 Personality: five traits 1-20 - temper, patience, discipline, ambition, loyalty (raises, offers from rivals, staying).
- Q12 Chemistry: coach personality v boxer personality + weeks together; a word on both cards (bond / fine / clash) with the
  reasons on hover; moves learning, morale, loyalty; shown only after 2 weeks together.
- Q13 Time: 5 evenings a week a coach; an evening = up to 2 boxers on pads, or 1 class; you assign in the week plan; a boxer
  with no coach that evening trains alone at a lower gain.
- Q14 A coach earns his wage on class nights (the decided +$350/week evening class; his teaching rating keeps members): every
  evening is a trade between class money and a boxer's progress.
- Q15 Finding coaches: a monthly list (local coaches, now and then a known name on a big wage, later your retired boxers) and
  a paid one-week trial that shows chemistry.
- Q16 Your coaches and candidates show exact ratings; an opposing coach's card starts "?" and each rating turns exact once learned.

## Round 3 (owner: "yes to all")
- Q17 Wages scale with ratings: local $150-300/week, solid $400-600, a known name $800-1,100 (today: $450 and $1,090). A local
  coach covers his wage with one class night (~$350); a known name must win fights to be worth it.
- Q18 Luke Reid stays the starting coach, converted: strong pads, average elsewhere, with a personality.
- Q19 You can cover 2 evenings a week yourself as a plain coach (every rating 8): a one-coach gym runs; hiring is how it grows.
- Q20 A coach with low loyalty and a rival offer hands in 2 weeks' notice; you can match (a raise). An ambitious coach asks for
  a raise after a title or a run of wins. Poaching by rival gyms waits for the careers phase.
- Q21 Three course levels: money + 2 of his evenings a week for 2-4 weeks; +1-2 in the ratings you pick; lifts his ceiling.
- Q22 Each rating has a ceiling, shown in words ("could reach 15 in pads"), exact after a season working for you.
- Q23 The other corner is learned: film -> tactics, film; a fight against him -> cutman, motivation; reputation -> his best
  rating from the start; his boxer's weigh-in -> weight management; the playbook style is the first clue (his fighters' records).
- Q24 Where coaches show: Club office Staff tab (FM-style table: ratings, wage, chemistry); the coach card (ratings,
  specialties, playbook, personality, ceiling, his boxers); the week plan (drag coaches onto boxers and classes); boxer cards
  (his coach, the chemistry word); the fight (your corner's cutman and tactics; "their corner" links to the opposing card).

## Left to the probe and the map, not to the owner

its rule) is sized by the probe, inside his bands. The order is his Q7.
