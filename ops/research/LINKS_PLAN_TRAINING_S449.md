## Ideas that are new to us (candidates; none approved or built)
1. A job for each round beside Push/Steady/Save:
   - Find out: the first lines test and reveal tells.
   - Exploit: lean on a confirmed tell.
   - Protect or change: decided by the scorecards.
   - It fits the built pace control and gives the corner minute a reason to change.
2. "Confirmed" before "exploit": a tell found on tape counts as a guess until the fight shows it. A line built on an unconfirmed tell is weaker in round 1, and a corner call confirms or drops it.
   - This is our tape study with one more step, and it makes the corner read matter.
3. Lines that fail when tired: a line whose answer is hard to do tired (a feint entry, a pivot) loses bite in late rounds once the tank is low. Simple lines hold.
   - Gives a reason to choose simple lines for a low-Cardio boxer.
