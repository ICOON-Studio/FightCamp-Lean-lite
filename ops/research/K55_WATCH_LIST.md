Gaps / how to use
True fixed side-on sparring with both men’s feet for an entire exchange is rare on YouTube; use the two sparring links and scrub, don’t expect every second to qualify.
FightCamp / Jeffries / Atlas rows are technique demos (bag, mitts, open floor), not bout film — best for clean kinetic-chain reading, weaker for inter-boxer spacing.
Prefer 50–60 fps rows (1–3 in the top five) when counting frames; 24 fps demos still work if you step with , / . carefully.
Do not invent centimetres from pixels; read travel in shoulder widths / stance lengths as the operator asked.
