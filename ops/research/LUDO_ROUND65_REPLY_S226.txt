0. GLOBAL PRODUCTION RULES & MATERIAL DEFINITIONS

To ensure every generated 3D prop transitions seamlessly into Godot without manual mesh reconstruction, the following rules apply to all assets in this specification:

1. The Proportions Rule
Base Asset Scale: Authored at real-world metric dimensions ($1.0\times$), scaled in Godot scene trees by exactly $1.2\times$ against the MetaHuman boxer rigs.
The "Hero Feature" (+25%): Every prop identifies one functional component (the telephone handset, the ice tub valves, the bell dome, the safe combination wheel) that is intentionally exaggerated by $+25%$ in scale. This delivers the Two Point Hospital / Gleason's chunky legibility from high camera angles.
Bevel & Chamfer Standard: No razor-sharp polygon edges. Every visible hard edge receives a 2-segment bevel/chamfer equal to $4%$ of the component’s smallest dimension ($2\text{ cm}$ to $8\text{ cm}$). Minimum wall/tube thickness is $3\times$ real-world thickness ($25\text{ mm}$ minimum diameter on pipes and legs).
