## 1. Savate

### 1. Is there a practitioner grade ladder?

YES. Coloured **gants**, not belts. Same sequence in FFSbf&DA règlement
sportif (maj. 09.04.2026), Cahier formation n°1 (maj. 31.10.2025), GBSF
syllabus, US Savate Federation ranking pages.

### 2. Level names, lowest first

Youth discovery only (pré-poussins / poussins). **Not adult practitioner
rungs:** `kangourou_1`, `kangourou_2`, `kangourou_3`.

Practitioner technical grades (official French names):

- `gant_bleu`
- `gant_vert`
- `gant_rouge`
- `gant_blanc`
- `gant_jaune`
- `gant_argent_technique_1`
- `gant_argent_technique_2`
- `gant_argent_technique_3`

Colour gloves bleu→blanc are each split into three *degrés de compétence*.
The 3° must be held before the next colour. **Those degrees are slices of one
rung, not extra rungs.** Honorifics (`gant_vermeil`, `gant_or`, medals) are
not practitioner grades.

### 3. Issuer

One **shape**, national issuance. FFSbf&DA writes the French sequence and the
exam authorities. FISav / GBSF / USSF use the same glove order and recognise
GAT 1–3 from FISav or FFSbf&DA. **Sequence does not change between bodies.
Passes the karate test.**

Who signs:

- kangourou: club moniteur (min. diplôme fédéral moniteur)
- bleu–blanc: club jury of two moniteurs SBF (exception: club moniteur alone)
- jaune: jury under CTD, comité départemental
- GAT1: jury under CTL, ligue; GAT2/GAT3: jury missionné by DTN, federal session

### 4. Time / age

**Time-in-grade: NONE PUBLISHED** in FFSbf&DA cahier formation or règlement
sportif.

Published age / category gates (not time):

- GAT1 reserved to candidates **minimum cadet 1ère année**. FFSbf&DA
  catégories d'âge: cadets = **16 and 17 years**. Source: règlement sportif +
  Cahier n°7 age table (pré-poussins 7–9, poussins 10–11, benjamins 12–13,
  minimes 14–15, cadets 16–17, juniors 18–20).
- Moniteur diploma: **18 years** and holding `gant_jaune`. That is a
  teaching-diploma age, **not a grade-award age**.

### 5. What it gates

- `gant_rouge`: assaut / young competition and challenge, moniteur permission
  required.
- `gant_jaune`: considered to know every Savate blow; entry to the combat-form
  competition path; entry to the GAT1 exam; entry to the moniteur exam if ≥18.
- `gant_de_bronze` is a **competition** grade (points), not a technical
  practitioner rung. **Do not put it on the ladder.**
- GAT1: Championnat de France Assaut (juniors/seniors/vétérans).

## 2. Sambo

### 1. Is there a practitioner grade ladder?

YES. FIAS qualifying-rank / belt system. Executive Committee register
24 Apr 2020; public teaching site `belt.sambo.sport` launched Jun 2026 as
"FIAS Belt System", "Ten levels. From Novice to Grand Master. White stripe to
burgundy." First two levels open: Novice and Student Level 1.

This is a **practitioner qualification, not a championship belt.** Distinct
from razryad / МС titles. Fedor Emelianenko (FIAS interview, Aug 2020) framed
it as the non-competitive / youth technical track sitting *beside* the Soviet
sports ranks.

### 2. Level names, lowest first

2026 FIAS comms publish endpoints only: Novice, Student Level 1, … Grand
Master. The complete named register is the 2020 FIAS *Regulation of qualifying
ranks in SAMBO* Appendix 1 (EN + RU). Tokens below use that official English
vocabulary, with the 2026 "novice / grand_master" substituted at the ends
where FIAS itself rebranded.

Athlete / practitioner track (chevrons on the red or blue belt or jacket
sleeve — **not** a change of belt colour):

| token | 2020 register | mark |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `novice` | Rookie / 1st student | no chevron; 2026 Novice, white |
| `student` | 2nd student | yellow chevron |
| `student_level_1` | 3rd student | orange chevron; 2026 name published |
| `student_level_2` | 4th student | green chevron |
| `student_level_3` | 5th student | blue chevron |
| `master_candidate` | 6th student | brown chevron |
| `master_level_1` | 1st master | black stripe / FIAS patch |
| `master_level_2` | 2nd master | |
| `master_level_3` | 3rd master | |
| `grand_master` | 2026 top name | |

The 2020 register continues master_4 / master_5 plus honoured titles and a
parallel **coach** track (1st–5th coach, square/pentagon/circle). **Coach marks
are not practitioner grades.**

> **INFERENCE, MARKED AS INFERENCE.** 2026 says ten levels white→burgundy but
> the live site has not listed levels 3–10 by name. The ten tokens above are
> the 2020 athlete/master register collapsed to ten practitioner rungs with the
> 2026 endpoint names. **Do not invent a burgundy-named rung** — burgundy is
> the top *stripe colour*, not a level name.

RU official words for the same shape: `novichok`, `uchenik`, `uchenik_1`,
`uchenik_2`, `uchenik_3`, `kandidat_v_mastera`, `master_1` … `grandmaster`.
The English FIAS register is the right const language.

National extras that **fail** the universal test and must not be used:

- Comité Français de Sambo / FFL colour animals (jaguar, guépard, puma, tigre,
  lion → jaune → … noir 1–10°). Different sequence.
- British Sambo Federation sleeve grades (student 1–7 white→brown, master
  black). Same idea, different rung count.

### 3. Issuer

**One FIAS system, delegated commissions.** National commission: student ranks
through 1st master. Continental: master 1–2 and coach 1–2. FIAS higher
commission: master 3–5 and coach 3–4. **Issuer varies, sequence does not.
Passes the karate test.**

### 4. Time / age

**NONE PUBLISHED** in the FIAS qualifying-rank regulation (EN or RU), and none
on `belt.sambo.sport` for the open levels.

Do not use BudoDragon's "roughly one year per colour" or BSF's "one grade at a
time" as a sourced minimum.

### 5. What it gates

Published function is certification of technical qualification and display
(chevron). Higher master ranks in the 2020 regulation can also be awarded from
**competition results** (national / continental / world places) — that is a
dual path, not a title belt. Coach patches gate coaching display, not fighter
grade. **No sourced rule that a given FIAS grade is required to enter a FIAS
championship.**

## 3. Pencak silat — REFUSE

### 1. Ladder?

**NO universal practitioner grade.** PERSILAT / IPSF is a sport-and-culture
federation. Competition regs (Feb 2026) use `sabuk` only as **corner colour**
(red/blue, 10 cm) or a **white unbound sash** for team jurus. That is kit, not
a grade.

### 2–5.

Perguruan ladders exist and **disagree in sequence**:

- **PSHT** siswa: hitam polos → jambon → hijau → putih, then warga titles
  (`dimas_satria_anom`, …). **Black is the *lowest* siswa belt.**
- **Tapak Suci**: kuning siswa → biru kader → hitam pendekar, with melati counts.
- **Pagar Nusa**: hijau + badge colours.
- **Western schools** (e.g. Inti Ombak): white → yellow → green → red → blue,
  Indonesian labels (`calon_keluarga`, `pratama`, `tamtama`, `utama`) that do
  not match PSHT.

Question 3 fails. No PERSILAT published time or age minima for a shared sabuk.
Competition entry is by age class (singa/macan, pre-junior, junior, senior,
master A/B), not grade.

## 4. Arnis — REFUSE

### 1. Ladder?

**NO universal practitioner grade.** WEKAF — the body the task named — is a
**tournament** federation. Its 2024/2025 competition rules cover sparring,
sayaw, padded stick/knife and weight classes. **No practitioner rank table.**

### 2–5.

Multiple incompatible ladders:

- **i-ARNIS** public table: `baguhan` (white) → `sanay_1` (yellow) →
  `sanay_2` (green) → `sanay_3` (blue) → `bihasa` (brown) → `lakan` /
  `lakambini` 1–10. The i-ARNIS India page publishes **minimum years and ages**
  for that table (brown ≥17, lakan 1 ≥18 and 1 year, lakan 10 ≥65 and 8 years
  at previous / 47 years total). **Those numbers are i-ARNIS's, not WEKAF's —
  do not paste them into MINIMUM_WEEKS.**
- **Modern Arnis**: black-belt titles `lakan`/`dayang` + Tagalog numerals;
  coloured kyu belts vary by organisation; some use a zero-degree black.
- **Doce Pares, Inosanto, Lameco, IKAEF, Bakbakan**: titles, not a shared belt
  sequence. "Most systems do not have a belt based grading system."
- **American Arnis Association**: white + college stripes (kali/sikaran/dumog),
  then coloured majors — a third sequence.

Question 3 fails: rungs differ in **sequence**, not only in who signs.

What grades gate, where they exist: school teaching titles, sometimes
tournament seeding inside that school. WEKAF entry is age/weight, not rank.

## 5. Capoeira — REFUSE

### 1. Ladder?

**NO universal practitioner grade.** Cordas exist in Regional and most
Contemporânea groups. Angola groups typically use **titles without cords**
(aluno, discípulo, treinel, professor, contra-mestre, mestre).

### 2–5.

Question 3 fails, and it fails on purpose — this was the one flagged as most
likely to.

- Mestre Bimba's Regional originally used handkerchiefs / three named levels
  (`calouro`, `formado`, `formado_especializado`), not the modern rainbow cord.
- Mestre Carlos Senna (1955) and Mestre Mendonça / CND-MEC *Regulamento
  Técnico da Capoeira* (homologated 26 Dec 1972, in force 1 Jan 1973) tried a
  national cordel. **Not adopted as the living standard.**
- Living groups pick their own colour order (Brazilian-flag green/yellow/
  blue/white; or red/orange/purple/brown mixes; mixed intermediate cords).
  ABADÁ, Senzala, Cordão de Ouro, Capoeira Brasil and Axé do not share one
  sequence.
- Confederação Brasileira de Capoeira is not accepted as a single issuer.

No published universal time or age minimum. Group folklore ("6–8 years to
instrutor") is a **population claim**. C55 / R89 refuse that class of number.

Cords, where used, gate *that group's* batizado, teaching permission, and who
can run a roda. **Not portable.**
