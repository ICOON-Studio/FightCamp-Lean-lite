## THE GAP, EXACTLY

Judo is **standing throws**. The named techniques our catalogue already carries
and cannot show: **uchi mata, seoi nage, osoto gari, harai goshi, tai otoshi,
ouchi gari, kouchi gari, tomoe nage, sumi gaeshi** and the rest of the gokyo.
115 judo nodes are authored and none has a clip.

**A judo throw is a PAIRED animation** — tori and uke, synchronised, contact
maintained through the entry, the lift and the landing. That is the hard kind.
A solo "throwing motion" with no uke is worthless to us.
