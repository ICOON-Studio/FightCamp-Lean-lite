## 0. Ruling

A trap is a GroundGraph state on a held seat: one limb or the head is isolated. Submissions do not open from "held mount N ticks." They open from a live trap. The defender always has two or three named answers. Finish rate is a consequence of that contest, not a second coin.

Today (GrappleResolve.submission_advance, FINISH_P = 1/7): seat held → bar 0..100 → flat tick lottery. No isolated arm, no hooks, no dilemma. S58 already proved the unmade choice is at mount (back was 0.11% of ground time until the chooser ran first). Traps sit on that chooser. They are not a parallel graph.

## 1. What a trap is

In combat sports a trap is control of a degree of freedom that forces a bad menu. The trapper spends mobility, striking output, or a hook to own one part. The trapped man must give something else to get it back. That is the whole mechanic.

### Grappling

| Trap | Controls | Opens | Costs the trapper | Trapped man's options |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Isolated arm (mount / side / north-south). Kimura grip as position, not yet as finish. Taught as a system (Grapplearts Kimura Position; Ryan Hall crucifix / "super murder" kimura). | Near or far arm at the elbow + wrist. Shoulder line. | Kimura, americana, mounted armbar, gift-wrap → back, wrist lock. | Posts and one hook come off. Bridge/upa window opens. GnP volume drops. | (1) Clasp hands / hug the body and wait. (2) Turn into the lock and give the back. (3) Explode the trapped side (upa / elbow-knee) and risk the armbar. |
| Head-and-arm (side, mount, crucifix). Kata-gatame / arm-triangle control before the squeeze. | Head + one arm on the same side. Air and the far hip. | Arm-triangle finish, mount-to-side ride, crucifix, far-arm americana. | Chest angle is committed. Far arm is free to frame. | (1) Hand-fight the locking biceps. (2) Turn into the choke (often worse). (3) Hip-escape the unpinned side into half. |
| Back control with hooks / seatbelt / body triangle. Modern UFC's finishing seat. | Hips via hooks or body triangle; upper body via seatbelt. | RNC, rear-naked short choke, armbar off the back, seatbelt-to-mount if hooks fail. | Cannot strike the face as freely. Body triangle kills own hip mobility and can be ridden until the referee stands. | (1) Two-on-one the choking wrist. (2) Slide off the underhook side. (3) Peel a hook and turn in — the gift-wrap / armbar window. Early defence (hands separated, before the pull to the side) is the only high-percentage window (standard back-escape pedagogy). |
| Kimura-trap system (half, 50/50, standing). Grip first, pass and finish later. | The figure-four on the wrist. | Armbar–triangle–omoplata dilemma (closed / high guard, S-mount). Ryan Hall triangle system: the triangle is a platform, not a squeeze. | One arm + the head inside the legs, or the shoulder under a swung leg. | Triangle if they stay stacked; armbar if they posture; omoplata if they drive forward; back-take if they roll. | Legs are committed. | Trap-and-roll / upa — the defender's trap from mount. | You trap their posting arm and the same-side foot, then bridge. | Reversal to their guard. (3) Extract and stand (risk the re-entry). |

Q74 already names the families the result screen must print: neck, arm, leg, body. bjj_arm_triangle is a neck attack despite "arm" in the id. Do not classify traps by substring.

### Clinch

| Trap | Controls | Opens | Costs | Options |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Underhook / overhook (over-under, body lock). | Hip and posture on one side. | Outside trip, inside trip, duck-under to the back, dirty boxing on the overhook side. | The underhook man is closer to the fence and to a throw. | Whizzer and turn the corner; drop to a single; pummel the other underhook. |
| Cage pin. UFC control-time definition includes this (J20: control clock covers a pinned clinch). | Hips to the fence, head position. | Knees, outside trip, mat return, referee break if inactive. | You cannot circle. You eat knees if the pin is loose. | Underhook and turn; level-change a single; frame and wall-walk. |
| Plum / double collar-tie (Muay Thai). | Head, incoming punches. | Knees to the body and face, off-balance, throw. | Both of your hands are used. You have no punch. | Posture up and break; drop your weight; dirty-box one arm free. |

### Striking

| Trap | Controls | Opens | Costs | Options |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hand trap / pinned lead hand. | The opponent's jab or guard hand. | Cross, hook, or entry behind the pinned hand. | Your own lead is occupied. You cannot parry. | Pull the hand free; step off line; clinch. |
| Feint-and-trap. | Attention and weight. | The real punch or the shot. | A feint that is not followed is just a missed beat. | Freeze; counter the feint; retreat. |
| Cornering / cutting off the cage. | Exit lanes. | Power shots, clinch on the fence, takedown against the cage. | You spend footwork instead of punching. | Pivot off the fence; underhook; timed shot. |

Striking traps are real. They are not the first slice. The defect is on the ground, at mount, on the submission branch.

## 4. Design — trap as a GroundGraph state

### 4.1 Shape

One extra kind on the existing edge table: kind: "trap" (alongside advance / hold / scramble). A trap edge has to_seat == from_seat like a hold, plus a limb. Winning the edge does not change the seat. It sets trap on the live pair.

```
trap: { id, kind, limb, seat, owner, held_ticks, quality }
```

owner is top or bottom (upa is a bottom-owned trap). Unknown seat → empty candidate list → shipped ladder. Same fallback S57 already uses.

This is not Shape 2 (subs as graph edges). S57 refused that. The submission branch stays where S58 put it: chooser runs first; if the man picks HOLD (or now TRAP) the shipped ladder including submission_advance may run. The change is the gate on that branch: sub_open requires a live trap, not held >= SUBMISSION_OPEN_TICKS alone.

Bottom-man graph wiring is still its own slice (S58: "Wiring the bottom man is its own slice"). Upa can ship as one authored bottom trap edge without wiring every escape.

### 4.2 Minimum trap types (v1)

Four. One per dominant finishing family in the UFC finish table. Nothing else.

| id | seat | limb | gates (catalogue ids already in Q74) | why this one |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| isolated_arm | mount, side | arm | bjj_kimura, bjj_americana, bjj_armbar, judo_jujigatame, judo_udegarami | Kimura is ~2.7–8% of UFC subs depending on table; armbar ~12%. The setup is the missing state. Mount is the seat S56 said holds the unmade choice. |
| head_and_arm | side, mount | head_neck | bjj_arm_triangle | 7.7% of UFC subs. Distinct limb from isolated_arm. Q74: this is a neck attack. |
| seatbelt_hooks | back | head_neck | bjj_rnc | 39% of UFC subs, 45% of positional contexts. If back is live and this trap is not, RNC must not open. |
| triangle_platform | guard | head_neck (primary), arm as the dilemma branch | bjj_triangle, bjj_armbar, bjj_omoplata | 5.5% triangles + the guard-share of armbars. The only v1 trap from the bottom that is an attack. Guard is 7.4% of UFC sub finishes — small, but it is the dilemma the operator named. |

v1 explicitly omits leg entanglements, kimura-trap-as-pass-system, plum, hand-traps, cage pins. Heel hooks are 1.4% of UFC subs. They get a trap type when D4 is stable and back/mount traps are measured.

Defender trap, same slice if the edge is cheap:

| id | seat | actor | limb | gates |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| upa_trap | mount | bottom | attacker's posting arm | reversal to guard (existing sweep/reversal points = 2) |

### 4.3 How a trap is won (the contest)

No new roll family. Grok's S57 constraint still holds: same formula, different scale.
- Candidate trap edges go through GroundGraph.choose with the existing four terms (skill, plan, reward, hold_value) × p_mod.
- If chosen, the attempt is resolved by GrappleResolve._success_probability × that edge's p_mod, using the existing four-draw contract. Do not add a fifth draw (S58: _run_grapple takes FOUR DRAWS, HERE, ALWAYS).
- skill_of is the same lookup S58 repaired onto ground_progress. A man who has not trained bjj_kimura scores 0 on isolated_arm the way he already scores 0 on an untrained advance. Per-technique saturation is still a known limit (S58 §6.1); do not invent a proficiency the records do not carry.
- Fatigue enters through FatigueCurve exactly as submission_advance already does.
- Plan tags: finish biases trap over hold; control / pressure bias hold; survive biases upa_trap. S58 measured the plan term at 0.00 on the S56 fixture (plan: "balanced"). Traps will look plan-blind until fight plans actually emit tags. That is a fixture bug, not a reason to hard-code trap rates.

p_mod on trap edges is AUTHORED, same status as every other p_mod on the graph, and must be swept against the control arm before anyone quotes it. Starting shape, not a target:
- isolated_arm from mount harder than from side (you give up a post).
- seatbelt_hooks easier once the seat is already back (the position is the trap; the contest is the second hook / the choking wrist).
- triangle_platform from guard harder than any top trap (MMA punches; UFC finish-share is small).

Do not publish a numeric p_mod in this memo. The graph already has 0.70–1.30 on other edges; copy that band, then measure.

### 4.4 What a live trap gates

| Gate | Today | With trap |
|---|---|---|
| sub_open | seat == "mount" (and not back) and held >= SUBMISSION_OPEN_TICKS | trap != {} and trap.limb matches the technique family's Q74 target_region |
| submission_advance progress | always ticks once open | ticks only while trap is live |
| Finish at progress 100 | roll < FINISH_P (1/7; operator is cutting toward 1/35) | still a roll, but only legal techniques for that trap are on the table, and FINISH_P is no longer the lever that carries D4 |

Finish odds are not a new constant. They are the FightMetric-era ~20% attempt→finish applied to attempts that only exist because a trap was won. Worked target, shown as arithmetic, not as a shipped float:
- D4 wants 17.2% of bouts to end by sub.
- D6 wants 0.5–1.0 attempts per fighter-fight.
- Pooled attempt success ~20% (FightMetric book) × 0.86 attempts/fight ≈ 17% bout-sub.

So the harness question is not "is FINISH_P 1/7 or 1/35?" It is:
1. Attempts per fighter-fight inside 0.5–1.0 (D6).
2. Finishes / attempts near 0.20 (FightMetric book), not 0.40.
3. Bout-method mix inside D4.
4. Finish composition: RNC share of subs in the high-30s to mid-40s; back as the positional context on ~45% of sub finishes; mount-origin and side-origin small.

If (1) and (2) hold, (3) follows. Cutting FINISH_P to 1/35 to hit (3) while leaving (1) and (2) unmeasured is how the current bar was born.

submission_advance's progress bar can stay as the squeeze once the trap is on. It no longer is the setup.

### 4.5 The defender's choice

When trap is live the bottom (or, for upa_trap, the top) is offered exactly the options in §1 for that type. Implemented as graph edges with actor opposite the trap owner. Two or three. No fourth "do nothing" that is safe — doing nothing is "clasp and wait," and it has a name.

| Live trap | Defender options (edge ids) | What each opens |
|---|---|---|
| isolated_arm | clasp_and_hide / turn_into / explode_trapped_side | clasp: slows advance, trap stays; turn_into: chance to kill the trap, on fail to_seat = back (the real cost); explode: upa-class reversal or the armbar becomes the only legal finish this tick |
| head_and_arm | hand_fight_lock / turn_into / hip_escape_far | hand_fight: can break trap; turn_into: usually worse; hip_escape_far: to half, trap dies |
| seatbelt_hooks | two_on_one_wrist / slide_underhook_side / peel_hook_turn_in | two_on_one: stall the RNC; slide: trap quality drops or dies; peel: hook dies, gift-wrap / armbar window for the attacker |
| triangle_platform | posture_and_strike / stack / hide_arm | posture → armbar legal; stack → omoplata / sweep legal; hide → triangle legal, strikes may still land |
| upa_trap | post_free_hand / insert_far_hook / accept_roll | post: trap dies, mount stays; hook: to_seat = back; accept: to_seat = guard |

Each option is an edge. Same chooser, same roll contract. The result screen can then print a sentence that is a log line, not flavour.

### 4.6 Cost and expiry

A trap is a hold with a limb. It pays what the seat pays (control_units_decayed — do not add a second decay). Extra costs, all existing channels:
- Trapper's GnP / strike branch is suppressed or cut while the trap is live (the real cost of two hands on one arm). No new stamina pool.
- min_hold_ticks on the trap edge stops a chooser from trapping every tick. Use the same 60-tick gate the advance edges already use where the seat is dominant; 0 where S57 already used 0.
- Expiry: trap dies on a successful defender edge, on seat change, on stand-up / stall referee (STALL_FIRE_TICKS untouched — G35), or on trapper choosing advance / scramble off the seat. Failed defender edges do not automatically expire it.
- Quality is held_ticks of the trap, not of the seat. A seat held 15 s with a trap held 2 s is a fresh trap. Do not reuse S58's 299.6-tick mean hold as a trap duration — that number is seat time, and S58 says DECAY_GATES is uncalibrated.

No authored "trap lasts N seconds." Duration is whatever the contest produces. Measure it. Compare later to NOT FOUND real-fight trap duration.

### 4.7 How the fighter decides: trap vs advance vs strike vs hold

Already a weighted choose among legal edges. Add the trap edges to EDGES. Do not add a behaviour tree, a learned policy, or a second chooser.

Qualitative biases the existing terms already express:
- reward on a trap edge is 0 position points (seat did not change). So a trap wins only if skill + plan + hold_value beat an advance that pays 3 or 4. That is correct: trapping is for a finisher, not for a wrestler farming control. MMA Manager's 2026 note ("wrestlers control and GnP rather than sub") is the same rule in English.
- After hold_value decays, hold loses to both advance and trap. S58 already wanted that.
- Strike / GnP stays on the shipped non-graph branch. A man with a live trap should strike less (cost above). A man with no trap and a pressure plan should strike or hold, not invent a kimura he has not trained.

Debug the stupid choice the way S57 required: one log line with id, skill, plan, reward, hold_value, difficulty. Add trap.id and defender_edge.id to that line.

### 4.8 Result screen

One sentence, fields the engine already has or this state adds:

{winner} trapped the {trap.limb} from {trap.seat}, {defender} chose {defender_edge}, {winner} finished with {technique.name}.

Example: "trapped the arm from mount, turned into it, took the kimura."

Q74 target_region is what prints the limb. Do not print "LEFT ARM" for an RNC.

## 5. What not to add, and the first slice

Do not add
- A second graph, a behaviour tree, or a learned policy. S57 already rejected those so a stupid choice can be read from one line.
- A minigame or a mode switch. UFC 4/5 already ran that experiment.
- Shape 2 in this pass (every sub and escape as an edge). Traps gate the existing sub branch.
- Leg-entanglement / clinch / striking traps in v1. They are catalogued so nobody "discovers" them mid-slice.
- Per-technique authoring of 534 rows. Four trap kinds, ~8–10 edges, reused catalogue ids.
- A new RNG draw.
- Invented escape percentages, invented trap durations, invented p_mod targets.
- Tuning FINISH_P to 1/35 to fake D4 while attempts still convert at 40%.
- Touching STALL_FIRE_TICKS or control-unit resolution (S58 5c: graded decay is a resolution change, not this job).
- Wiring every bottom escape. One upa_trap edge is the exception, and it is optional for slice 1.
- Animation / motion_id. S57 left it empty on purpose.

Build order
1. **Slice 1 (prove the idea).** One trap: isolated_arm from mount only. Top chooser already runs here (S58). Winning it is the only way submission_advance opens from mount. Defender gets the three-option menu. Control arm = current graph arm, integer-for-integer on seats, methods, draws, except mount-originated subs. Sweep the S56 pair (grapple 82 vs 34, 120 bouts, same seeds). Pass if: (a) mount-originated finishes drop, (b) attempts/fighter-fight stay inside D6, (c) attempt→finish moves toward 20% not 40%, (d) KO/TKO row does not move (S58's leak check), (e) result log contains a limb + a defender choice. Fail if bout-sub is forced onto D4 by a new constant.
2. **Slice 2.** seatbelt_hooks on back. This is where real UFC finishes live (45%). Same harness plus RNC share of subs.
3. **Slice 3.** head_and_arm from side. Side already has a verified clip; the operator asked for transitions from that seat first. Do not start here — S56 says the missing choice is mount.
