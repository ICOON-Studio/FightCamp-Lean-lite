## What would a human do?
The head coach (or a camp booker) phones other coaches or agents, or posts a spec: weight, stance, height, a style clone
of the opponent. Trusted pool first, then fly-ins. Before the first bell they agree intensity, rounds, who stops the
work, pay, travel/board, and usually no phones / no leak. Amateur/regional is reciprocity or a drop-in fee; a pro camp's
cost sits on the fighter (sometimes the promoter's camp budget). What fails: ego partners who try to win, unfit
"lookalikes", leaked clips, a local getting hurt.

## 1. Pay (who pays) - SOURCED talksport, sportingnews, dailymail, secondsout, boxingnewsonline, financial-advisors-for-athletes (2026-09-26)
- World boxing: Klitschko GBP 1,500/wk, ~10 partners, ~GBP 100k camp; Usyk ~GBP 1,000/wk; Fury ~GBP 100-120/session or
  200-300/wk (friends: petrol + cash); Joshua GBP 0 with mates/amateurs; Mayweather (2015) $2,500/wk, partner paid own
  flights; Canelo $1,750/2 wks + $50/day + flights; career partner Cunningham $800-1,100/wk; Conlan: flight ~GBP 1,500 +
  lodging + GBP 50-80/day food + ~GBP 500 for 8-12 rds, local kids ~GBP 50/rd. Elite: $500-1,500/session or $3-8k/wk
  resident; full camp sparring $40-100k.
- Regional pro boxing: cash + fuel, unpaid among friends (SOURCED); PROXY $200-800/wk.
- Amateur boxing/kickboxing: club-for-club, unpaid. Supervised ring hire ~$50-65/hr, drop-in $25-35, day pass ~$45
  (SOURCED stlboxingacademy, opensparring). Paying extra for class sparring is frowned on; hired camp partners are not.
- MMA: team-mates often unpaid; UFC camp $8-12k typical; Tsarukyan ~$40-60k/month with 11 partners + 4 coaches;
  camp/sparring 5-15% of purse; drop-in $25-50 (SOURCED groundedmma, sportskeeda, lowkickmma, forbes.ru 2019).
- Who pays: amateur = gym or fighter drop-in; regional = fighter; world = fighter/camp (promoter only inside a camp budget).

## 2. Choosing partners - SOURCED (Delphi coaches study, rossboxing, worldboxingnews, Conlan, Catterall)
Same weight class first (~90-95% of coaches), +-1 class common; equal or slightly better experience; a clone of the
opponent's stance/height/style. Variety beats one gym-mate. World camps: 3-4 partners per 12-round day, ~10 on the roster.

## 3. Gym-to-gym / open nights - SOURCED (rossboxing, fighters-corner, evolve-mma, boxingnews.jp, fightstyle.nl)
Amateurs visit neighbouring gyms so styles don't go stale; some clubs run invite open-spar a few times a month. Etiquette:
ask first, agree intensity, 16oz+, full kit, coach can kill the round, no film unless both gyms agree. Ban triggers:
beating a downed partner, ego "wins", filming without consent, injuring a house fighter. Unknown visitors often get drills
only until the coach has seen control.

## 4-5. Visiting pro; what goes wrong - SOURCED (gladiusfight, sportskeeda, 7news, talksport, boxingnews24)
Upside: reputation, clips, walk-ins. Downside: a local hurt, the visitor treats it as a fight, a leaked knockdown clip
(Shields 2018, Lee gym ban), poaching. Injury before the fight, unfit partners, fights left in the gym.

## 6. JUDGEMENT - first-release rules
- Prices (game cash): amateur 0-80 drop-in or a reciprocity token; regional 200-600/day or 800-1,500/wk + travel;
  world 1,500-4,000/wk resident or 400-1,200/session, camp sparring line capped 8-12k.
- Two booking gates: (1) coach network / rival gym - cheap, style-mismatch chance, a reciprocity debt; (2) paid hire -
  pick stance/height/style tags, pay by level, optional no-film clause.
- Events: pro visiting - drop him in? (hype + sign-ups vs a local hurt / leaked KO); open-spar night invite (variety vs a
  house-rule breach / ban); pay the southpaw clone (camp rating vs that opponent vs cash); partner going for the win -
  pull him (wasted fee) / keep him (sharpness + cut risk + possible leak).
- Player sees: cost, travel days, style match %, film-ban toggle; after: readiness/skill delta, injury roll, reputation
  with that gym, optional clip event. G117's weekly cap still applies.
