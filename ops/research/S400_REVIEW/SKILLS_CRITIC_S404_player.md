# Skills layer, veteran-player review (S404)

Read: CRITIC_PACK, CRITIC_ACCESS, docs/mockups/s403/profile.png. Not read: the code or the Playbook move list.

## 1. Score of the draft: 4.5 / 10
- It copies how a martial-arts coach talks (grappling words for ground, striking words for stand-up). It does not copy what a player can DO.
  Seventeen numbers is a spreadsheet, and the profile mockup shows it: 7 striking + 4 grappling + 6 ground numbers beside 10 attributes
  and 3 mind bars. That is 26 bars on one page. I would stop reading after the first block.
- Half the list is the same thing twice. Blocking and Head movement are both "defence". Kick defence is a move answer ("check the kick").
  Guard, Passing and Control are one top/bottom story. Escapes and Submission defence are one "survive the bad spot" story.
- Scrambling, Takedowns and Control are mostly Explosiveness, Strength and Speed already (see section 6). Counting them again makes
  two bars that move together, so there is nothing to decide.
- Good in it: the BJJ and wrestling splits (takedown, guard, pass, submit) are real and each points at a different drill. Timing and Footwork are real too.

## 3. Missing
- Top game: Passing plus Control, as one skill (above). Not missing, just renamed.
- Defence as one skill. It is the skill players ask for first.
- Clinch for Muay Thai is in the draft; Wrestling control against the cage (pin, dirty boxing) is covered by Clinch plus Takedown defence in MMA. Fine.
- Not missing from skills, but check the attributes: Reach/stance are facts, not bars, per the agreed frame. Good.
- Honestly I would add nothing else. The list is too long, not too short. A "Ring craft / Cage craft" skill is tempting; it is Fight IQ.

## 4. Recommended final list
- Boxing, 5: Accuracy, Timing, Defence, Footwork, Inside fighting.
- Kickboxing and Muay Thai, 6: the boxing five plus Clinch. (Kick checks and catches are moves.)
- No-gi BJJ, 6: Takedowns, Guard, Top game, Submissions, Escapes, plus Scrambling-as-part-of-Escapes means 5; add Clinch-free "Takedown defence" for 6 only if BJJ gets an opponent who shoots.
  My count: 5 (Takedowns, Guard, Top game, Submissions, Escapes).
- MMA, 12 in three clusters (they are the union, same names): Striking 5 (Accuracy, Timing, Defence, Footwork, Inside), Stand-up grappling 3
  (Clinch, Takedowns, Takedown defence), Ground 4 (Guard, Top game, Submissions, Escapes). That is the ceiling.
- How many is too many: 5 to 7 is a glance. 8 to 10 works in one block with a sort. 12 only with clusters and a collapsed view that shows
  the best and worst skill, and not the other ten. Above 12 a player stops reading. Add the 10 attributes and the 3 mind values and
  the MMA page is still 25 bars: dangerous. Show only the group the next fight uses, collapse the rest.
- Total distinct skills across the game: 13 (Accuracy, Timing, Defence, Footwork, Inside fighting, Clinch, Takedowns, Takedown defence,
  Guard, Top game, Submissions, Escapes, and Scrambling folded away). Draft had 17.

## 5. Crossover (my rough numbers, tune by play)
Rule: a skill with the same name is the same bar in every sport; moving sport applies a carry factor on day one and the rest is earned.
- Boxing to kickboxing: Accuracy 70%, Timing 70%, Footwork 70%, Defence 60% (no kick answers), Inside 60%. Clinch 20%.
- Boxing to Muay Thai: same, but Inside and Footwork 50% (different rhythm). Clinch 20%.
- Kickboxing to Muay Thai: striking 85%, Clinch 40%.
- Boxing to MMA stand-up: 70-80% of the five; Takedown defence 0; Clinch 20.
- BJJ to MMA ground: 90-100% of Guard, Top game, Submissions, Escapes (no strikes on top: a 10% hit). Takedowns 40% (pulling is not wrestling). Takedown defence 20%.
- Wrestling is not on the list; if it ever ships, Takedowns and Takedown defence carry 80% to MMA and 60% to BJJ.
- BJJ to boxing: nothing except Composure and Fight IQ, which are attributes anyway.
Strength of belief: the order (striking to striking is high, grappling to grappling is high, across is nothing) is solid. The exact numbers are guesses.

## 6. Overlaps with attributes or moves (count once)
- Accuracy: a hand-eye part of Speed and Fight IQ. Keep it as the trained, pad-driven part. Do not also give Speed credit for hits landed.
- Timing: leans on Fight IQ and Explosiveness. If it moves the same exchange dice as Fight IQ, the player cannot tell them apart.
  Test it: if the fight does not use Timing separately from Fight IQ, merge it into Fight IQ and drop it.
- Footwork: the game already derives it from Speed and Defence. Make it a trained skill, and stop deriving it, or it is counted twice.
- Takedowns: Explosiveness and Strength. Keep the skill, but give it the technique part only.
- Control: Strength. Guard: Flexibility. Submissions: Flexibility and Strength. Escapes: Explosiveness, Composure.
  These skills should mean technique, with the attribute as a cap on how far it can grow. That also explains why a strong beginner is no master.
- Scrambling: almost all Explosiveness, Speed and Fight IQ. Cut.
- Submission defence and Durability: neck and joint toughness is an attribute, the trained escape is a skill. Say which.
- Blocking and Kick defence: the trained block, check or catch is a MOVE in the Playbook. If moves carry it, the skill is a general
  mix, not a second copy.

## Unsure about
- I have not seen how the exchange dice use Timing, Accuracy and Fight IQ. If the code cannot tell them apart, keep one.
- Whether Clinch belongs in boxing at all (the pack gives it a boxing perk-free place). I left it a small number for boxing.
- Takedown defence in BJJ: no-gi opens may have shots; I left it out.
- All crossover percentages.
