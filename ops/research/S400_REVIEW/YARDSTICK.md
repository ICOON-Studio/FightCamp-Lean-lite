## What the game is
- A boxing gym management game (PC, Godot). You run ONE gym: members pay the bills, you develop a small roster from amateur to
  the first pro fights, plan their weeks, design fight plans, watch the fight and work the corner. No-gi BJJ ships beside boxing
  (Ana, road to black belt). Steam Early Access later.
- Core of Early Access (operator S354): three fun mechanics beside the corner - mornings sum up to a goal + intensity (the
  week), the evening combo/move planner, and a fight plan that is fun to design. "Deeper, not wider" (S395 structure design).
- The Playbook / Combos tab is FUN (operator S359: "really fun to try and also learn from the book").
- Rival studied: Corner King (live, wider). Our lead: the WHY (what decided a fight), the plan, styles, a club that can close.
