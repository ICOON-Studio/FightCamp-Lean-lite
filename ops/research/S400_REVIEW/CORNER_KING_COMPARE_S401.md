## Draft refinement of the structure (for the critics to attack)
Keep five rail buttons, one home per job, no mailbox. "Deeper, not wider" holds.
1. **One big button that says what it will stop for** (row 1, 13): "To Monday 9 Feb: week review" already exists; add the stops, and let the date open Simulate to the next pin.
2. **Home is the Monday page** (S400 proposal stands): week review, goal talk and news fold in; one next action; a fight-day state.
3. **Fights gets Ask for a fight** (row 7): pick a level and a date window; a refusal gives its reason.
4. **Week gets the coach pairing** (row 5, 11): who coaches her is a choice with a visible fit (specialty, overload). This ties to the idea "two fighters, one coach: who gets the pads".
5. **History gets a progress chart with markers** (row 5), every bout with its Why.
6. **Fight prep shows the risk of each camp choice** and offers "let the coach plan it" (row 8).
7. **Result shows the money line by line under the Why** (row 9). Add skip walkout, skip break.
8. **Money gets a sponsor line saying why one has not offered, and what each fighter costs or earns** (row 10).
9. **People shows fit on staff cards, members as a panel** (row 11), and one want per person as a card, not a chat system.
10. **World sorts by closeness to your fighters** (row 12). Rankings: take their place, as already built.
11. **Grow: a rival gym comes for your prospect or your fighter, with a counter-offer** (row 14).
12. **Start: two or three named starts that are the difficulty** (row 2).
Not taken: 17 sections, email as the decision pipe, 1,450 fighters, own promotion, press scenes, voiced commentary, face creator, global directories, day-by-day time.
