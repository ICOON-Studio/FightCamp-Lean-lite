# Miro MENU MAP (loop board uXjVETf7la4=, frame "MENU MAP - how the screens connect", drawn 5 Nov 2026) - text export S400

Title screen -> START: Resume, New career (country, city, gym), Quick start (a built gym), Load.
TOP BAR on every screen: Back / Fwd - gym name - date and speed - CONTINUE (says where it stops) - cash (hover: the week) - Mail - Menu.
Moments come as flags on the top bar and open a modal; nothing opens by itself.

LEFT RAIL (map: seven areas, one level of tabs)
- HOME (what needs me): Needs you (decisions that stop time); Next up (the next dated event, its countdown); Your fighters (one line
  each: weight, condition, bout); CONTINUE (runs the days, says where it stops); Fight day (GO TO FIGHT NIGHT).
- FIGHTERS (develop them): Roster (every fighter on one card); Training (her week strip: drag drills onto pad nights, S385);
  Fight camps (the camp running, days to the fight); Scouting (prospects; ranges until a trial); Fighter page (Overview, Attributes
  and moves, Body, Training, Record, Camp, Contract); Ana's page (road to black belt, opens on her game tree).
- PLAYBOOK (moves and plans): Moves (every move she owns; a click plays it); Game plan (the round-by-round plan); Combos (build a
  chain, see if it flows); Escapes (off the ropes, out of the corner); NEXT: one page, no tabs (S381 spec).
- FIGHTS (get them fights): Offers (bouts on the table; mismatch flagged); Bout page (opponent, purse, accept or decline);
  Calendar (booked bouts, camps, weigh-ins); Results (every past bout, what decided it).
- GYM (run the club): Floor (the 3D gym, comes later - no screen); Staff (hire and pay a coach); Members (dues-paying members,
  who stays); Facilities (equipment and its condition).
- MONEY (keep it alive): Overview (cash, week by week); Income (dues, classes, purses); Bills (rent, fees, staff); Deals (sponsors).
- WORLD (the boxing world): News; Rankings; Events (shows and championships); Search (find anyone); Nationals (her road).
MENU (top bar, right): Save / Load; Settings (screen, sound, speed); Handbook (how every system works); Quit to title; Mail.
FIGHT NIGHT (from GO on fight day): Weigh-in; Tale of the tape; Live round (3D: clock, energy, the shout bar); Corner minute
  (recover, talk, fix, the plan); Result (verdict, scorecards, body report); Goal talk; Ana's open (BJJ belt match on the tree).
MODALS (each from a flag or a click): Interview (meet the boxer, three week cards); Trial (a week, then sign or pass); Fight camp
  (phases, picture cards); Weigh-in; Week review (card flip, planned vs actual); Goal talk; Pro offer (seven wins: the debut);
  Rival file (any rival, any time); Info (any number explains itself - no screen yet).
