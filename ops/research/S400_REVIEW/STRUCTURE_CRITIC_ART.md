## Where art is missing that a player expects
1. THE GYM. There is not one picture of a gym: no ring, bags, mats, lockers, reception, no building. A management game about a
   place never shows the place. Home, Club and the title carry the whole "you run a gym" fantasy and are bare.
2. Fighters' faces at size. Comic portraits are strong but used as 60-120 px thumbnails; the polaroid (look B hero) is missing.
   Opponents have no face on the rankings or rival file.
3. Kit and posters: gloves, belts, fight posters, medals. The one reward moment (win, belt, skills award) has no object.
4. Members and the crowd: 30-70 members is the revenue, shown as a number.
5. Moves and plans: Playbook has thumbnails only on Combos/Training; Moves and Game plan are text.
6. Dark fight night and corner: unseen in this set, must be checked before anything is signed off.

## Six art-direction rules for the whole game (from look B and the Money concept)
1. **One surface per page, drawn as an object**: a real whiteboard (steel frame, marker tray, slight sheen) or a cork board; never a
   bare pale-blue field with white cards. The blue app shell is only the wall behind it.
2. **Everything on the board is a physical thing**: price tags and notes are paper with a magnet or pin, tiny tilt, soft shadow;
   offers are sticky notes; signs are chalkboards; the cash tin / cashbook are objects. No floating rounded boxes.
3. **Two hands only**: Permanent Marker for titles and numbers that matter, Kalam for notes and prose, one clean sans (Barlow) for
   buttons and real stat tables. Marker colour carries meaning (blue = plan, green = in, red = out/risk, circled = date).
4. **Faces and pictures at size**: a fighter or coach always appears as a polaroid or comic portrait, large enough to read an
   expression (>= 120 px on a page, 60 px only in lists). Anything on an image must match the fighter.
5. **Hand-drawn icons and charts**: ink line drawings for rooms, kit and sections (stick figures, gym doodles), charts as marker
   lines with a circled "now" - no default blue bars, no 2 px stub bars.
6. **One button language and a contrast floor**: one blue pill for the main act, outlined pill for the secondary, red only for
   danger; body text >= 14 px at 1080p and never grey-on-pale; dark mode only for fight night and the corner; popups and menus
   (gear menu, modals) use the same paper/board skin, never raw grey.

## Money concept v4 vs the box mockup
**Concept v4: 8/10 as a quality bar, 6/10 as a build spec.**
Keep: real whiteboard with steel frame and marker tray; paper price tags with pins and ink icons; chalkboard dues/kit signs;
leather cashbook; handwritten "Each week the tin grows" with the circled +$303; the cash line chart with circled "now" and a
dashed red closing line; sticky answers column with faces; it finally reads as a gym, not a finance tool.
Fix: text is AI-smudged and partly unreadable (price tag body copy, chalk text, labels like "Move", "Hire one"), it cannot be
built as a flat image; icons and layout must be rebuilt as real nodes over a textured frame and paper atlas; keep numbers in
live Labels; contrast of small tag copy against beige paper is low; dues/kit chalkboards sit crowded at the bottom; keep one
red/blue/green meaning; mockup-level overlap (pins covering titles) must go.

**Box mockup (S400_money_books.png): 5/10.** Correct structure, correct words, real numbers, readable at 1080p, buildable today
(white board with marker headings, price tags with red dots, chalkboards, sticky answers, cashbook). It is a proof of layout
only: flat white board on pale blue, no frame, no paper texture, no tray, tags are white cards with a dot, cash tin line chart
is thin. Keep its text and grid exactly; apply v4's surface (frame, paper, shadows, sheen) on top. Together they are the
target: v4 look, box layout and copy.
