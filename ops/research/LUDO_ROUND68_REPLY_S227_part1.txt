PART A: THE 6-SCREEN FORENSIC AUDIT (EVERY DEFECT CATALOGED)

The CEO’s assessment—"the game cards and the player profile is such a mess"—is clinically accurate. The current build displays a catastrophic collision between developer-debug scaffolding, misaligned screen-space projections, unanchored typography, and barren black deserts.

Below is the exhaustive, itemized audit of every flaw visible across the 6 captured screens.

SCREEN 1: THE QUICK-LOOK CARD (GYM FLOOR POPUP)
Unanchored World-to-Screen Projection Drift: The card floats arbitrarily over the center ring with no dynamic tether, needle, or leader-line connecting it to the 3D boxer clicked on the gym floor. If the camera pans, the card slips or obscures the active training zone.
Microscopic, Unweighted Typography: Stats inside the card (POW, SPD, END, REC) are rendered in an undifferentiated $\approx 8\text{ pt}$ monospaced grey font. At $1080\text{ p}$, it is completely unreadable without leaning into the monitor.
Absence of Tactical Context: The card displays naked numbers (47, 64, 72) but completely fails to answer the four core questions a manager asks when clicking a boxer on the gym floor:
What is he doing right now? (No active drill label).
Who is training him? (Assigned coach is absent).
What is his immediate stamina/fatigue status? (No active energy/recovery bar).
When is his next bout? (No bout countdown chip).
Sterile Neon Border Style: The card uses a generic, thin cyan-to-green gradient border (#00ffaa) completely alien to Maria’s 1970s brass, leather, and charcoal aesthetic. It looks like a cyberpunk telemetry widget dropped into a Brooklyn boxing gym.
Missing Primary CTA Anchor: The button at the bottom of the card ("VIEW FULL PROFILE") is a tiny, flat, low-contrast button with no chamfers, no icon, and no clear hover affordance.
SCREEN 2: PROFILE TAB 1 — OVERVIEW
The Decapitated 3D Boxer Bust: On the left flank, a 3D boxer bust is rendered inside a harsh, unclipped rectangular sub-viewport box with no pedestal, framing ring, lighting rim, or grounding shadow. The boxer is angled awkwardly at $45^\circ$, staring blankly into the side margin rather than engaging the player.
The "Traffic-Light Block-Man" Anatomical Abomination: Below the 3D head sits a crude, 8-bit-style vector diagram composed of colored solid rectangles (yellow head, orange torso/arms, teal legs). It looks like an MS Paint placeholder from an early 1990s prototype. It completely destroys the gritty realism of FightCamp.
The 9 Attribute Tiles Lack Material Architecture: The nine attributes are slapped onto the screen in a loose $3 \times 3$ grid with vast empty gutters.
The stat bars are rendered as tiny, disconnected horizontal micro-ticks.
There are no attribute categories (Physical vs. Technical vs. Mental).
The numbers (47, 64, 72) float without visual anchoring or grade badges (no S, A, B classifications).
The "NEXT BOUT" Card is an Abandoned Island: The right-hand card for the next bout contains two tiny lines of unstyled text ("VS. NIKOLAY SHEVCHENKO") floating above a bright orange, untextured flat rectangle.
The "LAST 3 WEEKS" Raw String Dump: Directly below the bout card, three weeks of training history are dumped as raw terminal text ("Training Load: 68%", "Sparring: 14 rounds", "Fatigue: +12%"). No sparklines, no progression icons, no performance deltas.
Massive Dead Bottom-Half Void: From $Y \approx 620\text{ px}$ to $1080\text{ px}$, the entire center and bottom of the screen is an empty, pitch-black void. More than $55%$ of the available canvas is totally unused.
SCREEN 3: PROFILE TAB 2 — TECHNIQUES
Three-Column Visual Collision: The screen is fractured into three chaotic, uncoordinated columns: a dense spreadsheet list on the left, an isolated 3D boxer standing in the center, and a second unstyled table on the right. The player's eye has nowhere to rest.
The Purple Placeholder Shorts on the 3D Boxer: The full-body 3D boxer in the center is wearing untextured, bright magenta/purple placeholder trunks. There is no lighting rim separating him from the pitch-black floor.
Flat Floor Pedestal: The circular stage below the boxer is a flat 1-pixel cyan vector circle that clips into his heels. It has no physical bevel, no cast shadow, and no metallic finish.
Spreadsheet List Styling (Left & Right): The techniques are rendered inside standard Godot Tree or ItemList nodes with raw blue/orange text highlights ("JAB", "HOOK TO THE BODY").
No mastery star ratings.
No prerequisite lines connecting combos (e.g., Jab $\rightarrow$ Cross $\rightarrow$ Left Hook).
The "LOCKED / IN THE DEMO" badges are bright red and orange flat stickers with zero typographic margin.
Bottom Playback Controls Look Like Media Player Debug: The "LOOP" and "SLOW-MO" buttons are raw grey UI buttons floating nakedly in the dark void below the fighter's feet.
SCREEN 4: PROFILE TAB 3 — PERFORMANCE & CAMP
The 75% Black Desert: This is the most barren screen in the entire build. Outside of a small cluster of table headers at the top, over $75%$ of the $1920 \times 1080$ display is pure black empty void.
ASCII Grid Table Slapped Across Top: Seven columns (DATE, OPPONENT, ROUNDS, LOAD, FATIGUE, SHARPNESS, NOTES) are rendered using raw monospace labels. The row spacing is cramped ($18\text{ px}$), making it look like a MySQL database dump rather than a fighter’s tactical training journal.
Isolated Action Buttons: An orphaned button ("CONFIRM CAMP PLAN") sits in the bottom-right corner, completely detached from the data above it. A player has no idea what clicking this button will commit.
Missing Camp Architecture: We have rich camp systems in FightCamp (sparring partner allocations, weight cutting regimes, conditioning phases). None of them are visually represented here—no timeline scrubbers, no phase markers, no conditioning curves.
SCREEN 5: PROFILE TAB 4 — MEDICAL & BIO (INJURED BOXER)
The Giant Low-Poly Traffic-Light Figure Returns (Full Screen): A giant, scaled-up version of the crude orange/teal rectangular block-man dominates the entire left third of the screen. It is flat, unshaded, and completely unaligned with any biological anatomy.
The Clipped 3D Chest/Head Floating at the Bottom: Bizarrely, a 3D chest/head model is wedged into the bottom center of the screen, floating with no torso, no neck grounding, and its face cropped off on the right. It appears to be an unanchored SubViewport debugging artifact left in the build.
Raw String Data Dump for Injuries: Under "CLINICAL DIAGNOSIS", injuries are listed as raw text strings ("Micro-fracture right metacarpal - 14 days remaining").
Misaligned Segmented Recovery Bars: The recovery progress bars are bright cyan and green modern flat bars with zero beveling, stretching unpredictably across the right panel.
Bio Section is Completely Missing: Despite the tab being titled "MEDICAL & BIO", there is no biography: no hometown, no amateur pedigree, no reach measurement, no reach-to-height ratio, and no personality trait tags.
SCREEN 6: PROFILE TAB 5 — CAREER & CONTRACT
Three Naked Monolithic Columns: The screen is divided into three arbitrary vertical rectangular blocks with thick, flat border outlines.
Naked Record Display: The boxer's professional record is rendered as tiny raw text: "8 - 0 - 0 (6 KO)" inside a vast, empty $400 \times 300\text{ px}$ box on the left. No trophy icons, no title belt display case, no streak indicators.
Raw Code Terminal in Center Column: The center contract block literally displays raw code parsing:
CURRENT CONTRACT DETAILS (REMAINING: 2 BOUTS)
PURSE SPLIT: 70 / 30
SIGNING BONUS: $12,500
EXPIRY: 14 WEEKS REMAINING

This looks like a JSON printout.
Neon Green Placeholder CTA Button: At the bottom of the center column sit two garish, neon-green buttons ("EXTEND CONTRACT", "OFFER NEW TERMS"). The neon green (#00ff66) violently clashes with the entire brass/charcoal visual identity of the game.
Empty Right Column: The right column, presumably intended for bout history or promotional contract clauses, is completely blank.
