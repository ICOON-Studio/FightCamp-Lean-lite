COACH'S PAD NIGHTS: the club's one coach holds the pad nights for all four fighters; each night shows whose it is, and a shared night
names the cost to the other fighter. Seven days: a MORNING tile (picture, ink icon ringed in its stat's colour, ONE number:
the gain, and Light / Normal / Hard) and an EVENING tile (ONE number: the move's gain, the film's win range, sparring's Fight IQ), and

1. LOGIC CHECK
Contradictions/confusing numbers:
"53–55% to win" vs "coach's week 51% +1.5 to +4" — the math does not align cleanly; should be 52.5–55%.
"Tank at Sat 63 → 68" then "tank Sat 63 → 68 taper=39" — taper=39 is unexplained and unclear.
"6 by Fri" / "4 (now)" — no explicit date anchor; ambiguous when counting starts.
Names outside headers:
"Kwame Asante", "Danny Cole", "Mara Ilic", "Rosa Ferreira" appear as portraits/labels — these are names shown outside the screen header and should be flagged per design rule.
Impossible/ambiguous states:
"Wed shared: the other boxer's jab +8, not +9" — unclear which boxer this refers to and where that value lives.
"Sparring Sat buys +2 hooks · Fight IQ +0.08 · Win 10–0.08 · no new tier · tank 63→68 · injury 1.1–1.00 in 100" — dense, mixed units, hard to parse cost/benefit at a glance.
Rule conflicts:
CRITIC_PACK states fighter names only in screen headers; this screen shows them everywhere in the fighter strip.
2. USABILITY & CLARITY — Score: 6/10
What works:
Drag-and-drop sessions is the right core interaction; weekly structure (7 days, morning/evening) is immediately readable.
Session icons are distinct and the 2-pane split (summary top, planner bottom) is sensible.
Fighter switching is fast — portraits are clearly visible at the top.
What hurts:
Five-second rule fail: it is not instantly obvious how to change intensity or what each number means without reading small text.
Numbers everywhere — gains, percentages, fractions, ranges — no visual hierarchy between critical info (tiredness, intensity, win chance) and fine detail.
Session icons: functional but not yet matching the Playbook's clean ink/red-blue style — they look like generic icons, not part of the same visual language.
Comparison: Better than Boxing Manager's clunky list menus, worse than Football Manager training — FM groups sessions clearly and shows effect bars instantly. This screen buries effects. Punch Club is cleaner but shallower; this screen has depth but hides it behind numbers.
Verdict: Functional but information-dense; you can plan here, but you have to work to understand it.
3. DECISION DEPTH — Score: 8/10
Strong trade-offs exist: intensity vs fatigue, pad time split between moves, shared coach time across four fighters, film vs sparring vs rest.
No single "best week": a hard week risks injury/fatigue; a light week misses gains; splitting pads speeds two moves but slows both vs focusing on one.
Coach's week provides a sensible baseline to deviate from, not a correct answer — exactly what a management game needs.
Only weakness: fatigue risk feels abstract; the cost of overreaching is not tangible enough yet.
4. MISSING OPTIONS · USELESS ELEMENTS · SUMMARY TAB
Missing
Intensity selector (Light/Normal/Hard) directly on each day — currently hidden/ambiguous.
Per-fighter fatigue trend — can't see if you're building toward a peak or a recovery week.
Quick-reset per day / copy-paste a day's plan across the week.
"See effect on fight night" preview — what exactly does +0.18 Stamina mean in round 3?
Conflict warning — when you've scheduled two fighters at the same time with only one coach.
Useless/under-explained
"taper=39" — unexplained number, no player action can change it. Remove or explain.
"6 by Fri / 4 (now)" — unclear what is being counted; label clearly or simplify.
"Film finds the last hidden tell. It counts only once…" — too wordy; needs one-sentence plain version.
"Coach's note" — flavourful but inconsistent length; should be short, consistent, actionable advice.
Profile Training tab
Right summary, wrong button placement: the read-only overview is exactly what belongs in the profile — quick glance at what the week buys and fight-night impact. "Open in Training" button works well to jump into the planner. Keep it; just keep the numbers consistent.
✅ To reach 10/10 — changes, most important first
Fix naming: move all fighter names to their panel headers only; use "your fighter / the opponent" or just weight class + style elsewhere per design rule.
Simplify & group numbers: remove unexplained values (taper=39); show only 3 key things per session: icon + gain number + intensity dot. Everything else goes on hover.
Unify visual style: redraw all session icons in the Playbook's ink-figure style — thin line art, single accent colour per stat (e.g. red=Power, blue=Speed, gold=Technique). Match the camp plan's warm tan/whiteboard aesthetic.
Make intensity visible & editable: add 3 small toggle dots (●●● Light/Normal/Hard) directly on each morning tile; show the fatigue multiplier visibly.
Clearer cost/benefit: when dragging a session onto a day, show 3 large plain cards below it: +WHAT · COST · RISK — not a paragraph. Example: +0.09 Stamina · +4 Fatigue · No injury risk.
Coach conflict alert: if two fighters share a pad night and the coach is busy, show a yellow warning badge — "Coach busy — one session reduced".
Explain fight-night impact: beside each stat gain, one small icon links to its effect — ⏱ lasts longer, 💥 hits harder, 🛡 takes less damage. Hover shows full sentence.
Simplify the "This Week Buys" panel: keep the move-mastery bars; show stat changes as small coloured lines (+0.18 Stamina) not a wall of text.
