# S310 - The corner minute: four jobs instead of one pick (plan for approval)

## His ask (S310, verbatim)
"the player needs a lot more to select during the corner time, one option for a whole minute is not so exciting maybe add
a mechanism to unlock a combo the fighter to knock the opponent or something, fight looks ok for now but can still be improved"

## What a real corner does in 60 seconds
- The second: stool, water, breathing, ice on the neck or ribs, grease. The cutman works a cut or a swelling.
- The head coach gives ONE or TWO instructions she can carry (J55: two get through) and often a SET-UP: "double jab, then
  the right hand to the body, the hook comes upstairs" - a planned combination aimed at a gap he saw ("she drops the
  right after her jab").
- He reads her: calms a fighter who is wild, fires up a flat one, tells the truth to one who is losing.
- Sports-game reference (mechanic, not content): corners that split a few recovery points between energy, damage and cuts.

## The design - the minute has four jobs, each on the clock (about 15 s each, skip any)
1. RECOVER (the second): 3 points to spend.
   - Water and breathing: +energy (more if she is tired).
   - Ice on the ribs: takes back part of the body hits' cost (the new BODY HITS counter goes down).
   - Clear her head: only when she is HURT - she starts the round not hurt.
2. THE PLAN (as today): one of the eight calls for the round + the optional fix for one habit.
3. THE SET-UP (new, your idea): pick one of HER combinations to set up this round, aimed at a gap the corner saw
   (the opponent's habit from film study, or one she showed this fight). When the gap shows in the round, the shout bar
   gets a gold button "NOW! Jab-jab-right hand!". Fired at the right moment: a big edge and a knockdown chance (the 3D
   plays the combination with a close-up). At the wrong moment or when she is tired: it misses and she is open to a counter.
   One set-up a round. Combinations come from the gym (pad time), so TRAINING UNLOCKS SET-UPS: no combinations learned =
   no set-up button - a reason to drill them.
4. THE TALK: Calm her down / Fire her up / Tell her the truth. Read off her personality (temper, coachability): fire up =
   more power and more energy spent, more risk; calm = steadier, fewer counts against her; the truth works on a
   disciplined fighter, can sink a fragile one.

## What changes on screen
The corner card becomes four rows (Recover, Plan + fix, Set-up, Talk), each filled while the film of the corner plays;
the round starts with the HUD already showing what the corner did (energy up, body hits down).

## Build order
1. Rules + tests (recover points, set-up trigger and payoff, talk) + measured balance (set-up fired right should lift a
   close fight ~5-8 points; spammed wrong it should lose). 2. Corner card rows (3D + words). 3. The 3D set-up (gold
   button, combination close-up, knockdown). 4. Update 13 + a test button on the board that jumps to the corner minute.
