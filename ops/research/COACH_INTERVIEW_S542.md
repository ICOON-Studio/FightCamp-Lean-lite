## Round 1 (owner: "Yes to all five")
1. WHAT IT IS FOR: a staff game, as in Football Manager - who you hire, when, what they cost, how they get on with your
   boxers. A few decisions a season, not a puzzle solved again every week.
2. REPLACES Q13 AND Q14: the week pairs coaches and boxers by best fit on its own, with a "change" button to overrule it.
   Class nights fill the evenings the boxers do not need, shown as a fact.
3. A WRONG CALL IS FELT AS STORY: a coach who clashes with a boxer makes the boxer unhappy and may drive them out; a strong
   bond shows in the corner on fight night. Not a few hundred dollars.
4. JUDGED BY PLAY: bots only check nothing is broken (no single hire always best, the known name not always a loss). Depth
   and fun = the owner's play + two critics at 8.
5. SCOPE NOW: your own coaches only (hire, card, chemistry, courses, wages, a coach leaving). The opposing coach's card and
   coach careers wait (his earlier Q7 order).

## Round 2 (owner: "Yes to all six")
6. THE HIRE IS THE MAIN DECISION: a monthly list of three candidates; each card shows exact ratings, wage, playbook style,
   personality and one plain line on whether the club can carry the wage ("pays for itself at about 80 members").
7. THE TRIAL WEEK BUYS KNOWLEDGE: a paid one-week trial shows the chemistry word (bond / fine / clash) with each boxer
   before signing; without it the word shows two weeks after he starts.
8. FOUR MOMENTS, each rare (a few a season), each a real two-way call:
   - a falling-out: back the coach, back the boxer, or split them up; whoever is not backed loses loyalty and may leave;
   - a raise or a rival offer: after a title or a run of wins the coach asks for more or hands in two weeks' notice; match
     it or let him go;
   - a style push: the coach wants a boxer to move towards his playbook; allow (faster learning later, shakier fights
     meanwhile) or refuse (the coach's patience drops);
   - a course: money and his evenings for a few weeks; his ratings rise after.
9. WHO WORKS THE CORNER: on fight night the player picks the coach in the corner. The best tactician gives better calls
   between rounds; the coach with the bond is the one the boxer listens to; a clashing coach gets one call ignored a fight.
10. CUT THE BUILT "class nights against pad minutes" RULE (CoachRoles.gd, S515, his Q-271): classes fill the free evenings.
    The four staff jobs in the game today (second club coach, strength coach, physio, cutman) stay until rated coaches
    replace them.
11. WHEN A KNOWN NAME PAYS: a known name on $800-1,100 a week loses money in every amateur club (measured). He pays only
    once the club has a pro or a title boxer, where he lifts the purse and the name; his card says so.
