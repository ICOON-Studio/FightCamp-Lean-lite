Give the fight plan teeth and connect it to training. A pre-fight plan should be a real tactic (pressure / box / counter / body-attack) whose effectiveness scales with related mastered skills — a counter-plan is only as good as the drilled counter-punch skill, a body-attack plan only as good as the body-work skill — and the corner should be able to adjust it between rounds the way FightBeatAdapter/corner calls already allow. That closes the "plan ← training → result" loop instead of the plan being a static authored weight.

Build the presentation layer that names cause and effect. Post-fight (and at the corner) surface the engine's existing data in boxing words: attempted/landed and accuracy, the head/body/leg target mix, which contact words dominated, and a plain-language verdict — "he lost the head-shot exchange, his reads were too slow to block clean power, and his conditioning collapsed in round 3" — plus a "what to drill next" pointer back to the gym.
