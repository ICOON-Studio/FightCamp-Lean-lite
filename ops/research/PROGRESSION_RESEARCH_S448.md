# How other games make the next unlock worth waiting for (S448 research for PROGRESSION_MAP_S448)

Our words throughout; numbers from fan guides where no official data exists (Football Manager, Motorsport Manager), so they may differ by
version. Boxing Manager facts are from our own read-only study (BOXING_MANAGER_COMPARE_S425, PLAYTEST_S426), not the web.

## Games
- FOOTBALL MANAGER: coaches carry 1-5 stars per training area built from their own ratings; one coach covering more areas loses stars
  (a fan test: 5 to 3.5 when a second area was added). One individual focus per player (three slots in FM26). Training can teach or
  remove a TRAIT, a preferred move that changes what the player chooses to do. Mentoring groups move personality (determination), which
  sets how fast everything else grows. Shown as green/red arrows checked monthly and a development graph.
  fmscout.com (FM12 training guide), fminside.net (5-star coaching FM21), passion4fm.com (youth development; player traits), operationsports.com (FM26).
- MOTORSPORT MANAGER: driver traits; driver-mechanic chemistry builds race by race and opens mechanic perks at levels; one part in the
  factory at a time with a finish date, each upgrade dearer; designer perks trade days for money. The pull: a delivery date.
  windowscentral.com preview, gameplay.tips guide 3580, autosport.com review 7139.
- BOXING MANAGER (Steam 1605370): about 30 ratings, five training areas under a 100% cap, neglected areas decay; experience from bouts
  (more for a win) spent on single ratings at a price set by trainer and rapport; potential is one visible pool. Weakness: training is
  set once, quiet weeks ask nothing. WORLD BOXING MANAGER: camp gains last only to the next fight; a trainer spread over many boxers gets
  worse. MMA MANAGER 2 (mobile): skill caps rise with level, which forces specialising.
- EA UFC 4/5 career: a weekly activity budget; sparring, drills and fights pay Evolution Points (bonus for sub-goals) spent on ratings,
  moves, perks; moves unlock at one star and level by being USED, faster when they land; UFC 5 can invite a guest to learn a move. Top
  levels criticised as a grind. help.ea.com (UFC 5 level up), gamingtrend.com, gamepur.com.
- FIGHT NIGHT CHAMPION legacy: training mini-games pay points by grade; training costs stamina, so rest competes with work; reviewers
  found the games repetitive. UNDISPUTED: menu activities with a coach bonus; weight, readiness and energy juggled; staff never improve,
  they are swapped. UFC UNDISPUTED 3: new partners or programmes raise a stat's cap. kotaku.com, gamesradar.com.
- SKYRIM: a skill rises by use and feeds the character level; one perk point a level; perks need skill lines (25/50/75). PATH OF EXILE:
  about 1,300 passive nodes, beginners told to read only the notable ones (a warning). PERSONA 5: limited free slots in the calendar,
  friends only on some days, 10 ranks each opening real abilities. DARKEST DUNGEON: hero skills levelled at the Guild; upgrading the
  Guild raises the top level and lowers cost. uesp.net, poe-vault.com, pushsquare.com, segmentnext.com.
- HADES: Darkness from each run buys permanent Mirror upgrades; keys open weapons and slots; four aspects per weapon change how it plays;
  several bars fill at once, each with a named next reward. primagames.com, saschb2b.github.io meta-progression notes.
- NBA 2K: badge tiers Bronze to Hall of Fame (Legend in 2K25) gated by a rating line, progressed by use, never lost since 2K25. MADDEN:
  development traits make skill points cheaper (about 20/35/50% in Madden 23) and only X-Factor players get Zone abilities.
  primagames.com, realsport101.com, diamondlobby.com.

## Principles (what makes a weekly unlock worth waiting for)
1 one bar near its line each week, with a date; 2 the unlock changes what the athlete DOES; 3 use teaches; 4 named tiers at visible lines;
5 a pick of 2-3 under a cap; 6 staff are the multiplier and the facility upgrades staff; 7 bonds that grow with time together; 8 the next
unlock shown before it lands; 9 a small tree (5-25 nodes, one screen); 10 a fast clock and a slow permanent one.
Failure modes: grind; invisible +1s; too many currencies; trees nobody reads; maxed athletes with no cap or decline.

## Boxing, how a punch is really learned
Gym coaching practice (gymdesk.com "how to teach boxing", evolve-mma.com shadowboxing drills; coaching custom, not research): a punch
goes shadow -> bag -> pads -> controlled sparring -> a bout; footwork comes in around week 4 once the straight punches and the hook are
in; combinations follow single punches; coaches let a beginner throw several reps before correcting and keep hard sparring back. This
matches the game's "a new move is a weapon in 2-3 weeks of pad time" and a ladder of known / drilled / sparring-proven / fight-proven.
