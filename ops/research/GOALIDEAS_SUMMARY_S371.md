## Where they agree (ideas named by 3+ seats)
1. SEE THE CHANGE, side by side - "Day 1 vs today": her first-week pad round beside today's (Grok #1 strongest, Gemini
   #5 ghost split-screen, Ludo #3 ghost runner on the curve).
2. A RECORDS WALL in the gym - bests in chalk, the old number crossed out (Grok #2, Gemini #3, Ludo #4 certificate in the
   hallway). BUILT S371: TestDay.gd + the AwardCard's RECORDS WALL + "NEW BEST" in the week review.
3. TEST DAY AS A SHOW - a 10-15 s pad audition with the coach calling the grade's combo, sound that cracks when it is
   clean, the sparring partner's reaction (Gemini #1 strongest, Ludo #1 + #2 strongest, Grok #5).
4. A PASSED GRADE CHANGES HOW SHE FIGHTS THE SAME WEEK - the move card upgrades (bronze/silver/gold border, a little less
   gas), the combo joins tighten (Gemini #2 strongest, Ludo #5, Dola #3, Grok #6).
5. FELT LINES, not decimals - "took the stairs at Holloway without stopping", "you'll fade in round 3, not round 2"
   (Grok #4, Ludo #6, Dola #2 + #6).

## Good single ideas
- The coach's bet: a date and a target ("level 8 by 14 Dec"), push harder for a bigger unlock (Grok #3 strongest).
- Taper before test day: freshness on the day decides the number (Gemini #7). The plateau week + a change of plan breaks
  it (Grok #7).
- A non-fighter earns club roles at Silver/Gold/Platinum: leads the warm-up, holds pads for beginners (Gemini #6).

## What to avoid (each seat named one)
- Bars that creep up by 0.1 every day (Grok) - show progress at moments only.
- Stats that decay when she switches focus (Gemini) - grades permanent, fitness sticky.
- A dice roll that fails her assessment after 8 weeks (Ludo) - TestDay reads the stat, no luck.
- Weeks that are the same checklist (Dola).
