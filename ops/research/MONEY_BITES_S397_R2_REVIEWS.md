# MONEY_BITES_S397 - round 2: two fresh blind critics on design v2.2 (MONEY_BITES_S397_V22_BRIEF.md)

## Critic A (sim economy + small clubs): 6/10
- Year 1 tight and real; year 2 one goal (the move); year 5 (club building, 250 members) nets ~$70-75k a year with nothing
  to buy; the pile returns around weeks 150-180.
- Faults: (1) nothing grows with success after the last move - classes $770 for 60 or 250 members, one sessional coach
  runs 250 members; fix: one coach per 50 members, over it members leave 5% a month, each extra coach $450/week.
  (2) Obvious and dead buys: fitness kit + assistant +$125/week pays back in 12 weeks (always first); Low kit saves $25 but
  loses ~$26 of dues + injuries (never picked); the -35% cap wastes buys (S&C + floor already reach it); S&C, physio, cutman
  always hired. Fix: show an injury's cost in money; Low kit saves $45, no member loss, +25% risk; S&C -15%.
  (3) The club show is a cash machine for a grown club (+$2-5k every 12 weeks); real clubs run 1-3 a year; fix: turnout
  -20% if under 16 weeks since the last; the gym's boxers on the bill lose a training week.
  (4) Contract talks always end the same way (the bonus pays back in 3-4 bouts); purses above $3,000 unspecified; fix:
  talks yearly, the ask a share of last year's earnings, a real chance of losing the pro to a promoter's gym.
  (5) Only one year measured; fix: 260 weeks, cash in months of costs at years 2, 3, 5; band 1.5-4, above 6 a fault.
- Strong: DBS, fees, doctor and ambulance, raffle, day job vs stipend (the best choice), winter heating. Gap: grants with
  conditions (a youth section, a women's session) as a year-1 choice.
- An 8 has: money tied to people (bids for boxers, an assistant asks for a raise or leaves, pros renegotiate); sinks that
  grow with the gym (coach ratios, repairs with members, a lease renewal every 3 years); one late ambition as a choice
  (promote your own pro shows: $40-60k with real risk); measured bands for years 1, 2, 5.

## Critic B (FM / Two Point / boxing-manager player, ran a small club): 6/10
- Year 1 works (show, stipend vs day job, the first move are real bets); years 2 and 5 are not designed; 60 weeks of
  measurement stop before the pile would return.
- Faults: (1) nothing to want in years 3-5; fix: one long ambition after the club building (buy the freehold, or a
  promoter's licence to stage pro shows); run to week 260. (2) Upgrades and staff are a checklist: S&C beats everything,
  floor and Good kit obvious, the -35% cap a solved puzzle; fix: each item takes something scarce (the strength corner
  costs member capacity or class slots; S&C and the assistant compete for the same coaching hours). (3) The player cannot
  set the dues ($35 fixed); fix: three dues bands (cheap / standard / premium) moving joins and leavers. (4) Contract
  talks are a calculator and a one-way loss; fix: refusing carries a real chance the pro leaves; a champion lifts the
  ticket and sponsor tier for the whole roster. (5) Parts unreal: club stipends and the away-bout ticket cut; grants unused;
  fix: an athlete fund from sponsors and grants; grant applications with a deadline and a yes/no.
- An 8 adds: a loan or overdraft with interest; a rival gym (members, poaching); one money story a season (boiler, rent
  review, sponsor in trouble) with 2-3 priced answers; ONE injury risk line (low / medium / high, with the reasons) instead
  of five percentages. Keep: the club show, seasonal heating, sponsor tiers with moves, "leaves $X, about N weeks", the
  youth intakes with the coach's hint.
