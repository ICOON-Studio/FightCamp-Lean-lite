# G131 - HOW OFTEN A JUDO THROW SCORES (Grok, collected S96 2026-09-27)
Source chat: grok.com/c/a50bbee8-2807-4390-b58c-0a25ae9a9642 (Expert, 63 sources). Verbatim numbers, Grok's SOURCED/PROXY marks kept.

Rule eras: yuko existed until 2017, then only ippon/waza-ari; yuko returns for the 2026-28 cycle (JudgeMate).

## 1. Attempts and score rate
- Elite standing attacks ~5-7 per athlete per match (both sides ~11-12). Olympic male medalists 2004-16: 6.4+-2.4 vs
  opponents 5.3+-2.2 (575 matches). SOURCED: nage-waza Olympic medalists study (also cites 4.4 London 2012, 5.5 WC 2011,
  6.6 tour finals). 2013 World Ranking: 1,462 attempts / 242 bouts = 6.0/bout. SOURCED: Courel-Ibanez 2014.
- Share of attempts that score anything: ~8-16%. Olympic medalists offensive effectiveness 15.7+-9.9% SOURCED.
  Euro Champs 2021: 6,733 projections / 398 bouts (looser count), 524 scored = 7.8% SOURCED (IPCB 2021). Other elite
  7.4-8.7% SOURCED. World Tour 2017-18: 139/481 = 29% of coded attacks (different coding - high outlier) SOURCED (Stankovic).
- **Working band: ~10% of attempts score; ~1 scored throw per match.**

## 2. Band split of scoring throws
- Post-2017: Stankovic 52 ippon / 87 waza-ari = 37% / 63% SOURCED. Euro 2021 projections 304/220 = 58% / 42% SOURCED.
  Women's OG Rio+Tokyo technical wins 36/65 = 36% / 64% (nage + ne-waza) SOURCED.
- Pre-2017 British Champs 2013 (548 nage scores): yuko 36% / waza-ari 28% / ippon 36% SOURCED.

## 3. What the referee scores (IJF)
- Ippon: speed, force, largely on the back, control through landing; back ~135-180 deg, all four present.
- Waza-ari: side/back >= 90 deg rear, or one shoulder + upper back; one criterion short (post-2017 it ate old yuko).
- No score: front of 90 deg, seated leaning forward, both knees, no identified technique.
- 2026 yuko: side ~90 deg, buttocks >= 90 deg rear, upper back; does not stack to waza-ari (JudgeMate / IJF seminar).
- **For our engine: a throw that lands the man SEATED/HALF (not on his side or back) scores NOTHING under the post-2017
  rules and at most yuko under the 2026 rules. So "side -> half" landings losing their yuko is a REAL-rules consequence,
  not by itself a defect - the question is only whether the landing mix moved for a real reason.**

## 4. How matches end
- Tokyo 2020 golden score 34.6% (156/450). WC 2018-21 (n=2,340): ippon 46.2%, single waza-ari 22.6%, two waza-ari 17.7%,
  3rd shido 13.5%. Paris 2024 hansoku-gachi 20% vs Tokyo 10.1%. Older Olympic: ~67% ended by nage-waza. All SOURCED.

## 5. The one number to match first, and the sample it needs
- ~10% of throw attempts score anything; ~1 scored nage-waza per elite match.
- **24 bouts with 4 candidate landings cannot separate a defect from noise.** 10% +-3 pp needs ~400 attempts
  (~30-35 matches at 12 attempts/bout); the ippon/waza-ari split +-10 pp needs ~100 scoring throws (~100 matches).
