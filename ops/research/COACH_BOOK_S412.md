# Each coach carries a playbook (S412, 2026-11-09): input for the coach design round

Owner: "maybe each coach carries his own playbook and the player learns more by rotating few coaches ?"
Earlier the same day he said yes to designing the coach ceiling, school and bond. This idea goes into that round as its centre. Open brief, critics paint with Ludo, nothing built yet.

## What the rules do today (read from the code)
- Any coach can teach any move. `MoveMastery.gain` uses only the coach's rating, up to 1.40x at rating 100.
- The rating also unlocks game-plan lines (`PlanLines.skill_for`, "A coach rated N+ teaches line N").
- A coach's `spec` ("Pad work", "Conditioning") is display text. COACH1 is replacing it with five area ratings.
So a coach's own book would be a new rule, not a new label on an old one.

## The shape to test (Claude's call, open to the critics)
- Each coach has a short book: certain moves, combos and corner calls. A move outside the book cannot be taught by that coach.
- What a boxer has learned stays with the boxer when the coach leaves.
- BREADTH COSTS DEPTH: rotating coaches widens the boxer's book; staying with one builds the bond and goes deeper. That choice is the mechanic.

## Must hold, or it is a bad rule
- Rotating cannot be the best answer every time. The bots harness has to show both a rotating and a loyal career can win.
- No hire-for-a-week exploit: a new move already takes 2-3 weeks of pad time. Hiring and notice must cost enough on top.
- Where the book is shown has to be picture-first: the coach card, not a list.

## Open for the round
Does the area rating cap how far a coach can take a move in the book (the ceiling), and does a coach pick up moves from the others in the gym (the coach learns too)?
