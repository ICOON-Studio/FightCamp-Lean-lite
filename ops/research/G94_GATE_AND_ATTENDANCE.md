Verdict

Gate is fill × price mix, not fill alone. On the one hard same-arena contrast (UFC 229 vs 226), price did almost all the work (2.65× ticket, 1.14× heads). Full-draw does not mean 2× ticket and 100% vs 35% — real numbered Vegas PPVs already sit at 60–100% of a 20k house. The 35% cold floor is not attested on commission arena cards; club-show floors were not published. Hometown +15 is unmeasured. Replace the invented curve with: floor ~0.60 of configured arena, price as the hype lever (avg ticket can move 3–7× in the same building), hometown as a flag with no counted coefficient.
