# K86 - Kalli - The first hour with every mechanic on

Answer first: the first hour teaches ONE fighter, ONE station loop, ONE week, ONE fight. Everything that multiplies (more fighters, wear on gear, debt, zones, capacity, open-spar night, quick money) waits for week 2 or later and arrives as a single card when it first matters. One new mechanic per minute block, never two.

- Week 4+: sandbox quick-money offers (not in story mode at all until Chapter 1 ends), the DEBT decision (only fires when cash stays below zero for 2 weeks).
- Fight night VFX, feints and clinch are ON in fight 1 but never explained; they explain themselves on screen. Referee and doctor lines only fire if the fight calls for them.

## 2. Eight places a first-time player gets confused, and the fix

1. Stats, stress, career wear and recovery all on the profile at once. Fix: HIDE stress and career wear until the first week review fills them; show the bars greyed with "Fills after week 1".
2. Station wear: a worn bag trains worse and the player doesn't know why. Fix: DELAY to week 2; the first worn station gets a one-line tooltip "Worn. Train here less, or service it in the office."
3. The day clock and the skip drawer both move time. Fix: MERGE: the skip drawer opens from the clock itself, one button on the timeline bar.
4. Routes: Mara walks across the floor and "wastes" time. Fix: one-line tooltip on her first walk: "Walking eats day time. Keep stations close."
5. Training zones that move vs stations you click. Fix: DELAY zones to week 3; before that the floor is fixed.
6. Pad chains vs technique stages look like two systems for the same move. Fix: MERGE the screens: the pad chain screen shows the stage bar of the move it is building.
7. Corner advice: three voices (chief, cutman, doctor) in 45 seconds. Fix: in fight 1 only the chief lane is live; cutman and doctor light up only if there is a cut or a knockdown.
8. Quick money and debt in the same week as the story promoter debt. Fix: HIDE quick money in story mode until Chapter 1 ends; the DEBT decision only after two weeks below zero, with a week-ahead warning card.

## 3. The 12 "how" lines

1. Click the office desk to keep or rename your gym.
2. Click Mara in the locker room to open her profile.
3. Click a station on the floor, then click Mara to send her.
4. Press Space or the clock on the timeline bar to pause the day.
5. Click the pads station, then drag three moves into the chain.
6. Open the Techniques tab in her profile to see the stage.
7. Click the clock, then Skip to Friday to jump ahead.
8. Click the letter on the office desk to accept the bout.
9. Click the training plan pinned on the wall to set camp focus.
10. Click Lock or Sacrifice on the Thursday spar card.
11. Click one shout in the corner panel before the bell.
12. Click Continue on the result card to go back to the gym.

## 4. The first fight night

Before it, the player must have: sent Mara to at least two stations, drilled the 1-2 to stage 2, skipped once with the skip drawer, accepted the amateur bout from the desk, set a camp focus, done the Thursday spar, and chosen a weight cut. Nothing else is required; a player who skipped every day can still fight (cards never block).

Three things they should feel:
1. Recognition: the 1-2 they drilled shows up in the ring, by name in the round strip.
2. Helplessness, then voice: during rounds they can only watch; between rounds their one shout visibly changes her next round.
3. Cost: the swelling or limb damage stays on her body in the result card and on the profile the next morning.

## 5. Three mechanics to switch OFF for the first playable build (operator's OK needed)

1. Training zones you can move. It is a layout tool, the floor is small, and it competes with station wear for the same "why is training worse" question. Fixed zones lose nothing in the first hour.
2. Sandbox quick-money promoter offers. The story already has a promoter debt; two money pressures in the first build make the economy unreadable and hide whether the upkeep numbers ($367/wk vs $25K) are tuned.
3. Open-spar night. It needs several fighters and members to mean anything; with one fighter it is an empty room, which breaks the "never dark or empty" house rule.

Arguing against my own strongest ruling (hide station wear until week 2): wear is the loop that makes the gym feel owned, and hiding it could teach players to ignore stations. It still waits, because in week 1 no station has worn enough to matter, so showing it would teach a meter that never moves.

NOT COMPUTED: whether the 60-minute timings hold at the real day length; whether two weeks below zero is a fair DEBT trigger at $367/wk upkeep.
