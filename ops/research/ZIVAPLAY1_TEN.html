The order the decision that decides whether a punch lands

A hand is out of its guard only in the moment it punches, and it is back up by the next one. Punch first and you meet his glove; punch into the moment he throws and you meet his open line — and a fast big punch beats his slower one to the punch and stops it. Same punch, same guard, different moment, different result.
You are down 1x — two more and the referee ends it, whatever the cards say. Level on points? The round goes to the man who backed off less: you 0, him 1.

What you must not forget the four rules that cost me bouts

One held hand blocks ONE line: lead high = straight, lead LOW = the only body block there is, rear high = side, rear low = under. A punch of his that misses or is blocked makes your later punches counters (+1).
A hand is back in its guard the moment after it punches, so step in only when the step does work. Clinch: Inside only, no punch lands, +2 stamina, never twice running.
