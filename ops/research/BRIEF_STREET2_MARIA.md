# STREET2 - art direction ask for Maria (and Ludo next): the street around Anvil Street Gym, seen from the gym-floor camera

Operator S267, on the side walls coming down (stills attached in the repo: previews/s267_sidewalls/sidewalls_compare.png):
"Design a proper street around the gym - Ludo/Maria first, then build it." The S224 street (STREET1: plain brick blocks, a road, a
skyline ring) was OFF since S238 and reads crude; in the iso view one block even covers the cardio/strength corner.

## The camera you design for
- A perspective "iso" camera at Two Point Museum's FOV 17 (research/TWOPOINT_ALIGNMENT.md), pitched ~35 deg, turning in 90-degree
  steps (yaw 45/135/225/315), plus a frontal "mid" view from the street side. The gym is a single-storey building ~30 m x 26 m:
  the training hall at the back (brick back wall stands full height), a row of front rooms (office, physio, lockers, reception)
  along the street. The side walls now stand knee-high so the player sees OUT on the left and right.
- Anything outside must NEVER cover the gym's floor from any of the four iso yaws or the mid view (the S224 block did).

## What we need from you (be concrete: sizes in metres, colours as hex, materials, what sits where)
1. The neighbourhood: what kind of street is a small working-class boxing gym on (UK/US inner city, the "Anvil Street" name)?
   What stands left, right, behind and across the street - heights kept low near the gym so the camera sees over them.
2. A plan sketch in words: a 80 m x 80 m square with the gym in the middle - each neighbour's footprint, height, material, distance.
3. Street life that sells it without moving parts: parked cars, a bus stop, bins, a corner shop sign, a mural, lamp posts, trees.
4. The ground: pavement, kerb, road markings, an alley; where the grey void is today.
5. Day light: our gym is lit warm inside (2700 K); outside should read as daytime, not dusk.
6. Two Point Museum reference: how does it show the land around a museum plot (fences, grass, paths, a boundary that fades)?
7. A low-poly budget: everything from primitives or a few small props (under ~150 mesh nodes, no shadows from far objects).
Give the longest complete answer you can; numbered sections; no code.
