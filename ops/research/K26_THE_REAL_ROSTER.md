Honesty flag: There is no public percentage census of “nationality share of all ranked fighters.” Claims below are from named champions + top-10/15 lists. That is enough to see division-level patterns; it is not a survey sample.

Professional boxing (men)

Heavyweight — UK + continental Europe / former Soviet sphere dominate; US secondary.

Ring HW (Sep 2026): champ Usyk (Ukraine); Dubois, Fury, Itauma, Wardley (UK); Kabayel (Germany); Hrgovic (Croatia); Sanchez (Cuba); Ajagba (Nigeria); Gassiev (Russia); Huni (Australia). Sanctioning champs in the same window: Gassiev WBA, Kabayel WBC, Hrgovic IBF, Dubois WBO, Usyk Ring. Contender pools keep returning UK + Eastern Europe / Caucasus / Central Asia, with Cuba and Nigeria present and US names (Wilder, Miller, Torrez) as a layer — not the centre of gravity.

Welterweight — Anglo-American + US Latino core; East Asia sparse.

Ring WW top 10 (Sep 2026): Haney, Norman Jr., Lopez, Garcia, Polanco, Curiel, Rocha, Romero (US, including Spanish-origin surnames), Catterall (England), Stanionis (Lithuania). Belts in the BoxingScene/PhilBoxing window cluster US (Haney, Garcia) and UK/Australia alternates (Catterall, Paro). Contenders: Benn (UK), Crocker (N. Ireland), Donovan (Ireland), Giyasov (Uzbekistan). This is not the flyweight map.

Fly / bantam — Japan–Philippines–Thailand + Mexico / Central America.

Fly Aug 2026 combined ladders: Sandoval / Olascuaga (US), Yabuki (Japan); contender sheets packed with Japanese, Mexican, Filipino, Thai names; UK present (Yafai, Kelsall) but thinner. Bantam: Inoue (Japan), Salas Reyes (Mexico), Medina Jimenez (Mexico), Casimero/Llover (Philippines) in recent multi-body lists, Jesse Rodriguez (US) at WBA Super. Lower weights are a different sport demographically from heavyweight.

Middle / super-middle — Americas + UK + Central Asia mix.

More Mexico/LatAm and US than heavyweight; less Asia than fly. (Ring middle / super-middle Sep 2026; Canelo/Munguia gravity at 168.)

Professional boxing (women) — not a mirror of the men

Upper / middle gravity: USA + UK/Ireland. Shields (USA) across middle–heavy listings; Crews-Dezurn (USA); Cameron, Price (GBR); Taylor (IRL); Thibeault (Canada) in middle belt notes (WBA female 31 Aug 2026; WBC female Sep 2026). This is not men’s HW’s Eastern Europe bias.

Fly / light-fly: US Latina champions + Mexico / LatAm depth. Fundora (USA) holds WBA/IBF/WBO fly in the WBA female list; Mexican names dominate light-fly/minimum depth (Osorio, Juárez, etc.). Women’s lower weights rhyme with men’s fly more than with men’s HW.

Shallower pools: WBA female sheets show many “NOT RATED” slots in upper divisions — do not invent global depth that isn’t on the page.

Professional MMA (UFC ranks, Sep 2026)

Men’s lightweight — polyglot. Champ Gaethje (US). Ranked layer: Brazil (Oliveira, Moicano, Ruffy), UK (Pimblett), Armenia (Tsarukyan), Georgia/Spain pathway (Topuria), France, Poland, Australia, Kyrgyz/Azeri pathways (Fiziev). Makhachev (Dagestan) is WW champ / #1 P4P after leaving LW — current LW is not a Dagestan monoculture, though Caucasus names remain near the top.

Men’s LHW — Russia/Dagestan + Brazil + NZ/US/CEE. Champ Ulberg (NZ). Ankalaev (Dagestan), Procházka (Czechia), Pereira/Costa (Brazil), Rountree/Hill (US), Murzakanov (Russia), Błachowicz (Poland). Stronger Russia/Dagestan signal than current LW.

Men’s HW — UK champ + Russia depth + France + US + Brazil. Aspinall (UK); Gane (France); Volkov, Pavlovich (Russia); US and Brazil layers. Closer to boxing HW’s Europe bias than to boxing fly’s Asia bias.

Women’s strawweight — Brazil depth + China elite + US. Champ Dern (US roster star). Zhang Weili, Yan Xiaonan (China); long Brazilian ranked band (Jandiroba, Gomes, Andrade, Lemos, Ribas, Ricci, Thainara…); Canada/Mexico/Japan also appear. This is the division where an all-European visual default fails hardest.

Women’s bantamweight — US + Brazil. Champ Harrison (US); Peña, Pennington (US); Dumont, Luana Santos, Cavalcanti, Rosa, Mesquita (Brazil); Argentina secondary. Less China than straw.

Bare-knuckle — too small to over-generalise

Fightmag BKFC world champs: men’s WW Pague (US) / interim Franco (UK); HW Arlovski (Belarusian-American, US-centred title ecosystem) over Rothwell (US); women’s Borga / Ferea / Hart — all US. Men’s other belts mostly US, with UK (Bonner) and Italy (Sakara) exceptions. BKFC UK exists (events + regional titles) but is secondary. 2026 calendars remain US-heavy.

Ruling: Bare-knuckle has a real US concentration and a UK alternate for men. It does not support boxing-style weight-class demography claims. Women’s BKFC has three classes (feather / fly / straw) — no women’s welter. “Too small / too US-centred to generalise globally” is the honest answer.

§5 — What you would NOT change

Same call as collapsing athletic-commission corner rules: if the player can’t see it, don’t spend authenticity budget on it.

Visible at profile + gameplay camera (spend here)

Skin tone and undertone; hair colour/texture; facial structure that reads at silhouette distance; body plan already measured (keep the ratios). Region-appropriate name on the card. Women’s vs men’s division geography when the slot’s sex is female.

Depth nobody sees (do not encode as prompt law)

Exact passport vs “fights out of” vs birthplace disputes; sanctioning-body politics; amateur federation genealogy; precise % shares by country; BKFC global demography by weight; invented women’s bare welter ethnography; per-gym ethnic folklore.

Do not “evenly distribute” the twelve. A roster that ignores that men’s fly is Asia/Mexico-heavy and men’s HW is Europe-heavy fails the same authenticity test as the current light-Med default — just in the other direction.
