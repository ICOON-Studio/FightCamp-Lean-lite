1. Analysis of Existing & Proposed Paradigms
Model 1: Today's Frozen Planning Card with Time Budget Bar
How it works: The moment the bell rings, the entire world freezes. The player stares at an accounting balance: $45\text{ seconds}$ of credit. They queue up jobs ($15\text{ s}$ enswell, $25\text{ s}$ coagulant), pick a call, pick a tone, pick a pace, and hit SEND HIM OUT.
Why it fails:
Zero Physicality: The player never watches the cut actually clot or the swelling reduce; it is pure numerical subtraction.
Loss of Urgency: Without the pressure of a ticking clock, there is no adrenaline. The player solves the corner like a static puzzle rather than coaching through a crisis.
Fatigue: Over a 10- or 12-round fight, opening a dead modal card 9 to 11 times breaks game flow entirely.
Model 2: Pure Unforgiving Real-Time (Strict 60 Seconds)
How it works: Time never stops. The bell sounds, the fighter walks to the stool (taking 6 seconds), sits for 44 seconds while the player manages actions in real time, and the referee yells at 50 seconds.
Why it fails in pure form:
A 12-round fight would feature 11 full minutes of real-world dead time spent sitting on stools. For an esport or pure action game, this works; for a deep management simulation where the player wants to test multiple fight nights an evening, 11 minutes of mandatory waiting per fight kills retention.
Model 3: THE VERDICT — "THE TICKING ACTIVE ENGINE" (HYBRID CORNER)
VERDICT: SHIP MODEL 3 (THE TICKING ACTIVE ENGINE).
The Philosophy: Real-time is the core presentation, but the player commands the clock's velocity.
Core Rules of the Ticking Engine:
The Clock Runs in Real-Time by Default: The rest period is a strict 60-second broadcast countdown. The corner audio is alive: the crowd murmer, the bucket clatter, the water bottle squirt, the trainer's urgent voice.
Three-Lane Parallelism: The Cutman, the Assistant Second, and the Chief Trainer work simultaneously in parallel, not sequentially. If the Cutman spends 20 seconds on a cut, the Second spends those exact same 20 seconds chilling the neck, and the Chief spends those 20 seconds speaking.
Visual Task Resolution: Jobs do not resolve on a timer bar; they resolve on the boxer’s body in the live 3D stool view. When the cutman applies adrenaline and pressure with the swab, the bleeding visibly stems in 3D over 12 seconds.
The "Send Him Out" Fast-Forward (Player Agency): The player is never trapped. If your instructions are locked in and the cut is treated at second 25, pressing SEND HIM OUT (SPACE) immediately accelerates the remaining rest seconds at $4\times$ speed, watching vitals tick up rapidly until the bell sounds at 60s.
Auto-Default Grounding: Because the camp plan (or AI default) is pre-slotted the instant the fighter sits down, a player who does nothing has their fighter properly attended to. The corner demands intervention, not busywork.

2. The Three Parallel Work Lanes

The corner team operates as three independent concurrent state machines running in parallel across the 45 usable seconds ($t = 5.0\text{ s}$ to $t = 50.0\text{ s}$):

Lane 1: The Cutman (Physical Damage & Hemostasis)
Equipment: Cold Enswell (Iron), Adrenaline 1:1000 Chloride Swab, Gauze Pads, Petroleum Jelly (Vaseline).
Job Portfolio:
Adrenaline Swab (15s, 1 AP): Halts active bleeding. Increases cut clotting integrity by $+40%$. (Reduces bleeding rate from $1.5\text{ ml/min}$ to $0.1\text{ ml/min}$).
Cold Enswell Compress (15s, 1 AP): Applied to orbital/cheekbone swelling. Halts swelling expansion; reduces current swelling tier by 1 level if caught early.
Vaseline Grease Layer (10s, 1 AP): Slicks over brow and cheekbones. Lowers friction coefficient on incoming punches for 2 rounds, turning direct cuts into grazing blows ($30%$ deflection bonus).
Deep Wound Pack (25s, 2 AP): High-tier emergency treatment for Grade 3/4 lacerations. Requires steady hands; keeps the ringside physician from halting the fight for at least 1 more round.
Lane 2: The Assistant Second (Thermoregulation & Cardiorespiratory Recovery)
Equipment: Ice Collar, Sponge & Bucket, Aeration Towel, Squeeze Water Bottle, Mouthpiece Disinfectant.
Job Portfolio:
Neck Ice Collar & Torso Mist (12s, 1 AP): Rapid core cooling. Drops body temperature, knocking $-15\text{ bpm}$ off elevated heart rate immediately.
Mouthpiece Cleanse & Deep Hydration (10s, 1 AP): Cleans airway of coagulated saliva/blood. Restores $+5%$ baseline aerobic stamina pool.
Vigorous Chest Aeration (Towel Flap) (15s, 1 AP): Enhances oxygen replenishment rate ($VO_2\text{ recovery}$ speed boosted by $+20%$ during the rest minute).
Deep Muscle Flush (20s, 1.5 AP): Vigorously loosens deltoids and traps to restore punching snap and guard recovery speed.
Lane 3: The Chief Trainer (Psychology, Tactics & Tells)
Equipment: Verbal coaching, eye contact, psychological alignment.
Job Portfolio:
Deliver Tone (10s, Mandatory first 10s): Sets fighter's psychological state using the State $\times$ Tone Matrix.
Tactical Call Confirmation / Audible (15s): Reinforces the Camp Directive or triggers an in-fight tactical pivot.
Activate Scouted Opponent Tell (15s): If unlocked in pre-fight camp scouting, primes your fighter to punish a specific recurring habit.
Deploy Exit Plan (15s): Defensive counter-measure against aggressive clinching or smothering.

What Happens on a Blunder?

If a blunder roll fails during a job:

Cutman Blunder: Swab slips into the eye socket (causes momentary eye sting, $-10$ composure) OR enswell tears delicate skin, converting a Grade 1 swelling into a Grade 2 bleeding laceration.
Second Blunder: Water bottle nozzle chokes the fighter; fighter coughs uncontrollably, aborting aerobic recovery for that rest period.
Chief Blunder: Crossed signals; fighter enters the round confused, failing to execute the tactical call for the first $45\text{ seconds}$ of the round.
5. Interrupts, Emergencies & Throwing the Towel
The Ringside Physician Inspection (The Cut Check)

When a facial cut reaches Grade 3 (Severe) or swelling completely seals an eye shut:

At $t = 30\text{ s}$, the referee signals the ringside physician to step onto the apron.
The 3D camera cuts to the doctor leaning over the fighter with a penlight.
An evaluation bar appears: PHYSICIAN THRESHOLD: [████████░░] 76% (CRITICAL).
If your Cutman achieved sufficient clotting and reduced swelling before second 30, the doctor taps the referee: "He can go, watch that eye."
If untreated: The doctor waves his hands, signaling a TKO via Doctor Stoppage.
Throwing the Towel ([ESC] or Dedicated Red Button)
A large, tactile, safety-latched red lever sits at the bottom-left: [ THROW IN THE TOWEL ].
Clicking requires a 0.6-second confirmation hold (to prevent accidental misclicks on mouse sweeps).
The Result: The chief trainer steps onto the apron and tosses the white terrycloth towel into the center ring canvas. The referee immediately waves off the contest, preserving the fighter from permanent neurological trauma, career-ending eye damage, or brutal knockouts. In career mode, this preserves fighter longevity and prevents extended medical suspensions.

COMPLETE IMPLEMENTATION CODE: GODOT 4.7 CORNER SYSTEM

To eliminate all ambiguity for the gameplay engineering team, here is the reference GDScript implementation tying the three parallel lanes, the real-time clock, and the fast-forward accelerator together.

res://scripts/combat/corner/corner_engine.gd
class_name CornerEngine
extends Node

signal corner_started()
signal usable_window_opened()
signal seconds_out_called()
signal corner_concluded()
signal vitals_updated(heart_rate: float, stamina: float, composure: float)
signal job_progress_updated(lane_index: int, job_name: String, progress: float)
signal doctor_inspection_triggered()

# Pacing & Timing Constants
const TOTAL_REST_TIME: float = 60.0
const ARRIVAL_TIME: float = 5.0
const SECONDS_OUT_TIME: float = 10.0 # Occurs when 10.0s remain (elapsed 50.0s)
const USABLE_DURATION: float = TOTAL_REST_TIME - ARRIVAL_TIME - SECONDS_OUT_TIME # 45.0s

# Time Scaling
var time_scale: float = 1.0
const ACCELERATED_TIME_SCALE: float = 4.0

# Runtime Variables
var elapsed_time: float = 0.0
var is_corner_active: bool = false
var usable_active: bool = false
var seconds_out_fired: bool = false

# Fighter & Staff References
var current_fighter: Fighter = null
var current_opponent: Fighter = null
var assigned_camp: CampData = null

# Lane Controller States (0: Cutman, 1: Second, 2: Chief)
enum LaneType { CUTMAN = 0, SECOND = 1, CHIEF = 2 }
var active_jobs: Array[CornerJob] = [null, null, null]
var job_queue: Array[Array] = [[], [], []]

# Pace Mode
enum CrewPace { CAREFUL, NORMAL, RUSHED }
var current_pace: CrewPace = CrewPace.NORMAL

func _process(delta: float) -> void:
	if not is_corner_active:
		return
		
	var effective_delta: float = delta * time_scale
	elapsed_time += effective_delta
	
	# Phase 1: Arrival Complete -> Open Usable Window
	if elapsed_time >= ARRIVAL_TIME and not usable_active and elapsed_time < (TOTAL_REST_TIME - SECONDS_OUT_TIME):
		usable_active = true
		usable_window_opened.emit()
		
	# Phase 2: Seconds Out Trigger (at 50.0s)
	if elapsed_time >= (TOTAL_REST_TIME - SECONDS_OUT_TIME) and not seconds_out_fired:
		seconds_out_fired = true
		usable_active = false
		seconds_out_called.emit()
		
	# Phase 3: Conclusion
	if elapsed_time >= TOTAL_REST_TIME:
		conclude_corner()
		return
		
	# Update active parallel lanes during usable window
	if usable_active:
		_process_lanes(effective_delta)
		_process_vitals_decay(effective_delta)

func start_corner(fighter: Fighter, opponent: Fighter, camp: CampData, is_auto_send: bool) -> void:
	current_fighter = fighter
	current_opponent = opponent
	assigned_camp = camp
	
	elapsed_time = 0.0
	is_corner_active = true
	usable_active = false
	seconds_out_fired = false
	time_scale = 1.0
	
	# Pre-seed camp default directive into Chief Lane
	_apply_camp_defaults()
	
	corner_started.emit()
	
	# If auto-send is enabled and no safety interrupts trigger, auto-accelerate
	if is_auto_send and not _check_safety_interrupts():
		trigger_fast_forward()

func trigger_fast_forward() -> void:
	time_scale = ACCELERATED_TIME_SCALE

func set_crew_pace(new_pace: CrewPace) -> void:
	current_pace = new_pace

func queue_job(lane: LaneType, job: CornerJob) -> void:
	if not usable_active:
		return
	if active_jobs[lane] == null:
		active_jobs[lane] = job
		job.start_job(current_fighter, current_pace)
	else:
		job_queue[lane].append(job)

func _process_lanes(delta: float) -> void:
	for i in range(3):
		var job: CornerJob = active_jobs[i]
		if job != null:
			job.process_work(delta)
			var progress: float = job.get_progress_percentage()
			job_progress_updated.emit(i, job.job_name, progress)
			
			if job.is_completed:
				job.apply_outcome(current_fighter, current_pace)
				active_jobs[i] = null
				
				# Pull next queued item
				if job_queue[i].size() > 0:
					var next_job: CornerJob = job_queue[i].pop_front()
					active_jobs[i] = next_job
					next_job.start_job(current_fighter, current_pace)

func _process_vitals_decay(delta: float) -> void:
	# Passive heart rate recovery on stool (-1.2 bpm/s base)
	var hr_decay: float = 1.2 * delta
	if active_jobs[LaneType.SECOND] != null and active_jobs[LaneType.SECOND].job_id == "ice_collar":
		hr_decay += 0.8 * delta # Boosted by neck ice
		
	current_fighter.heart_rate = max(115.0, current_fighter.heart_rate - hr_decay)
	
	# Stamina regeneration (+0.1% per second base on stool)
	var stamina_regen: float = 0.1 * delta
	current_fighter.current_stamina = min(100.0, current_fighter.current_stamina + stamina_regen)
	
	vitals_updated.emit(current_fighter.heart_rate, current_fighter.current_stamina, current_fighter.composure)

func _apply_camp_defaults() -> void:
	if assigned_camp != null and assigned_camp.focus_tactic != "":
		# Lock camp tactic with gold directive
		current_fighter.active_tactical_directive = assigned_camp.focus_tactic
		current_fighter.directive_origin = "FROM CAMP"
	else:
		current_fighter.active_tactical_directive = "Hold the Centre"
		current_fighter.directive_origin = "COACH'S CALL"

func _check_safety_interrupts() -> bool:
	if current_fighter.was_knocked_down_last_round:
		return true
	if current_fighter.get_worst_cut_grade() >= 2:
		return true
	if current_fighter.get_consecutive_lost_rounds() >= 2:
		return true
	return false

func conclude_corner() -> void:
	is_corner_active = false
	time_scale = 1.0
	corner_concluded.emit()

Residual Implementation Risks & Mitigations
Risk: Animation Popping on Stool.
Problem: A boxer switching rapidly between sitting breathing, receiving ice, and having a swab applied could suffer from harsh animation blending.
Mitigation: Layered additive skeletal animations on the MetaHuman rig. The torso heaving is on a base additive track; the neck ice and cutman swab run as localized IK arm/head constraint targets.
Risk: Accidental Towel Throws.
Problem: A player hitting ESC to bring up the pause menu accidentally forfeits the championship fight.
Mitigation: Strict 0.6-second radial fill hold requirement. A standard click on ESC simply opens the system options menu with the fight timer paused.
Risk: Sound Cacophony in Parallel Lanes.
Problem: Having the trainer scream, the ice crunch, and the crowd roar simultaneously can create an unintelligible audio wash.
Mitigation: Audio ducking priority bus. The Chief Trainer’s voice is assigned to the High-Priority Dialogue bus; the water squirts and swab tools are clamped to $-12\text{ dB}$, and the arena crowd is low-pass filtered (muffled) to simulate being locked inside the fighter's focused acoustic bubble on the stool.
