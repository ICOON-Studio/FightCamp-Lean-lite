# Hype around a fighter and a fight, and the coach's part (S432 research for HYPE1)

Operator, S432: "maybe do a research ... about how hype builds around a fight and a fighter , what role the coach can
play into that". Built from three web notes written this session (`hype_notes/angle1_fighter.md`, `angle2_coach.md`,
`angle3_fight_and_games.md`), the older top-level study `G89_FIGHT_HYPE.md`, and a few extra checks made for this page.
All wording is ours. "Anecdotal" = one fighter, one club or one promoter's account, not a measured sample. In section 6,
**[R]** = a number taken from the research, **[E]** = our estimate where no source gives one.

## The short answer

- At club and small-hall scale, hype is **people walking through the door**, and nearly all of them come because they
  **know someone on the card**. Small shows have no TV money; the fighters sell the tickets hand to hand.
  https://www.irish-boxing.com/?p=146189 , https://tidyhq.com/blog/boxing-game-day-planning-guide-uk
- Two different things get called hype: **bout heat** (this fight: rivalry, rematch, derby, close odds; spent on the
  night, gone in days) and **following** (the fighter's own people; grows a little with every good night, drains slowly
  when idle). Heat sells this show; following sells every show. (our reading of the sources in sections 1-2)
- The coach **builds** hype (puts the fighter in front of people, predicts, talks to the paper), **shields** it (picks
  opponents, paces the step-up), **lends** the coach's and gym's name, and **takes the blame** when it bursts.
- Games that do this well make hype **a visible choice that can blow up** (WMMA's hype gamble, WWE MyGM rivalry
  levels, Football Manager supporter groups). Players punish **canned text** and meters that change nothing.

## 1. How hype builds around a fighter (club and small-hall scale)

**Lever 1 - the fighter's own people (the base of everything).**
- Amateur club home shows in England fill 200-500 seats: Southport over 400, Team Shoe-Box 500+ for 15 bouts, Lions Den
  Newark 200+, Lydney about 300. https://www.englandboxing.org/?p=36235 , https://www.southportvisiter.co.uk/news/southport-west-lancs/southport-boxing-clubs-sell-out-11203681 , https://newarkadvertiser.co.uk/sport/home-show-sees-ten-winners-9290103 , https://www.theforestreview.co.uk/news/sell-out-for-towns-boxers-201375
- Tickets are cheap: about GBP 12-20 an adult, GBP 5-10 a child; dinner shows GBP 30-50. (search summaries) https://www.lincolnshireworld.com/sport/boston-abc-boxers-fighting-fit-ahead-their-home-show-724285 , https://theringboxingclub.com/an-evening-of-championship-boxing
- Each amateur boxer is typically asked to sell about 20-40 tickets to family and friends; the door makes up the rest
  (club-software guide, practice not a rule). White-collar first-timers commit to 10.
  https://tidyhq.com/blog/boxing-game-day-planning-guide-uk , https://ultrawhitecollarboxing.co.uk/tickets-boxing-events/
- A novice pro is expected to move roughly 100 tickets (one promoter's model). https://queensberry.co.uk/blogs/frank-warren-digest/just-the-ticket
- Reported debut sales (anecdotal, self-reported): Josh Warrington about 70; Tyler Christopher 30-40 a fight; Ryan
  Woolridge 140+; Luke Gibbo 200+; Mikey Harrison about 300; Sahil Khan 300+ (a six-time national amateur champion).
  https://www.frankwarren.com/just-the-ticket-for-warrington/ , https://www.fightcity.uk/news/small-hall-boxings-been-cursed-by-social-media-hull ,
  https://boxingnewsonline.net/inside-a-professional-debut , https://www.britishboxingnews.co.uk/blogs/luke-gibbo-wins-pro-debut-in-maidstone , https://www.thestar.co.uk/sport/boxing/popular-sheffield-boxer-is-chasing-a-dream-4694097 , https://www.fightcity.uk/news/khan-sold-over-300-tickets-for-his-debut
- Reading those: 30-70 = weak seller, about 100 = the floor, 150-300 = a good local draw, 300+ = strong (usually a known
  amateur, so **the amateur years build the pro gate**). A seller of about 1,000 by fight 4-5 is rare and "too big for
  small halls" (promoter Carl Greaves). https://www.boxingnewsonline.net/the-stress-of-small-hall-boxing-is-incredible/
- Claimed is not paid: Greaves says a fighter's "100 sold" often becomes about 50 once the money is collected
  (anecdotal). https://boxingnewsonline.net/news/carl-greaves-on-the-most-stressful-show-ever/

**Lever 2 - winning, and winning in a way people talk about.** Knockouts draw (Jaron Ennis's father says fans love
them; Ennis's hometown nights grew from about 1,300 at Philadelphia's 2300 Arena in 2018 to a 4,500 presale for an arena date). https://inquirer.com/sports/jaron-ennis-cody-crowley-welterweight-title-fight-20240513.html

**Lever 3 - activity.** Builders keep prospects busy: 3-4 fights a year is the common target (Josh Kelly, Woolridge);
Tyson had 14-15 fights in year one, about one every 3-4 weeks. https://ringmagazine.com/en/news/josh-kelly-set-for-three-quick-fire-outings-before-end-of-the-year , https://www.expressandstar.com/sport/boxing/ryan-rhino-woolridge-wont-run-before-he-can-walk-5927867 , https://www.boxing247.com/boxing-news/mike-tyson-hector-mercedes/91127

**Lever 4 - community and personality.** Johnny Fisher's father sold about 3,500 tickets by hand from the family front
room and a chip shop; Fisher and Dave Allen draw through self-mocking humour and showing daily life; about 8,000 came to
their rematch. https://boxingnewsonline.net/johnny-fisher-is-aiming-for-the-top-table/ , https://www.ringmagazine.com/news/johnny-fisher-dave-allen-ii-showed-magic-of-domestic-level-clashes-2fmJ89PGtRR9yOhnk4SAkV
Warrington's crowd grew as a football crowd (Leeds United songs), from about 70 at the debut to just under 10,000 for a
world eliminator by 2015. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Warrington , https://bleacherreport.com/articles/2431046-boxing-eddie-hearn-encourages-football-fan-culture-but-warning-signs-are-there

**Lever 5 - the local paper, not social media.** Local papers write up every club show with names and results; trainer
Anthony Hull says posts and pad clips bring viewers, not buyers, and the old way (posters in takeaways, phone calls,
meeting people) still sells. https://redditchstandard.co.uk/sport/boxing-wallop-boxing-club-to-host-first-home-show-this-weekend/ , https://www.fightcity.uk/news/small-hall-boxings-been-cursed-by-social-media-hull
TV research agrees names beat closeness: across 210 HBO/Showtime broadcasts, viewers preferred home boxers and
rematches; a close match did not lift viewing. https://cora.ucc.ie/handle/10468/10747

**Lever 6 - the record and how it is built.** The old build was a long unbeaten run on safe opponents; promoters now
say fans will not pay to watch that and push 50/50 or 60/40 fights at about 4-0 rather than 10-0. Journeyman fees rose
40-50% after the pandemic. https://www.boxingnewsonline.net/competitive-matchmaking-is-the-key-to-saving-small-hall-boxing/ , https://boxingnewsonline.net/news/how-to-build-a-prospect/

## 2. How hype fades, how fast, and what crashes it

- **No study measures a club fighter's draw fading week by week.** What exists is shape, not a rate.
- **Bout buzz fades in days.** In top-level MMA data, a fighter's audience demand turned down within about 3 days of a
  loss; it held for a winner and for a fighter who stayed visible between fights. https://www.parrotanalytics.com/insights/ufc-324-demand-volatility-and-the-new-talent-leverage-playbook/
  The same analysis: steady winners keep demand; **hype without pedigree collapses after a loss** (Paddy Pimblett).
- **Inactivity is the slow drain.** ESPN dropped fighters from its rankings for it; Keith Thurman says long gaps turn
  affection into suspicion; after about two years out, fans had given up on Gary Russell Jr (opinion). https://africa.espn.com/boxing/story/_/id/32252125/where-paging-gennadiy-golovkin-andy-ruiz-keith-thurman-gary-russell-jr , https://www.boxing247.com/boxing-news/keith-thurman-admits-inactivity-breeds-suspicion-ahead-of-fundora-fight/303049 , https://www.essentiallysports.com/boxing-news-nobody-noticed-these-boxers-who-didnt-fight-in-twenty-twenty-four
  Hamed's decline began when fights got rarer (promoter Frank Warren). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naseem_Hamed
- **The dull win.** A perfect record on the same journeymen bores the base even while it stays perfect. https://www.boxingnewsonline.net/competitive-matchmaking-is-the-key-to-saving-small-hall-boxing/
- **The loss: a hometown base forgives, neutral buzz does not.** Frank Buglioni lost the 0 and still sold 400+ for a
  Wednesday comeback; Callum Simpson sold 1,500+ for a rematch after losing titles; Warrington drew about 20,000 months
  after a first loss. But Fisher, built fast on a big following, was stopped and the promoter planned a slow rebuild
  with no main events. https://boxingnewsonline.net/frank-buglioni-i-have-learned-lessons-i-will-come-back-stronger-and-i-feel-i-am-ready-for-rocky-fielding , https://www.thestar.co.uk/your-world/callum-simpsons-supporters-give-him-lift-before-rematch-8837419 , https://boxingnewsonline.net/news/when-everything-changed-for-josh-warrington/ , https://ringmagazine.com/en/news/eddie-hearn-laments-johnny-fisher-s-inexperience-wants-dave-allen-to-finally-fulfil-his-potential
- **The pull-out crashes the night.** A Newark card planned with 7 bouts ran 3; another Greaves show lost a 300-ticket
  seller to a hand injury and had only 5 of 8 bouts matched two days out; an Irish card with eight fighters was cancelled
  about two weeks out because some had sold as few as 3. A Chester boxer refunded their own fans after a pull-out. https://www.boxingnewsonline.net/the-stress-of-small-hall-boxing-is-incredible/ ,
  https://boxingnewsonline.net/news/carl-greaves-on-the-most-stressful-show-ever/ , https://www.irish-boxing.com/?p=146189 , https://www.chesterchronicle.co.uk/sport/other-sport/more-frustration-chester-boxer-paul-9344891
- **Missed weight** is the documented fight-week shock that kills hype and sometimes the bout (G89 section 2).

## 3. The coach's role: build, manage, protect, deflate

**At club scale the coach does most jobs.** England Boxing says the coach decides when a boxer is ready and matches
them by age, weight and experience. https://www.englandboxing.org/?p=4521 Carl Greaves was promoter, manager,
trainer and matchmaker on one show, working four of the eight corners.
https://boxingnewsonline.net/news/carl-greaves-on-the-most-stressful-show-ever/ In club-show reports most quotes come
from the coach, who also builds the card and names the captain for a club-vs-club night (angle 3 note, A3).

**Build.** Brendan Ingle predicted in public, for years, that a child at the gym (Naseem Hamed) would be world champion,
paraded Hamed down a Sheffield shopping street before the debut, and pushed the showmanship; debut to world title took
about 3.5 years. https://www.irishtimes.com/sport/other-sports/brendan-ingle-obituary-trainer-whose-influence-reached-far-beyond-the-ring-1.3508448 ,
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naseem_Hamed Tyson's team sent tapes of early knockouts to TV news, so Tyson was on
magazine covers before a world ranking. https://www.boxing247.com/boxing-history/mike-tyson-the-1980s Trainer Hull ties
sponsors to a fighter selling about 20 tickets: the gym sets the ticket culture. https://www.fightcity.uk/news/small-hall-boxings-been-cursed-by-social-media-hull

**Manage (fight week).** Freddie Roach's public predictions (De La Hoya in 9, it ended in 8; Hatton in 3, it ended in 2)
and the feud with Hatton's trainer became the storyline. https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/roach-taunts-hatton-predicts-pacquiao-knockout-three
Teddy Atlas's corner speeches for the 2-1 underdog Michael Moorer are among the most remembered parts of that fight.
https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Teddy_Atlas Hiring a coach can itself be a public promise: Tyson Fury said SugarHill
Steward was hired for a knockout. https://boxingnewsonline.net/news/tyson-fury-hired-new-trainer-sugar-hill-to-get-the-knockout-against-deontay-wilder

**Protect.** Cus D'Amato kept Floyd Patterson away from Sonny Liston for years and defended against lesser names;
critics called it protection at the cost of credibility, and when the fight came it ended in two first-round knockouts.
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cus_D%27Amato Virgil Hunter's closed "us against the world" camp kept Andre Ward great in
the ring and weak at the box office. https://bleacherreport.com/articles/1668099-andre-ward-why-is-he-a-stud-in-the-ring-but-a-dud-at-the-box-office
Anatoly Lomachenko gives almost no interviews; the silence became its own mystique. https://www.si.com/boxing/2018/12/17/anatoily-lomacheko-papachenko-boxing-trainer-of-the-year
Coaches also warn a prospect off a step-up in public (Brian McIntyre on Frank Martin vs Gervonta Davis). https://sportskeeda.com/mma/news-we-ain-t-gotta-rush-terence-crawford-s-trainer-advises-gervonta-davis-upcoming-opponent-avoid-facing-tank-right-now

**Deflate (when it bursts, the coach is the cheapest change).** Amir Khan's new trainer Jorge Rubio suggested the
opponent, Khan was knocked out in 54 seconds, Rubio was let go after one fight; with Roach, Khan won a world title about
10 months later. https://www.skysports.com/boxing/news/4171367/khan-sacks-trainer-rubio ,
https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/sport/archives/2009/07/20/2003449113 After Ronda Rousey's upset, fans, media and
family blamed coach Edmond Tarverdyan, who accepted it. https://bjpenn.com/mmanews/rouseys-coach-takes-blame-for-loss-to-holm
Ingle warned that Hamed had stopped listening; that warning became the accepted story of the fall. https://www.irishtimes.com/sport/other-sports/brendan-ingle-obituary-trainer-whose-influence-reached-far-beyond-the-ring-1.3508448

**The coach's and gym's own reputation.**
- It rides on the star: all seven of Roach's Trainer of the Year awards came in the Pacquiao years; writers call Wild
  Card a shadow of that time now. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Futch%E2%80%93Condon_Award , https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/freddie-roach-reflects-ahead-of-manny-pacquiaos-last-dance
- Fame pulls fighters in: Eddy Reynoso's Canelo years drew Oscar Valdez, Ryan Garcia and Andy Ruiz (not all stayed). https://africa.espn.com/boxing/story/_/id/29139090/andy-ruiz-teams-canelo-alvarez-trainer-eddy-reynoso
- The gym as a brand: Kronk fighters wore red and gold, dressed neatly, travelled and cheered as a team; seven Steward
  amateurs won Golden Gloves titles in 1971; world titles came in 1980. https://www.si.com/boxing/2012/10/25/emanuel-steward-nack-1981
- **A local hero fills the club** (all club self-reports, no measure of how long it lasts): Bolton Lads' and Girls' Club
  took about 83 extra young people in one week after Amir Khan's 2004 Olympic silver (search summary)
  https://www.cypnow.co.uk/content/other/youth-clubs-olympic-win-swells-club-membership ; Ludlow went from 26 to 70
  after the 2012 Games (search summary) https://www.shropshirestar.com/news/2012/12/03/ludlow-boxing-club-enjoys-boost-from-olympic-games ;
  Kellie Harrington's first club was "inundated" but had room for 45, 60 at most with a refit
  https://www.irishtimes.com/news/ireland/irish-news/club-where-kellie-harrington-began-boxing-career-inundated-1.4643074 ;
  Cleary's in Warwickshire went 50 to 250 in two months after a move and a member's Commonwealth gold (the two not
  separated) https://warwick.nub.news/news/local-news/huge-increase-in-memberships-at-boxing-gym-following-move-to-new-hq-215202
- Coach pay rises with the fighter: about 10% of the purse is usual (Irish example: manager 15%, trainer 10%, cutman 5%). https://www.irish-boxing.com/?p=146189

## 4. A fight's build-up, and how hype becomes tickets and lasting fans

**What lifts one bout above "a friend is fighting":**
1. **Club v club with a team score.** Liverpool v Manchester amateur finals drew nearly 4,000 in 1973, a trophy from
   1957. https://boxingnewsonline.net/liverpool-vs-manchester-a-great-amateur-boxing-rivalry/ Today: Wallop BC v One
   Nation ABC, 15 bouts, a captain's bout headlining, team trophy, 250+ crowd. https://redditchstandard.co.uk/sport/boxing-wallop-boxing-club-win-trophy-after-beating-one-nation-abc-in-club-versus-club-home-show/
   Cambridge Town v Gown has run 30+ years, sold out. https://varsity.co.uk/sport/29145
2. **A local derby of two named fighters** (Crawley v Horsham sold out a social club; a rematch was called for at once). https://www.sussexexpress.co.uk/sport/other-sport/massey-the-star-of-crawley-boxing-clubs-big-show-3476593
3. **A rematch after a disputed result.** Crocker v Donovan 1 ended in a disqualification; the rematch moved to a
   football stadium with 15,000+ sold in advance. https://boxingnewsonline.net/news/windsor-park-eyed-for-crocker-donovan-rematch-after-controversial-first-bout/
4. **A competitive match.** See lever 6.
5. **Not a title alone.** The 2018 national amateur finals had a sparse crowd and three media. https://www.pressreader.com/uk/boxing-news/20180426/281556586434537
6. **The night out.** Dinner shows and a charity gala sell on atmosphere and food (Sioux Falls, four sell-outs). https://www.dakotanewsnow.com/2026/11/05/sioux-falls-boxing-classic-moving-convention-center-after-4-straight-sellouts/

**The build-up, step by step (club scale).** Poster and date (at top level the two-name poster is the one marketing act
with a measured lift, about +150,000 PPV buys, G89 / Watanabe 2017) -> ticket push -> weigh-in (closed at small shows;
big local names hold public ones, e.g. about 500 at Leeds City Museum) https://www.yorkshireeveningpost.co.uk/sport/boxing/heros-welcome-for-josh-warrington-at-trinity-leeds-warm-up-to-elland-road-title-fight-644168
-> the night. **Sales come late**: about 31% of sports-event tickets sell in the final week (vendor data), and a
promoter reads the curve about two weeks out. https://rsvpify.com/event-ticketing-trends-2026/ ,
https://www.irish-boxing.com/?p=146189 Six weeks is called the minimum selling runway (search summary). https://sports.yahoo.com/triller-ticketing-strategy-raises-questions-095527557.html

**Money from tickets.** Irish debut: about EUR 2,750 of costs before the fighter earns; at EUR 60 a ticket, 46 tickets
to fight for nothing, 67 to also earn EUR 1,250; above target the split is about 50-50.
https://www.irish-boxing.com/?p=146189 Philadelphia club shows: $60 general, $100-150 ringside. https://www.ringmagazine.com/news/hall-of-famer-lou-di-bella-is-bringing-broadway-boxing-to-broad-street-this-thursday-in-philly-2ZmAu7AGG2ZmgRpRCsMDgV

**From buzz to lasting fans.** Attendance is partly habit: people who come keep coming.
https://eprints.lancs.ac.uk/149556/1/Recession_and_Football_Final_Nov2020.pdf A new attraction's lift lasted about 8
seasons in baseball and 9 in basketball, and whether it lasts depends on how loyal the new fans are.
https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:bla:coecpo:v:23:y:2005:i:3:p:436-455 , https://papers.tinbergen.nl/24010.pdf
Online gains are lumpy (one card: mean about 212,000 followers gained, median about 3,950), and a loser with a show
can out-gain the winner (118K vs 53K). (fan analysis) https://medium.com/@wesley_russell/the-conor-mcgregor-effect-how-being-on-a-conor-mcgregor-card-impacts-a-fighters-following-ab5b5d59f01b , https://www.essentiallysports.com/ufc-mma-news-paddy-pimblett-beats-justin-gaethje-outside-octagon-with-one-twenty-one-thousand-gain-after-ufc-324/

**Liked:** hype as a gamble you choose that can blow up; hype that visibly changes something (a venue unlocks, a match
rating rises, a fan group reacts); a rivalry as an object that grows in levels and then ends; fans of different kinds
with different patience. **Disliked:** repeating canned text; a meter that changes nothing; grind to keep fame topped
up; choices whose effect is invisible (MMA Manager plans, https://gamebrain.co/game/mma-manager-fight-hard).

## 6. Design implications for FightCamp (one club, four fighters, amateur to a first pro fight)

**Model: two numbers per fighter, one per club, one per bout.**
- **Following** (people who buy a ticket for this fighter). Sticky. Grows after good nights, drains slowly when idle.
- **Buzz** (the fighter's "next big thing" talk on top). Fast up, fast down; crashes on a loss, hardest vs a weak
  opponent. (Parrot: hype without pedigree collapses; Buglioni/Simpson: the base stays.)
- **Club name** (gym and coach reputation). Rides on the current stars, drifts down without one, pulls new members.
- **Bout heat** (this fight: derby, rematch, club v club, 50/50). Spent on the night; gone within about two weeks.

**Decisions this research supports (each changes a number the player sees):**
1. **Matchmaking: safe, 50/50 or derby.** Safe protects the record but buzz stops growing (dull wins); 50/50 and
   derbies add bout heat and gate but risk the loss. The coach's core hype lever at club scale (England Boxing: the coach
   matches). Step-up timing is a real choice (about 4-0 vs 10-0).
2. **Coach stance per fighter: Sell, Shield or Neutral.** Sell (local paper, open session, prediction): buzz up fast,
   bigger crash on a loss. Shield: record and nerves protected, buzz grows slowly, "protected" talk if held too long
   (D'Amato, Hunter/Ward).
3. **Fight-week prediction by the coach.** "Calls a stoppage": heat now; landing it lifts the club name, missing costs
   both (Roach's calls).
4. **Ticket push vs training.** Hours spent selling cost training (Hull: fighters should not carry it all); a sponsor
   or the gym can take part of the load in return for a ticket target (about 20 tickets).
5. **Claimed vs paid tickets.** A fighter promises a number; about half may arrive (Greaves). The two-week read: cut or
   keep a bout when sales are low, at a cost to the club name.
6. **Club v club night with a captain's bout** for the hosted club show (already in scope after the four-fighter work).
7. **After a heavy loss: change the plan or the coach.** A changed coach can become the comeback story (Khan, Roach).
8. **Rematch offer after a close or disputed decision** (Crocker-Donovan, Fisher-Allen): extra bout heat, one time.

**What to avoid:**
- A single "hype" meter. It hides the most useful split: friends and family stay after a loss, buzz does not.
- Canned press lines and social posts (FM, EA UFC). If a coach talks to the paper, the line must change a number shown.
- Titles as the main pull at amateur level (empty national finals); derbies and rematches sell.
- Upkeep grind (Punch Club fame jobs). Following should drain slowly and only when idle, not need weekly feeding.
- Social follower counts as ticket sales: viewers are not buyers at this scale (Hull).
- A hype number with no receipt: it must show up as seats, the gate, new members or the crowd noise (G89 section 4).
- Overreading the estimates: no source gives a club-level fade rate; every [E] above needs a balance pass.
