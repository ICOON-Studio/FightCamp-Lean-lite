# VERDICT K87 - how many floor decisions a day (S236, for the operator's OK)

Source: Kalli K87 (research/K87_DECISION_PACING_REPLY.md, verbatim). Claude's verdict: take it as written.

## What the player gets
- At most **6 decision cards a day** stop the clock, and at most **2 in any game hour** (20 game minutes apart at least).
- Anything past that is **decided by the coach** and written as one line in the day log ("Coach: called the rep. Form +2, strain +4"),
  with 1-3 stars for how good his call was. Click the line to see the card he answered. Nothing disappears silently.
- A better coach makes better calls (best call 50% + skill/2). Hiring a good coach buys back your attention.
- **Injury first:** a niggle always jumps the queue; it is never dropped. A breakthrough is never dropped either.
- At **3x speed** only an injury over 60 soreness, a breakthrough, and a sparring knockdown/cut moment stop the clock; the rest go to the coach.
- **Quiet days** you can see coming on the Morning Day Plan as pips: recovery day 2 cards, fight week Mon-Wed 4, Thursday 3, Friday none
  (weigh-in), two days after a fight 2.
- Two cards at the same moment merge (sloppy reps + corner call = one corner card).

## Order (first to last)
Niggle > Sparring Flash > Breakthrough > Corner call > Sloppy reps > Floor event.

## Reference
RimWorld's letters: red ones pause the game, the rest stack quietly; quiet stretches after a big event.
