## DECISIONS
Hook pelvis° and thorax° (guard → contact), lead and rear, head and body: NO STUDY — no peer-reviewed paper publishes those yaw numbers for hooks.
Your 71–139° “chest only” wring vs rest pose is non-human. Thoracic axial ROM is only ~30–47° per side (DOCUMENTED). That alone kills upper-only mirror with fixed feet.
Closest boxing torso numbers are for the straight rear punch: pelvis/hip-line turn ~69–78°, shoulder–hip twist only ~17° in experienced boxers (DOCUMENTED) — whole trunk turns; it does not leave the pelvis and wring the spine.
Rear hand needs more trunk turn than lead (straights): trunk rotational ROM ~73° rear vs ~43° lead (DOCUMENTED). Do not expect a mirrored lead hook upper to equal a real rear hook.
Timing (DOCUMENTED, Stanley 2018): jab ~405 ms, cross ~495 ms, rear hook ~586 ms, lead hook ~657 ms (onset → contact).
Fist position at contact (cm in front / height / across centre line): NO STUDY for hooks. Coaching: mid-range ≈ length of upper arm, forearm near horizontal — SECONDARY.
Rear-foot pivot ° and pelvis° on rear hook vs cross: NO STUDY.
Orthodox rear hook = southpaw lead hook mirrored? NO STUDY. Ship an authored/captured rear hook (or upper layer over real rear-side turn), not a mirrored lead.

A human hook turns feet + pelvis + thorax together (plus shoulder). Fixing the feet and rotating only the chest forces illegal spine twist — matches Izumi: novices twist, experts rotate the trunk as a unit.

Replacement-clip criteria (practical)

Accept a rear-hook candidate if roughly:

Stance feet stay same lead (no mirror swap).
Pelvis and thorax both yaw the same way; shoulder–hip separation stays modest (straight-punch proxy ~≤ ~25–30° peak twist unless a future hook study says otherwise).
Duration to contact ~0.55–0.70 s for a max hook (Stanley band).
Fist path is lateral arc, elbow ~bent; height/range match head or body target without inventing cm.

Reject any solution that needs > ~50° of thorax-vs-pelvis twist with planted feet.
