# TASK K47 — KALLI — REAR HOOKS AND SOUTHPAWS IN SHIPPED FIGHTING GAMES: MIRRORED, LAYERED, OR AUTHORED?

Owner: Kalli (research, no shell). Stage: S4a (transitions) - forecast research. Deliver as ONE markdown answer.

## Why this exists

Our boxer's rear hook is his lead hook MIRRORED left-for-right. A mirror also swaps the FEET, so every rear hook
starts in the opposite stance and the feet jump inside a 0.27 s blend - the single worst foot-slide in our bout. We
are about to choose between (a) mirroring only the upper body over unmirrored legs, (b) authoring or buying a real
rear hook, and (c) handling a stance switch as its own move. We want to know what shipped 3D fighting and boxing games
actually do.

## Questions

1. **Mirroring**: do shipped 3D boxing/MMA games (EA SPORTS UFC 3/4/5, Fight Night Round 4 / Champion, Undisputed,
   Thrill of the Fight, Creed: Rise to Glory) and 3D fighters (Tekken 7/8, Street Fighter 6, Virtua Fighter 5) MIRROR
   animations for the other stance / other side, or capture both? Where a game mirrors, does it mirror the whole body
   or only part of it?
2. **Orthodox v southpaw**: how is a southpaw fighter animated - a mirrored set, a separate capture session, or a
   mix? Any published counts (e.g. "we captured N moves per stance")?
3. **Stance switching**: is a switch of stance (feet swapping) its own animation or transition? Any timing numbers?
4. **Upper/lower body layering**: do these games layer punches (upper body) over footwork (lower body)? Bone split
   point (spine, pelvis)? How do they stop the hips twisting against the steps?
5. **Foot planting during punches**: any GDC / developer talk describing foot locking / foot IK during strikes in a
   fighting game (EA UFC "Real Player Motion", Fight Night's physics-driven system, Tekken's authored frames)?

## Sources wanted

GDC talks and vault slides, developer interviews and blogs, official patch notes, published papers by the studios
(EA Vancouver, Bandai Namco, Capcom, Survios). **Label each claim DOCUMENTED (developer's own talk/doc), SECONDARY
(press, interview paraphrase, community datamine), or INFERENCE.** Give the link and date. Where nothing is published,
write **NOT FOUND** plainly - do not fill gaps with a guess.
