# Fight flows - flowcharts of positions, moves, plans and key ideas (proposal S339, for approval)

Operator S339: "can we Create flowcharts to map techniques, transitions, strategies, and key ideas like this:
grappleflows.com/v/octopus-guard-20-..." (a GrappleFlows study flow: ~21 numbered position/technique cards with short key
points, ~16 branches written as conditions - "if the opponent flattens -> ...", video moments linked).

## What we already have (the parts of a flow)
- Positions and transitions: StrikeMap - 8 ring cards, 30 moves (boxing); GrappleMap ground positions and the tie-ups (MMA).
- Branches by the opponent's reaction: the tie-ups ("if she leans in -> Snapdown"), PlanBoard reactions.
- Strategy: the coach's plan board (S338) and, since S339, the plan in the fight (PlanFight: she throws the arrow, the
  corner changes one a round, the round counts the arrows that worked).
- The clips to show each move: MoveClips (Punch PRO), GrappleMap clips.

## What a "flow" view would add (our own design, not a copy of GrappleFlows)
1. The plan drawn as a flowchart on the gym whiteboard (look B): cards top to bottom in the order she reaches them, each
   with 1-3 KEY IDEAS the coach writes ("chin down, turn her off the ropes"), arrows labelled with the move and WHEN
   ("if she presses: step back"). Click a card or arrow: the clip plays on our two women.
2. Branches by the opponent's game: the same card can have different arrows for "she presses / she boxes at range /
   she counters" - the fight already knows which game the opponent is playing each 30 s.
3. The scout's flow of the opponent: her favourite games and habits as a flow ("when hurt she digs to the body"), so the
   player builds the answer flow next to it.
4. After the fight: the flow lit up with what happened - which arrows fired, which worked (the round tally we now keep).
5. Saved flows: a few named flows per fighter ("Lucy vs pressers"), picked in camp before a bout.

## Scope
Boxing first (the demo). Wrestling/BJJ flows join with the MMA update (GrappleMap + tie-ups are ready for it).
Build order: (1) the plan as a flow on the whiteboard with key ideas, (2) branches by the opponent's game, (3) the
after-fight lit flow, (4) the scout's opponent flow, (5) saved named flows.
