## The proposed change (one question for the operator)
The camp's phase (build -> peak -> taper, counted back from fight night) writes the coach's default week; each day is ONE
card that carries its intensity ("Hard intervals", "Light roadwork", "Rest"); the player changes only the days he wants.
That turns 14 picks a week into 0-3. The goal lines, planned vs actual, the twist card and the peak-camp bonus stay as they are.
