# John - research: what boxing / fighting CARD games do well that a boxing management sim could learn (S536)

You are John, the research seat of a small studio making FightCamp, a boxing gym management game (Windows, 2D menus + a 3D
fight that SHOWS the result a numbers engine decides). The player is the gym's head coach: signs boxers, plans their training
weeks, books bouts, writes a fight plan (what to throw, guard, when-lines), shouts one call every 30 seconds during a round,
and changes the plan in the corner between rounds. Our weakness, measured: several choices barely move the result (pace,
the corner pick, the sparring partner), and a choice is only real when its value depends on the moment / the opponent.

The owner asked (S536): "do you think we should explore ideas from boxing card games?" and named:
- 1-2 Punch (The Game Crafter): https://www.thegamecrafter.com/games/1-2-punch
- Topps Chrome Boxing (trading cards): https://www.topps.com/pages/topps-chrome-boxing
- Starr Cards, make your own boxing card: https://starrcards.com/make-your-own-boxing-card/
- Uncaged Cards: https://uncaged-cards.com/
- Mixed Martial Cards (Steam): https://store.steampowered.com/app/4265810/Mixed_Martial_Cards/
Add any other well-reviewed fighting card / deck-building / simultaneous-choice games you find (e.g. Yomi, BattleCON,
Exceed, Punch Club's fight screen) if they teach something these do not.

## Answer, in your own words (never copy their rules text, card text or art)
1. For each game: what the player decides in a fight turn, how a clash resolves (simultaneous reveal? hidden hand? range?),
   what information each side has, and what makes READING the opponent pay. One short paragraph each; mark what you read
   on the page vs what you infer (SOURCED / INFERRED), with the URL.
2. The 5 mechanics most worth stealing for OUR screens, ranked: for each, which of our screens it would live on (fight plan,
   the 30-second call, the corner, the bout offer, the scouting / film file, the fighter card) and what choice it would make
   depend on the moment. Say what would NOT translate (a manager is not a player throwing each punch).
3. Collectible cards (Topps / Starr / Uncaged): how a fighter's card shows who he is at a glance - which 3-6 facts, how rarity
   or form is shown - as a reference for our fighter card and tale of the tape.
4. Pitfalls: where card fights become solved (one best card), too slow, or luck-driven, and how good designs avoid it.

Plain words, no jargon, tables welcome. Keep it under about 1,800 words.
