## Lessons that hold across every row
1. **Image first, check the still, THEN animate from it.** Text-to-video placed mount, closed guard
   and front headlock correctly but FAILED north-south and knee-on-belly (orientation, which part
   touches which). Both succeeded first time from a checked still.
2. **Content filter:** two 2x2 attempts on Seedance 2.5 (no gloves, studio floor) were blocked and
   refunded; the first segment-suit still ("skin-tight full-body suits") was blocked as nsfw. What
   passes: MMA gloves on, a cage or gym, "sports-science motion-capture session", "athletes".
3. **Multi-camera in one frame works** (2x2 grid held 8 s in sync; 1x2 opposite sides held 4 and
   8 s); overhead and end-on views are useless to the tracker (bodies stack: 9.8% / 3.6%).
4. **Hands smear and fuse** where the two men's gloves meet, every model, every clip. Never read
   hands from these clips.
5. **Segment colours survive:** 24 flat suit colours + a white grid held to the last frame of a
   4 s Seedance 2.0 clip (saturated-hue bins 13 -> 14 of 18, share drift 0.116).
6. **Real branding appears uninvited** (UFC / ESPN on canvas): reference only, never ship.
