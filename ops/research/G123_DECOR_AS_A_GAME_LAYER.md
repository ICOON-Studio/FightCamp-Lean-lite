# G123_DECOR_AS_A_GAME_LAYER (Grok, collected by Claude S90, 2026-09-26)

Headline: G122's rule stands - belts and named portraits EARNED, paper and neon BOUGHT. Shipped games treat decor as
a room-score shop, a scenery-visibility meter, or a zero-stat honour board. Players care when items are scarce and
named; they stop caring when the optimal layout is identical posters on every wall.

## 1. Placement
- Two Point Hospital: free placement inside a room blueprint; wall items and floor items share one room inventory;
  walls are not a separate grid. SOURCED (fandom)
- Two Point Campus: same room-edit model; duplicate items in one room lose value. SOURCED (fandom)
- Planet Coaster / PC2: free 3D scenery; score counts only scenery guests can SEE. SOURCED (Gamerant)
- Football Manager: no 3D walls; won silverware auto-lists on the honours / trophy-cabinet screen. SOURCED (FMInside)
- Punch Club 2: stations on a fixed gym floor; "renovate the gym" is a story beat, not a wall system. SOURCED
- The Sims 4: wall catalogue and floor catalogue are separate; wall hangings snap to walls. SOURCED (Gamerant)
- Boxing gym managers: floor equipment for training, no trophy-wall layer. PROXY

## 2. Effect model
- TPH: room prestige 1-5; published happiness P3 +10%, P4 +20%, P5 +30%; the Gold Star award is the prestige bomb
  players spam. SOURCED (GameFAQs)
- TPC: first 2-4 copies of an object give full prestige, further copies diminish. SOURCED (fandom)
- Planet Coaster 2: scenery rating = sum of visible scenery, the ride bonus capped. SOURCED (Gamerant)
- FM: the cabinet has no morale/stat effect - a history widget. SOURCED (SI community)
- Punch Club 2: stations change training gains; decor is not a prestige number. SOURCED
- Sims: decorations raise environment score and mood when viewed. SOURCED
- No shipped gym tycoon publishes a large % fight bonus from posters. PROXY

## 3. Earned displays
- TPH: awards unlock into the buy catalogue; the player places them; they do not auto-hang; players treat them as
  prestige tiles, not memory. SOURCED
- FM: win a competition -> the trophy appears in the cabinet and in presentation; community trophy packs exist because
  vanilla cups look generic - players want the NAMED object. SOURCED
- Punch Club 2 and boxing/MMA managers: belts are career flags or sheet lines, not a wall that fills. SOURCED / PROXY
- Player reaction: FM's cabinet with real trophy art loved as identity; TPH awards hated once they become wallpaper.
  SOURCED (Reddit)

## 4. Complaints
Decor spam and the optimal layout (TPH gold-star walls; players asked for diminishing returns and caps for years);
TPC poster spam to cap prestige; PC scenery stacking then nerfs; empty space punished so filling becomes work. FM has
no clutter problem because there is no placement loop - only a missing "this is our cup" feeling. SOURCED (Reddit)

## 5. JUDGEMENT for FightCamp
- **Placement:** wall SLOTS on the existing grid walls, not free 3D. Floor stays equipment (Sims split; TPH's
  anything-anywhere became spam). Slots make belts readable from the training floor.
- **Effect curve:** one gym attractiveness number with a HARD CAP. Bought paper/neon fill the first band only; past the
  cap extra items are cosmetic. No radius % to punch power, recovery or booking.
- **Earned items:** auto-occupy RESERVED slots on the win - belt rack, named portrait rail, plaque strip. The player may
  rearrange inside those slots, never bury a title behind a plant. Name + date on the object. Bought neon never uses
  those slots.
- **The one mistake to avoid:** the cheapest bought tile must never be the prestige-optimal tile (TPH's Gold Star). If
  paper beats a belt on the meter, the wall stops being a record of the gym.
