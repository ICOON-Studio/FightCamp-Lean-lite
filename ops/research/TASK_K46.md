# TASK K46 — KALLI — A BOXER'S FEET DURING A PUNCH: WHAT MOVES, WHAT STAYS, BY HOW MUCH

Owner: Kalli (research, no shell). Stage: S4a (the foot lock) - forecast research. Deliver as ONE markdown answer.

## Why this exists

We animate boxers in 3D and are building a FOOT LOCK: while a foot bears weight it is pinned to the canvas where it
landed, and the leg bends to keep it there. Before we pin anything we need the REAL behaviour of a boxer's feet
during punches, so the lock does not pin a foot that really moves, or let slide a foot that really stays.

## Questions (answer each with numbers where any exist)

1. **Rear foot on a cross / straight right**: does the rear heel lift and the foot pivot on the ball? Heel rotation
   angle (degrees) and heel lift height (cm)? Does the ball of the foot stay in place, or does the rear foot DRAG
   forward - and if so how far (cm)?
2. **Lead foot on a jab**: planted jab versus step-jab - how far does the lead foot step (cm) and how often do
   elite boxers step on the jab versus jab flat-footed?
3. **Hooks**: lead hook - the lead foot pivots on the ball (angle?); rear hook - the rear foot pivots (angle?).
   Does either foot translate?
4. **Body punches and uppercuts**: knee bend / level change (cm); do the feet move?
5. **Ground reaction forces / weight distribution** during a straight punch (percent of body weight on the front and
   rear foot, and how it shifts), from force-plate studies.
6. **Between punches**: typical stance width and length for a welterweight (cm), and how much the feet move in
   neutral bouncing (the "bounce" amplitude in cm and its frequency in Hz).

## Sources wanted

Peer-reviewed biomechanics first (e.g. force-plate or motion-capture studies of boxing punches - Lenetsky et al.,
Filimonov et al. 1985, Cheraghi et al., Dunn et al., Stanley et al.), then coaching texts with measured numbers, then
video analysis. **Label every number: DOCUMENTED (peer-reviewed / measured), SECONDARY (coaching text, secondary
report), or INFERENCE.** Give the citation (authors, year, journal or book, page/table where possible). If a
question has NO measured number anywhere, write **NO STUDY** for it plainly - do not fill the gap with a guess.
