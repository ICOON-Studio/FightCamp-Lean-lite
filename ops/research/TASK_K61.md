## Why this exists

The operator, verbatim: "A sloppy jab can be thrown just to measure distant the animations need to portray fakes and
when a fighter commits to a jab opening". Our fight engine already throws three different jab-like actions - a jab, a
"finding the range" jab and a feint - but the 3D room shows all three as the same full jab. We are going to animate each
one from what real boxers do on video, and we measure boxers with a pose tracker. The tracker needs BOTH men's whole
bodies, side-on, from a steady camera.

## What to find (links you could open, with timestamps)

1. **Range-finding jabs in real bouts or sparring**: the lead hand pawing, touching or tapping the other man's gloves or
   guard, a half-extended "measuring" jab, a jab thrown to set distance before a combination. Say at which times it
   happens.
2. **Feints in real bouts or sparring**: shoulder feints, hand feints, a jab started and stopped, a step-in feint, a
   head/level feint - with the times, and what the other man does in reply (flinches, raises his guard, steps back,
   counters, nothing).
3. **Committed jabs that leave the thrower open**: a jab thrown with the weight going forward, a step-in power jab, a
   lunging jab, where the thrower's rear hand drops or he gets countered. Times, and whether a counter lands.
4. **Coaching videos that NAME and demonstrate the difference** ("range finder jab", "pawing jab", "measuring jab",
   "jab feint", "how to feint in boxing", "committed jab vs flicker jab"), filmed with the whole body - preferably with a
   partner, not only on a bag.
