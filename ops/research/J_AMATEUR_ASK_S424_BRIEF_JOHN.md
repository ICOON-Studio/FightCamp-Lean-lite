Every row checked 2026-11-10. Labels: OFFICIAL (rule book or governing body) / MEASURED / DERIVED (my arithmetic) / ESTIMATE / OPINION (coach, vendor or journalist) / no source found. (?) means I only saw a snippet. "Rule Book" means the [England Boxing 2026 Rule Book](https://www.englandboxing.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2026-Rule-Book-Final-01-26.pdf).

**Headline:** in England, bouts are still made coach to coach. The host club's matchmaker builds the card, by phone, WhatsApp and Facebook. The region only issues the permit and appoints officials. England Boxing promised a matchmaking system on The Locker (its online membership system) for June 2025, but in Sept 2025 the CEO called it "work in progress", and no 2026 report says it has gone live. Private apps are filling the gap. The hard rules cover age and weight gaps, skills bouts, 12 h between bouts, a season cap and medical suspensions. Matching on experience is left to judgment. Nobody publishes how often a request fails or how long a ready boxer waits.

**Step by step (DERIVED from the rows above):**
1. Register the boxer on The Locker (Tier 2/3), with the medical and any past combat experience declared.
2. The boxer does at least 30 days of supervised sparring.
3. The coach judges the boxer ready.
4. The coach offers the boxer (weight, date of birth, bouts, club) to a show's matchmaker or another club's coach by phone, WhatsApp, Facebook or an app.
5. Both coaches agree the pairing.
6. The pairing goes on the bout sheet, which reaches the Supervisor 3 days to 24 h ahead.
7. On the day: weigh-in, then the medical, then the Supervisor confirms the bout.

There's no central queue; a request is one coach contacting another coach or a matchmaker.

**Who decides (DERIVED):**
- The two coaches agree the pairing.
- The matchmaker proposes the card.
- The Supervisor has the final word on the day.
- The doctor can remove a boxer at the medical or by suspension.
- The Supervisor and Referee together end skills bouts for a boxer.

Age and weight are hard limits; the experience gap is a judgment call.
