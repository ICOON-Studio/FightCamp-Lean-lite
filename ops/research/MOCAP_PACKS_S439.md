# Licensed boxing motion-capture packs we could buy (S439)

Researched on the web and re-checked on each seller's own page by a second reader. Nothing was bought, downloaded or signed. The purchase is the owner's call.

**Recommendation:** Checked against the sellers' own pages on 2026-11-12. The order of the original recommendation stands: buy (1) Punch Perfect: Fighting MoCap Animations, then (2) the animo-mocap Boxing Animation Pack.

1. Punch Perfect (Unity, EUR 36.76 + VAT). The 123-clip breakdown with 80 punch+step clips comes from the developer's own forum post, so it is the developer's claim, not an independent one. Unity's support article confirms the files are 'not restricted to Unity projects' and may be used 'with other engines provided you follow the EULA guidelines'. Two things change. First, the request for more shoulder turn and snap came from a buyer in the earlier thread ('MoCap Boxing Animations Pack', Feb 2025), not the discount thread. Second, the capture was made by the developer and a friend with no named hardware, possibly at home. Watch the Sketchfab preview (sketchfab.com/s/p9TJV) before paying. The USD 59.95 tools version adds a punch-to-target IK script; we do not need it, because we finish the arm with our own IK.

2. animo-mocap Boxing Animation Pack (Fab, EUR 58.57 before tax). All 67 clip names were confirmed: 4 stepping punches plus a step back, body versions of all 6 punches, 8 parries, guards and head-movement idles. The Fab EULA summary confirms 'usage is not limited to Unreal Engine' and allows commercial distribution inside a project. The page shows no licence tier today ('Select a License', licence terms '-') and no legacy-Marketplace note. Confirm at checkout that the tier is a Fab Standard (Personal) licence. Its sister pack, RenderHub's Animo Boxing Additional (USD 69.99), is confirmed under the Extended Use Licence: commercial use and computer games are named, and it covers one company on unlimited computers. It holds 12 knockdowns as 36 clips plus 34 hit reactions. Add it later if reactions and knockdowns become the gap.

Total for 1+2: about EUR 106 with VAT.

Do not buy:
- Wolff's 'Boxing Fight Style'. The page confirms it is flagged 'Generated with AI: Yes' and has 0 ratings.
- The Payhip 'Boxing - MoCap Pack' by Unreal Dystopia (USD 15). Its page links the 'Punch Pro – All Animations' video and the 'Punch Pro Animation List' PDF, uses the UE4 template skeleton and states no licence. That makes it almost certainly an unofficial resale of the MoCap Online pack we already own.
- Rapa Motion. The page has no licence; also, EXP01/EXP02 are update batches, not punch variants.
- Kubold (2016, generic; check for a legacy licence).

No strong option was missed. The other boxing packs found (AAAnimators Boxing Basic/Advanced, Unleashed Boxer AnimSet, Diamondhenge Boxing Fighter Animation Set) show no clip lists, capture method or confirmed licence on their pages.

As before:
- A pack will not by itself fix joins, reach and contact. Pick each clip by distance and finish the arm with IK, then judge every bought clip on our MetaHuman with the contact ruler.
- Keep every bought file out of the public FightCamp-lite mirror and out of every AI tool (the Fab AI flag 'No', Unity EULA 2.2.1.1(g), the MoCap Online AI clause).

Nothing was bought, downloaded, signed up for or accepted, and no project file was changed.
