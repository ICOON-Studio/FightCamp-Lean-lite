WHAT A HUMAN WOULD DO

Coach decides readiness (typically 6–12 months training, not the athlete). Club matchmaker/coach phones other clubs with age, weight, bout count (0), licence/medical status. First bout is a club show, never a championship. Opponent is another 0-bout or near-novice. Show is permitted 8–12 weeks out; the pairing firms over weeks and can still collapse on weight/withdrawal. Weigh-in and pre-bout medical are fight day. Same-day booking of a debut is not the club process.⁠Honourandglory

5. Amateur MMA and amateur Muay Thai — where they differ
Who matches: promoter + sanctioning body (ISCF/UMMAF/state commission / national MMA or MT federation), not a national “Locker” club-entry system. Coach still gates readiness.
Classes: C/beginner (0–3 fights, extra pads) → B (≈4–10) → A. Matching is class + weight, not 0 v 20 inside the same amateur cage.
Lead time: card submitted days to weeks before; event itself planned months out. Not same-day debut booking.
Weigh-in: more often morning-of for small amateur; IFMA champs use day-before official + morning-of.
Rest: IFMA KO/RSCH 30 days first occurrence — same order as amateur boxing. Unified amateur MT (ABC 2024) uses C/B class gear to stop mismatches, not a bout-gap statute.

VERDICT — game booking model (do not same-day the debut)

Lead time distribution (implement this, not instant):

Club debut: sample 14–28 days from “offer accepted” to bell (matchmaking already running inside an 8–12 week show).
Replacement/short-notice after a pull-out: 3–10 days (exists in combat sports; counted amateur frequency NOT FOUND — keep rare).
Regional champ: 4–8 weeks from entry close to first regional bout.
National: 6–8 weeks entry close → first national stage.

Matching limits (refuse the offer if any trip):

Debut (0 bouts): opponent 0–2 club bouts only. Skills/exhibition if 0 v 0 and age/weight tight.
Novice band: 0–10 vs 0–10 (USAB). Do not offer Open/Elite (≥11 or championship-elite) to a debutant.
Weight: ≤ smaller class span (≈2–3 kg / 5–10 lb by class). Skills/minors ≤2 kg.
Age: seniors unlimited within senior; youth/junior use 12/24-month caps.
Other-combat veterans: treat as experienced (EB conversion / USAB Open).

Bouts per year one: cap 6–10 club bouts (under EB’s 18-season rec; no sourced “typical 3”). Space ≥14 days by default; ≥30 days after KO/RSC-H; 90/180 if LOC (USAB).

Rest after loss: decision loss = no auto rest beyond coach/medical. Head stoppage = 30d minimum.

Info exchanged to make the match: age, weight class, bout count + other-combat record, medical/licence valid, show date/venue, round length. Club — not the athlete — accepts.

That kills same-day debut landing and most mismatch offer lists.
