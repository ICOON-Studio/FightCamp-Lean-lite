# C210 — Where real gym-drill motion can come from

Codex · 2026-09-26 · source research only; nothing downloaded or purchased.

## Finding

**No training take is owned.** The Q179 census counted 351 logical clips across `assets/anim/` and `assets/models/fighters/anim/`, with zero classified as training. Current disk contains 368 `.glb`/`.fbx` animation files (90 GLB, 278 FBX; no `.gltf`) in those roots. The counts are not one-to-one because some source motions have converted copies. C209's rendered test set was visually rejected as reusable gym loops: the heavy-bag take crossed through the bag/turned away, shadowboxing wandered, and skipping was closest but had a right-toe slip of 0.185 m/s. None is admitted as a station clip. The inherited census and C209 visual findings are in [Q179_CENSUS_OUTPUT.txt](Q179_CENSUS_OUTPUT.txt) and [C209_DIGEST.md](C209_DIGEST.md).

The 351-file inventory covers the already-owned RamsterZ, Motifect, CMU and Cascadeur sources named in the task. Its combat clips are strikes, fight movement, holds and reactions; its idle/rest clips are not drills. Nothing in those source families is a bag, pad, rope, weight, or stationary-cardio take. No candidate is promoted from a filename alone.

## Rig and evidence gate

C200 requires the existing f01 reference, with exactly 22 named bones, metres, +Y up, +Z forward, +X left, identity local rest bases, and body-specific proportions; see [C200_RIG_SPEC.md](C200_RIG_SPEC.md). Mixamo and UE Manny are foreign humanoid skeletons, while CMU ASF/AMC needs a conversion/mapping path. Do not rename bones and call that admission. The f01 track/rest, scale, hands and feet must be checked, then render each accepted cycle with the actual prop. For bag/pads/weights, a correct-looking punch or lift with no prop contact is still not a drill.
