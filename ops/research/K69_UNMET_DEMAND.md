# K69 — The "+ more" half: unmet demand in fight-management games

**Kalli, 2026-09-22, collected as inline chat text (NOT an attachment — see
K68's header for why that matters).** Commissioned when the operator sent five
Steam wishlist competitors and said *"i want these surveyd and make sure we have

**Punctuation normalised on collection; numbers, labels and sources untouched.**

Lead (three lines).

Most-requested missing feature: a living rival-promotion world — AI orgs that book, poach, and fight you for the same broadcast deals and talent (the thing four of five titles on the operator’s wishlist are selling).
Top retention killer: fight outcomes the player cannot trust (styles/AI that feel pointless, results that feel random) — that ends saves; UI gripes and missing belts only end sessions.
The thing nobody has built: gym-intimate fighter development + promoter-scale competitive economy + a fight engine people believe, in one product. WMMA5 owns promoter depth. FightCamp’s shipped surface owns gym + calibrated sim. The Coming Soon window is racing to own the economy layer. Nobody ships all three.
Method note

Every claim is DOCUMENTED (source) or INFERENCE. A forum thread proves a request exists, not how common it is — said explicitly. Sample sizes and dates where found. Prefer five solid claims over forty assertions.

1. Ranked unmet demand (what players ask for and do not get)

Ranked by how often the ask recurs across titles, not by how loud one thread is.

Rank 1 — Living rival promotions / talent wars / broadcast competition

DOCUMENTED (existence across multiple products’ marketing + community asks).

MMA Sim King (Coming Soon, Steam app 4534230): store copy leads with “compete against rival promotions,” “other promotions will be running events, signing talent and negotiating for the same broadcasting deals,” and “risk a rival company poaching them.” Tags include Simulation, Immersive Sim, Sports, Martial Arts, 2D.
PROMOTER: The Fight Game (Coming Soon / EA-tagged, app 4448300): tags include Sports, Simulation, Boxing, Economy, Management. Store sells rival promoters, broadcast/sponsor relationships, debt, PPV.
THE FIGHT BUSINESS: BOXING MANAGER (Coming Soon, app 5249680): sells building a promotion from small halls to stadiums, broadcasting/streaming/PPV, rival promoters; Trainer + Promoter dual mode is the explicit pitch.
Pound for Pound (EA, ~96% positive / ~138 reviews this pass): Steam feedback and roadmap talk call out international expansion, rival promotions, richer world simulation as still-growing systems; Rivals-style updates add competing orgs and gym relationships (DOCUMENTED as stated gaps/roadmap, not as review-frequency census).
WMMA5 community: r/WMMA5 wishlist threads for a hypothetical WMMA6 repeatedly ask for more promotions, child-promotion card control, and a world that keeps moving (DOCUMENTED existence — e.g. “What’s the 1 biggest feature or change for wmma6?” and related feature threads).

Vs FightCamp shipped surface: roster, booking, contracts, short-notice, tournaments, media/news, day feed — yes. Explicit AI rival promotions fighting you for broadcast deals and poaching talent — not listed among the shipped systems in the task brief. That is the gap the operator’s own wishlist is pointing at.

Rank 2 — Fighter-facing interaction (talk, praise/criticise, agents, career politics)

DOCUMENTED (existence; WMMA5 hardcore).

Quote shape from r/WMMA5 WMMA6 ask threads (paraphrase kept tight to source sense):

“The biggest feature I want is more interaction with fighters. Maybe even agents.”
Want to discuss opponents, suggest weight-class moves, praise or criticise boring performances.
Separate threads: “anyone else wish there is more to press conferences in WMMA5?”

MMA Sim King markets this as product: “Hold press conferences and field questions… some reporters… aim to instigate… classic press conference brawl.” (DOCUMENTED store copy.)

Vs FightCamp: ambitions, career goals, retention, fighter requests, rivalries — present. Interactive press conferences / agent negotiation theater / praise-criticise loops that change behaviour — not listed. FightCamp has the data of career politics; the ask is for agency inside it.

Rank 3 — Media spectacle that is playable, not wallpaper

DOCUMENTED (existence).

Recurring WMMA5 asks: weigh-in shows, deeper press conferences, social media, unique post-fight interviews, “revamped media” so the world feels lived-in (r/WMMA5 feature threads). MMA Sim King sell-sheet puts pre-fight hype and pressers in the bullet list. Vs FightCamp: media/news system + day feed — present. Interactive presser / weigh-in / interview gameplay — not listed.

Rank 4 — Economy as strategy (TV deals, PPV, sponsors, debt), not just club bills

DOCUMENTED (existence via wishlist composition + store tags).

THE FIGHT BUSINESS and MMA Sim King lead with broadcasting, venues, PPV, rival deal competition. Boxing Club Manager (the only shipped title on the list) is a gym game — and its Mixed score is the warning about shipping gym depth without enough world.

Vs FightCamp: gym/facility economy is implied by the gym fantasy; broadcast-deal / PPV / rival-promoter commercial war is not in the shipped-systems list.

Rank 5 — Believable fight outcomes and styles that matter

DOCUMENTED (existence; retention-adjacent).

Rank 6 — Titles, rankings, belts that feel consequential

DOCUMENTED (existence).

Rank 7 — QoL: UI, fonts, processing speed, less menu grinding

DOCUMENTED (existence).

2. Retention killers vs grumbles

These are different. Grumbles leave a 1-star review and keep playing. Killers trigger refunds or abandoned saves.

Killer (stop playing)	Grumble (keep playing, complain)	Label
Fight results you cannot believe — styles don’t matter, outcomes feel random, “why did that fighter win?”	Wish the 3D looked better; want more belt designs	DOCUMENTED existence for outcomes (BCM review themes; early BCM “styles pointless”); INFERENCE that presentation is grumble-tier matches K68
Price/content mismatch at purchase — pay €35 for a shallow build, refund or never return	“I’ll wait for a sale”	DOCUMENTED: BCM Steam Mixed; Niklas Notes themes include Overpriced for Current Content (~6%); Steam discussions thread “Price” (Dec 2022); store price ~€34.99 / US ~$39.99
Bugs that block a session (soft-locks, can’t assign training, white screen)	Occasional crash you can relaunch past	DOCUMENTED: BCM Bugs and Technical Issues top complaint theme (~7% in Niklas Notes breakdown); Believr display/white-screen threads
Feeling the game is abandoned — “Is this the final state of the game?” with no roadmap	Missing one feature on a live roadmap	DOCUMENTED: BCM thread title “Is this the final state of the game?” (Feb 2023); sparse post-2023 update signal in forum index
Progression you cannot see — train for months, numbers don’t move, loop feels fake	Progression is slow but visible	DOCUMENTED: BCM Amateur Training thread (players report months with no amateur attribute gains; “not seeing numbers go up… makes the game not fun”)
Menu grinding with no autopilot across a full roster every week	Dense UI that experts learn	DOCUMENTED existence in sibling boxing-manager forums (WCBM2 “Bugs and suggestions/frustrations”: menu navigation “keeping me from playing this endlessly”); INFERENCE it applies to any gym-manager with no batch training

INFERENCE (labelled): For FightCamp, the dangerous retention killers are (a) any moment the calibrated engine feels unfair or opaque, (b) shipping a €premium ask before the management loop is deep enough, (c) weekly chores without batch tools once the roster grows. 3D pain is real (operator verdict) but K68 + this pass both put it below “trusted outcomes” and “enough game for the price” as a quit reason in this niche.

The failure mode to avoid (INFERENCE, grounded in the above):
Ship a gym + 3D fight view at a premium price before (1) the fight model earns trust, (2) progression is legible week-to-week, (3) the world beyond your gym is deep enough to justify the price, and (4) UI stops fighting the player. Boxing Club Manager is the cautionary tale sitting on the same wishlist as four promoter-economy Coming Soon games: the one that shipped looks like FightCamp’s shape (gym + 3D) and it is the one with Mixed reviews.

5. The thing nobody has built

Claim (INFERENCE, with DOCUMENTED legs): No shipped title gives players all three of:

Gym-intimate development — this fighter, this camp, this cut, this injury clearance (FightCamp’s shipped centre of gravity; Boxing Club Manager’s failed attempt),
Promoter-scale living market — rival orgs, broadcast deals, poaching, multi-promotion world (WMMA5’s centre; MMA Sim King / PROMOTER / THE FIGHT BUSINESS store promises),
A fight engine the player trusts statistically — outcomes that match published sport shapes and visible styles (FightCamp’s calibration thesis; the thing BCM reviewers said was broken; the thing mobile animated managers also fail).

Legs:

WMMA5 has (2) deeply and (3) well enough for a cult audience, but not (1) as a gym-owner fantasy and not 3D — and its own fans still beg for richer fighter/agent/press interaction on top (DOCUMENTED asks).
FightCamp’s shipped list has (1) and a serious attempt at (3), plus media-as-feed; it does not list (2) as rival-promotion/broadcast-war systems (DOCUMENTED from task brief vs store copy of wishlist titles).
Boxing Club Manager attempted (1)+(3D presentation) without enough (2) or trusted (3) at a high price → Mixed (DOCUMENTED).
THE FIGHT BUSINESS markets Trainer+Promoter dual mode — i.e. the gap is obvious enough to sell — but it is not shipped (DOCUMENTED store; absence of reviews).
Pound for Pound is building (2) in EA with love for booking fantasy; it is not claiming FightCamp-grade statistical calibration (DOCUMENTED store/roadmap tone).

Narrower “nobody has it” paragraph (the valuable one):

Players keep asking to talk to fighters and the press in ways that change the save — criticise a boring champion, lean on an agent, survive a hostile presser, watch that ripple into negotiations and matchmaking. WMMA5’s hardcore asks for it every wishlist thread. MMA Sim King puts it on the Coming Soon page. No shipped fight-management game makes that loop deep. FightCamp already has ambitions, requests, rivalries, and a news feed — the missing product is turning those into playable conversations with consequences, sitting on top of the calibrated engine you already built.

If forced to pick one white-space bet aligned to the operator’s actual Steam wishlist (not the gym fantasy alone), it is: rival promotions + broadcast/economy competition. If forced to pick the white-space bet aligned to the deepest existing audience (WMMA5), it is: interactive fighter/press politics. The dual-role Trainer+Promoter game with a trusted engine is the synthesis — marketed, not delivered.
