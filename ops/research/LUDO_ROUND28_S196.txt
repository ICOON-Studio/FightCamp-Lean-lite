Competition Ring (7.8 × 7.8 m): Incorrect. 7.8 m is too large for a 20 ft canvas training footprint without outer walkways, but too tight for an AIBA competition platform with judging lanes. SECTION C - WEIGHT-MANAGEMENT PROTOCOL
1. Physiological Foundations & Core Constraints

Sources: British Journal of Sports Medicine (BJSM) Combat Sports Weight Cut Consensuses; UFC Performance Institute Nutrition Guidelines (2021).

Safe Chronic Fat Loss: Capped at $1.0%$ of Total Body Weight (TBW) per week (via a $500\text{ to }750\text{ kcal/day}$ deficit). Exceeding this strips Lean Muscle Mass (LMM) and drops power output.
Acute Water Cut (Hypohydration):
Day-Before Weigh-In (30–36 hr rehydration window): Max safe limit is $8.0%$ of TBW through water manipulation (water loading, low sodium, sweat suits, sauna).
Same-Day Weigh-In (2–4 hr rehydration window): Max safe limit is $3.0%$ of TBW. Dehydration above $3%$ directly reduces cerebral spinal fluid (CSF) padding around the brain, spiking knockout risk.
Rehydration Rate: Human intestines can absorb a maximum of $1.0\text{ to }1.2\text{ liters of fluid per hour}$. Attempting to regain $>8\text{ kg}$ in 24 hours causes gastric distress and hyponatremia.

4. Mathematical Formulas for In-Engine Evaluation
A. Final Weigh-In Weight Calculation

$$W_{\text{scale}} = W_{\text{base}} - \Delta W_{\text{fat}} - \Delta W_{\text{glycogen}} - (TBW \times %{\text{water_cut}} \times K{\text{sweat}})$$
Where $K_{\text{sweat}}$ is a fighter trait coefficient ($0.88\text{ to }1.08$, influenced by genetics, sauna level, and thermal gear).

B. Weigh-In Failure States
If $W_{\text{scale}} > W_{\text{limit}}$ by $< 1.0\text{ kg}$: 2-Hour Scale Grace Window. Athlete can run/sweat off the excess at the cost of $-25%$ base Stamina on fight night.
If $W_{\text{scale}} > W_{\text{limit}}$ by $\ge 1.0\text{ kg}$: Purse Forfeit Fine (20–30%) paid to opponent. Fight switches to Catchweight status; cannot contest or defend title belts.
If $W_{\text{scale}} > W_{\text{limit}}$ by $\ge 2.5\text{ kg}$: Bout Cancellation by Athletic Commission on health and safety grounds. Fighter loses purse, camp pays cancellation penalty, gym loses 10 Prestige.
C. In-Fight Penalties from Severe Weight Cuts

When acute water loss exceeds safe physiological limits:
$$\text{Water_Deficit} = \max\left(0.0,, %{\text{water_cut}} - %{\text{safe_cap}}\right)$$
$$\text{Chin_Penalty} = 1.0 - (4.5 \times \text{Water_Deficit}) \implies \text{Chance of KO knockdown increases}$$
$$\text{Stamina_Max_Penalty} = 1.0 - (3.0 \times \text{Water_Deficit})$$
$$\text{Punch_Power_Penalty} = 1.0 - (1.5 \times \text{Water_Deficit})$$

Example: A fighter making a $9.5%$ water cut on a $7.0%$ safe limit runs a $2.5%$ ($0.025$) deficit:

Chin durability drops by: $1.0 - (4.5 \times 0.025) = 0.8875$ ($-11.25%$ reduction in concussion resistance).
Max stamina cap drops by: $1.0 - (3.0 \times 0.025) = 0.925$ ($-7.5%$ to maximum aerobic gas tank).

2. Conflict Rules & Recovery Constraints
Fatigue Compounding ($FTG$): Tracks from $0$ to $100$. If a fighter enters a block with $FTG > 60$, any newly gained fatigue is scaled by $1.4\times$. If $FTG > 80$, the athlete enters an Overtrained State (skill gains drop to $0$, injury chance spikes by $4.0\times$).
Impact Consolidation Rule: High-impact blocks (Hard Sparring, S&C Maximum) cannot be scheduled back-to-back on the same calendar day. Doing so triggers an instant $+10%$ soft-tissue injury check.
Minimum Recovery Rule: An athlete requires at least 3 complete Rest/Recovery blocks per week to keep their endocrine profile and morale from decaying.
3. Simulation Handover & Auto-Pause Triggers

When the player hits "Run Week", the game runs through the 2-minute real-time gym floor loop:

Station Contention Check: Verifies whether the fighter’s assigned station is free or occupied by a civilian member (based on Member Access Policies).
Auto-Pause Event Triggers:
Medical Emergency: Fighter suffers an injury in sparring (Cut, Concussion, Ankle Sprain).
Fatigue Spike: Fighter's $FTG$ hits $85$ due to bad weight-cut recovery, warning the player to pull them from the session.
Sparring Knockdown: Either the fighter or their sparring partner gets dropped.
