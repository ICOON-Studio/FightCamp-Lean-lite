# Kalli on the CORE3 core loop (S354, 2026-11-02, via gbot)

Brief: E:/FightCamp/tmp/s354_seats/KALLI_CORE3.md (the three mechanics + numbers). Used on the Miro loop board frame "Core loop - Early Access" and in WeekPlan.gd.

**1. Fun or chore**
- **Fun:** the goal lines moving the moment a session changes. That's instant cause and effect. "Test it" showing where the plan breaks is the best toy here. Building a combo and seeing its odds against the next opponent is a real puzzle.
- **Chore:** choosing 7 mornings and 7 evenings by hand every week, for dozens of weeks. Rest days and neck work will be clicks with no decision in them.

**2. Biggest risks**
1. **A solved week.** Once a player finds the best pattern (say Hard, Normal, Rest, repeated), mornings become copy-paste.
2. **Test it kills the surprise.** If 20 simulated bouts predict the result well, fight night is a formality. If they predict badly, players stop trusting it.
3. **Too many dials and too little feedback.** 9 sessions × 3 intensities, plus drills, combos and plans, means the player can't tell which choice mattered.

**3. Changes, ranked**
1. **Week templates plus one twist a week.** Save a week as a template so CONTINUE repeats it. Each week, one card breaks the routine: a sparring partner is ill, it's a heatwave, or a sore wrist rules out the heavy bag. The only choice left is the interesting one.
2. **Make Test it fuzzy and limited.** Show a range ("11-15 of 20"), not an exact count. Allow 3 tests per camp, and make better film study narrow the range. The fight still holds surprises, and scouting gains value.
3. **A red day as a gamble.** A red day gets a 25% chance of a Breakthrough (a mastery jump of +5) as well as the injury risk. Hard becomes a bet, not just a trap.
4. **Habits fight the plan.** An unfixed habit overrides a WHEN branch 30% of the time, and Test it names it: "Lucy drops the right after the jab, so the 1-2-6 never starts". Evenings now have a target: drill the habit out.
5. **A peak-week payoff.** A camp that hits all three goal lines gives +1 to every stat for that fight only, and a gold line in the week review. The long camp gets a moment.

Arguing against item 2: a fuzzy range may feel like hiding numbers from a sim player. It's still right, because a sure thing kills the watch.

**4. Cut**
Picking the evening sparring partner each night. Make sparring a single weekly toggle (on or off, plus a partner level). It saves 7 clicks a week and loses no real decision.

**5. How others solve it**
- **Football Manager:** training runs from weekly schedules the player sets once and tweaks. The fun is in the match prep and the tactics, judged by the result.
- **Punch Club:** each session is a trade between stats, energy, hunger and money, so every click costs something. The loop is a resource squeeze, not a schedule.

**NOT COMPUTED:** the 25% breakthrough chance, the 30% habit override and the 3-test limit all need playtests.
