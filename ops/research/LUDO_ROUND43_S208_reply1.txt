1.3 Reference Game Dissection & Applied Lessons
Football Manager 2024 / 2026 (Sports Interactive): The Player Profile does not hide training or medical behind empty sub-pages. It uses a 3-column informational triage: Identity & Form (Left), Radial Attributes & Roles (Centre), Contract & Next Match Fixture (Right). Lesson: Zero dead margins; nested tabular widgets give context without opening modal dialogues.
Motorsport Manager (Playsport Games): The Driver Screen pairs the 3D helmet/driver bust with real-time trait telemetry (Marketability, Morale, Fatigue, Form trend). Lesson: 3D stages must never stand alone—they must be flanked by biomechanical readouts and financial telemetry.
Out of the Park Baseball (OOTP Developments): The master player card pairs scouting grades (20–80 scale) with acute workload splits and contractual service time. Lesson: Attribute numbers must be visually anchored by progression bars and percentile indicators.
EA Sports UFC 5 Fighter Hub: Uses live MetaHuman models showing procedural lacerations and localized swelling. Lesson: Medical screens must show the actual physical toll on the 3D model, not just a static 2D paper medical chart.
