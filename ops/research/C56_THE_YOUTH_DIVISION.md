# C56 — The youth division: age as a competition axis

Research date: 2026-09-08. This is a research record, not a schema or design
proposal. The six disciplines are the event disciplines already represented by
the project: mixed martial arts, boxing, judo, wrestling, taekwondo, and
karate. A number below is a governing-body eligibility number, not a suggested
game value.

## Executive finding

There is no portable “youth” rule. The bodies disagree on both the bands and
the clock used to assign them. IBA, IJF/EJU, UWW and WT publish calendar/year
rules; WKF assigns the category from age on the first day of the event; IMMAF
uses birth-year youth divisions from 2026 while retaining a first-day rule in
its junior/senior eligibility text. No single birthday transition is evidence
for all six disciplines.

The safest evidence statement about a youth medal is also non-uniform: judo
explicitly exposes separate cadet, junior and senior ranking lists, while the
other five bodies' documents reviewed here do not publish an automatic junior-
to-senior ranking or selection conversion. Those five results are therefore
**UNKNOWN**, not “nothing.”

## 1. Published age bands

| Discipline and governing body | Published competition bands | Senior upper bound / veterans or masters |
|---|---|---|
| **MMA — IMMAF** | The 2025 rules label Youth D **10–11**, C **12–13**, B **14–15**, A **16–17**; Junior **18–20**; Senior **18+**. From 2026, IMMAF changes the youth assignment to birth-year bands: D **2014–2015**, C **2012–2013**, B **2010–2011**, A **2008–2009**, with a Youth A cap at 17. These are two dated rule statements, not one flattened age table. ([IMMAF 2026 year-born notice](https://immaf.org/2025/11/18/immaf-adopts-year-born-system-for-youth-divisions-from-2026/), [IMMAF Youth Fouls 2025](https://immaf.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/IMMAF-YOUTH-FOULS.pdf), [IMMAF athlete eligibility](https://immaf.org/athletes/athlete-eligibility/)) | Senior has no upper bound in the eligibility text. IMMAF publishes Masters at **35+** as a real championship division, first added to the 2025 European Championships. ([IMMAF athlete eligibility](https://immaf.org/athletes/athlete-eligibility/), [IMMAF 2025 European Championships](https://immaf.org/2024/12/19/2025-immaf-european-championships/)) |
| **Boxing — IBA** | School-age **13–14**, Junior **15–16**, Youth **17–18**, Elite **19–40**; IBA also publishes U23 **19–22** in its 2026 event handbook. The classification rule uses year of birth. ([IBA Technical and Competition Rules](https://www.iba.sport/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/IBA-Technical-and-Competition-Rules_20.09.21_Updated_.pdf), [IBA U23/Youth 2026 handbook](https://www.iba.sport/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/20260210_Team_Delegations_Handbook_IBA_Asian_U23__Youth_Open_Boxing.pdf)) | IBA Elite has an upper bound of **40** in the cited technical rule. An IBA Masters/veterans competition band is **UNKNOWN** in the IBA competition documents reviewed. ([IBA boxing rules index](https://www.iba.sport/about-iba/boxing-rules/)) |
| **Judo — IJF** | Cadet: **15–17**; Junior: **15–20**; Senior: **15+**. All are calendar-year bands. The IJF SOR is the governing document; the 2025 EJU handbook reproduces the IJF age groups and states the **15** lower limit for official IJF competitions. ([EJU General Handbook 2025](https://uploads.eju.net/2020/09/EJU_Handbook_General_2025.pdf), [IJF SOR document index](https://www.ijf.org/index.php/ijf/documents/5)) | Senior has no upper bound in the IJF age-group rule. ([IJF veterans explainer](https://www.ijf.org/news/show/a-little-history-and-some-facts)) |
| **Wrestling — UWW** | U15 **14–15**, U17 **16–17**, U20 **18–20**, U23 **19–23**, Senior **20+**. UWW's age sheet separately records a minimum tolerance from **13** in the year for U15. Veterans are **35–60** in the cited international age sheet. ([UWW Age Categories 2023](https://cdn.uww.org/2023-01/age_categories_2023.pdf), [UWW harmonized age names](https://cdn.uww.org/2021-11/22_new_name_age_categories.pdf)) | Senior has no upper bound in the cited UWW sheet. Veterans are a genuine international competition category, not just a social label; UWW publishes veteran championships and veteran rules. ([UWW Grappling Rules 2025](https://cdn.uww.org/2025-05/grappling_rules_2025.pdf), [UWW 2025 Veterans World Championships guide](https://cms.uww.org/article/u15-u17-u20-veteran-grappling-world-championships-2025-guide?page=4)) |
| **Taekwondo — WT** | Senior: at least **17** in the tournament year; Junior Championships **15–17**; Cadet Championships **12–14**. The rule explicitly says the age limit is based on the year, not the date. ([WT Competition Rules and Interpretation, 30 September 2024](https://www.worldtaekwondo.org/att_file/documents/WT%20Competition%20Rules%20and%20Interpretation%20%28September%2030%2C%202024%29.pdf)) | The cited WT competition rule gives no senior upper bound. A WT Masters/veterans competitive category is **UNKNOWN** in that rule. This is not evidence that no national or other taekwondo body has one. |
| **Karate — WKF** | Under 14 **12–13**; Cadet **14–15**; Junior **16–17**; U21 **18–20**. Senior Kumite is **18+**; Senior Kata is **16+**, so the same governing body has different senior floors by format. ([WKF Kata Competition Rules 2024, Appendix 2](https://kai.is/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/WKF_Kata_Competition_Rules_2024-2.pdf), [WKF eligibility text reproduced from the competition rules](https://karate.ua/media/83a34988-f7c1-4919-8503-568ec39e8af6.pdf)) | No senior upper bound is stated in the cited WKF eligibility text. A WKF Masters/veterans pathway is **UNKNOWN** in the World Cadet/Junior/U21/Senior rules reviewed. |

The age edges above are deliberately not flattened. For example, “senior” is
**15+** in IJF judo, **17+** in WT taekwondo, **18+** for WKF Kumite,
**16+** for WKF Kata, and **19–40** for the cited IBA Elite boxing rule.

## 2. The floor, including contact and non-contact formats

The following is the youngest sourced international competition floor, not the
youngest age at which a child may train or enter an unsanctioned local event.

| Discipline | Youngest sourced floor | Contact / non-contact distinction |
|---|---|---|

A universal legal floor is **UNKNOWN** because IMMAF also defers to the host country's legal age. ([IMMAF athlete eligibility](https://immaf.org/athletes/athlete-eligibility/)) |
| Boxing | **13** for IBA school-age boxing in the cited technical classification. ([IBA Technical and Competition Rules](https://www.iba.sport/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/IBA-Technical-and-Competition-Rules_20.09.21_Updated_.pdf)) | IBA boxing is a contact format. A separate IBA non-contact competition floor is **UNKNOWN**. |
| Judo | **15** for official IJF events, calendar year. ([EJU General Handbook 2025](https://uploads.eju.net/2020/09/EJU_Handbook_General_2025.pdf), [IJF SOR document index](https://www.ijf.org/index.php/ijf/documents/5)) | IJF's cited age-group rule does not publish a separate non-contact/kata floor. **UNKNOWN**. |
| Wrestling | U15's official band starts at **14**, with UWW's minimum tolerance from **13** in the year. ([UWW Age Categories 2023](https://cdn.uww.org/2023-01/age_categories_2023.pdf)) | UWW wrestling is a contact format. A separate non-contact competition floor is **UNKNOWN**. |
| Taekwondo | **12** for WT Cadet Championships in the tournament year. ([WT Competition Rules and Interpretation, Article 4](https://www.worldtaekwondo.org/att_file/documents/WT%20Competition%20Rules%20and%20Interpretation%20%28September%2030%2C%202024%29.pdf)) | WT's cited rule is for combat competition; a separate non-contact floor is **UNKNOWN**. |
| Karate | **12** for WKF Under 14 categories. For senior competition, the cited rules distinguish contact Kumite at **18+** from non-contact Kata at **16+**. ([WKF Kata Competition Rules 2024](https://kai.is/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/WKF_Kata_Competition_Rules_2024-2.pdf), [WKF eligibility text](https://karate.ua/media/83a34988-f7c1-4919-8503-568ec39e8af6.pdf)) | Karate is the clear sourced example where format changes the senior floor: Kata **16+**, Kumite **18+**. |

These floors also show why the grade minimums in C52 cannot be reused as
competition-age rules. A grade age and an event age answer different questions;
where the two disagree, the disagreement is a finding, not an error to smooth
over. C52 remains the source for grade ground and is not re-derived here.

## 3. The one-way door: birthday, calendar year, or registration date?

| Discipline | Published trigger | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| MMA | IMMAF changed Youth D/C/B/A to **year-born** eligibility from 2026; the older policy and current junior/senior eligibility text use age on the first day of competition and require the athlete to remain eligible through the event. ([IMMAF 2026 year-born notice](https://immaf.org/2025/11/18/immaf-adopts-year-born-system-for-youth-divisions-from-2026/), [IMMAF age-banding PDF](https://immaf.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/IMMAF-Age-Banding-Criteria.pdf), [IMMAF athlete eligibility](https://immaf.org/athletes/athlete-eligibility/)) | This is a mixed rule, not a single universal IMMAF clock: youth is calendar/birth-year from 2026; Junior/Senior is first-day eligibility in the cited text. Registration date is not the published trigger. |
| Boxing | IBA says age is determined by **year of birth**. ([IBA Technical and Competition Rules](https://www.iba.sport/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/IBA-Technical-and-Competition-Rules_20.09.21_Updated_.pdf)) | Calendar-year assignment; not birthday or registration date. |
| Judo | IJF/EJU age groups are **calendar year** bands. ([EJU General Handbook 2025](https://uploads.eju.net/2020/09/EJU_Handbook_General_2025.pdf)) | Calendar-year transition; not birthday or registration date. |
| Wrestling | UWW publishes birth-date windows for the competition year and labels the categories by official age. ([UWW Age Categories 2023](https://cdn.uww.org/2023-01/age_categories_2023.pdf)) | The published international sheet is a year/birth-year rule with a stated in-year tolerance, not registration date. |
| Taekwondo | WT says explicitly: age is based on the **year**, not the date. ([WT Competition Rules and Interpretation](https://www.worldtaekwondo.org/att_file/documents/WT%20Competition%20Rules%20and%20Interpretation%20%28September%2030%2C%202024%29.pdf)) | Calendar-year transition. |
| Karate | WKF says allocation is determined by age on the **first day of the event**. ([WKF eligibility text](https://karate.ua/media/83a34988-f7c1-4919-8503-568ec39e8af6.pdf)) | Birthday/age-on-event-day transition; not calendar year and not registration date. |

No reviewed body uses registration date as the age-band trigger. Choosing one
global game trigger would therefore be an **authored invention**, not a
governing-body fact.

## 4. Masters, veterans, and whether senior competition ends

- **MMA:** Senior is **18+** with no upper bound in IMMAF's eligibility text;
  Masters is a real IMMAF championship division at **35+**, not a retirement
  label. ([IMMAF athlete eligibility](https://immaf.org/athletes/athlete-eligibility/), [IMMAF 2025 European Championships](https://immaf.org/2024/12/19/2025-immaf-european-championships/))
- **Boxing:** The cited IBA Elite band ends at **40**. IBA Masters/veterans
  rules are **UNKNOWN** from the governing competition documents reviewed;
  national masters boxing cannot be silently promoted to an IBA rule.
  ([IBA Technical and Competition Rules](https://www.iba.sport/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/IBA-Technical-and-Competition-Rules_20.09.21_Updated_.pdf))
- **Judo:** IJF Senior has a lower limit only, **15**, and no upper bound.
  IJF Veterans are a real, recurring world and continental competition from
  **30+**, although IJF's own explainer describes veterans judo as primarily
  leisure and community-oriented. Both facts matter: it is competitive, but it
  is not simply senior World Tour continuation. ([EJU General Handbook 2025](https://uploads.eju.net/2020/09/EJU_Handbook_General_2025.pdf), [IJF veterans explainer](https://www.ijf.org/news/show/a-little-history-and-some-facts))
- **Wrestling:** UWW Senior is **20+** with no upper bound in the cited sheet;
  Veterans **35–60** are published and contested internationally. ([UWW Age Categories 2023](https://cdn.uww.org/2023-01/age_categories_2023.pdf), [UWW Grappling Rules 2025](https://cdn.uww.org/2025-05/grappling_rules_2025.pdf))
- **Taekwondo:** WT Senior starts at **17** in the tournament year and the
  cited WT rule gives no upper bound. A WT Masters/veterans pathway is
  **UNKNOWN** in that rule. ([WT Competition Rules and Interpretation](https://www.worldtaekwondo.org/att_file/documents/WT%20Competition%20Rules%20and%20Interpretation%20%28September%2030%2C%202024%29.pdf))
- **Karate:** WKF Kumite Senior starts at **18** and Kata Senior at **16**;
  neither cited rule supplies an upper bound. A WKF Masters/veterans pathway
  is **UNKNOWN** in the reviewed World Championship rules. ([WKF eligibility text](https://karate.ua/media/83a34988-f7c1-4919-8503-568ec39e8af6.pdf))

The answer to R51's aging question is therefore discipline-specific: an upper
bound exists in the cited IBA Elite rule, while the other cited senior systems
either have no upper bound or leave the relevant masters rule UNKNOWN.

## 5. What a youth result is

### Judo: separate ranking systems, no automatic medal conversion found

IJF publishes separate Cadet, Junior and Senior World Ranking Lists. The IJF
also publishes event-specific senior eligibility that can use the Junior WRL
(for example, its 2024 Senior World Championship notice allowed athletes in
the top **16** of the junior WRL to participate), but that is a selection gate,
not a rule that transfers junior points into the senior WRL. ([IJF ranking downloads](https://www--gcp.ijf.org/wrl_downloads), [IJF 2024 Senior Worlds deadlines](https://www.ijf.org/index.php/news/show/deadlines-for-the-abu-dhabi-world-championships-seniors-2024))

Finding: a junior medal is not evidenced as an automatic senior ranking point;
its effect on a particular senior selection is event-specific and may be a
separate invitation/eligibility pathway. Do not collapse “can be selected” into
“senior ranking transfer.”

### The other five disciplines: UNKNOWN rather than “nothing”

The reviewed IMMAF, IBA, UWW, WT and WKF documents publish age eligibility,
formats, weight divisions, quotas or championship structures, but do not state
an automatic rule that a youth/junior medal becomes a senior ranking point,
senior selection, or senior quota. The result for each is therefore:

| Discipline | Junior/youth medal effect on senior ranking, selection, or quota |
|---|---|
| MMA | **UNKNOWN**. The IMMAF eligibility and pathway pages do not publish an automatic senior conversion. |
| Boxing | **UNKNOWN**. The IBA classification and event documents do not publish an automatic senior conversion. |
| Wrestling | **UNKNOWN**. UWW age-category documents establish separate categories but not a medal-transfer rule. |
| Taekwondo | **UNKNOWN**. WT Article 4 establishes age eligibility, not senior ranking or selection credit for junior results. |
| Karate | **UNKNOWN**. WKF eligibility/quota text establishes separate age events and one-athlete-per-category limits, not a senior conversion. |

## 6. Gap against the current model

This is a seam inventory, not a fix proposal.

1. **Result record and display.** `Godot/scripts/EventResult.gd:16` gives
   `age_division` an empty-string default; `:37` requires the key for validity;
   `:92–103` only prints it when a producer supplies it. The bounded grep found
   no production writer, so the field is structurally ready but currently
   decorative in live production. The same result record already carries
   `sex`, `weight_class`, and `grade_division`; real competition divisions are
   therefore a cross-product seam, not a replacement for weight class.
2. **Amateur/pro one-way door.** `Godot/scripts/GameState.gd:5496–5502`
   shows `_turn_pro` only stamps `turned_pro`, its week, and its cause. It does
   not read age division. The amateur circuit's age eligibility and the
   professional transition therefore meet at a seam with no age evidence in
   this inspected function. Whether another amateur pathway currently filters
   age is **UNKNOWN** under this bounded audit.
3. **Medical holds.** A hold can block availability independently of age. The
   inspected evidence does not show a published age-band interaction for a
   medical hold; whether any hold path is age-aware is **UNKNOWN**. An age
   division cannot be assumed to override a hold or make a held athlete
   selectable.
4. **Grand Prix announcement.** `Godot/assets/data/tournaments.json` contains
   the Grand Prix announcement surface named by the task, while the inspected
   `EventResult` path supplies no age division. Whether the announced bracket
   is age-open, senior-only, or age-segmented is **UNKNOWN** from the bounded
   evidence; the announcement must not be treated as a sourced youth rule.
5. **Aging.** The fighter already owns an age and ages yearly, but competition
   age is not the same clock in all six bodies. Any single birthday/calendar
   policy for every discipline would be an **authored invention** and would
   erase the WKF first-day rule and the current IMMAF youth rule change.
