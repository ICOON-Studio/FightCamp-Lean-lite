Failure 1: The Lighting Model & Absence of Grounding Shadows (WORST)
The Flaw: The hall is lit by a high-intensity, omnidirectional ambient light source with weak directional falloff. Equipment casts faint, undefined contact shadows, causing treadmills, mats, and characters to look like they are floating on top of the floor rather than resting on it. The scene lacks ambient occlusion in floor-wall junctures and beneath machinery.
The Fix:
Action Type: Engine / Lighting Environment Config (WorldEnvironment & DirectionalLight3D).
Direct Sun/Sky: Disable generic environment sky energy. Add a key DirectionalLight3D tilted at Pitch -55.0°, Yaw 35.0° (angled from the high-street clerestory side). Color #FFF7ED (Warm Daylight, 5400K), Energy 1.85. Shadows enabled: shadow_bias = 0.02, shadow_normal_bias = 1.5, shadow_blur = 1.2, 4-split Directional Shadow Cascades (PSSM) with maximum distance 45.0m.
Interior Fixture Lighting: Add 8 down-facing spot grids representing recessed high-bay warehouse LED troffers at y = 3.55m along the unseen truss coordinates. Attenuation 1.8, Spot Angle 65°, Color #F8FAFC (Clean 6000K neutral light), Energy 0.9.
SSAO / SSIL (Screen-Space Ambient Occlusion & Indirect Lighting): Enable Godot SSAO: Radius 1.4m, Intensity 2.8, Power 1.5, Horizon 0.12. Enable SSIL to allow light bouncing off the tan subfloors onto adjacent equipment bases.

B) CAMERA FRAMING SPECIFICATION: OPTION (A) VS OPTION (B) FOR THE 38% UI DRAWER

When displaying a persistent or contextual side drawer that takes up 38% of horizontal screen width (730px out of 1920px), centering the 3D scene creates severe composition problems: the right-hand slice of the gym is permanently occluded, burying Zone E, Zone F, and the administrative stations under opaque UI.

Option (a): Hall Centered Across the Full Screen (Full Width)
Camera Parameters:
Projection: Perspective, Vertical FOV: $44.0^\circ$
Camera World Position: $X = 15.00,\text{m}$, $Y = 22.40,\text{m}$, $Z = 35.80,\text{m}$
Camera Rotation (Euler): Pitch: $-36.5^\circ$, Yaw: $0.0^\circ$, Roll: $0.0^\circ$
Focal Look-At Target: Center of gym floor $(X = 15.00,\text{m}, Y = 0.00,\text{m}, Z = 9.00,\text{m})$
Frustum Margin: 1.8m clearance past perimeter walls on left and right borders.
Drawback: When the right drawer slides out, it covers the eastern 38% of the viewport. Players must manually pan left to inspect athletes on the agility turf or review tactical film at the coach's desk.
Option (b): Asymmetric Framing (Hall Offset into Left 62% Viewport)

To leave the right 38% (730px) clear for UI while framing the entire 30m × 18m facility within the remaining 62% (1190px), the camera must be translated rightwards and its focal target shifted, or Godot's frustum offset feature must be used. In standard perspective, the physical camera is translated and pitched to map the gym's bounding box precisely into the normalized screen coordinates $U \in [0.00, 0.62]$.

Camera Parameters:
Projection: Perspective, Vertical FOV: $42.0^\circ$
Camera World Position: $X = 22.25,\text{m}$, $Y = 24.80,\text{m}$, $Z = 38.60,\text{m}$

Alternative Native Godot 4 Frustum Offset Method (Cleaner):
Keep Camera Position: $X = 15.00,\text{m}$, $Y = 23.50,\text{m}$, $Z = 37.20,\text{m}$
Pitch: $-36.5^\circ$, Yaw: $0.0^\circ$, Roll: $0.0^\circ$

Recommendation: Option (b) is strongly recommended.
Core Justification: In a high-information PC management simulation (like Motorsport Manager or Two Point Campus), the player spends 70% of their operational time interacting with athlete dossiers, training cards, and schedule inspectors. Shifting the camera framing keeps the simulation world fully readable while the player adjusts training protocols, with zero equipment hidden beneath the UI.
