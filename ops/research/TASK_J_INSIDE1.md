# TASK J-INSIDE1 - JOHN - inside range in amateur boxing: how often, how long, what they do there

Discipline: boxing (demo). Research only (no code).

## Why
Our 3D fight now breathes between long range and the pocket, and clinches 2-5 times a round, but the boxers stand
inside range (pelvis to pelvis under ~0.65 m, chest close, hooks and uppercuts) only 4 % of a round. Our own footage
study of three women's world finals measured 17-31 % inside. Before we change the fight we need the real shape of it.

## Questions (give numbers with their source; say when a number is for men or pros, not women amateurs)
1. Share of round time at inside / mid / long range in amateur (ideally women's, elite or national) boxing.
2. How long an inside spell lasts (seconds) and how it starts (one boxer steps in behind a jab, a pressure fighter
   walks through, after a clinch break, on the ropes / in a corner) and how it ends (step out, clinch, referee).
3. What is thrown inside: share of hooks, uppercuts, body shots vs straight punches; punches per inside spell.
4. Defence inside: head position (forehead to shoulder/chest), forearms and elbows, rolling under, framing; how much
   of the inside time is spent holding vs working.
5. Does it change by style (pressure fighter vs out-boxer) and by round (more inside as they tire?).
6. Any time-motion or tracking study (BoxMind, CompuBox-style counts, Davis et al., sports-science papers) that gives
   distances in metres.

## What would a human do?
A boxing analyst would chart a few bouts by range band (long / mid / inside) with a stopwatch and read the coaching
literature on inside fighting (e.g. how a pressure fighter works inside, how an out-boxer escapes).
