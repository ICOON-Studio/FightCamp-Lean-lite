# J_FIGHTWEEK_S449: real numbers for the amateur fight week and camp locations

Checked 13 Nov 2026. Game money is $ = £ × 1.25. Labels: OFFICIAL is a rule book or official body, MEASURED is read from a cited page, DERIVED shows the maths, ESTIMATE is my judgement, and NOT FOUND means I searched and found no source.

The biggest change is from the rule book. The weigh-in is on bout day, 1 hour before boxing starts, not the day before. Bouts are matched on actual weight plus an allowed gap, not on class limits, and the rule book has no fee for missing weight.

## 1. Club-show matching at walking weight

**Matching uses actual weight plus a gap, not the class limit (OFFICIAL).** Seniors "must be matched at no greater weight difference than the smaller weight category of the 2 matched Boxers" (rule 2.7.8). The rule's example is 62.5–65 kg against 65–70 kg, which allows at most 2.5 kg. Youth uses the same rule (2.6.6). For juniors the rule is similar; its example allows at most 3 kg (2.5.5). Schools, minors and skills bouts allow at most 2 kg (2.3.4, 2.4.4, and Annex C2 3.8). Source: [England Boxing Technical & Competition Rules 2026](https://www.englandboxing.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2026-Rule-Book-Final-01-26.pdf).

- For the game's classes (DERIVED), the gap is 2.5 kg at 57.5 and 60 kg (men) and at 57 and 60 kg (women).
- It is 5 kg for men's 65–70 and 70–75 kg, and 2.5–5 kg for women's 60–65 kg.
- Class limits only matter at championships.

**Experience.** The 2026 Club Boxing Matrix says boxers are "matched on weight and experience" (OFFICIAL). The rule book gives no bout-count gap for club shows (NOT FOUND); the only numeric bands are for championships, such as at most 7 bouts for Junior Development Class A/B. The age gaps are under 12 months for senior against youth, and under 24 months for youth against youth (OFFICIAL).

**The weigh-in is on bout day (OFFICIAL).** "For domestic boxing, the weigh in should be completed 1 hour prior to boxing commencing" (rule 7.1.3); championships with a draw recommend 3 hours. It uses digital scales on a hard floor, the boxer weighs in shorts and vest, and the medical follows ([England Boxing Competitions Overview](https://www.englandboxing.org/competitions/competitions-overview/)).

- Minors through youth may gain or lose only 300 g under 50 kg, 500 g at 50–63 kg, and 700 g over 63 kg (rule 7.1.14). The rule doesn't say what this is measured against; probably the weight given when the bout was made (ESTIMATE).
- At senior championships the most a boxer may lose is 1 kg (rules 13.6–13.7).
- For senior club shows, no tolerance is printed (NOT FOUND).

**Missing weight carries no fine or fee in the rule book (NOT FOUND).** The only expenses rule is about optional refreshments (rule 1.4).

**Weight cutting:**
- England Boxing advises at most about 1 kg a week, with 0.5 kg "safer" (OFFICIAL, [EB weight management guidance, Dec 2024](https://www.englandboxing.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/EB-Weight-management-guidance-final-draft-1.pdf)).
- A study of 63 male club boxers from 10 England Boxing clubs found all had cut weight (MEASURED, [Brown et al., JSES 2021](https://doi.org/10.36905/jses.2021.05.03)). The usual cut was 2.9 ± 1.0 kg (4.2% of body weight), starting 8–10 days out, and novices cut 3.6 kg. Those figures are for championship weight, not club shows.
- England internationals lost 7.0% of body weight over 21 days, 5.2% of it in the last 7 days (MEASURED, [Smith, JSSM 2006](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3863921/)).
- A 5.16% cut in one week made performance and mood worse (MEASURED, [Hall & Lane, BJSM 2001](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1724425/)).
- At club shows, matching on walking weight means a boxer trims 0–1 kg (ESTIMATE). Real cuts are for championships and box cups.

**Late matches and pull-outs.** No rule sets a minimum notice (NOT FOUND).
- At Wellingborough ABC in September 2025, 18 bouts were matched five days out and only 9 were left on the day (MEASURED, [Northants Telegraph, 22 Sep 2025](https://www.northantstelegraph.co.uk/sport/wellingborough-abc-homeshow-shows-off-club-talent-5329674)).
- Bexhill ABC had 21 bouts on the Tuesday and hoped "15 or 16" would remain by Saturday (MEASURED, [Sussex Express, 4 Jun 2015](https://www.sussexexpress.co.uk/sport/boxing/club-set-to-host-first-open-show-2221853)).
- A boxer stepped in on the night as a late replacement (MEASURED, [Southport Visiter, 2015](https://www.southportvisiter.co.uk/sport/other-sport/veteran-lund-leaves-late-fights-9716119)).
- Clubs collect boxers' available dates months ahead (MEASURED, [H&G Boxing, Apr 2025](https://www.honourandglory.co.uk/blog/club-shows-2026)).
- A typical pattern is firm matches set 1–3 weeks out, 25–50% falling through in the final week, and same-week replacements being common (ESTIMATE). I found no source for 8–12 weeks of lead time (NOT FOUND).

## 3. Who pays

**It's a mix, mostly the boxer and the club.**
- Trips away are often crowdfunded. [Worthing ABC raised £950 for 3 women at a box cup](https://www.gofundme.com/f/support-worthing-abcs-women-at-the-womens-winter-box-cup-v58c2) (Nov 2026), and [3 juniors raised £600 for a weekend](https://www.gofundme.com/f/to-pay-for-midlands-box-cup) (Jun 2026).
- Club shows raise club funds (OFFICIAL, rule 1.6.2), and programme adverts sell for £25–100.

**Only funded GB athletes get a stipend.** The UK Sport A grade is up to £29,000 a year ([UK Sport, Mar 2025](https://www.uksport.gov.uk/news/2025/03/14/increased-athlete-performance-award-budget-confirmed)). I didn't find the lower GB Boxing bands (NOT FOUND), and club boxers get nothing.

**What a working amateur can put in:**
- Median full-time pay is £811.20 a week ([ONS ASHE, Oct 2026](https://www.ons.gov.uk/employmentandlabourmarket/peopleinwork/earningsandworkinghours/bulletins/annualsurveyofhoursandearnings/latest)), about £652 take-home (DERIVED).

- Spare cash is about £20–60 a week (ESTIMATE). At that rate a camp costing £600–1,000 takes 10–30 weeks of saving.

## 4. Fight week in amateur boxing

**Taper:**
- The evidence says cut training volume by 40–60% over about 2 weeks and keep the intensity (Bosquet et al. 2007 meta-analysis, cited in [Ruddock et al. 2016](https://shura.shu.ac.uk/11499/3/Ruddock%20strength%20conditioning%20professional%20boxing.pdf)).
- [Boxing Science](https://boxingscience.co.uk/programming-for-boxing-the-taper/) says amateurs taper differently because they box more often.
- No sparring in the final week, only pads, shadow and bag ([Buglioni, Boxing News, 2016](https://boxingnewsonline.net/features/frank-buglioni-how-to-handle-fight-week/); this is pro advice, but it matches common practice).
- No source fixes a number of rest days (NOT FOUND). A likely pattern is the last hard sparring 7–10 days out, light technical work until 2 days out, and 1 full rest day (ESTIMATE). Busy club boxers often just train through, lighter.

**Film study** is rare at club level.
- England Boxing accepts video as a coaching aid, with consent needed for under-18s ([EB video guidance, Jan 2026](https://www.englandboxing.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/PhotographyVideoGuidancesV2-2.pdf)).
- Coaches can see the opponent's bout record on The Locker, England Boxing's registration system.
- Club-level "film" usually means the opponent's club Facebook or Instagram clips, a show livestream, or a phone video (ESTIMATE).
- With 25–50% of bouts changing late, prep for a named opponent is often wasted.

## Numbers the game should change (most important first)

1. **Weigh-in:** on bout day, 1 hour before boxing starts, not the day before. Championships use 3 hours. OFFICIAL, EB Rules 2026, rule 7.1.3.
2. **Matching:** use walking weight plus a gap, not the class limit. The gap is 2.5 kg at 57–65 kg and 5 kg at 65–75 kg (men); schools and skills bouts allow 2 kg. OFFICIAL, rules 2.5.5, 2.6.6, 2.7.8 and C2 3.8.
3. **Missing weight:** drop the $300 fee, which isn't in the rule book (NOT FOUND). Make it "bout off, or rematched on the night if inside the gap". Youth tolerance is 300, 500 or 700 g by weight band (OFFICIAL, rule 7.1.14).
4. **Late withdrawals:** add them, with replacements offered 0–5 days out. Two shows had 18 bouts drop to 9 (Wellingborough 2025) and 21 expected to drop to 15–16 (Bexhill 2015), so the rate is about 25–50% (ESTIMATE).
5. **Offer lead time:** 8–12 weeks has no source. Firm matches come about 1–3 weeks out, from availability lists collected months ahead (ESTIMATE; H&G 2025 for the lists).
6. **City-club trip:** split it into two options instead of £320. Commuting is £80–155 ($100–195) and staying over is £535–1,220 ($670–1,525). DERIVED from hotel rates, session fees and fuel.
7. **Hill cottage:** £400–515 ($500–645) off-season and £770–830 ($960–1,040) peak. The current £700 is a near-peak price. MEASURED, Sykes, Nov 2026.
8. **Seaside house:** make it seasonal, £455–485 ($570–605) off-season and £840–1,000 ($1,050–1,250) peak, instead of a flat £800. MEASURED, Sykes, Nov 2026.
9. **Food for two for a week:** about £95 ($120), plus £30–50 of extras. DERIVED from ONS, with the × 1.5 an ESTIMATE.
10. **Weight cuts:** 0–1 kg for a club show and about 3 kg (4.2%) over 8–10 days for a championship. England Boxing advises 0.5–1 kg a week. Brown 2021; EB guidance 2024.
11. **TAPER:** 3 rest days is more rest than the evidence supports. Use 1 rest day, keep the session count, cut volume by 40–60%, and hold the last sparring 7 or more days out. Bosquet 2007; Buglioni 2016; the rest-day count is an ESTIMATE.
12. **Who pays:** club boxers get no stipend and have about £20–60 ($25–75) a week to spare. Trips are crowdfunded or club-paid. Only funded GB athletes get up to £29,000 a year (UK Sport 2025).
