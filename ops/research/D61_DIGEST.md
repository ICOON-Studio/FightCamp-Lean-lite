3. Single Highest‑Impact Change
Split the shared left‑wall label and mount each doorway’s name directly above its door frame.
Replace the single long "FRONT DESK · STREET OFFICE" sign with two separate, identically styled signs:
Lower door → FRONT DESK · STREET (matches floor mat)
Upper door → OFFICE (mounted above door, not just on floor)
Add small wall‑mounted plaques (Kenney kit has thin wall signs) at each doorway — 1 line, all caps, high contrast.
Why this wins: Every other station is self‑labeled; the hub floor fails because its primary entrances do not follow the same rule. Fixing this makes the floor instantly readable as a central corridor branching to distinct spaces.
