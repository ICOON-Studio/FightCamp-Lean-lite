# G130_WHAT_A_BETTER_GYM_LOOKS_LIKE

A contractor prices a gym from silhouette, not paint: mass, material, mount, hardware, seams. Tier-3 is thicker, heavier, quieter hardware, fewer seams, brand that looks like it was bought for daily traffic — not a recolor of tier-1.

## 1. Stations (budget / mid / pro)

- **Heavy bag.** T1: thin PVC/canvas, 40–70 lb, 4 cheap straps, saggy cylinder. T2: Powerhide/PU, 80–100 lb, foam liner, real chain+swivel. T3: full-grain cowhide, 100–125 lb, nylon-welted seams, bottom D-ring, hangs cylindrical. $35–80 / $150–250 / $300–400. SOURCED Titleboxing
- **Speed-bag platform.** T1: 0.75″ board, hook swivel, wall wobble. T2: 1–1.5″ hardwood, bearing/shackle swivel, 8–15″ height travel. T3: 11-ga steel V-frame, oak drum, commercial swivel, almost no bounce-back into the wall. $80–130 / $180–230 / $250–350. SOURCED Findingdulcinea
- **Double-end bag.** T1: PU, valve in strike zone, cheap bungee that stretches slack. T2: cowhide, riveted straps, industrial rubber cables. T3: goatskin/Cleto-class, welted seams, side valve, tight rebound. $25–40 / $50–70 / $100–150. SOURCED Askmeboxing
- **Lockers.** T1: thin powder-coat steel, punched louvers, padlock hasp, knock-down. T2: heavier steel, 3-tier banks, sloped tops. T3: HPL/phenolic doors, hidden vents, RFID, welded banks. $150–260 / $350–550 / $450–800+ per unit. SOURCED Latestcost
- **Dumbbell rack.** T1: A-frame 14-ga tube, vinyl/neoprene bells. T2: 2–3-tier 12-ga, rubber hex, chrome handles. T3: 11-ga 2×2/3×3, powdercoat, urethane bells, saddle or tray, gym logo. Rack $80–250 / $400–750 / $900–1,500; full 5–100 lb commercial run is thousands. SOURCED Workouthealthy
- **Plyo boxes.** T1: raw 3-in-1 plywood, hard edges. T2: branded foam/vinyl or resin, beveled. T3: nested steel frames, textured tops, one height each, coach-set look. $60–120 / $190–350 / $500–1,400 set. SOURCED Barbend
- **Bike.** T1: upright plastic shroud, light flywheel, toe cages. T2: spin geometry, 40 lb flywheel, belt, SPD+cages. T3: commercial frame (Keiser/Schwinn AC class), rear flywheel, no plastic dress, studio console or none. $200–400 / $600–1,000 / $2,000–4,000. PROXY
- **Rower.** T1: short magnetic rail, cheap LCD, 250–350 lb rating. T2: water tank, wood/steel, nicer seat. T3: Concept2-class air flywheel, PM5 brick monitor, nickel chain, 500 lb rating, splits for storage. $180–260 / $400–800 / ~$990. SOURCED Repreturn
- **Grappling dummy.** T1: unfilled canvas sack, no legs. T2: filled vinyl, 40–70 lb, crude limbs. T3: pre-filled 70–120 lb, articulated legs, Versys/GUS/Fairtex silhouette. $30–100 / $150–330 / $350–620. SOURCED Bestreviews
- **Physio table.** T1: folding beech/MDF, thin foam, cable braces. T2: hardwood portable, thick foam, face cradle. T3: stationary/electric-lift, wide top, paper-roll holder, clinic steel. $90–200 / $350–550 / $800–2,500. SOURCED Massageprices

## 2. Worn vs kept (glance reads)
- Duct-tape patches and open seams with fill poking.
- Cracked vinyl / delaminating leather, shiny dead strike zone.
- Pear-shaped sag (fill settled to the bottom).
- Orange rust on chains, swivels, D-rings, locker edges.
- Peeling powdercoat, missing locker doors, warped plyo corners, slack double-end cords. SOURCED Heavybag

Kept kit: even cylinder, tight hardware, matte conditioned leather, no tape.

## 3. Room upgrades (cheapest first)
- Signage — painted name vs vinyl cut vs backlit channel letters. Low $; reads "this is a business."
- Lighting — shop fluorescents vs high-bay LED, even, no flicker. Fit-out often $5–10k with HVAC/sound. PROXY
- Mirrors — retail glass vs floor-to-ceiling 1/4″ tempered, one full wall. ~$1.5–5k. SOURCED Startpermit
- Wall padding — bare cinder vs 2×6 vinyl-foam pads at throw height. ~$80–170/panel. SOURCED Centurymartialarts
- Flooring — biggest "pro" tell. Stall mats / EVA tiles (~$1.50–2.50/ft²) vs seamless rolled rubber or color-fleck commercial (~$3–8 installed). SOURCED Dgfloors

## 4. T1 → T3 prompt delta (one line each)
- Heavy bag: canvas teardrop → fat cowhide cylinder, welted seams, four-chain swivel.
- Speed platform: thin plywood disk → oak drum on 11-ga steel, shackle swivel.
- Double-end: shiny PU balloon + saggy cord → small goatskin, taut industrial cables.
- Lockers: dented grey school bank → phenolic sloped-top bank, RFID.
- DB rack: skinny A-frame + vinyl bells → 3-tier 11-ga tray, urethane hex.
- Plyo: raw plywood cube → nested steel boxes, rubber tops.
- Bike: plastic upright → bare commercial spin, rear flywheel, no shroud.
- Rower: short magnetic toy → long air erg, brick monitor, nickel chain.
- Dummy: stuffed canvas sack → articulated 90 lb vinyl human.
- Physio: folding picnic table → electric-lift clinic table, paper roll.

**JUDGEMENT:** Don't recolor T1. Change silhouette, mass, hardware, and seams. Room floor + mirrors beat a fancy logo on a sagging bag.
