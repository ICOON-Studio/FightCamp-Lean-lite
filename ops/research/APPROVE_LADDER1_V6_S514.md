# Season ladder v6 - for your yes (S514)

Both critics passed it: Dola 9 depth / 9 general, Goose 8 depth / 7 general (bar: 7+ from both).
Full map: ops/research/DESIGN_LADDER1_V6_DOLA.md. Critics: DESIGN_LADDER1_V6_CRITIC_DOLA_DOLA.md, DESIGN_LADDER1_V6_CRITIC_GOOSE.md.

What it does, in plain words:
- One ladder per region, sex and weight class, sorted by ladder points (0-100). Ranked from 4 wins, as today.
- You may ask for anyone up to 10 + (your points / 5) above you; asking far below is allowed but barely counts.
- A win over a better boxer pays more points, a loss to a worse one costs more (a chess-style table: level = +8 / -8).
- Before you ask, the card shows the whole price: points and place, members, club standing, the $450 ticket cut,
  morale, the taper week, the injury chance and, if stopped, the 8 weeks out and which show that would miss.
- Greyed rows say why (booked, resting, novice vs open, clubmate, met twice, too heavy, 18-bout season cap).
- "Will they say yes" is never random: Leans Yes / Leans No, with the biggest reason named.
- Mandatory challengers and eliminators only on the pro table, as agreed.

What is not settled yet (critics' lists): travel cost and home/away (need venue data), a few probe-checked numbers.
Nothing is built until you say yes.
