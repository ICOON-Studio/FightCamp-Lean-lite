# TASK D47 - DOLA - WOULD YOU TRUST THE PLAN SETUP AND THE CORNER READ? (S100)
Owner: dola. Assigned 2026-09-27 (S100). Player-view opinion, no code. Reply in chat; Claude collects it.

## What exists now (S99-S100)
Before a booked fight you set FIVE sliders against that opponent, each from one end to the other:
range (inside .. outside), pace (patient .. pressure), target (head .. body + legs),
grappling (keep it standing .. take it down; locked in boxing/kickboxing/muay thai), risk (defend first .. trade).
Your coach answers with three reads - OFFENCE FIT, DEFENCE FIT, ENGINE - each in a word
(EXCELLENT / GREAT / GOOD / OK / POOR / VERY POOR) and one line ("We're open. He's going to find us with the same
shot all night."). You never see the right answer; every slider moves two or more reads, so fixing one breaks another
(Motorsport Manager's car setup). A band on each read shows where the answer lies: full width at first, narrower after
a camp week sparring a partner of his style, narrow after film study.
In the fight, the plan changes how your man fights at the margin, and a plan that fits is a small real edge.
Measured: a well-read plan wins about 15-55 points more often than a badly read one, depending on the matchup.
Between rounds the corner has a 45-second minute: cutman, second, chief. If you earned the knowledge, the chief can
spend 20 seconds on a READ - "His tell: the hand drops after the jab - counter over it." or "His exit: he always
slides the same way off the ropes - be there first." - which helps for the next round only. On a bad night you cannot
fit "survive" and both reads into the minute.

## What would a human do?
A real head coach builds the plan in camp from tape and sparring partners, then in the corner gives ONE instruction
built on what he has seen. A fighter trusts it when it comes true in the fight. Answer as the player who has to trust
this coach.
