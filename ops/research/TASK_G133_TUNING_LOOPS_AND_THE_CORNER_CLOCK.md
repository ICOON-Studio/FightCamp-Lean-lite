## Why
FightCamp is a fight-gym MANAGEMENT sim (boxing, kickboxing, Muay Thai, MMA). The operator loves Motorsport Manager's car
setup: six sliders fold into three outputs (aero / speed balance / handling), the optimum is hidden, the driver reports his
opinion in five bands (Excellent / Great / Good / OK / Poor) and a direction ("understeer"), practice laps with a good-
feedback driver narrow a visible "optimum band", and the pit stop is a time-boxed job list where a FAST crew strategy
risks a mechanic mistake. We want the same pleasure for (1) tuning a fighter's camp and fight plan against a specific
opponent and (2) the 60-second corner between rounds, presented as problem cards the player can understand and address
against the clock. The operator's example dilemma: *do not blow the nose - the eye may swell - or risk blowing it for
some benefit?*

## What would a human do?
A real chief second triages in 60 s: what will end the fight first (blood in the eye, a closing eye, no air, a dead leg),
what one instruction changes the next round, and what is forbidden (blowing the nose with an orbital injury). A game
designer would study the management games people replay for their tuning loop and copy the loop, not the theme.
