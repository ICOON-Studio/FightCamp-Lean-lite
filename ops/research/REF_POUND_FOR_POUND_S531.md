# Reference: Pound for Pound: MMA Promotion Simulator (Steam 4289560), read S531 2026-11-20

Read from: the Steam store page, its last three news posts (Major Update 3 "Main Event", 19 Nov 2026; patch 0.8.4)
and the top-rated English reviews. Our words, our numbers; nothing of theirs copied.

## Facts
- Solo developer, Early Access since 20 Mar 2026, 19.50 EUR (25% off to 28 Nov), 6-12 months of EA planned.
- Reviews: Very Positive, 96% of 159 all time, 100% of 12 recent. A small but very happy audience.
- It is a PROMOTION sim (sign, book cards, TV, pay-per-view, markets), MMA, procedurally made world, 14 divisions.
- Money is simplified to "tokens" (one review praises the speed this gives).
- Update 3 (19 Nov) added a 2D top-down fight viewer to what was a text sim. It shows position in the cage, top
  control, fatigue rings, hurt states and the body part being protected, with radio-style play-by-play, a momentum
  bar and a crowd meter. Undercard can be simmed, the main event watched. "Prestige" was replaced by followers per market.

## What players praise
- The early game: a fighter from 0-0-0 to champion, rivalries, the first local card grows into PPV.
- Fast sim, clean UI.

## What players hate (the long helpful reviews, 75 h and 10 h)
1. Late game turns into a clicker: big rosters (150 fighters), TV deals demand a card every 4 weeks, short contracts
   renewed by hand, weak auto-booking. Fighters stop being people.
2. Progression is a forced checklist (tiers that need N divisions / champions / defences); no sandbox start.
3. Labels that ignore the record: a 23-0 world #2 still a "Prospect", a 35-0 two-division champion only "Superstar".
4. Fight ratings disconnected from what happened (a 21 s knockout of a top-5 rated B+; A grades almost never).
5. Rematches punished by a flat ~1 year timer, even when the rivalry system makes an instant rematch the story.
6. Scouting is a workaround (gym partnerships reveal stats, even of other promotions' fighters); potential unseen.
7. Weight misses far too common and repeated, fighters refuse to move up after winning there.
8. Impossible world history (a title held ~30 years by a 37-year-old).

## What it means for FightCamp (Claude's read)
- PROOF of the market: a solo, simple-looking combat-sport management sim at our price reaches 96% positive.
  Different seat from ours (promoter vs gym owner), so a neighbour, not a rival; worth a wishlist check of overlap.
- A SIMPLE VIEW WAS ENOUGH TO DELIGHT: they show the fight as a 2D top-down schematic with the state that matters
  (position, fatigue, hurt, momentum). Our 3D fight work has run many rounds; a schematic view of the settled round
  is a cheap fallback / companion worth keeping in mind (a scope call for the owner, not a decision here).
- Their late-game failures are our scope rules already: small roster (4-fighter test club, normal start 0), no
  checklist tiers, one gym. Keep it that way.
- Checks for our own systems, each a known trap they fell into:
  - Rank / status words must follow the record (our ladder points and HotStreak: a long unbeaten run must read so).
  - Our rematch rule (re-match only inside the class gap, DUCKED/OWED) must let a rivalry rematch happen when the
    story wants it - not a flat timer.
  - Knowledge (KNOW_STAKES map): knowing an opponent should come from film, sparring and fights, never a menu unlock.
  - Fight-week weight cut: misses must be rare and follow a cause the player can see and act on.
  - Any rating of a bout must read the fight (finish, stakes, ranks), never a formula that a fast knockout breaks.
