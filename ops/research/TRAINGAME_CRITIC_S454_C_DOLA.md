YOUR JOB (fresh critic): one fighter's Training screen, judged as a GAME screen, not as an information sheet
The owner's words on the last version: "it lacks art the info is too text heavy and it is not clear from the picture how you set the
training, what are your options and what each improves, fatigue and intensity meters maybe would be nice, how much do you rate it for a
game not as an info sheet?"
What the picture shows (the player's one boxer, 12 days before a club-show bout; this is the last week whose pad nights count for it):
Header (one strip): the fighter, the next bout and a countdown, a LAST PAD WEEK badge, a WIN dial (the coach's week against the
player's week), the fighter's goal bar, mood and the weekly card waiting.
THIS WEEK (the big centre board): seven days. Each day has a MORNING card (picture, what it grows as arrows, fatigue as flames, its own
Light / Normal / Hard lever), an EVENING card (pads with the coach - the punch drilled with a ring filling to 30, the level at which it
can be thrown in a fight - film of the opponent, sparring, or free) and a FATIGUE TANK that fills day by day, with a dashed line where
injury risk doubles. A green tag on each card = its points on the WIN dial. Changed cards say YOURS. A sparring card is being dragged
onto Saturday evening and warns before the drop.
SESSIONS strip (bottom): every session as a picture card to drag; mornings left of the divider, evenings right.
Right column: the clicked card (Wednesday evening's pads) with what it does on fight night and what it gives up; what the week builds
(stat bars with the week's gain hatched, the moves as rings with the word ladder); the weekly card with its two answers; the week load
dial and the diet switch with the weight against the limit.
A WEEK GOAL row (Peak for Sat 28 / Build / Recover) decides what the WIN dial counts; quick-set buttons for all mornings.
Pictures are comic-style illustrations; each session also has a round icon (on the day card and in the strip). Changed cards show the
coach's card struck out underneath; heavy bag and sparring show risk pips (hand / cut / head).
Undo / Coach's week / Last week / Clear above the board. CONTINUE (top right, the one pulsing button) runs the week.

1. LOGIC CHECK
No names outside header: All references use standard terms — compliant.
Numbers & rules consistent: Days-to-bout, phase labels, mastery thresholds (30=Usable), fatigue/injury risk markers, weight tracking, and win probability all align with CRITIC_PACK definitions. No contradictions.
Valid state: 12 days out = PEAK phase; this is correctly marked as the last week pad work feeds into the bout — valid game state.
One minor mismatch: "Last pad week" badge sits alongside 12 days remaining; the week ahead is the final camp week — technically correct but slightly ambiguous at a glance.
2. USABILITY & CLARITY — Score: 7/10
✅ Strong points:
Drag-and-drop sessions are intuitive; icons + art make each activity distinct
Immediate visual feedback: changed cards marked YOURS, hatched stat gains, mastery rings filling
Fatigue tank + injury threshold line = clear risk signal
Phase labels (PEAK/BUILD/RECOVER) orient the player quickly
❌ Weaknesses:
Levers for Light/Normal/Hard are subtle — not instantly obvious they're clickable
Stat arrows (+Stamina, +Power) are small and hard to parse at a distance
"Points on the WIN dial" is opaque — what exactly is being measured?
Dense text blocks on the right panel compete with visuals; hard to scan quickly
Comparison: Cleaner than Boxing Manager; less immediately readable than Football Manager training — it shows rather than explains. Art is present and consistent but some key controls still hide behind small UI elements.
3. DECISION DEPTH — Score: 8/10
✅ Strong trade-offs exist:
Intensity = faster gains → higher fatigue → injury risk
Pads/Sparring/Film = mastery + scouting → costs energy & weight control
Diet Cut vs Hold = make weight → lose condition
Moves trained this week = what fires in the fight → opportunity cost (cannot train everything)
Peak phase pressure: no "perfect" week — every choice leaves something behind
❌ One weakness: The WIN dial outcome is opaque; it's hard to tell which choices moved the needle without reading small text — blurs the cause–effect loop.
This is a real resource-allocation puzzle, not a checklist. Good depth.

To reach 10/10
Most important first:
Make intensity controls visible & consistent — Light/Normal/Hard toggles on every card (morning + evening); use distinct colour bands not just small text so intensity is readable at a glance.
Replace dense paragraphs with ± icons — Right panel shows only: Mastery +3.5, Fatigue +5, Stamina −2, then one short line on fight effect. Clean, scannable, instant.
Add end-of-week forecast — Fatigue tank shows today + projected Friday/Saturday/Sunday number. Red dashed injury-risk line stays; colour-fills the tank to show how close you land relative to it.
Explain the WIN dial — Click the 55% circle → 3-line breakdown: Training +4%, Weight cut −1%, Opponent edge −8%. Keep it short; players need to see what their choices moved.
Tooltips on everything that matters — Hover a mastery ring: "Usable (30): fires 50% of the time. Sharp (60): fires 75%. Signature (85): always fires +small damage bonus." No guessing.
Allow day-level drag + quick intensity set — Drag an entire day to reorder; put "All Mornings: Light / Normal / Hard" buttons directly above the Monday–Saturday row where they're seen first.
Simplify the phase row — PEAK / BUILD / RECOVER as three large, clearly distinct buttons at the top; the active one is highlighted. One click changes the whole week's focus baseline.
