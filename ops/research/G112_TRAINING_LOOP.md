# G112_TRAINING_LOOP

## What would a human do?
**(a) Real head coach on the floor.** He is not at a desk. He works the ring or mitt station, calls combinations, and
walks the floor between pairs. Roach's mitt work is live sparring-with-pads; the fighter's improvement is seen in timing,
not a spreadsheet (philstar.com 2014-11-23; youtube.com/watch?v=agMBZ_5xa8A 2013-05-06). At Kings MMA, Cordeiro "casually
strolls the mat… walking between the duos, correcting form" (ocweekly.com 2015-10-15). Session clock is the round timer:
3 min work / 1 min rest (theblogtimer.com 2026-05-27). Typical pro flow: 10-15 min warm-up + rope, 3-4 rounds shadow,
bags, mitts with the coach, then sparring (4-12 rounds in camp), then conditioning (westcountryboxing.com; linealboxing.com
2022-02-21). Fairtex: 5x5-min pad rounds after a morning run; afternoon repeats; sparring 2x week (muay-ying.com
2026-01-21). A coach knows someone improved by cleaner mitt rhythm, bag snap, sparring film, the cut, "he can do 8 hard
rounds now" - no HUD (JUDGEMENT).
**(b) Designer.** Compulsion loop = action -> visible reward -> upgraded capability -> next action (playtank.io
2024-02-12). Juice on the action (hit-stop, number, audio) must land in the same frame as the work (gamejuice.co.uk
2026-04-29). Variety + rare spikes (rainbow training, Drill Great!, crit) beat linear grind. Summaries beat spam.

Pattern: see the body work + see a number or arrow the same moment + a rare spike + a weekly rollup. Hide the math and the
loop dies (EVs); show only a spreadsheet and it becomes Punch Club late game.

## 3. Feedback design
- On-action juice, batched: one floating +1 Power over the bag at round end, not every punch; spawn, float, fade 0.6-1.2 s;
  combine ticks (gamejuice 2026-04-29).
- End-of-block card after the 3:00 bell: three lines (Power +2, Timing +1, Fatigue +8) - same data as the floats, once.
- Arrows on the profile, 24-48 h window (FM): green up / amber flat / red down on touched attributes; decimals hidden.
- Weekly one-pager: sparkline per fighter + one coach's note.
- Milestones not spam: drill mastery I-V, first clean 8-round bag, chemistry unlock, breakthroughs.
- The next-day pull: an unfinished mastery bar, a scheduled spar vs a rival, a fatigue gamble, a promised rainbow session,
  a station upgrade arriving.
- Avoid: +0.01 spam every tick; identical days; stats that move with no body animation; decay that wipes a week.

## 4. Recommendation (Grok)
Floor: Gleason/AKA hybrid on the player's grid - ring is the stage; bag bank; mitt pad next to the ring; weights/cardio on
a wall; mats/dummy opposite. Coach defaults to the mitt pocket or ring apron and walks to the station when the player
inspects a fighter. Diegetic bell: 3:00/1:00 ticks the whole floor. Select a fighter -> rounds play at that station;
round end: one stacked float, then gone; end of day: a locker-room slate, max 5 lines + fatigue/injury flag; profile FM
arrows 48 h; weekly one graph. Coach holds mitts for the featured fighter, walks others, bark lines on slop or
breakthrough, referees light spar when two share the ring.
Five features: drill mastery I-V per station; breakthroughs (~8-12% on fresh drill + high chemistry + low fatigue; rainbow
ring, double gain, coach shout, optional clip); coach-fighter chemistry; sparring rivalries between gym-mates; the fatigue
stake. Minimum slice: 3D floor with 3 stations animated, diegetic bell, coach at mitts, round-end float + day slate, one
mastery pip + one breakthrough VFX.
