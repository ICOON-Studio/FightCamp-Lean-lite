# BoxMAC (arXiv 2412.18204, Dec 2024) - read S131 on the operator's link

"BoxMAC - A Boxing Dataset for Multi-label Action Classification", Shashikanta Sahoo, IIT Madras.
https://arxiv.org/html/2412.18204v1 . Asked: "would this do?" (as the real-boxer data the S131 realism answer called for).

## What it is
- 15 professional boxers, two-boxer ring footage, 4 PTZ cameras, 25 fps, ~4 min per recording; 60,000+ frames, 2,314
  clips (8-40 frames), 13 per-frame multi-labels curated under a coach: jab, cross, lead/rear hook, lead/rear uppercut,
  stance, slip, block, guard, duck, clinching, no action.
- RGB + action labels ONLY - no 3D poses, no 2D keypoints, no mocap. The paper gives NO download link (email the author).

## Frame shares as published (their sampling - NOT CompuBox punch mix)
stance 34% | guard 12.8% | no action 12.2% | jab 10.2% | block 5.2% | rear uppercut 4.3% | rear hook 4.2% |
lead hook 4.0% | lead uppercut 3.9% | cross 3.6% | clinching 2.5% | duck 1.7% | slip 1.4%.
Uppercuts as common as hooks and crosses rarer than hooks = clip-balanced sampling; keep G99 CompuBox for punch mix.

## Verdict for FightCamp
- NOT motion data: getting 3D out of it means running two tangled, occluded pros through monocular tracking (QuickMagic
  merges wrapped bodies - S130/S131) from moving/zooming PTZ cameras. Not the fix for motion.
- USEFUL as timing/behaviour reference: how much of a round is stance/guard/"no action" vs punches, action durations
  0.3-1.6 s, clinch 2.5% of frames. Candidate input to the "70% that is not punching" (footwork/guard time budget),
  after the access question is answered.
- Does not replace filming boxers ourselves (fixed phones, separated drills QuickMagic can process).
