Promoters paper, shrink the house, and move price. They do not leave a 20k bowl 35% empty and call it a night.

1. Papering - paid / total

NSAC tax-free comps: 8% of house, spread across price bands. Over 8% is taxed. SOURCED NAC 467.332.

TX: tax on comps over 25%. SOURCED TDLR.

OR 6% of sold seats; UT/MO 4%; OK 10%; NE 5% of capacity. SOURCED statutes (Combatsportslaw +1).

Verdict - levers the game promoter should get
House scale (pick/config the room). Real. Apex vs T-Mobile vs half York Hall.
Paper (comp %). Real. Counted 11-31% of announced bodies; statute caps 4-10% tax-free, TX 25%.
Price ladder + cheap-seat dump. Real. Same building, avg ticket moves 3-7x. Shaw dumps the upper deck to fill; Paul holds price and sells late. Face-value drop on printed stock needs a commission in NV.
35% cold fill of max arena: not what they do. They shrink the house or paper it.
Hometown +15: still NOT FOUND.

Give the player: venue size, comp budget, tier prices (and a late dump on the cheap band). Do not give a magic 35->100 fill curve on a fixed 20k bowl.
