## WHAT THE BEST ONES SHARE
1. **One list of what needs you, with the deadline on every row.** All Your Wrestling delivers an inbox plus an advisor. Boxing World Simulator splits fights into Offered / Booked / Mandatories. Cage Empire's cancelled-event message names the fighters. The worst-reviewed calendar (WCBM2) has dates "referenced but not labeled" and booked fights you "can't tell … from the Calendar".
2. **Every booked fight shows in the calendar and on the fighter.** Corner King lists booked fights in the ladder's side column and shows booked fighters in red. Boxing World Simulator added a per-fighter Schedule tab.
3. **Time-skip stops only on decisions, and the player can choose.** Corner King's Simulate Days "only stops for important decisions" and offers skips of 5 days up to the next fight. Boxing Club Manager's players ask for "selected notifications". Ludus Magnatus is the counter-case: no speed-up drew a complaint.
4. **The ladder is a table with the player in it, and each gate is stated.** Examples are Ludus Magnatus' 16-club divisions, Corner King's ladder panel and its warning when a fighter's standing is above your gym's, Pro Wrestling Sim's Marketing and Back Office requirement, and Boxing School 2's "fights needed" count. WCBM2 is the opposite: its top complaint (111 up) is no rankings and random opponents.
5. **Tiers have names and a payoff.** Boxing School has 3 levels with a boss, Boxing Manager has amateur medals, and Boxing World Simulator has an amateur track with a four-year clock. Boxing Club Manager's amateur tier "does nothing" and drew a 9-up complaint.
6. **The tutorial lives inside the real game, is short, skippable and re-openable.** All Your Wrestling's first-week guide ("You can of course skip this help"), its yellow hints and its full-setup Jobber difficulty answered launch-week complaints. WCBM2's story intro "is a decent tutorial". Ludus Magnatus lets you reset it. Games that ship with none collect the top negative review ("No tutorial, nothing explained").
7. **Show the opponent before the player commits.** WCBM2's 111-up complaint is random opponents. Corner King warns when an offer is above the gym's standing.
8. **A marked "start here" and a first month that is a short script, not the whole manual.** A Corner King reviewer wants a marked "Recommended" start. All Your Wrestling pre-fills a roster, shows and titles on its Jobber setting. (The FightCamp fit is my JUDGEMENT.)

## Suggested FightCamp tutorial skeleton (JUDGEMENT, built only from the above)
1. A guided first month in a skippable, re-openable panel: sign one fighter, set the week's training, read a "next event" card that says "entry closes <date>, weigh-in <date>, fight <date>", book an amateur club show, then watch it.
2. Explain the "why" in one line when a gate matters, for example "To enter regional shows you need 3 wins and a Level-2 ring".
3. A ladder panel on the fights screen: club, regional, national and pro as rows, your fighters placed in them, and the next gate with its requirement.
4. An email-style notice when a gate opens, with the action button inside it (like Corner King's title-shot email).
5. Skip-time stops at the entry deadline, weigh-in and fight day, with a toggle for every day.
