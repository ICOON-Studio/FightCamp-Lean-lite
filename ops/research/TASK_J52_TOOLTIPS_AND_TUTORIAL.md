## Questions (numbers wherever they exist)

   Out of the Park Baseball, Crusader Kings 3 (nested tooltips), Frostpunk: delay before showing (ms),
   max words, whether they nest, how a term is explained the same way everywhere (glossary / encyclopedia).
2. On-screen text budget: what earns permanent screen space vs a tooltip. Any published UX guidance
   (GDC talks, developer blogs) with a rule of thumb.
3. Tutorials: coach marks / highlighted buttons vs a guided first week vs contextual hints that fire the
   first time a screen opens vs a separate tutorial mode. What do FM, Motorsport Manager, Punch Club,
   EA UFC / Undisputed career modes, WWE 2K MyGM do? Skip rules, how long the tutorial runs, how players
   rate it (reviews / Steam comments that complain about tutorials).
4. Failure modes: tutorials players hate (walls of text, forced clicks, can't skip), tooltip failures
   (too slow, cover what you are reading, missing on the thing you need).
5. A recommended spec for FightCamp: tooltip delay, max words, style, glossary rules; tutorial form,

   weigh-in -> fight night -> result), skip/replay rules.
