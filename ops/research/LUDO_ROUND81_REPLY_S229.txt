FightCamp — Round 81: Making the Gym Floor Playable
Part A: 10-Minute Playthrough Audit — 8 Failure Moments & Smallest Fixes

Playing as a first-time player running Leo Vance on Day 1 with the current floor build:

1. The 120-Second Void (Silent Accrual)
The Moment: You tap "Train at Heavy Bag" on Leo. He walks over, starts punching. The 2-minute timer ticks down. No numbers change on screen. His attributes look identical. You watch an animation loop for 90 seconds wondering if your inputs did anything or if the game is hung up until the day summary screen suddenly fires at 0:00.
Smallest Fix: A floating micro-tick pip directly over the station head every 15 game-seconds: +0.2 Power (Station) in cream text fading up, with an amber stamina burn pip -3 Stamina.
Number: Accrual pulse interval = 15.0 game seconds. Float delta = +0.1 to +0.4 stat points visually previewed, deducted from the station's daily cap pool.
2. Blind Station Queuing (No Planned Route)
The Moment: You queue "Speed Bag" while Leo is on the "Heavy Bag", but there is no breadcrumb, target marker, or queue pathline. You don't know if the queue registered or if clicking it cancelled his current workout, leading to anxious re-clicking that spam-resets his walk state.
Smallest Fix: A high-contrast dotted route spline / ground decal linking his current station anchor to the queued station anchor, with a tiny badge "NEXT: SPEED BAG (45s)" anchored over the destination station.
Number: Max queue depth = 2 stations. Ground route marker alpha = 0.75, pulse cycle = 1.2s.
3. Coach Proximity Feels Decorative
The Moment: You send Coach to stand at Heavy Bag 1 with Leo. A toast pops up saying "Coach assigned", but the fighter's animation and feedback remain 100% identical. You can't tell if the coach's bonus is 5% or 50%, or if the coach is actively talking or correcting form.
Smallest Fix: When Coach shares a station with the fighter, an active coach aura ring appears at their feet with a gold tag: "FORM COACHING: +35% GAINS, -20% FATIGUE", and Leo's floating stat tick changes color from cream #f6f0e0 to gold #f0c233.
Number: Coach bonus multiplier = 1.35x stat gain rate, fatigue drain multiplier = 0.80x.
4. Zero Warning On Exhaustion Death Spiral
The Moment: Leo runs his stamina down to 8% while hitting the bags. The stamina bar on his status card turns red, but there is no auditory or physical stall. You leave him there; at day end he suffers an unpreventable hamstring strain or exhaustion debuff that feels like a random penalty rather than an avoidable player mistake.
Smallest Fix: At stamina < 25%, an urgent flashing amber caution badge pops up over the fighter: "EXHAUSTED: INJURY RISK 4x". The fighter's idle/punch cadence slows by 40%, and a one-click floating action prompt appears right on him: [CALL TO BENCH].
Number: Warning trigger = 25.0 stamina. Risk escalation = base 0.5% per minute jumps to 2.0% per minute.
5. Pad Chains Feel Completely Disconnected From Floor Work
The Moment: The GDD promises pad chains drill techniques into muscle memory (e.g., slip-counter), but the Pads station just shows generic time ticking. You have no idea which chain is being drilled or how close you are to unlocking a real weapon for Saturday.
Smallest Fix: Station card on Pads displays active chain progress: "SLIP-CROSS: 142/600m [MASTERED IN 4 SESSIONS]". Each drill cycle shows an execution prompt pip: "CHAIN SYNC +1".
Number: Drill tick adds +15 drilled minutes to card every 15 game-seconds of active pad work with coach present.
6. The Day Goal Is Passive Text Nobody Reads
The Moment: Top header says "Day Goal: Build Conditioning". The player trains Leo on Weights, but nothing tracks toward the goal until 0:00. The player feels no positive reinforcement during the actual two-minute live loop.
Smallest Fix: A progress bar embedded directly inside the top goal plate: Conditioning: 6.4 / 10.0 mins [64%]. Filling it flashes the plate gold with an instant morale sound and float text: "GOAL MET: +5 MORALE".
Number: Goal target threshold = 10.0 station-minutes. Reward = +5 fighter composure/morale pool for Saturday.
7. Action Ring Target Blindness
The Moment: You click Leo to open the 6-action ring. The ring covers the station behind him, and clicking "Train At" opens a giant sub-list of 17 stations with no indication of which ones are occupied, broken, or optimal for his archetype (e.g., swarmer vs. out-boxer).
Smallest Fix: Stations in the "Train At" radial menu display tiny archetype affinity tags (BEST, OK, OFF-PLAN) and current travel walking time (e.g., "Heavy Bag 2 (3s walk)").
Number: Up to 3 stations tagged RECOMMENDED based on fighter primary gap attribute (e.g. Chin or Stamina).
8. Sudden Day End Jarring Cut
The Moment: Day clock hits 0:00. The screen abruptly freezes, floor fades to black instantly, and an overwhelming full-page multi-tab ledger opens with 40 numbers. You lose the physical sense of the gym club you were just standing in.
Smallest Fix: A 5-second end-of-day bell sound ("10-second wood clacker" at 0:10, triple bell at 0:00). Fighters naturally walk toward the locker room aisle (z: 16.4-18.0). A compact bottom-drawer slide-up card summarizes: "DAY 1 WRAP: +1.2 PWR, -38 STA, 0 INJURIES" with a clear [REVIEW FULL LEDGER] or [NEXT MORNING] button.
Number: Bell warning at T - 10s. Transition walk delay = 2.5s before UI overlay drawer appears.

# gym_stat_accrual_engine.gd
# Pure rules engine: extends RefCounted, zero preloads, plain dicts in/out.
class_name GymStatAccrualEngine
extends RefCounted

const TICK_INTERVAL_GAME_SEC: float = 15.0
const COACH_BOOST_MULT: float = 1.35
const COACH_FATIGUE_MULT: float = 0.80

# Station attribute targets and base yields per 60 game-seconds
const STATION_PROFILES: Dictionary = {
	"heavy_bag": {"stat": "power", "rate_per_min": 0.8, "sta_drain_per_min": 12.0},
	"speed_bag": {"stat": "speed", "rate_per_min": 0.9, "sta_drain_per_min": 8.0},
	"pads": {"stat": "technique", "rate_per_min": 1.1, "sta_drain_per_min": 14.0},
	"weights": {"stat": "power", "rate_per_min": 1.0, "sta_drain_per_min": 16.0},
	"treadmill": {"stat": "conditioning", "rate_per_min": 1.2, "sta_drain_per_min": 10.0},
	"air_bike": {"stat": "conditioning", "rate_per_min": 1.4, "sta_drain_per_min": 15.0},
	"slip_rope": {"stat": "defense", "rate_per_min": 0.9, "sta_drain_per_min": 9.0},
	"mirror_skipping": {"stat": "fight_iq", "rate_per_min": 0.7, "sta_drain_per_min": 7.0},
	"stretch_corner": {"stat": "recovery", "rate_per_min": 0.5, "sta_drain_per_min": -10.0} # Restores stamina
}

# Evaluates elapsed frame delta and yields an event if an accrual boundary is crossed.
# state: {
#   "station_id": String,
#   "station_kind": String,
#   "elapsed_at_station": float,
#   "fighter_stamina": float,
#   "coach_present": bool,
#   "fighter_attributes": Dictionary
# }
static func process_station_tick(state: Dictionary, delta_game_sec: float) -> Dictionary:
	var out: Dictionary = {
		"tick_occurred": false,
		"elapsed_at_station": state.get("elapsed_at_station", 0.0) + delta_game_sec,
		"stat_gained_key": "",
		"stat_delta": 0.0,
		"stamina_delta": 0.0,
		"new_stamina": state.get("fighter_stamina", 100.0),
		"new_stat_val": 0.0,
		"is_coach_boosted": state.get("coach_present", false),
		"toast_message": ""
	}

	var prev_intervals: int = int(state.get("elapsed_at_station", 0.0) / TICK_INTERVAL_GAME_SEC)
	var next_intervals: int = int(out["elapsed_at_station"] / TICK_INTERVAL_GAME_SEC)

	if next_intervals <= prev_intervals:
		return out

	out["tick_occurred"] = true
	var kind: String = state.get("station_kind", "heavy_bag")
	var profile: Dictionary = STATION_PROFILES.get(kind, {"stat": "conditioning", "rate_per_min": 0.5, "sta_drain_per_min": 10.0})

	var fraction_of_min: float = TICK_INTERVAL_GAME_SEC / 60.0
	var coach_mult: float = COACH_BOOST_MULT if out["is_coach_boosted"] else 1.0
	var coach_sta_mult: float = COACH_FATIGUE_MULT if out["is_coach_boosted"] else 1.0

	var stat_gain: float = profile["rate_per_min"] * fraction_of_min * coach_mult
	var sta_drain: float = profile["sta_drain_per_min"] * fraction_of_min * coach_sta_mult

	# Clamp if fighter is completely tapped out
	var cur_sta: float = state.get("fighter_stamina", 100.0)
	if cur_sta <= 0.0 and sta_drain > 0.0:
		stat_gain *= 0.20 # Exhaustion penalty: 80% loss of gain efficiency

	var stat_name: String = profile["stat"]
	var cur_val: float = 0.0
	var attrs: Dictionary = state.get("fighter_attributes", {})
	if attrs.has(stat_name):
		cur_val = float(attrs[stat_name])

	out["stat_gained_key"] = stat_name
	out["stat_delta"] = snappedf(stat_gain, 0.01)
	out["stamina_delta"] = snappedf(-sta_drain, 0.01)
	out["new_stamina"] = maxf(0.0, snappedf(cur_sta - sta_drain, 0.01))
	out["new_stat_val"] = snappedf(cur_val + stat_gain, 0.01)

	var boost_str: String = " (Coach +35%)" if out["is_coach_boosted"] else ""
	out["toast_message"] = "+%0.2f %s%s" % [out["stat_delta"], stat_name.capitalize(), boost_str]

	return out

	add_child(label)

	tween.finished.connect(label.queue_free)

Fix 2: Fatigue & Exhaustion Hazard Safety Interlock
# gym_fatigue_hazard_engine.gd
# Extends RefCounted: Pure logic for handling exhaustion triggers, slow debuff, and emergency rests.
class_name GymFatigueHazardEngine
extends RefCounted

const CRITICAL_STAMINA_THRESHOLD: float = 25.0
const BASE_INJURY_ODDS_PER_MIN: float = 0.005 # 0.5%
const EXHAUSTED_INJURY_ODDS_PER_MIN: float = 0.020 # 2.0%

# Evaluates whether the fighter is currently in an exhaustion state.
static func evaluate_fatigue_hazard(fighter_state: Dictionary, delta_game_sec: float) -> Dictionary:
	var stamina: float = fighter_state.get("stamina", 100.0)
	var is_critical: bool = stamina <= CRITICAL_STAMINA_THRESHOLD
	var was_critical: bool = fighter_state.get("was_fatigue_critical", false)
	
	var out: Dictionary = {
		"is_critical": is_critical,
		"entered_critical": is_critical and not was_critical,
		"exit_critical": not is_critical and was_critical,
		"speed_penalty_factor": 0.60 if is_critical else 1.0,
		"injury_triggered": false,
		"injury_type": "",
		"warning_text": ""
	}

	if is_critical:
		out["warning_text"] = "EXHAUSTED (Stamina < 25%). High Injury Risk!"
		var fraction_of_min: float = delta_game_sec / 60.0
		var check_roll: float = randf()
		if check_roll < (EXHAUSTED_INJURY_ODDS_PER_MIN * fraction_of_min):
			out["injury_triggered"] = true
			out["injury_type"] = select_exhaustion_injury(fighter_state.get("current_station_kind", "heavy_bag"))
			out["warning_text"] = "INJURY! Sustained %s due to overwork!" % out["injury_type"]

	return out

static func select_exhaustion_injury(station_kind: String) -> String:
	match station_kind:
		"heavy_bag", "weights":
			return "Wrist Sprain"
		"treadmill", "air_bike":
			return "Hamstring Strain"
		"pads", "slip_rope":
			return "Ankle Tweak"
		_:
			return "Muscle Tear"

# Resolves emergency intervention: send to bench or stretch corner
static func execute_emergency_rest(fighter: Dictionary) -> Dictionary:
	var updated: Dictionary = fighter.duplicate(true)
	updated["target_station"] = "stretch_corner"
	updated["is_recovering"] = true
	updated["status_tag"] = "FORCED RECOVERY"
	return updated

# gym_route_drawer_ui.gd
# Extends Control: Custom 2D HUD overlay that projects 3D station coords to 2D lines and breadcrumbs.
class_name GymRouteDrawerUI
extends Control

const COLOR_VOID: Color = Color("#0d0c0b")
const COLOR_GOLD: Color = Color("#f0c233")
const COLOR_PANEL: Color = Color("#22201d")
const COLOR_CREAM: Color = Color("#f6f0e0")

var _game_camera: Camera3D = null
var _source_station_pos: Vector3 = Vector3.ZERO
var _target_station_pos: Vector3 = Vector3.ZERO
var _target_station_name: String = ""
var _is_routing: bool = false
var _pulse_time: float = 0.0

func _init() -> void:
	name = "GymRouteDrawerUI"
	set_anchors_preset(PRESET_FULL_RECT)
	mouse_filter = MOUSE_FILTER_IGNORE

func setup(camera: Camera3D) -> void:
	_game_camera = camera

func set_route(origin_3d: Vector3, dest_3d: Vector3, dest_name: String) -> void:
	_source_station_pos = origin_3d
	_target_station_pos = dest_3d
	_target_station_name = dest_name
	_is_routing = true
	queue_redraw()

func clear_route() -> void:
	_is_routing = false
	queue_redraw()

func _process(delta: float) -> void:
	if _is_routing:
		_pulse_time += delta * 4.0
		queue_redraw()

func _draw() -> void:
	if not _is_routing or _game_camera == null:
		return

	if _game_camera.is_position_behind(_source_station_pos) or _game_camera.is_position_behind(_target_station_pos):
		return

	var p1: Vector2 = _game_camera.unproject_position(_source_station_pos)
	var p2: Vector2 = _game_camera.unproject_position(_target_station_pos)

	# Draw dashed pathline
	var line_color: Color = COLOR_GOLD
	line_color.a = 0.6 + sin(_pulse_time) * 0.25
	draw_dashed_line(p1, p2, line_color, 3.0, 6.0)

	# Draw origin circle
	draw_circle(p1, 5.0, COLOR_GOLD)
	draw_arc(p1, 9.0, 0, TAU, 16, COLOR_GOLD, 1.5)

	# Draw destination anchor circle
	draw_circle(p2, 6.0, COLOR_GOLD)
	draw_arc(p2, 12.0 + sin(_pulse_time) * 2.0, 0, TAU, 24, COLOR_GOLD, 2.0)

	# Destination Tooltip Tag
	var tag_rect: Rect2 = Rect2(p2.x - 60.0, p2.y - 32.0, 120.0, 22.0)
	draw_rect(tag_rect, COLOR_PANEL, true)
	draw_rect(tag_rect, COLOR_GOLD, false, 1.0)
	
	var font: Font = ThemeDB.fallback_font
	var text: String = "NEXT: " + _target_station_name.to_upper()
	draw_string(font, Vector2(tag_rect.position.x + 8, tag_rect.position.y + 15), text, HORIZONTAL_ALIGNMENT_LEFT, 104, 10, COLOR_CREAM)

# test_stat_accrual.gd
extends SceneTree

func _initialize() -> void:
	var failures: int = 0
	
	# Initial state: 0 sec on heavy bag, Leo has 50.0 power, 100 stamina, no coach
	var state: Dictionary = {
		"station_id": "heavy_bag_1",
		"station_kind": "heavy_bag",
		"elapsed_at_station": 0.0,
		"fighter_stamina": 100.0,
		"coach_present": false,
		"fighter_attributes": {"power": 50.0}
	}
	
	# Sub-interval tick (10 seconds) - should NOT trigger an accrual pip
	var res1: Dictionary = GymStatAccrualEngine.process_station_tick(state, 10.0)
	if res1["tick_occurred"] != false:
		failures += 1
		print("FAIL: Micro-tick fired before crossing TICK_INTERVAL_GAME_SEC boundary")

	# Update elapsed and push across 15-second boundary (another 6 seconds, total 16s)
	state["elapsed_at_station"] = res1["elapsed_at_station"]
	var res2: Dictionary = GymStatAccrualEngine.process_station_tick(state, 6.0)
	if res2["tick_occurred"] != true:
		failures += 1
		print("FAIL: Expected tick to occur at >= 15 seconds")
	
	if res2["stat_gained_key"] != "power":
		failures += 1
		print("FAIL: Incorrect stat picked for heavy bag")

	# Heavy bag rate per min is 0.8. In 15s (0.25 min) -> 0.20 power gain
	if not is_equal_approx(res2["stat_delta"], 0.20):
		failures += 1
		print("FAIL: Stat delta calculation wrong. Expected 0.20, got: ", res2["stat_delta"])

	# Test coach boost
	state["coach_present"] = true
	state["elapsed_at_station"] = 15.0
	var res3: Dictionary = GymStatAccrualEngine.process_station_tick(state, 15.0)
	# 0.20 * 1.35 = 0.27
	if not is_equal_approx(res3["stat_delta"], 0.27):
		failures += 1
		print("FAIL: Coach boost multiplier failed. Expected 0.27, got: ", res3["stat_delta"])

	prints(" failures=" + str(failures))
	quit(1 if failures > 0 else 0)

# test_fatigue_hazard.gd
extends SceneTree

func _initialize() -> void:
	var failures: int = 0
	
	var healthy_fighter: Dictionary = {
		"stamina": 80.0,
		"was_fatigue_critical": false,
		"current_station_kind": "heavy_bag"
	}
	
	var res1: Dictionary = GymFatigueHazardEngine.evaluate_fatigue_hazard(healthy_fighter, 1.0)
	if res1["is_critical"] == true or res1["entered_critical"] == true:
		failures += 1
		print("FAIL: Healthy fighter falsely reported as exhausted")

	var tired_fighter: Dictionary = {
		"stamina": 22.0,
		"was_fatigue_critical": false,
		"current_station_kind": "treadmill"
	}
	
	var res2: Dictionary = GymFatigueHazardEngine.evaluate_fatigue_hazard(tired_fighter, 1.0)
	if res2["is_critical"] != true or res2["entered_critical"] != true:
		failures += 1
		print("FAIL: Fighter below 25.0 stamina must trigger critical hazard")

	if not is_equal_approx(res2["speed_penalty_factor"], 0.60):
		failures += 1
		print("FAIL: Expected 0.60 speed penalty during exhaustion")

	# Test emergency rest execution
	var updated_fighter: Dictionary = GymFatigueHazardEngine.execute_emergency_rest(tired_fighter)
	if updated_fighter.get("target_station") != "stretch_corner" or updated_fighter.get("is_recovering") != true:
		failures += 1
		print("FAIL: Emergency rest did not redirect fighter to recovery")

	prints(" failures=" + str(failures))
	quit(1 if failures > 0 else 0)
