FightCamp path today: book → skip days → fight week → weigh-in → TOTT → bell. Reads as buttons. Real producers and praised career modes stretch that into stakes.

## 1. Real fight-week sequences (SOURCED)

### UFC (typical Saturday PPV)

Days out, public-facing:

| Day | Event | On-air / public length |
|---|---|---|
| Sun–Mon (−6/−7) | Travel, hotel, light work. Cut not started (~8–10 lb over). | Closed |
| Tue (−5) | Open workouts | 30–45 min public session |
| Wed (−4) | Main press conference | 60–90 min |
| Thu (−3) | Media day / 1:1s | Scattered |
| Fri (−2) | Official weigh-in AM (closed, commission). Ceremonial weigh-in + faceoff PM (public). | Ceremonial streams: ~30–65 min (Vancouver 29:35; OKC 64:35; Abu Dhabi 44:39) |
| Sat (0) | Doors ~4–5h before main. Early prelims → prelims → main. Walkouts + Bruce Buffer + ref instructions immediately before each bout. | Walkout + intro + rules: ~3–8 min per fight |

Recent UFC.com fight-week guides (UFC 320, 323, 324, 325, 330, 331) converge on Thu presser 5pm local, Fri ceremonial 5–6pm, Sat fight. Official scale is morning; ceremonial is theatre. (Mmafightbible)

### Pro boxing card

Same skeleton, often compressed:
- Tue: open workouts / grand arrivals
- Wed: final presser (~60 min) + staged faceoff
- Thu or Fri: public weigh-in (~30–60 min) + staredown
- Fight night: undercard then main. Ring walk + announcer + ref: ~3–6 min.

Examples: Usyk–Fury 2 (arrivals Tue, media workouts Wed, presser Thu, weigh-in Fri, fight Sat); Canelo–Crawford (arrival Tue, workouts Wed, presser Thu, ceremonial weigh-in Fri); Paul–Joshua (workouts Tue, presser Wed, weigh-in Thu, fight Fri). (Givemesport)

Broadcast producers treat presser + weigh-in faceoff + walkout as the three TV moments. Everything else is B-roll.

### Amateur / club show (FightCamp's first career fights)

Same-day, not a week. 3×3 min typical.
- Afternoon / 2h before first bell: weigh-in (underwear, certified scale, one step). Fail weight → bout scratched.
- Immediate pre-bout medical (BP, pupils, BCR1/passport).
- Coaches' meeting + bout sheet. Gloves/headgear table.
- Doors; warm-up in a back room. Waiting is the hard part.
- Ring announcer, corners, ref instructions, bell. 3–5 min between bouts.

Club shows: weigh-in often ~5pm for a 7pm first bell → ~2h to refeed. No presser, no ceremonial scale, no walkout production. Hype is local: packed hall, gym mates, first time under lights. (YouTube)

## 3. What makes the road feel HARD (SOURCED + player evidence)

Praised hardness is scarcity + irreversible cost, not extra cutscenes.

**Camp choices / time.** Punch Club: stats decay daily; work vs train vs eat vs sleep. Hardcore reviews: miss one week → no money → no food → can't train → lose fight → deeper hole. IGN: "constant, demoralizing struggle"; "vast majority of 20 hours not fighting." Steam: "the game doesn't want me to succeed." That's the feeling FightCamp is missing. (IGN)

**Weight.** Undisputed career: weight vs sharpness vs energy, one action per week. Reddit: second fight "gains a ridiculous amount of weight"; 6-week max camp can't catch both green weight and charged stamina; players sim empty weeks just to get back in range. IGN/Shacknews: miss weight → fight doesn't count / fame and purse cut. That is the mechanic players remember. (IGN)

**Injuries.** UFC 5: more damage in sparring than in fights; cuts steal training days and purse; partners KO you in camp. Game Informer: "repetition sinks in fast" after ~25 fights; sim camp = worse gains. Hardness is real; presentation of that hardness is a menu flag, so it feels like tax not story. (Gameinformer)

**Rejected / bad offers.** Undisputed: you pick opponents (except titles), negotiate media days and camp length vs purse. Players refuse media to keep camp clean. UFC 5 Career: long WFA grind before the roster "clicks"; "breaking in felt harder than it should, not in a satisfying way." Hardness without a visible gate = grind, not hype. (Eneba)

**What players praise vs what they skip.** Praise: Undisputed's early-career tension vs tomato cans while your ratings are trash; Punch Club's earned routine; FNC's scripted cost (broken hand, prison rules, Olympic amateur rules). Skip: UFC 5 walkouts after the 5th fight; WWE entrances after the 3rd; FNC training minigames. Hype that repeats identically dies. Hype that can fail (miss weight, show up cut, opponent pulls, offer too cheap) stays.

## 4. Proposed FightCamp sequence — 7 beats, offer → bell (JUDGEMENT)

Amateur first (club, 3×3, 2–4 week camp). Same skeleton scales to pro/UFC by adding presser + ceremonial.

| # | Beat | When | Duration on screen | Shown | Player decides |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Offer | Day 0 | 20–40s | Purse, rounds, opponent tape, date, medical risk flags. Club show vs "stay home." | Accept / counter / refuse. Refuse costs momentum, not a game-over. |
| 2 | Camp weeks | −28 to −7 (amateur 14–21d) | One screen per week, 15–30s if sim | Weight trend, region damage, spar risk, sharpness. Calendar, not a montage. | Train / spar / cut / rest / work. Spar can injure. Cut too fast = medical hold. |
| 3 | Fight week gate | −7 to −2 | 20s | Travel, "still X lb over," opponent quote. Amateur: skip; just "show night tomorrow." | Confirm camp plan or pull (reputation hit). |
| 4 | Weigh-in | Amateur: afternoon of show. Pro: Fri. | 30–60s | Scale number, pass/fail, opponent number, short faceoff still. Fail = bout off or catchweight penalty. | Last cut choice night before (risk vs make weight). Cannot skip if close. |
| 5 | Holding room | 30–90 min before bout (compressed to 20–40s) | | Gloves, wrap, coach last note, card running late, body readiness. Amateur truth: the wait. | Warm-up intensity (gas tank vs tightness). |
| 6 | Tale of the Tape + walk | Minutes before bell | 20–45s amateur (announcer + walk from dressing room). 45–90s pro (lights, song, TOTT graphic). | Names, record, reach, weight actually made, region heat. | Walk style only. No more training choices. |
| 7 | Instructions → bell | | 10–20s | Ref, touching gloves or not, crowd swell. | Touch / stare. Then sim takes over. |

**Why this is hard without becoming a cutscene farm.** Beats 1–4 can fail. Miss weight, fail medical, pull with an injury: the fight does not happen. That is the difficulty of reaching the fight. Amateur omits presser and ceremonial; the scale and the wait carry the weight. Pro/UFC inserts a presser beat between 3 and 4 (quote / staredown, 20s). Never auto-play the same 90s walkout after fight 3. First club show and first title get the long version; club #4 is scale + walk + bell.

**What a human producer does that the game should steal.** Announcement (offer) → one public collision (presser or weigh-in faceoff) → one private cost (cut, injury, missed week) → one irreducible ceremony (walk + instructions). Games that only ship the ceremony feel like buttons. Games that only ship the spreadsheet feel dry. Both, with failure on the spreadsheet, is the hype.
