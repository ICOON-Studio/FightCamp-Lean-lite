# S10 step 1 - a `movement`-class degrade, and what the body was doing

Generated by `Godot/tools/measure_movement_degrades.gd`. Population: the
S9 fixture to the digit - two differentiated men (reach 183/176 cm, every
stat different, neither carrying a `technique_progress` record so both are
legal for whatever rules they are put under), 100 bouts x 3 rounds per
ruleset from seed base 560000, rulesets boxing, kickboxing, muay_thai, mma.
Adapter options are the room's own: `beat_ticks: 1`, `grapple_beats: true`.

## The question

S9 ranked the degrades and found `movement`-class ids at ranks 2, 3 and 4.
Its handover said those beats leave the man frozen. `_thrower_clip` does
return "" for them - but `FightWorldLiveScreen._face_and_walk` drives a
CONTINUOUS walk overlay off the engine's velocity on a different path, so
the man may be visibly stepping while the counter calls it a degrade.

## The threshold, which is not the one the brief named

`_face_and_walk:1338` uses `speed > 0.0001` - but only to decide what to COUNT
in `locomotion_stats`. What PLAYS a walk is `FightStagePlayer.walk_at:466`:
`rate = speed / native_speed_mps`, and `rate < WALK_MIN_RATE` (0.17) drops
the legs and returns him to the stance. The eight walks are natively
1.705-1.781 m/s, so the floor that matters is about 0.29 m/s.
A body between the two thresholds is counted as stepping and is standing
still in the picture. Both numbers are reported.

## WHAT THIS SETTLES

**The man is not frozen.** 60.1% of `movement`-class degrades are a body
the walk overlay is already carrying, and a dead standstill is 9 beats in
30599. S9's handover said these beats leave him frozen; they do not, and
routing a `movement` beat to a locomotion role would have queued a clip for
a man who is already stepping - and occupied his body for the walk's whole
length, dropping the next beat's hit reaction (R110's trap, one costume
down). **The 39.8% below the walk floor ARE the frozen man**, and they are
3.6% of all action beats rather than the 9.1% the degrade queue carried.

**And the brief's own threshold would have answered 100%.** Asking
`speed > 0.0001` - which is `_face_and_walk`'s COUNTING cut, not `walk_at`'s
PLAYING cut - calls every one of these men a walker, including the 12185
whose legs `walk_at` drops. A threshold copied from the line that counts a
thing is not the threshold that does it.

**The beat carries no travel of its own, and that is the finding to plan
against.** A `movement` degrade's man is walking 60.1% of the time; ANY
action beat's man is walking 63.6%, a spread of 3.5 points.
Under 5 points is the same population. The engine naming a beat
`boxing_circle_lead` does NOT mean the man is circling - the walk under him
is whatever his velocity happened to be, which is why it travels the way
the technique says only 52.0% of the time, a coin flip. So BIASING THE
WALK TOWARD THE BEAT WOULD BE INVENTING A CORRELATION THE SIMULATION DOES
NOT HAVE. Making the footwork decision move the man is an ENGINE change
(`FightFootwork` / `FightIntent`), not a presentation one, and it is the
operator's call rather than this stage's.

## Is it the RIGHT walk?

Of the 18405 movement degrades whose man was visibly walking, the walk under
him carried him the way the technique's own `range_delta` says it travels
9563 times, **52.0%**, and the wrong way 8842 times, 48.0%.

That is the LOOSE reading - a closing step wants any forward component, a
circle wants any sideways one, so `loc_advance_left` (-45 deg) counts as a
circle. Asking for a PURE `loc_strafe_*` under a circle and a pure
`loc_advance` under a closing step gives 7621, 41.4%. The gap between the

## What this tool does NOT claim

The room's QUEUE is not reproduced here. `walk_at` also returns early when
the body is BUSY with a non-walk clip that `FightBodyLayers.wants_layering`
refuses (`walk_at:443`) - a kick, say, whose legs are the point. A movement
degrade landing 0.2 s after a kick on the same man would therefore show
less walk than this reads. Body layers are ON by default
(`FightWorldLiveScreen.gd:220`), which is what makes the punch-over-walk
case layer rather than refuse, so the gap is the kicks and the set pieces.
This is an upper bound on how often the walk is already there.
