## §1 — Overlap

**Read:** Interpenetration is an **instant falsehood**. Fight games and sports titles sell contact as “skin meets skin / cloth meets cloth.” Shared volume sells “meshes ignored each other.”

**Dominant or symptom?** **Dominant for the operator’s named pair of complaints.** Once volume is clean, residual skate (≤15 cm, M18 / For Honor-class) and stiff posing become the next layer — but they do not explain “painful” as fast as overlap does.

**Symptom case (when overlap would stop mattering first):** if the frame were so wide and silhouettes so dark that merge was illegible — we are not there; HOME is meant to read two bodies (M14/M20). Overlap stays on-screen.

**Art ask of S43 (not implementation):** the live frame must never show two chests occupying one space. Clean contact can be imperfect (slight gap) and still look better than 2 cm of merge. Prefer a hair of air over shared volume.
