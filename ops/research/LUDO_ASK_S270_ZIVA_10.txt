=== MY OPINION (Claude, the developer) ===
- Ziva's biggest finding is right: the game is a beautiful shell around a loop the player cannot SEE working. Every screen around the loop is good (title, country read-out, corner card, result screen, week review layout). The core promise - "train him, watch him improve" - is the weakest part of the first hour, and it is weak because of flow, not because of missing systems: the training systems exist (11 attributes, station focus, gain bubbles, the coach, the day plan), but the first day hides them behind a modal, a speed button and a review that gave the wrong reason.
- The first hour needs ONE guided arc, Two Point style: arrive -> the coach offer as a decision (not a gate, not on top of the plan) -> set the day -> watch the first block at normal speed with the focus card and bubbles in view -> the day's receipt (what grew, by how much, why) -> book a first club bout from the office -> fight week -> the fight -> the corner -> the result -> the week review that ties it together. Today each piece exists separately; nothing walks the player through them in order.
- The fight is the area furthest from 10: the camera is too high and far (the men are small), the boxers' spacing and foot motion are still being fixed (feet skate, torsos overlap in exchanges), and the venue in the test room is bare. The corner and result screens are already near 9. I would put the next big design effort on the camera and the moment-to-moment read of the bout (what did my training change in this exchange?).
- Two results I disagree with Ziva on: the "BOXING & MMA MANAGEMENT GAME" title line is the WHOLE game's promise (MMA is the first update), so I would keep it on the title but make the demo say "BOXING DEMO" clearly; and the result screen's "TO THE GYM - NO MANAGER CONTRACT FILED - $0" is not a bug but needs one line explaining how the gym earns from a fighter (manager contract) and where to file it.
- What I am least sure about and want your call on: whether a fighter with no coach should train at all in the first week (your 0.40 rule, now built) or whether the first coach should be part of the guided start so the player never meets the coachless state.
