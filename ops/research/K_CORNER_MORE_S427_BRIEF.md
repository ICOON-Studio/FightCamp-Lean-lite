## What the player has today (GAME)
- In a round, every 30 seconds: one of three offered calls (each graded Beats it / Works / Risky against what the
  opponent is doing, with what it costs her in words) or Say nothing; Pace (Push, Steady, Save); Aim (Own game, Head, Body);
  a NOW plate when a gap opens for her drilled combination. Since S427 the bar also shows a live board: punches landed
  both ways, the opponent's game now, each call used with a tick or a cross.
- In the corner minute: two instructions get through (a third is lost); change one plan line; the cut and swelling are
  not in the game yet (the cutman design is separate).

## The job
Design what ELSE a real corner says and changes, as decisions with a case where each option is right:
1. During the round (the freed space on the bar): 2 to 4 new things at most. For each: when it is offered, its options,
   what it changes in the bout using levers the game has (say plainly when a lever is new), what the player sees, and
   why it is not just a fourth call. Think of: a target (her left eye is closing: work it), a warning (the referee has
   warned her once), feints and tempo changes, switching stance for a round, "one more big round" against "bank it".
2. In the minute: what a real corner does in 60 seconds (water, ice, breathing, one technical fix, one tactical change,
   a lie to lift her, the truth to wake her), which of these the game should make a CHOICE, and what each costs
   (the two instructions that get through are the budget: say what competes for them).
3. What the live board should show so the choice is informed and not a lookup (and what it must NOT show).
4. The camp link: which of these are bought in the week (a drilled switch of stance, trust in the corner, a cutman).
5. The menus each appears on and what it reads from. The smallest version worth building first, then the order.
6. Your kill list (the hardest objection to each and your answer) and a hard critic's depth score 0 to 10 with the
   changes that take it to 10.
