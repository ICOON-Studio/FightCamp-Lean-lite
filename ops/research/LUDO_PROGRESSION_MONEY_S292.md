### A1 - pace
"Both are partially misaligned, but the mechanical disconnect is an RPG trap: treating a 0-100 attribute rating as the sole atomic
unit of progression." FM precedent: fractions of a point per month, carried by training ratings, familiarity bars, trait/move progress.
Real amateur arc: novice 0-10 bouts / 0-12 months (rapid adaptation: cardio base, sitting on punches); regional 10-25 bouts / 1-3 years
(refinement); turning pro 20-40+ amateur bouts / 3-5 years (attributes baked; growth moves to ring IQ, scouting, tactical counters).
- dStat/week = Base x Headroom x CoachFactor x IntensityFactor
- Primary focused stat: base **1.0 pt/week** (was 0.6). Secondary: **0.4** (was 0.1). Passive floor drills: 0.1 spread.
- Headroom = clamp((Potential - Current) / **25**, **0.10, 1.25**) - "rookie surge": a 52 with 85 potential gets 1.25x, up to
  1.25-1.4 pts/week on the main focus early.
- Age: <= 21 +20% learning velocity; >= 29 no ceiling growth, maintenance 0.0 to +0.1.
