# G105_CLUB_SHOW_WALKOUT

What a human director does with one aisle, a few lights, a handful of crew: house lights down, ring spots up, one entrance spot or moving head on the curtain. Music from the house PA. One camera walks backward in front of the fighter (or a side-aisle handheld). Cut to a locked wide of the ring when they hit the steps. No crane, no tunnel-to-god-ray, no empty hall. The crowd is the production. SOURCED for kit/scale [web:4][web:16][web:15]; rest JUDGEMENT from those constraints.

## 1. Amateur club show

**Is there a walkout?** Yes. The MC/announcer calls the bout; the boxer walks from the warm-up room with the corner. SOURCED [web:0] Honour & Glory, 2026-03-17; [web:6] TidyHQ UK club planning, 2025-08-20; England Boxing 2025 rules Annex C1 [web:55].

**Who walks with them:** Coach + one or two seconds. Clubmates often cheer from the front row, not in the aisle. SOURCED [web:1] TidyHQ experience guide, 2025-08-18; [web:0].

**From where / how long:** Warm-up area or behind a curtain/door, one short aisle through tables or chairs to the ring steps. Between-bout reset is 60–90 seconds, so the walk itself is short. SOURCED interval [web:6][web:1]. Distance PROXY: leisure-centre / school-hall floor, not a stadium ramp — typically a few dozen metres. JUDGEMENT: ~10–25 seconds of walking.

**Music:** Allowed. England Boxing: ring-walk music must not include expletives or offensive language. SOURCED [web:55] EB 2025 Rule Book (pub. 2025-04-22). Clubs treat the drop + spotlight as the "moment." SOURCED [web:1].

**Gloves / dress (club):** Wraps inspected ringside, then gloves on before the boxer enters the ring. Headguard on after they are in the ring. SOURCED [web:58] EB 2022/23 10.2.4 / 10.3.5; same logic in 2025 annex [web:55]. Walking kit: vest + shorts + boots already on; club robe or "gown" common for warmth; not barefoot. SOURCED gown+gloves image [web:1]; vest/shorts wait [web:62]; historic club: lights stay up, gloves laced ringside [web:59] Independent, 2002-07-20.

## 2. Small-hall pro and regional MMA

**Aisle / time curtain → ring:** York Hall (~1,250 cap) is the type specimen: ring dominates the room; Cage Warriors describes a truncated walkway from a curtain. SOURCED capacity [web:75] Wikipedia / [web:77] BBC 2022-08-29; walkway [web:83] Cage Warriors desk piece. JUDGEMENT: 15–40 s walk for undercard; main-event fighters sometimes stall 10–12 extra seconds to soak the room [web:83].

**Music:** Fighter-chosen track on the house PA. DJ + PA packages exist specifically for 150–1,500-cap combat shows (walk-in, between-rounds, interval). SOURCED [web:4] BoxingDJ.co.uk. MMA: song starts, fighter appears from backstage/curtain, crowd roar has a staggered onset because not everyone can see the door at once. SOURCED [web:14][web:27] Scally, JEA.

**Entourage:** Boxing — trainer leads, 1–3 corner; historically fighter's hands on the trainer's shoulders. Small-venue undercard still "fairly traditional… quiet anonymity." SOURCED [web:11] Sporting News, 2018-11-14. MMA — coach plus a few teammates/family; only coaches stay at the cage. SOURCED [web:14][web:27].

**What the camera does:**
- Small streamed club/pro: 3–4 PTZ locked on the ring; a separate follow cam needs a second body. SOURCED [web:16] YoloLiv / solo boxing stream, 2026-09-18.
- DAZN "standard" / smaller venue: 8–12 cameras, not stadium kit. Walkouts on big shows get a dedicated cinematic (Batcam Hold / Steadicam) with shallow DOF; that is the upscale version of the same idea — lead the fighter, blow out the background. SOURCED [web:15] SVG Europe, 2025-07-18; Steadicam walk-ons [web:25] SVG, 2024-09-23.
- Regional MMA at a hall: handheld on the truncated aisle, then cut to cage-wide. PROXY from [web:83][web:85].
- No crane / spidercam / helicopter on a club or York Hall card. Those are stadium upscales. SOURCED contrast [web:15].

**Cut to ring intro:** Fighter hits the steps → cut wide ring + announcer. Amateur: both boxers may be walked in sequence or together, then referee centre-ring briefing. SOURCED [web:55][web:0].

## 3. What the hall actually looks like

**Crowd**
- Club / community show: ~200 packed into a leisure centre; cabaret tables; families; clubmates on the front row. Opening bouts while people are still arriving. SOURCED [web:1]; AU club guide mid-card timing [web:8] 2025-11-07.
- Small hall (York Hall type): 600–1,250 around a ring that "looms large"; views good from everywhere; fighters rub shoulders with punters. SOURCED [web:77] BBC 2022-08-29; [web:75]; seating band [web:76].
- An empty grey hall is a COVID / failed-gate artifact, not the default. Empty rooms flatten fighters. SOURCED [web:35] Athletic 2020-03-15; [web:50] ESPN 2020-03-17; [web:52] ProBoxTV.

**Light**
- Club planning list: spots on the ring, house lights dimmable; walk-in = music + spotlights. SOURCED [web:6].
- Hire package for small combat shows: pars, moving heads, entrance truss, black drapes over the doorway, haze/CO₂ optional. SOURCED [web:4].
- JUDGEMENT: house down, ring bright, one hot source on the aisle. Not a black void. Not even stadium wash.

**What they wear walking**

| Level | On the walk | Feet | Hands |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amateur club | Vest/shorts; club robe or hoodie/gown over; headguard off until inside | Boxing boots | Wraps; gloves on before ring entry |
| Small-hall pro | Robe or hooded track top over shorts; hood up for focus/heat | Boxing boots — never barefoot | Gloves usually already laced (TV/pro habit). PROXY |
| Regional MMA | Team hoodie / walkout shirt; often still in shoes until the cage | Shoes off at the fence | Small MMA gloves, often already on |

Robe/hoodie function: keep warmup heat; hood cuts crowd noise. SOURCED [web:39] 2026-07-05; [web:37] 2025-05-24. Tyson-style no-robe is a star exception, not the small-hall default. SOURCED [web:39]. Pro fashion robes exist at the top end; they are not club kit. SOURCED [web:38] Athletic 2020-05-05.

**Do not ship:** barefoot + gloves-on + no robe in an empty grey box. That reads as "asset missing," not "authentic club." JUDGEMENT.

## 4. Game tropes to avoid (will read as copied)
- **Hooded silhouette from behind, walking a tunnel toward a blow-out light.** Fight Night Champion DNA; already rejected. JUDGEMENT + operator note.
- **UFC 5 locker-tunnel "build-up":** dark corridor, face-on close-up, teammates dab, then arena-wide reveal as they hit the light. Official feature set. SOURCED [web:67] EA UFC 5 presentation, 2023-10-06; player breakdown of the added tunnel [web:70].
- **WWE 2K ramp:** titantron behind, pyro, fighter walks toward camera down a lit ramp, arms out. JUDGEMENT (franchise-standard).
- **Steadicam hero lead with creamy bokeh and empty seats as "cinematic negative space."** That's DAZN Batcam Hold on a stadium walk, not a hall. SOURCED tool [web:15].
- **Octagon-climb / cage-hop as the button** (UFC 5 specials). SOURCED [web:72].
- **Hands-on-trainer-shoulders procession shot as the only composition** — historically real [web:11], now a period cliché if you hold it.

Safe difference: short aisle, faces and tables in frame, imperfect light, coach at the shoulder, cut on the first step up.

## 5. Three FightCamp shots (5–8 s)

**A. Club show — 6 s.** Path: handheld lead, three-quarter front, 2 m ahead. No arc. Stops when the lead foot hits the first ring step. Frame: boxer + coach in the same shot; tables and standing clubmates in the sides. Crowd / light: 150–250; house still half-up; one cheap spot on the door; ring already lit. Wear: club hoodie or thin robe over vest, boots, red or blue gloves on, no headguard yet. Feel: local, family, the room knows this kid. JUDGEMENT, built on [web:1][web:6][web:55].

**B. Small-hall pro — 7 s.** Path: start side-track on the truncated aisle (curtain → ring). At 4 s arc 60° to profile so the lit ring enters frame behind the ropes. Hold on the step-up. Frame: waist-up then profile; haze in the aisle; faces in the first two rows, not a blur. Crowd / light: 800–1,200, seated tight to the ring; house down; ring spots + one follow on the fighter. Wear: proper robe, hood down or mid-drop, boots, gloves laced. Corner of three. Feel: this is work now; tight room, real noise. JUDGEMENT, built on [web:77][web:83][web:4][web:11].

**C. Title night (still a hall, not Wembley) — 8 s.** Path: lead three-quarter, slightly lower, then a short rise as they take the steps; last 1 s hold profile with the ring and a standing crowd behind. No tunnel. No pyro wall. Frame: face readable the whole time; robe catches the follow-spot. Crowd / light: capacity hall, people on their feet for the home fighter; house out; ring + follow-spot only. Wear: custom robe, boots, gloves on; hood drops on the last two steps. Feel: earned spectacle; same geometry as B, more heat. JUDGEMENT. Stadium helicopters/spidercams stay out [web:15].
