| Gabbett's caveat | use "with caution" for individual sports | | Gabbett16 |
| Critique | "no evidence supporting the use of ACWR"; the ratio creates artifacts | review | Impellizzeri20 |

**Verdict:** session-RPE × minutes is solid and validated in boxing. The ACWR thresholds were never validated in combat sports and are seriously disputed, so treat them as a game-feel rule only.

## Weak evidence
The best-supported numbers are Foster's session-RPE method, its validity in boxing (Uchida14), and Bosquet's taper rule (cut volume 41–60%, keep intensity). Even Bosquet's data come mostly from endurance sports. Boxing weekly AU totals rest on a single 12-athlete amateur study, and there is no published weekly AU data for pros. Pro day schedules and sparring plans are anecdotal. Clock times, recovery-day content, stretching, rest gaps, fighters per coach and coach supervision minutes were not found. The ACWR zones come from cricket and football, were never validated in combat sports, and are heavily criticised. Treat them as a design device, not a fact.
