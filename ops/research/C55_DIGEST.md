# C55 DIGEST — THE PATH

## Decisions

* Actual start ages, typical amateur records, crossover rates, transition ages, and conversion times are **UNKNOWN** unless a source gives a competition/access rule rather than a population claim.
* The game needs discipline history, not a replacement scalar: crossover is separate from turning professional, and prior wrestling/BJJ/boxing records do not become an MMA record.
* Grade is independent, issuer-scoped, and never derived from the seven stats. Real benefits are discipline-specific gates for competition, teaching, coaching, or officiating; portability is never assumed.
* Federation money remains evidence-gated under C54. Event prizes, grants, stipends, and purses are distinct; no payment was generalized into a rate.

## Three recommendations

1. **Q12 — grade:** authored recommendation: use a gym decision once sourced minimums make the athlete eligible to be considered. Attach an authored cost/consequence; defer failed exams until a discipline-specific evidence and runtime seam exists.
2. **Q18 — turning pro:** authored recommendation: warn immediately before accepting the existing signing or paid-bout trigger, show the exact one-way cause and losses, and require confirmation. Do not add a reversible professional toggle.
3. **Q20 — highest return:** authored recommendation: add one dated `career_path` history at athlete creation and append transition/grade/pro-door events beside existing seams. It connects entry, crossover, grade decisions, and professional conversion without merging records or inventing rates.

## Explicit non-decisions

No typical age, amateur record, crossover percentage, time-to-competence, universal federation restriction, grade conversion, purse/stipend/funding rate, or cross-discipline record merge was established. No grade was derived from stats. No claim that a single athlete’s timeline is typical. Q12/Q18/Q20 are authored design, not sourced findings. No code, JSON, test, UI, or `HANDOFF.md` was changed.
