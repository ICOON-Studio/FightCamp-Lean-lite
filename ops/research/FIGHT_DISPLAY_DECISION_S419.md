No hard fail. All three give "teaches" the lowest score, for the same reason: B is a good still, and a still teaches nothing. The hints are in the motion.

All three agree:
- KEEP the body and face that bruise where the punches land, the three tanks, the clock and exchange blocks, the one big call, the honest unknowns on the opponent.
- Too many numbers at one size. A number appears only where it matters now.
- The last call must show whether it is working.
- The scouted habit must be tied to the ring: it fires when the opponent does it.
- Cut the thrown and landed row from the live picture; it belongs in the corner minute. Cut the tank tick marks, the word FIGHT, the three "?" rings that show nothing.

Where they differ, and the call:
- The second full body on each side. New player: cut it, keep the face and the tanks, it competes with the boxers in the ring. Cornerman and feel: keep, the ribs need a place. CALL: keep ONE figure per side, head to waist (a bust), large enough that the face needs no separate close-up. Six places on one picture, half the panel.
- Numbers on the places. New player: only on hurt places. Feel: only what moved in the last 4 seconds is big. CALL: both. A place that is fine shows its colour only; a place under 55 shows its number; a place hit in the last 4 seconds shows its number big, then settles.
- The fight bar. Cornerman: round cards. New player: a crown on the leader. CALL: A's scorebug: the round's exchanges won as two digits beside the clock, three round cards under it filled in the winner's colour (striped while an estimate). No tug bar.

## 2. What is on the screen

1. TOP CENTRE, the scorebug (from A): the clock, round "2 of 3", the six exchange pips (winner's colour, the running one ringed), the exchanges won this round as two big digits (red 1, blue 2), three round cards.
2. LEFT, the club's boxer: name; a bust that takes the damage on its six places; three tanks beside it (energy with a bolt, legs with a boot, guard with a shield), each with a painted danger zone at the bottom in place of tick marks and a ghost line at the level it had at the bell; the plan in force as one chip.
3. RIGHT, the opponent, mirrored: the same bust, coloured only for damage that has been SEEN; tanks hatched with "?" until scouted or shown by the fight, a range once known; the scouted habit as a chip with two small glove pictures (what the opponent does, what follows).
4. BOTTOM CENTRE, the corner: the best call as one large plate with its effect drawn as arrows on icons (guard up, head hits down, energy down); two smaller calls; a ring that drains over the 30 seconds; beside it a small "last call" chip with a tick or a cross.
5. Nothing else. At least 58% of the picture is clear; the boxers are never covered.

Off the live picture: punches thrown and landed by kind (the corner minute and the result), exact numbers for fine places (hold a key, or the tooltip), the opponent's file (Fight prep).

## 3. The hints: what is true, how it shows, what the player does

Ranked by how much they change a decision. Timings are the feel critic's; every effect is gated by size (3 points on a place, 5 on a tank, never twice inside 2 seconds), so a flash always means something.

| # | True in the fight | How the screen shows it, no sentence | What the player does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The last call is not working (2 of the last 3 exchanges lost) | The "last call" chip turns from a green tick to a grey cross; the call plate's glow drops to a slow amber pulse for 3 s; a falling two-note. Never on one lost exchange. | Shout another call; change the plan line in the corner. |
| 2 | A punch just cost a place | A burst at the impact in the ring (0.25 s), a short dotted trail (0.45 s) from there to that place on the bust, and the place takes the hit as the trail arrives: a flash, a white ghost arc that holds 2 s, the number counting down. Head: a crack. Body: a thud. Only shots of 8 or more get the trail. | Hands up; Defence next week. |
| 3 | An eye is closing; the next clean shot may bring the doctor | The eye on the bust narrows through four drawn stages; under 35 its ring breathes amber, under 15 a red cross badge and a bell. Never a pop-up. | Hands up; the cutman's job in the minute; or accept the risk. |
| 4 | Body shots are adding up on the liver side | One dot per landed body shot stacks under the ribs; at three the ribs pulse once; the colour follows the band. | Elbows in; ice in the minute; train the body. |
| 5 | The opponent's habit just happened (scouted only) | The habit chip's two gloves glow for one beat, an eye icon pops over that glove in the ring for 1.2 s, and a gold line lights the call that answers it. Unscouted: a locked eye, nothing fires. | Draw the lead; slip and counter; write it into the plan. |
| 6 | The opponent is tiring | Sweat drops on the opponent's bust every 3 s while truly under 40; the mouth opens; the energy tank's range bracket slides down. Honest without scouting: it is what a corner sees. | Go to the body; let the hands go; push the pace next round. |
| 7 | The opponent's guard drops when tired (scouted only) | A blue ghost zone on the opponent's guard tank and the bust's glove sagging; unscouted the tank stays hatched. | Go for the finish. |
| 8 | Energy is draining fast | The tank's ghost bar holds 0.4 s then drains over 1.2 s; a chevron under the tank, one for slow, two for fast. | Breathe; tie up; a lighter plan next round. |
| 9 | What a call would change | Before the shout, a gold dashed forecast on the tank or place it would move. After it, the result against that forecast. | Pick the call by its effect, then learn whether it held. |
| 10 | The club's boxer is rocked | A red vignette at the screen's edge on that side for the length of the hurt, the bust's head swaying, the call plate jumping to the survival call. | Cover up; tie up. |
| 11 | Ten seconds left in the round | The clock turns gold and ticks; the exchange pips pulse; nothing else moves. | A last push, or stay out of trouble. |
| 12 | The round is slipping | The scorebug's digits: the leader's digit grows by one with a slide; at the bell the round card fills. | Change something in the minute. |

What must never animate: a place that is fine, a tank that moved under 5 points, the opponent's unknowns, anything while the count runs except the count.

## 4. The moments
- Knockdown: both panels dim to 40%, the count takes the centre, big; the downed boxer's bust shows the place that did it.
- Cut: a red line drawn on the bust where it is, a drip that restarts each exchange it bleeds.
- Rocked: hint 10.
- Corner minute: the panels slide toward the centre and become the corner card (the same busts, now with the punch counts and the three choices).

## 5. Honest answers this design depends on
- The fight tracks six places today: head, left eye, right eye, nose, left ribs, right ribs. This display shows all six. Jaw, temple, solar plexus and stomach do not exist in the rules; adding them is a rules change and a question for the operator, not part of this build.
- "Last call working", "habit fired" and the forecast of a call need the fight to report them per exchange. Check what LiveRound already exposes before the brief; if a hint has no data behind it, it is cut from the build, never faked.
