## Overall: 4.5 / 10

The dark top bar, the isometric gym, the bout screen and the character select are a game. Everything else sits on a cream "paper" slab that fills the screen, so seven of ten screens read as a web page or admin form at a glance.

## Hard fails hit (each caps the score at 5)
1. Reads as a web page / admin form at a glance: 05 Staff, 09 Fighter, 36c Records, 26 Money, 30 Rankings, 34 Scouting, 23h Playbook. This caps the overall at 5.
2. Unreadable text: small grey and dark text on the dark photo in 23h (the Push/Save help line, Combination line, the "Hover an answer..." line); 12-14 px prose on the Money tags and in the Rankings key; "Declined. Another offer will come." toast sitting over the Money content.
3. Two pages that look like different games: 07 / 12 / 02 (dark, photographic, big images, painted headings) against 36c / 05 / 30 (a cream paper form). 12 uses full-bleed colour, 05 uses none.
4. Main button: 07 Home has no main action in the page body at all (the way forward is only the "Needs you" slab, which is in the bar and looks the same as a label). 09 Fighter has "Open the offer" at the far right of a banner, far from what you are reading. In 36c the top red "Applicant" and "Needs you" are red but unrelated to the page. Not a total fail, but the primary call to action is weak.

## Which of the owner's web-page features are still visible, and where

- Pale watercolour walls: yes. The cream paper texture is the base of every page slab: 09 (all panels), 05, 36c (the whole screen is one cream slab), 34, 30 (tan wall behind the whiteboard), 26 (tan wall), 07 bottom panels.
- Page scroll bars: yes. A scroll bar on the right edge of 07, 09, 05, 26, 12, 23h, 34, 30 (the thin grey bar at x 1915, plus an inner bar on 23h at x 1467). Content is cut at the bottom of nearly every page.
- Pill chips: yes. The "RATE AGAINST" chips (walks you down...) and "Push / Steady / Save" chips on 23h; "Beats it / Works / In bouts" tags on 23h; WHO / WHAT filter buttons on 36c; the goal pill on 09 ("Goal, 21 days: still needs a fight booked..."); "Boxing / MMA / Lucy Jackson" pills on 30; "SESSIONAL ASSISTANT COACH" tag on 05; "Buy / Book / Hire" mini pills on 26.
- Outlined link-like buttons and tabs: partly gone (tabs are now filled slabs), but underlined blue links survive: 36c names in every row; 30 fighter names (blue, bold, linked) and the news names. The sub tabs (Overview / Week / Playbook / History, People / Money / Records, Roster / Find a fighter) are still a row of small flat rectangles that look like website nav, with a red underline for the active one.
- Label-left value-far-right rows: yes, strongly. 05 (Training quality ... 91%, 640 px of nothing in between), 09 Body ("Weight ... 61.4 of 60.0 kg"), 05 staff job rows, 26 "In, a week" lists, 12 VS card rows (those are centred, so acceptable).
- Explanatory text beside everything: yes. 05 every staff job has a grey description line; 07 banner ("Weight: 61.4 kg now..." repeated twice); 23h three paragraphs of help; 26 prose on every price tag; 02 four paragraphs on four cards; 30 the key and "Wide band: not scouted..." line.
- Nothing reacting when pressed: cannot be judged from stills. I can say that no button shows a pressed / focus / hover look in any still, and the buttons are nearly all flat dark slabs.

## To reach 10/10 (most important first)

Shared theme (one change, hits every page):
1. Kill the cream paper slab as the page. Make the page body a dark panel family (as on the top bar and on 12 / 02): a dark glass or leather panel with a thin coloured top edge, plus the cream card only for small items (a card, a ticket), never a full-width screen. Remove the paper texture. Shared theme.
2. One primary button style: big, warm red or gold, 56-64 px tall, glow and a bevel, with a pressed state (drops 3 px) and a hover glow, plus a click sound. Every other button is a clearly quieter slab. There must be exactly one red button per page. Shared theme (plus per page the choice of which one).
3. Hide the page scroll bar. Fit each page into 1080 px, or scroll with a mouse wheel and a fade at the edge, with no visible bar. Shared theme; pages that overflow (05, 26, 34, 23h, 36c) also need a page-by-page cut (see below).
4. Type: one display face for headings (the brush font is good) and a bold condensed face for numbers. Minimum body text 18 px, minimum helper text 15 px, never grey on a photo. Numbers big (28+ px) and in the stat colour. Shared theme.
5. Replace pill chips with state icons or coloured bars. Keep coloured edge bars for status. Tag text goes to a tooltip. Shared theme for the chip style; page by page for what goes into tooltips.
6. Tabs as game tabs: wide, angled or tab-shaped, with an icon, active tab lit (not a red underline). Sub-tabs the same, smaller. Shared theme.
7. Stat bars: thick (24+ px), skewed, with coloured gradient fills and a ceiling tick, a 28 px number inside or next to the bar, never a far-right number. Shared theme (one bar widget).
8. Remove underlined links everywhere: names become buttons or portrait chips with a hover glow. Shared theme (RichText link style).
9. Pressed, hover, focus and disabled states drawn for every control, plus a 80 ms squash on click. Shared theme.

Page by page:
10. 09 Fighter: big full-height portrait of the boxer on the left (not a 60 px thumbnail), rating ring, bars in a dark panel, "At a glance" as three coloured chips with an icon, the goal as a ring with the Book the bout button inside the page. Drop the yellow sticky note. See the mockup below.
11. 05 Staff: coaches as two big portrait cards with three numbers each and a single Hire button; drop "Cost a week / Net a week / To sign" as rows: show them as one price tag ($450 / wk) and one result icon (Training 91%). The job list becomes icon tiles, not rows with sentences.
12. 36c Records: the log becomes a trophy-and-news wall: honours as medals and certificates on a wall, fights as ticket stubs, the rest a short feed with icons and no hyperlinks. Filters as icon buttons (7, not 14).
13. 26 Money: three big numbers (cash, net a week, weeks left) and one chart; the price tags go to a shop shelf as icons with price, text only on hover. Remove the duplicate Staff list.
14. 23h Playbook: each situation row as a big card with the move icon, not "->". Put the help text into a hover panel in the empty right column (it already exists, so use it). Rounds Push / Steady / Save as three big toggle buttons per round.
15. 30 Rankings: a ladder with portraits, the player row highlighted, a coloured win-chance bar; the key and news into a drawer.
16. 34 Scouting: fight-card posters (portrait, name, record, 3 coloured bars) in a 3-up grid that fits one screen; one Invite button each.
17. 07 Home: replace the cream banner by a short dark strip with one red button ("Open the offer"); move "The Job" / "Since last Monday" into two small dark panels or a drawer so the gym is the whole screen.
