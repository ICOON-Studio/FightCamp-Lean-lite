# Scouting in stages - every opponent a puzzle to decode (proposal S340, for approval)

Operator S340: "each opponent needs to be a big deal to study and decode for the player, so the scouting happens in stages,
first eg you learn about his stances, records, power left hook, poor conditioning etc then the player needs to counter/fix
whatever he learns as time progress about the opponent and adjust his strategy to have success".

## Verdict: yes - it is the hook that makes the plan matter
A coach's plan is only as good as what he knows about the other woman. Today the plan board, the flows, THE READ and the
corner calls all answer an opponent whose file is mostly open from the start. Hiding it and letting the player dig it out
turns every booking into a small detective story, and every answer he builds (a plan arrow, a camp drill, a corner call)
into a bet on what he has found. Real camps work exactly like this: tape study for weeks, sparring partners picked to copy
her style, the corner reading her round by round.

## The opponent's file (hidden until found)
Each opponent carries 6-8 TELLS, each with a counter the player can build:
| Tell | Example | The counter it unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| Record and look | 4-2, southpaw, 165 cm, reach 166 | free at booking |
| Style / main game | pressure fighter | plan branch "if she presses: step back" |
| Best weapon | left hook to the body | camp drill: elbow tight + slip right; corner call "Hands up" |
| Weakness | fades after round 2 | plan: "when she tires: let your hands go"; conditioning camp |
| Habit + trigger | drops her right after a jab | THE READ counter (already in the fight) |
| Chin | goes down to the body | aim: body |
| Her corner | changes nothing / always switches to the body in round 3 | the corner's own plan (OPPPLAN1) |

## How the file opens (stages, each costs time or money)
1. **Booking (free):** record, stance, height/reach, club, style word.
2. **Tape study in camp (a camp day each, 2-3 sessions):** one tell per session, best weapon first, weakness last.
3. **A scout at her club show ($, one evening):** a habit with its trigger, how her corner talks.
4. **Sparring partner of her style (a camp week slot):** tests the answer: shows whether your counter works before the night.
5. **In the fight:** THE READ and the corner card find what is still hidden, round by round (already built).
6. **Rematch / the region's rivals:** her file stays found; she has also studied you (her plan changes).

Unknown tells cost: her plan punishes what you have not answered (OPPPLAN1 makes her corner plan by her style).
