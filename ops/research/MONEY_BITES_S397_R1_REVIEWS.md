# MONEY_BITES_S397 - round 1 critic reviews (of design v1, section 3 of MONEY_BITES_S397.md)

Three fresh critics, blind to each other. Condensed by Claude; numbers kept.

## Club owner / small-hall manager: 5.5/10
- Most items are one-offs: the pile returns in year 2. Lasting pressure needs recurring bills, lumpy risky income and a
  pro contract that can go wrong.
- Upgrades: floor $2,400 and strength corner $3,600 fair; ice baths unreal for a small club (a physio by the session,
  ~$60); changing rooms + showers in a lease are $20,000-35,000 (landlord consent, plumbing), not $6,000; a second ring
  does not fit a 1,500 sq ft unit (it means moving); fitness classes +$260/week is realistic or low; upkeeps too low
  (insurance alone ~$1,000-1,500 a year).
- Staff: assistants are often volunteers (unreliable, may quit) vs paid $280; S&C $150/week right; no small club pays a
  scout retainer (the coach goes to Saturday shows); cutman $100-190 at small halls, and a cutman stops cuts ending fights,
  he does not heal anyone.
- Youth: the backbone; 20 juniors at $25/month right; the hidden cost is compliance (DBS ~$60 an adult, safeguarding course,
  welfare officer); grants are applied for repeatedly with a chance of refusal; 35% a quarter (~80% a year) of a
  home-grown prospect is generous - 15-20% a year is closer to life.
- Pro share: contracts are fixed % for up to 3 years (BBBofC); money is lost at RENEWAL when a bigger firm calls; trainer
  10% stays; a domestic-title camp with paid sparring is $2,500-6,000.
- Missing: heating and power $300-750/month in winter (half in summer); liability insurance; medicals and pro licences;
  coach courses; travel to shows. Amateur stipends do not exist at a community club; an away bout earns the club nothing -
  a ticket cut exists only when YOU host the show.
- Ideas: host a club show 2-3 a year (venue $2,000, officials/doctor/ambulance $1,200, trophies $300; tickets $25 x
  turnout + raffle ~$1,000; net +$2-5k or a loss); white-collar event (+$6-12k, costs coach hours and purist standing);
  lease renewal or a move (rent x1.6, $5,000 to move, room for a second ring); pro contract with a renewal date (match a
  rival or lose the boxer); grant applications ($300 of time, 40-50% success, $1,250-6,250).

## Sim economy designer: 5/10
- Model: spare cash ~$4.9k on day 1 + ~$100/week; the list ($25k, $184/week upkeep) is owned by weeks 85-110, then cash
  piles at $10-15k a year and rising. The real pile is MEMBERS: target 48 + 0.6 x reputation, cap 400, rent fixed - at
  reputation 200, 168 members = $1,360/week dues vs $416 rent. "+10 carried" means nothing with no space cap.
- Hiring all four roles is ~$550/week against a $100 surplus: a cliff from the other side.
- Payback: youth section dominant (grant > price, a free prospect saves a $13k/year stipend, 82%/year); fitness kit dead
  as cash (-$30 with the assistant); changing rooms 98 weeks; sparring room the cheapest route to places; S&C, scout,
  floor, recovery close to dead in cash and overlapping.
- The falling share breaks success: gym net per bout unranked ~$750, rank 10 ~$820, champion ~$390.
- Fixes: youth grant $500 and a 6 h juniors' coach; fitness +$420/week; capacity as a hard cap; floor $1,600; scout
  $60/week; S&C $100/week; stacked injury cuts floored at x0.65; shares 25/22/20/18/17% with a camp bill of 6% of the
  purse so net rises with rank; home-grown 20% a quarter, potential +3, half stipend in year 1; kit wear repair $3 a member.
- Years 3-10: premises tiers (capacity 70/140/250, rent $1,800/$3,400/$5,500, moves $12k/$25k); promote a fight night
  ($8-15k, return 0.6-1.8x); camp budget per bout (basic free / full 8% of purse +1.5 / elite 15% +2.5); coach raises after
  a title (+12% or leave); rent review +8% every 2 years; pro medical cover $40/week or a $2-4k bill.
- Better bounds: 520 weeks; sensible cash above 6 x a fixed $4,870 month in at most 15% of weeks; year-5 surplus at most
  2.5x year 1; chosen spend at least 25% of income in years 3-10; no dominant item (three buying orders within 15% net
  worth); no dead item; net per bout rises with rank (unit test); letting staff go from $0 recovers in 12 weeks or less;
  careless closes 20-70%, median close after week 26.

## Football Manager / Two Point veteran: 5/10
- A fixed ~$26k list is cleared around weeks 70-90; the pile returns just as pro purses arrive.
- Upgrades: one-off and done; the best games use levels, build time, rising running costs and a rating you can see.
  Effects as hidden multipliers ("x0.80") are never felt.
- Staff as fixed people on fixed wages make hiring a switch; letting go free removes tension (one week's pay).
- Youth: the best idea - make it a dated intake with a staff preview ("this year looks strong") and a visible potential
  range, not a silent roll.
- Pro share: a share that drops by itself reads as a penalty; make renewal a talk (keep 22% and promise a title shot, or
  drop to 18%).
- Kit wear: repair clicks are a chore; a weekly kit budget (spend less, kit wears faster, injuries rise).
- Places: yes - a hard cap makes every signing count.
- Presentation: each upgrade row shows the price, upkeep, the one number before -> after, when it pays back, what it
  needs, and "Buy $3,600, leaves $9,200 (7 weeks of costs)". After buying: a week of fitters, then a running tally ("since
  bought: ~3 injuries avoided, +$1,820 from classes, 48% paid back"); the year review lists each purchase. Staff rows:
  rating in words, wage, "this month: 2 cuts closed, camp bill -$200"; "Let go, one week's pay".
- Ideas: upgrade levels (3 each) with build time; host a club show; camp spend per fight (cheap / standard / elite); staff
  search with candidates and ratings; club standing tiers that open sponsor levels.
