# A week worth playing: four combined mechanics (S425, Kalli)

**Answer first.** The week gets deep when pad nights buy more than one card: they buy the counter to the opponent who waits for your best line, and they fill the quiet week with a block you chose and a Saturday that trades tape with a rival. Four mechanics do it: **The read war** (R25 + R19 + R21 + R16, the counter to the waiting opponent), **Reid's evenings** (R55 + R73 + R46 + R15, the deeper pad nights, with the show night), **The quiet block** (R52 + R08 + R23 + R46 + R85 + R74, the quiet week), and **The cut thread** (R28 + R12 + R10, from R28 v3). Each one feeds the next: the pads make a card Sharp, Sharp opens the switch, the switch beats the read, and the read depends on what your boxer showed on tape in the quiet weeks.

Number tags: **REAL** (outside source), **GAME** (in the game or measured on its bout, with where), **INVENTED** (a design value to tune). Measured facts kept as given: the coach's hands bring a card one week sooner; an unready plan answer costs 2 to 14 wins in 100 (40.0% with all four, 18.7% with no plan); sharp over usable adds 1 point today; an opponent who waits for your best line costs 6.5 points and nothing wins it back yet (GAME: R55 and R25 maps).

**A quiet week (nobody booked).** This is where the game sleeps today: one click and morale ticks down (-6 a month with no fight, GAME 3).
- **Monday:** each boxer with no block is flagged "No block set" on Home. You pick one 2 to 4 week block per boxer (section 2.3) and see what it costs before the click ("Sharpen the 1-2: 6 pad nights, the morning stat goes untrained").
- **Tuesday to Thursday:** Reid's free evenings go to the blocks, or to a beginners' night for new members (R74). The Coach row shows who holds each night.
- **Wednesday:** World news names who rose in the regional table this week (R85). The open-spar invite for Saturday shows that boxer's tells still hidden on your file, and the price.

## 2. Four combined mechanics

### 2.1 The read war (R25 adapts + R19 tape + R21 test-it follows scouting + R16 combos + R31 the Why's change), the counter to the waiting opponent
The opponent's corner reads a line used enough (2+ uses a round, GAME PlanRead FREE_USES) and waits for it. New: **the switch**, the same line thrown off a different start (a feint, a jab draw, another place), built in Combos from a **Sharp** card. When the opponent waits for the line, the switch turns the wait into a wrong answer for them.

| Step | Decision | Each option, and the case where it's right |
|---|---|---|
| Camp (Fight prep) | How readable is your plan? | **One best line plus its switch:** right against a high Ring IQ opponent who reads often. **Two strong lines, spread:** right when no card is Sharp yet; reads come later because each line gets fewer uses. **One best line, no switch:** right against a low Ring IQ opponent (reads about 1 in 3 at 50) |
| The week (Training) | Pad nights on the switch, or on a missing plan answer | **Switch:** right when all four answers are already Usable. **Missing answer:** right when one isn't, because it's worth 2 to 14 in 100 against the switch's up to 6.5 times the read chance. Both numbers show on the drill card |
| Corner minute | The note: "Maria is waiting for your 1-2" (the R25 note, 14 words at most) | **Throw the switch:** right when it's drilled. **Draw the lead** (existing shout): right with no switch; it costs one of the two instructions that get through. **Rest the line a round:** right with 2+ rounds left; the read lapses after a round unused. **Keep it:** right only when the note says "may be waiting" (no tape study, no Ring general perk) against a low Ring IQ opponent |
| After | The Why: "Maria waited for the 1-2 after round 1; the switch landed 3 times: +X." The line goes **on your tape** | Not a decision; it's the lesson and the next stake |
| Career | Your tape: lines used 2+ a round in your last 2 bouts are on tape, so a corner that studies your tape reads them from round 1 | **Hide the line on a club show:** right when a regional final or a ranked night is inside the tape window. **Show it:** right when nothing big is booked and you need this win |

- **Anticipation:** the note in the minute ("will they read it?"), and the tape window before a big night.
- **Reward:** a Sharp card stops being +1 point and becomes a new answer. A line beaten by the switch gets a "beat the read" mark in the Playbook.
- **Menus:** Fight prep (a "Their corner reads" row: Ring IQ word, read chance, your lines on tape); Playbook > Combos (Build the switch, locked below Sharp with the reason); Playbook > Game plan (a tape mark on a line); the corner minute (the note and four options); the live fight (an eye icon "Waits for your 1-2"); the Why (the line above); World > rival file (the lines they have on your boxer).
- **Reuses:** PlanRead, the corner's "change one plan arrow", Combos, tape study, the shouts. **New:** the switch rule in the bout (M), the tape window (S), the note's certainty (S).

### 2.3 The quiet block (R52 goals + R08 perk road + R23 habits + R46 open sparring + R85 living world + R74 new joiners + R09 test day), the quiet week
A **block** is a goal you set yourself on a quiet Monday: 2 to 4 weeks, a deadline, and a test on Fridays (test day, built). A met block opens a decision, not a bigger number.

| Block | The price shown before the click | The case where it's right | What it opens when met |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sharpen a card | 6 to 8 of Reid's evenings | The boxer's likely next opponents read well (World > Rankings shows their Ring IQ words) | The switch (2.1) |
| Chase a perk | 3 weeks of mornings on one stat, the others untrained | The perk's need is about 3 points away (Ring IQ 65 for Ring general) | The perk: Ring general makes corner calls 25% better (GAME 3) |
| Fix a habit | 4 weeks, matching the starting focus (GAME 5.9) | The habit is on your tape (a rival file lists it) | The habit leaves your tape: rivals lose that tell |
| Rest and heal | No gains for 2 weeks | After a stoppage, high tiredness, or scar of 40+ | Morale +4 a rest week (GAME 3), scar -3 a rest week (INVENTED, R28 v3), and a fresh boxer for the next offer |

**The open spar (Saturday, Fights > fixtures).** One invite a week, three choices, each priced:
- **Invite a ranked rival** (one place above, a place to take by the rank rule, GAME 4.5): one of their hidden tells is found now, before any offer, but one of your boxer's lines goes on their tape. Right when an offer against them is likely in the next month.
- **Invite an unranked visitor** (free sparring, no tape either way). Right when the block needs sparring and no rival is worth the trade.
- **Decline:** no injury roll. Right in the fortnight before a block test, or with a sore boxer.

**The gym's evenings in a quiet week.** Reid's free evenings go to the blocks, or to a beginners' night (R74: new joiners stay). The beginners' night is right when members are falling ("dues too steep" or "less room" on the review). The blocks are right when a boxer's next offer is near.

- **Anticipation:** Friday's test line, Saturday's spar, and the Monday row that opens the reward. Two to four weeks of something set in motion, which is what the quiet week lacks today.
- **Menus:** Home (the "No block set" need; block progress per boxer on the fighter row); Overview (GOAL card shows the block); Training (the block's cards and mornings, starred); Fights > fixtures (the open spar invite with its trade); World > rival file (the tell found, the tape they hold on you); week review (the block's row, last); Club > People (beginners' night against the block on Reid's evenings).
- **Reuses:** goals, test day, perks, habits, the rival file, tells, members. **New:** blocks as player-set goals (M), the open spar (M), the beginners' night (S).

### 2.4 The cut thread (R28 cutman + R12 scars + R10 injuries + R27 trust), from R28 v3
In order: hire the cutman tier for the night (None / Club $50 or $150 / Seasoned $120 or $300 / Name $700, pro only), a Cutter tell that competes for a tape session, and the "Protect the eye" plan line, which takes a plan place. In the round, the cut is a hidden depth shown as a range that narrows with the tier, and from the next round the other corner **hunts** it. That's the read war again, aimed at the eye. In the minute: 3 cutman plus 3 coach points, then stop / survive / race, decided by the range, the cards and the cause. After: scar per eye, which raises the cut chance, and healing is a quiet block (Rest and heal, or a style change on the pads: Hands high as a drilled card).
- **The links:** scar decides which cutman tier you hire and which block you pick; the hunt is answered with the same "rest it" and shout logic as 2.1.
- **Numbers:** see R28 v3. All prices and depths are INVENTED. REAL: only an accredited person may work an amateur cut (England Boxing 2.4.1), and cuts are 51% of Nevada pro injuries (J55 3b). The head-clash rule wording is CHECK (John).
- **Menus:** Club > People (the tier); Fight prep (the Cutter tell); Playbook (the line); the live fight (band and range); the corner minute (CUT card); the after-bout report and profile (scar bar); Home (the healing block). **New:** CutRules (M), CornerHands (M), two screens.
