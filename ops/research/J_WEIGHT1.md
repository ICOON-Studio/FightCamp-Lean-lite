**Q2. Novice man 2–3% over the limit, offered a bout 2.5 weeks out**
| Guidance | What it says | Label | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| England Boxing weight-management guidance, 2024 | Lose at most 1 kg a week, and 0.5 kg a week is safer. Keep fluid loss under 1–2% of body weight. | Reale 2017 | With a same-day weigh-in, keep the short-term loss at or under about 5% of body mass. | MEASURED (recommendation) | [Reale 2017](https://www.gssiweb.org/docs/default-source/sse-docs/reale_sse_183.pdf?sfvrsn=2) |
| Arithmetic | For a 70 kg boxer, 2–3% is 1.4–2.1 kg. At 0.5–1 kg a week, 2.5 weeks covers 1.25–2.5 kg. | DERIVED | rows above |
| Coach's answer | Accept, and make the weight by diet alone with no water cut. Ask for the next class up only if the boxer is more than about 3.5% over, or it's the boxer's first cut and the scales are still high in the last week. A later date is rarely needed. | DERIVED | England Boxing guidance |
