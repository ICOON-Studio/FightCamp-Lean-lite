## What gets built (Claude's verdict on Ludo's design)
1. **Home promoters first.** A country with a verified promoter (two sources agree) books its boxers at home:
   UK (Matchroom, Queensberry, BOXXER), USA (Top Rank, Golden Boy, PBC), Japan (Ohashi, Teiken),
   Mexico (Zanfer), Russia (RCC), China (Max Power), Canada (Eye of the Tiger).
2. **Away circuit everywhere else (Cyprus, Greece, Georgia...).** The boxer gets 0-3 paid offers every few weeks
   from REAL promoters in a neighbouring market. Cyprus -> United Kingdom (Matchroom, Queensberry, BOXXER).
   Nothing invented: Ludo's test used a made-up "Spartan Ring Promotions" - dropped.
3. **What an away fight costs you:** 3-6 weeks notice, 1-3 travel days taken out of camp, and close rounds
   lean to the home fighter (a clear win or a stoppage beats it).
4. **Purse bands in words:** debut (4 rounds), prospect (6), contender (10). One plain line per offer, e.g.
   "Matchroom Boxing (United Kingdom) offers an away 4-round debut slot in 4 weeks (2 travel days)."
5. **Not taken:** Greek promoters (only one source named them; the second found none) - they join when a
   second source confirms them.
