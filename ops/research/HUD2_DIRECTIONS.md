# HUD2: three mood directions (each differs in ONE big idea)
All three share the zones described in HUD2_STYLE §7. What changes is the material and where the fighter lives. Concepts: previews/hud2_A.png, hud2_B.png, hud2_C.png (shown in the paused state).

**A "Fight-poster brass". Big idea: money and buttons are brass objects you could hold.** The resource cluster top right is a heavy chamfered brass-edged plate (2px `#c9a24a` on walnut), with the cash set in Bebas Neue at 38px beside a red `trending-down` arrow with a signed number. Fame, reputation, hype and members sit under it on 6px meters. The dock is four big 92px square walnut buttons (`#7a5232→#3a2414`) with brass edges and a 32px line icon. The lit one turns teal with a `#7fd0cf` underline. Next come three brass-framed 2×2 groups with count badges and a flyout chevron, plus the gear and the (i) at the far left. Objectives are chamfered walnut cards on the right with Bebas title strips. GUIDE is the teal one. *Readability at 1080p:* the best of the three. Opaque walnut at 91% plus a dark keyline holds over the bright floor, and brass on walnut keeps a strong value contrast. The risk is weight: the bottom band reads heavy if more groups are added. *Boxing?* Yes: fight-poster type, belt brass, the M34-A look the operator already picked.

**B "Clean broadcast". Big idea: the fighter is the show, as a TV lower-third.** The fighter leaves the top-left and becomes a broadcast strip above the dock: a teal name block (BASTIEN TORRES / 08:00 MORNING · PADS) cut on a slant into a dark-glass data strip (STA/STR bars with numbers), a FRESH plus move-card cell, and portrait slots for the multi-fighter future. Every plate is slim, borderless dark glass (`#0d0a07` at 78%, blur 14px), with a 4px teal accent bar on the left or top. That gives it scorebug manners. The resource cluster is a cash scorebug with a teal edge. Objectives are thin glass cards with small caps titles. The dock is 76px glass squares where the lit one gets a teal fill and underline. *Readability:* good for numbers. The glass and blur separate it from the floor, but thin 12px caps on glass go soft over the bright white walls, so keep glass at 78% or more and never go below 12px. It is also the most "generic sports app" of the three. *Boxing?* It reads as fight-night TV more than as a gym. That is strong for fight week and weaker for a sunny morning at the gym.

**C "Gym chalkboard / corner card". Big idea: the HUD is stuff pinned up in the gym.** Objectives become a walnut-framed chalkboard (`#24342e`, "TODAY ON THE BOARD") with chalk Bebas titles, hand-drawn boxes and dashed chalk dividers. The fighter is a taped-up paper sheet. The resource cluster is a cream corner card with a red corner stripe, like a fight-night scorecard, taped at the top. The dock is a rack of kit: a walnut rail with the four big buttons hanging from brass pegs as rounded red, blue and gold kit bags, with the smaller grouped buttons in walnut cubbies beside them. *Readability:* mixed. Paper and chalkboard are big, flat, high-contrast blocks and read well. The danger is that cream paper sits close in value to the gym's white walls, which needs the dark keyline and a shadow, and the chalk style must stay a font, not a texture over text. It is the most charming and the most costly to keep tidy as items grow. *Boxing?* Very much "a boxing gym" (corner card, kit, the coach's board), more gym than fight night.

**Recommendation (art director's view, EXAMPLE):** A as the base, B's lower-third for fight week only, and C's chalkboard as a skin for the objective stack if the team wants warmth.
