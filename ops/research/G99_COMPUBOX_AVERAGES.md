VERDICT — bands the game should target

Your current card (~25 thrown, 50–60% landed, ~20% jabs) is too quiet and far too accurate, and under-jabs.

Band	Target (average pro, per fighter / 3-min rd)	Why
Thrown	45–56 (HW ~45; all-class ~51–56; volume styles 70+)	CompuBox 45–56 + Puchol 50.8
Landed	13–17	Puchol 13.6; CompuBox 17
Total connect	27–32% (elite winners ~32–36; 50%+ is outlier)	Puchol / CompuBox
Jab share of thrown	40–44% (not 20%)	CompuBox 41%; Puchol 44%
Jab connect	16–22%	Puchol 16%; 2013 implied ~22%
Power share of thrown	56–60%	33/56 and 28.5/50.8
Power connect	34–38%	CompuBox 36%; Puchol 34.6%; MW ~38%
Jab share of landed	~25–30% (so ~1 in 4 landed is a jab, not 1 in 5 thrown)	Puchol 26.5%
Body % of landed power	do not invent	NOT FOUND
Late-round output	optional fade, not a fixed −X%	no league curve published
Amateur 3×3	~60 thrown / rd, same connect bands until you have amateur film data	Davis ~20/min

A “normal” CompuBox-style between-round card for an average pro: ~50 thrown, ~15 landed (30%), ~22 jabs / ~28 power, jab ~17% / power ~35%. Volume lower-weights and pressers sit above that; HW technicians and low-output A-sides sit at 35–45 thrown.
