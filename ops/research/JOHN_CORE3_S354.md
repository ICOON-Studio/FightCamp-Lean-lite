# John on the CORE3 core loop (S354, 2026-11-02, via gbot)

The S354 check is done. Most numbers hold; change running to 9.3, heavy bag to 5.8, the weekly cut to 0.45 kg, and fatigue kept per day to about 87%.

1. **Energy.** From the [Compendium 2024](https://pacompendium.com/running/): running at 10 km/h is **9.3**, not 9.8, which gives 425 kcal for 45 min at 61 kg (DERIVED). Intervals at 11.0 match vigorous HIIT ([Compendium 2024](https://pacompendium.com/conditioning-exercise/)). Weights at 6.0 is right only for vigorous lifting; ordinary 8–15-rep work is **3.5**. Heavy bag is **5.8**, not 5.5. The 7,700 kcal/kg rule overstates loss after a few weeks because the body adapts (Hall 2008*).
2. **Diet.** A 500 kcal deficit gives **0.45 kg/week** (0.75% at 61 kg), not 0.42. About 0.7%/week kept muscle better than about 1.4%/week (Garthe 2011*). I found no boxing-specific safe band, so 0.5–1% stays an ESTIMATE. Watch the low-energy warning signs in women, such as missed periods (IOC REDs 2023*). "Hold +300" is an ESTIMATE. The boxer weighs in **every bout morning**, one attempt, with the first bout at least **3 h** later ([World Boxing 2024](https://worldboxing.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/World-Boxing-Competition-Rules-Nov-2024-Approved-4.pdf)). A big water cut makes no sense with repeat daily weigh-ins, so model at most about 2% (ESTIMATE).
3. **Fatigue.** Session RPE × minutes is valid in boxers ([Uchida 2014](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3918568/)). Keeping 75% per night means fatigue fades in about 3.5 days. The standard Banister model fades fatigue over about 7 days and fitness over about 42 (Banister 1975*), which is about **0.87 kept per day** (DERIVED). Coaches also track monotony and strain, flagging monotony above 2 (Foster 1998*), and the ratio of the last week's load to the last month's, safe at 0.8–1.3 and risky above 1.5 (Gabbett 2016*). Your 80/100 threshold, ×0.6 gains and ×2 injury are ESTIMATE; tie ×2 injury to that ratio going above 1.5.
4. **Week.** Experts suggest 1–2 sparring days a week ([MLS Sport Res 2023](https://www.mlsjournals.com/Sport-Research/article/download/2277/1314)). Hard and rest days are UNKNOWN; a fair game value is 3 hard days and 1 rest day (ESTIMATE). Taper for 14–21 days, cutting volume **40–60%** and keeping intensity ([Boxing Science](https://boxingscience.co.uk/programming-for-boxing-the-taper/), an undated coaching article). In elite boxers, a 10-day taper brought the final week to 13–17% of peak load ([Bradley 2023](https://www.mdpi.com/2673-9488/3/4/43)).
5. **Sparring.** 3–4 rounds a session and 1–2 sessions a week gives **3–8 rounds a week** (DERIVED; [MLS Sport Res 2023](https://www.mlsjournals.com/Sport-Research/article/download/2277/1314)). When sparring stops before a bout is UNKNOWN in papers; 7–10 days is a common coaching ESTIMATE.

\* Cited from known literature; I haven't reread these papers yet.
