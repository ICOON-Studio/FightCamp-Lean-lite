# MENUDIR research S388: which games were praised or hit for their dashboards

Operator: "can you check what games have been praised for their dashboard presentation , what they right , what they did wrong".
Claude's own summary of web sources, 2026-11-06. Nothing here is copied from any game.

## Evidence quality
Strong: FM26 (many reviews, the studio head's own words), FM27 (news of the developer video), OOTP (reviews across versions),
MLB Front Office Manager (Game Informer). Medium: Motorsport Manager (several reviews, none about one screen), WCBM2 (two reviews).
Weak: Papers, Please (fan and commentary sites), CK3 tooltips (mostly previews). Pages that failed: Galaxus FM26, FRVR list, Overtake
F1 Manager 22; PC Gamer's Stellaris and Anno 1800 pieces returned titles only, so nothing is claimed about them.
Gap: no review found that praises a room-style hub in a management game. The office idea has no proof either way.

## Findings
- Football Manager 26: side menu replaced by top bars, dashboard cards, pop-ups. Navigation was the top complaint; reviewers said simple
  jobs took twice the clicks and that looks beat speed. Studio head: one interface for PC, console and phone was too clever.
- Football Manager 27: drop-downs reach sub-pages in one click, players set where a menu lands, custom bookmarks, messages coloured by
  priority, scout reports readable unopened, duplicates grouped, fewer cards.
- Out of the Park Baseball: called spreadsheet-like and easy to get lost in; a manager home with the important things in one place helped;
  later every main screen became customisable.
- MLB Front Office Manager (3/10): only one quick sort; the rest buried in menu layers, so comparing players was a chore.
- Motorsport Manager: clean, everything clearly labelled, depth without too many choices at once; simple options, stylish look.
- Crusader Kings 3: hover a term for its meaning; hints expand into hints. Learning curve still steep.
- Papers, Please: the desk works because the objects are the game: you handle them. Its clutter is deliberate stress.
- World Championship Boxing Manager 2 (6.5 and 4 of 10): calendar with unlabelled dates, no feedback on tactics, choices that change
  nothing, repetitive menu loop. Quick training scheduling was liked.

## For FightCamp
1. Keep one-click reach from anywhere (the left menu); the room must never be the only route.
2. Show the answer on the surface; colour by priority; group duplicates.
3. A room object must be used, not decorative. 4. Choices must change something (career goal needs real rules).
5. Let the player sort and compare fighters. 6. Avoid pop-ups for everyday jobs. 7. Later: player-set shortcuts.
