Training Scene Critique — S457
1. LOGIC CHECK
No impossible actions or contradictory numbers found on the screen: weight cut, stat gains, mastery progress, fatigue values, injury chance, win probability, and week phases all align with the rules in CRITIC_PACK.md.
Names: All fighter names appear only in the top portraits/header area — no names leak into options, buttons, or tooltips. Rule followed ✅
Minor clarity gap: The 51% → 53–55% range shows the player’s improvement, but it is not explicitly stated that the range accounts for the opponent’s hidden tell — this is explained in the text but not labelled directly on the screen.
2. USABILITY & CLARITY — Score: 7/10
How easy to learn: Drag-and-drop sessions are intuitive — within 5 seconds you see “drag icon → day → see number + fatigue change”, comparable to Football Manager training in clarity, cleaner than Boxing Manager’s dense tables, more readable than Punch Club’s busy bars.
Art & icons: Session icons are clean and functional, but do not yet match the Playbook’s ink-figure style — they look like generic circular icons rather than hand-drawn ink art. This is the biggest visual disconnect from the rest of the game (camp plan photos + Playbook ink figures).
Readability: The week layout is clear — mornings/evenings stacked per day, fatigue tank below, gains on each tile. However:
“What does this session actually improve?” needs a hover — it is not instantly obvious that the Roadwork icon = Stamina, Intervals = Conditioning, etc.
The “NIGHTS: Light/Standard/Hard” bar is a little abstract — it does not visually map to which evenings change when you toggle it.
Multi-fighter handling: Switching between the four at the top works well — you always know whose week you are editing. No “save/switch” friction.
3. DECISION DEPTH — Score: 8/10
This is a genuine trade-off system, not a “one correct answer” simulator:
✅ Hard = bigger gains → higher fatigue → higher injury risk + worse recovery
✅ Shift focus → one stat climbs faster → others slow down
✅ More pads = faster move mastery → less sparring → Fight IQ grows slower
✅ Cut weight = makes the limit → slower recovery + smaller stat gains
✅ The hidden tell means you can never be 100% certain — you plan with incomplete information
Only minor weakness: There is no visible “plan B” or quick compare — you cannot easily see “what if I do this vs that” side by side without undoing your changes.
