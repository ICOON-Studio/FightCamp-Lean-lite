# G110_ROOMS_MEDIA_SCOUTING

A gym owner does not buy "amenities." He buys throughput (more training days before a body breaks), retention (fighters who do not quit or leave town), and recruiting (a reason to relocate). A PR hire is bought for attention that converts (tickets, sponsors, card placement), not follower count. A head coach keeps an opponent file because habits outlive highlight reels; he treats old tape as suspect and recent tape as law.

## What a human does

**Rooms.** Owner prices square footage + wet work (drain, 240V, hood, code) against missed camps. Recovery suite pays if it cuts injury weeks. Dorms pay if they pull out-of-town talent and keep them in the building 14 hours a day. Kitchen pays if it stops dirty cuts and food-poisoned camps. Film room is cheap and is how Jackson-Wink sold itself as a strategy gym. Media office only pays after you have a name to film. Wrong order: kitchen/dorms before mats are full; recovery toys that nobody books; media studio with no producer. Payback is months for a used plunge + sauna if the roster uses it daily; years for housing if occupancy is thin. What goes wrong: mold, chiller failure, roommate fights, health-code kitchen, empty bunks.

**Media hire.** Weekly: calendar, short-form, fight-week clips, press lists, sponsor decks, crisis mute. Amateur/regional is a freelancer or split manager; elite is a retainer plus a cut. Return is slow then stepwise (one viral clip, one sold local card, one apparel deal). What goes wrong: posting without a hook, inflating vanity metrics, fighter who will not sit for content, scandal that the hire cannot contain.

**Scouting file.** Coach pulls last 3-5 bouts first, older tape only to see if the man can change. Notes: stance, go-to series, what he reaches for when hurt, cardio fade, TDD, guard drop, clinch side. File stays useful ~6-18 months on tendencies; specific setups rot faster after a camp with a new coach. What goes wrong: training the 2023 version of a 2025 fighter; drowning the athlete in 40 hours of tape; treating one fight as a census.

## 2. Media hire

Weekly work: calendar, vertical clips, live-window posting, athlete collabs, sponsor assets, community/DMs, weekly KPI deck. SOURCED assessment.com 2026-09-25.

**Pay.** Sports SMM employee $45k-$85k; senior $75k-$120k+ (assessment.com 2026-09-25). Combat SMM job (JP) ¥4.2M+ (~USD 28k+) (Indeed.jp 2026). BJJ athlete SMM freelance $10-$40/hr, <30 hrs (Upwork 2026). MMA manager (broader than PR): 5-20% of purse/earnings; high-% deals include content + media training; avg ~$6k-$25k per fighter per year on mid purses (groundedmma.com 2022-12-23). Pop-MMA PR package (RU) 200k RUB / 3 months - PROXY, not US elite.

**Measurable effects.** UFC org-scale 300M+ followers, 26B video views - promotion, not a gym hire (emplifi.io 2025-05-22). Regional truth: fans who buy tickets > follower count; sub-20k accounts still sponsor if engagement is real (mmafutures.com 2026-08-26; mymmanews.com 2025-08-19). A 500k-engaged fighter can out-earn a higher-ranked quiet one on digital deals (yorkshirestpauli.com 2026-05-28). Top-end sponsored IG post (McGregor, 2023 study) ~$153k (planetsport.com 2023-05-03). Karate Combat agency case: 250k->800k IG in a year - paid org campaign, not one staffer (brand-knew.com 2026-04-24).

**Games.** FM: no "PR staffer who prints hype"; press officer + manager media handling; pressers move morale; opposition reports are a staff product (fullerfm.com 2025-05-22; SI FM24 manual 2023-10-12). Hollywood Animal: PR department, monthly wage, research (reputation / charity / outreach); staff a cost center with a % bonus (YT guides 2025-06-29 / 2025-11-19). WWE 2K MyGM: tweets, promos, invasion promo - hype is a booking action, not a hired media officer (2K Ringside 2026-02-16). Motorsport Manager / Punch Club 2 / Cage Legacy: no sourced PR-staff loop - PROXY: flavor or omit. Players use FM pressers as a morale lever, not a growth engine (JUDGEMENT).

## 3. Scouting file

**How coaches build it.** Gather as much tape as exists; last 12 months first; >2 years = caution (style can rewrite). Watch for patterns: go-to series, panic tool, what has already gotten him hurt (invertedgear.com 2018-07-24; The Athletic 2020-11-27). Krause: trends across fights "probably not going to change too much" (same). Cormier: early self-scout, then coaches drill the tell (ESPN 2019-06-30). Jackson: notebooks, game trees, logged tape - the file is a gym asset, not a camp sticky note (mixedmartialarts.com 2012-08-15; Bloody Elbow 2017-01-18).

**Hours.** Fight Mind pitches 20-40 hours tape -> 20-minute cited report (fightmind.ai 2026). Betting primer: 2-3 hours as a floor for last 3-5 fights (shurzy.com 2026-09-13).

**Validity / credit.** Tendencies persist; specific entries die after a new camp or a lost limb of the game. One old fight can be a trap. Credit is real but unquantified (JUDGEMENT on magnitude; SOURCED on method).

**Games.** FM: Next Opposition Scout + Performance Analyst; reports land 1-2 days pre-match; knowledge % rises with assignments and rots without coverage; staff Judging / Tactical / Presenting Data gate quality (passion4fm guides 2020-2024) - the right analog: persistent, staff-gated, decaying knowledge, not a one-camp buff.

## 4. FightCamp table (designer numbers)

Scale: mid gym, not UFC PI. One effect per room. Build is one-off; upkeep weekly.

| Room | Build | Weekly upkeep | One effect | Payback (sim) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Recovery suite | $12k-$25k | $80-$180 | -15-25% injury days / +1 recovery tick per assigned fighter | 8-20 weeks if roster >= 6 uses it |
| Kitchen / nutrition | $18k-$40k | $120-$300 + food | Weight-cut fail -40%; energy/recovery +small; food-poison event rare but brutal | 1-2 camps |
| Dorms (4-8 bunks) | $25k-$60k (or unlock + furnishing) | $40-$80 per occupied bunk | Recruit range +1 region; morale +; quit-from-rent -; roommate incident risk | Occupancy > 50% |
| Film room | $4k-$10k | $20-$40 | Scout file fill rate +40%; camp gameplan quality +1 | Immediate if film study exists |
| Media office | $6k-$14k | $30-$60 | Required to hire Media; content quality cap +1 | Dead without the hire |

**Media hire (new staff type, not a coach).** Wage: amateur/regional $400-$900/wk; good $1.0k-$1.8k/wk; star-retainer $2.0k-$3.5k/wk or 8-15% of that fighter's off-cage cash. Weekly effect per assigned fighter: hype +2-5; followers +0.5-2% of current (diminishing); ticket modifier +5-15% on home cards; sponsor roll +small if hype >= threshold. Needs Media office. No assignment = wage burned. Fail states: scandal spreads faster; inactive fighter (no camp, no fight) yields ~25% effect.

**Scouting file (per opponent, persists).** Fields: stance; last-12-mo record slice; go-to series (2); panic tool; cardio fade round; TDD / guard drop / rear-hand drop flags; clinch side; notes; confidence 0-100; last_updated_week. Fill: Film room + coach hours (or camp film action), +15-30 confidence per studied bout, cap 90; no film room: half speed, cap 60. Decay: -4 confidence / month idle; -20 if opponent fights someone else; -35 if opponent changes gym. Below 40: flags 50% chance to be stale (wrong tell). Below 20: flavor only. Combat use: confidence >= 60 and a flag matching a player tool gives a first-time exploit bonus (accuracy or setup chance); never an auto-win; refresh in camp to keep it live.

**Build order that matches the humans:** Film room -> Recovery -> Kitchen -> Dorms -> Media office + hire. Scout file first (cheap, every fight). Media last (needs a face). Dorms only after you have people who would move.
