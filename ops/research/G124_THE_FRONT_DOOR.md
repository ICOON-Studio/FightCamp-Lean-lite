# G124_THE_FRONT_DOOR
Grok (Expert, 98 sources), collected by Claude S91 from grok.com/c/43ff8546-b4c3-4775-9eb7-7ef68d615c0e.

Walk-in is a sales funnel: door -> desk greeting + waiver -> wall of belts/photos -> trial class -> close or leave.
Small clubs run it with the owner. Academies staff a desk.

## 1. Lobby, m2, who stands there
**Small club.** Reception is a desk at the door, not a room. Typical reception slice 15-20 m2 inside a 80-150 m2 hall.
Bench, waiver clipboard/tablet, maybe a merch crate. Owner or a coach between classes. SOURCED
gymrehberi.com/brans/boks-salonu-acmak, seedoosports.com (40 m2 boxing gym design case). PROXY from French MA-school
planning: reception ~15 m2 + visitor chairs ~20 m2 + merch ~15 m2 (borilastvo.com, Budo International 460). Compact
boxing layouts put the desk on the street door (youtube WwvrhGVEebI, ~149 m2 hall).

**Established gym.** Front desk + sign-in scanner, pro shop, trophy case, benches. ATT tour: desk, logo, merch shop,
trophy case as first beat (youtube qg4WUajFG5c). Open Mat: "Help / Front desk and sign in scanner"
(openmatacademy.com/facility). Pentagon MMA: lobby coffee + pro shop + check-in (youtube CgsYbT-KjLM). Fight Centre:
staff greet 10-15 min early, waiver, gear, tour, hand-off to trainer (tfcgym.com.au/faqs). Total floor 250-600 m2
common (Matrix Gold Coast 600 m2). Boxing planning floor: 2,500 sq ft min / 4,000-6,000 full (startpermit.com).

**Academy.** 1,000 m2+ (Soul Fighters HQ 12,000+ sq ft). Dedicated desk staff, kids club, locker rooms, medals on the
wall (youtube 4L_Jlcln9MU). Paid receptionist holds the door so coaches stay on the mat. JUDGEMENT: the hire appears
when the owner cannot greet and teach the same hour.

## 2. Walk-in funnel, convert, stay
**Offers.** Free first class: Fight Ready, MMA District, KO Zone. Paid: $20 / 1 week (ThinkTank, Matrix), $29 / 2 weeks
(Marcus Soares BJJ), $30 / 3-day all-access (Syndicate LV), $30 / 2 classes (Fight Academy SG), $25 trial month (DC MA).
Paid trials filter intent; Gymdesk and Zen Planner both push structured/paid over open free.

**Trial -> member.** No single combat census. Fitness/vendor band: completed-trial convert ~20-30% average, 30-45% with
a protocol, 10-20% open-door no follow-up (rework.com, strikingweb.design, stackmatix.com). FitBudd MMA: 12-15% typical
of people who try a class; 20%+ top; 50-60% only with a sales process. Martial-arts vendors claim 60-80% with one class
+ same-day close - a sales-school ceiling, not street walk-in. Paid $19-29 two-week intros quoted 35-50% (PROXY,
growth blogs).

**Stay.** Gymdesk 2026 martial-arts benchmark: ~1 in 5 new members quit inside 90 days; ended memberships last ~3
months; after that 2-3.5%/month drip; kids-program gyms avg tenure 5.3 months, adults-only 4.8. PushPress: 50-70% of
white belts gone by month 6. One-gym BJJ log (54 tracked): 19% of the 18-month-4.5-year cohort still training. HFA
2025 fitness annual retention 66.4% (PROXY, not combat). French club note (thin): BJJ ~24 months mean stay, striking ~8.

## 3. What moves conversion
- Follow-up, not the walls. Structured trial + visit-triggered close lifts 30-35% -> 45-55%. Open trials with no
  follow-up die at 10-20%.
- Show-up is half the leak: no-shows 40-50% without a confirm call.
- First class + coach hand-off (arrive early, waiver, tour, loaner gear, intro to the trainer).
- Paid intro raises intent vs free.
- Attendance after join predicts stay: rare visitors cancel 44% over 24 months vs 23% for 3+/week.
- Belts/portraits at the door are the Kronk move (G122) - prestige, not a published lift. JUDGEMENT. Cleanliness and
  greeting-by-name are operator dogma; no combat RCT.

## 4. Desk wage and when hired
US: BLS receptionists median $18.27/hr (May 2025); gym front desk ~$16/hr avg (Salary.com); Gold's $15-19/hr; Houston
BJJ desk $15/hr part-time. UK ~GBP 13-15/hr. EU PROXY: DE EUR 28-34k/yr, FR ~EUR 1.9-2.3k/mo gross, NL EUR 26-32k/yr.
**When:** under ~100-200 members the owner or a coach covers the door. A part-time desk is the first non-coach hire
when greet/phone/waiver steals class time. Combat studios often stay owner-desk longer than big-box gyms.

## 5. JUDGEMENT for FightCamp
- Five lobby pieces: street door, front desk + waiver/sign-in tablet, the G122 wall slot (belts, founder portrait,
  champ photos), bench, merch rack. Trophy case only once the gym is "established". Skip the coffee bar.
- **Conversion, capped.** Street walk-in -> completed trial: 50-70% if greeted. Completed trial -> member: **25% base /
  40% cap** if the desk is staffed + the first class is coached + follow-up fires. Do not use vendor 60-80%. Member
  half-life ~5 months mean; the 90-day cliff eats ~20%. Reputation and class quality move the rate; the wall score does
  not mint infinite bodies.
- **Desk hire.** Part-time, $15-18/hr US / GBP 13-15 / ~EUR 12-16. Buys: greet, waiver, trial book, same-day follow-up,
  merch. Does not buy extra mat capacity. Fire the hire when walk-in volume is too low to cover the wage.
- **ONE mistake:** a lobby that multiplies members without a cap. Door traffic is finite; conversion is a rate on that
  traffic; retention cliffs at 90 days. A pretty desk with empty classes is a cost centre.
