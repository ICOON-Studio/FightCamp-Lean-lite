Gaps / use notes
Two-person moves: clinch, takedowns, throws, guard work — only use takes where both bodies stay in frame for the whole technique; re-scrub if the camera pans tight.
Cage clinch: Couture / cage-dirty-boxing clips are usable but often ¾-front; step frames carefully for feet.
Karate reverse punch: clean long side-on 50–60 fps demos were scarce in this pass; use the two kihon links and prefer the 60 fps mae-geri for kick timing.
Same rule as K55: read travel in shoulder widths / stance lengths, not invented centimetres from pixels.
