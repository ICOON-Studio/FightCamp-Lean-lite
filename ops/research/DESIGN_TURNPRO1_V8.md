# Turning pro — design map v8

Boxing gym management, from the first call through the debut ledger. The existing pro ladder is a handoff, not a new mechanic or screen. **CODE** means installed, even where a source comment calls the constant an estimate. **ESTIMATE** means proposed or illustrative, not shipped or verified in play. **CUT** means do not build or show.

## 1. What the game does today

- **CODE — `Roster.gd:185–213` (`pro_ready`, `check_pro`), `ProOfferWire.gd:24–39` (`make`), `ProCall.gd:331–337` (`calls_open`, `expires`):** One live offer for the gym. No call for a pro, a boxer with a booked bout, or a boxer in an active championship. First call: 7 wins and regional top 3, 9 wins at any rank, or a national final. No separate day-job gate. At age 32 or older no call with zero declines; return calls after a refusal bypass that age check. The offer lasts 14 days.
- **CODE — `ProDebut.gd:27–32` (`tickets_sold`), `ProOfferWire.gd:19–20,24–37` (`forecast`, `make`), `ProCall.gd:63–69` (`who_calls`):** Base sales are 20 + half the members rounded down + 2 per amateur win + 10 per title, plus club-show followers in the wrapper, capped at 100. At 45 or fewer only the guarantee house calls; 46–74 both; 75 or more only the ticket house. A recorded refusal locks the returning house instead. The offer stores the call-day forecast; `Roster.gd:246–250` (`debut_night`) recomputes base sales on the night.
- **CODE — `ProCall.gd:35–59,99–120` (`promoters`, `debut_purse`, `money`):** Ticket house: $1,500 base, $25 off per sale short of 60 (shortfall floor $500), half of $50 per ticket above 60; 4 weeks, opponent gap +1.0. Guarantee house: $1,800 guaranteed, 30% above 70, 6 weeks, gap +1.5. Positive gap means easier: `Roster.gd:303–310,320–321` (`turn_pro`, `debut_day`) subtracts it from no-camp fight power at signing and books Saturday. `WeekPhase.gd:43–51` (`phase`) places Taper in fight week.
- **CODE — `ProCall.gd:112–139` (`money`), `ProOfferWire.gd:117–140` (`money`, `night`), `ProDebut.gd:18–24` (`start_costs`), `Roster.gd:222–227,274–300` (`signing_share`, `share_of`, `turn_pro`), `Loyalty.gd:230–233` (`pro_share`):** $230 licence, $875 initial medicals, $140 if the trainer needs a licence. Gym cuts of purse and ticket share are separately rounded. Usual signing cut is 25%, limited by the existing deal and by the 10% trainer cut under 50 loyalty. Signing ends the stipend. Weekly comparison in the card is stipend saved minus $75 of assumed lost amateur cut, not a weekly charge.
- **CODE — `ProCall.gd:112–123,263–284` (`money`, `_costs_entry`), `ProOfferWire.gd:107–140` (`signed`, `money`, `night`):** Worked from the formulas, holding forecast/night sales at or below 70, first costs $1,245 and gym cut 25%: plain guarantee costs $1,245 now, pays the gym $450 later (net −$795), boxer $1,350. Covered costs at the $100 fee subtract $1,345 before splitting: $455 gross, gym $114 and boxer $341. The gym gains $909, not just cash runway, and the boxer loses $1,009. At the $200 fee the gym gains $884 instead. At $230 costs the same comparison gives gains of $148/$123. The fee is $200 below 8 runway weeks, otherwise $100. There is no special loyalty or morale charge for taking this ask (`Roster.gd:264–300`, `turn_pro`).
- **CODE — `ProCall.gd:99–123` (`debut_purse`, `money`):** At 74 the ticket house pays $1,850 gross, the guarantee house $1,860, not $1,800. At 46 the figures are $1,150 and $1,800. Up to 60 the shortfall shrinks; past 60 the ticket house gains $25 per seat, and past 70 the guarantee house gains $15 per seat. Plain guarantee therefore leads on gross throughout the overlap.
- **CODE — `ProCall.gd:151–284` (`asks`, `ask_answers`, `_apply_later`, `_apply_softer`, `_split_entry`, `_price_entry`, `_costs_entry`), `ProOfferWire.gd:66–96` (`answers`, `ask`, `back_to_plain`):** One accepted ask/counter per house. Later adds 2 or 4 weeks for $150 or $300 off the base (base floors at zero here, distinct from the ticket shortfall floor). Softer adds 0.5 to the gap and two tune-up sets. Split takes at 20 over break-even (+10 percentage points and 0.5 harder), counters at 5–19 (+5 points), otherwise refuses. Own price is ticket-house-only: $40 and 15 extra sales subject to capacity; the calculation already reads the stored price. Covered costs is guarantee-house-only. Returning to plain after a counter leaves the ask spent.
- **CODE — `Roster.gd:185–195,325–335` (`pro_ready`, `decline_pro`), `ProOfferWire.gd:24–37,166–187` (`make`, `on_decline`, `lapse_if_due`, `waited`), `ProCall.gd:73–95` (`terms`), `Loyalty.gd:120–159` (`move_for`, `apply`, with causes at :61):** Stay amateur clears the offer, records the first card's house and title count and costs 8 loyalty. Used/left: 0/3 first-call rules; 1/2 requires 9 wins and top 3 or 11 wins at any rank; 2/1 requires No. 1, no minimum wins; 3/0 closes the door. Terms multiply base, step and top by 0.9 or 0.81 unless a title was added since the latest refusal. Lapse instead clears the offer and sets `pro_wait_w` to wins at expiry +2, with no refusal, loyalty or morale penalty. An existing offer makes `check_pro` return even on a lapse tick.
- **CODE — `Needs.gd:62–65,134–164` (`needs`, `blockers`, `flags`), `Main.gd:195–208,282–292` (`on_go`, `_unhandled_input`), `Clock.gd:71–86` (`step`, `stop_now`), `Sim.gd:293–414` (`end_week`):** Offer is a red need and red flag. Continue dispatches the first blocker instead of starting the clock. Esc can close the unlocked offer modal, not clear the offer or unblock time. The clock also stops for blockers while running. Simulation has an expiry path via the weekly check, but closing the modal is not a playable countdown today.
- **CODE — `NextStep.gd:15–69,93–137` (`road`, `_need_for`), `FighterPage.gd:323–339` (`_road_input`):** S531 already passes `pro_wait_w`, checks it before marking the offer step done and gives remaining wins when that step is lit. The decline-adjusted text already exists. The road is still based on readiness, not whether a live offer belongs to this boxer; it can light a prior unfinished step and the unranked sentence can print “1 more wins.”
- **CODE — `Recruit.gd:122–126` (`ambition`), `ProCall.gd:298–328` (`morale`, `ambition_kind`, `age_line`), `Roster.gd:274–277,325–332` (`turn_pro`, `decline_pro`):** Title ambition can cost 5 morale on signing when the championship eligibility check is true; staying does not trigger that title cost. The +5 sign / −5 stay branch for pro ambition has no matching recruit text. The age line from 29 warns about first calls; at 32+ it can appear even on return calls.
- **CODE — `Roster.gd:293–310` (`turn_pro`), `BoutFinish.gd:56–117` (`run`), `ProLadder.gd:220–226,272–281,336–358` (`offer_axes`, `offer_rows`, `book`), `Matchmaking.gd:215–225` (`pay_of`), `ProWeekLines.gd:46–55` (`next_line`), `ProLean.gd:12–48` (`extra`, `line`):** The debut pays the gym from the debut deal. Later pro bouts use the booked ladder purse, calculated from matchmaking's unranked base and rank/draw additions; matchmaking also computes fan tickets. Contract purse, step and ceiling are stored and the purse ticks up on non-debut pro wins, but ladder pay ignores them. The promised win step is not live pay. What does carry over is the extra step-up or tune-up in early offers.
- **CODE — `ProOffer.gd:22–79,103–148` (`build`, `_house`), `ProCall.gd:112–147` (`money`, `pays_back_line`):** The card calls net signing-plus-debut money “at signing”; the footer shows signing costs even for covered costs. Payback can print “1 weeks” or imply ranking guarantees recovery, even when the debut is already cash-positive.
- **CODE — `FighterPage.gd:97–149` (`_tab`), `MoneyPanels.gd:282–287` (`_earns`), `RosterTable.gd:44–47` (record and road row):** The Fighter status line can still show the full club stipend for a pro, Money can project the amateur $450 cut for a pro booking, and the roster labels the amateur record “pro.”

## 3. What to ask for? One change to the deal

- **ESTIMATE — Cover signing costs (guarantee only):** Rename the ask and keep the installed fee tiers, but repay principal plus fee **only from the gym's pro fight cut**. On signing save the balance and fee on the boxer; pay $0 from the bank. At debut and each paid ladder fight: compute each side's full cut as on plain terms, repay `min(balance, gym cut)`, credit only the remainder to club cash, and store the new balance. Do not estimate a fixed payoff fight or forgive the balance after an arbitrary number of bouts. No repayment from stipends, dues or boxer money. On departure or retirement collect any unpaid balance from the gym, disclosed up front, not from the boxer. Existing signed covers settle by their old deal version. Example **ESTIMATE**, at $1,650 and 25% the debut pays the gym $413 before repayment; at $1,245 costs and $100 fee that leaves $932 owed and the boxer keeps $1,237, exactly as on plain terms. Plain is cheaper by the fee once repaid; cover is for a gym that needs cash today. No loyalty penalty: there is no transfer to the boxer. Show actual fee, debt left after debut and bank-now difference, not a fixed dollar callout.
- **ESTIMATE — Own ticket price (ticket only):** Keep the installed price/sales rule, show the forecast and the live change in gross, gym cut and boxer pay, and compute using the same payoff helper as settlement. Holding base sales fixed at 74 gives $2,080 gross vs $1,850 plain. Sales uplift clips above 85. At 92 it ties ($2,300 each); at 95 it loses $75 gross. This is the crossover in a same-sales comparison, not a forecast of future growth. Price can lose on the actual night even if it wins on the call day. Show the crossover before committing, including a warning when the debut estimate straddles it. Do not hide a credible downside inside hover text.
- **ESTIMATE — Later debut (either):** Keep only +2 weeks, at **$100 less debut base**; do not reduce the ladder base. Show it when those weeks can change the week plan (e.g. an existing weight plan reaches class limit with the extra time but misses on the plain date, or a known recovery date would cut into plain camp). Use existing weight/recovery inputs or omit the option if no actionable difference can be shown. No guaranteed healing or extra Peak. Show both Saturdays, extra Build time, forecast sales range and dollar cost. Hold current members constant for one scenario and project weekly member drift with current reputation, price and cap for another; member losses are possible. Ticket gains alone do not justify keeping the option. A ready boxer with a tight bank can keep the earlier date or take the house-specific ask.
- **CUT —** Softer duplicates the safer house and its later lean; better split overlaps price or hides a harder matchup; Later +4 adds little over a single useful delay. Keep old saves readable. No outdoor venue/weather, no new harder-gap bonus for taking extra prep. One accepted ask per house; back to plain after a counter does not reset the ask.

## 4. When to turn? Sign, hold the offer, or free the amateur calendar

**ESTIMATE —** Make the live offer a non-blocking amber need **and** flag. `Review later` and Esc both close the modal without creating a refusal or changing terms. Keep reopening through the existing Home or top bar. Before blocker checks on each simulated day, clear due offers once and record lapse; also reconcile stale offers on load and before signing or asking. No red zero-day blocker, no automatic modal and no same-tick replacement. Keep the 14-day window and 2-win wait from expiry. Do not reset these by reopening or reloading.

**ESTIMATE —** While the offer is live, reserve that boxer's calendar: training and other boxers' bouts continue, but accepting or asking for an amateur bout, committing a home-show slot, and entering nationals for this boxer require answering the offer first. Enforce at the booking/entry write path as well as in the menu; show “Answer the pro offer to book” with a link to the existing offer. Repeat the booked-bout/active-draw checks before signing. This is the price of holding the offer, not an invisible loyalty tax. If an entry closes tomorrow or an amateur date needs booking today, **Stay amateur** releases the calendar now; waiting for free lapse may miss that date, cut, or title and the ticket pull it brings. If nothing needs booking soon, holding lets the gym check cash and prep without burning a refusal. Signing starts `bout.day` and ends `weekly_salary` now. No separate “wait until nationals” contract: Stay is the way to enter during a live call; where entry remains open after expiry, holding is an honest alternative.

**ESTIMATE —** Keep the refusal ladder and no extra lapse penalty or artificial wait extension. Keep the current first-card return lock on a joint refusal and identify the returning house by marking that card's header before pressing Stay; do not imply both come back. Spell out next gate on the button: “9 wins and top 3, or 11 wins” / “Only No. 1” / “No more calls”; also show loyalty −8, applicable morale and next terms (title since refusal can restore the money, never reopen a closed door). Do not say “two more wins” for a refusal; compute the gap to the next gate. For expiry show “2 more wins after expiry, then normal call rules”; age, rank, active draw and house lock still apply. If Next Step has an actual offer for this boxer, show “Offer open · N days”; retain S531's wait logic and keep earlier roadmap milestones separate from this notice.

**ESTIMATE —** On Next Step and the modal put the live base sales against both house thresholds and the call band. Add compact scenarios for +2 wins (+4 tickets), +4 wins (+8), and a title (+10 by itself), all capped, holding other inputs fixed. A title and wins can add together, but neither ensures both calls: a forecast of 72 goes to 76 with two wins and can lose the guarantee call. No manual switch: show how the forecast opens or closes the second card before the first refusal, and show the lock afterward. Warn from age 29 about first calls ending at 32; no time-in-months guess, and suppress that warning on a return after refusal.

**ESTIMATE —** Show actual full/half stipend or day-job training status from existing schedule inputs, not an invented earlier gate. Day-job boxers get fewer mornings and use the same call rules; surface this in Next Step and Stipends with the player's actual eligibility gap. Do not promise a two-year route or lower the gate without a separate route probe. Show the title ambition consequence where the actual eligibility check triggers it; add a pro ambition for non-title recruits with bout experience in `Recruit.ambition` so the installed +5 sign/−5 stay runs. No flat debut-stat penalty: morale is the lever, no second hidden punishment for the same choice.

## 6. Evidence and checks for a later build

**ESTIMATE — gap choice, grounded in measured evidence, not a claim about this checkout's live win rate.** `docs/TURNGAP1_RESULT.md` measured 20 boxers × 10 seeds per gap with an override: −4 was 35.5%, −2 41%, −1 67%, zero 78.5%, +1 and +2 79%, +4 83.5%, +8 96.5%. The useful separation is near −1 to zero, not between +1 and +2. The pooled rates do not give any one boxer a reliable percentage; at −1 and zero the measured lower boxer percentile was 29%. The signing reads of 45.3% and 50.5% at the proposed gaps are not calibrated probabilities. Use plain matchup wording until a paired probe books real offers, sets the opponent at signing and runs the respective camps through settlement. **ESTIMATE** check at least 200 per house, reuse boxer/seed pairs, split by route and readiness, and look for ordered rates around 62–72% and 75–83%; calibrate the read on separate bouts, with reliability error within 10 percentage points in supported groups before showing a numeric range. Widen or omit the range if sparse. Do not tune to a single average or paste probe averages on cards.

**ESTIMATE — economic and timing checks:** Sweep every overlap sale count and the 45/46/74/75 boundaries. Check price at 85/86, 91/92/93 and 100, with flat, rising and falling membership; compare both boxer and gym cuts with the price ask. Compare plain vs cover across the two fee tiers, both common cuts and both signing bills; after full repayment the boxer has identical pay and the gym has exactly the fee less than plain. Before repayment verify that cover can actually bridge a cash shortfall, and that holding a bout open is not a loophole for cancelling debt. Check offer/rank/age changes, missing boxer, last-day save and reload, button double-press, lapse on a non-week-end day, and all amateur booking paths. Test a near-term entry deadline where Stay wins, a quiet week where holding wins, and a fit boxer where signing wins. Check that one accepted ask is never refreshed by reopen and that a settled ledger agrees with the booked cash quote under the same inputs. Recheck readiness forks, title chase and day-job samples at week 104 and 156 before any gate change. Use pick rates to investigate dominance, not as quotas: test results depend on the mix of runway, readiness and sales. No Godot run for this map.
