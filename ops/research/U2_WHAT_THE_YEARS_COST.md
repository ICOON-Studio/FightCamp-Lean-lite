## Q1 — Does the player see the age effect on recovery, or only feel it?

**Question.** A veteran taking longer to heal must be plan-able. "This fighter is old" must never appear. Silent weeks, a neutral forecast figure, or a wordless M11 mark?

**Options considered**
- A) Silent only — heal slower in the sim; no forecast change; player discovers by being burned.
- B) **Neutral forecast figure** — `forecast_weeks_to_training()` (and any twin read) reports the real weeks; no age word, no age mark.
- C) Wordless M11 mark on the body / readiness read (MUTED tick, TRACK wear) that means "slow to bounce" without saying age.

**Ruling: B.**

- The player plans on **weeks**, not on a biography label.
- No CHIP / badge / MUTED glyph whose job is to mean veteran recovery.
- Wear stays the medical-adjacent wear grammar already ruled elsewhere: `TRACK` recess + `LINE_SOFT` + DIM ticks for wear — **not** a stand-in for age, and never BAD / AMBER / GOOD (injury / readiness family).

**Why.** A is a lie-by-omission against planning. C invents a second channel that players will decode as "old" within an hour. B keeps K24's law and still lets the player schedule camps.

## Q2 — The forecast is the honesty problem, not the number

**Question.** If R54 ages `_apply_recovery` but leaves the forecast young, the game promises four weeks and takes six. What is the player owed when the factor is authored?

**Options considered**
- A) Forecast stays age-blind; only the silent heal clock ages (faster to ship, dishonest).
- B) **Forecast carries the same factor as the heal clock**; the weeks print as an ordinary operational figure (no "age-adjusted" label).
- C) Forecast carries the factor **and** prints MUTED + a `why` confessing the figure is soft / invented.

**Ruling: B.**

- One factor, both paths. The forecast is not a separate optimism layer.
- Authored does **not** change the duty to match clock and forecast; it changes whether the game may pretend the weeks are researched medicine (see Q4) — not whether it may strand the player.
- No copy that says the forecast is longer "because of age."

**Why.** A is the quiet lie K25 already refused for decision-grade surfaces. C turns every injured veteran into a hedge essay. Matching weeks without naming the birthday is the minimum honesty the player is owed.

## Q3 — Price: where it shows, and whether WHY shows

**Question.** An age-adjusted `Retention.market_salary()` is a negotiating fact. Where does it appear, and does the player ever see why it moved? K25's table has no AUTHORED row yet.

**Options considered**
- A) Same as UNVERIFIED_SECONDARY — MUTED figure + optional one-line `why` on detail; never on `squad_row`.
- B) Stronger — withhold the adjusted offer, or force a visible "soft money" mark everywhere salary appears.
- C) **None of the attribution treatment** — print the offer as ordinary money; never explain the age term; no salary `why`.
- D) Show WHY only in contract / OFFICE detail, phrased without decline words.

**Ruling: C** (with one surface rule).

| Surface | What prints |
| --- | --- |
| Offer / contract / hire money | The dollar figure the player acts on. Normal money ink (`TEXT` / existing salary styling). No MUTED "soft" cast on the whole purse. |
| `squad_row` | Wage if that row already shows pay — figure only. **No `why`.** (K25) |
| Detail / profile / negotiation pane | Still **no** age `why`. Not "veteran rate," not "years," not "past peak value." |
| Dev / data | `grade: UNKNOWN` (authored) on the age term — builders only. |

**Why.** Salary is a decision number; hiding or MUTING the purse (B) sabotages play. A and D both create a channel that says the birthday moved the price — that is K24 with a ledger. The player learns the market by comparing offers across fighters, the same way they learn anything else the sim prices, without a footnote that teaches "old costs less."

## Q4 — Does an authored number get the same grammar as a sourced one?

**Question.** Research grades (DIRECT / INHERITED / UNVERIFIED_SECONDARY / UNKNOWN) are about evidence. An AUTHORED game constant is a fifth thing. Is the player ever told?

**Options considered**
- A) Treat AUTHORED as UNVERIFIED_SECONDARY (MUTED + optional `why`).
- B) Treat AUTHORED as UNKNOWN peak — withhold (em-dash); never print the invented figure.
- C) **Fifth row: AUTHORED prints as an operational figure with no player-facing mark**; grade is data-only. No "we invented this" candour line.
- D) Stronger than secondary — LINK candour on every authored figure ("estimate").

**Ruling: C.** Extend K25's table:

| Kind | Player surface |
| --- | --- |
| DIRECT / INHERITED | Print normally. No mark. *(K25)* |
| UNVERIFIED_SECONDARY | MUTED figure; optional one-line `why` on detail — never on `squad_row`. *(K25)* |
| UNKNOWN peak / window | Withhold — em-dash. *(K25)* |
| invented / absent (corner rules) | CornerRulesRead: slot + absent/invented + `why`. *(K25)* |
| **AUTHORED** (game constant / play factor, e.g. R54 recovery or salary age term) | **Print the operational figure the player must act on (weeks, $). No player-facing grade mark. No `why` that names age or invention.** Store `grade: UNKNOWN` (or an authored flag) in data for Claude / audits. |

**Carrier (no new token).** Same M11 weights K25 named: `MUTED` / `DIM` for research-soft figures; authored operational numbers use the surface's ordinary `TEXT` / `TEXT_2` money and forecast styling. `LINK` candour stays for real CornerRulesRead / detail honesty — not for confessing simulation knobs. `TRACK` + `LINE_SOFT` + DIM ticks remain wear, never age chrome.

**Why.** Asterisk wallpaper and "estimate" chips (A/D) teach the player to distrust every number, including DIRECT ones — K25 already called that worse than the quiet lie. Withholding (B) makes R54 unplayable: you cannot negotiate a dash or schedule a dash. Authored play numbers are allowed to be quiet; researched doubt is what earns MUTED + `why`.
