Combat Manager Reference Survey

Forty-nine installs across two drives were opened, not just listed. Where the engine allowed it, the interface assets, scene trees, shaders and menu strings were pulled out. This is what they can teach a combat-sport manager, what can be borrowed, what a six-region body diagram should look like, and whether any of those engines should host the fight-night simulator.

2 · Could any of those engines host the fight-night simulator?

Godot 4 keep

Two shipped Godot managers sit on these drives, one on our exact version, and a Godot card game with 97 shaders shows the ceiling is high: SDF-based card frames, additive glow, ripple, dissolve, radial blur, Spine-animated characters. Our simulator is pure GDScript with no engine dependency, so nothing is gained by moving it.

Unity no advantage

Motorsport Manager, Corner King and MMA Arena prove the genre ships on Unity, but porting means rewriting every screen and the day loop. The sim's logic would port unchanged, which is exactly why it does not need to.

Unreal 5 only for 3D

The repo still carries a dormant Unreal project. Unreal earns its weight only if fight night becomes a 3D scene with real fighters. For a broadcast-style 2D presentation it is the heaviest possible choice.

Electron / web no

MMA Empire is a React app in a browser shell, which is why its glow and gradients are CSS. Godot has no web view; embedding a web UI would split the game in two runtimes.

Frostbite, 2K, GameMaker no

Closed or unsuitable. Their screens are references, not platforms.

Verdict

Stay in Godot. Borrow techniques, not engines: SDF rounded frames and glow from the card game, a nine-patch bar and additive glow sprite pattern from the gladiator manager, and the CSS glow recipe from MMA Empire translated to a canvas shader.

3 · What the comparables actually do

MMA Empire promotion manager

Its menu strings and components were read straight from the bundle.

  • Menu logic: Front Office, Training Center, Recovery Spa, scouting tiers from Gym Talent Scouts to Legendary Talent Brokers, TV Deals, Event Production, Arena, Upgrades & Perks, World Hub & News, weight classes, contracts with card roles (Main Event, Featured, Main Card).
  • Damage HUD: a photo-real full-body fighter with four regions, head, torso, left leg, right leg. Each region gets a radial-gradient glow whose colour steps red, orange, amber, green by damage and whose alpha scales with it, plus a box-shadow past 65. That is a clean recipe and it maps to our six regions directly.
  • Effects: backdrop blur on panels, a sheen sweep gradient, radial red washes behind headers.

Gladiator Command Godot 4.7 stable manager

Two hundred and fifteen scene trees were read by node name.

  • Hub: training ground with clickable actors, a training HUD of stat icon columns with an intensity slider and four result cards: Progress, XP, Injury, ETA.
  • Arena: tiers Local, Regional, Grand, Boss; a match preview panel with names, ratings, VS, reward summary, crowd expectation.
  • Detail scene: a showcase column with the actor on a stage and an equipment rail; a record scene split into victory record, financial record and progression gains.
  • Reports: status report cards with portrait frame, class badge, milestone badge and summary slots; injury alert panels; morale refusal rows; relationship cards; hall of fame inductions.
  • Art: hand-painted Roman fantasy, not our register, but every button ships with a separate additive glow texture and every bar is a nine-patch.

Corner King boxing manager

Everything lives in one 992 MB bundle. It is a 3D game underneath: UMA character bodies with albedo, normal and roughness maps, a boxing ring, arenas with lighting and LOD groups, crowd characters, commentators.

  • No 2D body diagram: its "body" assets are skin textures for 3D fighters. Its interface is drawn with text and vector shapes, so no chrome textures surfaced.
  • Portrait library: 409 rendered 128-pixel heads on disk, coded by region, face type and variant (af, as, eu, la, na × t01 to t04 × v01 to v24). That naming scheme is worth copying for a generated roster: region-appropriate faces without art per fighter.
  • News as data: media templates with an outlet, a title template, summary segments that link to entities, audience impact, preferred beats and journalist personalities. A cleaner model than free-text headlines.
  • Web inside Unity: a character-creator page in HTML with Bebas Neue and DM Sans, a red and gold palette on near-black, hard offset shadows instead of glow, tabs and sliders. Its fonts match ours; its palette is the boxing-poster register.
  • Fight night: a real 3D ring with a mesh crowd. The only combat manager on the drives that renders the bout in 3D, and it needed Unity, UMA and hundreds of assets to do it.

Hollywood Animal glow masks

  • The chrome technique: every glow is a white blurred mask, a beveled square, an inner-glow hexagon, a rounded rectangle, a star burst, tinted at runtime by the accent colour. One texture per shape, any colour. That is how the rounded glowing cells should be built in Godot: a white glow mask behind a StyleBoxFlat panel, modulated gold, red or cyan.
  • Frames: thin double-bevel frames, octagon borders, corner frames and wireframe grids as separate overlays, so a card is layered: mask, panel, frame, content.

Your Football Life Godot career sim

  • Loop: start, main menu, daily screen, match screen, result screen, negotiation, transfer, purchase, skill tree, deck edit, detail. A twelve-screen game, which argues for fewer, denser screens.
  • Injury: a physio image and an injury icon, no body diagram.

Motorsport Manager the dial

  • Your reference image is its part dial: a letter grade in the centre, a red-to-amber arc for performance, a small letter at the corner for the part's class. Parts, drivers and staff all read the same way, which is why the game feels coherent.
  • Structure: HQ buildings, staff with contracts, car parts by category, a season calendar with practice, qualifying and race, sponsors with objectives.

Slay the Spire 2 Godot effects

  • Shaders in text: button_pulse (additive sine on the colour), card_ripple (SDF ripple, additive blend), outline_texturerect (neighbour-sampled outline), doom_bar (a health bar with two scrolling noise layers and a gradient texture), dissolve, radial_blur, power_flash, texture_transition, hsv, fade_transition, plus a vfx toolkit: flipbook, polar ring, ray, poof, smoke.
  • Cards: an SDF card frame texture, glow textures per rarity, additive overlay scenes.

FC 26 and NBA 2K26 by knowledge

  • FC: the player bio leads with the overall badge and position, an attribute hexagon, playstyle icons, form arrows, and an injury view on a body silhouette with regions.
  • 2K MyGM: badges by tier, tendencies, a body injury map, contract and morale panels. Both give the overall number the most gravity, then the next fixture.

3b · MMA Career Simulator Playtest Godot 4.4 · the one that matters most

Installed on E: after the first pass. A Godot 4.4.1 build by MMADev, 1.2 GB, pack unencrypted, export presets shipped. It is a 3D MMA career game, and because it is Godot, our own 4.7 editor loads its pack directly: the fighter, the animation library and every scene instantiate headless, and the fighter wrote back out as a 33 MB glTF with all 23 clips.

The fighter rigged, animated, exportable

  • Mesh: one skinned body, 15,882 vertices, 28,880 triangles, diffuse, glossiness and normal maps, plus a "Low Poly MMA Character" variant at 7,148 triangles in six meshes with hair, beard, moustache and tattoo parts on bone attachments and its own 32-clip library. Both exported from our editor as glTF (33 MB and 6 MB).
  • Rig: a Mixamo auto-rig, 65 bones named mixamorig_Hips, Spine, Head, arms, hands down to the finger joints. That naming is the giveaway: the clips came from Mixamo.
  • Animation library, 23 clips: idle fighting stance, walk forward, step backwards, jab, straight punch, hook, lead hook, uppercut, lead uppercut, elbow strike, knee strike, front kick, body kick, leg kick, roundhouse kick, spinning heel kick, hit to head, hit to body, takedown and strike, taken down and hit, knocked out, victory, T-pose. Clip lengths run 0.6 to 7.5 seconds.
  • Separate clip files, 33 more: Body Block, High Block, Leg Block, Head Hit, Body Hit, Knocked Down, Knocked Out Body, Takedown Attacker, Takedown Victim, Dodge Counter, Jumping Knee, Spinning Backfist, Spinning Body Kick, Step Left, Step Right, Breathing Idle, Warming up, Sitting Angry, Sitting Cheering, Sitting Talking, and a grappling set: Clinch Idle, Clinch Hit, Top Idle, Bottom Idle, Top Movement Left and Right, Bottom Crawl, Standup Top, Standup Bottom, Reverse From Top, Ground Strikes Top.
  • State machine: an AnimationTree with a state machine and blend trees per move family, Step trees, Block trees, Clinch, Takedown, Overhand, time-scaled by pace. Exactly the structure a fight-night scene in Godot wants.

The venues and the loop 3D fight night in Godot

  • Octagon arena: a 332 MB imported glTF of a cage inside a show arena: 12,016 mesh instances and 9.3 million triangles once instantiated, seats with LODs, railings, ceiling lamps, trash cans, ad boards, thirty spot and omni lights, a spring-arm camera rig, cage bounds as walls. It instantiates headless in our editor; it is archviz-grade and far heavier than a fight-night scene needs.
  • Boxing hall: a training gym glTF, 64 meshes and 100,320 triangles, with ring, punchballs, mitts, paws, pear bag, cushions and helmets, used as the training-center scene. Exported from our editor at 114 MB.
  • Fight HUD: a top HUD with both fighters' name, health and stamina bars and a round clock; a choice popup with a button row; a round popup with damage and score; game speed at pause, half, one and two times.
  • Pre-fight game plan: tabs for fight state, pace, takedown anticipation, striking focus and defence, grappling focus and defence, clinch, and a stats grid. Their gameplan is our plan archetypes and contingencies, chosen live.
  • Career model, from the fighter screen labels: striking skills, head movement, wrestling skills, chain wrestling, takedown defence, grappling top and bottom with their defences; stamina; workout and sparring cost; weight cut success chance and cost; off-camp diet; contract, league, rank, card placement; a twelve-week training camp; a character creator with skin, hair, beard, tattoos, kit and glove colours, weight class.
  • Interface art: the Kenney UI pack and a synthwave loop. The polish is in the 3D, not the chrome.
What this changes. Fight night in 3D is proven in our engine at indie scale: one Mixamo-rigged body, a few dozen Mixamo clips, an AnimationTree state machine, a bought arena glTF. The legal path is the same one MMADev took: rig our own body on Mixamo, download the same stock clips under Mixamo's licence, buy or commission the cage and hall. The playtest's own mesh, clips and arena stay in the scratchpad as a reference and never ship.

3d · Pound for Pound demo Godot 4.5 · C# · the design bar

Installed on E: after the playtest. Pound for Pound: MMA Promotion Simulator, Early Access since March 2026, 96% positive across 132 reviews. Godot 4.5.1 with the game logic in a 12 MB C# assembly, Sentry crash reporting, and a Godot MCP add-on in the pack, so it was built with an AI coding assistant at the keyboard. The pack is unencrypted; the C# is compiled, so the teardown reads its scene names, shaders, fonts, colour literals, data files and store screens.

Folders run sex, then weight class, then ethnicity. That is a portrait library as data, the Corner King idea taken to its end.

How it looks, and where it stops

  • The shell. A persistent left nav with expandable groups, a date block and an Advance Week button at the top, a status bar with roster, champions, prestige and key hints at the bottom. Content panels are flat near-black cards with a square yellow bullet before each section title. It reads like Football Manager for MMA and it is coherent everywhere.
  • The bout screen is the best composition: two half-body portraits fill the sides, the centre column carries star power and matchup quality as coloured letters, then insights, tape and fallout tabs with side-by-side skill grades. Confirm Bout sits at the bottom of the column.
  • The fighter profile is a modal: portrait left, name, record, flag, style tags, a recovering status, an overall A- badge beside a relationship ring, six tabs, and the relationship tab with trust, career and morale, active requests with a promise bar. Letter grades everywhere, numbers almost nowhere.
  • Results are a reveal wizard: header with portraits and winner tag, strike and takedown comparison, a three-judge scorecard grid, then a performance grade.
  • Where it stops. No body diagram, no dial, no gauge beyond bars, no motion in the fight itself: the fight is a stat reveal with commentary text. The portraits have the sameness of one AI style. Glow is present in the kit but used sparingly, so the screens are disciplined rather than exciting. Depth is its selling point, and reviews reward it.

4 · The six-region body

No install holds a realistic six-part body ready to use. MMA Empire has a photo-real fighter with four code regions, FC and 2K keep theirs locked, the gladiator manager has crowd bodies only. The honest options:

green 80 and up · amber 60 to 79 · red under 60

Option A, in-house vector body. A single anatomical silhouette drawn as vector paths, with six polygons lit by readiness colour and a blur glow, exactly as above. In Godot this is one Polygon2D per region under a CanvasGroup with a glow shader, front view, optionally a back view toggle. No licence risk, crisp at any size, animates for free when a region takes damage on fight night.

Option B, rendered fighter with overlays. MMA Empire's approach: a photo-real or rendered body with soft radial glows per region. Realistic, but it needs a body render we own; the extracted PNG is theirs. A commissioned or generated render would do.

Recommendation: A now, using the six regions the game already tracks, with the glow recipe from MMA Empire translated to a canvas shader. B can replace the silhouette later without touching the logic.

5 · HUD and effect recipes, mapped to Godot

Region glow by damage

// MMA Empire's CSS, as a Godot canvas shader idea
// colour steps: red >=75, orange >=50, amber >=25, green
// alpha = min(0.72, 0.08 + damage / 120)
shader_type canvas_item;
uniform vec4 tint : source_color;
uniform float strength = 0.0; // 0..1 damage
void fragment() {
    float d = distance(UV, vec2(0.5));
    float a = smoothstep(0.72, 0.0, d) * (0.08 + strength * 0.64);
    COLOR = vec4(tint.rgb, a);
}

Button pulse and ripple

// Slay the Spire 2, verbatim
shader_type canvas_item;
uniform float pulse_strength;
void fragment() {
    COLOR.rgb += abs(sin(TIME * 2.0)) * 0.2 * pulse_strength;
}
// card_ripple: render_mode blend_add; an SDF alpha
// modulo(TIME * speed) sweep with smoothstep easing

Living health bar

doom_bar samples a noise texture twice at different scroll speeds, sums them and reads a gradient texture. Our stamina and readiness bars can carry a quieter version: one noise layer, gold gradient, only while the value is changing.

Rounded glowing cells

StyleBoxFlat with corner radius 16, a one-pixel steel border, and shadow_size 14 with the accent colour at low alpha gives the halo without a texture. For a stronger bloom, a CanvasGroup with the card game's canvas_group_mask_blur behind the cell.

The grade dial

A Control that draws two arcs with draw_arc: a dark track and a gradient arc from red through amber to gold, a letter grade in Bebas Neue at the centre, a small class letter at the corner, exactly the Motorsport Manager part dial. Animate the arc on open with a Tween.

Fight night effects

The card game's vfx include set covers what a broadcast fight scene needs: flipbook impact sprites, a polar ring for a knockdown flash, a ray burst for a finish, round smoke, and a screen-space radial blur for a hurt moment. All are canvas shaders that run on a Sprite2D.

7 · Borrowable assets and the licence line

Pulled to the scratchpad for reference: 607 MMA Arena textures (rank badges, neon border strips, flat icons), 266 Gladiator Command interface textures (nine-patch bars, glow buttons, painted stat icons), 134 Slay the Spire 2 shaders and vfx scenes in text, 33 Hollywood Animal glow masks and frames, MMA Empire's bundle and its fighter image, Corner King's news templates and a sample of its portrait library, and Motorsport Manager's grade gradient strips. Everything here belongs to its publisher. Shaders are code and can be re-implemented from the idea; textures and the fighter photo should not ship in a distributed game. For our own build the honest path is: draw the chrome in Godot, generate or commission the body render, and cite the techniques.

8 · What to do next

Build the profile as previewed, with three additions the survey earned: the six-region vector body with damage glow, the Motorsport-style dial as the shared grade language across fighter, coach and station screens, and the card game's pulse and ripple shaders for the buttons and the next-bout cell. Fight night gets the vfx set when its scene is redesigned.
