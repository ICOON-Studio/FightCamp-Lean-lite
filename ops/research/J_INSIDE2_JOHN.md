**1. Head**
- **Position:** forehead or chin on, or just outside, the opponent's lead shoulder, chin tucked, head over the hips, never bent at the waist.
- **Best spot:** "when you can see the back of your opponent's head", with your chest leaning on her. Either side works.
- **Movement:** keep making small side-to-side sways.
- **Distance apart:** no source gives it; about 10–20 cm sideways is my ESTIMATE.
- **Sources:**
  - [BoxingWiki on inside fighting](https://boxingwiki.org/technique/inside-fighting)
  - [Honour & Glory lesson plan](https://www.honourandglory.co.uk/coaching/short-punching-inside-fighting)
  - [ExpertBoxing guide](https://expertboxing.com/the-boxers-guide-to-inside-fighting)

**2. Arms**
- **Elbows:** glued to the ribs. "If you can see daylight between your elbow and your ribs, the punch is too wide" (Honour & Glory).
- **Gloves:** at the cheeks or temples, as in D'Amato's peek-a-boo style.
- **Framing:** use the forearms and shoulders against her chest to make room to punch (BoxingWiki). No source describes a forearm across the collarbone.

**3. Punches**
- **Dempsey's shovel hook:** elbow pressed to the hip for a body shot or to the lower ribs for a head shot, fist tilted about 45°, power from a hip "hunch".
- **Distance:** inside punches travel 15–30 cm ([DrillGrid](https://drillgridboxing.com/drills/inside-pivot-escape/)).
- **Target:** mostly the body.
- **Elbow angle:** about 60–90° is my ESTIMATE from Dempsey's "sharply bent".
- **Punches before separating:** no source gives a count.

**4. Getting in, staying in and getting out**
- **Getting in:** slip the jab and step in with the lead foot between her feet. Pressure means cutting off the ring, not just walking forward ([Honour & Glory, Pressure Fighting](https://www.honourandglory.co.uk/coaching/pressure-fighting)).
- **Staying in:** angle slightly to her side with your chest on her.
- **Getting out:**
  - pivot about 90° on the lead foot behind a check hook;
  - tie up her biceps and wait for "Break";
  - jab and move sideways;
  - roll under a wide punch.
  - Sources: BoxingWiki's [rope escape](https://boxingwiki.org/technique/rope-escape) and [corner escape](https://boxingwiki.org/technique/corner-escape) pages.

**5. Referee calls inside** ([IBA referee regulations 2023](https://www.iba.sport/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Referee-Judge-Regulations-2023-1-2.pdf), [IBA rules 2021](https://www.iba.sport/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/IBA-Technical-and-Competition-Rules_20.09.21_Updated_.pdf))
- **Minor:** a slight low head, slight holding, or not stepping back on "Break".
- **Major:** holding, holding and hitting, pushing, or a low head.
- **Flagrant:** a head butt, hitting with the elbow or shoulder, or punching without a full step back on "Break".
- **Also fouls:** ducking below the opponent's belt, and locking an arm or the head.
- **Penalties:** each warning costs 1 point with every judge, and a third warning means disqualification.

**What the 3D boxers should look like inside:**
1. The head is off the centre line, on or just outside the opponent's lead shoulder, with the chin tucked and small sways.
2. The elbows stay glued to the ribs, and the gloves sit at the cheeks or temples.
3. The punches are short shovel hooks and uppercuts with a sharply bent elbow and a fist travel of 15–30 cm, mostly to the body.
4. The attacker steps in with the lead foot between her feet and angles to her side, chest on her, but never bends at the waist with her head below the chest, because that's a foul.
5. The defender gets out by pivoting about 90° behind a hook, or by tying up her arms until "Break" and stepping fully back.
