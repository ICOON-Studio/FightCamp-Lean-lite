# J_TESTS1 - John: the tests a boxing coach runs and real values by level (S370, gbot thread John Research)

**J_TESTS1 is done, but with big gaps.** The solid numbers are punch force, punches in 10 s and 30 s, bleep-test norms, VO2max (the standard aerobic-fitness measure) and a 5-week elite training block. England Boxing runs a real six-grade skills award scheme. I found no boxer values at any level for runs, sprints, press-ups, skipping or reaction time. No study groups boxers as "club boxer" or "carded", so those two columns are mostly empty.

**Q1. Tests coaches run**
| Test | How it's done | What it measures | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Max punch force | Force plate or bag with a sensor; best straight punch with each hand | Power | [Smith 2000](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10902679/), [2024 elite women study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11532089/) |
| Punches in 10 s and 30 s | Straight punches at full speed into a target with a sensor | Speed | 2024 elite women study |
| Bag rounds, 3×3 min | Partner or sensor counts each round, round 1 vs round 3 | Engine | Fight output from [Davis 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24912199/); no club protocol found |
| Bleep test | Cones 20 m apart and the audio track, 10–15 min | Engine | [BrianMac norms](https://www.brianmac.co.uk/beep.htm), [Korean national team study 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11079553/) |
| Jump height and lifts relative to body weight | Jump mat; bench press and squat | Power and strength | 2024 elite women study, [junior/senior study 2020](https://doi.org/10.7752/jpes.2020.06466) |
| Pad and footwork skill check | Coach assesses at the end of each 8-session award | Skill | [GB Boxing Awards](https://www.boxingawards.co.uk/levels-of-award) |
| Runs, 30 m sprint, press-ups, plank, skipping, reaction time | – | – | No boxing source found |

**Q4. Skill awards.** The [GB Boxing Awards](https://www.boxingawards.co.uk/levels-of-award) run through [England Boxing](https://www.englandboxing.org/get-involved/community-form/). Each award is 8 sessions, ends with an assessment, and earns a medal and certificate.
| Grade | What it tests |
|---|---|
| Preliminary | Basics, no contact |
| Standard | Jab and rear hand while moving, double jab, straight combinations, skipping |
| Bronze | Hooks to head and body, defences, rules, non-contact sparring |
| Silver | Uppercuts, counters, condition sparring, circuits; needs a medical |
| Gold | Tactical sparring, equipment circuit, coaching, a short written project |
| Platinum | A skills bout in the ring |

I didn't check USA Boxing, Boxing Ireland or Boxing Australia.

**Q5. What keeps non-competing members coming.** From [study abstracts](https://doi.org/10.1177/1012690218815139) only, with no survey numbers: fitness, mastering skills, self-defence, stress relief, community, and feeling stronger. White-collar boxers want a real but safe experience that ends in a bout. Coaches mark progress through award assessments, medals and certificates.

**Six tests for the game:**
1. Punches in 30 s, because it has measured elite women's values and measured 5-week gains.
2. Max punch force, because it has beginner, middle and elite values (men only).
3. Bleep test, which any club can run, with published adult norms for women and men.
4. Three bag rounds counting round 1 vs round 3, which links to real fight output and shows fatigue.
5. Jump height or a lift relative to body weight, because both are cheap to run and have measured gains.
6. A pad assessment in the style of the GB Awards, which is a real graded skill test that ends in a medal.
