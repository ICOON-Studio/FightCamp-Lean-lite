# FIGHTWALK3 design draft (agy / Gemini, designer seat, S397) - the 8/10 corner minute

Written from the blind critic's review (FIGHTWALK3 brief, Why). A designer's draft: Claude keeps what fits the brief's bars; it is not the spec.

TIMING, WALKS, ROUND-2 GAP, AND STAGING

Minute clock and speed: Run a full 60.0 s corner minute locked to 1x real time (never inheriting the 2x fast-forward round speed), handing straight from the Round 1 bell into the corner at 0.0 s.

Walk to the corner: 3.5 s of tired walking (0.0 to 3.5 s), followed without a cut by a 0.4 s turn (3.5 to 3.9 s) and a 1.6 s sit (3.9 to 5.5 s), 5.5 s unbroken from bell to stool. Both boxers blend directly from their fight guard (pro_idle) over 0.6 s into ow_walk at 0.54x speed (0.85 m/s tired pace, arms relaxed low rather than passing through af_idle), walking roughly 2.9 m to their sit marks 1.10 m in front of each corner post.

Walk out to centre: 3.7 s of measured walking (53.5 to 57.2 s) in ow_walk at 0.48x speed (0.76 m/s with smooth ease-in and ease-out over 1.8 m), turning directly into pro_idle guard (57.2 to 57.8 s, skipping af_idle) and holding ready for 2.2 s until the Round 2 bell at 60.0 s.

Round-2 start gap: 2.0 m pelvis-to-pelvis (marks at 1.0 m each side of ring centre along the corner diagonal, about 1.6 m glove-to-glove or two full steps apart), replacing today's ~1.0 m jab-contact mark so Round 2 opens at realistic ring-centre range on Round 1's exact broadcast camera side.

Corner positions and 180-degree rule: Every red-corner camera stays on her left side inside the ropes. Boxer sits on the stool 0.62 m in from the red post along the corner diagonal, facing centre. Coach stands and kneels 1.02 m in front of the stool and 0.08 m toward her left, angled 20 degrees open toward her left so the overhead centre-ring spotlights key-light his face instead of throwing it into self-shadow. Second stands inside the ropes at her front-right (0.52 m right and 0.55 m forward of the stool, 0.46 m clear inside the ropes, 0.76 m from her, 0.83 m clear of the Coach), with the spit bucket tucked behind him near the right-side rope. Cutman (pro bouts only) waits on the left apron outside the ropes (0.88 m left, 0.20 m forward of the stool) and steps inside to her front-left (0.48 m left, 0.52 m forward of the stool) only during his window. Minimum body spacing stays at least 0.68 m; nobody ever clips or blocks a lens.


CUE-BY-CUE PLAN FOR THE MINUTE

0.0 to 5.6 s | Shot: behind (unbroken dolly-in from her left side of ring centre).
Stools and buckets are already down at 0.0 s. Both boxers drop from pro_idle into ow_walk (0.54x), walk 3.5 s to their sit marks, turn outward at 3.5 s, and sit via af_sit_enter (3.9 to 5.5 s) into af_sit_idle. Coach waits at his front mark in af_idle looking at her and reaches one hand to guide her left shoulder as she turns; Second waits at his front-right mark in af_idle watching her sit.

5.6 to 8.2 s | Shot: corner_wide (moved to her left side as our lit three-shot, all three heads framed inside the letterbox).
Within 0.9 s of her sitting, Coach lowers onto his haunches using the downward bend of af_pickup (5.5 to 6.4 s) blended smoothly into upright af_crouch_idle, placing both hands onto her rested gloves by 6.4 s. Simultaneously (5.6 to 7.4 s), Second plays af_interact, reaching his right hand to her mouth to take out her gumshield, dipping it toward the bucket, and lifting the water bottle at 7.5 s.

8.2 to 11.0 s | Shot: water (moved to her front-left to stay on one side of the corner).
Second plays af_interact, left hand tipping the bottle to her lips (8.2 to 9.8 s) while Boxer tilts slightly to drink in af_sit_idle, followed by a brief right-hand bucket rinse reach (9.8 to 10.8 s). Coach stays crouched with hands steady on her gloves.

11.0 to 15.5 s | Shot: ots (over Coach's right shoulder onto her lit face; Corner Card fades in on the right at 11.5 s).
Coach holds upright af_crouch_idle, left hand on her right glove, right hand lifting toward her chin and tapping her glove on the beat of his opening line while his head nods lightly while speaking. Behind her right shoulder, Second plays af_interact (11.0 to 18.8 s), wiping her brow and cheek.

15.5 to 19.0 s | Shot: reverse (three-quarter over her left shoulder onto Coach's key-lit face; also triggered for 3.0 s whenever the player picks a call).
Coach stays in upright af_crouch_idle while his hands gesture in rhythm with the spoken plan: tapping chest-low on a body call, extending twice along the jab line on a long call, pressing palms-down on her gloves on a calm call, and touching his temple if a habit fix follows. Boxer answers in af_sit_talk (after a lost round or habit fix) or af_sit_idle with a slight nod.

19.0 to 24.0 s | Shot: side2 (left-side three-shot beside the Corner Card).
Coach stays engaged hands-on-gloves while Second plays af_interact (19.0 to 28.0 s), pressing the cold towel compress against the back of her neck and steadying her right shoulder.

24.0 to 28.0 s | Shot: ice (left-shoulder close insert on her neck and profile).
Frames the cold compress held against her nape while she breathes in af_sit_idle and Coach speaks across the foreground.

28.0 to 36.0 s | Shots: cutman (28.0 to 32.0 s, moved front-left) then corner_wide (32.0 to 36.0 s) in pro bouts; ots (28.0 to 32.0 s) then corner_wide (32.0 to 36.0 s) in amateur bouts.
Amateur: Second plays af_interact (28.0 to 35.0 s), reaching to apply grease across her brow and cheekbones.
Pro: Second eases half a step back toward her right hip in af_idle while Cutman walks in from the left apron (af_walk) to her front-left mark, plays af_interact with right fingers working her swollen eye or cheekbone (28.5 to 35.0 s), then steps back onto the left apron at 35.2 s (af_walk with trunk duck under the top rope).

36.0 to 40.5 s | Shot: side2 (left-side three-shot).
Second bends via af_pickup (35.5 to 36.3 s) into upright af_crouch_idle beside her right leg, both hands squeezing and shaking out her right thigh and calf through 48.8 s. Coach stays crouched with hands on her gloves.

40.5 to 44.0 s | Shot: other (long-lens cutaway across the ring).
Blue boxer sits in af_sit_idle while Blue Coach leans in playing af_talk and Blue Second works beside her in af_interact.

44.0 to 47.2 s | Shot: ots, then 47.2 to 50.0 s | Shot: corner_wide.
Default call fires at 45.0 s if unpicked (Coach gestures, Boxer nods in af_sit_idle). At 48.8 s Second rises via af_pickup into af_interact and reaches to her lips (49.2 to 50.0 s) to put her gumshield back in; Coach gives a final tap on her gloves.

50.0 to 53.5 s | Shot: push (tracking push-in from front-left).
"Seconds out!" sounds, Corner Card closes, and compress clears. Boxer rises via af_sit_exit (50.2 to 52.0 s) straight into pro_idle. Behind her, Second lifts the stool (af_pickup into af_walk_carry) and ducks out through the right-side ropes onto the apron, followed by Coach rising (af_pickup into af_walk with trunk duck) onto the apron by 53.4 s.

53.5 to 60.0 s | Shot: broadcast (restored to Round 1's camera side).
Apron crews and props hide once clear at 53.8 s. Both boxers walk out in ow_walk (0.48x, 53.5 to 57.2 s), settle into pro_idle at the 2.0 m marks, and hold guard until the 60.0 s bell.


WHAT CHANGES FROM TODAY AND WHY

1. One unbroken 5.5 s entry instead of three cuts and arms-out af_idle: Today Round 1's bell freezes 2.6 s and puts boxers into af_idle (sticking gloved arms out), then delays walking to 0.3 s and cuts across part, behind, and low_out before she sits. Blending straight from pro_idle into slower ow_walk on a single behind take fixes the clipped <1 s walk and stiff arms.
2. Smooth 0.9 s Coach kneel, key-lit angle, and active hands instead of folded arms, 1-frame crouch snap, and black stare: Today Coach waits in af_idle_foldarms, teleports into af_crouch_idle at 9.0 s with no hand targets, and faces into the dark corner post during a long reverse close-up. Lowering via af_pickup within 1 s, angling him 20 degrees toward the centre lights, capping reverse at 3.0 s, and driving hands onto her gloves, chin, and ribs in sync with captions makes him look alive and lit.
3. Second moved inside with five real jobs and a rope exit instead of 44 s of apron af_idle: Placing Second 0.76 m front-right inside the ropes lets his arms reach her without clipping the Coach, replacing Coach's water duty and 44 s of dead apron idling with gumshield out, water, wipe, cold compress/grease (restoring the unused ice prop and ice shot), leg massage, gumshield in, and a ducked af_walk_carry exit rather than vanishing in one frame.
4. Locked camera lines: Moving corner_wide, water, and cutman to her left side stops 180-degree corner jumps, and saving Round 1's broadcast side before film shots prevents the Round 2 camera flip.
5. True 1x walk-out to a 2.0 m gap: Locking corner time to 1x, moving Seconds out to 50.0 s, skipping arrival af_idle, and doubling the centre marks from ~1.0 m to 2.0 m stops the 1.5 s dash and clinch-range Round 2 opening.
