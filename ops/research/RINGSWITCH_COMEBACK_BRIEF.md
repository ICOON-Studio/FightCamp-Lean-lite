# Research wanted: how often does the man who is BEHIND win a fight, and how often do kicks land - in numbers

You are a combat-sports researcher. We need SOURCED facts, each with where it comes from (a link) and whether it is a
measured number, a coach's teaching or your own memory. Plain words. If a number does not exist, say NOT FOUND; do not
estimate.

## Why
Our small fight game (Ring Switches: two men, three rounds, each round six exchanges) is adding a comeback move for the
man who is losing: one all-in power punch, open only when he is behind. Before we set how often it should turn a fight,
we want to know how often real fights are turned. The same game has a kickboxing version, and we want its kicks to land
about as often as real ones.

## Part A - comebacks (boxing first, then kickboxing / MMA if boxing has no count)
1. Of fighters who are KNOCKED DOWN FIRST in a bout, what share go on to win? Any counted study or database figure,
   with its sample (BoxRec, CompuBox, academic papers on professional or amateur boxing).
2. Of fighters who are BEHIND ON THE SCORECARDS after the first third or the first half of a bout, what share win - by
   stoppage and by decision, separately if counted?
3. Of all knockouts, what share are scored by the man who was losing the fight at that moment (come-from-behind
   knockouts)? Any count by round.
4. Does the losing man throw MORE power punches late, and do they land less often? Any CompuBox-style figures for
   power punches thrown and landed by the trailing fighter in the late rounds.
5. One paragraph: in real boxing, what does a losing fighter actually risk when he "goes for broke" - what happens to
   him most often?

## Part B - kicks (K-1, Glory, ONE kickboxing, Muay Thai; MMA only where kickboxing has no count)
6. Landing rate BY KICK TYPE: low (leg) kick, body (middle) kick, head (high) kick, push kick (teep). Counted figures
   with the sample and who counted.
7. How often is a low kick CHECKED (blocked with the shin), and how often does a checked kick hurt the kicker? Any count.
8. At what distance is each kick thrown compared with punches (any measured ranges), and which kick is most often
   thrown as the opponent steps forward?
9. Share of kickboxing knockouts and knockdowns by weapon: punch, head kick, body kick, low kick, knee.
