Looking directly at the rejected build image reveals why the operator rejected it. It does not feel like a sports management title; it looks like a flat accounting form with an unrendered, broken right half.

Problem 1: The Catastrophic "Dead Zone" in the Right Column ($X = 1400\text{ to }1920$)
What is seen: Over $40%$ of the $1920 \times 1080$ screen area—from the middle-right all the way to the border—is empty dark brown void. The right column contains only a faint two-line text string (NICOSIA BOXING CLUB / NICOSIA • CYPRUS) and a lonely, floating yellow OPEN GYM button.
Why it hurts: In modern management titles (Football Manager, Motorsport Manager, F1 Manager), screen real estate is balanced. Having hundreds of thousands of unallocated black pixels makes the game look unpolished, unbuilt, or bugged. It deprives the player of visual payoff at the very climax of their career setup.
Problem 2: The Incomprehensible "Treemap" Floor Layout
What is seen: The middle-left section features an arbitrary grid of brown rectangles of varying sizes (OLD CLUB BOXING RING, BASIC HEAVY BAG, MODULAR COMBAT MAT, FIRST AID CABINET, etc.).
Why it hurts:
It is an abstract CSS/GUI Treemap that bears zero spatial relationship to the actual 30x18 m gym hall.
The boxing ring is shown as a medium box on the top left; heavy bags are stacked next to it; the first-aid cabinet is shoved into a bottom corner.
The player has no idea where these stations physically exist inside the building, what faces what, how fighters walk between them, or where the doors and ring steps are.
It is purely decorative data that frustrates mental mapping.
Problem 3: Complete Spatial Blindness (Zero 3D Preview)
What is seen: Not a single pixel of the actual 3D engine or gym geometry is rendered on this screen.
Why it hurts: The player just spent time selecting a country, a city, a head coach name, and a gym identity. They are about to begin their career, yet they cannot see their physical gym. Dropping the player into a full 3D hall only after clicking OPEN GYM creates a jarring visual transition.
Problem 4: Zero Mechanical Agency (Cannot Move, Rotate, or Swap Zones)
What is seen: The tiles are static, immutable blocks with a cryptic • OPEN micro-label at the bottom of each tile.
Why it hurts: The operator's core requirement is explicitly violated: "ability to move zones etc." If the manager wants the heavy bag run along the brick wall and the strength platform near the south entrance, they cannot touch them. They are locked into a predetermined template with zero management strategy.
Problem 5: The Generic 3-Fighter Roster Stack
What is seen: A stacked card list of three fighters (NASIR LARKIN, RICO VARGAS, RAFAEL NOVAK) displaying only basic metadata (AGE 20 • BOX • 1-0-0).
Why it hurts:
In the story mode (The House That Holds), the narrative anchor is Mara Solano, not three randomly generated fighters.
For the Sandbox modes, the gym is meant to start empty, with fighters scouted on the market.
Zero fighter attributes are shown (no Power, Speed, Stamina, Ring IQ). The player cannot judge the quality, fighting style, or roster depth of what they are inheriting.
Problem 6: Incomplete Financial Telemetry (Missing Runway Burn)
What is seen: Three tiles: STARTING CASH: $25.0K, FLOOR SPACE: 36/40, and UPKEEP: $367 / WEEK.
Why it hurts: Displaying $$25.0\text{K}$ alongside $$367/\text{wk}$ upkeep leads the player to believe they have $\approx 68$ weeks of financial safety. But in the real simulation engine, total operating burn (including coach salaries, facility lease, licenses, and utilities) is closer to $$2,700/\text{wk}$, meaning real runway is only 9 weeks. Concealing this reality sets the player up for immediate bankruptcy.
Problem 7: Illegible Equipment Data & Debug Nomenclature
What is seen: Text lines like COND 84% • 4×4 and • OPEN rendered in tiny $9\text{ px}$ low-contrast muted brown text.
Why it hurts: The equipment condition numbers (which tie directly to the station wear mechanics from Round 56) are completely unreadable from a normal desktop viewing distance. The word • OPEN is ambiguous—does it mean the station is currently open for use, or does clicking it open an inspection card?

3. WHAT IS WORTH KEEPING FROM THIS SCREEN

Not everything on the rejected screen is bad; several core design elements established the right foundational direction and should be preserved in the redesign:

The Warm Dark Brown Palette (#181310 to #2D2319):
The operator's decision to reject clinical corporate sci-fi blue in favor of rich, warm walnut wood tones and industrial golds gives FightCamp an unmistakable visual identity that evokes classic boxing gyms (brick, oiled leather, hardwood). KEEP.
The Top Global Shell & Breadcrumb Hierarchy:
The top navigation bar (FIGHTCAMP, BACK, STEP 3: THE GYM, CYPRUS > NICOSIA) is clean, legible, and structurally sound. KEEP.
The Starting Mode Chip Toggle Format:
The pill-style mode selectors (THE HOUSE THAT HOLDS, GUIDED SANDBOX, OPEN SANDBOX, ADVANCED) provide immediate mental framing. They simply need proper active gold accents instead of cyan. KEEP.
Input Field Proportions (Gym Name / Coach Name):
The two text input fields at the top left are well-proportioned, accessible, and clean. They transition cleanly into the left drawer. KEEP.
The Primary Action Button (OPEN GYM):
The high-contrast, rounded warm gold CTA button (#E5B842) is visually satisfying. It just needs to be anchored properly in a dedicated bottom action footer rather than floating adrift in an empty column. KEEP.

Controls & Input Mapping
Left-Click: Selects and pins a zone, immediately opening its full metadata in the Left Inspector Panel.
Left-Click + Drag: Initiates translation across the floor plane.
R Key (or On-Screen Rotate Icon): Rotates the active zone $90^\circ$ clockwise around its geometric center.
Delete / Backspace (Sandbox Modes Only): Stows the selected zone into the facility inventory, returning its square footage to the free floor pool.
Middle-Mouse Drag / Right-Mouse Drag: Pans the 3D camera across the hall.
Mouse Scroll Wheel: Zooms camera field of view ($35^\circ \leftrightarrow 60^\circ$).
Snapping & Precision Rules
Grid Snap: Zone origins snap strictly to $1.0\text{ m}$ intervals along the $X$ (0 to 30) and $Z$ (0 to 18) axes.
Wall Clearance: All apparatus boundaries enforce a mandatory minimum $0.4\text{ m}$ structural clearance from external brick walls.
Thoroughfare Clearance:
Main Hall Transit Spine ($Z = 10.4\text{ m}$ to $11.6\text{ m}$): Zones cannot infringe on this $1.2\text{ m}$-wide strip. Dropping a zone here is prohibited.
South Entrance Vestibule ($Z = 16.4\text{ m}$ to $18.0\text{ m}$): Must maintain an open clear path to the double doors.

The Synchronization Pipeline
Raycast Floor Projection (Camera3D.unproject_position):
The 3D scene contains an invisible horizontal StaticBody3D at $Y = 0.0$ covering the $30\text{ m} \times 18\text{ m}$ floor.
When the player's mouse moves over GymViewportContainer, the coordinates are converted into normalized viewport space:
var local_mouse = gym_viewport_container.get_local_mouse_position()
var ray_origin = camera_3d.project_ray_origin(local_mouse)
var ray_normal = camera_3d.project_ray_normal(local_mouse)
# Intersect with horizontal plane Y = 0
var t = -ray_origin.y / ray_normal.y
var world_floor_pos = ray_origin + ray_normal * t

2D Overlay Tracking (ViewportOverlay2D):
Rather than placing complex UI nodes inside 3D space as billboards, the 2D Control overlay calculates where the four 3D corners of each zone exist on screen using camera_3d.unproject_position(corner_3d_pos).
It draws vector boundaries directly in 2D using _draw(). This completely avoids texture filtering blur, perspective aliasing, and Z-fighting, ensuring crisp lines at all resolutions.
Data State Machine (GymLayoutManager Resource):
All zone positions exist within a pure data resource (GymLayoutData.tres).
When a zone is moved, the data model validates the coordinates against an array of Rect2 footprint bounds.
If valid, the model emits layout_changed(zone_id, new_transform), prompting both the 3D apparatus mesh to slide to its new world coordinates and the 2D overlay to redraw its bounding lines.
The Left Sidebar's financial calculations subscribe directly to layout_changed, instantly updating Weekly Upkeep and Runway.
