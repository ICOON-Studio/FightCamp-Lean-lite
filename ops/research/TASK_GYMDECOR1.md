# TASK GYMDECOR1 - free 3D assets to decorate a boxing gym, room by room (research)

Model: John (deep research). Discipline: BOXING gym (demo).
Operator S276: "find assets for decals and things you can decorate a gym also ? jus tlike how those 2 games build their rooms"
(Two Point Museum / Two Point Hospital and Mad Games Tycoon 2: every room has a catalogue of decorations the player places).

## Ask
Find downloadable 3D models (glTF/GLB or FBX/OBJ convertible) and decal textures we can ship in a commercial Steam game, for decorating
a boxing gym in a stylised/low-poly Two Point look. Group by ROOM: training floor, office, reception, locker room, physio room, lounge,
toilets, outside plot (trees, benches, bins, lamp posts, fences, street props). Wanted: fight posters/frames, flags, banners, floor
decals (logos, tape lines, numbers), wall decals, plants, benches, water coolers, clocks, trophies, belts on display, vending machines,
lockers, mirrors, mats, rugs, lamps, signage, trash bins, fans.

## For every pack
name, URL, licence (CC0 / CC-BY / paid-royalty-free - quote the licence line), format, polycount range, style (low-poly / realistic),
how many useful items, and which room they fit. Prefer CC0 (Kenney, Quaternius, Poly Pizza, Sketchfab CC0, itch.io free packs, Fab
free). Mark anything with an AI-training, no-commercial or attribution-only restriction. Rank the top 10 packs for our use.

## Output
One markdown table per room + the ranked top 10 + total item count. No downloads - links and licence lines only.
