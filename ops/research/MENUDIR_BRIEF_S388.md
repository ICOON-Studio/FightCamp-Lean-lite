# MENUDIR S388: how should FightCamp's management screens be presented?

FightCamp is a PC boxing gym management game (Godot, tiny budget, one developer plus AI seats). The player runs a small gym:
signs fighters, plans their weeks, books fights, watches them in 3D, keeps the money positive. Almost all play time is in 2D
screens, not the 3D fight. The owner has 40 years of playing games and is deciding the presentation direction now.

TODAY: a top bar (date, speed, CONTINUE, cash, mail) and a left menu: Home, Fighters, Playbook, Fights, Gym, Money, World. Each opens
tabs of cards. Home is a whiteboard panel with the next 4 weeks, plus cards for needs, sponsor, cash and news. Fighter pages hold
training (a drag-and-drop week of picture cards). Owner's recent notes: too little information on fighters; RPG elements should show
more often; each playthrough should feel unique; Home should be a dashboard with shortcuts and fighter and coach cards.

THE CHOICE:
A. Keep the menus and improve them: same structure, richer cards and more information, better dashboard Home.
B. Desk Home: Home becomes a simple stylized 2D top-down office desk. Objects are live shortcuts (folder = finance with cash shown,
   computer = mail, rankings, scouting; phone = bout offers ringing; fighter and coach cards on the desk). All other screens stay menus.
C. Interactive office everywhere: most screens become illustrated places or objects (desk, filing cabinet, whiteboard, window on the
   gym floor), with transitions between them instead of a menu.

ANSWER IN YOUR OWN SEAT'S ROLE, SHORT, UNDER 300 WORDS, PLAIN WORDS:
1. Your pick (A, B, C or another) and the one main reason.
2. The biggest risk of your pick.
3. Two or three real games that did this well or badly, and what exactly worked or failed. Name only games you are sure about.
4. One idea nobody listed that would make these screens more fun or more interactive.
5. Score each of A, B, C from 1 to 10 for a solo tiny-budget team.

Do not copy any game's look. Say so if you do not know something.
