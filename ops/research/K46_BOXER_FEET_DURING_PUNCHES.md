## DECISIONS
Cross / straight right: do not hard-lock the rear heel. Rear ankle extends ~12° (DOCUMENTED); heel-lift height (cm), heel yaw (°), and rear-foot drag (cm) are NO STUDY. Soft-plant the ball; allow heel lift + yaw.
Cross: lead foot is the better lock candidate at impact — Elite GRF ~61% lead / 39% rear (DOCUMENTED). Lock lead after plant/brake, not during the step-in.
Planted jab: rear leg peaks higher than lead (Stanley 1.56 vs 0.63 N/kg DOCUMENTED). Prefer locking rear (or neither) over locking lead on a flat jab.
Step-jab: unlock lead for the step. Step length and elite step frequency = NO STUDY (coaching only ~5–8 cm SECONDARY).
Lead hook: unlock lead for ball pivot; rear hook: unlock rear. Pivot angles and translation (cm) = NO STUDY.
Uppercut / body: do not assume big foot slide. Impact CM height ~0.9 m across punches (DOCUMENTED); knee/level Δ cm = NO STUDY; GRF ~50/50 on uppercut.
Between punches: unlock both for bounce. Bounce amplitude (cm) and Hz = NO STUDY; only vertical-hip rate is documented (~47–56 /min).
Never invent heel-lift cm or pivot ° for the FOOT LOCK.

% of total GRF at impact — Dinu & Louis, 2020 (Elite n=15; IMU-estimated from CM projection) — DOCUMENTED
Punch	Lead %	Rear %
Cross	60.6 ± 24.9	39.4 ± 24.9
Hook	58.9 ± 25.5	41.1 ± 25.5
Uppercut	49.9 ± 14.6	50.1 ± 14.6

Junior cross: lead 54.1±7.1 / rear 45.9±7.1. Lead–rear imbalance significant for cross only.

Not foot BW% — Filimonov et al., 1985 (NSCA reprint) — DOCUMENTED but different construct

Masters: leg drive 38.46%, trunk 37.42%, arm 24.12% of punch force contribution — not % body weight on each foot. Do not use as stance load %.

Shift narrative — Tong-Iam et al., 2017 — DOCUMENTED mechanism

Stages: start → lead toe off → lead toe in. At LTI, lead GRF rises, rear falls; lead acts as brake/pivot while rear pushes. Matches “rear drive → front brace” without giving %BW time series.
