│ - Epic's MetaHuman skeleton follows Unreal Engine rigging architecture: the RIGHT side bones     │
│   (thigh_r, calf_r, foot_r, hand_r) have their local X and Y coordinate axes inverted 180°       │

│ - Mixamo, UAL, and general mocap rigs DO NOT invert right-side bone axes; both limbs share      │
│   identical local coordinate spaces.                                                             │
│ - In `FightClipRetarget.gd`, local rotational quaternions/Euler deltas calculated from standard  │
│   mocap are applied across both sides with identical signs.                                      │
│ - THE PROOF: In the gate metrics, the LEFT foot sole tilt is within tolerance (1.5-12% fail),   │
│   while the RIGHT foot is 94-97% out-of-band on EVERY body. A 180° axis flip mirrors pitch and  │
│   roll, transforming a standard 15° plantar arch into a 75° hyperextended ballet point!          │

2. What is NOT the Cause
The MetaHuman GLB Models Are NOT Corrupted:
MetaHuman skeletons exported from Unreal Engine to GLB conform strictly to glTF 2.0 specifications. The bone rests, bind matrices, and vertex weights are mathematically sound.
The 341 Joint Count Is NOT Causing the Foot Ballet:
Helper and twist bones do not independently fire unless driven. The foot pointing occurs on foot_r and ball_r, which are primary kinematic chain bones, not twist helpers.
Godot 4.7’s GLB Importer Is NOT Inverting Bones on Load:
Godot faithfully preserves the bone rest bases defined in the glTF file. It does not arbitrarily flip right-side axes. The inversion exists because the source model is an Unreal-native rig.
Metric vs. Imperial Scale Is NOT the Primary Rotational Problem:
While unit mismatches (cm to meters) can cause stride overextension (explaining the 2.4-shoulder-width stance), pure scaling errors affect translation, not angular pitch/roll inversions on a single limb.
3. The Single Fix to Implement First (The Silver Bullet)

Fix the Right-Side Local Basis Transform in FightClipRetarget.gd or normalize bone rests.

BEFORE FIX (S223):
Source Delta (Pitch +20°) ──► Applied to Left Foot  (Rest Z-Up) ──► Pitch +20° (Normal Heel Lift)
                          ──► Applied to Right Foot (Rest Z-Down) ──► Pitch -20° or Roll Inversion 
                              ──► 75° Ballet Plantarflexion Point!

AFTER FIX:
Source Delta (Pitch +20°) ──► Basis Inversion Matrix ──► Pitch +20° (Symmetric Heel Lift on Both Feet)

How to Prove It Worked (Acceptance Test):

Run Godot/tools/foot_orientation_probe.gd on codex/flyfoot1:

Pass Criterion: Under identical seeds, RIGHT sole tilt max drops immediately from $55^\circ$–$88^\circ$ down to $15^\circ$–$25^\circ$, matching the left foot, with $0%$ of frames outside the acceptable tolerance band.
4. Should Our Pipeline Be Replaced?

YES. IT MUST BE REPLACED IMMEDIATELY.

No professional studio running a high-fidelity sports title executes runtime retargeting across 500 heterogeneous mocap clips in GDScript using 49 procedural bone-writing scripts.

What Real Game Studios Do:
Unified Target Skeleton: Pick ONE master skeleton: Epic MetaHuman Medium Normal Male (mh_rig_master).
Offline Batch Retargeting: Use an offline tool (e.g., Cascadeur, Blender Auto-Rig Pro / Remap, or MotionBuilder) to retarget and clean all 500 clips offline.
Export as Native GLB Animations: Save clips as native .res or .glb animation libraries matching the MetaHuman bone names and axes 1:1.
Zero Runtime Retargeting: In-engine code does zero mathematical basis adjustments. It simply plays the clip on the skeleton.
