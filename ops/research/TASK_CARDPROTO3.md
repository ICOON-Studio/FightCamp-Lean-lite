## Part A - five shipped effects that are wrong (fix first, with tests)
- D4 REFEREE: the point deduction is subtracted from the wrong boxer's card (the fouler gains a point). Deduct from the OFFENDER's own card. Two consecutive clinches = warning + 2 wind to the holder; the third = a point.
- D5 SIT ON THE STOOL: its cost lands on the opponent and the opponent's stool is a buff. The picker pays (self +20% first exchange taken, opponent draws one extra card).
- D2 FILM ODDS FROZEN: `habitChanceInHand` reads the full deck; use the unseen pool (deck + draw) so the printed chance climbs as his discards grow (0.60 -> 0.85 by exchange 5 of round 2). Show the change.
- D3 ZERO-DAMAGE CARDS COUNT AS LANDED (39.7% of "landed" events): split landed into punches and defensive wins; judges score punches on damage, defence on exchanges won without being hit.
- D8 DEAD DATA: the seven `counter` blocks and `shape` are never read, yet printed on card faces. Either implement them (defensive points only when you win the exchange and take no damage) or delete them from data and UI. `shape` should build the resolution table so "hook beats straight" is a rule, not 144 hand-typed letters.
- Breathe's "first exchange is light" applies at exchange 1 of every round: scope it to the first exchange after the corner only.
- A just-landed knockdown protection stays.

## Part B - depth (ordered, most important first)
1. THE TELL IS FREE AND DOMINANT: "play the card that beats the tell chip" wins 76.7% against the engine (random 35.5%, the engine's own chooser 51.6%). Information must be a scarce resource: the chip names a SHAPE category only ("loading a looping shot"), correct about 60% (never the exact opposite when wrong), ONE free look per round, further looks cost 4 wind. Target: the tell-following one-rule bot scores 58-62% over 10,000 bouts; a reading player (film + tell + wind) beats it. Add the tell-follower and the largest-damage policy as reference arms in `balance.mjs` and fail the run if either beats the engine by more than 20 points.
2. TABLE: only 1 W and 1 X among 144 cells, so "clean win" and "countered" never happen. Make at least 8 W/X pairs with a named reason shown in the log: Cross beats Jab at mid/inside; Slip counters Cross and Lead hook; Uppercut counters Slip and Roll (slipping into it); Cut off counters Step back and Pivot out is NOT free - Pivot out must lose to something (Lead hook catches the turn); Roll counters Body hook; Clinch smothers Uppercut. Keep it anti-symmetric. Every card beats 2+ cards and loses to 2+; none over 55% when played.
3. DEFENCE MUST PAY WHEN READ: a correct Predict with a defence card converts to W AND applies its effect (Block refunds 2 wind, Slip/Roll set an opening, Step back moves out, Clinch drains 4). Target: Predict-when-a-read-is-active scores at least all-React on the same seeds.
4. COMBINATIONS: up to 3 cards in one exchange, cost = sum + 1 wind per extra card, resolved in order against his card (jab-cross, jab-cross-hook, slip-then-cross counter). Jab sets up only on a win, not a trade.
5. NEW CARDS: Feint (2 wind, no damage, next punch +accuracy, cancelled by a clean hit), Catch breath (once a round, restore 6 wind, vulnerable to body shots), Check hook (beats Cut off / Clinch / pressure), Pull counter (beats jab and cross at range, loses to body hook), Cross counter. Write each one's counter first in the table.
6. WIND MUST BIND: no exchange ever lacked wind in 14,175. Raise costs about 40% and cut between-round recovery to +8 so a round really spends the tank; target 3-6% of exchanges offer a card he cannot afford, and Breathe becomes a real tempo choice.
7. RANGE MUST COST: out 10%, mid 65%, inside 25%, forcing a range moves a policy under 1 pp. Each card gets a range it is thrown from; out of range = -1 extra wind and -6 accuracy; add a ropes state after two Cut-off wins (Step back off the table). Target: forcing inside moves a policy 6+ pp.
8. CAMP AS CONSTRUCTION: each pick is add one AND remove one; deck cap 16; Double-jab drill converts up to 2 cards into Jabs, pads convert 1; a drill cannot be offered twice; Ring craft (-7.2 pp) and Head movement (-6.6 pp) are trap drills: re-price them. Target: an informed camp beats a random camp by 5+ pp.
9. AMATEUR BOUT: no draws (a level card goes to whoever won more exchanges), five judges, each judge's taste shown as a one-line card, round scores shown after every round. Target: draws 0%, judges-vs-damage disagreement at most 12%.
10. THE SHIPPED GAME MATCHES THE TESTED GAME: `startFight` never trains the opponent while balance.mjs does. Give the opponent its camp in the game, show it in the scouting file, and have balance.mjs run exactly what the UI runs.
11. HOUSE RULES (tests): opponents come from OTHER clubs, never your own boxers or the same pairing twice in a row; no fixed "he" in text (the boxer's own data decides her/him; otherwise "the opponent"); names only in headers.
12. Corner offers need at least two competitive picks in most offers (the AI picked Gloves high 37% of the time and the stool 2%); Gloves high vs Breathe etc. must be honest trades.
