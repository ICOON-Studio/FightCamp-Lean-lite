## Why
Stage S72 lets a retiring fighter stay on at the player's gym as a coach. Grok's G91 (`research/G91_FROM_FIGHTER_TO_COACH.md`)
found two numbers NOT FOUND: (1) the SHARE of retired professional boxers / MMA / kickboxing / Muay Thai fighters who go on to

## Questions
1. **A counted sample.** BoxRec lists trainers and managers as people with their own pages, often linked to their own boxing
   record. Take a defined sample of retired professional boxers (e.g. every boxer who held a world title in three named
   divisions between 1985 and 2005, or every British title holder in a decade - define it, name the rule) and count how many
   later appear as a TRAINER of a professional boxer. Report n, k, k/n, the rule, the date read. Do the same for a defined MMA
   sample (e.g. UFC fighters retired 2008-2016 with 10+ UFC bouts) using Sherdog/Tapology coach listings or gym staff pages.
2. **The undercard.** The same count on a sample of journeymen (e.g. boxers with 20+ losses) - G91 says it is rare; is it?
3. **The lag.** For every coach found in 1-2, the year of the last bout and the first year they appear training a pro. Report
   the distribution (median, share within 1 year, within 3, over 5).
4. **Player-coaches.** How many were already cornering/training others BEFORE the last bout (a date on a corner or a trainer
   credit that precedes their retirement)?

## Rules
Every number SOURCED (link + date read) or NOT FOUND. State each sample's rule before its count, and its size. A number that
cannot be counted is NOT FOUND - never estimated. Money figures carry year and currency.
