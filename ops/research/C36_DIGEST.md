# C36 digest — FORMER under OFFICE

Built FORMER as the fourth OFFICE tab because the retirement list grows across
the career and does not belong on the already-dense desk. It reads
`get_retirements()` newest first, shows `name · retired age · retired week`,
uses an em dash for missing figures, and routes the stored `athlete_id` to the
existing `fighter_card` profile. No belt note was added because the retirement
record does not carry one; that remains available for a later save-shape task.
