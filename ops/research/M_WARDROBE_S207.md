[send-message t45s383] ## 2. Variety rule

**What changes per person**
| Axis | Minimum choices | Reads from the management camera? |
|---|---|---|
| Body build | the 23 bodies grouped into 5 builds: lean, athletic, heavy, older, small | yes, by silhouette |
| Skin tone | 6 (the S206 range) | yes |
| Hair style | 10: shaved, buzz, crop, fade, curls, afro, ponytail, bun, braids, bald | yes, it's what the camera sees most |
| Hair colour | 6 NEW: black `#1c1916`, dark brown `#3b2a20`, brown `#6a4a32`, blond `#c9a66b`, red `#9c4a2a`, grey `#a8a6a1` | yes |
| Outfit variant | 3–5 per role | yes |
| Colour variant | 3+ per piece within the role's family | yes |
| Accessories | 0–2 from the role's list | mostly close-up, but headwear reads from above |

**The rules**
1. Colour family and one signature piece fix each role, and variety only happens inside the family. Coaches are navy, the clerk is black with gold, the physio is white with mint, and the sparring partner is blue with headgear.
2. Each person gets a seeded combination of build, skin, hair style, hair colour, outfit, colourway and accessories.
3. The read-from-above signature is top mesh + top colour + head (hair or headwear) + build. No two people on the floor share a signature.
4. Within one zone (bag line, ring, cardio), no two people share even the top colour + head pair.
5. Roster fighters are hand-authored, not seeded. Each one's signature colour is unique in the gym, and no member wears it as a top.

**Minimum counts for a gym of 40**
- **Target:** at least 120 distinct signatures (3 per person), so the seed never has to force a repeat.
- **Members** (about 25 people): 5 outfits × 6 top colours × 10 hair styles × 6 hair colours × 5 builds gives about 9,000 combinations.
- **Uniformed roles** (about 6 people): their colour can't change, so 3–4 outfits × 60 hair combinations × 5 builds gives 900+ per role.
- **The hard floor:** at least 6 member top colours and at least 8 hair styles. With fewer, a zone of 8 people starts showing twins. With 6 × 10 there are 60 top-and-head pairs per zone, which is plenty.

## 4. Club uniform
The club colours are brick `#b8402e`, cream `#efe4cf` and black `#22201f`, with gold `#f0c233` as a small accent. Coaches keep navy and get club brick on the yoke.

**Logo (EXAMPLE):** a round crest with crossed gloves on a brick field inside a gold ring, and the gym name in cream on the ring. The single-colour version is cream on dark or brick on light. It must read as a badge at 2 cm in close-up. From above, the camera sees the coloured yoke, not the crest.

- **Staff polo (T3):** black with a brick collar and cuffs. It has an 8 cm crest on the left chest, the gym name 3 cm high across the back of the neck, and the gold name tag on the right chest. From above it reads as black with a brick collar ring.
- **Coach jacket (T5):** navy with a brick shoulder yoke and cream piping. It has a 9 cm crest on the left chest, a 28 cm crest between the shoulder blades and "COACH" in cream below it. The 3/4 camera looks straight at the yoke and back, so a coach reads from across the gym.
- **Fighter robe (A9):** satin-look flat colour in the fighter's signature colour with a slight gloss gradient, cream hood and cuff trim, and a gold sash. His name runs large in cream across the back, with the crest under it and a 10 cm crest on the left chest. From above, the hood and the name are what show.
- **Club tee (T1):** brick with a 22 cm chest crest and a small cream crest at the back of the neck. The kids class gets a sunflower edition and the women's class a plum edition.
- **Club hoodie (T4):** brick or black with a large cream crest on the back, for the locker room and fight-night arrival.

**Close-up rules:** collars, seams and zips are modelled shapes with bevelled cuffs and hems, never painted on. Fabric gets a subtle gradient and baked occlusion only, and logos are flat-colour decals, never photo textures.
