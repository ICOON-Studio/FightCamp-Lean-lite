# Kalli - game design: corner ice and cut work as a player choice (S321)

FightCamp is a PC boxing gym-management sim. In the 60-second corner between rounds the player (the boxer's coach/manager)
already picks a call for the next round and the cutman's ONE job (enswell left eye / enswell right eye / close the cut /
nothing). The operator proposes: the boxer's figure on the side panel shows blood that runs from an open cut; the player
taps the figure to choose where to work and where to ice "to calm the fighter and gain condition".
Real-world limits (our research): ice on a swelling slows it (eye stays open); ice does not close a cut (pressure +
adrenaline 1:1000 does, 10-30 s); ice on the neck lowers how tired she feels but gives no measurable heart-rate gain in 60 s,
so ice must NOT refill stamina. Amateur bouts have no cutman: one person in the ring does cuts AND coaching.
Claude's proposal (attached plan): one tap per job; cutman works one place, second ices one place (neck = +composure, or a
swelling); amateur coach who works the cut gives only one instruction instead of two; an open cut bleeds into her eye next
round and can bring the doctor at a stoppage.

Answer in full, recommendation first:
1. Is this FUN across a career (up to 12 corners a fight, dozens of fights)? Where does it get repetitive and how do we stop it?
2. What makes the dilemma real (two bad places, one pair of hands)? How often should a corner have a real choice?
3. What should the player SEE right after the choice (this corner and next round) so the choice feels like it mattered?
4. What would you cut, change or add? Name real game-design patterns, never copy a specific game.
