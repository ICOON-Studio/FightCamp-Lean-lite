## What would a human do? (layout order)
Ring or cage first - immovable, needs height, sightlines and a walkway. Then ONE contiguous mat block (grappling cannot be
split). Then the bag line on a ceiling/wall grid along a long wall. Then S&C (racks face a wall/mirror, never a walkway).
Cardio and recovery last, against the remaining walls. Circulation rings the combat piece; never dead-end a bag row.
SOURCED fit-out practice (compoundfitness.com.au) + JUDGEMENT.

## 3. Density
- 8-20 hanging bags on a commercial boxing floor; a 10-bag row is ~37-46 m2 (startpermit, compoundfitness). One bag per
  two class members at peak is JUDGEMENT.
- MMA: 40-60% of the training floor is mats (spartanathletique; Creed 279 of 465 m2 = 60%).
- Walkways 0.9 m minimum, 1.2-1.5 m main aisle; >= 1.0 m clear around a ring or cage.

## 4. Recommended presets (use zones + 15% circulation, JUDGEMENT)
- **Tier-1 boxing ~48 m2:** 2 heavy bags, 1 speed bag, 1 double-end, skipping, stretch corner, 1 air bike. No ring.
- **Tier-1 MMA ~50 m2:** 5 x 5 m mat, 1 heavy bag, 1 dummy, stretch corner, 1 bike. No cage.
- **Tier-2 boxing ~280 m2:** 16 ft ring (64), 8 bags (72), 2 speed, 2 double-end, skipping, 1 rack, 2 bikes, 1 rower, stretch.
- **Tier-2 MMA ~300 m2:** 20 ft cage (72), 8 x 8 mat block (64), 6 bags (54), 1 dummy, 1 rack, bike + rower, skipping, stretch.
Snap use zones to the 1 m grid; bags on 3 m centres, not 1 m; the ring/cage is one block, never fragmented.

## Claude's note (S83) - what this changes for S84
Our catalogue's footprints are DEVICE sizes, not use zones: `heavy_bag` is 1x2 cells against a real 3x3 use zone,
`club_ring` 4x4 against a real 8x8 (16 ft). The starter floor's "45 floor spaces" therefore holds far more kit than a real
48 m2 back room could. S84 draws each item at its DEVICE size inside its USE ZONE, and the tier presets follow section 4.
Whether the catalogue footprints (and so floor capacity) change is a balance decision for S84 - the draw must be honest
either way.
