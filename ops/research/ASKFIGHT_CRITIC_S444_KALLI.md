## 5. To reach 10/10

Most important first. Each line is specific enough to build.

1. **Stop picking down the ladder.** A NAME YOU PICK reaches up to 4 places above, or level, never below. (The other way: below is allowed, but a win over a lower-placed picked name does not move the ladder. That needs a RankLine rule the game does not have yet.) This is the probe's smallest change that removes the always-best. Keep the $450. Do not raise it to $1,800.

2. **Only call it a step up when it is one.** For an unranked fighter, "nearest better record" must not offer a softer night than the sheet name. Define the step up by rating, or drop the card when that name is not actually harder. The chance band will already show the difference; the title must not lie.

3. **Print the camp the date actually allows.** Three lines, from the game's own phases: "3 weeks or more: camp can be set" (BUILD); "2 weeks: PEAK camp on this date"; "fight week: no camp, no tape". A 14-day show is PEAK, not "no camp".

4. **When the scale forecast misses on that weigh-in, do not leave Ask armed.** Close with the reason, or move the date the way the sheet already does (makeable_day). A "Weight at risk" chip beside a live booking button is how a $300 empty slot happens.

5. **Close the ask until the first bout is fought** (pack 2.5), with the reason and the day it opens.

6. **Set the teaching words from the measured range** (0.47 to 1.05), so the three cards can differ. The probe's 0.6 / 0.85 split is the starting cut. A word that reads the same on every card comes off.

7. **Do not switch the lead to 7 days in this map.** Measure AMATEURWEEK1 first. The probe did not. A 7-day night is fight week.

8. **Do not raise step teaching.** x1 is the setting that keeps GROWTH a choice. x1.5 makes STEP win 39 of 60.

9. **Re-run the cases the probe did not actually test, before a drawing:** the map's 4-1 fighter on day 40 (not a 2-0), and a style pick (plan already written for pressure, presser two places up, name above a counter) without a win-chance oracle. A host refusal with a reason can wait.

Without the reach rule I would not score this above 6, and I would not defend it. The 10 needs that rule, an honest step-up label, and the camp and scale lines to match the game.
