## Grok's answer (verbatim)

No motion-capture or grip-locus study exists for these five holds. Judo papers measure gi collar/sleeve time, not body-segment placement. Rows are **DERIVED** from instructionals that show the grip, or **JUDGEMENT** for the 0–1 locus. "MEASURED" is unused.

Along-segment: 0 = proximal (shoulder / hip crease / skull base), 1 = distal (elbow / knee / neck base). MMA, no-gi.

### 1. Mount (top controlling)

Default: low–mid mount, knees pinched on ribs, hips heavy. Two schools disagree on the feet.

| limb | who | other-man segment | along | grip type | freq | label | shown by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L+R hand | top | hip or mat beside head | hip ~0.2 / post | posting | usually | DERIVED | Nicky Ryan: posts on mat, sits weight back vs kipping; not both hands glued to chest |
| 1 hand | top | head (occiput / collar-tie) | 0.1–0.3 | cupping | usually (no-gi) | DERIVED | Ryan collar-tie lift vs kip; Danaher chest-wrap school uses this less |
| 1 hand | top | upper back / far lat (underhook or chest wrap) | 0.2–0.4 | wrapping | usually | DERIVED | Danaher 4×4: single/double chest wrap, far-trap underhook |
| L+R foot | top | thigh (inside, grapevine) | 0.3–0.5 | hooking | sometimes (MMA / old school) | DERIVED | Gracie/Danaher Mastering Jujitsu; common G&P flatten |
| L+R foot | top | own other foot / mat (toes in, not crossed) | — | posting | usually (modern no-gi) | DERIVED | Ryan: toes touch, ankles not crossed; crossing dumps weight forward |
| L+R hand | bottom | hip | 0.1–0.3 | posting / framing | always | DERIVED | standard elbow-escape frame; BJJ Graph mount-bottom |
| L+R forearm | bottom | waist / lower ribs | 0.0–0.2 | posting | usually | DERIVED | elbows inside top's knees; Fighter's Notebook |
| L+R foot | bottom | mat | — | posting | always | JUDGEMENT | legs flat so top cannot grapevine |

**School split:** grapevine (MMA flatten, kills bridge) vs modern no-gi "hooks/toes-together, no crossed ankles" (Ryan; some gyms call grapevine easy to invert). Danaher control is arms (underhook / chest wrap), not feet.

**Animator miss:** crossed ankles behind the hips (looks "locked," actually loads the chest and invites the kip). Hands parked on the sternum instead of one collar-tie + one post. Grapevines drawn on the calves instead of mid-thigh. Bottom's frames on the chest (armbar bait) instead of the iliac crests.

### 2. Closed guard (bottom attacking + top postured)

Default no-gi attack: posture already contested, not a finished triangle. Top is vertical, not broken.

| limb | who | other-man segment | along | grip type | freq | label | shown by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L+R foot | bottom | waist / lower back | 0.0–0.2 | wrapping + crossed ankles | always | DERIVED | every closed-guard definition; heels into lumbar |
| 1 arm | bottom | upper arm (overhook) | 0.3–0.6 | wrapping | usually (attacking school) | DERIVED | Ffion Davies, Fornarino, Fowler: overhook system |
| other hand | bottom | head / neck | 0.0–0.3 | cupping | usually | DERIVED | same school: head control + overhook |
| both hands | bottom | wrist + forearm (2-on-1) | wrist 1.0 / forearm 0.7 | cupping | sometimes | DERIVED | alternate no-gi school; HubPages / 2-on-1 posture break |
| 1 hand | bottom | head (pull from crown/occiput) | 0.0–0.2 | cupping | sometimes | DERIVED | Reddit/coaching: hang on the skull when they posture |
| L+R hand | top | hip | 0.1–0.3 | posting | usually | DERIVED | posture vs closed guard; elbows on thighs |
| L+R hand | top | upper arm (biceps ride) | 0.4–0.7 | posting | sometimes | DERIVED | Mark Hatmaker MMA Mastery: palms on biceps, thumbs down |
| L+R foot | top | mat (knees or stood) | — | posting | always | DERIVED | Gordon Ryan: stand; dangers of being pulled forward > being knocked back |

**School split:** overhook + head control (Davies / Fornarino / classic attack) vs 2-on-1 / elbow-drag then overhook vs "don't play closed guard in no-gi — stand" (Ryan). Collar-tie equivalent from bottom is the occiput cup, not a gi collar.

**Animator miss:** bottom wrists glued to top's wrists at 1.0 (the C170 failure mode) instead of an overhook at mid-upper-arm + a head cup. Top's hands on the chest (feeds triangles) instead of hips or biceps. Ankle lock drawn on the ankle of the top man — closed-guard feet hold the waist, not the legs. Uncrossed feet = not closed guard.

### 3. Half guard

Default controlling picture: top has one leg trapped, trying to flatten; bottom is on a side with a knee shield (Z-guard), not already smashed.

| limb | who | other-man segment | along | grip type | freq | label | shown by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 hand | top | neck / jaw (crossface) | 0.3–0.6 | wrapping / posting | usually | DERIVED | standard half-pass; BJJ Graph crossface pass |
| other hand | top | upper arm or far hip | arm 0.2 / hip 0.1 | wrapping or posting | usually | DERIVED | underhook or hip staple |
| free foot | top | mat | — | posting | always | JUDGEMENT | base |
| trapped foot/shin | top | trapped in bottom's legs | thigh ~0.5 | — (held) | always | DERIVED | definition of half |
| 1 shin (knee shield) | bottom | waist / lower ribs / sternum | 0.0–0.3 | posting (bone frame) | usually | DERIVED | Danaher GFF half; Fowler; knee-to-sternum |
| bottom hook foot | bottom | thigh (far / trapped leg) | 0.4–0.7 | hooking | always | DERIVED | half-guard hook |
| inside arm | bottom | upper arm / armpit (frame or underhook) | 0.1–0.4 | posting or wrapping | usually | DERIVED | frame on bicep vs swim to far lat |
| outside hand | bottom | head, wrist, or far hip | varies | cupping / posting | usually | DERIVED | no-gi: head/wrist; blocks crossface |

**School split:** knee-shield distance game (Danaher / Fowler) vs smashed half + tight-waist underhook (dogfight). Top's "control" is crossface + hip; if bottom wins the underhook to the far lat (~0.2 on upper back), top is no longer controlling.

**Animator miss:** bottom's shield foot on the hip bone like open guard (it's a shin across the torso). Top's free hand on the neck and both of bottom's wrists. Hook drawn at the ankle instead of mid-inner-thigh. Both men square and flat — real half is angled.

### 4. Side control

Default: perpendicular chest-to-chest, crossface + far underhook (kesa and north-south are not this hold).

| limb | who | other-man segment | along | grip type | freq | label | shown by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| near arm (head side) | top | neck / jaw / far trap | 0.2–0.6 | wrapping (crossface) | always | DERIVED | NAGA / BJJ Graph / every basic pin |
| far arm | top | upper back / far lat (underhook) | 0.1–0.3 | wrapping | usually | DERIVED | same; kickstand underhook variant posts fingers out |
| near hand (alt) | top | hip (far hip block) | 0.0–0.2 | posting | usually | DERIVED | "3-point staple": knee + elbow + chest on hip |
| near knee | top | hip / glute | 0.0–0.2 | posting | usually | DERIVED | kickstand / hip staple videos |
| far leg | top | mat (sprawl) | — | posting | usually | DERIVED | base vs shrimp |
| near forearm | bottom | neck / shoulder of top | 0.0–0.3 | posting | always | DERIVED | frame on shoulder, not throat punch |
| far forearm | bottom | hip / waist of top | 0.0–0.2 | posting | always | DERIVED | Heisman / hip frame |
| L+R foot | bottom | mat | — | posting | always | JUDGEMENT | shrimp platform |

**School split:** classic gable-grip underhook vs "kickstand" underhook (fingers out, more mobile). Some no-gi pins drop the underhook and staple the far hip with a hand + near knee.

**Animator miss:** both of top's hands clasped under the head like a gi clock-choke. Crossface drawn across the mouth instead of jaw-to-far-trap. Far hand floating instead of on lat or far hip. Bottom's frames on the chest (kimura gift) instead of shoulder + hip. Top's hips floating off the near hip — that's knee-on-belly, not side control.

### 5. Back control (seat-belt + hooks)

Default: chest on back, diagonal seat-belt, two hooks inside the thighs. Body triangle is a sibling, not this hold.

| limb | who | other-man segment | along | grip type | freq | label | shown by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| over-arm hand | top | upper chest / far pec | ~0.3 on torso | wrapping (seat-belt, hands clasped) | always | DERIVED | every seat-belt definition |
| under-arm hand | top | opposite chest (meets over-hand) | ~0.3 | wrapping | always | DERIVED | gable / palm-to-palm / wrist-grab on own hand |
| over-arm | top | contacts neck / far shoulder | 0.0–0.3 neck | wrapping | always | DERIVED | the "over" half of the harness |
| under-arm | top | through armpit onto ribs | 0.0 upper arm / ribs | wrapping | always | DERIVED | |
| L foot | top | thigh (inner) | 0.25–0.50 | hooking | always | DERIVED | hooks inside thighs, not over hips |
| R foot | top | thigh (inner) | 0.25–0.50 | hooking | always | DERIVED | heels pull in; feet not crossed |
| L+R hand | bottom | top's over-arm wrist / forearm | 0.8–1.0 | cupping | always | DERIVED | defend the choking arm first |
| 1 hand | bottom | own chin / neck | — | posting | usually | DERIVED | chin-to-chest |
| L+R foot | bottom | mat or top's hook | — | posting / peeling | sometimes | DERIVED | clear hooks by clapping knees |

**School split:** two hooks (IBJJF points, this task) vs body triangle (many no-gi players, not requested). Seat-belt hands: clasp on the chest, not the belt line. Corey Guitard hierarchy: cross-wrist control on bottom's hands beats straight same-side wrist when attacking; that is offense, not the default hold.

**Animator miss:** feet crossed in front of the hips (ankle-lock gift, also weak). Hooks at the knees (shallow — they pop). Seat-belt clasped on the waist. Both of bottom's hands on the underhook arm (the over-arm is the choke). Top's head stacked on the crown (head-butt / peek-out) instead of tucked beside the ear.

**For the solver:** grips are surface contacts on the other body. Top's posts on the mat are not GRIP rows for the contact solver — only the rows whose segment is the other man. Highest-confidence always-grips: closed-guard crossed ankles on waist; side-control crossface on neck; back hooks on inner thigh ~0.3–0.5 + seat-belt clasp on chest; mount-bottom hands on hips.
