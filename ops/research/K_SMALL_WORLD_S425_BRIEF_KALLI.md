# A small living world with a belt ladder (S425, Kalli)

**Answer first.** One national pro ladder per division, five rungs (Debut, Ranked, Top 10, Area belt, British belt), with one mandatory challenger and one final eliminator, both at the British belt. The world gives each of your boxers **at most one decision a month** and always something dated to wait for. The decisions come from five world events: a mandatory named, an eliminator offered, a rival moving, a champion vacating, and a judge panel announced. Three named rivals per division (the Rival, the Prospect, the Champion) fight, age and study your tape while you're not looking, and you hear only what changes an option. Judges have one public lean each (Work rate, Clean shots, Ring control), shown before the bout, and it only decides close rounds. It all hooks into the week design: the tape window before a mandatory, the open spar with a ranked rival, the read war, and the cut thread on title nights.

Sources: ops/research/CRITIC_PACK.md (fetched today, read whole) and ops/research/BOXING_MANAGER_COMPARE_S425.md (found, read whole; its "take" items 1, 2, 4, 6, 7 and 8 are used here). Number tags: **REAL** (a source I have), **GAME** (CRITIC_PACK section), **INVENTED** (a design value to tune), **CHECK (John)** (a real-world rule I'm not sure of). I have no BBBofC rulebook, so every BBBofC rule below is CHECK.

**What exists already (GAME 4.14, 4.5, 8):** world pro tables for four divisions (about 20 ranked each), ageing, peak, decline and retirement, new boxers each year, belts at four levels (area, national, continental, world) with mandatories and vacated belts, rivals who improve, the rank rule, promoter offers (TUNE-UP, STEP UP, PAYDAY), Ask for a named boxer, the $250 scout, rounds 4 / 6 / 8 / 10, purse $1,500 + $400 a place, the gym's 25% share, contract talks at the first top 10 and the first belt. The screens for this are on Fights, not World. Rival gym, poaching and the career clock are AGREED, NOT BUILT. So most of this page relabels and cuts rules that exist and adds the decisions on top.

## 1. What the world gives you each month

**What you wait for** sits on Home > COMING UP as dated lines: "Mandatory due by Fri 14 Aug", "Panel named Mon 3 Aug", "Champion Okafor, 34, last 2 bouts lost on the cards". **The decision** comes as one flag on the top bar that stops the clock on its last day (existing rule: nothing opens by itself). Cap: one world flag per boxer a month (INVENTED). A second event that month waits as a COMING UP line.

| Event | Option | Right when | What it changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mandatory named** (you hold the British belt) | Fight the mandatory now | Your boxer is fresh, and your best lines are off the tape (not used 2+ a round in the last 2 bouts) | Belt on the line. A win resets the mandatory clock (12 months, INVENTED; CHECK John) |
| | A voluntary defence first, inside the window | The mandatory is a bad style match, and one more fight gets a card to Sharp (the switch opens, week design 2.1) | A purse now (champion rate). The mandatory gets one more of your bouts on tape |
| | Vacate | Your boxer is 33+ and declining (WorldAge arrow down), or the purse offered for the mandatory is under the cost of a 12-round camp | The belt goes vacant, your boxer keeps a top-3 place (INVENTED), and the Lonsdale count stops |
| **Mandatory named** (your boxer is the mandatory) | Wait for the shot, one stay-busy fight at most | Your boxer beats the champion's style on the Playbook's Rate against | A dated title fight. A loss in the stay-busy fight loses the slot |
| | Take step-aside money | Your boxer is hurt, scarred 40+ (cut thread), or the champion's style is wrong this year | $2,000 to the gym (INVENTED) and next in line after the champion's voluntary (step-aside: CHECK John) |
| **Final eliminator offered** (your boxer is top 5) | Accept | Tape study on that opponent found the weakness, and your boxer holds less of their tape than they hold of yours (World > rival file) | The winner is named mandatory. The loser drops below the winner (rank rule, GAME 4.5) |
| | Ask for a different opponent from the top 5 | The offered one is a Cutter or a southpaw you haven't answered yet | The body answers with its reason (compare doc, take item 2). Yes in 1 in 2 (INVENTED). No means the eliminator goes to the next two names |
| | Pass and wait for a vacancy | The champion is old and declining, so a vacant-title pairing of the top 2 is likely inside 6 months | No risk now. Your boxer drops 3 places after 6 months without a fight (section 2) |
| **A named rival moves** (wins a place above you, changes gym or weight) | Ask for the rival now | Your boxer has the rival's weakness on file and the rival's new gym is still settling (first bout there) | The fight comes at STEP UP pay. Their place is yours to take (rank rule) |
| | Invite the rival's club to the Saturday open spar (week design 2.3) | An offer against them is likely inside a month | One of their tells found, one of your lines on their tape |
| | Let them climb | The rival is headed to the champion first, which hurts the winner (damage ledger, GAME 4.10) | Nothing now. The winner of that bout comes out worn |
| **Champion vacates** (retires, moves weight, or is stripped) | Take the vacant-title fight on the date offered | Your boxer is in BUILD or PEAK for another bout at the same weight, and on the scale | A title fight at short notice. No full camp, so no PEAK CAMP bonus (GAME 4.3) |
| | Ask for a later date (4 more weeks) | Your boxer needs a camp: a plan answer is still Raw | The body may pair the next name instead (1 in 3, INVENTED) |
| | Decline | Your boxer is hurt or suspended | Your place stays, and the winner becomes the new Champion rival |
| **Judge panel announced** (14 days before a top-10 or title night, INVENTED) | Write the plan toward the panel's lean | 2 or 3 judges share a lean and the bout looks close (win band 40 to 60%) | Close rounds tilt your way (section 4). A changed line costs its drill time |
| | Keep the plan | The bout isn't close: a stoppage or a wide win is likely | Nothing. The lean only touches close rounds |

**Menus:** Home (COMING UP lines, the flag as a need); Fights (the event card with the options, under OFFERS ON THE TABLE); Fights > bout (the panel on the terms, the purse step); World > Rankings (arrows, the mandatory and eliminator marks beside names); World > Championships (the belt, its holder, the mandatory, the clock); World > rival file; the week review (a met or missed world event as the last row). **Reused:** the belt, mandatory and vacancy rules, Ask for a fight with its reason, offers. **New:** the event cards and their options (M), the one-a-month cap (S), step-aside money (S).

## 2. The ladder

**How few rungs:** five. Three table steps and two belts are enough for a climb, because each rung changes the fight itself (rounds, opponent kind) and not just a number. English titles and the continental and world belts that exist in code stay switched off for the first release. Target: a well-trained boxer reaches a British title shot about 2 to 3 game years after the debut, at a pro bout every 8 to 12 weeks (INVENTED, to probe).

| Rung | How you get there | Rounds | Purse | Opens | New kind of opponent |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Debut, unranked | Turn pro (GAME 2.9) | 4 (GAME) | $1,500 (GAME) | The promoter's three offers (GAME 4.5) | Journeymen with no tape on you |
| 2 Ranked 11 to 20 | Beat a ranked boxer (rank rule, GAME) | 6 (GAME) | $1,500 + $400 a place (GAME) | Ask for a named boxer about 5 places up (GAME); the first named rival | Ring IQ "sharp" corners that read your lines (read war, week design 2.1) |
| 3 Top 10 | Take a top-10 place; needs a winning record (GAME) | 8 (GAME) | Same formula | Contract talks (GAME 4.13); judge panels announced; area title offers | Opponents who studied your tape window (last 2 bouts) |
| 4 Area champion | Win the area belt (area exists in code, GAME 4.14) | 10 (GAME title rounds) | $5,000 (INVENTED) | A belt sponsor at $400 a week while the belt is held (INVENTED; "bigger sponsors later", GAME 4.13); eligible for the eliminator | Other belt holders with a Seasoned cutman (cut thread) |
| 5 British champion | Win the British belt as mandatory or for a vacancy | 12 (new; British title 12 rounds: CHECK John) | $15,000 (INVENTED) | Contract talks at the first belt (GAME); the mandatory clock; the Lonsdale belt to keep after 3 British title wins (CHECK John) | The best of the table, a Name cutman, full tape on you |

**How a boxer falls:**

| Fall | Rule | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Loses to someone below | The winner takes the place | GAME (rank rule from the winner's side) |
| Losing record | Never top 10 | GAME 4.5 |
| Stoppage loss | 30-day suspension, longer for repeats, then a "hard night" gap | GAME 4.10, 4.5 |
| Inactive | 6 months without a bout: down 3 places. 12 months: off the table | INVENTED |
| Champion with no defence | Stripped after 12 months, or for refusing the mandatory | INVENTED; CHECK John |
| Ducks | The promoter refuses you after you duck the step up twice | GAME 4.5 |
| Ages | Decline after the peak shows as a down arrow on the rival file and your own Overview | GAME (WorldAge) |

**Divisions:** one ladder per division the gym has a boxer in, same rules for men and women. Women only ever meet women. The code's men's limits (57.5 / 60 / 70 / 75, GAME 4.4) differ from the brief's provisional 57 / 60 / 67 / 75: CHECK (John) which one ships. Women's British titles: CHECK (John).

**Menus:** World > Championships (the five-rung ladder drawn as a climb with your boxer's tag on it); Fighters > Overview (NEXT STEP names the next rung and what it needs); Fights > bout (rounds and purse step); Club > Money (the belt sponsor). **Reused:** belts, rounds, purse, contract talks, the table. **New:** the five-rung cut and relabel of the national belt to British (S), 12 rounds (S), the belt sponsor (S), inactivity drops (S).

## 3. Rivals

**How many named:** three per division you have a boxer in, plus nobody else. The other 17 or so on the table are names with records, no story.

| Rival | Who | What they do while you're not looking |
|---|---|---|
| **The Rival** | Same age band as your boxer, from Frank Doyle's Eastside ABC (named in the news after the first bout, GAME 2.6), or from the rival gym once it exists (AGREED, NOT BUILT) | Fights on the same shows, climbs by the rank rule, studies your tape (holds your lines from the tape window), invites your boxer to call-outs |
| **The Prospect** | Younger, unbeaten, protected by its promoter (compare doc 2d) | Climbs fast on TUNE-UPs, improves each month (GAME), fixes a habit (it vanishes from your rival file) |
| **The Champion** | Holds the belt your boxer is climbing to | Defends, ages, declines, retires, moves weight or is stripped (GAME rules). When it goes, the new holder becomes the named Champion |

**What they do, per month (all from the world sim, nothing scripted):** fight (GAME), age (GAME), improve (GAME), win or lose belts (GAME), get hurt or suspended (GAME 4.10 rules), move gym (new, M), move up a weight (new, S), take a block of their own (new, S: a habit leaves your file), study your tape (new, S: from the week design).

**How you hear of it (only when it changes an option, owner rule in CRITIC_PACK 7):**
- **Home > SINCE LAST MONDAY:** one line with its cause and a button: "The Prospect beat no. 6 on points: now 1 place above Danny. Ask for the fight."
- **World > Rankings:** the movement arrow and the Developing column (agreed, CRITIC_PACK 6), with the three named faces marked.
- **World > rival file:** "since you last looked": a tell gone, a new gym, the lines they hold on your boxer.
- **The week review:** a rival's result as the last row only if it opens a world event.
- News that changes nothing (a rival wins a bout below your boxer) doesn't show.

**Reused:** the world sim, ageing, the rival file, news lines, Frank Doyle. **New:** the three named roles and their marks (S), rival tape on you (S, shared with the week design), rival blocks (S), gym and weight moves (M).

## 4. Judges with taste

**The rule:** the owner held back rules on scorecards the player can't see (R29, compare doc take item 8), so the lean is **public before the bout** and **only decides close rounds**. Three judges a pro bout (GAME 4.8). Each judge has one lean, one word:

| Lean | In a close round, gives it to | Your plan answer | Your corner answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Work rate | More punches thrown | Pace Push in that round (GAME 4.7) | "Let your hands go" (GAME 4.8) |
| Clean shots | More clean landed, more damage | Counter lines ("When they press, Step back, Cross") | "Breathe, pick your shots" |
| Ring control | More exchanges won while going forward or holding the centre | Pressing lines at mid range, Ropes lines | "Go to the body" while pressing; avoid "Tie up" (GAME 4.8) |

| Number | Value | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| A close round | the two boxers' round edges within 10% | INVENTED |
| Share of rounds that are close | target 20 to 30% | INVENTED (probe) |
| A full panel's worth in close fights | target 2 to 5 wins in 100, never more than the plan's 21 | INVENTED target; the plan's 21 is GAME (compare doc 5) |
| Panel named | 14 days before a top-10 or title night | INVENTED |
| A judge's lean strength | "slight" until you've seen 3 of that judge's cards, then "slight" or "strong" | INVENTED |
| Non-title British bouts scored by the referee alone | if true, one lean decides close rounds on 4 and 6 rounders | CHECK (John) |
| Amateur judges and scoring | no leans in the first build | CHECK (John) |

**How you learn it:** the panel's three names and lean words show on Fights > bout terms and on Fight prep (a "The panel" row beside "Their corner reads"). Strength fills in from cards you've seen: your own bouts, and World > News results with their cards. **What you do:** in camp, a plan line toward the lean (costs a plan place and its drill time); in the round, Pace and shouts; in the corner minute, the coach's read of the round ("Close. This panel likes work rate.") is one line on the corner card, not a scorecard (scorecards after each round are held back, CRITIC_PACK 6). **The Why:** "Round 4 was close; Hart and Ola like work rate and gave it to you. Push did that." **Reused:** the three scorecards, Pace, shouts, the corner card, the Why. **New:** judge leans and the close-round rule (M), the panel row (S).
