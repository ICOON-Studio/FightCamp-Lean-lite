FightCamp
Block type

FightCamp is a deep boxing gym management simulation for PC where you assume the role of the head coach and gym owner. Unlike traditional fighting games, you never throw the punches yourself; instead, you are responsible for the strategic decisions that shape a fighter's career. You manage a small gym, scouting ordinary kids and transforming them into professional athletes through rigorous training camps. The core experience focuses on the weight of leadership, where every victory on a fighter's record is a direct result of your preparation and corner-side guidance. The game aims to provide a realistic portrayal of the sport, blending management depth with high-stakes tactical decision-making during 3D-rendered fight nights.

The Corner and Tactical Control
Add Image, Video, 3D Asset & More...
Block type

The 3-minute 3D rounds play at 1x, 2x or 4x on one fight-night screen: each fighter's card top-left/top-right (head and stamina bars, a live pulse in BPM that turns green, amber or flashing red), round, clock and speed top-centre, up to 3 named opponent tells, and a THROW TOWEL tray bottom-right. There are no mid-round shouts. Between rounds the corner is one drawer read in under 10 seconds. STOOL: the fighter's state word (Focused, Complacent, Panicked, Groggy) from his pulse and pupils; you pick one of three tones (Calm, Fire Him Up, Tell Him the Truth) - the right tone restores composure, the wrong one backfires. Every crew lane has 2 action points; a rushed 3rd action risks a 35 percent blunder. CUTMAN: ice the swelling, swab the bleed (doctor risk minus 20 points), grease, or the deep coagulant pack (2 AP, doctor risk minus 45 points). Composure (0-100) drives plan adherence and guard: Focused 70+, Rattled 45-69, Shaken 20-44, Broken under 20 (a Broken fighter cannot fire his pad chains). Cuts run 0-5 pips per brow; at 3+ pips the referee can call the ringside doctor, who may stop it (35, 70 or 100 percent at 3, 4 or 5 pips, minus the cutman). If he lets it go on you choose: the towel (a loss, the eye heals clean) or wave him on (a win pays more, but finishing at 3+ pips leaves a permanent Cut-Prone scar).

The Training Lap
Add Image, Video, 3D Asset & More...
Block type

Training days are a lap on the top-down 3D gym floor: the clock runs (about 2 real minutes a day) and you send your fighter between stations - pads, heavy bag, speed bag, treadmill, bike, weights, the ring for sparring, defence drills and recovery. Each station targets attributes and costs stamina; training tired raises injury risk. The head coach stands at one station and boosts it. If you skip the rest of a day, your assistant coach runs it at 80 percent, shown in a one-second confirm drawer (amber warning if the weight plan is off). Move cards: you pick 3 techniques to drill; a card is usable after 120 drilled minutes and mastered at 600. A mastered card can be a 3-step PAD CHAIN (trigger -> evasion -> counter, e.g. opponent's lead jab -> slip outside -> right cross): in the live fight it fires on its own when the opponent gives the trigger (chance = mastery x ring IQ / 70, from 25 percent mastery, 4-second cooldown) with a banner, and the stool reports 'executed 2 of 3'. You build the script during the week and watch the boxer execute it on Saturday.

Camp Preparation and Strategy
Add Image, Video, 3D Asset & More...
Block type

A camp prepares one bout against a named opponent. Weeks 1-3: camp focus, a training protocol (hard sparring to scientific recovery), a sparring partner cast to look like the opponent, a visiting coach, film study depth 0-3 and a diet. The last week is the FIGHT WEEK, one decision a day: MONDAY pads and routes (program the pad chain; burns 35 percent of the week's stamina); TUESDAY hard sparring, 'lock one, sacrifice one' - permanently lock one weapon (+15 percent speed/crit for that drill) and sacrifice one defence (-10 percent guard against his secondary weapon); WEDNESDAY active recovery (rest to regenerate chin and snap, or light conditioning for the weight); THURSDAY film: 3 coach hours lock up to 3 NAMED tells (e.g. 'Drops his right hand when throwing the lead hook'); FRIDAY the weigh-in roulette: Safe Sweat (never misses, stamina x0.95), Sauna Push (10 percent miss, chin capped at 85 percent) or Extreme Drain (30 percent miss, +10 percent power, chin capped at 75 percent and flashing red); a miss forfeits 20 percent of the purse; SATURDAY fight night. A tell comes from sparring (TELL: counter window +15 percent) or from film (EXIT: trap defence +20 percent, clinch break +25 percent); the corner spends 1 action point to switch one on for the rest of the bout.

Fighter Attributes and Traits
Add Image, Video, 3D Asset & More...
Block type

Every fighter has power, speed, defense, conditioning, fight IQ, technique and chin on a 0-100 scale, a style (out_boxer, swarmer, counter_puncher, slugger, boxer_puncher), height, reach, stance and weight class, hidden potential and a limited career. Traits change how he fights (Iron Will, Heavy Hands, Stiff Neck, Track Legs, Point Scorer, Fragile Confidence, Touch of Sleep, Early Gasser, Glass Jaw). In the fight he has COMPOSURE 0-100: it starts at 30 + fight IQ x 0.35 + chin x 0.20 + traits (Iron Will +15, Fragile Confidence -15; clamped 20-90), falls with clean head shots, being rocked, knockdowns, cuts and time trapped on the ropes, and rises with fired pad chains, knockdowns scored and counters. Wear is permanent. Pushing through a bad cut leaves a Cut-Prone SCAR: Level 1 (cuts that eye 1.35x as easily, fades after 6 months with no cuts), Level 2 (1.75x; surgery for 1,200 dollars takes it back to Level 1), Level 3 'Torn Grasp' (2.5x, and he starts every fight already bleeding 1 pip).

Honest and Readable Outcomes
Add Image, Video, 3D Asset & More...
Block type

A core pillar of the game design is transparency through data. The user interface provides clear, readable outcomes for every fight to ensure the player never feels cheated by hidden mechanics. Post-fight summaries explicitly name the three pivotal moments that decided the match, accompanied by official scorecards and detailed punch statistics. During training, live stats panels and warnings from assistant coaches keep the player informed about camp progress and health risks. By eliminating 'dice you cannot see,' the UI reinforces the simulation's integrity, ensuring that every win or loss can be traced back to specific choices made during the training week or in the corner.

Fix 1: Micro-Tick Stat Accrual & Floating Feedback Engine
# gym_stat_accrual_engine.gd
# Pure rules engine: extends RefCounted, zero preloads, plain dicts in/out.
class_name GymStatAccrualEngine
extends RefCounted

const TICK_INTERVAL_GAME_SEC: float = 15.0
const COACH_BOOST_MULT: float = 1.35
const COACH_FATIGUE_MULT: float = 0.80

# Station attribute targets and base yields per 60 game-seconds
const STATION_PROFILES: Dictionary = {
	"heavy_bag": {"stat": "power", "rate_per_min": 0.8, "sta_drain_per_min": 12.0},
	"speed_bag": {"stat": "speed", "rate_per_min": 0.9, "sta_drain_per_min": 8.0},
	"pads": {"stat": "technique", "rate_per_min": 1.1, "sta_drain_per_min": 14.0},
	"weights": {"stat": "power", "rate_per_min": 1.0, "sta_drain_per_min": 16.0},
	"treadmill": {"stat": "conditioning", "rate_per_min": 1.2, "sta_drain_per_min": 10.0},
	"air_bike": {"stat": "conditioning", "rate_per_min": 1.4, "sta_drain_per_min": 15.0},
	"slip_rope": {"stat": "defense", "rate_per_min": 0.9, "sta_drain_per_min": 9.0},
	"mirror_skipping": {"stat": "fight_iq", "rate_per_min": 0.7, "sta_drain_per_min": 7.0},
	"stretch_corner": {"stat": "recovery", "rate_per_min": 0.5, "sta_drain_per_min": -10.0} # Restores stamina
}

# Evaluates elapsed frame delta and yields an event if an accrual boundary is crossed.
# state: {
#   "station_id": String,
#   "station_kind": String,
#   "elapsed_at_station": float,
#   "fighter_stamina": float,
#   "coach_present": bool,
#   "fighter_attributes": Dictionary
# }
static func process_station_tick(state: Dictionary, delta_game_sec: float) -> Dictionary:
	var out: Dictionary = {
		"tick_occurred": false,
		"elapsed_at_station": state.get("elapsed_at_station", 0.0) + delta_game_sec,
		"stat_gained_key": "",
		"stat_delta": 0.0,
		"stamina_delta": 0.0,
		"new_stamina": state.get("fighter_stamina", 100.0),
		"new_stat_val": 0.0,
		"is_coach_boosted": state.get("coach_present", false),
		"toast_message": ""
	}

	var prev_intervals: int = int(state.get("elapsed_at_station", 0.0) / TICK_INTERVAL_GAME_SEC)
	var next_intervals: int = int(out["elapsed_at_station"] / TICK_INTERVAL_GAME_SEC)

	if next_intervals <= prev_intervals:
		return out

	out["tick_occurred"] = true
	var kind: String = state.get("station_kind", "heavy_bag")
	var profile: Dictionary = STATION_PROFILES.get(kind, {"stat": "conditioning", "rate_per_min": 0.5, "sta_drain_per_min": 10.0})

	var fraction_of_min: float = TICK_INTERVAL_GAME_SEC / 60.0
	var coach_mult: float = COACH_BOOST_MULT if out["is_coach_boosted"] else 1.0
	var coach_sta_mult: float = COACH_FATIGUE_MULT if out["is_coach_boosted"] else 1.0

	var stat_gain: float = profile["rate_per_min"] * fraction_of_min * coach_mult
	var sta_drain: float = profile["sta_drain_per_min"] * fraction_of_min * coach_sta_mult

	# Clamp if fighter is completely tapped out
	var cur_sta: float = state.get("fighter_stamina", 100.0)
	if cur_sta <= 0.0 and sta_drain > 0.0:
		stat_gain *= 0.20 # Exhaustion penalty: 80% loss of gain efficiency

	var stat_name: String = profile["stat"]
	var cur_val: float = 0.0
	var attrs: Dictionary = state.get("fighter_attributes", {})
	if attrs.has(stat_name):
		cur_val = float(attrs[stat_name])

	out["stat_gained_key"] = stat_name
	out["stat_delta"] = snappedf(stat_gain, 0.01)
	out["stamina_delta"] = snappedf(-sta_drain, 0.01)
	out["new_stamina"] = maxf(0.0, snappedf(cur_sta - sta_drain, 0.01))
	out["new_stat_val"] = snappedf(cur_val + stat_gain, 0.01)

	var boost_str: String = " (Coach +35%)" if out["is_coach_boosted"] else ""

# gym_fatigue_hazard_engine.gd
# Extends RefCounted: Pure logic for handling exhaustion triggers, slow debuff, and emergency rests.
class_name GymFatigueHazardEngine
extends RefCounted

const CRITICAL_STAMINA_THRESHOLD: float = 25.0
const BASE_INJURY_ODDS_PER_MIN: float = 0.005 # 0.5%
const EXHAUSTED_INJURY_ODDS_PER_MIN: float = 0.020 # 2.0%

# Evaluates whether the fighter is currently in an exhaustion state.
static func evaluate_fatigue_hazard(fighter_state: Dictionary, delta_game_sec: float) -> Dictionary:
	var stamina: float = fighter_state.get("stamina", 100.0)
	var is_critical: bool = stamina <= CRITICAL_STAMINA_THRESHOLD
	var was_critical: bool = fighter_state.get("was_fatigue_critical", false)
	
	var out: Dictionary = {
		"is_critical": is_critical,
		"entered_critical": is_critical and not was_critical,
		"exit_critical": not is_critical and was_critical,
		"speed_penalty_factor": 0.60 if is_critical else 1.0,
		"injury_triggered": false,
		"injury_type": "",
		"warning_text": ""
	}

	if is_critical:
		out["warning_text"] = "EXHAUSTED (Stamina < 25%). High Injury Risk!"
		var fraction_of_min: float = delta_game_sec / 60.0
		var check_roll: float = randf()
		if check_roll < (EXHAUSTED_INJURY_ODDS_PER_MIN * fraction_of_min):
			out["injury_triggered"] = true
			out["injury_type"] = select_exhaustion_injury(fighter_state.get("current_station_kind", "heavy_bag"))
			out["warning_text"] = "INJURY! Sustained %s due to overwork!" % out["injury_type"]

	return out

static func select_exhaustion_injury(station_kind: String) -> String:
	match station_kind:
		"heavy_bag", "weights":
			return "Wrist Sprain"
		"treadmill", "air_bike":
			return "Hamstring Strain"
		"pads", "slip_rope":
			return "Ankle Tweak"
		_:
			return "Muscle Tear"

# Resolves emergency intervention: send to bench or stretch corner
static func execute_emergency_rest(fighter: Dictionary) -> Dictionary:
	var updated: Dictionary = fighter.duplicate(true)
	updated["target_station"] = "stretch_corner"
	updated["is_recovering"] = true
	updated["status_tag"] = "FORCED RECOVERY"
	return updated

var _target_station_pos: Vector3 = Vector3.ZERO

	name = "GymRouteDrawerUI"
	set_anchors_preset(PRESET_FULL_RECT)

func set_route(origin_3d: Vector3, dest_3d: Vector3, dest_name: String) -> void:
	_source_station_pos = origin_3d
	_target_station_pos = dest_3d
	_target_station_name = dest_name
	_is_routing = true
	queue_redraw()

func clear_route() -> void:
	_is_routing = false
	queue_redraw()

func _process(delta: float) -> void:
	if _is_routing:
		_pulse_time += delta * 4.0
		queue_redraw()

func _draw() -> void:
	if not _is_routing or _game_camera == null:
		return

	if _game_camera.is_position_behind(_source_station_pos) or _game_camera.is_position_behind(_target_station_pos):
		return

	var p2: Vector2 = _game_camera.unproject_position(_target_station_pos)

	# Draw dashed pathline

	draw_arc(p1, 9.0, 0, TAU, 16, COLOR_GOLD, 1.5)
