## Body — how to use this

Operator ask answered: space between exchanges is first-class ground truth. For boxing, JPES inactive 2.3±1.8 s (and long idles 5.6±1.7) is the DOCUMENTED neutral gap; do not omit it from the first realism test. Other sports have their own inactive/prep/pause numbers above — copy the matching row, not boxing’s.

Do not mix definitions. Ouergui “HIA/(LIA+P)≈1:1” is not Glory summed E:P 1.8:1 is not Del Vecchio MMA 1:2–1:4. Same word “pause,” different coding.

Davis vs JPES: Davis gives punches/min and vertical hip oscillations — useful for bounce rate proxies — and does not replace Šiška phase durations.

Bag vs LAB: bag retraction (Menzel) and kick return (Robalino) are the best limb-recovery numbers we have; they are not bout measurements. A realism test that cites them must say LAB.

KO vs stagger: tens-of-ms tone loss is DOCUMENTED for clear KOs on video. Graded “hurting hit” stagger duration is NOT FOUND — do not invent 200–800 ms “hurt react” from coaching lore.
