# TASK M32 - MARIA - THE MENU LOOK: HOW BLIZZARD, EA AND SEGA MAKE MENUS FEEL FINISHED (art direction)
Assigned S77, 2026-09-25, by Claude. Research + art direction; no code.

## Why
Operator, 2026-09-25, verbatim: "the whole ui is pretty densed, needs proper refinement visual,textual everything
how blizzard,EA,Sega make their menus perfect ?" and earlier "the gui in the menus it looks llike plcaholders".
FightCamp's menus today: flat grey-blue panels, every panel the same weight, long uppercase developer captions,
monospace labels everywhere, equipment and fighters shown as text boxes (initials where portraits should be),
empty dark slots, bare numbers with no explanation. Screens: new career (country -> city -> gym), THE DAY (home),
schedule/offers, fight week, weigh-in, fight night, result, gym, staff, training, scouting, rankings, office,
fighter profile (overview, skills & moves tree).

## What would a human do?
Answer FIRST: how does a UI/UX art director at Blizzard (Hearthstone, Overwatch, Diablo menus), EA (EA SPORTS
UFC 5, FC career mode) and Sega (Football Manager 24/26, Total War) take a dense management screen to "finished"?
What is their process (style guide, hierarchy pass, density budget, iconography, motion, sound, playtest)?

## Questions
1. Hierarchy and density: how many focal elements per screen, how much text is allowed, what goes to tooltips /
   second panels. Football Manager is the closest dense-data precedent - how does FM 24/26 keep it readable?
2. Typography: how many typefaces and sizes, when uppercase is used, why monospace is rare.
3. Colour and surfaces: panel depth, accent use, how "state" (good/bad/locked/new) is coloured.
4. Imagery: portraits, equipment illustrations, backgrounds - what a small studio can afford (generated art,
   icon sets) and still look finished.
5. Motion and sound: hover/press feedback, screen transitions, timings in ms.
6. A FightCamp style guide proposal: palette, type scale, spacing grid, panel styles, iconography, motion timings,
   and a before/after mock of TWO screens - the gym creation screen and THE DAY - as images.

## Deliverable
`M32_MENU_ART_DIRECTION.md` (style guide + sources, each number SOURCED/PROXY/JUDGEMENT) and the two mock images.
Claude saves them under `research/`. The S78 menu clarity stage builds from this guide.
