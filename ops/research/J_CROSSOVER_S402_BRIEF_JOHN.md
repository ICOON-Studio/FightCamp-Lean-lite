Heart and lung fitness carries over. In fit people, local muscle fitness doesn't: forearm and grip endurance, and holding the hips and trunk under load. Both sports reach similar lactate levels, but grappling loads the grip, which falls 13–16% within one BJJ fight. OPINION: model grappling stamina as reduced at first and recovering over about 6–10 weeks.

People do mix the two. MMA athletes spend a fifth to a third of their week on striking drills, but nobody has counted how many boxers also do BJJ. The only hard slowdown numbers are for strength and power, where combined training keeps about 60–82% of the strength-only gain. OPINION: a split schedule should slow each skill roughly in proportion to the hours it loses, plus a fatigue penalty. That's a design assumption, not a finding.
