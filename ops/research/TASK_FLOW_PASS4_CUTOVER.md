## WHAT PASS 4 IS

Passes 1–3 gave the screen map, the route words and the hub/HUD golds. Pass 4 is
the **cutover sheet** — the thing a build session works straight down without
having to re-derive a single decision.

**One table. Every row is a string that changes.** Columns:

| current string | where it is | new string | chars | why |
|---|---|---|---|---|

- **`where it is`** must be concrete enough to find — a screen name plus the
  control, e.g. `FightResultPanel / primary CTA`. You listed the `today`
  call-site registries in pass 3; use that level of precision.
- **`chars`** is the character count of the new string. You have been giving
  budgets all along; give the actual count here.
- **`why`** is one clause, not a paragraph.

**Then three short sections:**

**1. THE DUAL CONTINUE.** Exactly which Continue survives and which dies, named
by screen and control. Maria's M29 art pack places the surviving one — *"one
green Continue, left foot"* — so the two of you must agree. **If her placement
and your word choice conflict, say so explicitly rather than quietly choosing;
that conflict is worth more to us than a resolution you made alone.**

**2. THE LEAVE CTA.** Its word, and the one sentence that says why that word and
not the two you rejected. You kept `ENTER FIGHT WEEK` after self-arguing in pass
3 — do the same here and show the losers.

**3. ROUTES THAT DIE.** Pass 2 said drop ~64 and use 31. List the ones that go,
by name. **A route that has a string in the table above must not also be on this
list** — that contradiction is the single most likely defect in this sheet, so
check it yourself and say you did.
