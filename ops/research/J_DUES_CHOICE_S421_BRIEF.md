# John: what makes a boxing club's dues a real choice (S421)

We make a boxing gym management game (one amateur club, UK-style, money in game dollars = GBP x 1.25). The player sets the
monthly dues in three bands. Real numbers we already use: community boxing clubs GBP 20-35 a month, premium boxing gyms
GBP 50-80. Today's rule: cheap $25 draws 15% more members, standard $35, premium $45 draws 15% fewer (the gap closes as the
gym's name grows). Result: premium always earns most and cheap never wins, so the choice is fake.

Please research, with sources and numbers:
1. Price elasticity of demand for gym / combat-sports club memberships (UK or comparable): how many members does a 30% price
   rise lose? Does it differ for community clubs vs premium gyms?
2. What lets a premium boxing gym charge GBP 50-80: facilities (space, kit, showers), coaching (named coaches, class count),
   reputation (champions), location. Which matters most to members who pay it?
3. What a cheap community club gets back for low dues: more juniors, more raw talent walking in, council or charity grants
   (Sport England, England Boxing club funds), volunteer coaches, community standing. Any numbers.
4. Churn: monthly cancellation rates for budget vs premium gyms.
5. Two or three real UK boxing clubs at each price band, with their dues and what they offer.

End with the three most important facts for a game rule, each with its number and source.
