## The question
What makes hiring, keeping or leaving a coach one of the most interesting decisions in this game?
Do not start from a staff screen. Start from a moment in a career when the player has to choose, and say what they feel.

## What it has to answer
1. What does a coach know that another does not, in terms a boxing person would recognise? Real gyms first: what a boxer actually gets from changing trainer, what they lose, what real trainers are known for. Name real patterns, not numbers from memory.
2. How does a boxer outgrow a coach, and how does the player see it coming?
3. What is the bond, what builds it and what breaks it? It must change something on fight night or in training that the player can feel.
4. Each coach's own book: what is in it, how big, how does the boxer take it away? Rotating coaches must buy breadth and cost depth.
5. Where does the coach learn? Does a coach pick things up from others in the gym?

## Limits
Scope: boxing, one gym, a small roster, amateur to first pro fight. No new screen count unless it replaces one. Every mechanic in the pack that it touches must be named. Numbers are ranges to test on the bots, never promises.
A rule must be able to lose: say how the bots would prove it false. Rotating cannot be the best answer every career, nor can staying.
Only what the player can act on goes on screen.

SANDBOX (owner S412: "please stop adding things like this that make it all about her, it is a sandbox game"): the player runs any gym with any boxers, men or women. Write about the boxer by name or as "the boxer", never a fixed he or she, and never a story around one person.

## How to answer
Three to five mechanics, smallest first. For each: the moment, the rule in plain words, what it costs, what it changes on fight night or in the week, what breaks if it is overdone. Then which you would cut. Then a list of what would make this 10/10, most important first, and draw it: a 1920x1080 HTML page rendered with tools/render_mockup.py, picture first, words few.
Write to ops/research/S412_COACH/<your role>.md and docs/mockups/s412/coach/<your role>.png.
