# Tale of the Tape: what makes its fight look good (S392, 2026-11-06)

Operator: "look how good the fight looks". Sources: Darhkseid's 15:56 review (youtube RLeNjWYyfX0) and the first Steam trailer, watched with eyes at 0:00-0:23 and bursts at 1:39, 2:19, 5:30. Sampled, not played: nobody here has held the controller. Notes in our own words, nothing of theirs copied. Steam app 4086830.

## What the footage shows
1. **The camera sits with the fighters.** Bodies fill the frame from about chest height. Ours shows small figures in a big crowd.
2. **Stances are long and low**, with a real bend and a big hip turn on power shots. Ours read tall between punches, and the coach said so.
3. **Up close they do something.** At 1:39 one fighter ducks under and works. Our inside phase stands chest to chest.
4. **Hits have an impact language.** At 1:39.6 the whole picture warms and blurs on contact, then a red tint lingers. We have a hit-stop and a screen with nothing on it.
5. **A clean room.** A plain lit gym, few objects, so the fighters are the picture.
6. **Replay camera.** Free camera on a knockout, and a clip can be saved.

## What the reviewer rates
- His headline is three rules: poor stamina is punished, counters do real damage, a shot's impact depends on what the other man was doing.
- His favourite moment (2:44-3:19): a punch that is hit mid-throw STOPS. He hates that Undisputed and UFC let it finish.
- His complaints (15:04): wants more torque on punches, better hit reactions, more hit stops. Footwork is planted, closer to Fight Night Champion than to Undisputed's free flow, and he does not mind.

## What we take, most value first
1. **A landed counter interrupts the punch.** Our beats know who lands; the punch being thrown should be cut. Cheap, and the thing a player notices.
2. **Do something inside.** Inside spells need short punches, frames or a push. Our top judged fault, from both judges, three rounds running.
3. **Impact language on big hits:** a brief warm flash and a short hit-stop on significant shots only.
4. **Fight lens closer and lower.** Test against our rule: every lens inside the ropes, never behind a boxer.
5. **Lower, longer stance** between punches: FOOTWORK `DROP` and the guard.

## What we do not take
Their HUD, their look, their controls. Real-time control is not our game: the player plans and watches, so the fight must look right on its own, which is a harder bar than theirs.
Planted footwork being fine for this reviewer is useful: we do not need free-flowing feet to look good.
