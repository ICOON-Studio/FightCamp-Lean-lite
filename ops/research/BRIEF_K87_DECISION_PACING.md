The gym floor (a training day in game minutes, speed 1x / 3x / pause) now has SIX kinds of player decisions, each a card on ONE shared
decision card (FIFO queue, the clock holds while a card is open, 10 s timer with an auto-pick on some):
1 FORM BREAKDOWN (sloppy reps: correct form / call the rep, 2-4 a day), 2 NIGGLE (physio / push through, by soreness), 3 BREAKTHROUGH
(lock it in / bank it, max 1 a day), 4 FLOOR EVENT (Dola's deck, A/B, 2 a day at seeded times), 5 CORNER REHEARSAL (your K8: push / swap / ice
at every block end, 4-5 a day), 6 SPARRING FLASH (your K86 / Ludo's code: 1-3 per ring_spar block). Plus the Morning Day Plan before the day.
Worst case that is ~15 cards a day. Ludo cut QTE clicking because it breaks a manager sim.

Design the PACING so the floor feels like a manager's day, not a nag:
1. A per-day card budget (number) and a per-hour cap; what happens to a card over budget (auto-resolved by the coach with a one-line note
   in the day log? dropped? merged?).
2. A priority order when two arrive together (injury risk first? breakthrough before event?), and which ones may wait in the queue vs expire.
3. 3x speed: which cards still stop the clock and which auto-resolve with the coach's call (and how the coach's call quality depends on the
   coach's skill - numbers).
4. A "quiet day" rule: should some days have almost no cards (recovery day, fight week)?
5. A table: card type | max per day | priority | stops clock at 1x | at 3x | auto-pick rule | coach-skill effect.
6. One real game that paces interrupts well (Football Manager match-day, RimWorld letters, Frostpunk) and what to copy.
