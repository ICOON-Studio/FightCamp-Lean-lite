## 1. Logic check

**1. How a combination line shares sparring is not written.** "A 4-line plan gives each move about 45" counts lines as moves. A line can be a combination (a One-two is Jab and Cross), and the code already says "a drilled combination also holds its punches". The 4-line figure also leaves out the pad cards, which share the same 180 (pack S448). Two pad cards on a 4-line plan gives 30 each, not 45: that holds Sharp, not Signature. Section 6 also counts 5 lines and leaves out the featherweight's Body hook card. The headline trade, "plan size holds less", has to be counted in moves, not lines.

**2. "PEAK counts sparring double" may count it twice.** PEAK already has two sparring nights (CODE). If each night also counts double, that's 4 times. Say which one is meant.

**3. Can a second assistant add pad nights?** "Two at once (each paid)" doesn't say whether the club's 8 pad nights become more. If yes, scarcity, the map's main trade, can be bought away for $380. If no, the second hire only gives the slot and the Signature rate, and the market table should say so. This is the most important unwritten rule.

**4. Shadow mornings contradict the slot rule.** M2 says "only a move in a slot is drilled while under 30". M5 says steps fill in shadow mornings, and that "a shadow morning names its step". A shadow morning on a step under 30 that isn't in the slot either does nothing or breaks the one-move rule. Own chains have the same question if they can hold a move under 30. CHECK (Claude): whether `OwnCombos` lets a chain carry a move under 30.

**5. The tree slows the real Silver test.** Silver is Uppercut and Check hook at 30 (CODE). In the tree, the Check hook sits in row 3, behind 9 moves Usable, Lead hook 60 and Pivot 30. Today it can be drilled any time. Nothing locks the award, but the tree delays it by several picks. The season clock's "an award every 6 to 10 weeks" is unlikely to survive Silver. PROGPROBE1 should report weeks to Silver in arm A against arm B.

**6. The Check hook belongs to no style.** It isn't in Counter-puncher (Lead hook, Step out, Step back). The counter punch belongs to no style, and Cut off, Herd and Push off are in no set either. Out of the five row-3 nodes, only the Feint feeds a style. Row 3's draws are the Silver test, plan answers and the cover against the read. That's thin for the stage of a career where the player is most invested.

**7. The day-0 table breaks its own tolerance.** Its spread is 105 to 129 (24 points), against "no set more than 20 from the others". Out-boxer (129) already fails before any probe. Its gate move is also Counter-puncher's third, so Out-boxer is built through Counter-puncher.

**8. A cliff at Footwork 50.** Footwork 50 starts both steps at 30. Footwork 49 starts Step out at 0 and only 4 moves Usable at Technique 60, so row 2 stays closed and there's no week-1 pick. One point of a hidden number decides whether the player gets the first-hour moment. PROGPROBE1 "for all four starts" won't catch walk-ins and signings with Footwork under 50.

**9. Injury freeze versus code.** The map says "CODE: no pads, no sparring" while out. The pack says "a quarter of gains" while out (4.10), not "no pads". CHECK (Claude): whether `PadWeek` runs for an injured or suspended fighter. Also, a suspended fighter with no injury can train. Freezing learning for that fighter is a choice the map should own, not quote as code.

**10. READ-PROOF can light by accident.** Rule 2 splits the read when a second line is fired "within a fifth as often". A 6-line plan against a mixed opponent can do that with no Signature, Feint or plan change. READ1's "not by a careless one" will fail on that route. "Within a fifth" also reads two ways: the second line at 80% or more of the first, or a gap of 20% or less of the first. In practice these are the same, but the map should write it as a ratio.

**11. Learning speed for veterans is listed twice.** M2 sets "31+ x0.9" and M8 sets "learning x0.9" for veterans. Written as two rules, a builder may apply 0.81.

**12. A busy amateur gets cheap holds.** Club shows run every other Saturday. With AMATEURWEEK1 fight weeks, a fighter booked often has frozen fades for up to half the weeks. HOLD1's policies don't include a busy amateur.

**13. Names outside headers (owner S448).** The map itself keeps names to headers: section 6 uses weight classes, the rows use portraits, and the Monday lines use roles. Good, but the screens it touches still carry names in buttons (CODE):

**14. The pronoun rule is settled.** The map's "her / him, the fighter's own, never one fixed for all" squares the pack's line with S412. Keep that wording in the pack too.
