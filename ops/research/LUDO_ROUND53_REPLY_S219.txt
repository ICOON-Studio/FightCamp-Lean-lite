## PART B — UNIFIED IN-GAME MENU PRESENTATION SYSTEM

To elevate *FightCamp* to the visual standards of *Football Manager*, *Motorsport Manager*, and *Two Point Hospital*, the UI must abandon ad-hoc floating panels in favor of a cohesive **Design System**.

### 1. Unified Architectural Presentation Rules

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                       GLOBAL PRESENTATION ARCHITECTURE                                                 │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  LAYER 0: 3D WORLD CANVAS     Real-time Godot 4.7.2 Gym Viewport (Maintains lighting, ambient lifecycles, and depth)   │
│  LAYER 1: PERSISTENT SHELL    Top Telemetry Bar (64 px) + Left Modular App Dock (80 px)                                │
│  LAYER 2: ACTIVE WORKSPACE    Full Canvas Panels OR Slide-Over Inspection Drawers (Blurred Backdrop)                  │
│  LAYER 3: MODAL DIALOGS       Focused Decision Windows (Office Contracts, Towel Calls, Financial Overrides)            │
│  LAYER 4: TOAST & TOOLTIPS    Non-blocking feedback, detailed telemetry inspectors, systemic notifications            │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

#### Screen Types:
1. **The Full Management Canvas (Replaces 3D Viewport):** Used for deep tactical or operational work (`Roster Profile`, `Technique Tree`, `Financial Ledger`, `Schedule`). The 3D gym is occluded or blurred using a rapid Gaussian depth-of-field effect (`radius: 16px`, brightness: $-40\%$) to keep typography readable.
2. **The Contextual Inspection Drawer (Slide-Over Panel: $640\text{ px}$ from right):** Used for quick inspections while maintaining situational awareness of the gym floor (`Equipment Detail`, `Single Fighter Status`, `Scouting Dossier`). The 3D gym stays live and animated in the background.
3. **The Focus Modal Window (Centered: $800\text{×}540\text{ px}$ or $960\text{×}640\text{ px}$):** Reserved exclusively for high-impact crossroads decisions (`Story Event Cards`, `Contract Signing`, `Debt Restructuring`, `Corner Towel Stoppage`).

#### Typography Scale (1080p Target):
* **Display Titles (Screen Headers):** `24 px` | Bold (Weight 700) | Letter-spacing: $+0.5\text{ px}$ | Uppercase
* **Section Headers / Card Titles:** `16 px` | Semi-Bold (Weight 600) | Letter-spacing: $+0.2\text{ px}$
* **Standard Data & Readouts:** `13 px` | Medium (Weight 500) | Tabular Figures (Monospace numbers)
* **Secondary Labels & Meta Tags:** `11 px` | Regular (Weight 400) | Uppercase | Letter-spacing: $+0.8\text{ px}$
* **Micro Tooltips & Badges:** `10 px` | Bold (Weight 700) | Uppercase

#### Color Roles:
* **Base Canvas Surface:** Deep Navy Slate `#0B101D`
* **Card / Panel Surface:** Industrial Midnight Navy `#121A2B`
* **Card Surface Raised / Hover:** Steel Navy `#1B263E`
* **Primary Interactive Accent (Gold):** `#D19A66` / `#E5B842` (Primary action, progress, cash)
* **Secondary Selected Accent (Teal):** `#4EC9B0` (Active selection, positive technical traits)

### 2. High-Impact Refinements for Core In-Game Screens

#### (1) The 3D Gym Hub (Main Operational Workspace)
* **Fix 1: Integrated Telemetry Strip.** Drop the detached top floating tiles. Replace them with a flush, unified 64 px top bar containing an integrated cash runway gauge (`$25,000 | 68.1 wks remaining`) and live calendar engine controls matching *Motorsport Manager*.
* **Fix 2: Spatial Floor HUD Overlays.** Display subtle, non-intrusive floor projection markers over apparatuses (e.g., ring, heavy bags) indicating who is currently training on them, their current exercise, and real-time wear progression.
* **Fix 3: Floating Goal Drawer.** Anchor the daily/weekly objective tracker cleanly to the lower-left corner ($X=96, Y=860$) with an auto-collapsing drawer tab, ensuring the gym floor remains visually clear.

#### (2) Fighter Profile & 54-Node Technique Tree
* **Fix 1: Two-Column Split Architecture.** Place fighter vitals, the 9 player-facing attribute bars, and the biological wear mannequin on the left ($W=520\text{ px}$); dedicate the remaining space ($W=1320\text{ px}$) to an expansive, pan-and-zoom node canvas for the 54-node Technique Tree.
* **Fix 2: Color-Coded Biological Wear Mannequin.** Replace raw text lists with an anatomical wireframe model showing wear levels across the 6 body regions (`head_neck`, `torso_core`, `left_arm`, `right_arm`, `left_leg`, `right_leg`) from green to deep red.
* **Fix 3: Interactive Prerequisite Tracing.** Hovering over any technique node (e.g., `boxing_counter_combination`) instantly draws glowing vector traces back through its requirements (`boxing_pull_counter`, `boxing_one_two_hook`), highlighting missing stat thresholds in crimson.

#### (3) Schedule & Fight-Week Hub (Tue–Sat Grid)
* **Fix 1: Micro-Block Calendar View.** Build a 7-day tactical planner showing training intensity, sparring slots, weigh-in check-ins, and rest days, modeled after *Football Manager's* training calendar.
* **Fix 2: Fatigue Accumulation Heatmap.** Display a projected fatigue graph beneath the week's schedule to show players how scheduled training sessions will affect recovery ahead of fight night.
* **Fix 3: Opponent Scouting Split.** Position a persistent scouting dossier on the right side of the screen during fight week, displaying the opponent's style, key tendencies, and recommended tactical countermeasures.

#### (4) Live Fight HUD & Corner Management
* **Fix 1: Telemetry-Driven Vitals bug.** Place clean, broadcast-style telemetry strips in the upper corners showing real-time stamina bars, composure levels, and subtle cut/swelling badges.
* **Fix 2: Tactical Corner Tray.** Replace cluttered button grids during the 60-second break with a 3-column tactical board: *Fighter Readout* | *Corner Adjustments* | *Cutman Resource Allocation*.
* **Fix 3: High-Visibility Stoppage Control.** Place a large, high-contrast `THROW TOWEL` action in the lower-right corner ($W=220, H=52\text{ px}$), surrounded by hazard striping, allowing managers to step in and protect their fighter's career.

#### (5) Post-Fight Analytics & Aftermath Screen
