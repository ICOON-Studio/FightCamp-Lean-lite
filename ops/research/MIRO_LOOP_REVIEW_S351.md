## WHAT IS MISSING (merged; who raised it)
- The bad days: a lost fight, an injury and time off, missing weight, money running short, a sponsor, member or coach leaving, and turning down an offer. (all four)
- How often each chapter or screen comes up: once, every week, every fight, any time. (Claude, Kalli, Maria, fresh)
- What the player DECIDES on each screen, compared with what he only reads. (Claude, fresh)
- Clicks from each overview node to its chapter, and a way back. (Claude, Maria, fresh)
- The Nationals: the Home screen and the World tab point to them, but no screen shows them. (Claude, fresh)
- The new-career steps (country, city, gym name). Quick start skipped them. (Claude, fresh)
- The long arc in one picture, a whole season or the ladder first bout > nationals > pro > title, with the goal written in the title. (Kalli, Maria, fresh)
- The first hour as a guided path, and any first-time help the player gets. (Kalli, Maria)
- The gym itself: the Floor/build view, and the gym visibly growing. (fresh, Kalli)
- A rival who keeps coming back in the news, the rankings and the offers. (Kalli)
- A week with nothing booked, which is most weeks. Also her mood or stress, and what the player sees after the pro debut. (Kalli)
- A legend for the colours and arrows, plus a date and build number on the board. (Maria, fresh)
- The time buttons (pause, >, >>, >>>), and a short video of a watched round. (Claude)
- A tag for each screen's kind (menu, tab, pop-up window, tooltip), which the operator asked for. (Claude)
- Tabs that exist in the game but are not on the board: Fights > Calendar and Results; Fighter > Body, Camp, Contract; Money > Income. (fresh)

## TOP 8 IMPROVEMENTS (most value to the operator first)
1. Fix the wrong pictures above. Take each chapter from one moment of the same career, so dates, money and record only move forward. (fresh)
2. Add a red "bad days" side-loop (lost fight, injury, missed weight, money short), each with an arrow back into the week. (all four)
3. Make the core loop stand out. Use a gold band on chapters 2, 4 and 5 and grey on the any-time chapters. Move "Grow the gym" into the loop as the goal (wins, then the nationals, then the pro offer). (Maria, Kalli, fresh, Claude)
4. Let him click each overview node to jump to its chapter, with a "back to the loop" button on every chapter. (Claude, Maria, fresh)
5. Wrap long chapters into rows of 3 to 5 screens. Beside the 2 or 3 key screens, add a full-size crop so the real numbers can be read. (Maria, Kalli, fresh, Claude)
6. Put a small tag on each screen: what the player decides there, how often it comes up, and its kind (menu, tab, window, tooltip). (Claude, Kalli, Maria, fresh)
7. Show the corner once, or mark the 2D one "old". Stamp "OLD BUILD" on the S350 3D stills. Mark the training proposal "NOT IN THE GAME YET" and move it off the loop. (Claude, Kalli, Maria, fresh)
8. Add the missing parts of a career: the new-career steps, the Nationals, the gym floor, and one "whole season" timeline strip. (Claude, fresh, Kalli)
