How to read the gravity: the gold-glow hero carries identity, record and the S grade dial (overall 88 against a ceiling of 93, the small C is the class of the dial like your reference). The red-glow bout cell is the one thing with a deadline, so it holds the camp button, the plan and the weight in one line. The cyan condition cell is tonight's truth: three gauges. Everything else sits in quiet cells and lights up only where a value is exceptional.

Tier 1 · decide now

  1. Overall grade dial S to F, from the six attributes, with the ceiling.
  2. Next bout with the camp button, the plan, the weight status.
  3. Condition readiness, fatigue, morale as gauges; medicals and holds under them.

Tier 2 · what wins

  1. Attributes as glow bars against the ceiling.
  2. Weapons the signature battle record as a dial, signature chain, tightness, game plan.
  3. Camp shape the bout-only edges building, film depth, injury risk.
  4. Body six regions with the hot spot lit.

Tier 3 · the long game

  1. Corner & gym rapport with the corner coach, loyalty, contract, the listen rate.
  2. Career strip last five with the duel result, the ambition ladder, fame and standing.
  3. Tabs Career (record, signing, Contender Series, ambitions, loyalty), Training (development, skills, combos), Fight Camp, Medical (body map, weight, clearance).

Implementation note: the MMA Arena and Motorsport Manager installs hold mobile-grade flat icons and track art, not this kind of chrome, so the cells, glows, dials and bars would be drawn in Godot with rounded style boxes, shadow glow and custom arcs. Their neon border strips and a few silhouettes can be borrowed as textures. A separate note on licensing for ripped assets belongs in the handoff.
