# YOUR JOB (fresh critic): one fighter's Training screen, judged as a GAME screen, not as an information sheet

The owner wants: art where it matters, little text, and from the picture alone it is clear how you set the training, what your options
are and what each improves, with tiredness and intensity shown. The art must match the rest of the game's screens (the camp plan).

What the picture shows (the player's one boxer, 12 days before a club-show bout; this is the last week whose pad nights count for it):
- Header (one strip): the fighter, the next bout and a countdown, a LAST PAD WEEK badge, a WIN dial (the coach's plan against the
  player's plan), the fighter's goal bar, and a Weekly card button (an event waiting for an answer).
- THIS WEEK (the big centre board): seven days. Each day has a MORNING card (picture, a round icon ringed in the colour of the stat it
  grows, what it grows, tiredness as flames, its own Light / Normal / Hard lever), an EVENING card (pads with the coach - the punch
  drilled with a ring filling to 30, the level at which it can be thrown in a fight - film of the opponent, sparring, or an open night)
  and a TIREDNESS TANK that fills day by day, named Fresh / Working / Tired / Overreached, with a dashed line at 72 and a solid line at 80.
  A green tag on each card = its points on the WIN dial; the player is hovering Monday's +0 tag, which explains itself. Changed cards say
  YOURS and show the coach's card struck out. A sparring card is being dragged onto Saturday evening and warns before the drop.
- SESSIONS strip (bottom): every session as a round icon to drag, ringed in its stat's colour; mornings left of the divider, evenings right.
  The legend gives the tiredness bands and what each does.
- Right column: the clicked card (Wednesday evening's pads) with what it does on fight night and what it gives up; what the week builds
  (stat bars with the week's gain hatched, the moves as rings); the weigh-in with its date, the forecast against the limit and the diet.
- A WEEK GOAL row (Peak for Sat 28 / Build / Recover) decides what the WIN dial counts; Light / Normal / Hard buttons set all mornings.
- Undo and Coach's plan above the board. CONTINUE (top right, the one pulsing button) runs the week.
