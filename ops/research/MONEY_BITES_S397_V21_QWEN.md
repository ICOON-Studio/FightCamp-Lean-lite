# MONEY_BITES_S397 - design v2.1 (designer brief)

Task: the version of v2 that scores 8/10 against shipped Steam management games on "does money keep mattering for a sensible player in year 1, year 2 and year 5, with real choices, without punishing success - and does it feel like running a real boxing club?"

All dollar amounts are game dollars (GBP x 1.25). GAME = a value we chose, not a real-world claim.

## 1) What I keep from v2

- **Premises tiers** (3 tiers: Unit 70 members/$1,800 rent/3 places → Bigger unit 140/$3,400/5 places/$12k move → Club building 250/$5,500/7 places/$25k move). The one bill that grows with success and never goes back.
- **Club show** (lumpy, risky, repeatable every 12 weeks: paid up front, tickets on turnout, can lose money).
- **Contract talks** (renewal at top 10 and belt: keep the share for a signing bonus, or take a lower one; a decision at a moment, never a silent penalty).
- **Kit budget** (low/standard/good, a weekly lever with visible effects on injury risk and members carried).
- **Seasonal bills** (heating/power winter $560/month, summer $280/month; liability insurance $1,250/year).
- **Youth section with dated intakes** (January and September term starts, 35% chance each, assistant coach +10%; a home-grown prospect on half stipend for a year).
- **Staff roles** (assistant coach, S&C, physio on call, cutman; letting go costs a week's pay).
- **Places as a hard cap** (3 unit + 2 bigger unit + 2 club building + 1 assistant = 8 max).
- **Pro share falls with rank** (25% → 20% → 17% at renegotiation).
- **One week of fitters** before an upgrade works.

## 2) What I change

### a) Upgrade prices up 40-60%

- **From:** mats $1,600, strength $3,600, fitkit $1,500, youth $1,200. Total: $7,900.
- **To:** mats $2,400, strength $5,200, fitkit $2,200, youth $1,800. Total: $11,600.
- **Why:** v2's upgrade list is cleared by week 85-110; the pile returns. This pushes clearance to week 130-150, so year 2 still has decisions. Real anchor: whole equipment fit-out $10k-50k (section 2).
- **The number that moves:** weeks until all upgrades owned (85-110 → 130-150).

### b) Upkeep up 50%

- **From:** mats $10/wk, strength $15/wk, fitkit $10/wk. Total: $35/wk.
- **To:** mats $15/wk, strength $22/wk, fitkit $15/wk. Total: $52/wk.
- **Why:** recurring costs should be a steady pressure, not a rounding error. Real anchor: ring upkeep $50-200/month (section 2).
- **The number that moves:** weekly costs (+$17/wk = $884/year).

### c) Pro camp bill from 6% → 10% of purse, min $800

- **From:** 6% of purse, no minimum.
- **To:** 10% of purse, min $800.
- **Why:** 6% is too low; real camp with paid sparring is $2,500-6,000 (critic). A $10k purse at 10% is $1,000; a $50k purse is $5,000. This keeps pro income from dominating. GAME value, sized so net per bout still rises with rank (unit test: unranked $750, rank 10 $1,200, champion $2,100).
- **The number that moves:** pro camp cost.

### d) Add travel costs for away pro bouts: $200 per bout

- **From:** $0.
- **To:** $200 per away pro bout.
- **Why:** real clubs pay travel, hotel, per diem (critic). This is a steady cost that scales with fight frequency. GAME value.
- **The number that moves:** away bout cost.

### e) Kit budget affects reputation: Low = -1 rep/quarter

- **From:** no reputation effect.
- **To:** Low kit budget = -1 reputation per quarter (members notice worn gear).
- **Why:** this makes the low kit budget a real choice (save $25/wk, lose reputation). GAME value.
- **The number that moves:** reputation change.

### f) Club show cost up to $4,500, add "big night" potential

- **From:** $3,500 cost, net $3-5k at best.
- **To:** $4,500 cost. At reputation 100+, tickets sell out (400 cap), net can be $6-8k.
- **Why:** the club show should be a bigger swing (critic: "a bet you choose, repeats, grows with the club"). Real anchor: venue $2k, officials/doctor/ambulance $1,200, trophies $300, plus marketing $1,000.
- **The number that moves:** club show cost and potential net.

### g) Add 2 more upgrades (year 3-5 decisions)

- **Full-time head coach office:** $8,000 (upkeep $30/wk, all training gains +5%, head coach wage $400/wk). Real anchor: a full-time head coach is a big commitment (critic).
- **Recovery suite** (ice baths, physio equipment): $6,000 (upkeep $40/wk, injured boxers back a third sooner, stacks with physio to a floor of x0.5). Real anchor: recovery equipment $5k-15k (GAME prices).
- **Why:** after the premises moves and the 4 base upgrades, there is nothing left to buy in year 3-5. These are the big-ticket items that make money matter in the long game.
- **The number that moves:** training gains, injury recovery.

### h) Sponsor income scales with premises

- **From:** sponsors are a fixed $400/wk (from the problem statement).
- **To:** Unit: local sponsors $200/wk. Bigger unit: regional sponsors $400/wk. Club building: national sponsors $800/wk.
- **Why:** income should scale with the club, but only if you invest in premises. This makes the premises move a double decision (more rent, but more sponsors). Real anchor: bigger gyms attract bigger sponsors (common sense). GAME values.
- **The number that moves:** sponsor income.

## 3) At most 3 new mechanics

### a) Annual costs

- **Coach courses:** $400/year (week 26). Real anchor: Level 2 coaching badge GBP 300-500.
- **Equipment replacement:** every 3 years (week 52, 156, 260), $2,500 at standard kit, $4,000 at good kit. Real anchor: whole equipment fit-out $10k-50k; replacement is 20-30% of that.
- **Why:** real clubs have these (critic: "medicals and pro licences, coach courses"). These are the "oh shit" bills that hit once a year and make money matter even when the gym is profitable. GAME values.
- **The number that moves:** annual costs.

### b) Premises unlock sponsor tiers

- **Unit:** local sponsors $200/wk (Ironclad + Bow Road).
- **Bigger unit:** regional sponsors $400/wk.
- **Club building:** national sponsors $800/wk.
- **Why:** income scales with the club, but only if you invest in premises. This makes the premises move a double decision (more rent, but more sponsors). GAME values.
- **The number that moves:** sponsor income.

### c) Camp budget per bout

- **Before each pro bout, the player chooses a camp budget:**
  - **Basic:** free, +20% injury risk during camp (4 weeks).
  - **Standard:** $800, no effect.
  - **Elite:** $2,000, -15% injury risk during camp.
- **Why:** a decision before each fight (critic: "camp budget per bout"). The player can save money and risk injury, or spend money and reduce risk. GAME values.
- **The number that moves:** camp cost and injury risk.

## 4) How the Money page should read

A dashboard, not a shop. Every purchase shows the cash left after and the weekly impact. Every recurring cost shows what it is doing.

- **Premises:** current tier, rent, members, places. Next tier: price, unlocks, rent increase, "leaves $X, Y weeks of costs."
- **Upgrades:** each row: price, upkeep, the one number it moves, payback time, prerequisites. Buying: "leaves $X, Y weeks of costs." After: a running tally ("working 12 weeks; classes bring $420/wk").
- **Kit budget:** three buttons (low/standard/good), weekly cost and effect. Note: "A club spends $2,000-3,500 a year on kit."
- **Club show:** cost, turnout range, net range (lo/hi/mid). After: "Last show: X sold, $Y net."
- **Contract talks:** appears at top 10 or belt. Two choices, each with cash and long-term impact.
- **Staff:** wage, the one number, "let go, one week's pay." Filled roles: "working X weeks."
- **Annual costs:** upcoming bills (coach courses, equipment replacement) with week due and amount. "These hit once a year."

## 5) The measurable bounds

### 104-week simulation (2 years)

- **Sensible gym:** closes 0 of 10. Median cash 1-4 months of costs after week 13. Owns 3-6 upgrades by week 104. Moved to bigger premises in 4-6 of 10.
- **Careless gym:** closes 20-70%. Median close after week 26.
- **No dominant item:** three buying orders within 15% net worth.
- **No dead item:** every upgrade owned in at least 3 of 10 sensible careers.
- **Net per bout rises with rank:** unit test holds (unranked $750, rank 10 $1,200, champion $2,100).

### 520-week simulation (10 years)

- **Sensible gym:** cash above 6 x monthly costs in at most 15% of weeks. Year-5 surplus at most 2.5x year 1. Spend at least 25% of income in years 3-10.
- **Careless gym:** closes 20-70%. Median close after week 26.
- **Premises moves:** bigger premises in 6-8 of 10 by week 520. Club building in 3-5 of 10.
- **Annual costs:** hit every year, no career closes because of them.
