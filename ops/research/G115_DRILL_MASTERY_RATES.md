## What would a human do?
A coach calls a drill "got" when the sequence fires under fatigue, movement and a live target without the fighter counting
punches - automaticity, not a clean pad round. A short combo is fluent after weeks of spaced reps and automatic under
pressure after months. Strength coaches plan 3-6 week blocks, then change the quality.

## 1. Hours / sessions to automate
- Novice, discrete striking/self-defence techniques: mean 27-38.3 sessions to rated proficiency; ~29 h for 21 techniques;
  nobody proficient on every technique at the end. SOURCED pmc/articles/PMC3289195 (2026-09-26).
- Novice boxing (gym pathway): clean 3-punch without thinking about feet 2-3 months; smooth combinations 3-6 months;
  automatic under pressure 6-12 months at >= 2 sessions/week. SOURCED honourandglory.co.uk; PROXY griffinsboxing.com.
- Pad programming: beginners 3-4 weeks on three core combos; intermediates multi-combo rounds by weeks 3-4. SOURCED
  maxxproboxing.com.
- Experienced fighters learn new serial taekwondo tasks with better next-day retention and less load (chunking of parts
  they own). SOURCED sciencedirect S0167945725000375. PROXY: a mid-level fighter installs a combo in far fewer hours.
- Everyday skills often plateau after < 50 h once "good enough"; elite work fights the plateau. SOURCED Ericsson et al. 2009.
- Spacing: 50 reps a day x 3 days beats 150 in one block. PROXY.

## 2. Curve shape
Time or error ~ a * P^(-b). Motor-task exponents (Newell & Rosenbloom): mirror tracing 0.26, cigar rolling 0.21,
inverted text 0.44. SOURCED. Individual curves often fit an exponential better; the power law on averages is partly an
averaging artefact (Heathcote et al. 2000). SOURCED. No combat-sports exponent exists. JUDGEMENT: mastery in log-hours.

## 3. Strength and conditioning blocks
- Mesocycles 3-6 weeks (accumulation 3-5, transmutation 3-4, realization 1-2). SOURCED (PMC4637911 and a block summary).
- Back squat over an ~11-week block: weak +25.9%, moderate +18.2%, strong +11.3%. SOURCED PMC7693826.
- Pooled: untrained ~33% over a study (~2.5%/wk), trained ~18% (~1.6%/wk). SOURCED strongerbyscience.
- VO2max over 8-12 weeks: untrained +10-15%, trained +2-7% (Helgerud 4x4 ~7% in trained, 8 wk). SOURCED.

## 4. Breakthrough days
- One night's sleep after a novel motor sequence: ~18-20% faster, no accuracy loss; the same time awake ~0-2%; tied to
  late-night stage-2 NREM. SOURCED Walker et al. 2002 (pubmed 12123620). Offline gain is strongest in early learning.
  SOURCED PLoS Biol 1002263.
- NO measured rate of "breakthrough sessions" in boxing/MMA exists. JUDGEMENT: a breakthrough is uncommon offline
  consolidation after a focused session plus sleep, not a random insight day. Sim rate: **6% of quality sessions while
  at pip I-II, 2% from pip III**; on a proc, cut the current pip's remaining hours by 25-40% - not a free pip.

## 5. Five pips, mid-level fighter (JUDGEMENT on a log-hours curve)
| Pip | Cumulative focused hours | ~Sessions at 20 min | Bonus | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | 6 | 18 | +1.5% | clean pattern, still thought through |
| II | 16 | 48 | +1.2% | fluent in isolation |
| III | 36 | 108 | +1.0% | holds under mild fatigue |
| IV | 72 | 216 | +0.8% | holds under movement / pads |
| V | 140 | 420 | +0.5% + named combo | automatic; combination unlocked |
Total +5.0% per station. Novice hours x1.8, elite x0.65, same curve. Strength/cardio stations: same gates, bonus
+1.0 / +0.8 / +0.6 / +0.5 / +0.4% (total ~3.3%) - trained athletes gain only ~2-11% per real block. Do NOT use linear
20 h x 5: it overpays late pips and underpays the first automaticity jump.
