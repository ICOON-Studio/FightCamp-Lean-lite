**How I checked:**
- **Licence filter:** CC0 or CC BY 4.0 only. Most Poly Pizza office models turned out to be CC-BY 3.0, so they're out.
- **Kenney, Poly Pizza and the MrEliptik pack:** I downloaded these and counted the triangles myself.
- **Sketchfab:** downloads need a login, so those counts come from Sketchfab's own figures. Texture size and format are "not stated", and the licence label "CC Attribution" maps to CC BY 4.0, which needs a credit.

## 3. One pack for the whole office: Kenney Furniture Kit (CC0)
- **Style:** it's bright, flat-coloured, chunky and untextured, with most props under 300 triangles. That reads cleanly at a narrow FOV 17 camera.
- **One download:** a single CC0 zip with 140 GLB models that Godot imports directly. No credit is needed, so there's no licence risk on Steam.
- **Every slot from one artist:**
  - door: `doorway`
  - desk: `desk`, `deskCorner`
  - PC: `computerScreen` + `computerKeyboard` + `computerMouse`, or `laptop`
  - chair: `chairDesk`
  - lamps: `lampSquareTable`, `lampRoundTable`
  - TV: `televisionModern`
  - coffee: `kitchenCoffeeMachine`
  - storage: `bookcaseClosedDoors`, `sideTableDrawers`
  - extras: plants, bin, rug, speaker
- **Swaps for the dated items:**
  - CRT: `computerScreen`
  - tape TV: `televisionModern`
  - credenza and safe: `bookcaseClosedDoors` or `sideTableDrawers`
- **Scale:** Kenney models are about half real scale (the desk is 0.38 m tall), so apply one uniform 2x scale.

**Gaps and fills:**
- **Filing cabinet:** jampre's cabinet (CC BY 4.0, 516 triangles), recoloured, or a kitbash from Kenney drawer parts.
- **Whiteboard:** game_travel's board (CC BY 4.0), or the CreativeTrio corkboard (CC0).
- **PC tower:** the CreativeTrio tower (CC0), recoloured light grey.
- **Headset and phone:** CreativeTrio Headphones and Quaternius Phone (both CC0).
- **Mesh-back chair and slim LED lamp:** no stylised CC0 or CC BY 4.0 version was found. These are the only two items that might need Ludo to make them in 3D.

**Runner-up:** the MrEliptik Office pack (CC0) looks the most like a "proper modern PC", with an ultrawide monitor and a mini PC. But it's dark and minimal, its keyboard and laptop are about 94k triangles each, and it has no whiteboard, coffee machine, cabinet, TV or door.

**Not checked:**
- **Sketchfab models:** none were downloaded, so their texture sizes and formats are unverified.
- **Unopened packs:** I couldn't open the Quaternius pack zips (on Google Drive). OutcastT2 and the OpenGameArt SweatyIce computer weren't counted.
- **Mixed-licence bundle:** the items in Poly Pizza's "The Office Pack" bundle each need their own licence check.
- **Not searched:** Polyhaven (photoreal scans, so the wrong style) and Fab.
