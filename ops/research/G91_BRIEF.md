# G91 - FROM FIGHTER TO COACH: what a retired fighter becomes, and how good a coach he makes (Research, no code) - FightCamp, 2026-09-24

FightCamp is a combat-sports gym management game (Godot). You can read the repo through your GitHub connector (this brief is research/G91_BRIEF.md). Stage S72 will let a retired fighter stay in the player's world as a COACH (later hype man, official, promoter - S73/S74/S80). Coaches today are staff with four numbers: `technical_teaching`, `tactical_rating`, `youth_development`, `capacity` (Godot/scripts - grep `technical_teaching`). Your own G90 (research/G90_OFFICIALS_AS_PEOPLE.md section 3) found the real world ranks coaches by their pupils' year. We need the real path from fighter to coach before we design it.

## The questions

THE PATH: what share of retired pro boxers / MMA fighters / kickboxers / Muay Thai fighters go on to coach (head coach, assistant, own gym, S&C, pad man)? Any survey, athletic-commission or union data, retirement studies (e.g. boxer career-transition papers, UFC Athlete Health, PFA-style reports in other sports that transfer). How long after the last bout, and with what certification (national coaching licences, USA Boxing / England Boxing coach levels, BJJ black belt, kru status)?
DOES A GOOD FIGHTER MAKE A GOOD COACH: evidence either way - champions who became top coaches (and the many who did not), unremarkable fighters who became elite coaches (Freddie Roach, Firas Zahabi, Greg Jackson, Cus D'Amato). Any study linking playing level to coaching success in any sport (football managers' playing careers vs results is well studied - say how far it transfers). Which fighter attributes carry over (fight IQ, technique, a style) and which do not (power, speed).
THE MONEY AND THE PULL: what a new coach earns (per session, per camp, % of a fighter's purse - the 10% corner cut), and whether a famous ex-fighter draws members or fighters to a gym (name value), with any figure.
HOW GAMES DO IT: Football Manager (retired player -> staff, attributes carried), Fight Night / EA UFC / Undisputed legacy modes, WWE 2K / TEW (retired wrestler -> trainer / road agent), Punch Club 2. What carries across, what players liked.
WHAT THE ENGINE SHOULD MODEL: a table - share who coach, lag to first coaching job, which fighter stats seed which coach numbers and how strongly, the name-value pull, the pay band - each with its source, NOT FOUND where there is none, and what should stay invented.
