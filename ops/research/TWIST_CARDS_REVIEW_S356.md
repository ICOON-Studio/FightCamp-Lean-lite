## The big idea is right
The game's 7 cards (S355 WeekTwist: sore wrist, rain, weights room shut, partner off ill, head cold, visiting pro, quiet week)
HAPPEN to her: the week changes and the player works round it. The draft makes every card a CHOICE with a visible price,
settled in the week review. That is the better design: it is the one decision a week the loop is missing, and it matches
Kalli ("the only choice left is the interesting one") and Miro AI's own sticky ("every random swing needs a readable reason").
Best of all: on Monday the week board can show both answers moving the goal lines before you pick.
