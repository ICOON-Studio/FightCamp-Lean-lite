# J56 - Where the hand goes on the neck (Grok Expert, ex-John; collected S94 2026-09-27)
Source: grok.com/c/be99dbb3-7398-4d43-b849-ab8ca2959acd (51 sources). No published degree maps exist: placements are coaching consensus (SOURCED), angles are JUDGEMENT (neck ~12 cm dia; spine = 0 deg, ear ~ 90 deg).

## Collar tie
- Palm cups the NAPE AT THE BASE OF THE SKULL (C0-C2), not mid-neck C3-C7; may slide up to occiput/crown to snap. SOURCED (AIBJJ; coaching video; wiki)
- Same-side hand; elbow inside and low, framed into same-side pec/collarbone (the frame is the control).
- Workable arc: ~0-40 deg off the spine toward the gripping side; past 45-60 deg it becomes a trap grab. JUDGEMENT

## Plum (double collar tie)
- Hands STACKED palm over palm on OCCIPUT/CROWN, fingers up, not the back of the neck. SOURCED (Fighters Only; Titans Grip; Mastering Muay Thai). Neck-gable variant exists (forearm blades on the neck sides).
- Elbows pinched on the collarbones, down; forehead often on theirs.
- Workable arc: +-20 deg of the midline on the occiput; control dies at C4+. JUDGEMENT

## Front headlock
- Wrapping arm round the head; contact is a CHIN STRAP under the mandible or lock at the neck front, not a posterior palm. Shoulder on the upper back, free hand on far triceps, elbow pinched. SOURCED (Sportmentary; coaching)
- Workable arc: anterior-lateral band ~90-140 deg from the spine (jaw / C1 side). JUDGEMENT
