## 3. Member Lifetime Value (LTV)
**Formula**: `LTV = avg monthly dues × avg tenure in months`

| Scenario | Dues | Tenure | Gross LTV |
|---|---|---|---|
| Real-world mid-tier | $140 | 14 mo | **$1,960** |
| Game baseline ($48/mo) | $48 | 14 mo | **$672** |

**How owners use it**: LTV ÷ 3 = max sustainable acquisition cost; if LTV is $1,000, gaining a new member is worth up to ~$330 in wages/marketing. One retained member pays back a $320/week desk hire in ~1–2 weeks of their tenure. **SOURCED**
