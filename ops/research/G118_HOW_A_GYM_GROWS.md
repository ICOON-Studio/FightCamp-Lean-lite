## What would a human do?
Owners add FLOOR and parallel class slots first, kit second. A cage in the same footprint is vanity; a second mat that
runs kids while adults roll is growth (Gymdesk). Typical order to a 150-400 member MMA/boxing gym:
1. second mat / kids hall (~50-80 members or an evening class clash; kids fund the rent),
2. real changing rooms + showers (women's classes, after-work adults),
3. a strength corner (a fight team; cheap used iron, high yield per m2),
4. ONE cage or ring (a team that needs camp work; installed late - 25-50 m2 that kills open-mat revenue in the only hall),
5. office + front desk with sightlines (paid staff, walk-ins),
6. physio room - usually a tenant/partner (Jackson Wink leased chiro/PT into its 2015 campus),
7. a pro-team room - last, destination gyms only.

## 1. Stages (SOURCED examples checked 2026-09-26)
| Stage | Floor | Members | Rooms |
|---|---|---|---|
| Garage / back room | 30-80 m2 | 8-20 | mats, 1-3 bags, no showers |
| First unit | 140-280 m2 | 40-80 (economics flip ~50-60) | one mat, bag wall, toilet/lockers |
| Established club | 250-420 m2 | 80-180 | two training zones, showers, small S&C, desk |

NEO Martial Arts (basement maxed at 62, later 250+); Rise Boxing LA (garage -> 4,000 sq ft in two years); Jackson Wink
2015 campus 30-36k sq ft (~$3M, destination tier); Nairobi Jiu-Jitsu Academy 2026 650 m2.

## 2-3. Triggers and layout
Fill > 90% -> new class or second floor (healthy 60-80%). Bags and racks on the perimeter, open core; sparring away from
the door; changing near the entrance; desk sightline across the floor; circulation bag <-> mat <-> iron without crossing a
live drill; rubber under bags/iron, tatami on grappling, sprung only under a ring. A 4.5 m ring needs ~7.5 x 7.5 m.

## 4. Costs (USD)
Fit-out $30-90 / sq ft mid-US city. Cage new $8-20k, used $1.2-6k. Ring new training $3-15k, competition $15-30k, used
$1.2-8k. Lockers/showers $8-25k. Rent target 10-15% of gross (strain > 20%; UK BJJ often 20-35%).

## 5. Capacity
50-75 sq ft per rolling student, 40-60 striking drill; ~7 sq ft per active MEMBER is packed. Peak 17:00-21:00;
throughput = classes x seats. Staff: < 75 members owner + PT; 75-150 one-two FT coaches; 150-250 two-three FT + desk.

## 6. JUDGEMENT - tiers 3-4 and the wing
- **Tier 3, established club** ~280-400 m2, cap 120-180: main mat + second mat/kids hall, showers, bag wall, small S&C
  alcove, desk. Unlocks concurrent kids + adults, women's evenings, +40-80 member cap, +2-3 peak classes. No cage here.
- **Tier 4, academy** ~500-750 m2, cap 200-350: two full halls, one cage or ring, dedicated S&C, office, partner physio
  bay, optional pro locker. Unlocks a fight team, camp blocks, simultaneous striking + grappling, private PT.
- **Build-a-wing event:** buy m2 and a second hall, not decor. $40-80 / sq ft for the new bay plus one major piece
  (used cage/ring $4-12k, new $8-25k); 6-10 weeks of dust, lost evening classes, capacity -15-25% meanwhile (PROXY);
  money scales with the new hall's class slots (~+40-80 members), not with more bags on the same wall.
