# Fight distances, punch timing and impact feel in two shipped boxing games (S439)

Read-only from the installed files, each number checked by a second reader. SEEN = read in a file; INFERRED = reasoned (says from what). Notes and numbers in our own words; nothing of theirs is copied.

## Blood & Canvas: Bareknuckle Boxing (demo), build of 2026-09-25, at E:/SteamLibrary/steamapps/common/Blood&CanvasBareknuckleBoxingDemo

Engine: Unreal Engine 5, project BKKO, packed as IoStore (utoc v8, Oodle, directory index readable, not encrypted). Gameplay is Blueprint only. Properties are cooked unversioned. I confirmed this by decoding the unversioned headers of the capsule components myself. Units are Unreal centimetres (1 unit = 1 cm). The bodies are human scale (capsule half-height 90 cm, so 180 cm tall), so these numbers can be compared one to one with ours in metres (divide by 100).

Yes, the files do hold usable distance numbers, and most of the first report holds up. Every distance is between the two fighters' body centres, in centimetres. I confirmed this: the hero blueprint fills both the camera value and the AI's DistanceCM from one actor-to-actor distance.

Numbers we can rely on to set our fight distances, all SEEN:
- The AI attacks only below 130 to 135 cm. It is in range below 140 and out of range above 130.
- A combo resets past 150 cm.
- Being crowded starts at 115 cm (the pressure ramp runs from 115 to 170).
- The unused tree throws at 100 to 120 cm.
- The capsule radius is 54 cm on both fighters. The camera's distance input starts at 108. So the bodies most likely never get closer than about 108 cm centre to centre (this last step assumes the capsules block each other).
- New: their own debug 'reach' marker sits 100 cm in front of the body centre at chest height.

So their working distance is about 1.05 to 1.35 m centre to centre. A punch is not fixed to one spacing: it lands where the moving hand first touches the body, mid-travel. Our measured jab and cross reach of 0.91 to 1.11 m fits that band almost exactly. Our fixed 0.93 m spacing is closer than their closest possible stance, so setting the jab and cross spacing near 1.05 to 1.15 m (as reach allows) would match them.

Corrections:
- 'Closes to 110 cm' is wrong. That variable is never read. The AI simply walks to the player with a 30 cm acceptance, so it ends up near capsule contact.
- The camera mapping is mostly flat: only its position moves with distance.
- StepRange 160 stays INFERRED and unused.
- The 130 and 135 gates belonging to the combo and single-shot branches is INFERRED.
- The 180 cm hero compare has no known purpose. Do not use it.

The timing numbers (hit live at 0.07 to 0.10 s, chain at 0.23 s, feet free at 0.43 s), the 0.04 s hit stop, the rumble ladder and the reaction blends were re-read and confirmed. Root motion and step distance are still not found.

## Undisputed (Steam install, E:/SteamLibrary/steamapps/common/Undisputed)

Engine: Unity 2022.3.45f1, IL2CPP, Addressables, MxM motion matching, Cinemachine impulses (legacy envelope mode), PuppetMaster-style active ragdoll

Most of the first report holds up: the values match the files. I made five corrections. (1) Each boxer's reach IS stored, in cm, and it matches the real fighters (Fury 216, Crawford 188, Lomachenko 166). (2) The 'range 5' on punch records is character-creator preview data, not fight data. (3) The '_R2' clips are not 'second variants' but longer-range versions of each punch: the enum PunchRange has Range0 and Range2. The R0 clips move the body 0.01-0.03 m, R2 clips 0.07-0.16 m, lunge clips 0.35-0.63 m. So they match the distance by choosing a clip, then finish the reach with arm IK. (4) The camera shakes use Cinemachine's legacy envelope, so the 0.2 s duration field is not used. A little shake lasts about 0.2 s, a medium one about 1.6 s, a big one about 5 s. (5) The style distance fields (minEngagement and the rest) do not have 'Reach' in their names, so treating them as reach multiples is my inference. Numbers we can rely on to set our fight distances: punch reach = 0.55 of wingspan for straights and hooks and 0.535 for uppercuts (values SEEN), about 1.0 m for a 183 cm wingspan (the formula is INFERRED from method names); a step-in counts as 1.5x reach; the AI engages at 0.75-1.45x reach and punishes at about 0.52x (values SEEN); IK limits on straights: up to 0.30 m of depth correction, up to 30 degrees of lean, elbow never under 35 degrees, IK full within 0.35 and gone by 1.25 (SEEN); body travel per clip type (SEEN). Use with care: the seven range bands 0.84 / 1.04 / 1.19 / 1.34 / 1.54 / 1.74 / 2.14 m. The stored values are SEEN, but that they are squared metres, centre to centre, and what each band triggers are INFERRED. For the owner's question: yes, these numbers exist now; the earlier study (R121) had only clip names and lengths. In their game a straight reaches about 1.0 m, close to our 0.91-1.11 m, so the reach itself is fine. Two things differ from ours. They stand inside 0.75-1.45x reach and pick a longer-range or stepping clip when the opponent is further away. They let IK add up to 0.3 m and lean so the arm lands extended. And their uppercut reaches 97% as far as a straight, while ours reaches about 65%.
