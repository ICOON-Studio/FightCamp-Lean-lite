# M19 — THE MISSING CLIP (presenter when R101 names a technique we have no clip for)

Maria Art Director · 2026-09-10 · art only (no `.gd` / `.tscn` / `HANDOFF.md` / assets/data / anim graph)

Cage Desk continuity. R101's engine will name techniques from a ~272-node catalogue. Purchase list stays **~13–14 clips**. Overwhelming majority of throws have **no clip — CORRECT, not fixed by buying more.** Absolute rule: **never substitute a different technique's animation** for the one thrown. Dropping permitted; inventing forbidden. Grounded in M14 HOME, M16 collapse + reaction-out-reads-arm, M17 2 clean reacts (~86.6%), R97 92.6% beat drop (G45), G42 presentation escape hatch.

## DECISIONS

1. **ONE-LINE RULE (Q7):** **"If we have no clip for the named technique: play matching-region reaction on the receiver; leave thrower on current loc/idle; never play a different technique's clip."**
2. **Q3 DIRECT: YES** — receiver reaction alone is enough, and the **cheapest honest headline**. Thrower stays on current loc/idle (stillness / continue-guard — not T-pose). Condition: region matches + HUD/beat already wrote contact. *(Invented visual judgment; boards m19_03.)*
3. **BUY ONE GENERIC? NO.** Prefer reaction-only first. A neutral straight-ish silhouette *may* exist (M16 jab+cross collapse = good news) but must never be sold as the named technique. Do **not** grow the ~13–14 list unless later frames prove idle+react still reads broken at high no-clip rate — current boards do **not**. Optional M16 `#4 straight` stays taxonomy spend, not a missing-clip patch. *(Invented spend advice.)*
4. **DROP-RATE JUDGMENT (INVENTED — not a measured %):** R97's **92.6%** (830/896) **does** change the answer — no-clip technique drops can sit in a similar band **IF** reaction + instruments carry. Broken threshold: **receiver also silent**, or **HUD claims contact with zero visual on either body for consecutive beats**. Do not invent a false "measured" broken percent. 10%/50% thresholds rejected as purchase pressure.
5. **HONEST options:** drop thrower anim; play region-matched reaction; compress/skip beat window; widen stay HOME (already fixed — not a new exit); idle + log/commentary OK. **FORBIDDEN-in-a-hat:** hook for uppercut, body for head, any wrong technique clip, "generic" secretly sold as the named technique, casual list growth, CLAIM-TIGHT as missing-clip patch.
6. **Q2 — "Nothing" at HOME:** stillness (continue guard/loc) — **not** a broken T-pose. Becomes broken when HUD/beat claims contact and **neither body answers**. Frames: `m19_02` (broken path), `m19_03` (honest path).
7. **SHIPPED (Q6):** Madden Super Sim/GameCast, FM highlight densities, OOTP text-wins — **drop picture, keep ledger** (G42/G45 DIRECT). UFC/Undisputed/For Honor **no-clip technique policy = NOT FOUND**. Do not invent an EA "generic punch fallback."
8. Art only — no animation graph, no enums, no `.gd`.

## §2 — What does "nothing" look like at HOME? (Q2) — frames required

**Call:** at M14 HOME, thrower continuing guard/loc with no arm path is **stillness**, not a T-pose / broken mesh. It reads as **broken game** when:

1. HUD / beat already wrote contact, **and**
2. **Neither** body answers (no thrower path, no receiver reaction), **especially across consecutive beats**.

A single silent beat inside the R97 sample window can pass. Repeated dead beats with claimed contact teach the wrong density and look like a bug.
