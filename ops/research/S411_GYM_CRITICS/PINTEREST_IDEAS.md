# What Pinterest showed (S411, 2026-11-09, his Chrome; ideas only, nothing saved or copied)

Searches: sports management game main menu ui; diegetic game menu concept art; boxing gym interior game art stylized; stylish game hub screen character menu ui comic; isometric cutaway room diorama dramatic lighting game; vintage boxing fight poster illustration; management sim game screenshot desk office interface papers.

## Ideas worth taking
- **Cutaway rooms that feel big come from light and height, not floor area.** The strong dioramas have tall dark walls, a warm pool of light on the working area, deep shadow at the edges and dense clutter on every surface. Ours is even-lit with low walls.
- **Real boxing gyms in game art are dark and full:** hanging lamps over the ring, posters over every wall, brick or timber, a wet shine on the floor, kit hung everywhere. Nearly none are clean and bright. That sits against our standing rule "bright, alive, never an old garage": his call.
- **A boxing poster is the sport's own graphic language:** two figures facing each other, huge stacked type, flat red, yellow and cream, ink and halftone. It is a ready identity no management game uses, and it costs little to make.
- **Comic and angular screens are the ones that do not look like forms:** slanted panels, one large character breaking the frame, type at an angle, strong flat colour, things on screen that animate on every press.
- **Sports titles put one huge athlete in the middle** with a few big tiles round them, and very little text.
- **A desk covered in real paper** (folders, phone, stamps, photos) is a warm way to show a lot of data, and stays readable.

## What to avoid
- Generic dark fantasy RPG panels, purple mobile-game menus, and dashboard tables: these are most of what the searches return, and they are the "website form" he rejected.
