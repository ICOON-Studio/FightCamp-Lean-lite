## PART C — SANDBOX PARITY NUMBERS & SYSTEMS LOGIC

### 1. Procedural Predatory Promoter Offers

Predatory promoters are opportunistic operators who exploit vulnerable gyms by dangling quick liquidity in exchange for punitive contractual terms, mismatched opposition, and long-term financial leakage.

```
                  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
                  │          WEEKLY EVALUATION ENGINE (PER GYM)             │
                  └────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────┘
                                               │
                                               ▼
                         ┌───────────────────────────────────────────┐
                         │       IS GYM ELIGIBLE FOR PREDATORY OFFER?│
                         │ • Active career week ≥ 4                  │
                         │ • Active predatory contracts < 1          │
                         │ • Active unexpired offers == 0            │
                         │ • Cooldown timer == 0 weeks               │
                         └─────────────────────┬─────────────────────┘
                                               │ YES
                                               ▼
                         ┌───────────────────────────────────────────┐
                         │   CALCULATE OFFER PROBABILITY:            │
                         │   P_base = 0.04 (4% / week)               │
                         │   + Financial Distress Mod (0.00 to 0.25) │
                         │   + Fighter Hot-Streak Mod (0.00 to 0.15) │
                         └─────────────────────┬─────────────────────┘
                                               │
                                               ▼
                                      [ ROLL ≤ P_offer ]
                                      ├── NO  ──> Decrement Cooldown; Next Week
                                      └── YES ──> GENERATE CONTRACT TERMS
```

#### A. Trigger Probability & Cadence Formulas

* **Base Cadence:** Evaluated once per game week during the Monday morning processing cycle.
* **Prerequisites:**
  * Active career week $\ge 4$.
  * Total active predatory promotional contracts currently bound to gym $= 0$ (strict limit: $1$ per gym at early tiers).
  * Cooldown timer after a declined or expired offer $= 6\text{ weeks}$.
  * Cooldown timer after a completed or bought-out predatory contract $= 16\text{ weeks}$.
  * Max pending unexpired predatory offers in inbox $= 1$.

$$\mathbf{P}_{\text{offer}} = \mathbf{P}_{\text{base}} + \mathbf{M}_{\text{distress}} + \mathbf{M}_{\text{streak}}$$

Where:
* $\mathbf{P}_{\text{base}} = 0.04$ ($4\%$ chance per week in normal operating conditions).
* $\mathbf{M}_{\text{distress}}$ scales inversely with current cash reserves and burn:
  $$\mathbf{M}_{\text{distress}} = \begin{cases} 
  0.25 & \text{if } \text{cash} \le \$0 \\
  0.15 & \text{if } 0 < \text{cash} \le \$2,500 \\
  0.08 & \text{if } \$2,500 < \text{cash} \le \$5,000 \\
  0.00 & \text{if } \text{cash} > \$5,000 
  \end{cases}$$
* $\mathbf{M}_{\text{streak}}$ scales with the target fighter's recent success:
  $$\mathbf{M}_{\text{streak}} = \begin{cases} 
  0.15 & \text{if fighter on } \ge 2\text{-fight KO/Decision win streak} \\
  0.05 & \text{if fighter won last bout} \\
  0.00 & \text{if fighter coming off a loss or debutante} 
  \end{cases}$$
* **Maximum Cap:** $\mathbf{P}_{\text{offer}}$ is hard-capped at $0.40$ ($40\%$ per week under maximum financial duress and athletic momentum).

1. `CLAUSE_WEIGHT_TRAP`: Opponent weight limit contractually fixed at $+4\text{ lbs}$ above official division limit without financial penalty to opponent. If your fighter misses weight, forfeit $30\%$ of your remaining cut.
2. `CLAUSE_SHORT_NOTICE_RESET`: Promoter reserves right to shift bout date up by up to 14 days with only 5 days' formal notice.
3. `CLAUSE_REMATCH_LOCK`: If the player-fighter wins against the odds, the promoter holds a unilateral contractual option for an immediate rematch at a fixed $-25\%$ purse cut.
4. `CLAUSE_MEDICAL_INDEMNITY`: Gym assumes $100\%$ of all surgical, hospitalization, and facial cut-repair costs incurred during the bout.

### 2. The Debt Resolution Engine

A gym that cannot service its operations enters financial distress. The simulation enforces consequences through structured choices rather than an abrupt game-over screen.

```
                                CASH BALANCE DROPS BELOW $0.00
                                               │
                                               ▼
                                      [ WEEK ENDS: CASH < 0 ]
                                      State: `W1_DEFICIT` (Warning Notice)
                                               │
                                               ▼
                                  WEEK 2: CASH REMAINS BELOW $0.00
                                               │
                                               ▼
                      ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
                      │    DEBT RESOLUTION EVENT FIRES (BLOCKING MODAL) │
                      │       "THE LEDGER CLEARS OR THE DOORS CLOSE"    │
                      └────────────────────────┬────────────────────────┘
                                               │
         ┌─────────────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────┐
         ▼                                     ▼                                     ▼
┌──────────────────┐                 ┌──────────────────┐                  ┌──────────────────┐
│ OPTION A:        │                 │ OPTION B:        │                  │ OPTION C:        │
│ COMMERCIAL LOAN  │                 │ EMERGENCY BOUT   │                  │ AUSTERITY AXE    │
├──────────────────┤                 ├──────────────────┤                  ├──────────────────┤
│ Instant Cash:    │                 │ Instant Purse:   │                  │ Slash Upkeep:    │
│ +$10,000         │                 │ $2,500 – $4,500  │                  │ -40% to -60%     │
│ Interest Rate:   │                 │ Notice:          │                  │ Penalty:         │
│ 6% per week      │                 │ 7 to 10 Days     │                  │ Facility wear,   │
│ Term:            │                 │ Opponent:        │                  │ morale crash,    │
│ 16 Weeks         │                 │ Over-matched     │                  │ member drop      │
└──────────────────┘                 └──────────────────┘                  └──────────────────┘
```

#### A. Trigger Verification
* **Trigger:** Current Cash $< \$0.00$ at the conclusion of **two consecutive weekly financial processing ticks** (Week $N$ and Week $N+1$).
* **Event Interruption:** The calendar halts on Sunday midnight of Week $N+1$. A modal interrupts before the new training week can be scheduled.

---

#### B. The Three Options (Formulas & Parameters)

#### OPTION A: Hard Commercial Debt Restructuring (The Lender)
* **Capital Injected:** Fixed amount scaled to current tier debt:
  $$\text{Loan Principal} = |\text{Current Deficit}| + \$5,000 \quad (\text{Minimum } \$7,500, \text{ Maximum } \$20,000 \text{ at Tier 1})$$
* **Interest Rate:** $6.0\%$ compounding weekly ($0.06 \times \text{Remaining Principal}$).
* **Amortization Term:** Exactly $16\text{ weeks}$.
* **Weekly Mandatory Debt Service:**
  $$\text{Payment}_{\text{wk}} = \frac{\text{Principal}}{16} + (\text{Remaining Principal} \times 0.06)$$
  *(e.g., On a $\$10,000$ loan, Week 1 payment is $\$625.00 + \$600.00 = \$1,225.00$).*
* **Penalty for Missed Amortization Payment:** If cash cannot cover the weekly debt service payment:
  * Week 1 Missed: Bailiff Notice; Gym Prestige drops by $-50$.
  * Week 2 Missed: Bailiff impounds highest-value apparatus (e.g., Old Club Boxing Ring removed from inventory, reducing floor space used by $16\text{ m}^2$).
  * Week 3 Missed: Foreclosure (Career Game Over).

#### OPTION B: Emergency Short-Notice Banger (The Blood Sacrifice)
* **Mechanic:** The regional matchmaker arranges an unsanctioned or short-notice slot on an upcoming Friday card ($5\text{--}9\text{ days out}$).
* **Purse Structure:**
  $$\text{Emergency Purse} = \$3,500 + (|\text{Current Deficit}| \times 0.50)$$
  $80\%$ paid directly into gym bank account immediately upon signing; $20\%$ paid post-fight.
* **Matchmaking Severity:** Opponent is generated from the local circuit with an attribute advantage:
  $$\text{Opponent Power} = \text{Fighter Power} + (6 \text{ to } 12)$$
  $$\text{Opponent Technique} = \text{Fighter Technique} + (4 \text{ to } 10)$$
* **Training Interruption:** Fighter immediately drops all technical learning for the current week; stamina reduced by $-25\%$ due to crash weight cut. High probability ($35\%$) of sustaining a facial cut or soft-tissue injury.

#### OPTION C: Deep Facility Austerity (The Hatchet)
* **Mechanic:** Mothball non-essential facility operations, fire utility contractors, turn off facility heating, and terminate clean towel and medical laundry services.
* **Cost Reduction:**
  * Fixed Weekly Upkeep reduced immediately by $-50\%$ for the next $8\text{ weeks}$ (Starter upkeep drops from $\$367/\text{wk}$ down to $\$183.50/\text{wk}$; a running club burning $\$1,971/\text{wk}$ drops to $\$985.50/\text{wk}$).
* **Systemic Penalties (Active for the full 8-week austerity window):**
  1. `RECOVERY_RATE_CRIPPLED`: All roster fighters recover biological wear and fatigue at $-40\%$ normal speed (gym is cold, damp, unheated).
  2. `EQUIPMENT_DECAY_ACCELERATION`: Equipment wear degrades at $2.5\times$ normal rate due to lack of maintenance.
  3. `COMMERCIAL_MEMBER_MUTINY`: If gym uses commercial membership revenue, subscription income drops by $-35\%$ as casual paying members leave.
  4. `SQUAD_MORALE_DROP`: All signed fighters lose $-20$ Composure points during training camps.

#### C. Persistence, Secondary Breaches, and Bankruptcy

```
                                  DEBT RESOLUTION EXECUTED
                                             │
                                             ▼
                                   ADVANCE TIME 4 WEEKS
                                             │
                                             ▼
                                  IS CASH STILL < $0.00?
                                  ├── NO  ──> Gym stabilized; normal play resumes.
                                  └── YES ──> SECONDARY DEFICIT BREACH FIRES
```

* **Grace Period:** Following the selection of Option A, B, or C, the gym is granted a **3-week statutory immunity grace period** during which the debt resolution modal will not re-trigger.
* **Secondary Breach (Week 4 post-event, Cash still $< \$0$):**
  * The board / municipal authority issues a **Final Receivership Notice**.
  * The player is barred from signing new fighters, purchasing equipment, or expanding floor space.
  * Only one final action is permitted: **Liquidate 50% of the gym's physical assets at auction** ($0.30$ on the dollar of original equipment value).
* **Final Bankruptcy (Terminal Failure):**
  * If cash balance is negative for **6 cumulative weeks** following a Secondary Breach, the career is declared insolvent.
  * Game terminates with an analytical closure screen showing final managerial record, fighters lost, total debt accrued, and operational lifetime.
