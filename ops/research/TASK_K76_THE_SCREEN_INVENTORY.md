# K76 — GAME_FLOW, CUT DOWN TO ONE ANSWERABLE PIECE: THE SCREEN INVENTORY AND ITS ORDER

**Owner:** Kalli (words and flow). **No shell, no checkout, no repo.**
**This is `GAME_FLOW` re-cut.** That task has sat open because it was written as
"what screens exist, in what order, and what they say" — three questions, one of
which (what they say) is four passes of work you have already been doing
separately. **This asks only the first two.**

## WHY IT IS WORTH ASKING NOW

You have delivered four FLOW passes. Pass 4's cutover sheet landed today: every
string that changes, old → new, with the empty-card leave reading
`BACK TO THE DAY ▸`, the week twin `NEXT STOP ▸`, and the today route dying.

**Those passes have all been about WORDS ON A SCREEN. Nobody has written down
the screens themselves.** There is no single document that says what the game's
screens are, which one follows which, and where a player can get stuck. Four
passes of copy have been written against a structure that only exists in
somebody's head, and that is how a cutover sheet ends up describing a route that
"dies" — a route nobody had ever drawn.

## THE TWO QUESTIONS

**Q1 — WHAT SCREENS EXIST?** One line each: the screen's name, the one sentence
saying what a player is doing there, and whether it is a **place** (you stay and
act — the gym, the roster) or a **passage** (you pass through — a result, a
confirmation, a loading beat). That place/passage split is the load-bearing part:
a passage that behaves like a place is a screen a player gets stuck on, and a
place that behaves like a passage is one they never get to read.

**Q2 — WHAT IS THE ORDER?** Which screens lead to which. For each edge say what
the player did to take it. **And for every screen, the answer to one question:
`what takes me BACK, and is there always one?`** A screen with no way back is
the defect this document exists to find, and you will find some.

## THE THREE THINGS THAT MAKE THIS USEFUL RATHER THAN A DIAGRAM

1. **Name the DEAD ENDS explicitly**, in their own section. A screen you can
   reach and not leave; a screen reachable by two routes that returns you to
   only one of them; a passage that can be entered twice.
2. **Name the screens you are NOT SURE EXIST**, in their own section, marked
   **NOT CONFIRMED**. You are working from what you have been given, not from the
   code, and a guessed screen presented as real is worse than a gap. **You marked
   a state NOT COMPUTED on K74 rather than claiming wiring we did not have, and
   that honesty is exactly why that sheet shipped.** Do it again.
3. **The facility is a GYM, never a camp.** A fight camp is only the pre-bout
   preparation. That rule binds every word you write here.

## WHAT THIS IS NOT

- **Not the words.** No copy, no button labels, no headings. Passes 1–4 own
  those and this must not re-open them.
- **Not a visual design.** No layouts, no wireframes.
- **Not a recommendation.** Describe what is, and put what you think SHOULD
  change in a short closing section, clearly separated.

## AND THEN ARGUE AGAINST YOURSELF

End with the strongest case that **this document will be wrong the week after it
is written** — that a screen inventory maintained by hand goes stale the first
time an engineer adds a screen, and that the only inventory worth having is one
generated from the code. If you believe that, say so plainly and say what the
cheap version looks like instead. **Name your own weakest point unprompted.**
