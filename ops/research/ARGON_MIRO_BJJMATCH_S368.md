# Argon on the Miro frame 'BJJMATCH' (S368)

gemini-4-argon; tokens in 3,126, out 789, $0.0141, 15 s; pictures: grapple_walk_2380.png, claims_sheet.png.

Score 6/10. Biggest risk: Reading a text panel every few seconds pulls the player's eyes off the 3D grappling animations and turns a tensely watched coaching sim into a repetitive menu-clicking chore over dozens of belt matches.

## Checks
- **Only offer choices on heavy settles, never mid-scramble** - Calling moves during fast sweeps or transitions contradicts 'one flowing chain' and turns watching the 3D grapple into a frantic UI-reading test. (on: Card 1 & Card 4)
- **How does the graph pick the 2-3 moves?** - The prototype screenshot shows 25+ moves from Mount, so the design must specify that her drilled repertoire filters the panel down to 2-3 options. (on: Card 2 & Prototype image)
- **Show failure consequences alongside success odds** - In BJJ, a 40% armbar from mount that loses top position on failure is a terrible call when ahead by 2 points with 30 seconds left. (on: Card 3 & Card 5)
- **Calling every node becomes puppeteering, not cornering** - If the player overrides her every 5 seconds, her autonomous 'game plan' and weekly gym prep feel meaningless compared to direct player micromanagement. (on: Card 4 & Card 6)
- **Missing match clock, takedowns, and guard pulls** - No-gi scoring means nothing without a clock to force urgency, and the opening standing-to-ground choice dictates the entire match flow. (on: Card 5)

## Ideas
- **Gym drilling builds her 3-move position hand** - Link the weekly management sim to the match by having her only offer moves in the panel that you actually scheduled her to drill in camp.
- **Corner Composure limits how often you override** - Give the coach 3-4 'Corner Shouts' per match so her trained plan runs the routine chains and you only step in at crucial crossroads.
- **Coach posture first: Stall, Cook, or Hunt** - Let the player set a persistent pacing stance that shifts her default plan's odds and cardio drain without clicking every single node.
- **Opponent scouting reveals hidden counter traps** - Tie gym prep to the UI by hiding when an opponent baits a high-percentage pass into a leg-lock counter unless you scouted their tape.
- **Fighter tunnel vision locks out calls when gassed** - When her cardio bottoms out or she is caught in deep submission defense, grey out the panel so she falls back on raw habits.
- **Slow-mo breath window on heavy settles only** - Trigger a 3-second audio-muffled slow-down when chest-to-chest or guard settles happen so the player can read the 3D tie-up and the panel together.
