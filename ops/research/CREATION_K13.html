CAREER CREATION

Country → city → gym is the last stretch of the pre-kit game. No rail, no top career bar, no Continue — ShellChrome is career-only. This study picks the shape, designs CreationChrome, shows what each decision needs from the listed data APIs, and draws the seam into THE DAY.

A · THE SHAPE

PICK: ONE FLOW WITH STEPS — a single CreationFlow under CreationChrome. Steps COUNTRY → CITY → GYM; committed choices stay on the trail strip so the third decision is never blind.
REJECTED · THREE SCENES — separate CountryMap / CityScreen / GymHub loads kill the memory of market + city unless every scene rebuilds a summary. That is how the 2026-08 look stayed half-blind.
REJECTED · ONE BOARD SWAP MIDDLE — swapping only the middle without step chrome leaves "where am I / how far" unanswered once ShellChrome is gone. Middle-swap is the implementation detail inside the flow, not the product shape.

B · THE CHROME

Own CreationChrome — do not reuse K7 set-piece frame (way-out / stakes / clock / names is fight gear). Do not extend ShellChrome (rail + Continue are career furniture). CreationChrome = brand strip + horizontal step strip (1/2/3) + BACK + committed trail + body. Step 2 of 3 reads as CITY lit, COUNTRY checked, GYM dim — no rail required.

Gold ×1 is the forward action (CHOOSE MARKET / ENTER CITY / OPEN GYM). Green appears only when ShellChrome arrives on the handoff settle frame.

C · WHAT EACH DECISION NEEDS

COUNTRY — on screen from get_country_combat_profile() + get_country_data(): economy, talent depth, combat popularity (and specialty / gym count as supporting). Leave out rivals, orgs, events, coaches, prospects — those are city / later.

CITY — on screen from get_rival_gyms(), get_world_organizations(), get_world_events(): rival gyms, orgs headquartered / assigned here, upcoming cards. Leave out full coach / prospect lists (get_world_coaches / get_world_prospects belong to SCOUTING after the save exists) and floor editing.

GYM — name field + starter floor take (generate_starter_boxing_floor) + starter roster take. Leave out store, repair, assignment — those are GYM hub after day one.

D · HANDOFF → THE DAY

After begin_new_career → generate_starter_boxing_floor → finalize_new_career, CreationChrome folds and ShellChrome locks onto THE DAY. K7 shutter language, inverted: K7 removes the shell for a set-piece; creation installs the shell for the career. Same "changing gear, not a load" seam feel — not the K7 frame itself.

E · BOOT MENU (one line)

MainMenu is its own rebuild stage — slots / load / settings is boot plumbing, not the country→city→gym decision chain.

F · KIT GAPS

Tried gym / training / schedule / scouting families first. Reuse pair (slate), badge, alert_strip, filter_strip, squad_row rhythm, capacity_meter, floor_grid cousin for starter preview, illegal_refusal for empty name.
