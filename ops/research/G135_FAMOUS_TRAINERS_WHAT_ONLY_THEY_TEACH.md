## 2. Off-balancing - who owns what

- Kuzushi / judo throws in MMA: the Dagestani judo-sambo pipeline (gi judo forced on wrestlers, then stripped for MMA). PROXY: no single MMA-famous judo coach.
- Body-lock trips / suck-back: Danaher (half-guard body lock, knee picks); Dagestani body lock on the fence.
- Cage chain wrestling: Abdulmanap / Eagles / AKA. Punch -> grab -> throw as the stated two-stage fight.
- Handcuff: folkstyle wrist ride popularised in MMA by Khabib; not invented in Dagestan; later sold as an instructional.
- Trips / elevation from guard: Marcelo Garcia (butterfly + X-guard).
- Oblique kick as anti-level-change: Winkeljohn / Jones.

Time to acquire: camp visit (2-8 weeks) buys entries and a few high-percentage trips, not the chain. One dedicated
camp (8-12 weeks) installs a narrow package if the athlete already wrestles. The real chain is years (Dagestan:
wrestling ~8, judo ~15, sambo ~17). PROXY coach line: "a week to learn, a year to ride under punches".

## 3. How exclusive a signature is

Almost nothing stays in-house once it wins on TV.

| Mode | How it spreads | Example |
|---|---|---|
| In-house only (rare) | daily room + language/culture barrier | Dagestani daily schedule; commercial camps now sell a slice (~EUR 1,690 / 14 days) |
| Lineage | students become coaches | Danaher -> Zahabi; Chute Boxe -> Kings |
| Instructionals | paid leak | Danaher heel-hook sets; Bravo rubber guard |
| Short camp visit | 1-6 weeks, drop-in or invite | Jackson Wink drop-in ~$50/class; elite title camp ~$40-60k/month (coaches + partners); typical camp 6-12 weeks, $2k-$12k mid-card |
| Private block | pads with the name coach | Wittman keeps a small stable on purpose - access is the product |

Rule: the MOVE is copyable in a camp; the SYSTEM under fatigue, with the coach in the corner, against people who
already know the counter, is what you only get by living there.

## 5. Bad fits

- Cloning the gym's champion onto a different animal (Rory MacDonald as a GSP copy - critics' opinion, PROXY).
- Coach-fighter temperament mismatch (Strickland left a low-energy coach; Winkeljohn and Jones split 2023).
- Boxer who would not commit to wrestling at an MMA gym (PROXY).
- Pattern: mismatch of style or temperament fails more often than "he didn't know the move".

## Game archetypes (Grok's proposal)

1. **Film Monk** - opponent-specific parameters; 8-12 week live-in camp; round-1 trap rate up, plan collapses under damage.
2. **Low-Damage Scholar** - clean high-rep work, no hard spars; months on the roster; durability and decisions up, KO rate down.
3. **Leg-Lock Architect** - ashi system + back hub; year-round or only one entry; scramble submission threat; ruleset-limited.
4. **Mountain Sambo Father** - chain wrestle -> handcuff -> ground-and-pound; years, or a 4-week block that sticks only on a wrestling base; control time.
5. **Thai Pad Animal** - live pads, kicks off the fence; 6-week camp with him on pads; creative KO entries.
6. **Forward-Pressure Muay Thai** - walk-down, clinch knees, ground elbows; daily wars (high injury); pace and damage, weak vs elite wrestlers.
7. **Quiet Corner Boxer** - jab that breaks stance then one power shot; long private block; better between-round adjustments.
8. **Room Captain** - culture + situational sparring; join as a resident; consistency, fails if the fighter rejects the energy.
9. **Dutch Cannon** - power kickboxing + low kicks; 8-week residency; one-shot threat, predictable without wrestling.
10. **Guard Inventor** - rubber guard / butterfly-X off-balance; flexibility block + months; bottom control, dead if hips are stiff.
11. **Range Conductor** - long kicks + volume boxing; long technical camp; point control at range.
12. **Lineage Gate** - only students of students get the full chain; seasons of apprenticeship.

**Design rule: a signature is a system trained under that coach's constraints. A 2-week visit unlocks a module; a year
on the roster unlocks the tree.**
