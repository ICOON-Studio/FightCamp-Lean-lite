### Damage
Every blow is priced in two stages: **who it lands on and with what contact** (attacker), then **whether it was blunted** (defender), then a pure wear/knockdown model.

- **Force** (`FightRealtime._run_impact`, ~line 5942): `force = FORCE_BASE(0.18) + FORCE_POWER_SPAN(0.42) × power + flush`. `power` is the attacker's `power` stat × a fatigue multiplier. `flush = randf() ^ exponent`, where `exponent = 2.2 − 1.0 × technique`.
