## What would a human do?
A kickboxing analyst or cornerman watching film classifies each landed low kick by what the receiver's body did:
nothing visible, a weight shift / lifted foot, a stumble step, a buckle (knee dips), a stance switch, a fall. They
note what raised the odds of the big ones (calf vs thigh, kick landed as he stepped in, leg already damaged).
