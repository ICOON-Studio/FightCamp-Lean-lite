1. During-round display
All three, layered—body language first, then subtle markers. The limp/guard-drop/grimace is primary because it’s immersive and matches what you’d actually see ringside. Add small, unobtrusive colour chips on the on-screen silhouette (not floating UI): pale orange = hurt, deep red = critical, plus a tiny pulse when fresh damage lands. Purely visual cues feel too ambiguous; big pop-ups break the flow. The markers confirm what you suspect, so you’re not guessing—"he’s favouring that leg → yes, it’s hurt"—without pausing or cluttering the fight.

3. Payoff / failure visibility
Show it immediately on the bell:
Success → limb icon brightens; fighter moves sharper, puts weight back on the leg, holds guard higher
Partial → colour fades but doesn’t vanish; still favours it, just less
Neglect → darkens further; movement visibly stiffer, they avoid using it entirely
Text overlay brief: "Right leg: eased" / "Worsened — movement limited" — then get out of the way.
4. Reference & trap
Nail it: Motorsport Manager pit stops — every choice has clear cost vs gain, seconds tick down, parallel work feels alive. Also EA UFC 4 corner: simple priority choices that directly change how the next round plays.
Trap: too many options — don’t turn 60 seconds into a spreadsheet. If I can’t decide in 5 seconds, it’s bad design. Don’t separate "talk strategy" from "treat injury" — combine them so every second matters.
5. Motorsport Manager-style corner — YES
Absolutely exciting. Problem cards pinned to the body silhouette, each action with:
⏱️ Time cost (fast = 10s, careful = 20s, rushed = 15s + fumble chance)
Crew skill modifies success — your top cutman reduces risk; a rookie messes up more
Tradeoff example: "Clear airway (12s, low risk) vs Eye compress (18s, high reward)" — skip the compress and vision narrows; skip clearing and gas tank drains fast.
Why take the risk? Only if the cost of not risking is immediate and worse:
"If eye swells shut → -40% strike accuracy, can’t see left-side attacks"
"If breath stays tight → stamina drains 2× faster"
Show the outcome within the first 30s of Round N: blurred vision overlay, heavier breathing animation, or — if you gambled right — clear movement and sustained output.
