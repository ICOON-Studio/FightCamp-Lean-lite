## GROK'S VERDICT IN ONE LINE
"A mapping that seats 190 unnamed techniques onto those 21 with zero leftovers is a name-family
map. ... Until saddle/inside-ashi, knee-on-belly, plum, sprawl and fence ride exist, any map that
reports zero unmapped is still a name map." bjj_5050 is the wrong picture most often; grapple_takedown
second (a standing-to-floor film played for floor-to-floor moves); mma_dirty_boxing third (strikes
are a layer, not the grip). The eight holds with a referee-runnable body test (guard, mount, side,
back, half, north-south, turtle, front headlock) lie only when misused as destinations or cousins.

## Section 1 - positional hierarchy as the sports teach it
Tree: standing -> clinch -> transition -> ground-top -> ground-bottom. Aliases are the same body
arrangement under another art's name.

### 1.6 What the sports reward
BJJ (IBJJF 2024, 3 s): back 4 = mount 4 > pass 3 > takedown 2 = sweep 2 = KOB 2; side/N-S cash the pass; guards 0; crucifix/turtle/ashi 0. Folkstyle: takedown 3, escape 1, reversal 2, near-fall 2-4, pin. UWW: points for the action, ordered par terre is a restart. Judo: 10 osaekomi; mount/side/N-S collapse into osaekomi. Sambo (FIAS): standing vs ground sub-states; painful holds on arms and legs, no chokes, no twisting heel hooks; broken when the defender stands. Muay Thai clinch: plum / single collar / over-under / double under; the prize is posture break + knees + dumps, no ground ladder. MMA: damage, then effective grappling, aggression, cage control; "grounded" = any body part other than hands/feet (ABC 2024).

## Against itself - which owned hold is the wrong picture most often
grapple_control is the easy target (no body test; emptiness is visible). **bjj_5050 is the wrong picture most often**: a measured IBJJF cell (both seated, each with a triangle on one leg) standing in for saddle, inside ashi, SLX and sambo entanglements that differ in facing, trapped-leg count and legal finishes - the audience can see the legs. Runner-up **grapple_takedown**: a neutral-to-floor film played for mat return (starts attached, off the floor), guard pull (attacker ends on the bottom), knee-slice and toreando (never leave the floor), Thai dump (starts in a locked plum), seoi nage (loads a back); only high crotch, ankle pick and a clean double may honestly use it. Third **mma_dirty_boxing** as plum, body-lock, fence ride and whizzer: strikes are a layer, not the grip. Not over-indicted: bjj_guard, bjj_mount, bjj_side_control, bjj_back_control, bjj_half_guard, bjj_north_south, bjj_turtle, bjj_front_headlock have body tests a referee could run and lie only when misused as destinations or cousins.
