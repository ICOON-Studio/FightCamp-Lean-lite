## 2. Coach's read: a decision, not an order

**ESTIMATE** Change `OfferCard.facts` and `CoachRead.read` to build one structured read for each fixed-opponent offer. Order: actual booking block; projected kg over at the day-before weigh-in and days since the last bout on bout day; then the strongest verified gain and cost. Use the goal's actual progress and deadline, not its title; if a bout cannot affect it, say so. Use `BoutRead.rank_move` only after checking its forecast matches the actual points-settlement rules; otherwise show points/place as unknown, not a promised promotion. For pro rows use their own stakes and purse rules. A debut with no fixed opponent gets terms, ticket exposure, costs and deadline only, never invented style or odds. Connect `BoutPage.build`, `ProFights._row` and `ProOffer._house` to the same builder with explicit missing fields.

**ESTIMATE** Change `CoachRead.read` and `OfferCard.facts` to compare *known* opponent style/tells with plan lines that `PlanFight.can_do` permits at the relevant situation. Show one reason and the condition: “Pressure is known; your usable exit covers the ropes. Estimate, not a guarantee.” If no relevant evidence is known, say “not enough information”, not a fabricated weakness. A short rest under 21 days or forecast 2 kg over suggests “Look for a later date”, not “camp hard”; link to the existing week or ask flow only where legal. Never present an unsafe booking as a recommendation.

**ESTIMATE** Change `CoachRead.read` and `line`: confidence is 1–3 marks for evidence coverage, not a win probability: 1 for unknown style or wide rating range, 2 for partial evidence, 3 only for known style plus a narrow range. Staff quality may narrow an existing estimate, never reveal undiscovered tells, change eligibility, or manufacture certainty. With no hire label the same evidence “Your estimate”. Clicking a reason expands the actual input and the smallest known change (for example, 7 more days of rest); a brief/full toggle changes 0 combat numbers. Use “No confirmed gain/cost” rather than filler.

**ESTIMATE** Change `CoachRead.read` and `BoutFinish.run` to snapshot forecasts at acceptance and score only measurable predictions after settlement. The history shows correct/comparable out of the last 15 *resolved predictions*, excluding unknowns, postponed/void bouts and subjective advice; retain the denominator, not “12/15 correct” for untestable orders. This changes displayed trust count by 0–15, never odds. Avoid retroactively grading old saves without snapshots.

**ESTIMATE — choices:** accept an even bout for activity (moves booked bouts +1, consumes preparation time); pass an unsafe novice mismatch (booked +0, loses this opportunity, and show any existing loyalty price); seek a later date when legal (rest +7 days for a one-week delay, but an offer may expire); accept a legal step up for possible goal/ladder progress at greater loss risk. None dominates: a pass can be right when the bout is unsafe or forecast weight remains over, and an easy match may bring little progress. Check rested even, unsafe novice, heavy cut, higher rank with no actual place gained, and a pro tune-up where purse and progress disagree.

## 3. The visit: coaching earns attention, not a purchase

**ESTIMATE** Change `LiveRound.exchange`, `PlanRecord.summary` and `BoutFinish._card_ask` to persist one bounded evidence record: situation, intended usable line or actual call, whether applied, and settled outcome. Eligible initially only for a beaten amateur opponent, and only when a fitted call is followed by a successful escape or the same planned answer works at least twice under recorded pressure. Change `CardAsk.reason` to quote that exact action; no evidence means no ask, but `BoutFinish.run` still writes a useful Why. This deliberately does not reward a loss with a visit in v2: the evidence is still recorded for learning; widening eligibility needs separate identity and fairness design.

**ESTIMATE** Change `CardAsk.chance` and `asks`: after eligibility, seeded chance = clamp(0.05 + min(reputation, 100) × 0.001 + 0.03 for qualifying evidence, 0, 0.18); count the evidence bonus once per bout regardless of repeated moves. Publish this in the handbook, including gates and seed; do not pretend wins are club size. Omit a club penalty until real club standing is available. No rep 40/70 prospect tiers: current standing cannot honestly establish those thresholds. No multi-bout spectator pathway yet: the game has no verified watcher/identity evidence; add only after that exists. This prevents farming weak opponents for ever-increasing chance: repeated easy wins without pressure evidence yield 0% and repetition adds no extra credit.

**ESTIMATE** Change `BoutFinish._card_ask`, `CardAsk.walkin`, `Roster.sign_fighter` and `Sim.sign` to keep a stable rival link and move that identity on signing, preserving record including the just-settled bout, history and any medical rest; remove the rival from active competition exactly once. If the offer has no resolvable stable identity, do not create a visitor until identity can be reconciled; never synthesize a duplicate or silently invent missing history. Check `GymSpend.used < places` both at the ask and at signing; same shared capacity for boxing and no-gi, no spot reserved by a visit. If full on signing, explain and leave the visit open through expiry.

**ESTIMATE** Change `RosterActs.act`, `Roster.pass_on`, `Sim.step_day` and `CardAsk.asks`: pass or expiry records a 120-day per-rival cooldown; a future eligible bout may produce a new seeded ask after that, never an automatic return or referral. Persist link, evidence, expiry and cooldown through save/load; migrate old visitors without a link as non-transferable rather than guessing. Show the inclusive expiry date in `ResultPage.summary`, `Needs.needs` and `Interview.build`; warn on Continue only if its next advance crosses that date, with Continue still available. Keep the amber need non-blocking.

**ESTIMATE — choices:** Sign now: shared places used +1, weekly stipend + the shown actual amount, existing coach schedule changes as calculated, and choose the existing starting focus/goal. Pass: places +0, stipend +0, cooldown +120 days. Trial first: extend `Interview.build` and `RosterActs.act` to use the existing trial flow with a single dated appointment, no roster place committed and no guaranteed signing; expiry pauses only until that appointment, then give 1 day to sign or pass. Show any actual trial scheduling cost, and recheck capacity at sign. Delaying is simply leaving the visit open until the inclusive deadline, not an unlimited promise to return. Display club, rank and style only when known; otherwise “unknown”. Do not promise a coach-time split the scheduler does not enforce. These options trade information and time against cost and scarce capacity, rather than making every visitor a free signing.

## 4. Corner's eye: see a settled clue, choose at the next opening

**ESTIMATE** Change `PlanFight.choose` and `LiveRound.exchange` to record intended line, actual selected line, and one of no line, not ready, deliberately held, fired and worked, or fired and lost. Do not change resolution odds. Change `RingMap.plan_mark`, `angle_at` and `RingTopDown._plan_arrow`: faint = intended but not yet attempted; solid = fired and worked; cross = fired and lost; dotted = unavailable/ignored with reason; held is separately labelled “held”, not failure. Use the actual fired step for movement; never turn the map for an unfired arrow. Include a small accessible legend and a Show plan/Clean view toggle (0 odds change). Retain the last two settled rounds' attempted lines as dim ghosts, with round labels; no fabricated past intent for old saves.

**ESTIMATE** Change `RingMap.place`, `owner` and `RingTopDown._draw_frame` to remove the win-streak wash; highlight only the side pinned by the recorded ropes/corner card or duel state, and otherwise say position unknown/open. No “walking down” claim without recorded directional movement. Change `_tags`/`_boxer` so hurt and gas below 35 can both be shown, with a subtle pulse and static/reduced-motion alternative. Role labels on figures; names, if shown, only in the coloured header. Show the last settled exchange and when the next live call or bell is available; no extra broadcast meter.

**ESTIMATE** Change `FightOptions._refresh_top`, `BoutNight.fight_step` and `CornerJobsRow.menu` to surface only legal existing controls at their existing timing: on ropes with an unavailable exit, offer the existing tie-up call if legal, plus say nothing; at the bell suggest a usable exit swap or keep the plan. A call adds 1 to the existing shout count when taken up and spends its existing energy cost; repeated calls reduce uptake. Say nothing adds 0 calls, saving attention but leaving the position to the existing simulation. A swap replaces 1 plan line subject to the existing limit, sacrificing its former answer; keeping it changes 0 lines. Do not execute a call from a passive overlay or add an odds modifier. Persist one evidence-backed clue in `BoutFinish.run`/`ResultPage.summary` for watched and skipped bouts alike, with “no recorded clue” for old data.

**ESTIMATE — checks:** held is not failed; a winner retreating is not shown pushing; two consecutive plan attempts are distinguishable from a two-round ghost; hurt and tired coexist; clean view hides arrows only; shouting, saving attention, and replacing a line each have a situation where the other is better. Verify the same settled result with the overlay on/off.
