## 1. Tuning loops
- **Motorsport Manager.** Hidden: aero / speed / handling optima per track. Shown: 6 sliders -> 3 outputs; 5-band faces +
  direction ("understeer"). Learn: practice laps from a high-Feedback driver shrink a visible band; two drivers give two
  points on the curve. Fun: cheap experiments, visible "98%" payoff. Chore: same sliders every weekend if feedback is weak.
  Pit: speed vs safety; Fast raises error (wrong compound, loose wheel, fire) - tired crew worse (Steam MM pit-crew patch).
- **F1 Manager 22-24.** 5 sliders vs 5 confidence axes (oversteer, brake, corner, traction, straights); shrinking blue
  window; 5 feedback pips per stint. Fun: bars move now. Chore: 3 practice sessions or leave bonus points on the table.
- **Football Manager.** Familiarity bar, opposition instructions, half-time talk in 6 tones. Fun: one instruction that
  changes the next 45. Chore: constant flips reset familiarity.
- **Punch Club 2.** Opponent school, buffs, move list; retune offence/defence between rounds. Fun: loadout vs a named style.
- **Blood Bowl.** Reroll stock; fun = spend the token on the drive-ending action; chore = burning it on a safe push.
- **XCOM.** A shown % is a decision; misses feel unfair because humans read 90% as "should hit".
- **Slay the Spire.** Intent icon = plan this turn against a known next beat; removing intent is the anti-pattern.
- **Copy MM + F1:** few sliders -> 3 outputs, hidden target, band-narrowing practice, 5-word verdict + direction. Copy the
  pit: time-boxed job list, Fast = mistake chance.

## 2. Real corner (card fuel)
- Official rest 60 s; usable work ~45-50 s after entry/exit (The Athletic); some cutmen cite ~35-40 s. Charge the ring at
  the bell to steal seconds.
- **Why no nose-blow with an orbital/nasal injury:** a medial-wall / floor fracture opens sinus to orbit; a Valsalva (blow,
  sneeze) pumps air in and fat acts as a one-way valve -> **orbital emphysema**, lid balloons, rare vision threat. Advice:
  no blow/sneeze ~2 weeks. Up to 75% of such fractures show some emphysema (Healthline; EyeWiki).
- Task times (PROXY): wipe 2-3 s; adrenaline swab + pressure most of the minute; enswell on immediately and held; Avitene
  swap ~20 s if rebleed; Vaseline plug last 3-5 s. Parallel: cutman on the face, chief second talks, second man ices a calf.
- Mistakes + cost: shoving a hematoma sideways (swelling grows); no ready swab (10-15 s lost); enswell left at home
  (Tyson-Douglas); treating everything = treating nothing; adrenaline in the eye; swab before pressure.

## 3. Five two-sided cards
1. **Blow the nose.** + clear air, next-round gas. - orbital emphysema / eye slams shut. Shows: puffy lid, doctor look.
2. **Shove the mouse** (push the swelling). + sight for one round. - bigger hematoma later. Shows: eye closes mid-round 2.
3. **Avitene + hard press on a gash.** + doctor lets it go on. - packs poorly, reopens on the first jab. Shows: blood sheet.
4. **Ice the dead leg hard.** + stands up next round. - numb calf, check/teep lands clean. Shows: planted foot fails.
5. **One instruction vs two treatments.** + "jab, circle off the right". - cut still open. Shows: same cut, same doctor.

## 4. Presentation (5-second read)
One screen: body silhouette, 1-3 pulsing hotspots, severity WORD (STOP / CLOSE / BLEED / NO-AIR / DEAD-LEG), a 60 s bar,
2-3 cards each with Benefit | Risk | Time cost. Parallel jobs share a lane. Fast crew = higher miss pip (MM pit).
References: F1 Manager practice (shrinking band + comment), FM half-time panel, MM garage faces, Into the Breach / StS
intent icons - never a paragraph.

## 5. Camp knowledge loop
Treat the opponent as MM's track. Start: wide band ("southpaw pressure, body work"). Style-matched spar = installation
lap: unlocks slot 1 (tells, range, first combo). Film study = slot 2 (habit on the fence/ropes, exit side). After both: a
visible optimum band on 3 plan axes (range / pace / target). Same-style spar without film = one point, not a band;
mismatched spar = noise. Cap 2 slots per opponent. Unlock AFTER the session that produced feedback, not on purchase.
