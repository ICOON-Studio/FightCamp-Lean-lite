Chat: grok.com/c/8aba02a5-465a-438c-90a1-f9c1da3d51ff. Condensed from the reply; figures and grades as Grok gave them.

**Headline:** the 11-12 attacks per match figure counts BOTH athletes. Olympic medallists make 6.4±2.4 attacks per match
and their opponents 5.3±2.2 (SOURCED, injoma 28514). One fighter makes about 5-7 standing attacks per match, or 1.4-1.8
per minute (SOURCED, MDPI 18/11/5841).

1. **Time-motion** (Soriano 2019, 150 IJF matches)
   - 9.7±4.9 effort sequences per match, 22.4±7.1 s each.
   - Within a sequence: try-grip 6.6±3.2 s, accomplished grip 6.7±3.8 s (men 6.1±3.5), the throw 1.3±0.5 s.
   - Grip dispute is 49-56% of effort. About 10 hajime-matte blocks per 4 minutes (PROXY).
2. **Attacks and scores (men)**
   - Standing attacks per fighter range from 4.6 (heavyweight) to 7.2 (half-lightweight), n=548 (SOURCED).
   - Share of standing attacks that score: 11-16%. Calmet 973 attacks gave ~11-12%; medallists 15.7±9.9%; the 2011
     Worlds 19%.
   - Across ALL technical actions, EJC 2021 had ~8% score.
3. **Grip to attack**
   - The accomplished grip lasts about 6 s for men and 7 s for women before the attack. Heavyweights hold 9.5±3.7 s,
     lightweights 4.6±1.7 s.
   - A non-standard grip must attack within 5 s or draw a shido (the IJF rule).
   - About 40% of grip exchanges break with no throw (JUDGEMENT).
4. **Weight and style:** lighter fighters attack more on a shorter grip. Heavyweights grip longer (heavyweight grip time
   165 s against 60 s for the extra-lightweight).
5. **Recommendation:** attack every 6 s of accomplished grip (8-10 s above 100 kg). 12% of standing attacks score
   (range 11-16%). Combined attempts per match: 11-12.

The attempt count is just under G136's 11-12, and the score share is below the 11-16% band. That makes it a follow-up
