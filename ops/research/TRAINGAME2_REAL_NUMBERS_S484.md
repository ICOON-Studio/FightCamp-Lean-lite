## Design finding for the critics
Dola's story ("five more minutes Friday and the hook is ready") cannot happen: minutes on a night move mastery by ~0.03 a
minute at 20-30 mastery, so five minutes is ~0.15. The lever that makes a move ready is a NIGHT, not minutes.
