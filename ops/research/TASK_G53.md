## The questions

1. **THE INTER-PUNCH INTERVAL INSIDE ONE COMBINATION.** Not between exchanges —
   G52 covered that. **Inside a single combination**: a two- or three-punch
   burst, how many milliseconds separate punch one from punch two? Is there a
   measured figure in boxing biomechanics, time-motion analysis, or notational
   research? Give it in **seconds or milliseconds with the denominator named**,
   and say whether it differs between a jab-cross and a hook.

2. **HOW MANY PUNCHES IS A COMBINATION?** The distribution, not the average, if
   the distribution exists: what share of combinations are 2, 3, 4, 5+? Boxing
   and MMA separately if both are published. G49 said combinations are roughly
   **half of offensive actions** — this asks how long they are once they start.

3. **DOES THE INTERVAL STRETCH AS THE COMBINATION GOES ON?** Is punch four
   later after punch three than punch two was after punch one? Or is a
   combination a fixed rhythm the whole way through?

4. **WHAT DOES THE OTHER MAN DO DURING ONE?** While A is throwing a three-punch
   combination, is B measurably silent — covering, slipping, backing out — or
   does he throw inside it? This is the difference between a combination being a
   **run of one thrower** and a **fast trade**, and our engine currently produces
   74% SHARED bursts, which is what a coin flip does. Is there any measured
   figure for how often a combination is interrupted by a return shot?

5. **WHAT MAKES A MAN THROW ONE AT ALL?** G49 said range gates the action set.
   Is a combination started by anything measurable — a landed jab, an opponent
   backing to the ropes, a stance change? **Refuse this if it is only coaching
   lore**, exactly as you refused pressure in G50. A refusal here is worth more
   than an invented trigger.

6. **THE ONE CHECK.** If we simulate a round and want to grade our combination
   structure against ONE published quantity, which is it? Name the quantity, the
   source, and the band we should expect to land in. If the honest answer is
   that no such quantity is published and the structure is ungradeable, **say
   that** — we would far rather know the number does not exist than have one
   approximated for us.
