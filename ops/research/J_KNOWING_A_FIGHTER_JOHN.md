# J_COACHREAD1: how long a boxing coach takes to really know a boxer
Every row checked 2026-11-10. Labels: MEASURED / OFFICIAL / DERIVED / ESTIMATE / OPINION (a trainer's or author's view) / no source found. (?) means I only saw a snippet or abstract. In quotes, [brackets] replace a fighter pronoun with a name.

**Headline:** tests read the body fast, within 1–2 sessions after a practice session. Sparring reads the person over weeks; GB Boxing says "two or three weekends". Nothing reads potential well: junior results explain about 2% of senior results. No study measures how long a coach takes to judge chin, heart or composure.
