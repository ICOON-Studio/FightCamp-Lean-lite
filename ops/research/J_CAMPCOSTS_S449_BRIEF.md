# John - real numbers for the amateur fight week and camp locations (S449)

You are checking real-world numbers for a boxing gym management game set in England (amateur club boxing under England Boxing,
then a first pro fight). Prices in the game are UK prices x 1.25, shown in dollars. Give sources (name + year) for every number,
say when a number is your estimate, and keep each answer short.

## 1. Club-show matching at walking weight
- How are amateur bouts matched in England Boxing club shows (skills/novice/open)? Is it by weigh-in weight with an allowed gap
  rather than the class limit? What gaps are allowed (by weight and by age/sex), and is there a bout-day weigh-in?
- Do club boxers ever cut weight for a club show, and how much is normal?
- How late can a bout be made (days before the show), and how often does an opponent pull out? Is a late replacement common?

## 2. Camp location costs (one boxer + one coach, one week)
- A rented hill cottage in the UK for one week (Peak District, Lake District, Wales): typical weekly rent, off-season and peak.
- A small seaside house for one week (Cornwall, Devon, Norfolk): weekly rent.
- A sparring trip to a bigger city club (e.g. Sheffield, Manchester, London): mat fees per session, travel, a cheap hotel per night.
- Food and extras for a week away for two people.
Our first guesses (check them): cottage GBP 700 a week, seaside house GBP 800, city-club trip GBP 320 a week. Say whether each
is low, fair or high.

## 3. Who pays
- In UK amateur boxing, who usually pays for a camp or a sparring trip away: the club, the boxer, a sponsor, or a mix?
- Do amateur boxers have savings or stipends at all? What is a realistic weekly amount a working amateur could put in?

## 4. Fight week in real amateur boxing
- What do club coaches do in the 7 days before a club show: train through, taper, or rest? How many days of rest before a bout?
- Do they study film of the opponent at club level? How?

End with a list: "Numbers the game should change", most important first, each with the real number and its source.
