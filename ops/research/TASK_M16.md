## Why this exists — and it is a spending check, not a style question

A research desk (J17) has just rewritten our animation purchase list. It says:
**buy punch TYPE, not target.** The proposed boxing set is `idle`, `jab`,
`cross`, `hook_lead`, `hook_rear`, `uppercut`, plus reactions and knockdowns.
Six of the ten slots are now punch types.

**Your M14 camera is what decides whether that is money well spent.** You fixed
the live camera at fov 50, pitch −20 to −22°, eye 3.8–4.2 m off the floor,
stand-off **9–11 m** outside the apron, shooting over the near ropes — and fixed
it *while live*, deliberately. At that distance and that angle, **a fighter is a
small figure seen from above and to the side.**

So: if a viewer cannot tell a jab from a cross from a hook at your camera, then
buying six punch types instead of two is waste, and the money belongs on
locomotion or knockdowns instead. **Nobody has looked. You are the one who can.**

## The question

1. **The read test.** At M14's HOME camera, board each of the six punch types
   at the moment of impact and at mid-throw. **Which of them are actually
   distinguishable from each other, and which collapse into "he threw a punch"?**
   Be blunt. If jab and cross are indistinguishable at 10 m, say so — that
   finding saves real money.
2. **Rank the six by read.** Order them from "unmistakable at HOME" to
   "invisible at HOME". Our purchase is ordered by your ranking, so the ranking
   is the deliverable.
3. **What makes a punch legible at distance?** Silhouette against the
   background, the shoulder line, the arc versus the straight, the body rotation,
   the follow-through, the opponent's reaction. Which cue survives 10 m and a
   −20° pitch, and which needs a closer camera? **The reaction may carry more
   read than the punch** — if the target's body sells what hit him better than
   the thrower's arm does, that reverses our spending priority toward reactions.
4. **Would a small camera change rescue the read** without breaking M14's
   fixed-camera ruling? You allowed exactly two exits — CLAIM-TIGHT and the
   knockdown. **Do not add a third casually**; if a punch needs a closer camera
   to read, the honest answer may be "then do not buy that punch".
5. **The lens on the same question for MMA.** Kicks and knees have far bigger
   silhouettes than punches. Does the read ranking change, and does that argue
   for buying kick footage for MMA even though it is refused for boxing?
6. **The verdict we can spend against.** Name the number of punch types worth
   buying at YOUR camera. If it is six, say six. If it is three, say three and
   name which three. If it is two, say two — that is a finding, not a failure,
   and it frees four slots.
