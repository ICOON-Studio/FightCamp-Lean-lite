2. **HOW LONG A CLINCH ACTUALLY LASTS.** Any study, fight-metrics dataset or

   median and spread, not a single mean. Name the sample size and the promotion.

3. **HOW IT ENDS.** Of clinches that end, what share end by referee break, by one

4. **THE WORK RATE INSIDE IT.** Strikes landed per minute inside a clinch against
