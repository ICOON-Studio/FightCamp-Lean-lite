# COACHFIT1_V9 — independent design critique (Arena)

SCORE depth=5 general=4

I judged the mechanic against this game's own rules, not against a generic management sim. I read the whole brief, its critic pack and the design map at the end. I read only game code under `scripts/` to check claims, plus `ops/research/CRITIC_ACCESS.md`, which the brief tells critics to open. I did not open any other critic's answer and did not run Godot.

## DEPTH — 5/10

There is a real game here, and it is the right game for this club. Coach time is scarce, two fighters in camp want the same pad nights, and the week's assignment forces a choice between weapons now and a usable plan on Saturday. The worked bout week (§6.2) is the best part of the map: giving up a little pad speed to keep the peak camp and the corner intact is exactly the sort of decision this game is about, and the reason given — you do not need a new move on Wednesday, you need energy on Friday — is sound. Bond and morale already have teeth in the game (a Low boxer stops listening), so attaching fight-week value to them fits.

What stops it going higher is that most of the apparent depth is four names on one number. Fit is already in the code as a gain multiplier, tiredness multiplier and a stack cap; "LEARN, LAST, TRUST PLAN, CORNER LISTENS" are four places where the same input is displayed, not four independent choices. The player never chooses a fit value; the player chooses an area assignment, and several of the options around that choice are free or dominant:

- Shared sparring gives both boxers the full 180 drill units and costs 2.0 instead of 3.0 time (§4.2). When two fighters are in camp this is strictly better than two separate sparring nights. There is no decision to make.
- SHIELD has no cost and no downside (§5.1: "None", cost 0). LIFT costs 0.5 time. A free insurance mode that never hurts cannot lose to a priced repair mode in a week without a loss; in a week with a loss it is plainly better.
- "Rest next week to reset" (§4.3) restores time with no stated price. In the code a rest week already adds morale; if there is no loss of sessions, fatigue or goal progress, the reset is free.
- The head coach has no temper and no mode toggle, and starts at Working. The head coach is therefore a fixed number, not a choice, yet option D and C + LIFT are scored as if choosing a head-coached corner were a strategic act.

The four channels also change nothing measurable in the examples. The pass line for the bout effect is +0.06 to +0.10 wins per 100 bouts (§11.3) — the same invisible +0.08 per 100 that the map itself says made v8 a failure. §1 promises this will be "visible in every round exchange", but a tenth of a win in a hundred bouts cannot be seen in an exchange. Either the effect has to be made bigger in the fight or the display has to show a counterfactual, not a percentage.

Depth also fails "it interacts with the rest of the game" in the strict sense: the four channels are not safely joined to the existing rules. I checked this in the code (files listed below). An unqualified change to any one of them double-counts or cancels a rule that is already live. Until that is resolved, the choices change numbers on a board, not outcomes a player can feel.

## GENERAL DESIGN — 4/10

The map is detailed, uses the game's words, keeps names out of the body, sets pass lines and writes build files with RED tests. The wiring table reaches every menu and says when each row appears. That is a good skeleton.

It is not buildable as written, because the load-bearing numbers disagree with each other and with the code:

1. **Bond is in the formula and out of it.** §1 prints `Fit = 50 + temper/mode + coach time + bond + taught out + focus`, then says "Bond no longer modifies fit." §3.3 then puts fit back into the bond channel as a `fit_bonus (mode only)`. The claim of "two levers, two effects — no hidden interaction" is false on the page itself.
2. **The fatigue formula is arithmetically wrong.** §3.2 and §11.2 give `FatigueDrift = 1.05 − (fit ÷ 100)`, clamped 0.92–1.08. At fit 75 that is 0.30, not the 0.92 in the next column; at fit 50 it is 0.55, not 1.00. The tier table, the formula and the probe pass line (fit 80 keeps 8–12 more energy) cannot all be built.
3. **Plan retention has two different bases.** §3.3 says `70 + bond_bonus`, so Stranger is 70%; §3.3's own table and §11.1 say Stranger is 55%, Working 65%, Trusted 75%, Corner 85% (a 55 base). §1 shows "Plan pressure retained: 92% (Trusted)" where both tables say 75%. §5.1 gives POLISH +15% retention where §3.3 gives +5% mode bonus, and says "Usable+ moves" where §3.3 says "mostly Sharp cards".
4. **Trust arithmetic does not resolve.** §3.4 defines correction absorption as `0.75 + (corner bond step × 0.25)`. With the bond steps in §9 that gives 0.99 / 1.00 / 1.01 / 1.03, not the stated "Stranger 75%, Trusted 90%". §9's worked line `0.85 × 1.00 × 0.90 = 0.765` calls 0.90 the coachability step, but its own formula gives 1.00 at coachability 50.
5. **Coachability is counted twice.** `Exchanges.uptake` in the code is 0.7–1.0 and is already multiplied by morale trust in `BoutRead._game_edge_of`; §9 replaces that with a 0.90–1.10 step without saying the old factor is removed. Stacked, a Flat, low-coachability boxer falls to about 0.44 of a call, below the stated 0.60 floor, while §9 also says Low boxers stop listening altogether (a floor of zero).
6. **The named code levers do not match the code.** §11's corrections log correctly says pressure is a 0..1 score with `GAIN`, `EX_KEEP`, `ROUND_KEEP`, but §3.3 still says a *line* "loses 45% pressure per use" and §11.1 still lists per-tier retention. `PlanPressure.gd` holds one bout-wide pressure scalar, not a per-line store. The log also says `EX_KEEP` controls first-use preservation; in `PlanRead.gd` first-use protection is `FREE_USES`, and `EX_KEEP` is the per-exchange decay.
7. **Focus names break a live key.** §0/§6.1 say the FOCUS pill uses attribute names. `BoutRules.gd` uses `CAMP_FOCUSES = [conditioning, power, technique, defense, speed]` as the actual key for the gain multiplier. "Power" and "technique" are not attributes in `Attrs.gd`; a rename without a mapping removes the camp effect.
8. **The money line is off by a factor of three or four.** A raise is +$170 a week (§10.2) = $8,840 a year. One member at $35 a month is $420 a year, so one raise costs about 21 members' annual dues — not "3–4 raises/year = 19–25 members' dues" (§0).
9. **An extra plan line can reduce current capacity.** `PlanBoard.arrow_limit` allows 4–9 arrows by coach skill. §3.3 caps the Trusted bonus at 7 lines. If that is a hard cap, a good coach loses two lines when a bond tier is reached.
10. **The document is unfinished.** The build table stops in the middle of C8; sections 13–16 were never written; three CHECKs remain open (both assistants, Level 2 timing and fee, and the lost class income). A design whose owner rule is "price every option" cannot price the promotion path while the price and the class loss are CHECKs.

One smaller but consistent breach of the owner's "only actionable information" rule: rows marked "None — informational only" or "Hover for detail only" (M7, M8, M14, M16, M20, M21) put fit numbers on screens where the player cannot act on them. The "APPROACHING LIMIT: pads 69/74, about 5 weeks remaining" tag is the clearest case — a red warning with no button.

### Code checked (what each file told me)

- `scripts/rules/CoachFit.gd` — the live v8 fit: `gain_mult = 0.5 + fit/100`, `tired_mult = 1.125 − 0.0025×fit`, stack cap 1.6, coach time pools 10/14/4/2/8, over-budget penalties, bond thresholds (Working at 8, Trusted at 45).
- `scripts/rules/PlanPressure.gd` — bout-wide pressure: `GAIN 0.3`, `EX_KEEP 0.85`, `ROUND_KEEP 0.6`, `CUT 0.4`; there is no per-line retention value.
- `scripts/rules/PlanRead.gd` — `FREE_USES = 2`, `ADJUST = 0.7`, reads reset at the bell.
- `scripts/rules/FireLevel.gd` — planned lines fire 0.5 / 0.75 / 1.0 at Usable / Sharp / Signature; `StepMastery.fire_mastery` supplies step bars.
- `scripts/rules/CornerTrust.gd`, `scripts/rules/FighterProfile.gd`, `scripts/rules/Exchanges.gd`, `scripts/rules/BoutRead.gd` — a wrong call followed costs 2 morale, a right one gives 1; Low is morale below 30 and stops calls landing; morale trust is 1.05/1.0/0.85/0.7; coachability `uptake` is 0.7–1.0 and multiplies both good and bad call edges.
- `scripts/rules/CoachedMornings.gd`, `scripts/rules/CoachAreas.gd`, `scripts/rules/StepMastery.gd`, `scripts/rules/PadWeek.gd`, `scripts/rules/MoveMastery.gd` (via StepMastery) — coached mornings already multiply gains by 1.25 solo / 1.15 shared; coach area ratings already set training lift; pad nights are 240 units and sparring nights 180.
- `scripts/rules/Attrs.gd`, `scripts/rules/BoutRules.gd`, `scripts/rules/PlanBoard.gd`, `scripts/rules/StaffRoles.gd`, `scripts/rules/HeadCoach.gd`, `scripts/rules/DuesLevers.gd` — attribute names, camp focus keys, arrow limits, staff wages, head-coach edges and the $35 monthly due.

These files show the largest design risk: the map never says whether fit *replaces* or *stacks with* the existing multipliers. At neutral fit 50 the code's `gain_mult` is 1.0 while a coached morning is 1.25; if fit replaces the coached-morning multiplier, hiring a coach stops doing anything in an average week.

## PLAY — a note, not a third score

Week to week the anticipation is there: you can see a weapon getting closer to Usable, you can see the fight date circled, and you can regret the corner choice on Saturday. The comeback hook is weak at v9's stated effect sizes. A percent change in energy drift or a 5% change in word taken in is not a reward a player feels; a Monday row that says "−2% drift" is bookkeeping. The felt reward has to be "one more round before you faded", "six punches taken off the opponent", "the line you wrote still worked in round three".

## Missing options — and what each one changes

1. **Choose which fighter owns the FOCUS morning when two are in camp.** The FOCUS pill is currently just the camp focus, not a pick. Adding "focus this boxer / focus the other / split" changes who gets the +1.2–1.8 weekly gain and makes the shared-pad row a real decision instead of a display.
2. **Cover when the assigned coach is unavailable.** M19 says the twist card offers a cover, but no cover options or prices are given. Add "use the other coach (cost to their boxer)", "head coach (0.9 factor)", "no session (fatigue +, gain −)". This changes the week's plan and stops an unavailable coach being a free event.
3. **Pay to buy coach time.** Money is information-only in M16. Add "buy an evening coach for this week ($X)" beside OVER BUDGET. This turns cash into an alternative to Rest and to dropping a pad night.
4. **A priced Rest week.** Define what Rest loses (sessions, mastery, camp progress) and what it gains (time reset, less fatigue, morale). Without this, Rest is a free button that breaks the budget rule.
5. **Raise answers with consequences.** "Raise due" is a flag; the options are not. Add "Pay / Refuse / Pay after the next bout" with effects on weekly cost, bond, morale and the risk of leaving. This makes the raise a choice rather than a bill.
6. **Level 2 timing and the week away.** Add "Send now / Wait until next camp" with the 52-week gate, the course fee and who covers the classes while away. This changes both the staff cost and the sparring availability.
7. **A deliberate bond-building action.** Bond now grows only as a by-product of coached sessions and post-bout bonuses. Add "one extra one-to-one session (0.5 time)" or "film together (no time, no mastery)" with a visible bond gain. This lets the player buy trust instead of hoping it accrues.
8. **A "no mode" baseline.** Without it there is no proof that a mode is worth using. Adding it changes the mode probe from a ranking among four always-on choices into a real question.
9. **Resolve the second-assistant path as an option.** "v9 works either way" leaves the Release 1 choice set undecided. Add an explicit hire-one / hire-both choice at signing, with the mode availability and total time shown on both cards.
10. **A decision attached to APPROACHING LIMIT.** Add "change the week / switch coach / accept the slow gain" on the tag. This turns a useless red warning into the ceiling trade-off it claims to describe.
11. **A pre-fight morale repair on the week board.** word taken in collapses at Low, but the week offers no priced "talk / rest / keep drilling" action before the bout. Adding one changes the corner pick from a label into a preparation choice.

## Useless options — and what removing or wiring them changes

1. **SHIELD as a mode.** Free, no downside, so it is chosen whenever it exists. Remove its cost-free status (e.g. it takes 0.5 time, or it lowers LEARN that week), or delete it. Change: the Motivator now has a real SHIELD/LIFT choice.
2. **Steady Hand shown as a mode.** It is automatic and has no toggle. Show it as a fixed rule on the coach card, not as a mode option. Change: the mode comparison stops containing an unselectable row.
3. **Film on the coach-fit board.** It has no fit, no cost and no new effect. Remove it from the fit grid, or make the choice "which opponent's tape this coach studies" with a cost in time. Change: the row stops being decoration.
4. **"Informational only" fit rows (M7, M8, M14, M16, M20, M21).** Either put an action on each number or hide it behind the relevant actionable row. Change: no red or grey number sits where the player cannot respond.
5. **The 92% / 75% / 90% display numbers.** Until one formula produces them, they are noise. Replace them with a single value computed from the chosen lever. Change: the player can compare two corner picks honestly.
6. **Shared sparring as written.** It is strictly better than two separate spar nights. Give the 40% side a fatigue or output cost, or make the pair cost 2.5. Change: the player has to weigh both boxers' energy, not just click the cheaper option.
7. **"Uncoached morning = fit 50 exactly."** With a head coach always present, "uncoached" and "head-coached" are the same cell in the code. Define whether a head-coached morning uses 0.9 of the assistant factor, 0.60 alone, or fit 50 with no factor. Change: the morning column gains a real choice between the head coach and an assistant.
8. **The extra plan line capped at 7.** It can lower a skilled coach's current 9-line limit. Make it "limit +1, cap 10" or state the new cap explicitly. Change: Trusted becomes a reward, not a penalty.

## The 10/10 board, described as a 1920×1080 page

Club > People, warm whiteboard, one screen, no pop-ups. Top strip: the week and the fight countdown, one flagged need ("OVER BUDGET: 11.5 of 10 — Rest, buy time, or drop a pad night"), cash and running weekly net. Left 400px: three coach cards with portrait, role, temper, mode with an explicit "No mode" choice, time pips used/max, one school, area ratings and the next raise with both answer buttons. Centre 1040px: one row per boxer with a FOCUS pill, the five area cells, each cell showing assigned coach, fit word, the mastery date and the energy drift from one shared formula; shared nights show a 60/40 split with both boxers' costs; a fight-within-14-days strip shows the three named corner choices with one word-taken-in value each. Right 400px: Monday review rows that land last — milestone reached, plan pressure kept, energy left at the bell, bond step, raise answer — each with a button that opens the thing it changed. Every number has a hover line naming the file-level rule and the counterfactual ("with a neutral coach: 6.9 weeks, 19 energy"). No names outside the header, no fixed pronouns, no colour that means anything other than risk.

## To reach 10/10

1. **Write one numeric spec sheet before any UI.** One table: fit formula (bond removed), tier cut-offs, `gain_mult`, drift, retention, word taken in, correction absorption, coachability, bond thresholds and growth, time costs, caps and rounding. Each printed example must be recomputed from that table, including fit 50 = neutral, fit 75 drift, the 55/65/75/85 bond ladder, and the `0.85 × 1.00 × 1.00` trust example. Add a RED test that asserts every example in that table. Nothing else on this list can be built until this exists.
2. **Join each channel to exactly one existing lever, and delete the double count.** In `scripts/rules/CoachFit.gd` keep fit as the gain and drift input; state in code comments whether it replaces or multiplies `CoachedMornings.mult` and `CoachAreas.lift_for`. In `scripts/rules/PlanPressure.gd` define retention as a named change to `GAIN` and `EX_KEEP` (not a new per-line percent); in `scripts/rules/PlanRead.gd` define the extra full-use as `FREE_USES 2 → 3`; in `scripts/rules/Exchanges.gd` and `scripts/rules/BoutRead.gd` make word taken in *replace* `uptake × trust` or *multiply* it, never both, and apply it to the negative call edge as today. Keep Low as a hard zero or delete that rule from the brief — one of the two.
3. **Fix the fatigue formula and reprove it.** Use a drift that is 1.00 at fit 50 and about 0.92 at fit 75 (the code's `1.125 − 0.0025×fit` with the 0.92–1.08 clamp is a candidate). Rerun the two identical four-round bouts: fit 80 must finish with 8–12 more energy and fit 35 must be 15–20 down by round three. If it cannot hit both, change the constant, not the pass line.
4. **Make four choices, not one, and price every alternative.** Weekly area assignment, shared-night split, corner pick and mode each need at least two priced options with different best cases. Remove SHIELD's zero cost or the "no mode" baseline will expose it; give Rest a cost; price the cover coach; add the raise answers and the Level 2 timing. Then run MODEPROBE1 and BOTSUITE: each option must be the best pick in 30–70% of distinct weeks, and the best hand-built board must beat the auto board by 2–5 wins per 100 with no single setup above 60%.
5. **Make the fight effect felt or stop showing it.** Put the counterfactual in the Why: same bout at neutral fit, showing energy left at the bell, punches cut from the opponent and plan lines still working in round three. If TOP FIT cannot beat average by more than about 4–8 wins per 100 in a real bout once the channels are joined, raise `PlanPressure.CUT`/`GAIN` or the drift until it can; do not label a +0.08/100 effect "visible every exchange".
6. **Close the three open CHECKs and correct the money display.** Decide whether both assistants can be hired and show both options at signing; confirm the 52-week gate and the $600 fee against the real rule (or make both plain game values); show the exact class and member loss on promotion. Recompute the raise line: one $170/week raise is about 21 members' annual dues, so three to four raises are about 63–84, not 19–25.
7. **Repair the words and the caps against the code.** Map each legacy camp focus to its `Attrs` attributes while keeping `BoutRules.CAMP_FOCUSES` as the internal key; make the Trusted extra line `limit + 1` with a cap of 10; correct the 60/40 unit example (the 60% side loses 96 of 240 units, not the 40% side); make POLISH's retention one number everywhere; state the new head-coach factor instead of leaving "0.9 of the best assistant, or 0.60 alone" as a story about a coach who is always present.
8. **Finish the build list and wire every row to an action.** Write C9 onward for the M1 board, mode rules, raise answers, bond, retention and corner trust, each file under 500 lines with a RED test run before the build. Delete or wire the "informational only" rows so no fit number sits where the player cannot press something. Write the held-back list, the open questions and the self-score that were never written.
9. **Rejudge against the owner's bar with real numbers.** After items 1–8, run the probes again and score honestly: 7+ on depth and general design only if each listed option has a measured best case, every printed number comes from one formula, and a player can feel the result in a Monday review and in a round without being told a percentage.
