2. USABILITY & CLARITY — Score: 7/10
Why:
✅ Drag-and-drop is intuitive; 5 seconds is enough to understand "drag session → onto day → see what changes".
✅ Each session icon + colour-coded stat ring makes what it trains instantly readable.
✅ WIN dial + green tags show impact directly; tiredness curve with preview drag is excellent feedback.
✅ Matches Camp Plan visual style — consistent dark theme, handwritten coach notes, clear typography.
⚠️ Hard/normal/light labels (N/H/L) are small — easy to miss that intensity changes gains and fatigue.
⚠️ Evening cards: what the move does in a fight needs a hover; "Check hook 12→21" is mastery but not instantly meaningful.
⚠️ Some numbers lack unit/label clarity without hovering — still too much text vs visual cues.
3. DECISION DEPTH — Score: 8/10
Why:
✅ Real trade-offs everywhere: more sparring = better mastery + fight edge BUT higher fatigue + injury risk + worse recovery. Every meaningful choice costs something.
✅ Coach's plan is a baseline, not the answer — player can improve or deliberately choose safer/harder paths.
✅ Opponent scouting ties training to fight plan — e.g. drilling the check hook because film found the opponent drops his right. Not just "max all stats".
✅ Phase constraints (Peak → Taper) force different thinking week-to-week — no single "best template".
⚠️ Missing: why one stat matters vs another is not always clear — sometimes the safe default wins because impact is opaque. Still, depth is strong.
