Real corner first. One minute. Cutman works the face; chief second talks, water, stool, towel call. Cutman does not coach.

## 1. Playbook (a-f)
- (a) Leg that ate kicks. See limp, dropped stance, delayed check, foot-drop after calf/peroneal hits. Corner: ice pack on
  thigh/calf, elevate on stool, "check or switch stance," sometimes "sit him down / wrestle." Ice buys numbness + less
  swelling for one round, not structure. No adrenaline on a limb. Massage is post-fight, not 60 s. (ESPN)
- (b) Cut over the eye. Blood into the eye stops fights. Cold towel, pressure, epinephrine 1:1000 on swab, Vaseline plug.
  Avitene/thrombin where legal. Buys one more round if the vessel clamps. Do not wipe; press.
- (c) Swelling / closing eye. Enswell (iced steel) immediately, hold, do not smear sideways. Buys vision for the next
  round; closing lid is a doctor-stop risk. Tyson-Douglas: no enswell, eye gone.
- (d) Hurt body. Folded posture, short breaths, dropped hands. Ice on ribs, sit upright, breathe, "move, don't trade."
  Body work saps output; not closed in 60 s.
- (e) Hurt hand/arm. Dropped punch power, guarding the glove. Almost nothing legal in 60 s except ice and "jab / don't
  load the right." A broken hand is a next-week injury, not a corner fix.
- (f) Wobbled. Delayed feet, vacant eyes, missed instructions. Water, sit, simple plan ("hold, clinch, survive"). Towel when
  he cannot defend. US boxing: towel often ignored; chief second tells the inspector. MMA: corner stoppage legal; UFC
  practice is verbal to ref/inspector.
- Broadcasts (UFC, DAZN, Matchroom, ONE): live strike tallies by head/body/leg, replay of the damaging shot, close-ups of
  eye/leg. NO persistent body-map HUD. Commentary names the compromised limb. Replay + limp reads faster than a diagram.

## 3. Live display - RECOMMENDATION
On-fighter cues first (limp, held calf, blood, swollen lid) + the existing letter chips (L-LEG / EYE / BODY: fine -> hurt ->
critical -> doctor). "Body outline at fight speed is a map you stare at instead of the 3D bout. Chips-only is a health bar.
Cues sell pain; chips tell the corner which 60 s job to pick." Replay insert of the damaging kick = what UFC does.
Full body-outline HUD: SKIP (health-bar feel, art cost, fights the 3D view).

## 4. Thresholds
- Compromised leg = cannot check, plant or circle; visible after a handful of clean calf/thigh kicks (Poirier-McGregor;
  Barboza-Oliveira 23 leg strikes then collapse). SOURCED espn.com / mmafighting.com.
- UFC outcomes: KO/TKO 32.6-33.5%, sub ~19%, decision ~47% (GrapplerHQ 8,591 bouts). Kick as KO method ~7.3% of KO/TKOs.
  PROXY: leg-kick TKO = low single digits of all bouts; a visible limp is far more common than a finish.
- Significant strikes: head 64%, body 20%, legs 16%.
- Round-break recovery PROXY: 60 s = bleed control + 10-20% sting off, not a reset; legs persist.

## 5. Engagement
Interesting (1-2 per fight that has damage, not every round): ice the calf OR enswell the eye (one pair of hands);
"switch stance / wrestle" vs "stay and check"; hunt their red limb vs protect yours; stop it now vs one more round;
cutman time vs coach time. Busywork: grease every round; "breathe"; ice with no region; eight same-effect shouts.
Cadence: zero choices in a clean fight; one hard choice when a region hits hurt; a second if two regions or doctor lights.

full body-outline HUD (skip).
