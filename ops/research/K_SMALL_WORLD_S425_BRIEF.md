# Kalli - a small living world for FightCamp (S425)

The owner approved, after the week design is built: "a small living world with a belt ladder". Small means ONE national
ladder with a mandatory challenger and an eliminator, rivals who age and move, and judges with taste. Not four world bodies.
Read ops/research/CRITIC_PACK.md (what the game has: rankings, championships, the pro ladder, rivals, WorldAge) and
ops/research/BOXING_MANAGER_COMPARE_S425.md (a competitor with a huge world, and the ideas worth taking from it).
Design it as decisions for the player, not as a database:
1. What does the world give the player to WAIT for and to DECIDE each month (a mandatory named, an eliminator offered, a
   rival moving up, a champion vacating, a judge panel announced)? For each: the choice, the case where each option is
   right, and what it changes.
2. The ladder's rungs for our game (how few can it be and still feel like a climb?), what each rung opens (a longer
   fight, a purse step, a new kind of opponent, a sponsor), and how a boxer falls.
3. Rivals: how many named ones, what they do while the player is not looking, and how the player hears of it.
4. Judges with taste: how the player learns a panel's lean, and what he can do about it in the plan and the corner.
5. The smallest version worth building first, and the order after it.
6. Your own kill list: the hardest objection to each part and your answer.
Write the whole page as plain markdown TEXT in your reply, nothing as an attachment; in parts if it is long.
