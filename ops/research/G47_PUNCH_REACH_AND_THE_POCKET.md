## THE WARNING THAT REFRAMES OUR WHOLE COMPARISON

> **Lab punch studies measure fist travel from guard and foot-to-bag, not
> sternum-to-knuckle. Those two are not interchangeable.**
>
> **Do not treat 0.75 m as "jab reach from COM."** Guard already parks the fist
> in front of the face; Cheraghi's 0.66 m is *extra travel from that guard*,
> plus a 0.28 m hip step.

Reference body: 180 cm wingspan ≈ 180 cm stature. Pheasant / RoyMech British-male
tables (50th percentile: stature 174.5 cm, span 174.5 cm, upper limb 79 cm,
shoulder-grip 74.5 cm, chest depth 24.5 cm, biacromial 40.5 cm). Scaled to
180 cm, shoulder-grip ≈ **0.77 m** — so a 0.75 m shoulder-to-knuckle figure is
consistent with anthropometry, and is **not** a punch-mocap number.

## 2. The pocket — metre band

**NOT PUBLISHED as a measured centre-to-centre band. Coaching only.**

| Band | Coaching definition | Metre figure in the literature |
|---|---|---|
| Long / end of jab | straights only; "outside" | Sanda discussion: typical non-attacking stand-off ~1.0–1.5 m. Not fight mocap |
| Pocket / mid | hooks, uppercuts, combinations land; "inside the opponent's punching range" | **no metres**. One coach heuristic: mid-range ≈ own upper-arm length from the opponent (~0.35 m *surface* gap, not C2C) |
| Clinch starts | chest-to-chest or collar-tie possible; punches smothered | **no metres**. Begins when arms can wrap; chests need not be flush |

> The pocket is a **technique band, not a published interval.** A usable holding
> range *against anatomy, not a citation*: long ~1.1–1.5 m C2C; pocket
> ~0.6–1.1 m; clinch ≲ 0.5–0.6 m. **Those edges are inferred, not measured.**

## 3. Stance cost — do two men at 0.89 m overlap?

**No.** Chest depth, 50th-percentile male **0.245 m** (5th 0.21 / 95th 0.28).

- If 0.89 m is COM–COM, the chest-surface gap ≈ 0.89 − 0.25 ≈ **0.64 m**. Plenty
  of air.
- If 0.89 m is chest-to-chest, the COMs sit ~1.1 m apart. Still no overlap.

A bladed stance puts each lead shoulder and lead fist in front of that man's COM.
Coaching: lead fist ~15 cm in front of the face; AP foot base about shoulder
width (~0.4–0.5 m). **How many centimetres of C2C that "eats" is NOT PUBLISHED.**

Cheraghi's rear-cross geometry is the closest check: front foot 0.50 m from a
thin pad, COM behind that foot by a fraction of stance. **A scored rear straight
at ~0.8–1.0 m C2C against a real torso is compatible with that study. 0.89 m
does not require interpenetration.**

## 4. Reach disparity versus landing C2C — the load-bearing question

**NOT PUBLISHED as C2C-at-impact by reach pair.** What exists:

- Coaching is unanimous: the short man closes to kill the long man's straight;
  the long man holds long range and scores the jab there. **Landing distance is
  therefore not one number.**
- UFC fight-ending punches: reach difference changes *which punch type finishes*
  (more straights/hooks when the winner has +20 cm reach), not that both men land
  at the same C2C.
- Kimm & Thiel (via Stanley's thesis): stature correlates with straight-punch
  velocity because the hand has a longer acceleration path — limb length, not
  measured C2C at contact.

> A 200 cm-reach man **can** score a straight at a greater C2C than a 170 cm-reach
> man. The short man does not always equalise distance; he equalises *some*
> exchanges by stepping in, and those land shorter. Frequency and punch-type mix
> change. **A single 1.10 m gate for every fighter is a same-size approximation,
> not a measured law.**

## 5. Height 165 vs 195, vertical only

No fight mocap of knuckle-versus-chin for mixed heights. Scaled from Pheasant
ratios (shoulder height ≈ 0.83 × stature; eye ≈ 0.94 × stature; chin ≈ eye − ~0.12 m):

| | 165 cm | 195 cm | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shoulder height | ~1.37 m | ~1.61 m | 0.24 m |
| Chin height (standing, upright) | ~1.43 m | ~1.70 m | 0.27 m |

If the tall man throws a level punch at his own shoulder/chin line, his knuckle
sits **~0.18–0.27 m above the short man's chin**. In a real exchange he aims
down; that offset is a posture choice, not a published impact height. The
head-height mismatch is real: the tall jab at long range is often a downward
stick, and the short man is punching up into a high guard.

## VERDICT

**(a)** 0.89 m C2C is a **plausible** scored-punch distance for two ~180 cm men,
especially a stepped cross or an inside shot. It is **short for a fully extended
jab at the end of range**, and **it does not make two adult torsos overlap**.

**(b)** A single flat 1.10 m gate is **defensible as a same-size
simplification**. It is **a defect across a 30 cm reach gap**: the long man's
straight can be live outside the short man's gate, and the short man's scores
happen after he closes.
