# C163 digest

Created the blind `CapsulePresence` contract test. It skips green while the
named module is absent, and once the module lands it checks pure ratio math,
clamping and invalid authored extents; all five classification outcomes; the
three D11 arms; exact inclusivity on both sides of exposure 90.0 and present
50.0/95.0; `passes` agreement over a boundary grid; and the exact pair format.

The strongest argument against this contract is that five bands may encode more
product semantics than the next grappling decision can use. A caller may only
need a boolean pass/fail plus the raw pair, making `PARTIAL` versus `ABSENT`
and `OBSCURED` versus `DELETED` reporting noise that invites UI branching without
changing the move. The thresholds are also calibrated from one neck-like
segment, while D11 explicitly warns that the shoulder girdle may not preserve
the same 95% present window. If those distinctions do not drive different
measurements or repairs, this should collapse to a two-axis diagnostic and a
single pass gate rather than hardening five labels as universal truth.
