## Decision (lead developer)
- **PROPS: buy Unity "Boxing Gym" (80286), EUR 18.39** - it covers the stations the training floor greyboxes (ring,
  heavy bag, speed bag, dummy, lockers, stools, benches, weights) in one style, FBX inside; the Fab Studio-Lab twin
  (EUR 26.32) is the fallback. **Tripo Pro fills the gaps** no store sells (double-end bag, tatami/grappling mats,
  first-aid cabinet, cold tub, reaction grid, capoeira/arnis racks) - the operator's 2026-09-26 ruling. Buying is the
  operator's hands (`blocked_on_operator: buy-gym-prop-pack`, repointed). Unity EULA allows use in Godot (G114 s3).
- **STAFF: from our own generator (S93 MPFB, CC0)** plus CC0 MPFB clothes - the same bodies and faces as the fighters,
  so a coach and his fighter read as one world. Buying single photoreal coaches (EUR 35 each) would give three styles in
  one room. Revisit only if the generator pilot fails.
