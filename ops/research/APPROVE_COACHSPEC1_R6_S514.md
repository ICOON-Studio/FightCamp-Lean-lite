What it does, in plain words:
- You are the head coach; hired coaches each do one job: boxing (pads and classes), strength and conditioning, BJJ.
- The boxing coach's evenings are a trade: each class night dropped = +60 minutes on the pads but -$175 a week.
- A Week Priority switch (Stats / Moves / Freshness): the coach's Monday plan follows it, and moving a session shows
  what you gave up ("gave up 0.3 stamina -> +8 on the uppercut").
- A second boxing coach adds a roster place, coached mornings and fitness classes; costs about $130 a week net.
- BJJ belts: a coach can only promote below their own belt; an "Academy Month" ($450, 4 weeks away) gets a
  promotion signed by an outside professor.
- Every hire card shows how many weeks of money you have left before and after.
