# Progression plan - the short version for your OK (S448)

You asked: something to be eager to unlock each week that makes the fighter better, across the whole career, with moves, combos,
movement, the body, coaches and staff tied together. Full map: `ops/research/PROGRESSION_MAP_S448.md`.

## What the player gets
1. **A move tree.** 16 moves the fight already uses (6 punches, 7 footwork moves including the feint, 3 defences), in the order a real
   coach teaches them. Each move has a filling bar: Usable at 30 (it is used in the fight), Sharp at 60, Signature at 85. Each line
   crossed is the "XP threshold" moment, shown on Monday, and opens the next moves and the combos it completes.
2. **Pick what to learn.** One move in learning at a time (two with a good pads coach). When it is learned, the coach shows three next
   moves with reasons (what the next opponent does, the fighter's body, the style being built). You pick one. First pick in week 1.
3. **Footwork is learned too.** Step back, pivot, cutting off the ring and the rest fill a bar like punches; a step not learned yet
   is not done in the fight.
4. **The body decides how well.** Each move is graded by two of the fighter's attributes (a pivot by footwork and composure, an
   uppercut by explosiveness and inside fighting). Height and reach count. In the 3D, faster and taller fighters take longer, quicker steps.
5. **Who the fighter is: what you keep sharp.** Sharp and Signature moves need a bit of pad time each week or they fade. The moves in
   the fight plan keep themselves sharp in sparring; fight weeks freeze fading. Three kept-sharp moves of one style name the style
   (Out-boxer, Pressure fighter, Counter-puncher, Boxer-puncher, Volume puncher, Slugger) and each style changes how the fighter fights.
6. **Coaches set the speed.** A better pads coach fills bars faster, opens the second learning slot and makes Signatures possible.
   Five coaches to hire (fast moves now, fast bodies now, or a young coach who grows). Coaches improve with the sessions they run.

## How amateur and pro differ
- **Amateur:** learn the basics, pass the skills awards (Standard to Gold, now testing the tree's moves), name a style, the nationals,
  the pro offer.
- **Pro:** every morning free for training; the other corner now studies the fighter's favourite line and waits for it. A second
  Signature or a sharp feint covers it; "read-proof" is the pro season's goal.
- **Veteran:** the body slows, but kept moves fade half as fast, and a veteran with a Signature teaches it faster to a club mate.

## Before anything is built
Seven tests through the game's real fights check each piece pulls its weight (no pick always right, no style always best, the pro
read fair, coaches all worth hiring). Anything that fails is redesigned before it is drawn.

## One thing this changes that you approved before
The club's pad-night board (who gets the coach's 8 pad nights) becomes the top row of the Week EVERY week when two or more fighters train,
not only when two are in camp. With moves to learn and moves to keep sharp, the nights are shared every week, so the trade has to be seen.
