# STORY1_KALLI.md
# FightCamp campaign pitch: THE HOUSE THAT HOLDS
**Writer:** Kalli  
**Structure used:** three-act career ladder (amateur → regional → national → world), rival heat arc, mentor-as-pressure (not mentor death), calendar-as-spine. No plot or characters taken from any named reference.

**Word target:** 2,200–3,200. Sections 1–8 and 10. Section 9 skipped (Dola only).

## 1. Logline + tone

You inherit a gym that still smells like someone else's work, and the sport will not wait for you to learn it. The campaign is about whether a house can hold: whether the people under your roof last long enough for the ladder to mean something. Tone is quiet and procedural, with the dry humour of people who count rounds and bills in the same voice; it must never become a sports-movie montage or a villain speech.

At the **10-hour mark** the player should feel competent and watched: they know the weekly loop, their first fighter has a name the city knows, and someone across town wants that name. At the **credits** they should feel the house is theirs: the gym still runs if the campaign flag turns off, the rival is a living number on a wall, and the next fighter walks into rooms that already remember the last one.

---

## 2. The spine

**Protagonist (player):** a new owner who was never a star. You were a corner second for twelve years under **Vince Calder**, who built Calder House Gym and walked away the night the lights stayed on and the bank account did not. You buy the lease with a thin loan and a promise you did not write down: keep the doors open. You are not the chosen coach. You are the person who still has a set of keys.

**First fighter: Mara "Wire" Solano.** Amateur welter, quiet, left hand that arrives late and honest. She walks in looking for a gym that will not sell her week to a promoter she does not trust. She becomes Chapter 1's body: if she lasts, the house lasts.

**Antagonistic force: the calendar wearing a human face.** The sport itself is the pressure (bouts on fixed dates, bills on fixed dates, ladders that do not care). The human face is **Rook Hale**, owner of Hale Yard, a rival gym that books the same weight, hunts the same regional belts, and treats heat as a business tool. Hale does not hate you. Hale needs your fighter to look small so his look big. Behind him sits **Northline Promotions**, which prefers gyms that hand over talent early and smile about it.

**Three acts on the ladder**

- **Act I — Amateur / early regional (Chapters 1–4).** Keep Solano fed, trained and booked. Learn the week. Survive the first bill crisis. Meet Hale when heat first ticks up.
- **Act II — Regional → national (Chapters 5–8).** Solano becomes a name. Heat with Hale's fighter **Jace Vellin** climbs past 50. Northline offers a bad contract that looks like help. A career injury or a towel decision tests the corner.
- **Act III — National → world gate (Chapters 9–12).** A title chase or a eliminator that the story frames; the sim still decides the result. Endings branch on money, heat, morale and whether the house still owns its fighters.

## 5. Choices that matter (5)

1. **Northline deal (Beat 6).** Sign: money now, purse cut, Orth stays in the inbox. Refuse: thinner cash, higher loyalty, harder early books. **If ignored:** Orth's offer expires; campaign continues on the "independent" branch; sandbox booking unchanged.
2. **Answer the callout (Beat 5).** Answer: heat spikes, rematch path opens. Ignore: heat cools slowly, Hale books someone else, story shifts to a different rival pair. **If ignored:** rivalry wall stays quiet; free play still has heat system.
3. **Shared spar (Beat 7).** Accept: better reads, injury risk, heat. Refuse: safer camp, Hale uses it in press. **If ignored:** no spar event; training week normal.
4. **Towel trust (Beat 8).** Trust corner: longer recovery, higher trust. Override: possible short-term booking, trust drop, Len Park may refuse hire. **If ignored:** default corner AI; story skips to Beat 9 with neutral trust.
5. **Lease buyout (Beat 11).** Keep: hard money, full control. Sell stake: cash injection, Hale gets a say in one booking per quarter. Walk: campaign ends early into free play with a "sold" flag. **If ignored:** timer ends; default Keep with a debt event.

Sandbox rule: every beat is a flag layer. Turning story off hides cards and keeps ladder, money, heat and fights fully playable.

## 6. Fight-night moments (4)

1. **Corner talk tied to a flag.** Between rounds, if heat ≥ 50 and opponent is Vellin, Len (or default coach) opens with one line about the last spar or the callout. Reads still come from the sim; the line is flavour on top.

3. **Grudge round.** If heat ≥ 70, round start can show a short "this is the one they sold" press sting; judge scoring and engine unchanged.
4. **Injury callback.** If Solano is fighting on a shortened injury recovery, physio Ivy's pre-fight note sits on the corner tray: one risk line. The engine already models the body; the story only names it.

Never: forced knockdowns, scripted stoppages, or auto-wins.
