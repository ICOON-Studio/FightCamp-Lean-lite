## THE SITUATION

We will turn short videos of real people into animation with a video-to-motion tool (Rokoko Vision,
DeepMotion or Move.ai on a paid commercial plan). Our game is commercial. Our fighters are missing:
several different **stance / guard idle** takes, **footwork** (step forward, step back, step left, step
right, diagonal steps), **pivots and turns**, **circling**, and the **return to guard after a jab or a
cross**. Fast punches are NOT needed - we have bought clips for those.

**THE HARD RULE: NO UFC, NO PROFESSIONAL LEAGUE OR BROADCAST FOOTAGE OF ANY KIND.** Only amateurs,
coaches and gyms demonstrating technique - and only where WE ARE ALLOWED TO USE THE VIDEO
COMMERCIALLY. Every upload is copyrighted by whoever filmed it; "amateur" alone is not permission.

## WHAT TO FIND

**List A — videos whose licence already allows commercial reuse.** Accept only: Creative Commons
**CC BY** or **CC0**, public domain, or a stock-site licence that permits commercial use and
modification (for example Pexels, Pixabay, Mixkit - quote the licence name as shown on the page).
**Exclude NC (non-commercial) and ND (no-derivatives).** Flag **SA (share-alike)** separately - it may
not suit animation data. Look on YouTube (its Creative Commons filter), Vimeo (CC licences), Wikimedia
Commons, and the stock sites.

For each video: link; creator; the licence exactly as the page states it; duration; which of our needs
it shows (stance, footwork direction, pivot, circling, guard return); discipline (boxing, kickboxing,
muay thai, MMA, karate); and how usable it is for motion capture - **full body in frame, static camera,
side or front view, plain background, one person, frame rate or slow motion if stated**. Include women
performers where they exist - half our roster is female.

**List B — strong demos without a reuse licence, worth asking permission for.** Coaches or amateurs
whose videos show exactly our needs cleanly. Give the link, the creator, and the public contact route
if one is listed (website or business email on their channel page). Do not contact anyone.
