## The bar (owner S534, verbatim: "mechanics need an 8 critic's to stay in the game for depth and fun")
A mechanic stays in the game only with critics' 8+ on DEPTH and 8+ on PLAY (fun) - raised from the S425 7+. And because
five maps passed critics at 7+ then failed in the game (the audit), the order is:
1. PROBE on a greybox first (tools/ + docs/, the proposed rule as a flagged local copy). Pass: the choice moves the result
   (3+ wins in 100, or real money), and no pick is best in more than 6 of 10 cells.
2. Then two fresh critics, one of them code-checked (Fable console or Arena). 8+ DEPTH and 8+ PLAY, else another round.
3. Then build, with a test that goes red first; the menu table wired and shown (S425).
FREE HAND (owner S534: "you can remove,add from scratch or interconnect current and current with needed mechanics"): any change - remove a mechanic, add one from scratch, interconnect existing ones - is open
when it is the way to 8; each is decided by the probe and the critics, then reported to him with that evidence.
