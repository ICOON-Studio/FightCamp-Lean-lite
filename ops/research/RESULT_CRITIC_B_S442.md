## 1. Score: 6

The numbers are honest and they add up (I checked them). It reads like a stat sheet a promoter gives out, not a corner's debrief. Everything you need for the lesson is on the page, but the page never puts the pieces together, the lessons are not ranked, and the one big button ("Train Cardio next week") points at the smallest cause of the loss.

What I checked and found consistent: exchanges 4-2 / 3-3 / 1-5 = 8 v 10, and the exchange bars match. The three cards add up (10-9, 9-10, 9-10 = 28-29; Judge 3 29-28 with the R2 swing). Punch sums add up: Lucy 26+25 = 51 of 88+68 = 156, Priya 29+43 = 72 of 198, head + body = landed. Priya's game splits 4 / 4 / 10 = 18, Lucy won 3+2+3 = 8. Plan lines on the timeline match the plan box: A 2 of 2, B 1 of 2, C 0 of 3, "7 of 18" fired. Shouts won 2 of 4. Damage colours match the bands (hurt 55, critical 35). The road: 4 bouts, so 1 more for the nationals. This is a solid base, which is why it is not lower.

## 2. Does the story hold together as boxing?

Mostly yes, but a corner would call these wrong or empty:
- **The real story is left untold.** In round 3 the corner shouted "Jab and move" (graded "Big edge") while plan line C said "Step in" when she presses. It fired twice in R3 and lost both times. The fighter got two opposite orders. That is the lesson of the fight, and the page grades the plan and the corner in two separate boxes without ever linking them. The result is "Big edge, round lost", which reads as nonsense to a coach.
- **"Legs went" has no cause.** Energy 36 v 50, but why? Lucy's right ribs are at 33 (critical) and Priya landed 21 to the body. In boxing, body work takes the legs. The pace Lucy was set (Push / Steady / Save) is not on the page at all. Neither is a fitness comparison. The page jumps to "Train Cardio" with no evidence that fitness was the cause rather than ribs or pace.
- **The cut has no timeline.** "Cut over the left eye: out 3 weeks" sits under WHAT IT COST. We are not told in which round it opened, whether the cutman worked it, or whether it changed the fight (vision, the judges). The corner minute (cutman, water, ice, talk, one change) is missing from the page, though the game has all of it.
- **"Priya's clearer" (round 2)** is not a reason. Amateur judges score clean scoring blows. Say "Priya landed 17 clean shots to 13" or whatever the number is. This round decided the fight (split decision), so it deserves the clearest line on the page, and it gets the vaguest.
- **The energy lines lie.** In round 3 the red and blue lines end almost on the same pixel while the labels say 36 v 50. The picture contradicts the number. Same in round 2 (60 v 68).
- **The regional rank move** "No. 5 -> No. 4" for Priya after beating an unranked boxer goes against the stated rank rule ("beat someone below and nothing moves"). Either the rule is different for amateurs or the sample is wrong. Check it.
- **The 42% before the bell is not used.** An underdog lost a split. A corner would say "she out-did the odds; this is a fixable loss, not a ceiling". The page shows the number and draws no conclusion from it.

## 3. Rows that lead nowhere, and lessons not drawn

Rows with an action: Rewrite C (good, but it does not say what to rewrite it to), Drill the 1-2-3, Train Cardio next week, Replay the plan, Continue.

Rows that lead nowhere:
- The two condition panels (half the top of the screen). No action: no "Elbows in" corner fix for the ribs, no physio, no link to the cut's layoff.
- Record / Regional rank / Before the bell: outcome only. That is fine for a result, but they take up prime space.
- ROUNDS / ENERGY strip: no link to the pace setting that drove it.
- The whole punch table (14 rows): nothing to press. "Uppercut: Learning, not a weapon yet" is the only actionable fact in it, and it has no "drill it" link.
- AGAINST PRIYA'S GAME (3 of 10 against pressing): no "rate my plan against pressers" link, though the Playbook has that function.
- CORNER, GRADED BY FIT: no action, and it misleads (see above).
- "Retreats in straight lines, punished 2": a habit has a known fix (a corner one-change or a drill), and it has no button. The button in that box is "Drill the 1-2-3", which is about a different row. The 1-2-3 landing 2 of 6 did not lose this fight.
- WHERE IT WAS FOUGHT: ring time by range, but not who WON each range. Who won each range is the thing that changes a plan (Lucy wins long, loses inside). "Range wanted 9 v 12 of 18" is a puzzle.

Lessons the page fails to draw:
1. The plan and the corner contradicted each other in R3.
2. Line C dragged her inside, where she loses. Keep it long.
3. Body work plus Push pace took the legs; fitness was a minor part.
4. The habit put her on the ropes, and that is where the cut and both hurts came from.
5. The suspension or cut changes what next week can hold (no sparring). The Cardio button should say what gains it gets while she is out.

## 4. Information missing, useless, and what each would change

Missing:
- The pace set per round (Push / Steady / Save). This would change: the player's pace setting for the next plan.
- Where the energy went (body shots / pace / fitness split). This would change: whether the player trains Cardio, adds a corner fix, or changes pace.
- Exchanges won by range. This would change: the plan lines.
- When each body place went (round), and when the cut opened. This would change: corner choices in the minute, and the defence drill.
- The corner minute actually used (cutman, recover points, talk). This would change: the player learns whether the corner work paid.
- The suggested replacement for line C, with its rating. This would change: Rewrite becomes one click, not a trip to the Playbook.
- Lessons ranked by exchanges they cost. This would change: what the player fixes first.
- The clean-shot count behind the R2 split. This would change: understanding of the judges.

Useless or too much for the space it takes:
- Six body places each, shown twice, on two big anatomical figures with only two callouts each. One compact list sorted by damage, with the round each place went, says more in a third of the space.
- The left-side mastery labels and percentages in the punch table: tiny and grey, no header. Mastery belongs on the drill button, not on a stats row.
- Priya's per-punch percentages for every punch type: the player cannot act on 7 of 16 to the body for her. Keep the body total and the target side.
- "Range wanted": cut, or replace it with range won.

## 5. Words and legibility

- Vague: "Priya's clearer", "Big edge" (edge of what?), "MIXED", "lines fired", "landed whole", "Range wanted", "fits / half / wrong", "Level after 2 rounds" (say "Even after 2 rounds").
- Codes that need a legend to read: the exchange-strip glyphs (star, back-arrow, gate icon), the "C x" / "megaphone -" chips, the slashed hatch patterns (blocked v slipped is almost the same pattern at this size).
- Font: the dot of the "i" is lost at this weight, so it reads "Rlght eye", "Rlght rlbs", "Left rlbs", "Avolded". Change the font or the weight.
- Too small: the body legends (fine / hurt / critical / doctor / cut), "R2 (3-3) to Lucy" under Judge 3, the grey percentages, the exchange-strip legend, the "of 100" captions. All about 14-15 px grey on near-black.
- A paragraph that should be a picture: the WHY line is a chain of arrows across two lines, a sentence written in symbols. It should be the ranked cause cards. The energy story should be bars with a split of where the energy went, not two overlapping lines.

## 6. To reach 10/10 (most important first)

1. **Add a "WHAT DECIDED IT" column**: the three causes ranked by exchanges each cost (line C 3, legs 2, habit 2). Each card has one line of evidence and its fix buttons, with the expected effect on the button. This replaces the WHY arrow line.
2. **Name the clash between plan and corner**: "R3: corner said Jab and move, line C said Step in, she did both, won 0." Show it on the timeline (both chips on the same exchange, linked) and on the cause card.
3. **Rewrite C offers the answer**: "Rewrite C: Step back + one-two (Beats it, +6% v pressers)", plus "Rate my plan v pressers". The plan box shows "without line C the plan was worth +11%".
4. **Explain the legs**: replace the energy lines with end-of-round bars (true length, numbers beside), plus a split bar of where the energy gap came from (body shots / pace / fitness). Show the pace set each round. The buttons become "Steady in R1", "Corner fix: Elbows in", and "Cardio mornings (what gains she gets while cut)". The single "Train Cardio" button goes.
5. **Add a range-won row**: Long 5-2, Mid 2-3, Inside 1-5, with the one-line lesson "keep it long". Cut "Range wanted".
6. **Add a round-by-round story**: each round card has its six exchanges (W/L, plan line, call, hurt, cut), the opponent's mode with the minute she changed, the pace, the judges for that round, and ONE plain sentence of why.
7. **Make the R2 swing explicit**: the clean-shot count behind the two judges, beside the cards.
8. **Give the habit its own button**: "Drill: pivot off the ropes (habit gone in about 4 weeks of pads)". Link the cut and both hurts to it. Remove "Drill the 1-2-3" unless a combo actually cost exchanges.
9. **Make damage a compact sorted list per boxer** with the round each place went. Add a corner-minute line (cutman closed the eye, held 2 minutes, then reopened; water, ice). Drop the two big figures, or shrink them to a thumbnail.
10. **Use the 42%**: "underdog, lost by one round", so the player knows the result is fixable.
11. **Collect the fixes at the bottom**: "3 fixes ticked go to the plan and the week: Rewrite C, Steady in R1, Pivot drill", untickable, and the button becomes "Apply and continue". The fight's lessons land in one press.
12. **Clean up the type**: no dotless-i font, nothing under 16 px for anything the player must read, no glyph-only legends. Fix or explain the regional rank move for beating an unranked boxer.
