A. PLAN OVERVIEW & SPATIAL PHILOSOPHY

This floor plan is designed around the three optical and operational axes of a world-class professional boxing club: the Player’s Camera Line, the Member Walk-In Path, and the Head Coach’s Apex Sightline.

From the camera’s high-3/4 angle (looking from $z > 18$ down toward the north back wall at $z = 0$), the elevated 16 ft regulation ring sits slightly offset to the north-center ($x \in [10.6, 18.4]$, $z \in [0.4, 8.2]$), functioning as the heroic visual anchor without occluding the back-wall stations. The continuous Bag Line flanks the ring across the north-west brick wall, keeping all heavy impacts grounded where structural load belongs.

When a fighter enters through the main reception double doors ($x = 11.3, z = 18.0$), they step directly into an unobstructed 2.0 m arterial corridor running straight to the heart of the gym. Immediately to their left is the wrap and glove preparation alcove; ahead lies the open canvas and mitt-work floor.

The Coach’s observation spot sits dead-center on the floor ($x = 10.8, z = 8.5$), granting an uninterrupted $360^\circ$ vantage point over the Bag Line (north/west), the Ring apron and steps (north), the technical Mitt & Slip Drill floor (west), and the Agility & Turf lane (east).

Non-boxing noise—the clanging of Olympic iron and the hum of secondary cardio machines—is sequestered into the south-west rubber corner ($x \in [0.2, 8.8]$, $z \in [11.8, 16.6]$), cleanly isolated from the fighters' footwork lanes. The recovery and physio transition zone sits far east on high-density tatami mats ($x \in [25.4, 29.8]$, $z \in [0.4, 6.8]$), safely away from flying ropes, swinging leather, and entrance foot traffic.
