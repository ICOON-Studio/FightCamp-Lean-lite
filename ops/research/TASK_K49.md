# TASK K49 — KALLI — THE TORSO IN A HOOK: HOW FAR THE HIPS AND CHEST TURN, AND WHERE THE FIST ARRIVES

Owner: Kalli (research, no shell). Stage: S4a (the rear hook) - forecast research. Deliver as ONE markdown answer.

## Why this exists

We animate boxers in 3D. Our rear hook is a lead hook mirrored left-for-right, and mirroring also swaps the boxer's
stance. We tested five ways to keep the stance and turn only the upper body: the ones that turn the chest the way the
mirrored clip does wring the spine 71 to 139 degrees away from its rest pose, which no human spine does. Before we
choose a replacement clip we need the REAL numbers for a hook, so a rear hook can be judged against a human, not
against another clip.

## Questions (answer each with numbers where any exist)

1. **Pelvis and thorax rotation in a hook**: how many degrees do the hips (pelvis) and the chest (thorax) rotate about
   the vertical axis from guard to contact - lead hook and rear hook separately, head and body hooks where measured?
2. **Separation ("X-factor")**: the peak angle between pelvis and thorax during a hook, a cross and a jab (degrees),
   and when in the punch it peaks (ms before contact).
3. **The fist at contact**: for lead and rear hooks to the head and to the body - how far in front of the boxer's body
   (cm, or as a share of arm length or height), how high (cm, or relative to his own shoulder or chin), and how far
   across his centre line.
4. **Timing**: a lead hook's and a rear hook's duration from first movement to contact (ms), against a jab and a cross.
5. **The rear foot and the hips in a rear hook**: does the rear foot pivot (angle), and does the rear hip drive through
   (degrees of pelvis turn), compared with a cross?
6. **Southpaw against orthodox**: is a rear hook thrown from orthodox stance the same motion as a southpaw's LEAD hook
   mirrored, or does the torso rotation differ (degrees) - any study comparing the lead and rear hand's hook?

## Sources wanted

Peer-reviewed biomechanics first (3D motion capture of boxing punches - e.g. Dinu, Lenetsky, Cheraghi, Stanley,
Kimm & Thiel, Whiting; X-factor studies from striking and throwing sports only if boxing has none, labelled as the
analogue), then coaching texts with measured numbers, then video analyses. **Label every number: DOCUMENTED
(peer-reviewed / measured), SECONDARY (coaching text, secondary report), or INFERENCE.** Give the citation (authors,
year, journal or book, page/table where possible). If a question has NO measured number anywhere, write **NO STUDY**
for it plainly - do not fill the gap with a guess.
