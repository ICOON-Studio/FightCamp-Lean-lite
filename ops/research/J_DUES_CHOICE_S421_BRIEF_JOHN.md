## Three most important facts for a game rule
1. **At about +25%, stated elasticity is about 1 (0.97).** That's exactly premium's break-even point: a 22% loss of members for $35 → $45. Source: [EASM 2007](https://easm.net/download/2007/ce8381b677ab2968f4c5347f332eed5b.pdf), MEASURED, stated (?). Small rises at budget chains cost nothing: The Gym Group's +4.5% left volumes flat ([TGG FY25](https://www.tggplc.com/media/4pvnsiwf/rns-fy25-final_.pdf)).
2. **Cheap clubs earn grant money tied to deprivation.** Sport England's Movement Fund pays £300–£15,000 ($375–$18,750) per project, and England Boxing inclusion grants go up to £2,000. Funders turn away "nice area" clubs. Sources: [Movement Fund](https://www.sportengland.org/funding-and-campaigns/our-funding/movement-fund), [EB inclusion programme](https://www.englandboxing.org/news_articles/england-boxing-community-inclusion-club-programme/), [EB community study](https://www.englandboxing.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/England-Boxing-Workforce-Research-Evaluating-the-impact-of-boxing-clubs-on-their-host-communities.pdf). OFFICIAL.
3. **Community drives churn more than price does.** A friend at the club cuts cancellation risk by 40–53%. No-contract budget churn works out at ≈5.6% a month, against ≈2–2.5% for coached studios. Sources: [HCM "10,000 members"](https://www.healthclubmanagement.co.uk/health-club-management-features/What-do-10000-members-say/29553), [TGG Annual Report](https://www.tggplc.com/media/qurllrzp/tgg_annual-report-2025_web-ready-spreads.pdf). MEASURED / DERIVED.

**OPINION (a design suggestion, not a finding):**
- Premium loses 22–30% of members at low reputation, shrinking to about 10% with a high reputation, a named coach or premium facilities.
- Cheap pays back four ways:
  - grant events worth $375–$18,750, open only below a dues threshold;
  - a bigger pool of juniors and walk-ins;
  - a yearly chance that a member turns into a volunteer coach;
  - 1–2 points less monthly churn.
- Premium could get contract lock-in in exchange for needing open-gym and one-to-one coach hours.
- Consider moving the bands to real GBP, since $45 is only £36.
