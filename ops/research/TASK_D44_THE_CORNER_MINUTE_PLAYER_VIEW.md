# TASK D44 - DOLA - THE CORNER MINUTE, FROM THE PLAYER'S CHAIR
Owner: dola (Cici, NEW chat). Reply inline, <= 500 words, titled D44_THE_CORNER_MINUTE_PLAYER_VIEW. Opinion + design;
no repo, no test.
## Why
Our game is a fight-gym MANAGEMENT sim: the player runs the gym and sits in the corner while the bout plays out in 3D.
We are adding LIVE LIMB DAMAGE: when a fighter's leg (or body, arm, eye) takes enough, that limb turns red, he limps or
holds it for a moment, and it stays compromised. The operator wants this live on screen ("pointing" at the damage) and
tied to what the corner can do between rounds. We already have: three damage levels (fine / hurt / critical, plus a
separate "doctor looks at it" state), corner sentences between rounds, and the one-minute corner rules.
## What would a human do?
A player who loves sports-management games (Football Manager, Punch Club, EA UFC) would say what makes a 60-second
between-rounds screen exciting instead of a chore - the ONE or TWO decisions they actually want to make - and how they
want to SEE damage building during the round without pausing the fight.
## Questions
1. During the round: body figure pointing at the damaged limb, small letter chips, or only what the fighter's body does
   (limp, hand on ribs)? Which would you want, and why?
2. The corner minute: list the decisions you'd want (e.g. ice the leg vs close the cut, tactical instruction, stop the
   fight), and which would feel like busywork.
3. How should the player's choice visibly pay off (or fail) in the next round?
4. One idea from another game that nails this, and one trap to avoid.
