## Why this exists

We are scripting each boxing exchange for BOTH fighters on one clock. The thrower's side is measurable from the
technique videos you found (K55, K59). The RECEIVER's side is not: when a jab, cross, hook or body shot LANDS, which part
of him moves first (head, neck, shoulders, hips, feet), how far, which way, how fast he recovers his guard, whether his
feet step back - and what a BLOCK on the gloves and a SLIP look like on his whole body. Your K57 slow-motion list is mostly
face close-ups and broadcast replays that cut; the pose tracker needs the receiver's WHOLE BODY and the thrower in the
same frame.

## What to find (links you could open, with timestamps)

1. **Sparring or fights with punches LANDING, filmed side-on, both whole bodies in frame, 50-60 fps or slow motion**:

   punches land clearly at the timestamps (jab, cross, hook, uppercut, body shot) and whether the receiver's feet are in
   frame.
2. **Coaching videos about TAKING a punch**: "rolling with the punch", "absorbing a jab", "how to take a body shot",

3. **Pad or partner drills where the holder REACTS like a hit man** (Thai pad holders, "reactive mitts" where the holder's
   head moves on contact).
4. **High-frame-rate impact footage with the whole body** (not the face alone): sports science lab captures, 240 fps phone
   sparring, "super slow motion sparring".
