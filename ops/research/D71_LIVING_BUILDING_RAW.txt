## 7. Recommendation — FightCamp Living Entity (10 points)

1. **Street layer**: Narrow animated strip along bottom edge — pedestrians pass 07:00–22:00; some pause, glance at gym windows, then enter. JUDGEMENT
2. **Entrance + reception**: Glass doors swing open; arrivals walk to reception desk, pause, then either speak to receptionist or walk straight to your office door. JUDGEMENT
3. **Your office**: Positioned upper-left, large window overlooking floor — camera starts here behind your desk; you look out onto the gym. JUDGEMENT
4. **Fighter candidates**: Walk in from street → reception → your office door; knock → portrait card appears with stats → interview/converse right there. JUDGEMENT
5. **Coach/trainer candidates**: Same entry path but carry clipboards; stand at reception briefly before being directed to you. JUDGEMENT
6. **Regular members**: Enter → reception → wristband/register → walk to floor; no office visit; volume rises with reputation/ads/time of day. JUDGEMENT
7. **Promoter/reporter**: Rarer, dress distinct; arrive at scheduled times; walk directly to office door — trigger story/event not just hire. JUDGEMENT
8. **Tasking**: Drag coach icon to ring/pad/trainee; drag cleaner to area; they visibly walk across floor — Two Point + Chef hybrid. JUDGEMENT
9. **Reputation gate**: Street traffic + quality of candidates improve as rating rises; empty street early → lively district later. JUDGEMENT
10. **Camera control**: Default = your office view; click window/button to pan down to floor; click desk to return — anchor the player’s place. JUDGEMENT
