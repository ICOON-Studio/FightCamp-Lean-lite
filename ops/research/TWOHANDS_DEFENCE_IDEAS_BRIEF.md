# Ideas wanted: how does a boxer DEFEND in this card game, with no timed button presses?

You are a game designer. We want your own ideas, not a review. Nothing below is a proposal to judge: it is the game as it
stands and the problem.

## The game (a small standalone prototype, boxing only, one man against one man)
- Each boxer has TWO small decks: one for his lead hand (jabs, lead hook, lead uppercut, lead body hook, feints) and one
  for his rear hand (cross, overhand, rear uppercut, body cross). Two moves of each hand are always face up, so a hand
  is never empty. The player packs both decks before the bout.
- A bout is 3 rounds; in a round each man leads 3 times, turn about. A lead is up to three moves, in order, picked from
  his two hands. Changing hands inside a combination makes it better.
- Three bars: Health (0 = knocked down; three knockdowns = stopped), Stamina (pays for moves; throwing fewer moves gives
  some back; body punches shorten the bar for the bout), and a Guard bar in one version.
- Every move says WHAT IT HITS (head straight, head from the side, chin from below, ribs, stomach) and what the other man
  can do about it (block, slip, duck, parry, lean back, step back, cover the ribs). An uppercut goes through a block.
- Each exchange is told move by move, with a picture of the punch landing, being blocked or missing.
- The player wants: fast and punchy bouts (under 5 minutes), every result shown before he commits, something that does
  not feel like one more deckbuilder, and moves that follow how a real boxing exchange goes.

## What has been tried for defence, and the owner's verdict
1. A prompt at every incoming punch: "block / slip / take it". Verdict: "ithink this is dumb , need to find better
   mechanic" (the take-it button was never a real choice).
2. Timed presses: each punch sweeps toward an impact and you press a defence inside its band (wide for a block, narrow
   for a slip that gives a counter). Verdict today: "i dont like the timed mechanic fornow".
3. A guard held high or low that wears down, plus timed slips. Same verdict (it still has the timed presses).

## His hint
"can you find another way to block guard and defence ? maybe a block cards can be generated if some criteria are met".
So one direction he likes the sound of: defence cards are not in the deck; they are CREATED during the bout when the
boxer does something (you decide what).

## What we ask you for
Give FIVE different defence mechanics for this game. No timing, no reflexes, no dice the player cannot see. For each:
1. THE RULE in at most five plain sentences (what the player does, when, what it costs).
2. ONE EXCHANGE walked through: he leads jab - cross - lead hook; say exactly what the defender does and what happens.
3. THE DECISION it gives the player: what is he weighing, and when is each choice right?
4. HOW IT CAN BREAK: the lazy strategy that would win too often, and the cap you would put on it.
5. THE REAL BOXING it stands for (one line).
At least two of the five must build on "defence cards are generated when a condition is met": name the conditions
(for example: a hand that did not punch, a jab that landed, a feint he bit on, ground given) and what each creates.
Then: which ONE of your five would you build first, and why. Finally, name any published game (card, board or video)
that defends in a similar way, so we can study it.
