## Claude's translation for the engine (14 s per exchange, `AVG_SECONDS_PER_EXCHANGE`)

- **Matte has no clock.** IJF SOR Art. 11.2(e): the referee calls Mate! when "during ne-waza there
  is no evident progress". The engine equivalent is an event, not a timer: a ground exchange that
  produces no score, no osaekomi progress and no submission progress ends ground work.
- **A ne-waza sequence is shorter than one exchange.** Modal ~5-8 s; scored or held ~12-24 s. So an
  unproductive ground exchange should stand them up after ONE exchange, and only a sequence that is
  scoring or holding earns a second.
- **A pin held across two exchanges is 28 s - an ippon** (20 s). Under the 2025-28 cycle one held
  exchange (10-19 s) is waza-ari, and 5-9 s is yuko.
- **Anchors for the judo commit's measurement, population named:** senior World Championships

  of contest time (elite women, Barreto/Miarka 2022). The S1d measurement had judo at 77% of
