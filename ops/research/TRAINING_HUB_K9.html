RECOMMENDATION

ONE rail item: TRAINING holds ordinary week + fight camp. Home tab THE WEEK. SESSION is a day-opened editor panel (STORE-drawer pattern), not a tab. FIGHT CAMP is a mode the hub enters when the selected fighter is booked (surfaced as a soft tab that hardens when someone is in camp). Reason: same act at two intensities — splitting rail items would recreate the SCHEDULE orphan K8 erased for GYM.

FLOOR ÷ TRAINING

FLOOR shows who occupies stations; TRAINING shows what each fighter does with their week — TRAINING never restates station occupancy.

KIT GAPS

Reuse R21 gym family first. Genuine new gaps only: week_band, focus_chip, body_delta, session_panel, camp_week_rail, illegal_refusal. No drag primitive — click-arm-click for copy/paste.
