# Turning pro — fresh design critic, v8
SCORE depth=6 general=6 play=7

## Verdict

**Not designed yet against the 7/7 bar.** This score is for the proposed mechanic, not the look of the page or a claim that the proposal is already in the game. The two houses, a real signing bill, the championship deadline and a choice between refusing and letting an offer expire can make this a strong gym-management decision. The proposed debt also fixes a real subsidy in today's covered-cost deal.

The remaining problems are material. Holding an offer can collide with other clock-stopping offers and loyalty rules. The one-off price ask is almost free money across much of the two-house range, while two useful risk choices are cut even in cases where the safer house is absent. The stated training and cash forecasts do not yet follow all of the actual week. Those are not cosmetic problems: they can change which button a player should press.

- **Depth 6/10.** Money now versus money later, risk versus preparation, and a live amateur deadline are genuine forks. But the overlap between asks, the missing single-house risk choices, the unpriced queue for other boxers, and an unproven gap mean there is not yet evidence of several reliably different good answers.
- **General design 6/10.** The map is unusually specific about expiry, debt and old saves, but it misses live offer collisions, idle loyalty, bill timing, the day-job simulation mismatch and the actual location of club news. An answer that cannot be reached or a cash date that is wrong makes the rules harder to trust.
- **Play 7/10.** A deadline, a week to prepare, the first pro booking and a post-fight accounting give the player something to anticipate and a reward to see. It loses points because the best reason to keep checking the week can disappear behind a red blocker, and the proposed opponent gap has not been proved on the actual path.

This is a **static review**. I read the whole brief and map, traced the named source and relevant callers, and checked the examples with arithmetic. I did not run Godot or the game. The real-screen files named in the access guide are not in this checkout; I used the available mockup and screen rules, not an imagined screenshot.

## The choice, with numbers

All figures in this subsection are **design estimates**, not installed terms. Assume a first call, a 25% gym share, first signing costs of $1,245, no other cash movements, and base sales on the night equal to the call-day count. The existing rules round the gym's purse and ticket cuts separately.

| Base sales | Ticket deal, plain | Guarantee deal, plain | What the player is trading |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 46 | $1,150 gross; gym $288 | $1,650 gross; gym $413 | The earlier fight pays $125 less to the gym and exposes a weak gate; the other deal gives more preparation but pays two weeks later. |
| 66 | $1,650 gross; gym $413 | $1,650 gross; gym $413 | Same debut gross, so the useful difference is fight date, opponent, preparation and later contract terms. |
| 74 | $1,850 gross; gym $463 | $1,650 gross; gym $413 | The ticket deal pays the gym $50 more on the night; the other deal buys more time and a less difficult starting matchup. Neither win chance is established. |

These are **fight-night receipts**, not money received on signing. At 74, paying costs takes $1,245 from the bank immediately and the ticket deal forecasts $463 later. The boxer forecasts $1,387 later. The gym has spent $782 across those two events before any other income or bills, not $782 on signing day.

**The price ask is too free in the common case.** At 46–74 base sales it raises the ticket deal's gross by $230–$370 at the same sales, with no extra cash cost or tougher opponent in v8. The 92-seat gross crossover is real, but the range of both calls ends at 74. Normal membership drift is capped at six members per week; four weeks of that drift alone raises the base forecast by at most 12 seats, not enough to get from 74 to 93. Other events can change members, so this is not a claim that 93 is impossible. It is a reason to test the *reachable* night range, not treat the distant crossover as a regular downside.

For the 10/10 candidate, price the extra selling work at **$45 from the gym at signing** at a 25% share, a tuning estimate. The candidate rule is `round(180 × actual gym share)`, or $18 at 10%; it is a proposed selling charge, not a real-world fee. At 74, $45 now raises the expected gym receipt by $57 and the boxer receipt by $173: the gym is $12 better over those two events if base sales stay at 74. At a 25% share, separately rounded, it ties plain at **84 base sales** and is worse from **85**. At 10%, 83–84 tie and 85 is worse. Starting at 74, normal member drift alone could reach 86 over four weeks if there is room and demand for 24 more members, so the downside is within the mechanically possible range, though not a prediction it will happen. Hide an ask when the supported range makes it strictly worse, and retune it if tests show it routinely dominates all alternatives.

**Covered costs must be a loan, not a transfer from the boxer.** At $1,650 gross, a 25% share and a $100 fee, cover means $0 leaves the bank at signing; the $1,345 balance takes the full $413 gym cut at the debut; $932 remains, while the boxer receives the same $1,237 as on plain terms. The balance is due from the gym on departure or retirement. Show the full balance due if that happens *before* the debut too. Once fully repaid, the gym has paid exactly the fee more than plain. At a 10% share, repayment takes longer; do not promise a particular payoff fight.

**A later fight is useful only if it changes something.** For a ticket offer, the candidate +2 weeks costs $100 of debut base. At 74 fixed sales that changes gross $1,850 → $1,750 and gym receipt $463 → $438. Offer it when a real plan shows a relevant difference, such as a weight forecast missing the limit on the first date and reaching it on the second, or a known recovery date cutting into the first camp. It must not invent healing, a second Peak, or a change to the later contract base.

## What each option changes

| Option | Effect and real cost | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Sign the ticket deal | Book the earlier pro debut; pay the actual signing bill now; end amateur eligibility and the actual stipend; fight-night pay varies with ticket sales; later offers lean toward a step up when a suitable candidate exists. | Keep. Earlier cash and higher gate upside compete with the other deal's preparation and matchup. |
| Sign the guarantee deal | Book the later debut; pay the same actual signing bill unless covered; protect the gross at low sales; early offers lean toward a tune-up when a suitable candidate exists. | Keep. Its opponent and extra time must be measured, not described as a guaranteed win. |
| Ask for a lower ticket price | Use the house's one ask; at $40, forecast 15 extra sales, clipped at capacity. The 10/10 candidate also pays $45 now at a 25% share ($18 at 10%). Boxer and gym can gain or lose versus plain as actual base sales change. | Keep with visible net, fee and a reachable crossover; otherwise it is a default pick. |
| Ask to cover signing costs | Use the other house's one ask; owe actual costs plus the fee; repay only from future gym pro cuts, or settle the remainder on departure/retirement. The boxer's cut is unchanged. | Keep. It buys cash today at a known later cost. |
| Ask for +2 weeks | Use the one ask, pay $100 less debut base and receive the fight money later; gain real time for the selected plan. | Keep only when a weight or recovery comparison can change the decision. |
| Ask for a safer opponent | A smaller opponent gap can matter even when only the ticket house calls. The installed ask also promises tune-up sets, but does not always deliver them. | **Missing from v8's cut in the single-house case.** Keep conditionally there, for example +0.5 safer gap for $150 less debut base, without promising future tune-ups. Probe whether the difference matters. |
| Ask for a higher ticket share | An installed full answer gives +10 share points for a 0.5 tougher opponent. At 87 base sales, plain gross is $2,175, the current price ask $2,300, and the split ask $2,310; the split pays more but is harder. At 92 it has a larger advantage over the price ask. | **Not useless at high sales**, when only the ticket house calls. Retain it only where the actual range gives a meaningful pay-for-risk choice, with both changes in the button. Do not keep the weak counter where a price ask dominates it. |
| Stay amateur | Release the calendar immediately; apply the actual, clamped loyalty and applicable morale changes; spend a refusal; preserve the first card as the return house and show the exact next gate and adjusted terms. | Keep. A title entry closing during the offer can make paying this price worthwhile. Do not promise two new wins. |
| Review later / close | Keep the existing offer, asks and deadline; continue training and other boxers' bouts; reserve only this boxer's amateur booking/entry. | Keep, after resolving other offers and idle loyalty. Closing or reloading must not refresh anything. |
| Let the offer expire | Release the calendar, spend no refusal and set a wait of two wins **from expiry**, then apply normal age, rank, house-lock and availability rules. An entry deadline can be missed. | Keep, with a clear last usable day and no invisible penalty. |
| Return to plain after a counter | Restore the original terms but leave that house's ask spent. | Keep. Do not describe this as another free ask. |
| Open the week plan | Close the offer and go to the existing week; the amber need reopens the same offer. | Keep: it lets the player act on the weight and recovery comparison. |
| Softer, better split and +4 weeks as v8 cuts | The installed +4 changes date and costs $300, but adds little if +2 already reaches the relevant threshold. The two risk asks are not always redundant with another house. | Cut +4. Replace the two risk asks as described above rather than cutting them in every case. Old saves keep their original signed terms. |

Boxer pay is worth showing honestly, but do not imply that it currently changes loyalty or buys something: `ProOfferWire.night` calculates the boxer's amount, while `BoutFinish.run` pays the gym's cut. No boxer-wallet consequence was established on this path.

## Missing interactions and misleading information

1. **A held offer can still stop the clock indirectly.** `Bouts.day_events` can create an ordinary amateur offer for the same free boxer; `Needs.needs` makes it red; `Bouts.passed` can charge loyalty for passing or expiry. `RivalOfferWire` and `LateAskWire` can also open red decisions. Reserving the calendar only at acceptance creates a forced pass or a blocker the player cannot legally resolve. At call creation, preserve any already-open sheet, rival or late-host opportunity and its real deadline; while the pro offer is live, suppress new conflicting offers and their red needs. If an existing one expires during the hold, close it without the pass, loyalty or rival-ladder penalties and say the opportunity was missed. If Stay is chosen before its deadline, restore the original opportunity with its original consequences. Do not extend its date for free; unrelated decisions for either boxer or gym still work.
2. **Holding also consumes the gym's only pro-offer slot.** `Roster.check_pro` returns while `career.pro_offer` is nonempty, so another ready boxer does not receive a call during the hold. Queue eligible boxers internally in arrival order, start a queued offer's 14 days only when it becomes the active offer, and disclose the queued count. Store the arrival's eligibility/age, house and terms; do not silently lose a first call because the queue crossed age 32. Skip temporarily booked or active-draw boxers without erasing their place; remove departed or already-pro boxers. There is still only one active offer and no automatic modal.
3. **A reserved calendar can incur the normal idle penalty.** `LoyaltyWire.idle_week` counts a fit, unbooked boxer and can apply −3 after four Mondays. Pause, but do not reset, that counter while this offer prevents booking; resume it on release. Other independent changes, such as sponsorship, still happen and should be reported normally.
4. **Do not let a home-show promise spend money before the guard.** `ApproachWire.answer` can book the show and write a promise before `HomeBout.book`. Check the hold in `HomeBout.reason` and in the approach's availability and commit path, before any show payment, promise or loyalty write. Apply the same write-path guard to sheet offers, asked bouts, rival and short-notice answers, and `Champs.enter`. A rejected action must leave all state unchanged.
5. **The shown work mornings are not the simulated work mornings.** `DayJob.lost_days_of`, `ClubWeek.model` and `MorningWire.rows` mark one or two mornings as work. But `Sim.step_day` still runs the planned morning's `WeekPlan.full_day`, including load and calories; `GymSpend.gain` instead multiplies both morning and evening stat gains by 5/6 or 4/6. `WeekData.plan_of` calls `WeekPlan.week` without that `GymSpend.gain` multiplier, so its stat forecast also does not match settlement. The weight forecast uses the planned morning too. Do not advertise an actual lost-session benefit or use these forecasts as proof until the shared simulation is fixed.
6. **The title goal and the moment of entry are different things.** `Champs.check` is basic eligibility; it is not `Champs.cannot_enter`, which also checks the entry window, existing draw, prior run and a clash. The title morale branch can therefore fire even when entry is closed or the year's run is already over. `Recruit.ambition` is recomputed from the current record, so a recruit's displayed goal can change as the record changes. Store a stable, specific goal at recruitment, mark it met when its target is achieved, and charge morale only for a still-open goal with a viable amateur route. Show the actual delta, including zero and clamping, before signing.
7. **A weekly saving is not weekly bank cash.** `GameMoney.settle_week` accrues wages and upkeep and pays the bill every 28 days. Stopping a $250 stipend reduces future accrual; it does not put $250 into the bank every Monday. The $75 lost amateur cut is an assumed comparison, not a ledger payment. Show the next bill and actual signing/fight events by date; label any counterfactual income as an estimate. Do not publish a cash break-even date until the dated model agrees with the bill and settlement.
8. **The present pay and record leaks need fixing together.** A later ladder quote uses a fixed 25% in `ProFights._row`, while `Roster.gym_cut` uses the boxer's actual share, which can be 10%; the same row calls purse plus ticket income a “purse.” `BoutFinish.run` also labels the debut ledger as 25% even when the paid amount used another share. The offer's shared ticket header remains $50 after the $40 ask. The roster labels amateur wins/losses as pro and reads the amateur rank; a Fighter header can still say amateur, its contract row is fixed at $250, and Money uses an amateur cut for a pro booking. A correct offer followed by wrong receipts is not a correct mechanic.
9. **The 10% share can silently rise later.** This is a downstream handoff issue, not a false v8 CODE claim: `StarDeal.terms` calculates the yearly “lower” share as `max(0.12, current − 0.02)`. At a current 10% it returns 12%. `GymDeals.answer_talks(false)` stores that value, and `GymSpend.end_week` also takes that answer on expiry. The screen can call an increase “Drop to 12%”. Do not promise that 10% persists indefinitely; clamp the lower choice to at most the current share, and test the explicit answer and timeout.
10. **Names leak outside headers today.** The offer's sign buttons include the house name; the Home note's body and Next Step line name houses; the pro-lean line, signing toast, event and ledger text include names; the booked-fight body can name the opponent. Replace these with “your boxer”, “the opponent” or “the promoter” in body text and controls. Keep identifying names only in their headers, without changing which person a record belongs to.

### Evidence, not a new rule

In Britain, the licensing board's 2026 rules require medical checks, including an initial brain angiogram and an annual MRI report [1](https://www.bbbofc.com/build/documents/Rules_and_Regulations_2026.pdf). A US association's model contracts call for itemised deductions and a post-bout accounting [1](https://www.abcboxing.com/boxer-and-bout-sample-contracts/). These support showing what is paid and when; US model terms are not UK law, and neither source validates this game's fictional amounts, house formulas or proposed debt.

The repository probe, `docs/TURNGAP1_RESULT.md`, is useful warning evidence, not a card's win chance: 20 boxers × 10 seeds per gap produced 67.0% at −1 and 78.5% at 0 in the pooled probe, while the displayed signing reads for one case were 45.3% and 50.5%. The actual booking, camp and settlement paths still need paired tests. Use matchup words until a separate calibration supports a number.

## The 1920 × 1080 proposal

The companion file `DESIGN_TURNPRO1_V8_CRITIC_ARENA_10.html` is a self-contained, interactive **design study**, not game code. It shows one offer modal over one top bar: one neutral boxer, equal house cards, a shared sales bar, dated bank and fight-night amounts, weight and title-deadline warnings, and card-local sign actions. The sample is fictional: 74 base sales, 52 members, nine wins, one earlier title, an open national-title ambition, a 25% gym cut and $2,400 cash. It assumes $1,200 already accrued, $1,000 weekly income, $1,200 weekly costs before signing and $950 after. On 30 March, three new weeks add $3,000 income and $2,850 costs, producing a $4,050 bill. Plain leaves $105 after that bill, the price ask $60, and cover $1,350, before any further changes. The weight forecasts and $45 price-ask fee are candidate inputs, not measured game results. The page starts as if the player had opened the existing offer; the game must not open it automatically. Click an ask to see its consequences; review later closes the modal without resetting the ask. The study does not simulate the clock. No percentages for winning are invented.

## To reach 10/10

1. **Make the hold actually playable and fair.** Reconcile expiry before blocker checks, on load, and before sign/ask; define the last legal day as `day < expires`, expire once and never replace on the same tick. In `Bouts`, `AskFlow`, `RivalOfferWire`, `LateAskWire`, `HomeBout`, `ApproachWire` and `Champs`, guard all amateur write paths and deal with already-open offers as specified above. Pause the idle-bout counter during the hold. Test pre-existing and newly arriving offers, a show promise, stale saves, the last day, double presses and another boxer's uninterrupted bout.
2. **Queue other eligible boxers without changing the one-active-offer rule.** Persist order, eligibility/age and house/terms at arrival; start the 14-day clock at activation; skip a temporarily unavailable boxer without losing the place; cancel a departed or already-pro boxer. Show the count without opening a popup. Test two simultaneous eligibilities, age 31→32 while queued, an intervening booking, departure, reload and FIFO promotion after sign, refusal and lapse.
3. **Make one dated money source authoritative.** Quote signing cash, fee, expected fight-night gym and boxer amounts, actual bill accrual and debt from the same helper used by `ProOfferWire`, `BoutFinish`, `MoneyPanels` and `ProFights`. For new cover contracts, repay `min(balance, actual gym cut)` once per paid bout; do not charge on a cancelled night. Hook departure before the fighter is erased, and wire player retirement explicitly rather than assuming a world-table flag settles a club debt. Debit the full remainder once, allow the existing negative-cash rules, and disclose it before signing. Preserve old contract versions. Test 10%/25%, $230/$1,245 bills, both fee tiers, no-show, departure before and after debut, retirement, reload and duplicate settlement.
4. **Repair the signed contract handoff, not the ladder design.** Version new deals; save the house's refusal-adjusted base/step/cap before one-off debut asks. Replace only `Matchmaking.pay_of`'s unranked base with that stored base for new contracts, keeping rank/draw additions and fan tickets. Use the helper in offered, named and booked quotes, apply the fighter's actual share and outstanding debt, and increase the base once per settled **post-debut** win. Never reprice a booked fight or reinterpret an old save. Fix the 25% label and the false win-step promise until this works. In `StarDeal.terms`, cap a yearly lower share at the current share; test 10% through explicit and timed-out talks.
5. **Make asks differ for the offers that actually exist.** Test the proposed `round(180 × gym share)` price-ask fee and exact gym/boxer crossover across plausible rising and falling membership, showing the downside on the card. Preserve a priced safer-opponent ask and a visible higher-share/harder-opponent ask only when one house calls and the supported range makes each materially different. Keep conditional +2 weeks and remove +4. Test a choice at 46, 66, 74, 75, 83, 84, 85, 86, 87, 92 and 100 sales at both shares, including a poor, ready and weight-at-risk gym. Tune or remove an ask that has no distinct use.
6. **Make training, work and forecasts one calculation.** Apply the actual work-day substitution to morning stat gain, coaching allocation, calories, load and fatigue in both simulation and `WeekData.plan_of`; keep evening work independent. Rebuild weigh-in comparisons from a copy representing the boxer *after* signing. Test full, half and no stipend, rest-day shifts, one and several boxers, taper and injury; compare the displayed daily plan with the actual week and the two weigh-in dates. Until then, omit the later ask when its benefit cannot be shown honestly.
7. **Make goals and return gates truthful.** Persist a specific ambition and its completion state; migrate old saves once, make pro ambition reachable, and distinguish basic eligibility from an available or still-viable title route. Show the actual morale and loyalty changes, including floors, pre-signing share and the exact refusal gate. Preserve the first-card return lock, the separate expiry +2-win wait and the first-call age check. Test a met title, closed entries, a previous run, age 31/32, rank 1 after two refusals and a gate already met without new wins.
8. **Validate matchup and play rather than copying averages.** Run at least 200 paired real-path bouts per house over the same boxers/seeds, split by day-job route, preparation and readiness; include draws, cancellation and both dates. Compare risk, cash timing and choices in a near-deadline title case, a quiet hold, a cash-short cover and a fit early debut. Calibrate any numeric read on separate bouts before displaying it; otherwise use plain words. Check pick rates for domination, not against a quota.
9. **Finish the existing surfaces.** Fix the offer price header, roster's pro record and world rank, Fighter's pro header and actual stipend/share, the ladder's purse-and-ticket label, Money's booked pro quote, history's empty-list milestone, names outside headers, and the club-news location. Use the existing Home need, top bar, Week review, History and Fights page; no automatic or nested modal. The debut ledger must compare the saved signing forecast with actual sales, signing outlay, gym receipt, repayment and remaining balance.
