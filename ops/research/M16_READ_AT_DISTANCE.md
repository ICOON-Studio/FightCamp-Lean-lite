## DECISIONS

1. **RANK (unmistakable → invisible at HOME):** `hook_rear` ≈ `hook_lead` (arc) → `uppercut` (vertical) → `cross` (rotation only if loud) → `jab` (shortest / collapses). **Jab vs cross collapse** at 10 m / −20° elevated ¾ — both read as “straight / he punched” unless shoulder+hip rotation is authored as a broadcast tell. Do **not** buy jab AND cross as separate taxonomy for HOME read.
2. **BUY COUNT AT THIS CAMERA: 3 punch types** — `hook_lead`, `hook_rear`, `uppercut`. Optional **#4 only if needed:** one `straight` (shared jab/cross clip or cross-as-power-straight) — not two. Finding of 3 frees slots vs J17’s six-type shopping list.
3. **Q3 one-liner:** at HOME, **opponent reaction out-reads the thrower’s arm** — silhouette path of the strike still matters for hooks/uppercut; fine straight taxonomy does not.
4. **CUES THAT SURVIVE 10 m / −20°:** (1) **whole-body silhouette path** (arc / vertical) (2) **opponent reaction** (head snap / torso fold / stumble) (3) **follow-through mass** (4) shoulder open — marginal (5) glove detail / face / finger — dead. Arc vs straight is the primary type cue; shoulder rotation alone is not enough for jab≠cross.
5. **NO THIRD EXIT.** Do not add a punch-type camera. Do not misuse CLAIM-TIGHT as a per-type highlighter (would reopen M13 claim rule). If a punch is illegible at HOME, **honest answer = don’t buy that punch for type taxonomy** — not “get closer.”
6. **MMA LENS:** kicks / knees / (teep, roundhouse silhouette) **outrank** boxing straights for distance read — bigger limb path. Argue **buy kick footage for MMA** even while boxing punch taxonomy shrinks to 3. Ranking changes: kick/knee → hooks/uppercut → straight. Reactions still high priority.
7. **SPEND FLIP:** prefer **reaction clips** (and KD already on M15 list) over buying jab as distinct from cross. J17 already said far fewer reaction SKUs than strike SKUs as library craft — M16 adds: those few reactions carry more *type-feeling* at HOME than a sixth punch.
8. **REJECTS:** third live camera for punches; CLAIM-TIGHT as type highlighter; buying all six blindly; reach×jab/cross; face inserts for type read; punch-truck; using HUD text (“JAB”) as the only type teach without silhouette.
9. **OPEN / INVENTED:** board silhouettes are **art invent** under M14 geometry (not mocap stills). Ranking is **visual judgment** at locked HOME, not a mocap vendor census. Optional single `straight` is art spend advice — J17 still listed jab+cross as types; M16 says only one is worth the HOME camera dollar.
10. Art only — no animation graph, no enums, no `.gd`.

## §1 — Read test (Q1)

**Camera:** M14 HOME locked. Small figure, elevated ¾, stand-off 9–11 m, shoot OVER near ropes. Boards invent silhouette poses at **mid-throw** and **impact** — not engine truth.

### Mid-throw (arm still travelling)

| Type | At HOME mid-throw | Blunt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **hook_rear** | Wide lateral arc; torso torque; arm clears silhouette envelope | **Distinguishes** — arc is readable at postage-stamp scale |
| **hook_lead** | Same arc family, opposite side / lead shoulder | **Distinguishes** as arc; lead vs rear is “which way the mass swings,” not glove ID |
| **uppercut** | Vertical lift through the pocket; elbow under | **Distinguishes** — vertical path ≠ lateral arc ≠ straight poke |
| **cross** | Long straight; rear hip if authored loud | **Marginal** — without loud rotation reads as “long punch” |
| **jab** | Shortest extension; least envelope change | **Collapses** — “he twitched an arm” |
| **jab vs cross** | Both straights on the same axis family | **Indistinguishable** at 10 m without shoulder/hip tell — blunt |

### Impact (contact frame)

| Type | At HOME impact | Blunt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **hook_*** | Glove at side of head/body + bent arm still arcing | Still readable as hook family if reaction cooperates |
| **uppercut** | Glove under chin / body + vertical residue | Readable if chin lifts / torso lifts with it |
| **cross / jab** | Glove on face on a straight line | **Both look like “a punch landed”** — type dies; reaction + HUD claim do the work |

**Which distinguish:** hooks (lead/rear as arc), uppercut.  
**Which collapse to “he threw a punch”:** jab, and jab↔cross pair. Cross alone only survives if rotation is a loud authored tell — do not bank on it.

## §3 — What makes a punch legible at distance? (Q3)

| Cue | Survives 10 m / −20°? | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Silhouette path** (arc / vertical / straight) | **YES — primary** | Hooks + uppercut win here |
| **Opponent reaction** | **YES — often louder than arm** | Head snap, torso fold, stumble, guard break |
| **Follow-through mass** | Partial | Wide finish helps hooks; jab has none |
| **Shoulder / hip rotation** | Marginal | Needed to save cross≠jab; easy to lose at stamp scale |
| **Glove shape / face / finger curl** | Dead | Faces deferred; 10 m kills micro |
| **HUD / claim chip** | Instrument, not picture | Must not be the only type teach |

**One-line (DECISION 3):** opponent **reaction out-reads the thrower’s arm** at HOME. Spend priority may flip toward reaction clips over fine punch taxonomy — compatible with J17’s “far fewer reaction SKUs” (few, but high-value) and M16’s camera fact.

## §5 — MMA lens (Q5)

Kicks and knees paint **larger silhouettes** (long limb arc, chamber, hip turn) than boxing straights. At the same HOME stand-off:

- Roundhouse / low kick / teep: **outrank** jab/cross for distance read.
- Knee: vertical/short but mass + clinch context — often clearer than a jab.
- Boxing hooks/uppercut still good; MMA adds limb families boxing never had.

**Argue:** buy **kick (and likely knee) footage for MMA** even if boxing punch taxonomy shrinks to 3. Ranking for an MMA ruleset home camera: **kick/knee → hook/uppercut → straight**. Do not refuse kick SKUs because boxing refused legs (J17 boxing bout drop of strike_leg) — different ruleset, different silhouette economy.
