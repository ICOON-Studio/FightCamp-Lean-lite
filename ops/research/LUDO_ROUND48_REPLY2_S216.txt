The Liver Shot Mechanism (boxing_body_hook to Target Left Flank)

A flush hit from boxing_body_hook landing on the defender’s right-hand anatomical side (the liver quadrant, hit from the attacker's lead left hook) bypasses the immediate head-snap mechanics. In real boxing, a liver shot triggers an involuntary autonomic response via the hepatic vagus plexus. The visual engine handles this with a two-stage sequence:

[MOMENT 0.00s: IMPACT]
  └── Play #87 (Receiving_A_Hit_To_The_Liver) at half-depth on upper body.
  └── Boxer's right elbow clamps tightly down against the floating ribs.
  └── Fighter freezes in place, trying to breathe; legs stiffen.
        │
        ▼  [DELAYED NEURAL LATENCY: 0.35s to 0.70s]
[MOMENT +0.45s: AUTONOMIC CRUMPLE]
  ├── IF chin / composure check SUCCEEDS:
  │     └── Stumble backward into defensive shell, playing #5 (Big_Rib_Hit__Receiving_A_Big_Hit_In_The_Ribs).
  │     └── Stamina regeneration drops to 0% for 6.0 seconds.
  │
  └── IF chin / composure check FAILS (KNOCKDOWN):
        └── Legs fold involuntarily under the body.
        └── Transition to #59 (Knocked_Down__Knocked_Down_To_Stomach) via delayed knee crumple.

The 10-Count Timeline
[0.0s: IMPACT] ──► Fall clip plays (#29, #59, #92, or #107).
[1.2s: CANVAS] ──► Fighter hits mat. Last frame blends into grounded pose.
                   Engine checks chin, conditioning, and composure to determine recovery count (4-10).
[1.2s - 5.5s]  ──► Lying prone on mat while referee counts from 1 to 5.
[6.0s: EFFORT] ──► Blend into get-up clip (#36, #37, #38, #39, #94, or #106) over 0.10s.
                   Fighter pushes to hands and knees, fighting for balance.
[8.2s: UPRIGHT]──► Fighter beats count at 8 or 9; transitions into #56 (Injured_Stumble_Idle).
                   Referee steps in, holds fighter's gloves, and checks their eyes.
[9.5s: RESUME] ──► IF Composure > 30%: transitions back to active stance.
                   IF Composure <= 30%: referee waves both arms, signaling a TKO stoppage.


For Clean Knockouts (#30, #60, #109), no get-up animation is queued. The boxer remains prone on the canvas while the referee steps across to signal the immediate knockout.

1. Clean Guard Absorption (Steady State)

When the attacker's power falls below the defender's effective block threshold, the fighter holds their defensive posture. The incoming blow is caught cleanly on the gloves or forearms:

High Guard vs Straight Head Shots: Plays #23 (Center Block) or #7 (Block__Blocking_The_Chest_Area_With_Both_Arms).
Lead Parries vs Straight Jabs: Plays #8 (Blocking__Blocking_Inward) or #57 (Inward_Block__Inward_Block).
Lateral Deflections vs Looping Hooks: Plays #9 (Blocking__Blocking_Outward), #62 (Left Block), or #75 (Outward_Block__Blocking_With_Arm).
Low Elbow Shells vs Body Shots: Plays #10 (Body_Block__Blocking_Body_With_Arms).
Visual Feel: Footwork continues without a stumble. A subtle camera shake and a small procedural offset to the forearm bones convey impact without breaking the fighter's balance.
2. Heavy Block Rattles (Guard Strain)

When a power strike (power > 0.65, such as boxing_overhand or boxing_rear_uppercut) connects with a high guard:

Triggers #96 (Standing_Block_React_Large__Block_Reaction).
The gloves are driven back into the forehead, forcing the fighter into a short backward reset step.
Guard integrity drops by 15% to 25%, leaving the defender open to fast follow-up combinations.
6. SKELETAL LAYERING, BLENDING & COMMON PITFALLS
Upper-Body Layering vs. Full-Body Animation

To avoid the stiff, wooden movement seen when fighters freeze in place to react, the engine splits animations across two distinct tiers:

[BONE HIERARCHY ROOT]
  ├── Hips / Legs / Feet   ──► LAYER 0: Master Locomotion (Tracks Ring Movement, Circling, Steps)
  └── Spine / Neck / Arms  ──► LAYER 1: Hit Reactions & Blocks (Blended via Godot AnimationTree)

For Light Hits (power < 0.45): Clip #42, #43, #48, #49, #50, #51, #52, #63, #64, #65, #102, #103, #104 plays exclusively on Layer 1 (from Spine1 upward). The fighter's hips, legs, and feet continue their existing circling or retreat patterns without hitching.
For Medium Hits, Big Hits, and Knockdowns: Full-body playback takes over, blending Layer 0 and Layer 1 together to show real momentum and weight displacement.

Avoiding Common Animation Pitfalls in Boxing Games
The "Feet Skating" Artifact: Occurs when a full-body hit reaction plays while the character is translating across the ring.
Solution: Light hits use upper-body bone masks only. Medium and heavy hits lock the translation vector of the root bone until the reaction completes.
Animation Cancellation Popping: Occurs when an incoming punch interrupts an ongoing reaction, snapping the skeleton abruptly.
Solution: Never snap back to an idle pose. Use an inertializer node in the Godot AnimationTree to carry forward existing bone momentum smoothly into the next hit reaction.
Floating Knockdown Transitions: Occurs when a ragdoll or fall clip doesn't match the elevation of the get-up animation.
Solution: Align the root bone of fall animations (#29, #59, #92, #107) so their final rest position matches the starting hips elevation of get-up clips (#36, #37, #38, #39, #94, #106) within a tight 0.10-second crossfade.
