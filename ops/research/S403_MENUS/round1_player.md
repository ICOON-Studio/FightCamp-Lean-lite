# S403 menus, round 1: the player's review

Reviewer: a long-time management/sports-game player who follows MMA and boxing, using these seven pages every week.
Read: CRITIC_PACK, CRITIC_ACCESS, MOCKUP_RULES, YARDSTICK, roster_mma8.json, and the seven PNGs. I judged tooltips only where the stills draw them open; I did not open the HTML title text or the real-game screens.

Scores: Home 7, Fighter Week 6.5, Profile 6.5, Playbook 6.5, Fight night 7, Money 6, World 6. None is an 8 yet.

## Faults that hit all seven pages (fix once, lifts every score)

1. The pages disagree about the world. Lucy's next fight is Priya Nair on Sat 22 Aug (Home), Maria Ortega on Sat 21 Mar (Week), Maria Ortega on Sat 31 Jan (World), and "Priya Ortega" (a mash of two fighters) on fight night; all say "19 days". The date is Mon 3 Aug on Home and Money, Mon 2 Mar on Week/Profile/Playbook, Mon 12 Jan on World. Cash is $14,820 on some pages and $16,841 on others; the weekly figure is +$303 on some and -$680 on others. Maria Ortega is injured and out 3 weeks on Home, yet she is Lucy's opponent in 19 days elsewhere. I cannot judge a weekly loop when every page tells a different week. Pick one save and one date and use it on all seven.
2. Top bar. NEEDS YOU (2) on Home but six notes on the board; (3) elsewhere; CONTINUE on Money. Three counts for one state. The red pill never says how I advance time while a need is open; say "opens the first need" on it.
3. Grey-with-triangle is a good convention, but the triangle is used for "not in this release" and also sits on things that look like "locked until you do X" (Home: Hear it, Talk, Set pads, Ask for a fight). Give "locked, here is why" a padlock and the reason in plain text, keep the triangle only for "not built".
4. Eight fighters do not fit a top bar. The needs text truncates at three names. Show a count and the first reason only.
5. Small text. Much grey microcopy (Home right column, Week sub-lines, Money notes) is about 11-12 px at 1080p. Check every page at the "Largest" text setting.

## 1. Home: 7/10

Needs as sticky notes with red/amber/green read instantly; the fighter table has one row each with a status icon, weight, freshness and this week's plan; "since last Monday" has coloured dots that separate good news from bad. Eight fighters fit without scrolling.

What I want to do and cannot.
- Act on a row without leaving it. Aisha is 2.6 kg over, cut diet, 26 days to go: I want a one-click weight plan on that row. Josh has "no fight booked" but Ask for a fight is greyed (planned: fine, but until live the row has no action).
- Sort or filter the roster. Fine at 8, not at 16: sort by next fight, weight problem, fatigue.
- See whether Lucy will be at peak on the fight date. "Fresh" now says nothing about 19 days out.

What I would never look at or press.
- Club-this-week block: four numbers with four unlabelled icons. I hover each once, then ignore. Show net and runway, and "Open the books".
- Coming up repeats what the notes and the table already say (Megan's offer ends, Danny's contract ends, Lucy v Priya). Keep only bills and dates shown nowhere else.
- The anvil crest under the rail does nothing visible.

Words vs icons.
- Good: the status icons in TODAY (calendar, camp, injury cross, offer envelope, contract, retire hourglass), each with words beside it. Keep both.
- Unclear: the bar colours. Amber on Aisha seems to mean over-weight, amber on Tomas tired. One colour must mean one thing; split weight and fatigue into two columns or label the bar.
- Note buttons (Open, Talk, Hear it) are right as words; they are decisions.

With the eight.
- Veteran (Kwame): "talks of retiring" works. Prospect (Danny): contract note works.
- The injured wrestler has a row but no note, while Money gives her a physio sticky. Be consistent: an injury with a decision is a need on Home.
- Seven of eight rows need something; a real gym will be mostly quiet. I cannot judge the quiet state; draw one.

Fixes to 8, most important first.
1. One consistent state across all pages (above), starting with Lucy's next fight.
2. One colour meaning per bar column; legend or split weight/fatigue.
3. Top-bar count and board agree; the board holds every red/amber need and says "+3 more" on overflow.
4. Row actions on hover: small plan-week / weight / ask-fight icons at the row end, greyed with reason when unavailable.

6. Label the four club-week icons with one word each, or reduce to net plus runway.

## 2. Fighter Week: 6.5/10

What works. The whiteboard week is right: seven mornings and seven evenings, each card showing the stat it adds, a load figure and a nightly fatigue number. The fatigue bar with the amber peak marker is the best single control on any page. The coach's one change with a Do it button and the twist sticky with two priced answers are good.

What I want to do and cannot.
- See the arc to fight night. "Fight in 12 days" and PEAK are there, but not a fatigue line across all 12 days with the peak and taper marked.
- Drag a session to another day. The cards look like magnets but the page says "click a day to change a session".
- Repeat this week next week, or save a template week. Without it week 20 is a chore.
- See what to train FOR. The opponent's one tell shows on Saturday; nothing on this page says "answer her takedowns: Guard 21". One line in the goal row would close the loop.
- Train the premise. Lucy is "a boxer learning the mat" and all five MMA session chips (kick bag, takedown drills, mat rounds, cage work, mobility) are greyed. Accepted as planned, but this page is only right when those five are live.

What I would never look at or press.
- The open tooltips are large and cover the day headers; they will cover live data in play. Make them small and anchor them to the icon.
- Light/Normal/Hard chips repeated on every card. Use the card edge colour.
- The "Evenings: Pads, Sparring, Film, Rest" line plus the grey "not built" line: two sentences for one fact.

Words vs icons.
- Good: stat icons (pulse, dumbbell, bolt, bulb, fist), each with its word.
- Bad: the battery with a number per session (+5, +6, +2) and "-9 night" under each day. I cannot tell fatigue added from energy gained. Use a signed fatigue number with an up arrow, one legend at the top, and delete "-9 night".
- Target icon for Pads and pencil for Sparring would not be guessed; keep the words.

With the eight.
- Grappler with no striking (Aisha): her week is mat work, flexibility and recovery. The tray shows boxing move cards; her mat cards are "not built". At release her page is empty unless mat sessions are first-class. This is the stress test and the page draws no answer.
- Veteran (Kwame): no room to grow means every card reads +0.00. He needs a maintain mode (sessions that hold a stat) and the page should lead with injury risk, not gains.
- Prospect (Danny): everything rises by +0.1. Lead with the one stat with the most headroom.
- Injured (Maria): needs a rehab week with a return date, not a normal week with a fighter who cannot spar.

Fixes to 8, most important first.
1. Fatigue line across all days to fight night, peak day marked, taper visible.
2. Replace the battery numbers with a signed fatigue number and one legend; delete "-9 night".
3. Draw an injured week and a veteran maintain week on this page (variants or tabs).
4. Drag sessions between days; keep click for load.
5. Repeat-to-next-week and save-as-template buttons.
6. Put the opponent's one thing to answer in the goal line.
7. Shrink the open tooltips and anchor them to their icon.

## 4. Playbook: 6.5/10

What works. The plan as "where the fight goes" lines with the best answer rated Beats it / Works is very readable. The blackboard preview is the fun part the owner likes. The rate-against row (walks you down, boxes at range, waits to counter...) is great. The move book grouped hands, kicks, knees and elbows, takedowns, submissions, ground makes sense.

What I want to do and cannot.
- Drag a move from the book onto a line. Today it is Change and Clear on every line: ten buttons for five lines.
- Tell the four top buttons apart: Test it, See the flow, Open the map, Load a real plan. I cannot guess which does what.
- Write a full fight. Cage and ground lines are greyed (planned), so Megan's plan is stand-up only. Accepted.
- Tell "locked" from "not built". "On top / On the bottom: no move at Usable" (a lock) and "Not built yet: the cage" (a triangle) are the same grey, differing by a small icon. Make the lock a visibly different state.

What I would never press.
- Test it / See the flow / Open the map as three equal outline buttons: I press one once and never learn the others.
- Watch, Steps, Drill, Replay, Slow motion: five buttons; Watch and Slow motion are enough.
- The Rounds row (nine chips for R1-R3 Push/Steady/Save) is a good setting in a bad shape.

Words vs icons.
- Move chips read "JAB: USABLE" in caps, repeating move and level. Use colour for level and the move name only; level on hover.
- Wrong icons: dumbbell for takedowns (says strength), padlock for submissions (collides with locked), scales for ground. Pick distinct glyphs.
- Triangle on locked groups: consistent, good.

With the eight.
- Megan (Muay Thai, shown): works; five of six lines filled.
- BJJ specialist (Aisha): her natural lines are On top and On the bottom, both locked or not built. Her page is nearly all grey; MMA cannot ship from this page until ground lines are live.
- Tomas (one big shot): fine, two or three lines make a plan; say so, so empty lines do not feel like failure.
- Veteran: a wall of gold Signature chips; fine.
- Prospect (Danny, all Raw): the worst case. Every line is locked ("only New"), so he cannot write any plan. Allow a Raw move on a line with a "weak, will fail often" tag, and show at what level each line unlocks.

Fixes to 8, most important first.
1. Drag a move onto a line; keep Change as fallback.
2. One "Test" menu, or icons plus a sentence under each of the three buttons.
3. Allow Raw moves on a line with a weakness tag so a prospect can write a plan.
4. Distinct icons for takedowns, submissions, ground; a different grey for lock vs not built.
5. Rounds as a three-column grid with the chosen pace big.
6. Move chips: name only, level by colour.

## 5. Fight night: 7/10

What works. The dark corner with handwritten coach notes has the right mood. Round in numbers (landed 24 of 58 vs 9 of 21, takedowns, control time) with red/blue bars is the panel a fight fan wants. The corner minute has a good structure (habit fix, plan call, recover, set-up, talk, cutman, shouts) and prices every choice ("-0.5 power, tiring, riskier"). The result with three judges and the Why on cream paper is the hook and it works.

What I want to do and cannot.
- See the shape of the fight: who won each round, energy by round. The only cues are a single "62" and "10-9 on your card".
- See damage by place (head, body, legs, cuts) during the minute. The cutman block appears only when an eye closes; ribs are in the result only.
- See the adapted line without reading two places (the sticky and the plan row).

What I would never look at or press.
- "Two instructions get through this minute: 2 left" and "Every 30 s: shout 3 calls or say nothing": a rule explained in a sentence every minute. Use two pips.
- The adapted-note footer ("Replaces the coach's note from round 2. The line that stopped working is pre-marked below.") is design commentary; cut it before ship.
- The result says "Split card" while all three judges score Priya 28-29: that is unanimous. Label is wrong.
- The greyed On the floor block takes a third of the result panel for three numbers.

Words vs icons.
- Good: strike, takedown and clock icons; water drop and snowflake on Recover.
- Bad: the bullseye in the middle of the numbers panel and the lightning chip "62" by Lucy's name are unlabelled. Say energy.
- Talk and cutman choices stay words; they are priced decisions.

With the eight.
- Boxer vs wrestler (shown): ground and takedown rows are greyed, so what the wrestler does best is missing. Accepted as planned; at release those rows are the story.
- Tomas (gone by round 3): needs an energy curve across rounds; one number cannot show fading, and fading is his whole life.
- Kwame (chin going): damage by place must show every minute.
- Aisha (grappler): her strikes are tiny and control time is everything; the panel handles it, but those rows are the greyed ones.

Fixes to 8, most important first.
1. A round-by-round track (winner of each round, energy per round) above the numbers.
2. Damage by place in the corner minute with the hurt place highlighted.
3. Fix the unanimous/split label; delete on-page design commentary.
4. Two pips for the two-instruction rule.
5. Label the lightning chip and the bullseye.
6. Collapse On the floor to one strip until built.

## 6. Money: 6/10

What works. Books on a whiteboard works: cash curve, in and out with bars, and "each week the tin grows +$303" as the one line I care about. The right-hand stickies carry real decisions with exact numbers (contract talk $2,500 bonus, physio $75). Dues and kit on chalkboards are a good touch.

What I want to do and cannot.
- Know what to buy next. The price tags are an unordered grid. The green sticky "68 of 70: the place is nearly full" is the real signal; tags should sort by the current problem (full gym: bigger unit first).
- See payback. "Strength corner $3,600: +$22 a week" is there; weeks to pay back (about 160) is not.
- See per-fighter money: what each of the eight costs and returns after stipend and share. Only stipends appear, and the page says six of eight "take a share" with no figure.
- See a forecast: cash in 4 and 8 weeks with known bills. Only the past 12 weeks show.
- Give sponsors weight. They are a footer strip with a tiny sign link, yet they are income.

What I would never look at or press.
- The dark red CASHBOOK box looks like a warning and is a log link.
- The handwritten "Dues alone cover the costs..." paragraph: long, slow to read.
- The pins on the tags (red, grey, green) carry meaning with no legend.

Words vs icons.
- Good: premises, strength, youth, coach, show and cage icons; runway tooltip.
- Bad: price lines at 10-11 px. Price is the decision; make it the biggest text and the effect one line.
- Cheap / Standard / Premium stay words.

With the eight.
- The page serves the gym, not the roster. The eight differ most in cost (a hard-cutting heavyweight, a flyweight in camp, a stipend prospect, a veteran wanting a bigger share), yet only two appear, as stickies. Six cost nothing here, which cannot be right.
- No MMA costs (licence, medicals, cage-side doctor). Pro MMA has a larger bill; add it to one-offs.
- Cage tag "Price not set": honest, fine.

Fixes to 8, most important first.
1. Sort or highlight tags by the current problem; show weeks to pay back.
2. A per-fighter money line: cost and return, with stipend and share.
3. A cash forecast at +4 and +8 weeks including known bills.
4. Pin legend, or replace pins with card-edge colour.
5. Price as the biggest text on each tag; cut the paragraph to one line.
6. Sponsors beside the income bars, with terms.
