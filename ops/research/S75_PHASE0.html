Product sheet

Recommended by Claude; it becomes the product when you sign it off. Until then every stage is judged against it anyway, so scope stops growing by default.

Three pillars

The fight is real

Every bout is simulated from sourced fight statistics (J20, >7,000 UFC bouts; CompuBox). Watch it in the 3D arena or sim it to the bell - same engine, same result.

You run the gym

You are the coach and manager, not the fighter: sign, train, pick the fight, run the camp, call the corner, split the purse.

It tells you why

A win or loss is explained on screen - the corner's read between rounds, the result's "why", the stats that back it.

The sheet

One line

The most authentic fight-night management sim: run a combat-sports gym from a debutant's first club show to a title.

Target player

Management-sim players who follow combat sports: Football Manager, OOTP, Motorsport Manager, Punch Club, World Championship Boxing Manager. PC, plays in long sessions, reads menus, wants the numbers to be true. ESTIMATE

Platform

Steam, Windows 10/11, keyboard and mouse, 1080p. Godot 4.7.2, Forward+ (Vulkan). Minimum spec set in phase 4 from a low-end test machine.

Price

Early Access $19.99; 1.0 at $24.99. Sim buyers pay for depth; the comparables sit at $15-30. ESTIMATE

v1 scope

Stand-up sports: boxing, kickboxing, Muay Thai. Amateur club shows through pro titles; the career loop, the 3D arena, SIM THE FIGHT, corner calls between rounds.

First major update: MMA and grappling, once paired ground motion passes its own gate judged from the game camera.

Frozen behind a switch: the other rulesets (BJJ, judo, wrestling, sambo, karate, taekwondo, ...). No new rulesets, techniques or research topics outside the current phase.

Roster

Fictional athletes, gyms, promotions and belts by default - built in S74. Real names only through a player-installed swap file; the studio never publishes one.

Options

Mic corner calls with crowd noise: a settings option, off by default, keyboard fallback always there (D18). A selling point only if it works perfectly - it ships when it does.

Wishlist target

10,000 wishlists before the Early Access date is fixed. Tracked weekly from the Steam page. ESTIMATE - revisit with phase 3 data

Not in v1

Licensed athletes or leagues, multiplayer, consoles, a fighter-controlled action mode, localisation beyond English hooks.

Phase 0 gate
You sign off this sheet and the v1 scope. Changing any line is your call; say which in chat.
