1. WHAT EACH KEY ITEM SWITCHES ON

To respect the design philosophy of deep management titles (Mad Games Tycoon 2, Two Point Hospital, Football Manager), an empty room is merely designated dry-wall. A room only transitions from PLANNED to FUNCTIONAL when its single Key Item is placed inside its perimeter with valid pathfinding clearance to its interaction node.

Below are the operational rules, unlock conditions, and the zero-minute onboarding fail-safes for each of the four inactive key items.

│      EMPTY ROOM       │  ────────────────────────────> │     FUNCTIONAL ROOM    │
│  • Upkeep: $0/wk      │    (Path cleared to door)      │  • Upkeep active       │

A. The Office: Management PC Desk ($750)
Without It (What the Player Loses):
External Digital Comms are severed. The player cannot receive professional fight purse offers, cannot review sanctioning body rankings, cannot post job listings for secondary staff (assistant coaches, cutmen, conditioning scouts), and cannot view the detailed multi-tab financial balance sheet (only raw cash in the top bar is visible).
With It (What It Switches On):
Unlocks the Inbox / Communications Drawer, the Bout Contract Negotiation Screen, and the Staff Marketplace.
Enables passive generation of Gym Prestige Points based on the office occupant's Fight IQ / Business stat.
Rule (Learnable in One Click):

"No Desk = No Contracts. Placing the Management PC Desk connects your gym to promoters, sanctioning bodies, and hiring agencies."

Benchmark: Mad Games Tycoon 2 (Dev Studio cannot code without a Dev Desk; Office cannot take publisher contracts without a Management Desk).
B. The Locker Room: 4-Tier Metal Locker ($620)
Without It (What the Player Loses):
The Paying Member Hard-Cap is clamped to ZERO. Even if the front desk is open, civilian members will turn around at the threshold and leave with the status banner: "No secure storage / Nowhere to change."
Amateur and pro fighters suffer an automatic $-15$ Composure penalty starting any training lap because they must change on the training floor or floor mats.
With It (What It Switches On):
Unlocks the Member Capacity Pool (the first bank creates space for $12$ active paying members).
Restores fighter baseline Composure to normal ($100%$ efficiency) upon entering the training floor.
Rule (Learnable in One Click):

"No Lockers = No Members. Each 4-Tier Locker bank allows 12 civilian members to subscribe and pay weekly dues."

Benchmark: Two Point Hospital (Staff room without seating/lockers cannot restore energy; guests leave immediately if basic amenities are absent).
C. The Physio Room: Treatment Table ($1,400)

(Room design unlocks when 2 active fighters are under contract.)

Without It (What the Player Loses):
In-house medical treatment is impossible. Any soft-tissue injury (sprained wrists, torn cartilage, cut brow reopening) must be referred to the city hospital, which costs $$450$ flat per visit and benches the fighter for $100%$ of the natural medical duration (e.g., $14$ days).
Weight-cut rehydration recovery on Saturday morning is capped at $80%$.
With It (What It Switches On):
Unlocks In-House Physio Diagnostics and Direct Treatment.
Cuts injury bench time by $-35%$ (reduced to $-50%$ if an assistant coach with the Medic trait is assigned).
Allows your fighter to treat a Cut-Prone Scar between camps to prevent it from progressing to the next severity tier.
Rule (Learnable in One Click):

"No Table = Hospital Bills. The Treatment Table cuts fighter injury recovery time by 35% and eliminates external clinic referral fees."

Benchmark: Two Point Hospital (Diagnosis rooms are dead floor space until the core console/table is placed; patients queue indefinitely or die).
D. Equipment Storage: Heavy Duty Shelving ($380)

(Room design unlocks at the first mechanical breakdown of a training station.)

Without It (What the Player Loses):
No spare parts inventory can be stored. When a treadmill belt tears, a cable snaps, or a heavy bag bracket bends, the station enters the BROKEN state. Without stored parts, an external technician must be dispatched ($3\text{ days}$ downtime, $$220$ emergency call-out fee).
Conditioning coaches lose $20%$ drill efficiency because mitts, jump ropes, and medicine balls are scattered across the training floor.
With It (What It Switches On):
Unlocks the Auto-Maintenance System. Spare cables, chains, leather laces, and chalk are stocked automatically at wholesale cost.
Broken machines are repaired by gym staff within $2$ in-game hours at zero call-out fee.
Rule (Learnable in One Click):

"No Shelving = 3-Day Equipment Breakdowns. Shelving keeps replacement parts in stock, enabling instant station repairs."

Benchmark: Mad Games Tycoon 2 (Server Room / Production requires rack space to hold media inventory and network blades; without it, games cannot be pressed or patched).
E. Minute-Zero Gate Fail-Safe: Preventing the New Player Soft-Lock

Question: Should any key item gate something the player uses at minute 0 (scouting, basic fight booking, initial training)? If yes, how do we stop them from being stuck?

The Problem: If the Management PC Desk is required to book bouts, and a player spends all $$25,000$ on heavy bags and a ring without building an office, they cannot book Leo Vance’s first bout to earn purse money. They are dead in the water.

The Solution (The "Kitchen Table / Corkboard" Fallback):

Minute-0 Mobile Phone / Clipboard (Emergency Fallback):
If no office desk is built, the player-coach operates from a free Clipboard & Cell Phone on the edge of the ring.
The Penalty: Without a PC Desk, the player only receives Low-Ball / Late-Notice Bouts (promoters take a $40%$ cut of the purse, and bout notices arrive with only 7 days of camp instead of 21 days). Scouting reports have a $\pm 25%$ error margin on opponent attributes.
The UI displays a persistent amber notification badge over the Mail icon:
[!] RUNNING FROM CLIPBOARD: Promoters offering low-tier purses only. Build an Office with a PC Desk to access direct sanctioned contracts.
The Onboarding Script (First-Run Prompt):
The demo tutorial forces the construction of:
Training Zone (Bag + Mats)
Front Desk ($800)
Locker Bank ($620)
Office PC Desk ($750)
These core items consume exactly $$2,170$, leaving over $$22,800$ for the rest of the gym. A player cannot accidentally soft-lock unless they intentionally demolish the starter layout.

A. Lockers (Linear Pool with Space Saturation)
Item: 4-Tier Metal Locker ($620).
Capacity Formula:
Bank 1: $+12$ Member Capacity ($100%$ efficiency).
Bank 2: $+12$ Member Capacity ($100%$ efficiency).
Bank 3: $+12$ Member Capacity ($100%$ efficiency).
Bank 4+: $+8$ Member Capacity ($-33%$ efficiency; crowded locker rooms create diminishing utility).
Hard Cap: Maximum of $6$ locker banks ($64$ total member cap) per $30\text{ m}^2$ of Locker Room floor space. Beyond this density, members refuse to join due to overcrowding.
B. Treatment Tables (Physio Staff Gating)
Item: Treatment Table ($1,400).
Mechanic: A table has zero autonomous healing power; it requires a physical medical worker.
Table 1: Supported by your base player-coach or assigned cutman. Provides $100%$ of base injury recovery buffs.
Table 2: Requires hiring a dedicated Staff Physio ($350/wk wage). Without an unassigned staff member, Table 2 provides $0%$ benefit and incurs a $$15/wk$ cleaning upkeep cost.
Table 3+: Unlocks multi-fighter simultaneous post-sparring treatment. However, injury recovery speed gains drop by $50%$ on the third table due to shared medical focus.
C. Management PC Desks (Staff Expansion)
Item: Management PC Desk ($750).
Mechanic:
Desk 1: Occupied by the Head Coach. Unlocks core contracts, emails, and staff marketplace ($100%$).
Desk 2: Occupied by an Assistant Coach / General Manager. Automatically handles routine admin, boosting member renewal retention by $+10%$ and unlocking automatic fight-offer counter-negotiations ($+15%$ purse bids).
Desk 3+: Diminishing returns drop to zero practical benefit until the gym reaches $5+$ contracted professional fighters.
D. Storage Shelving (Inventory Capacity)
Item: Heavy Duty Shelving ($380).
Mechanic:
Shelf 1: Holds up to $4$ spare part kits (handles $1$ breakdown event every $2$ weeks).

5. THE HEAVY BAG MOUNT: REAL GYM HARDWARE INTEGRATION

In response to the current issue where a newly bought heavy bag floats mid-floor suspended from nothing:

    │  [FRAME] │                         ║──[BRACKET]                           │ [SLIDER]
    │    │     │                         ║     │                                │

The Solution: We Sell Three Distinct Bag Configurations

To support realistic gym progression and layout problem-solving, the store offers three clear mounting options:

Option A: The Free-Standing Heavy Bag Station ($950)
Mounting: Built-in four-leg triangular steel chassis with bottom ballast weight plates.
Footprint: $2.2\text{m} \times 2.2\text{m}$ (Large spatial footprint; cannot be placed within $0.8\text{m}$ of any other station).
Flexibility: Can stand anywhere on the open floor. Does not require walls or beams.
Downside: Expensive; takes up massive floor area; swings less naturally (slight technique penalty of $-5%$).
Option B: Wall-Mounted Heavy Bag Arm ($550)
Mounting: Industrial triangulation bracket anchored directly into structural brick/concrete walls.
Footprint: $1.4\text{m} \text{ wide} \times 1.6\text{m} \text{ projection from wall}$.
Placement Restriction: Must snap directly to a structural boundary wall. Cannot be placed in the room interior.
Downside: Locks bag work to the perimeter; fighters cannot circle $360^\circ$ around the bag (cannot practice inside angle-stepping drills).
Option C: Overhead Beam-Clamp Assembly ($350 + Bag = $750 Total)
Mounting: Clamps directly to the gym’s steel ceiling I-beams (present in the industrial club hall architecture).
Footprint: $1.8\text{m} \times 1.8\text{m}$ work envelope around the dangling bag.
Placement Restriction: Can only be placed along structural ceiling beam lines (visualized on the floor as subtle dashed yellow grid lines in the Godot build mode).
Upside: Best price-to-performance ratio; allows full $360^\circ$ movement and natural swing physics.
Speed Bag and Double-End Bag Rules:
Speed Bag ($450): Strictly Wall-Mounted or Beam-Pod Mounted. Must snap to a wall or a structural beam strut. Footprint: $1.2\text{m} \times 1.2\text{m}$ (including the fighter's standing work zone).
Double-End Bag ($380): Requires both a floor anchor and a ceiling anchor. Can be placed anywhere on the open floor, but requires a $1.6\text{m} \times 1.6\text{m}$ clear safety circle so snapping elastic bands do not strike passing members.
