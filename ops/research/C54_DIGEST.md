# C54 digest

- Federation pathways are not a universal income stream. Olympic Solidarity,
  WT scholarships, UWW participation assistance, military employment, and
  event prizes are selective, conditional, and often team/NOC/NF-routed.
- Published fees exist, but incidence varies: UWW publishes age/event fees;
  FIAS publishes licences and delegation costs; IBJJF publishes membership and
  event prices; IWUF publishes event-condition fees; WT/IJF ordinary athlete
  fee amounts remain UNKNOWN here.
- World-level prize money is event-specific: FIAS 2025 publishes USD 4,000 /
  3,000 / 2,000; IWUF 2025 Sanda publishes RMB 10,000 / 6,000 / 3,000; IJF's
  €500,000 table is the 2017 Open Worlds only; other cited UWW/WT/IBJJF figures
  are not general World Championship rates.
- Recommendation: keep `national_selection` as an evidence-gated pure-cost
  default. Debit only a sourced fee with known payer incidence; book only a
  sourced event award or grant with known amount, event, payer, and recipient.
  Do not invent a recurring stipend or turn one payment into a rate.
- “No purse, ever” survives: a stipend/grant or placing award is distinct from
  consideration for a professional bout and must not be routed through
  `purse_cents` without a separate sourced rule.

## Non-decisions

- No universal stipend, club salary, private-sponsorship rate, police programme,
  or expected net balance was established.
- No current six-discipline prize-money scale was authored from a single event;
  no historical figure was generalised into a career or season rate.
- No payer entity, recurring-income schema, ledger mutation, federation field,
  or code change is approved by this research record.
- No conclusion was reached that every event prize preserves amateur
  eligibility; that remains event- and rule-specific UNKNOWN.
