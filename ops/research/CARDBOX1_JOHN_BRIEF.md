# CARDBOX1 - John: three real boxing bouts, round by round, with sources (research only)

We are rebuilding a boxing card game from how real bouts go (boxer against boxer only). Another seat is writing the
card logic; your job is the FACTS it will be checked against. Use the web; give a source link for each bout.

Pick three well-documented professional bouts: (a) an out-boxer against a pressure fighter, (b) a bout that ends by
stoppage after a turn-around, (c) a close decision. For each:
1. The two styles in two lines.
2. ROUND BY ROUND: who won the round on the official cards (all three judges if published), punches thrown and landed
   (jabs and power punches, CompuBox or the broadcaster's count) for each man, body punches where counted, knockdowns
   and the count, clinches or warnings worth a line, and the one thing that decided the round.
3. What each corner changed between rounds, where reported.
4. How it ended and the final cards.
Then, across the three bouts plus anything published on larger samples (name it): the share of pro bouts that go to a
decision by weight band; the usual share of jabs among punches thrown; the usual landed share of jabs and of power
punches; the usual share of body punches; how often the man who wins a round out-lands the other; how many clinches a
round. Say "not found" where you could not find a number - never estimate without saying so.
End with: the ten things that most decide a professional boxing round, most important first, each in one line.
