# J_DUES3: what pays for a cheap community boxing club
Every row was checked on 2026-11-10. Labels: OFFICIAL / MEASURED / DERIVED / ESTIMATE / OPINION / no source found. (?) means I saw it only in a search snippet. Game $ = GBP × 1.25. Amounts in US dollars are real USD, not game $.

**Headline.** Dues don't pay for real cheap clubs. In the accounts of three real English ABCs, subs cover 18–67% of income. The rest comes from grants (£10k–£55k a year in good years), home shows (£0.8k–£11.5k net) and donations. Coaching is free: none of these clubs paid a coach. In 2025/26 Sport England alone made 67 small awards to boxing organisations (median £9,875). That's about 6% of England Boxing's 1,084 clubs in a year (DERIVED).

Scale check (DERIVED): premium minus cheap = $240 per member per year. Carlisle's non-dues money ($46,154) covers that gap for about 192 members, St Mary's ($6,095) for about 25, and Redhill's grants alone ($68,318) for about 285.

**OPINION (a game rule, not a finding):** let the cheap band unlock a yearly grant roll. Start it at about a 6% chance per funder round, raised by flags for a deprived area, juniors, a constitution and committee, and a safeguarding officer. Payouts would sit in the bands above. Coaches at $0 wages come only from volunteers, members who grow into coaches. Add a home-show income of $1k–$14k a year that grows with the competitive squad. Premium keeps its higher dues but can't get grants and must pay its coaches.
