## 1. Real terms

Mark STATUTORY or CUSTOMARY. NOT FOUND where no primary.

### Term length

| Jurisdiction | Rule | Kind |
|---|---|---|
| NV | Max 4 years. Commission will not honor more. Form: "MAXIMUM OF 4 YEARS, with no extension option." | STATUTORY — NAC 467.102(4); NSAC boxer/manager form notes. ⁠Law.cornell |
| CA | "No contract shall be approved … for a period exceeding five years. No option to extend the initial period." Filed CSAC forms in later arbitration used 5-year terms. | STATUTORY — 4 CCR 222. ⁠Carules.elaws |
| NJ | Max 5 years, unless the manager releases the boxer or the Commissioner voids it. | STATUTORY — N.J.A.C. 13:46-5.6(a). ⁠Law.cornell |
| NY | Max 3 years, unless the Commission authorizes more. First-time pairs may cancel without penalty by written notice before month 11. | STATUTORY — 19 NYCRR 209.4. ⁠Law.cornell |
| MMA (no commission overlay) | Reported 1–3 years, sometimes "2 years or 8 fights, whichever is longer," sometimes 3 + auto-renew. | CUSTOMARY — lawyer summaries and published contract excerpts. Not a rule. ⁠Mixedmartialarts |

England/BIBA (not a game jurisdiction): max 3 years, commission cap 25%. CUSTOMARY/foreign regulator — do not import as a US default. ⁠Boxbiba

### Renewal
- NV: no extension option on the filed form. STATUTORY (form + NAC 467.102(4)).
- CA: no option to extend the initial period. STATUTORY (4 CCR 222).
- NJ: "No boxer-manager contracts … may be renewable at the exclusive option of the manager." STATUTORY — 13:46-5.6(b). ⁠Law.cornell
- NY: first-year walk-away on first pairing; otherwise a new filing at term end. STATUTORY — 209.4.
- MMA private forms: auto-renew and injury-pause extensions appear in published excerpts. CUSTOMARY / fighter-unfriendly. Do not author as the game default. ⁠Mixedmartialarts

A new term after expiry is a new filing, not a silent roll.

### Exclusivity
- Manager may not negotiate or sign for a combatant not under contract. Combatant with no filed manager signs the bout and receipts the purse themselves. STATUTORY — NAC 467.102(5); 4 CCR 221(b). ⁠Law.cornell
- Manager may not bind the fighter to a bout after the management term. STATUTORY — NAC 467.102(2); 4 CCR 226; 19 NYCRR 209.4 / 209.7. ⁠Carules.elaws
- MMA private forms: "sole and exclusive worldwide personal representative" is standard lawyer language. CUSTOMARY. ⁠Bbbusinesslaw
- Promoter-as-manager on the same card is separately forbidden (J4 E8). Do not merge desks.

### What the manager provides
STATUTORY (NV form §4): "use their best efforts to secure remunerative contests and exhibitions." That is the duty on the filed paper. Travel-with-manager language exists on the same form. ⁠Boxing.nv

STATUTORY (NY 209.8): the filed contract must state duration and the purse split. Licence loss auto-terminates; suspension unbinds the other party for the suspension. ⁠Nyrules.elaws

NOT FOUND as a statute: manager must pay camp, sparring, rent, visas, or travel. CSAC arbitration has ordered managers to pay half of training fees / rent / vitamins in a specific dispute — that is an award, not a rule. ⁠Dca.ca

CUSTOMARY (MMA journalism / counsel pages): bookings, bout negotiation, medicals, visas, travel, sponsorships, brand. Percent often claimed on whatever income the paper names (purse only vs purse+bonus+sponsors). Not a commission table. ⁠Fightomic

J4 E13 already forbids a 13-row camp-cost table. Keep it.

### Termination and buyout
STATUTORY ends:
- Term expiry.
- Mutual release.
- Manager licence revoked / not renewed (CA 4 CCR 221(a): ≥60-day suspension voids contracts on day 30; shorter suspension → boxer may void. NJ 13:46-6.3: unrenewed/revoked licence → contract need not be recognized. NY 209.8: auto-terminate on revoke or manager non-renewal within 30 days).
- Permanent NV manager suspension: contestant may terminate for Nevada contests. NSAC form §7. ⁠Boxing.nv

CUSTOMARY / case, not a formula:
- CSAC will terminate a live contract "in the best interest of boxing" and still order the filed % through the original end date (Cruz/Espinoza: contract broken; 33⅓% still owed through July 2011). That is a trailing-share remedy, not a published buyout price. ⁠Dca.ca
- MMA: split is a civil fight at the fighter's expense. No published statutory buyout schedule. ⁠Fightomic

Buyout dollar / week-multiplier: NOT FOUND. Do not author one.

### Share when the fighter changes gym
The filed object is manager ↔ combatant, not gym ↔ combatant.
- Assignment of either side's interest needs commission approval (NV NAC 467.102(7); CA 4 CCR 221(b); NV form §19). Changing the training gym does not, by itself, assign or kill the manager share. STATUTORY.
- A gym that is the filed manager and then loses the fighter as a trainee still collects the filed share until term / release / licence loss. That is the point of the paper.
- New gym taking ≥10% of purse is itself a manager and needs its own filed contract (E3 / E24). Two managers cannot stack past the cap: NV/CA/NJ cap is on managers separately or collectively.

What a new gym owes the old manager: NOT FOUND as a statute. Trailing share through term is the only evidenced pattern (CSAC awards).

## 2. Why a fighter signs — or refuses

Published relationship between quality and share: NOT FOUND. No commission series, no regression, no org table. Anecdotes only.

What the record actually shows:
- **Caps are not the deal.** Filed CSAC contracts in public awards used 18%, and J4 already logged 16⅔ / 18 / 25 / 30 — all under the 33⅓ ceiling. One CA prospect fight had the father arguing 10% and the boxer 20% against a manager holding 33⅓. The number is bargained; the statute is the lid. ⁠Storage.courtlistener
- **Career stage, inverted anecdotes.** Prospects accept higher % because they cannot book themselves (Centeno family vs 33⅓). Veterans publicly push the % down: Jones 2019 — "no manager should ever be getting 20%… 10%, maybe 15 if they are really getting it done." O'Malley 2023 attacked 20% of purse plus 20% of bonuses. That is speech, not a market curve. ⁠Mmamania
- **Who pays camp.** Perez/Vital CSAC: boxer signed 33% believing managers would cover food, rent, vitamins, medicals; they did not. The award made managers pick up half the training fees and rent. Acceptance often rides on cost-shift, not on the printed %. ⁠Dca.ca
- **What the % attaches to.** MMA counsel pages: purse-only vs purse+bonuses vs "all fight and non-fight gross." Fighters refuse (or later explode) when the paper eats win bonuses, discretionary bonuses, and endorsements. CUSTOMARY. ⁠Mixedmartialarts
- **Record / access.** Firms sell connections and "we get you signed." Monte Cox: early 20% covered travel when purses were $200–$300; they left it at 20% as purses grew because the work grew. Anecdote, not a formula. ⁠YouTube
- **Self-management is real.** NAC 467.102(5) / 4 CCR 221(b): no manager → fighter signs and receipts the full purse. Jones / O'Malley-style walk-offs are the live version of that rule.

Do not ship: quality → % lookup. The game already has market value, wage-vs-market, and loyalty. Use those as acceptance pressure, not as an authored percentage.

## 4. The minimum contract for FightCamp

FightContract.payee_split(gross, expenses, manager_contract) already reads:
- empty manager_contract → filed false, share_percent 0, gym 0 (E23)
- jurisdiction, share_percent
- cap from manager_share_caps.json (E21: no evidence field → ignored; untagged → strictest cap 33⅓)
- NY floor: combatant keeps ≥50% after manager share and uncovered travel/hotel

That is the settlement seam. Do not add a second one.

### Smallest field set

| Field | What reads it | Why it exists |

| filed (non-empty dict) | payee_split; AthletePurseRead._manager_line (manager_filed / manager_share) | E23. Empty = 0. |
| share_percent | payee_split (clamped to cap / NY floor) | The bargained number. Never pre-filled with 10/15/20/33. |
| jurisdiction | cap_for() | Picks NV/CA/NJ cap or NY floor. Blank → strictest cap, not an invented mid. |
| expires_week | renewal prompt; booking refusal after expiry | STATUTORY term. Honor NV 4y / CA·NJ 5y / NY 3y as maxima, not as the offered length. |
| manager_licensed (bool) | booking + share collectability (E7, E9) | Flag only. No exam career. False → cannot file, cannot collect. |
| athlete_id | roster bind | One combatant per paper. |

Optional later, not minimum: camp_costs_covered as a yes/no the acceptance check can see. No dollar table (E13).

### Who proposes, who answers — with no default %
Player types a share. Cap clamp is silent and statutory.

Fighter ask is not a stored house rate. Derive a reservation from live fields only:
- **Market value vs weekly wage.** Wage already paid by the gym. A fighter whose wage is at or above market treats the share as extra tax → reservation moves down. A fighter whose wage is below market will tolerate a higher share if the gym is already carrying them.
- **Loyalty / time-on-roster.** Higher loyalty lowers the reservation (will grant more). Low loyalty or a recent poach attempt raises it.
- **Self-file option.** The statute already lets them sign alone. If loyalty is low and wage ≥ market, they refuse any share and stay E23 = 0.
- **Licence flag.** If the gym is not licensed, there is no offer to accept.

Answer space: accept / counter (their reservation) / refuse (stay 0). One counter, then done. Do not invent ACCEPT_RATIO constants here — that is a later build pass.

## 5. The trap: wage and purse share on the same fighter

Is it real? Yes, but they are different papers, and the honest jurisdictions keep them apart.
- Manager share is a commission on ring earnings, filed with the athletic commission, capped, paid on the bout. Promoter → contestant → manager. That is E1.
- Weekly wage is a private employment (or stipend) contract between gym and athlete. Commissions do not publish a gym-wage table (J4 E3: monthly dues / training fees are private, unpublished). UFC-side fighters are treated as independent contractors; no 2026 order reclassifies them (J4 E14). A US gym paying a salary plus taking a filed manager share is a gym that is also the manager, not a second purse tax.
- Where it actually happens: prospect/stipend culture (gym covers rent/food/camp, later files as manager); UK/EU amateur-to-pro gym retainers (outside our four commissions); the Perez CSAC file, where the boxer thought the 33% bought the living costs. The fight in that room was exactly this trap: wage-shaped expectations sitting on a purse-share paper. ⁠Dca.ca
- What is not real as one blended number: "gym takes 15% and also pays $400/week, netted into one purse line." Commissions receipt the purse to the combatant, then cut the filed manager. The wage never touches the bout form.

Screen rule. Two rows, two verbs, two clocks:

| Row | Verb | Clock | Ledger |
|---|---|---|---|
| Employment | gym pays fighter | 26-week term, weekly debit from gym.cash | operating cost |
| Manager contract | fighter pays gym | NV≤4y / CA·NJ≤5y / NY≤3y; collected only on bout settlement | payee_split.gym |

Never net them. Settlement block:
- gross (show + win + perf + gate)
- − uncovered travel/hotel (fighter expense, E5)
- − filed manager share (gym credit, or 0)
- = athlete wallet

Wage does not appear on that block. Manager share does not appear on the weekly payroll.

If the player files a share ≥10% (NV/NJ) or >10% (CA), flip manager_licensed or refuse the file (E3, E7). That is the only coupling.
