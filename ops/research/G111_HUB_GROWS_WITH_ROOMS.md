## What would a human do?
Paint one finished gym that already contains empty architecture: a corridor of closed doors, a dark annex pad, a boarded
wing. New rooms flip a door, light a window or swap a small overlay; the frame is not redrawn. Live counts stay on the
four public objects; extra rooms hide behind one "back of house" pin so the picture never fills with plaques. JUDGEMENT.

## 1. How other games show growth
- **Game Dev Tycoon** - a new picture per office tier (garage -> small office -> remodel of the same room -> new
  building). SOURCED gamedevtycoon.fandom.com/wiki/Unlocks (2026-07-30); gamerant.com move-to-new-office (2022-08-15).
- **Two Point Hospital / Campus** - rooms drawn in place on a live floorplan; extra wings bought as shells. Not a painted
  hub. SOURCED two-point-hospital.fandom.com/wiki/Rooms (2026-09-16); itemlevel.net (2022-08-11).
- **Motorsport Manager** - HQ campus illustration with EMPTY PADS; wind tunnel, simulator etc. drop onto fixed sites
  after a build period; click a building for its menu. SOURCED motorsportmanagerpc.fandom.com/wiki/Headquarters
  (2026-07-29).
- **Hollywood Animal** - isometric lot; departments appear as new volumes on empty ground. SOURCED thegamer.com
  construction guide (2025-04-16).
- **Football Manager** - facilities are graded lists plus a stadium still; community packs swap the photo. SOURCED
  passion4fm.com (2021-03-13).
- **Punch Club 2** - home garage gains machines as props; new gyms are separate map nodes. SOURCED
  punchclub.wiki.gg/wiki/Locations (2023-08-04).
- **Cage Legacy** - menu-first, no published hub-growth art. SOURCED Steam app 5002990 (1.23.0 news 2026-08-29). PROXY.
- **THE FIGHT BUSINESS** - coming soon, no dated hub screens. SOURCED Steam app 5249680 (retrieved 2026-09-26). PROXY.
  Boxing Club Manager (2022) upgrades gym level via UI only (Steam 1652490).

Pattern (JUDGEMENT): four methods - swap the plate at 2-4 career gates; drop a prefab onto a reserved pad; live tile

## 2. What players say
- Growth lands when the frame itself changes (GDT leaving the garage is the cited rite of passage). SOURCED Greenheart
  forum (2013-05-18/22); Game Rant (2022-08-15).
- MM / Hollywood players enjoy "watching the base fill in" because the empty pads were visible first. PROXY.
- Two Point: owning your rooms is the joy; clutter and awkward extra shells the complaint (Steam 2025-07-28; Reddit
  2025-11-30).
- FM players install stadium packs because the default facility screen is a stat list. SOURCED FM Scout (2023-11-08).

## 3. Recommendation for FightCamp
Build **(c) one "back rooms" plate behind one existing pin, with (a) as the door language on it**. HOME keeps its four
pins and four plaques. One pin opens plate 2: a hallway with five door slots - unbuilt = dark door + lock, built = lit
door + name; enter the room from its door. Not (a) on HOME (breaks the four-plaque ceiling); not (b) as the room system
(5+ full plates) - keep one optional mid-career wing swap as a GDT-style beat.
**Art cost:** 0 new HOME plates; 1 back-rooms plate; 5 door-lit overlays or one sheet.
**Growth beat:** on the first room built, light the corridor or warm the office window on HOME - one region overlay.
