THE BOOT — TITLE, SLOTS, SETTINGS

Pre-career gate. No rail, no top career bar, no ShellChrome. K13 already ruled this is its own stage — not creation. This study picks the shape, designs BootChrome, names what a slot row must say (vs get_save_summary), and draws the two seams into CreationChrome step 1 and THE DAY.

A · THE SHAPE

PICK · TWO BOARDS — TITLE THEN SLOTS — first frame is brand + Continue affordance; slots open when the player must choose a life (LOAD / NEW into a taken slot). Splash does not survive as a title — discarded with every other pre-kit scene; any load wait is the shutter, not a branded splash scene.
REJECTED · ONE COMBINED BOARD — packing brand + three dossiers on the first frame forces the player to compare saves before they have decided to load. Title should invite; slots should decide.
REJECTED · SPLASH-AS-TITLE — Splash.gd is a load screen pretending to be a title. Pre-kit discard ruling: rebuild from the kit, never migrate. Brand lives on BootChrome TITLE, not on a spinner.

B · THE CHROME

PICK · BOOTCHROME (FOURTH) — brand bar + body + foot. No rail, no Continue strip, no step strip, no committed trail.

Reason stronger than “different”: CreationChrome’s identity is steps + trail (K13). Mounting it on boot with empty steps would lie about being mid-creation. ShellChrome is career-only (rail + green Continue). K7 is fight gear (way-out / stakes / clock). Boot is the gate before any step or career exists — it needs the shared pre-career brand DNA without borrowing a frame that implies progress or a running gym.

Shared DNA with CreationChrome (mark / gym blue brand / ground) so NEW→step1 feels continuous; separate mount so CreationChrome stays honest.

C · THE SLOT ROW

A save slot is a decision surface: which life to re-enter. At a glance the player needs identity + progress + money + freshness — not a second GYM hub.

CarrySource todayLeave off
Gym name, city/country, career week, cash, roster count, discipline label, last played (updated_at), newest markget_save_summary returns all of theseAlerts list, payroll/upkeep projection, injured/recovery/training minutiae, staff/coach counts, leading athlete portrait path, gym tier deep stats

What get_save_summary lacks for the row / STATES:

  • Incompatible ≠ empty — unsupported saves collapse to {"exists": false} via _is_supported_career. STATES needs incompatible: true + save_version exposed. Codex read/extend before build.
  • W–L record — not returned; leave off the row (cannot be judged at a glance without inventing a career record API).
  • Ruleset id — lives on career (selected_ruleset_id) but not in summary; optional chip later, not required to tell two saves apart if discipline label is present.
  • Fame — only leading_athlete_fame / gym_reputation; leave off the boot row (in-career meters).

D · THE TWO SEAMS

Same shutter language as K13’s 780 ms creation handoff and K7 enter — “changing gear, not a load.” Both seams share fold → shutter → lock → settle so NEW and LOAD feel related.

E · SETTINGS

Boot owns: master / music / SFX levels, resolution + window mode, ruleset selection (career-binding — choose before NEW; locked once a career is running).

In-career: audio only (same control rows, reached from shell). Resolution may reopen the same board. Ruleset stays read-only mid-career.

No form family yet — do not mint sliders/toggles as a family. Compose with pair (slate), track fills, and chip segments already in the kit. One gold = APPLY / SAVE SETTINGS.

F · FIRST RUN

Empty slots are deliberate invitation, not an error. Three dashed rows + copy that the gym is waiting — gold NEW CAREER is the only forward path. No BAD chrome, no “missing save” alert.
