Dead Space Reads as "Abandoned": A 1080p card with only 250 px of active content signals to the player: "We ran out of time or ideas here." Shipped PC games (Motorsport Manager, Crusader Kings III, Victoria 3) treat cards like physical docket folios filled with dense, useful intelligence.
Three Naked Horizontal Bars Look Like a Mobile Benchmark: Stretching three identical 100% width colored lines across a black box is the universal shorthand of wireframe prototypes. It has no framing, no gauge feel, and no material depth.
Missing Boxing Culture: FightCamp is a combat management sim. Showing only generic numbers (ECONOMY, TALENT) while burying the rich data we already have (Boxing vs. MMA culture, London/Manchester rivalry, the British Boxing Board of Control, local venues) strips out all the thematic texture of setting up a fight gym.
No Visual Craft or Physical Skeuomorphism: In Maria’s aesthetic, paper is cream newsprint or aged parchment; brass is screwed-in or stamped; leather is dark and stitched. Today’s card is a flat, extruded CSS box.

PART F: ARCHITECTURAL EVALUATION & CRITICAL PRODUCTION RISK
Art Director Confidence Score: 9.7 / 10
Why 9.7: This design completely eliminates the "blunt" accusation by filling every vertical millimeter of the $480 \times 1080$ card with meaningful, authentic, actionable boxing management lore that we already have in the database. It transforms a dead telemetry window into an engrossing tactile dossier that players will spend minutes reading before picking their gym’s home country.
The One Critical Production Risk: Data Asymmetry Across Small Countries
The Risk: Large boxing nations (UK, USA, Mexico, Japan) have extensive rosters of cities, federations, rival gyms, and combat sports. However, if a player picks an emerging or smaller market (e.g., Iceland, Ghana, or Uruguay), the database might only contain 1 city, 2 gyms, and no distinct secondary combat disciplines.
If the UI blindly expects 3 cities and 5 rival gyms, the card will display blank rows, broken tab alignments, or glitchy empty lists—reintroducing the exact "blunt" void we just fixed.
The Mandatory Mitigation: Author the Godot Control script with a strict graceful fallback container flow:
If a country has only 1 city, hide the tab bar completely and expand the single city’s arena/gym card to full height.
If a country lacks listed rival gyms, replace the rival block with a styled Regional Scouting Lore Quote (e.g., "Emerging Frontier Market: Low institutional rivalry provides unchecked local amateur scouting monopoly.").
This guarantees that whether the player selects the United States or New Zealand, the card remains 100% full, balanced, and visually bulletproof.
