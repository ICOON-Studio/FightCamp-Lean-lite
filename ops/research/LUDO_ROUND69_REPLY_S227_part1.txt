The 650-Pixel Black Abyss (Single-Fighter Disaster): In Week 1, a gym has 1 or 2 fighters. Today's UI renders a single $62\text{ px}$ tall row at $Y = 380$, leaving $650\text{ px}$ of vacant canvas below it. It looks like a broken database query rather than a professional game.
Missing Fighter Identity & Tactical Anchor: Danny Reyes is reduced to raw $11\text{ pt}$ text in an unbordered grey cell. There is no fighter portrait, no weight class, no current stamina gauge, no record, and no indication of who is coaching him.
The "Recovery Infection" (Uninformative Monoculture): The 7 day cells display REST REST REST REST REST REC REST. There is no visual distinction between active recovery (pool/physio), complete bedrest, or injured suspension. It looks like an inactive spreadsheet.
The Naked Drag Shelf ("Airport Luggage Belt"): The drag bar (REST, RECOVERY, BAGS, RING, STRENGTH, CARDIO) stretches $1400\text{ px}$ across the screen as flat, low-contrast grey pill boxes. They lack stamina burn indicators, primary attribute targets, coach requirements, and tactical icons.
Microscopic Telemetry Lines: READY 100% and COND 100% are squeezed into a single $180\text{ px}$ cell using $3\text{ px}$ thin flat bars. The text overlaps the bar boundary. There is no indicator for next week's fatigue carry-over.
The Unanchored Presets Bar: BALANCED, POWER, CONDITIONING, TECHNIQUE, RECOVERY sit directly above the drag shelf in identical grey buttons. There is no explanation of what applying a preset will overwrite, no confirmation preview, and no undo.
Displaced "WATCH THE FLOOR" Button: The primary bridge to the 3D world—WATCH THE FLOOR—is an oversized yellow pill slapped onto the top right. It breaks the visual hierarchy of the tab bar (THE WEEK / FIGHT CAMP).
Incoherent Tab Bar Framing: THE WEEK and FIGHT CAMP are jammed against the top right without an active baseline or container framing. They look like utility pills rather than the core screen mode selector.
Invisible Filter Chips: ALL, NEEDS YOU, UNPLANNED, IN CAMP, BLOCKED have no numerical counters (e.g., NEEDS YOU (1)). They provide no immediate signal of why a filter is useful.
The Obscure "FLAG >" Column: On the right edge of the fighter row sits an isolated FLAG > element. No player knows whether this flags a fighter for attention, marks a country flag, or sets a tactical priority.
Cryptic Load Breakdown String: WORK 0  REC 1  REST 6 is rendered as raw monospaced text. It gives no tactical warning that this schedule results in rapid conditioning decay and zero technical progression.
Hidden Session Drawer Affordance: Nothing indicates that clicking a day cell opens the deep tactical session drawer. There are no chevron indicators, no hover lift, and no click cues.
Missing Facility / Station Capacity Telemetry: The manager has no idea if the Ring or Heavy Bags are currently full. You can assign Danny Reyes to the Ring without knowing if Coach McGee is already running another fighter there.
Barren "HINT LINE" at Screen Bottom: A tiny string of grey text ("DRAG A TILE ONTO A DAY... CLICK A DAY FOR THE SESSION...") is shoved into the bottom-left corner at $8\text{ pt}$. At $1080\text{ p}$, it is invisible.
Clashing Yellow "GUIDE - Set his training" Sticker: A bright yellow button is stuck to the bottom right corner like a sticky note, completely detached from the screen's design language.
Lack of Materiality & Physical Depth: The entire screen is constructed from raw, flat CSS-style grey rectangles (#2b2823). There are no chamfers, no brass trims, no stamped metal plates, and no shadows.
PART B: THE TARGET DESIGN ARCHITECTURE

The new screen replaces the empty spreadsheet with a tactical command board. It solves the 1-fighter problem by pairing the fighter roster with an active gym capacity overview & tactical inspector when roster counts are low.

Football Manager: FM abandoned the monolithic training slider decades ago. Each day is broken into explicit sessions where the player sees exact attribute gains vs. injury risk. FightCamp adopts this inside the Session Drawer: you never wonder what "Bags" trains; you see Power +0.4%, Stamina +0.2%.
Motorsport Manager: When setting up a race car or training mechanics, Motorsport Manager provides immediate ghosted preview deltas. Hovering over a preset shows how readiness and fatigue will shift before you click.
Two Point Series: When a room or doctor is busy, Two Point shows an unmistakable visual lockout with an actionable tooltip. We implement this in our Refusal Engine so the player is never left asking "Why didn't my fighter train today?"
