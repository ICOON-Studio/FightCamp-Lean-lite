# TASK D46 - DOLA - WOULD "ONLY HERE" MAKE YOU PICK A TRAINER? (S98)
Owner: dola (player's chair, no repo). Assigned 2026-09-27 (S98). Reply inline, <= 450 words, titled
D46_A_TRAINER_ONLY_HE_TEACHES.

## Why
We are about to make trainers matter in a fight-gym management game. Our design (from a study of Football Manager,
Motorsport Manager and UFC Undisputed 3): each famous (fictional) trainer has 1-2 SIGNATURES - e.g. "off-balancing from
the clinch" - shown on his profile as a locked card that says "Only here". A 2-week camp visit unlocks one entry move
(the module); a year on his roster, with a strong coach bond, unlocks the whole system (the tree). Generic skills (pads,
conditioning) are shown as 1-5 stars.

## What would a human do?
A playtester who manages a gym reads a trainer's profile for ten seconds and says whether they would pay for a camp,
hire him, or skip him - and what on the card made them decide.

## Questions (one or two lines each)
1. Does a locked "Only here" card make you want him more than a higher-star trainer? Why?
2. Module after a 2-week camp, tree after a year: fair, too slow, too fast?
3. What must the card show so you believe the signature is real in the fight (a clip? a stat? a famous win?)
4. What would make you feel cheated?
5. The ONE thing to get right.
