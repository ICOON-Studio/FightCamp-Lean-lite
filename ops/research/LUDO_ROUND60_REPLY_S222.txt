1. Trigger, Delay, Placement & Dismissal Logic
Hover Delay: Standard micro-interaction delay: 350ms on stationary mouse position. Instantly appears (0ms) if moving between adjacent elements within the same group.
Glyph / Affordance: Inline clickable (i) circular icon (14x14px, stroke #7A6C5D, text #E5B842) or dashed underline on clickable financial/simulation metrics.
Gamepad / Keyboard Navigation: Focus highlight with [D-Pad / Tab]. Pressing [X / Square / F1] toggles modal into a "Pinned / Inspectable" floating window.
Screen Edge Clamping & Flipping:
Auto-evaluates viewport boundaries (1920x1080).
Default position: Top-Right of target with 8px offset.
If mouse_x + tooltip_width > 1900px, flips horizontally to Top-Left.
If mouse_y + tooltip_height > 1060px, flips vertically above the target.
Dismiss Rules:
Mouse exits boundary by $>24px$ (grace zone prevents jitter).
Any primary input action (mouse_left_down, esc, pan_camera, spacebar_pause) instantly fades alpha to 0.0 over 80ms.

3. Motion Rules & Accessibility
Input-First Rule: Motion never crosses the player's foveal focus during input. When the user types into "Gym Name" or drags a zone card, camera motion dampens to zero ($0.0$).
Reduce-Motion Accessibility Toggle: When Settings > Accessibility > Reduce Motion is active:
Camera tween transitions convert to 150ms cross-fades.
Pendulum bag sway, fan rotations, and GPU dust particles freeze to static positions.
Performance Budget:
Background SubViewport capped at 60 FPS even on 144Hz monitors.
Particle count limited to $\le 180$ total sprites.
Tri budget for background view: $< 45,000$ tris. Draw calls: $< 32$.
