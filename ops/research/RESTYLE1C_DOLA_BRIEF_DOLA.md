rival demo and said our menus should look "much more polished". Your job: draw that polish for OUR game, in OUR palette.

What "polished" means here (read from the rival demo, our words)
Every block is a CARD: rounded corners (8-10 px), a 1 px border a little lighter than the card, a very soft inner glow on
the active card. Cards sit on a darker page with 24 px gaps. Nothing floats on a photo.
Section labels are SMALL CAPS, letter-spaced, in the accent colour (11-12 px); the content under them is plain and larger.
One BIG headline per screen (the person or the night), with a quiet sub-line under it.
KEY-VALUE TILES: three or four small cards in a row, each with a tiny label on top and one bold value (e.g. RESULT / Win,
METHOD / Points, ROUNDS / 3).
Tags as PILLS (rounded, outlined, small) in the top-right of a card.
Comparisons as TWO-COLOUR BARS meeting in the middle with the numbers at the ends.
Exactly ONE primary button per screen, filled with the accent, everything else outlined or plain text.
Tables with roomy rows (56-64 px), a portrait at the left of each row, muted column heads in small caps.
Our palette and type (decided by the owner, keep it)
Warm dark brown page (#1c1712), cards a step lighter (#262019), borders (#3a3127); text cream (#efe6d6), muted (#a89a86).
Accent YELLOW/amber (#e8b54a) for labels, the primary button and highlights. Green (#3fb27f) = good, red (#d0503c) = bad.
Blue (#4a7fd0) only for "your boxer" in two-colour bars; the opponent's side is the red.
Font: a clean sans (Barlow or Inter from Google Fonts); the gym name in our hand-lettered display style may stay as plain
bold italic.
Words: plain game words, no jargon. Names appear only in headers; body text says "your boxer", "the opponent".

✅ Three lines — what changed and why
Everything sits on rounded cards with soft borders — no floating text; eyes land on clear containers and readability improves at a glance.
Small-caps amber section labels + distinct hierarchy — you instantly scan "what is a title vs. what is data" without extra visual noise.
Consistent pill colours + form bars + portrait row markers — form/morale/rank can be read peripherally; you don't have to read every word to spot who's ready and who isn't.
⭐ To reach 10/10
Add click-sort on every table header so you can reorder by form, morale or next bout instantly
Add a quick-edit inline toggle for morale notes/short-term goals without opening a full profile
Add a mini comparison bar in the detail card showing your boxer vs. division average on each stat — tells you exactly where they stand without extra clicks
