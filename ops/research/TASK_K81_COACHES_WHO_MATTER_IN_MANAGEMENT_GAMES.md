## Why
A coming stage makes trainers matter: a famous trainer teaches things only he teaches (e.g. off-balancing), a two-week
camp visit unlocks a module, a year on his roster unlocks the whole system (Grok's G135 study). Before we design it we
want to know how shipped management games made staff feel different from each other - and where they failed.

## What would a human do?
A game designer starting this feature plays the genre's best examples and writes down, per game: what a coach/staff
member actually changes, how the player learns what he is good at, what he costs, and why players hire one over
another. They note the systems players call boring (a +2% stat) and the ones they talk about.

## Questions
1. Football Manager (coaches' attribute stars, specialist coaches), Motorsport Manager (engineers/mechanic traits),
   EA UFC / Undisputed career-mode trainers, Punch Club 1/2 trainers, WWE 2K MyGM, any other fight or sports sim you
   know: for each, what a coach changes, how it is shown, what it costs, what is exclusive to him.
2. Which of those made a coach feel like a PERSON with a signature, and which felt like a stat buff? Evidence from
   reviews / forums where you can.
3. Exclusivity: does any game lock a technique or system to one coach, and how does the player find out it exists?
4. Your ONE recommendation for how our game should show "this trainer teaches something nobody else does".
