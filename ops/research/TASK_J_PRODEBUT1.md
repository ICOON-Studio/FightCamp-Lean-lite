## The question

tickets". We need each number sourced, for a female lightweight's 4-round debut on a small-hall show in England, 2024-2026:
1. BBBofC licence fee (boxer), and the medicals before the first licence: MRI brain scan, eye exam, blood tests (HIV/hepatitis),
   ECG - price of each in the UK private sector, and who pays.
2. The opponent ("journeyman") fee for a 4x2 or 4x3 debut, and who pays it (promoter, or the boxer out of ticket money).
3. Ticket selling: how many tickets a debutant is expected to sell, the ticket price, and what share the boxer keeps.
4. Purse for a debut and the usual split (manager 25 %, trainer 10 %), and other costs: corner licences (trainer/second),
   hand wraps/gloves rules, travel.
5. The net: a typical debutant's money after the night (a range), and how often debutants lose money.
