# Verdict for your OK (S288): make our front rooms read like MGT2's

You asked why MGT2 rooms feel bigger and their objects sit better. Ludo read our game-camera still, and the numbers were measured
(research/MGT2_SCALE_S288.md, Ludo's full answer in research/LUDO_MGT2_ROOMS_S288.md).
**Our rooms are not smaller.** In person heights they are the same size as MGT2's. They read small for these reasons:

| Cause (Ludo's weight) | Now | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Wall height (40%) | room walls 3.6 m = 2.0 x a person (MGT2: 1.16 x) | room partition walls **1.8 m**; outer shell 2.2 m; camera-facing walls keep the 0.4 m cut stub (already our rule) |
| Prop scale vs people (20%) | true real-world scale | room **hero furniture x1.3 wide/deep, x1.15 high** (desk, counter, treatment table, lockers, racks). Training-floor equipment and people stay true scale |
| Furniture floats mid-room (15%) | items 0.5-1 m off the walls | big pieces **flush on the walls**, the middle ~60% of each room left clear |
| Rooms bleed together (10%) | similar pale floors | **one distinct floor per room**: office dark wood/carpet, reception slate tile, physio clinical light, store concrete, lockers blue tile |
| Clutter specks | 7-12 items per room, small dressing | **3-5 hero pieces** per room; phones, bells, clipboards and the like go into the parent piece's texture |
| Grid | free placement | 0.5 m grid for room furniture (our settled Two Point rule, now applied inside rooms too) |

Hero pieces per room (Ludo): office = big desk + chair, credenza, safe, client chairs. Reception = L-counter facing the street door,
trophy case, vending block, welcome mat. Physio = treatment table mid-room on a coloured floor patch, roll-cart, anatomy chart, basin.
Store = two solid-back racks (no wire shelving: it shimmers at this distance), gear trunk, glove bin. Lockers = two locker banks on
the walls, a centre bench, a shower block, a mirror strip.

**Keep unchanged:** the ring, the bag wall, the 30 x 22.5 m hall, the floor planks, the front-room ribbon.

**Build order** (each step closes on a still from the same camera): 1 wall heights, 2 room floors, 3 hero furniture scale,
4 flush-to-wall + clear middle + item limits.

Approve: build all four steps. Or name which steps to keep or change.
