## What it adds, in plain words
- **Fit:** each coach suits some boxers and some areas (pads, bags, conditioning, corner). A good fit makes evening drills land
  faster (the move reaches its next level sooner) and morning stat work grow faster.
- **Coach time:** each coach has hours a week. Borrowing past them costs fit, and next week starts short ("Rest next week").
- **Growth and raises:** a coach gets better in the areas they run. They ask for a raise; paying gives them more time for good.
- **Bond:** working together builds trust. A trusted coach's word in the corner counts for more, and adds one line to the fight plan.
- **Release 1:** one assistant, the promoted club coach, and you. The club coach can't take sparring nights until a Level 2 course
  (England Boxing rule).
- **One new board:** "Coaches this week" (Club > People): coach cards left, who runs what per boxer in the middle, what Monday
  brings on the right. Mockup to you first.
