# C87 — The Retarget Ladder

## Counting contract

Receiver coverage uses Q46's denominator of **9,715 shown beats** pooled, and
each ruleset table below names its own shown-beat denominator. Thrower coverage
uses Q45's denominator of **9,992 named shown throws** pooled; Q45's **145
unnamed shown throws** are outside that denominator because no unnamed throw
can have a named clip. No percentage below uses the separate R103 population.

A receiver role is credited only after its prescribed clip bundle is
retargeted. Thus the clip totals count every supplied variant or phase: four
head reactions; seven body reactions; one low-hit leg reaction; two knockdowns
plus one get-up exit; one idle; three slips; and both four-clip block sets.
For `react_body`, the Small and Medium staggers are assigned to torso reactions
because they are the non-low-hit stagger sets; `H2H_StaggerLarge04_LowHit`
remains exclusively the leg reaction.

For throwers, a clip is credited only when its filename names the demanded
technique. A category, a partial combination, a different side, or a different
position is only NEAR. The four armed Att/Vic pairs are **8 weapon files out of
Pack B's 37 files** and are excluded from every receiver and thrower coverage
calculation.
