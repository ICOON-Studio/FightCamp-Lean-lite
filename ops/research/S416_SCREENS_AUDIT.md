## Screens that need designing, most important first

1. Fight night corner between rounds: critic 3/10, nine controls with no order, the most important question last; design on Miro is scored 8 but the build does not follow it.
2. Game plan suggestions: the owner's complaint that the player guesses; no screen shows a coach's pick per line.
3. The weekly path (checklist and guide): the loop train, book, camp, corner, review is never shown; the guide bubble blocks pages.
4. Mail inbox: moved to the top bar today, only drawn as an open state on the top bar sheet; no list, no marking of the message that needs an answer.
5. Club People: MENUS FINAL says "needs a new layout" and none was drawn.
6. Fight camp: nine unranked choices; MENUS FINAL has it as a tab, the build is a pop-up.
7. Fight result: needs the Next-step button and plain words on why.
8. Home as the gym picture, plus the gym builder: not on this board as designs; the owner's art-consistency comment sits near it.
9. Title and New game: the title is only a flow box; the coach cards are scored 6.
10. Pro offer as its own picture (asked for in "14. GROW").
11. Week review: half blank, shows before the player has done anything.
12. Records tab and History tab: no design.
