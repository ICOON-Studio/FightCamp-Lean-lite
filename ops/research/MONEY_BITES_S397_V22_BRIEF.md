# FightCamp money design v2.2 (for review)

FightCamp is a boxing (+ no-gi BJJ) gym-management game for PC. One small club; amateurs, then pros; careers are
open-ended. Game dollars = GBP x 1.25. GAME = a chosen value, not a real-world claim.

## The problem it answers
A one-year simulation showed the careless player always goes broke, and a sensible one-boxer gym piled $18-21k with nothing
to buy. With a second boxer on a stipend, even the sensible gym ran out of money. Money was a cliff or a pile, never a
choice.

## The week's money (a sensible year-1 gym)
In: members' dues ($35/month each, ~50-70 members), evening classes $350/week with a coach, sponsors $250 + $150/week,
a $450 ticket cut per amateur bout. Out: rent $1,800/month, governing-body fees ~$7/week, the sessional coach $450/week,
$250/week per boxer on the club stipend, heating and power $560/month October-March and $280/month April-September,
liability insurance $1,250/year, a kit budget. Bills are paid at each 4-week month end. 8 weeks in a row below $0 closes
the gym. Start cash = 3 months of costs ($17,200).

## What money buys (each moves ONE number the player sees; each row shows the price and "leaves $X, about N weeks of costs")
- **Premises** (the bill that grows with the club): Unit (holds 70 members, 3 roster places, rent $1,800/month) ->
  Bigger unit with a second ring (140 members, 5 places, $3,400/month, $12,000 to move) -> Club building (250, 7 places,
  $5,500/month, $25,000 to move). Members walk toward what reputation draws, capped by the premises. Each move brings a
  bigger sponsor to the table ($400 then $800 a week).
- **Upgrades** (bought once, a week of fitters, a small upkeep): matted floor and ring canvas $2,400 (+$15/week, training
  injury risk -15%); strength corner $3,600 (+$22, power and conditioning sessions +10%); fitness class kit $1,500 (+$15,
  +$420/week of morning classes while an assistant coach is on staff); youth section $1,800 incl. DBS checks and a welfare
  course (juniors' coach $168/week, 20 juniors' dues +$115/week, home-grown prospects).
- **Staff jobs** (sessional; letting one go costs a week's pay): assistant coach $280/week (+1 roster place, runs the
  fitness classes); strength and conditioning coach $100/week (training injury risk -25%, a pro camp's bill halved);
  physio on call $75/week (injured boxers back a quarter sooner); cutman $150 a pro night (half the cut layoffs after a
  pro bout). All training-risk cuts together stop at -35%.
- **Kit budget a week:** Low $20 (training injury risk +15%, 3 fewer members stay), Standard $45, Good $80 (-8%, +2).
- **Stipends:** per amateur, the club stipend $250/week (trains full time) or a day job (costs nothing, every session
  gains 15% less). Most amateurs work or study; a stipend is for a top prospect.
- **Youth intakes:** at the January and September term starts a junior may turn 18 and ask for a trial (35%, +10% with the
  assistant coach); two weeks before, the juniors' coach says how it looks and the potential range. A home-grown prospect
  has 3 more points of potential and half the stipend for the first year.
- **Club show** (every 12 weeks at most): $3,500 up front (hall ~$2,000, officials + doctor + ambulance ~$1,200, trophies
  ~$300); tickets $25 x turnout (members, reputation, the gym's own amateurs on the bill, juniors' families) x a luck
  roll 0.7-1.15, plus a raffle $600-1,400; the page shows the expected ticket range and net range before booking. A young
  club's show can lose money; a grown club's pays $2-5k. A paying night brings new members.
- **Roster places:** 3 at the start (boxers and no-gi athletes), up to 8 (assistant +1, each premises move +2). A trial
  needs a free place.
- **Pros:** the gym takes 25% of a pro's purse and tickets (manager 15% + trainer 10%). Contract talks open when a pro
  first reaches the top 10 (pay a $2,500 bonus to keep 25%, or drop to 20%) and wins a belt ($5,000, or 17%); 14 days to
  answer. A pro camp after the debut has a bill of 6% of the purse (halved with the S&C coach). On one share the gym's net
  per bout rises with rank.
- **Yearly:** coach education and safeguarding renewals $400 a coach.

## Real anchors used (GBP x 1.25)
Club rent GBP 1,450/month for 1,500 sq ft; dues GBP 28/month; juniors GBP 20-43/month; club coach GBP 22/h; S&C GBP 40-60 a
session; sports physio GBP 55-90 a session; cutman $100-1,500 a night; trainer 10% full camp; filed manager contracts ~18%,
caps 33 1/3%, established boxers push to 10-15%; heating $300-750/month in winter; insurance ~$1,000-1,500/year; kit
$2,000-3,500/year; club shows net +$2-5k or a loss; Sport England grants GBP 250-5,000.

## A first measurement (2 careers a player, 60 weeks, both players sign a second boxer after week 13 and turn the first pro)
Sensible: never closed; cash at weeks 13 / 26 / 39 / 52 = 1.7 / 1.9 / 2.1 / 1.3 months of costs; owned 2-3 upgrades and
2-3 staff jobs; 13 bouts a career, turned pro. Careless (head coach, unsafe bouts, hard camps, no sponsors, spends
whatever the cash covers - each habit at 50%): both careers closed, weeks 20 and 40.

## The screens
Club > Money (one page): cash chart and the week's in and out as bars; contract talks (when open); stipends; premises;
upgrades; kit budget and club show side by side; one-offs in and out; deals; repairs. Club > People: the coach, then each
staff job with Hire / "Let go, a week's pay". All plain lists, no gym floor.
