## 5. Argue against yourself — then land

### Strongest case: **do not ship the shortlist; keep / extend in-engine synthesised PCM**

1. **Licence trail friction.** Mixkit’s full SFX Free License text is behind a JS modal (live reopen needed). Sonniss GDC bans AI/ML training — conflicts with any future “generate variants from our library” tool. Freesound Gen-AI preference panels (J22) add per-file traps if anyone trains. CC-BY rows force credits screens.  
2. **Quality mismatch.** Community punches (OwlStorm retro, Kenney punch, Mixkit designed hits) will sit next to a sim that already aims at sport honesty (J22B Atha/silicone timing). A bicycle-bell “boxing bell” and Hollywood 1930s optical crowd are *legible*, not *authentic*.  
3. **Variant count vs 491 beats.** Even stacking every USABLE row above does not yield Boom-CK-scale variation. Repetition will read faster than synth parameterisation.  
4. **Sync control.** Synth PCM is already frame-owned (Pass 2 budget). Sampled WAVs add fixed envelopes that fight lead/lag policy.  
5. **Ship weight + bus complexity.** Dozens of WAVs vs a few generators.  
6. **Org-identity risk.** Real-arena / named-bout recordings (even CC-BY) can make FightCamp sound like a bootleg broadcast — the exact failure J22B banned ripping to avoid.  
7. **Maintainability.** Attribution ledger, licence PDF archive, “which Mixkit ID is in the build?” forever. Synth owns its provenance.  
8. **Mat + breath + idle crowd** — the categories that matter for `grapple_control` — are exactly where free world is weakest (**NOT FOUND** loops/sets). Shortlist does not close the hard gap.

### Recommendation (land)

**Keep synthesised PCM as the default ship path** for impacts + crowd bed (as the build already does), and treat this shortlist as an **operator-gated optional layer pack**: if/when quality of synth fails a listen gate, pull **only** CC0/Mixkit **USABLE AS IS** rows (bells first; then Mixkit 364 murmur; then Mixkit glove/flesh one-shots; harvest kyles/felix for mat), never named-event crowds, never BBC RemArc free, never download without operator yes. Commission Foley (Foley Walkers / One Step Up quote) only if authenticity of mat + flesh becomes a release blocker — that is still cleaner than broadcast DNA.
