## 1. The idea in five lines
1. The SHOW is the unit: you ask one show's host for a bout on that night's card for one of your fighters, instead of waiting for the next sheet.
2. Four ways to fill the night, compared row by row against WAITING: a FAIR MATCH (free), a STEP UP (free and 6 in 10, or certain for a favour), A NAME (a favour, two for a top-3 name), the fighter's OWN ASK, or the sheet.
3. Every column prints what a win and a loss leave: the ladder place, the road to the pro offer, the regional seed, mood for this fighter, members, money, favours; and the stoppage's full price.
4. The season is a timeline: the shows, the close, the regional, and the 30-day suspension from each date. Enter first (safe, seed frozen, no more asks) or box first (a win lifts the seed, a stoppage can cost the entry).
5. Favours run both ways and stay scarce: hosts call you at five days' notice, you spend favours on names, certainty and a kilo, and a host you used calls back.

## 3. What is thin (each answered in section 4)
1. NO AMATEUR ASK: between offers the Fights page has nothing to press (operator S416: "there is nothing to do in this menu wtf?").
2. WHO YOU BEAT DOES NOT COUNT (net-wins sort): Lucy 4-1 beating Priya (#4) gives #4; beating Chloe (#5) or Aisha (#9) gives #5; a loss to anyone about #8.
3. THE RANK BUYS ONLY THE 7-WIN PRO ROAD; the regional draw ignores it (shuffle, then rating).
4. THE ENTRY ORDER IS INVISIBLE: book-then-enter is allowed and nothing on any screen says so.
5. ONE-WAY NOVICE RULE in offers: Tess at 11 bouts would be offered novices.
6. THE STOPPAGE ROW CANNOT TELL NAMES APART (no `ko`).
7. EVERY BOUT PAYS THE SAME $450; no show has a host; nothing is owed between clubs.
8. THE OPEN CLIFF: past 10 bouts, seven of eight classes have one open name.

## 4. The mechanics, each a decision with its options and their prices
CODE = in the code today. NEW = a build task in section 7. ESTIMATE = a design value to be measured (ASKPROBE1).

### D1. THE NIGHT AND THE ENTRY ORDER (the season timeline)
The panel lists the shows 5 to 33 days out, one a Saturday in turn (SHOWCAL1, NEW). Each card prints the ticket terms, the names above you free
that night, the scale forecast, the fight weeks of pads, and TWO season lines worked from the code's dates:
- BOX FIRST: a stoppage there keeps the entry only if bout day + suspension <= close day (70). (`ko_days`, `Injuries.out`, `SeasonPath.eligible`, CODE)
- ENTER FIRST: a stoppage there keeps the regional only if bout day + suspension <= regional day (84). (`Champs.book_next`, CODE)
And the price of each order (LADDER1 seeding, NEW; the draw-at-entry is CODE):
| Option | Gives | Costs |
|---|---|---|
| ENTER FIRST (as soon as the bout is booked, or now) | the regional is safe for any bout to day 54 (Sat 28 Feb); the seed is fixed now | the seed from TODAY's place (Lucy #6: the semi is against a lower seed about 64 in 100); no more asks or offers until the run ends (`Bouts.day_events` 119, CODE; `can_ask` NEW), so a promise to the fighter (D4) cannot be kept |
| BOX FIRST, enter after | a win's place lifts the seed (Lucy beating Priya: #4, about 87 in 100); asks stay open | a points loss drops the seed (#7, about 50); a stoppage blocks the entry unless the bout is on or before day 40 (Sat 14 Feb), and then only on the close day itself (the clock stops for it, NEW need) |
| A later night (28 Feb, 7 Mar) | 3 or 4 fight weeks of pads (a Raw line can reach Usable), an easy cut | 28 Feb: only ENTER FIRST keeps the regional on a stoppage (cleared Mon 30 Mar, the regional day); 7 Mar: a stoppage misses the regional either way (cleared Mon 6 Apr) |
Seed numbers: the field is the fighter and 3 of the 12 others at random (CODE shuffle); seeds 1-4 by ladder place (NEW); 1 meets 4, 2 meets 3. The semi
is against a lower seed when at most one of the 3 drawn is above you: at place p, P = [C(13-p,3) + (p-1) x C(13-p,2)] / C(12,3). #4: 87, #5: 76,
#6: 64, #7: 50, #8: 36, #2: 100 (in 100, CODE arithmetic on a NEW seeding rule).
Why not a lookup: for Lucy on Priya (about 40 to win), box first averages 0.40 x 87 + 0.60 x 50 = 65 against 64 for enter first: even, and it
turns on the win chance (writing the counter line, +3 to +4 in the ESTIMATE, tips it to box first) and on the promise (enter first breaks it). For the
fair match (a win does not climb) enter first wins clearly; for Erin it is box first. The general rule, not this season's dates, makes the choice.

### D2. WHAT YOU ASK FOR (each names who it brings, on the chosen card)
The pool on a card (ASKPOOL1, NEW): names of the division legal BOTH ways (`experience_match_ok` for each side, CODE function), not booked that night,
14 days rested (ESTIMATE), within 1.0 kg of your boxer's walking weight (NEW field), not suspended, travellers (any card) or the host's own (that host's
card only); names above within 4 places (ESTIMATE; the pro ask's band is 5, CODE).
| Option | Who it brings | Gives | Costs |
|---|---|---|---|
| A FAIR MATCH (free) | the host's own boxer nearest your rating if within 2 points, else the free name nearest your rating | the best win chance; YES about 9 in 10 | a win below does not climb; a loss to a name below costs 2 places (LADDER1) |
| A STEP UP (free) | the host's own boxer above you if on its card, else the nearest free name above | a win takes that place; a loss to a name above costs 1 place | YES about 6 in 10, else a COUNTER (a later date or another name, take it or leave it) |
| A STEP UP, MADE CERTAIN | the same name | YES certain | 1 favour with the host; that host will call you within 4 weeks |
| A NAME | the free legal name you choose, 2 or more places up | YES certain if free; the bigger climb | 1 favour; a top-3 name 2; the host calls you within 4 weeks |
| A REMATCH | a name on your fighter's record that beat this fighter (legal, within 4 places, any distance down) | as A NAME; a win also lifts mood by "revenge" (+5 base, NEW event) | as A NAME (1 favour); not shown for a fighter with no named loss (Lucy has none) |
Why not a lookup (Lucy #6, York Hall, 1 West Ham favour): FAIR Aisha #9 (about 55, a win stays #6, a loss #8, places on average -0.9); STEP UP Chloe #5
(about 44, +1 / -1, -0.1; free at 6 in 10 or certain for the favour); A NAME Priya #4 (about 40, +2 / -1, +0.2, the favour). The one favour buys either
a certain Chloe or Priya: the higher win chance or the bigger climb. ASKPROBE1 bar: no kind best in more than 60% of ranked asks.

### D4. THE FIGHTER'S OWN ASK
Raised in the 7 days after a bout (NEW, AskForFight.own_ask). Target, in order: a REMATCH of a named loss on our record (legal); else, with confidence 65
or more (`personality.confidence`, CODE trait), the top legal name within 4 places ("the big night"); else no ask. Lucy (confidence 70, ESTIMATE) asks
for Erin Doyle (#2, 7-2, 9 bouts: a novice, legal both ways; a top-3 name: 2 favours).
| Option | Gives | Costs |
|---|---|---|
| HEAR IT | sends Erin now; mood +5.2 ("heard", CODE scale); a win: #2 (top 3: the 7-win pro road opens) and a certain top seed | 2 favours with one host for certainty; holding 1, it goes as a request at 3 in 10 (ESTIMATE), else a counter; the lowest win chance (about 33) and stopped about 19 |
| PROMISE THE NEXT ONE | send your own pick now; nothing moves | Erin booked within 6 weeks or "promise_broken" (-8 base, NEW event; about -7 for Lucy); impossible once entered (no asks), so it pushes box first and a second bout before the regional |
| OVERRULE | a free hand | about -5 for Lucy now (-5 x lerp(1.3,0.7,heart) x lerp(1.4,0.7,coachability), CODE) |
Why not a lookup: Overrule is the cheaper loss if you will enter first (-5 sure against -7 sure); Promise is free if the season plan has a second bout
(box first, the favours from a host's call); Hear it trades the win chance for the top-3 place. The answer moves with the order (D1) and the favours (D6).

### D5. THE REPLY
| Reply | When | Your answer and its price |
|---|---|---|
| YES | the pick agrees | an offer card with 2 days to take it (ESTIMATE); Accept books it (`Bouts.accept`, CODE); turn it down: -1 favour with that host (NEW) |
| COUNTER | a free step up's name says not that night (4 in 10), or the weight gap is 1.0 to 2.0 kg | one change, printed: "Sat 28 Feb instead", "Chloe Bennett instead", "+0.8 kg". Take it, or leave it (free) |
| NOBODY | the pool is empty | the reason from the rule that emptied it. Free |
A fallback is set before sending a free step up: "If they say no: take the counter / ask the fair match / wait for the sheet" (saves the 2 days of a second visit).

### D6. A HOST CALLS YOU
At most one call a week (ESTIMATE), and only when one of your boxers is a fair fit: about 40 to 60 in 100 and inside 1.5 kg of the class on the forecast (NEW).
| Option | Gives | Costs |
|---|---|---|
| Send | +2 favours with that host (cap 2); the bout counts (record, ladder, bouts toward the nationals) | 5 days' notice: no fight week of pads; the host's opponent; a keeps-the-tickets host pays no $450 and the van about $40 (ESTIMATE); a miss on the scale $300 (CODE) and -1 favour; the stand-down after |
| Send, ask a kilo | the same, 1.0 kg of room on that card | 1 favour with that host (greyed at 0) |
| Say no | nothing moves | no favours; if you OWE this host (you used a favour there in the last 4 weeks): -1 favour and no names from that host for 6 weeks |
Send switches an over boxer's diet to Cut from today when the forecast says it makes weight that way (the code's cut has no fight-night price, so Cut is
a setting of Send, not its own option).

### D7. FAVOURS (FAVOURS1)
Per host; at most 2. Earned: a host's call answered +2; a bout on a card where the host keeps the tickets +1. Spent: a name 2+ places up 1, a top-3 name 2,
a step up made certain 1, a kilo 1, turning down your own YES 1. Lost: a missed weight on their card 1, pulling out of their card 1, saying no to an owed
call 1. One unused favour fades every 12 weeks. A host you used calls you within 4 weeks. Favours never buy a win, a judge or a place, and cannot be bought
with cash. Two of your boxers on one card share one van.

### D8. THE RANK AND THE SEED (LADDER1)
A stored ladder per division (start: today's sort). A win over a name above (within 4 places) takes that place; a win over a name below: no move; a loss
to a name above: -1 place; a loss to a name below: -2. Rivals' bouts at shows move by the same rule. The regional draw at entry seeds by ladder place
(ChampDraw.first_round takes the place as its sort key). Every candidate prints "a win #6 to #4 / a loss #6 to #7" and "regional seed: a win 87 / a loss 50".

### Units (CORE_LOOP C13)
Chances in 100 with a band where the code has one; money in $; mood and favours as counts; days and dates.

## 8. Rejected, and why
- An idle mood cost on WAIT: `month_no_fight` has no caller, and accepting the sheet is a booking. An idle system, if wanted, is its own task.
- Changing Champs to forbid book-then-enter: the order is now the decision (seed against safety), so the rule stays and is shown.
- The gate terms as their own decision: fixed by the night; they are a property of the card.
- The novices' night for ranked boxers; Enter now beside the asks; "Every loss" as a row; "Your plan has 4 lines"; favour dots; "Left out" as a line.
- The sheet earning a favour: the sheet is a name and a date with no host in the code; favours come from calls and keeps-the-tickets nights.
- "Send, cut harder" as its own option: the code's Cut has no fight-night price, so it would be a free better Send.
- Reputation x2 for a step-up win; the File found row; buying favours with cash; a bout-finding app; skills bouts; a free date slider (as round 1 and 2).

## 9. Risks
- THE CLOSE DAY: a boxer stopped on Sat 14 Feb can enter only on Mon 16 Mar; without the clock stop the window passes unseen.
- SEEDS BY PLACE make the ladder matter; LADDER1 must re-run `ranking_test`, `rank_line_test` and the champs tests.
- FAVOURS: the cap, the fade and the owed call are ESTIMATE; ASKPROBE1's 50% bar decides them.
- MOOD TRAITS: Lucy's numbers are ESTIMATE until the screen reads the fighter.
- THE OPEN CLIFF: POOL8 is a hard prerequisite.
- CLICKS: three presses (night, who, send) plus the order; the fighter's ask once after a bout.
