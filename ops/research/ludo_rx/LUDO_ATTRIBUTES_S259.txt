1. THE FINAL ATTRIBUTE LIST

To make 3D combat look like two distinct human beings rather than mirrored animation rigs running identical scripts, we split the over-compressed legacy stats (speed and defense) into kinematic drivers, elevate core psychological mechanics, and establish strict boundaries between Trained Attributes, Innate/Ceiling Constants, and Derived Parameters.

Summary of Actions on Legacy & r114 Stats
technique (Keep & Sharpen): Stripped of defensive responsibility; purely offensive biomechanical precision, trajectory efficiency, and hit-angle consistency.
power (Keep): Kinetic force, impact velocity, tissue displacement, and knockdown probability.
speed (SPLIT into TWO): Split permanently into hand_speed (fist velocity, frame duration of extensions/retractions) and footwork (step velocity, pivot snap, ring geometry maintenance). A fighter with fast hands and cement feet (e.g., late-career James Toney) is now expressible.
defense (SPLIT into TWO): Split permanently into guard (isometric limb barrier integrity, forearm coverage, blocking absorption) and head_movement (cervical/torso slips, ducks, pull-counters).
conditioning (Keep): Aerobic/anaerobic gas tank, total joule output capacity, and round-to-round recovery ceiling.
fight_iq / Ring IQ (Keep): Tactical recognition velocity, opponent habit extraction, adaptation rate, and positional decision-making.
chin (Keep as INNATE CEILING): Structural brain-trauma threshold. Cannot be trained upward; only decays through career concussive load.
composure (Promote from state to TRAINED ATTRIBUTE): Emotional regulation under fire, panic resistance, flinch suppression, and freeze-frame recovery.
recovery (Promote from derived to TRAINED ATTRIBUTE): Inter-round and in-bout physiological reset velocity (heart rate decay, lactic acid clearance, swelling resilience).
aggression (Keep as MENTAL ATTRIBUTE): Forward-engagement drive, territorial assertiveness, and willingness to exchange in pocket.
grapple (DROP): Parked completely. Boxing demo only. Zero clinch/grappling training in this slice.

Age Curves and Biological Baselines
The Twitch Group (hand_speed, footwork, recovery):
Peak: Ages $21–26$.
Decline: At age $27+$, decay accelerates by $+12%$ compound annually. At age $32+$, training returns drop by $-40%$.
The Density Group (power, guard, technique):
Peak: Ages $26–31$.
Minimal natural decay (retained through neural muscle memory).
The Wisdom Group (fight_iq, composure):
Peak: Ages $30–36$.
Zero natural decay. Gains accelerate with total career rounds boxed ($+0.05\text{ pts/round}$ in sanctioned bouts).
The Chin Attrition Rule (chin):
Starts at a rolled genetic constant ($40–95$).
Every clean flash-knockdown suffered permanently deducts $-1.5\text{ pts}$ from the max cap.
Every concussive KO loss permanently deducts $-4.0\text{ pts}$. Chin cannot be trained back.

Conditioning & The Fatigue Degrade Vector

As a fighter’s gas tank empties during a round, kinematic values degrade linearly per $10%$ tank drop beneath $50%$:

Glove Drop: For every $10%$ tank lost below $50%$, resting glove height drops $-0.03\text{ m}$. At $10%$ stamina, gloves sit on the upper abdomen.
Stance Narrowing: Feet pull inward by $-0.04\text{ m}$ per $10%$ tank loss, destroying base stability and increasing stumble probability by $+20%$.
Extension Slowdown: Extension times lengthen by $+0.015\text{ s}$ per $10%$ tank loss.
Breathing Morph: Chest-cavity morph target scales from $0.0 \to 1.0$ (mouth gaping, shoulders heaving at $0.03\text{ m}$ vertical displacement).
4. HOW ATTRIBUTES DRIVE THE FIGHT SIMULATION ENGINE

Every simulation tick ($10\text{ Hz}$ decision tick, $30\text{ Hz}$ trajectory evaluation), the engine resolves hit outcomes, spatial targets, and tissue damage using clean deterministic equations.

       OFFENSIVE RESOLUTION                           DEFENSIVE RESOLUTION
+---------------------------------+           +---------------------------------+
| technique (0.60)                |           | head_movement (0.50)            |
| fight_iq  (0.25)                |    vs     | footwork      (0.35)            |
| hand_speed(0.15)                |           | fight_iq      (0.15)            |
|   -> ATK_ROLL [0..100]          |           |   -> DEF_ROLL [0..100]          |
+---------------------------------+           +---------------------------------+
                 \                                     /
                  \                                   /
            [ ATK_ROLL - DEF_ROLL = HIT_MARGIN (-100 to +100) ]

Mathematical Engine Formulations
1. Hit Resolution (Trajectory Overlap)

$$\text{AtkRating} = (\text{technique} \times 0.60) + (\text{fight_iq} \times 0.25) + (\text{hand_speed} \times 0.15) + \text{MoveMasteryModifier}$$
$$\text{DefRating} = (\text{head_movement} \times 0.50) + (\text{footwork} \times 0.35) + (\text{fight_iq} \times 0.15)$$
$$\Delta = \text{AtkRating} - \text{DefRating} + \text{RNG}_{[-12, +12]}$$

$\Delta < -15$: Clean Miss (Whiff). Attacker commits to full recovery frames; opens counter-vulnerability window for $0.25\text{ s}$.
$-15 \le \Delta \le 5$: Glancing Contact / Deflected. Damage reduced by $75%$.
$6 \le \Delta \le 30$: Guarded / Blocked. Resolved against defender’s guard: $$\text{DamageThroughGuard} = \text{RawDamage} \times \left(1.0 - \frac{\text{guard} \times 0.85}{100}\right)$$
$\Delta > 30$: Flush Hit. Full damage applied directly to jaw/body tissue.
2. Damage & Trauma Calculation

$$\text{KineticForce} = (\text{power} \times 0.65) + (\text{technique} \times 0.35) \times \text{WeightModifier}$$
$$\text{TissueDamage} = \text{KineticForce} \times \text{PunchTypeMultiplier} \times \text{FatiguePenalty}$$

Clean counter punches landing inside the opponent’s vulnerability window receive an automatic multiplier: $$\text{Damage}_{\text{final}} = \text{TissueDamage} \times 1.45$$
3. Consciousness Check (Knockdown / Rocked State)

$$\text{TraumaThreshold} = (\text{chin} \times 0.60) + (\text{composure} \times 0.25) + (\text{conditioning} \times 0.15)$$
$$\text{StunRatio} = \frac{\text{Damage}_{\text{final}}}{\text{TraumaThreshold}}$$

If $\text{StunRatio} \ge 1.0$: Flash Knockdown. Ragdoll/get-up state machine triggered.
If $0.70 \le \text{StunRatio} < 1.0$: Rocked / Stunned. Legs enter wobbly IK state for $3.5\text{ s}$; player loses all defensive inputs; auto-retreat forced.
4. Spatial Selection & Ring Positioning
Target engagement distance is computed via fight_iq and physical reach: $$\text{IdealDistance} = (\text{FighterReach} + \text{OpponentReach}) \times 0.52 \times \text{RangeBias}$$
A fighter with $\text{fight_iq} = 80$ adjusts their footwork trajectory within $0.25\text{ s}$ of the opponent breaking distance threshold; a fighter with $\text{fight_iq} = 20$ stands frozen for up to $1.40\text{ s}$ while being trapped on the ropes.
5. MIGRATION SPECIFICATION: 9 LEGACY STATS $\to$ 11 CANONICAL STATS

To preserve save compatibility and ensure existing stable rosters remain intact, use this deterministic mapping script.

def migrate_legacy_fighter(old):
    new = {}
    
    # 1. Direct Preservations
    new['technique']    = old['technique']
    new['power']        = old['power']
    new['conditioning'] = old['conditioning']
    new['fight_iq']     = old['fight_iq']
    new['aggression']   = old['aggression']
    
    # 2. Speed Split (Hand Speed vs Footwork)
    # If a fighter was fast, distribute into hand speed and footwork based on technique bias
    tech_ratio = old['technique'] / 100.0
    new['hand_speed']   = int(round(old['speed'] * 0.70 + old['technique'] * 0.30))
    new['footwork']     = int(round(old['speed'] * 0.60 + old['fight_iq'] * 0.40))
    
    # 3. Defense Split (Guard vs Head Movement)
    # High agility fighters lean head movement; low agility / high chin lean guard
    new['guard']         = int(round(old['defense'] * 0.70 + old['chin'] * 0.30))
    new['head_movement']= int(round(old['defense'] * 0.60 + old['speed'] * 0.40))
    
    # 4. Composure (Elevated from Fight IQ + Defense)
    new['composure']    = int(round(old['fight_iq'] * 0.50 + old['defense'] * 0.30 + old['chin'] * 0.20))
    
    # 5. Recovery (Elevated from Conditioning + Chin)
    new['recovery']     = int(round(old['conditioning'] * 0.70 + old['chin'] * 0.30))
    
    # 6. Chin (Clamp Innate Value)
    new['chin']         = old['chin']
    
    # 7. Drop Grapple completely
    # del old['grapple']
    
    # Clamp all attributes safely to [1, 99]
    for key in new:
        new[key] = max(1, min(99, new[key]))
        
    return new

6. THE 4 ATTRIBUTES THAT MATTER MOST TO MAKE THE FIGHT LOOK HUMAN

If we only had engineering budget to hook up four attributes to 3D animation parameters before Steam Early Access, these are the four, ranked in order of human visual perception:

[RANK 1: FOOTWORK]        --> Stops the ice-skating; anchors the fighters in space.
[RANK 2: GUARD]           --> Stops the open-torso mannequin stare; creates visual defense.
[RANK 3: COMPOSURE]       --> Stops the robotic indifference to pain; creates vulnerability.
[RANK 4: HAND SPEED]      --> Breaks uniform punch cadences; creates human timing variation.

1. footwork (Rank 1: Ground Contact & Spatial Authority)
Why it matters most: Humans are exceptionally attuned to gravity and ground locomotion. When two boxers float, slide, cross their legs, or stand static at $0.55\text{ m}$, the brain instantly reads them as cheap video game meshes. Giving fighters variable step cycles, crisp pivots, and distinct approach velocities instantly makes them read as real athletes carrying physical mass.
2. guard (Rank 2: Combat Posture & Defensive Readiness)
Why it ranks second: A real fighter’s posture is defined by their guard. A fighter whose hands hang at their upper chest with flaring elbows looks untrained or broken. Dynamic guard height (tucking behind the lead shoulder, keeping the rear glove welded to the zygomatic arch, tightening the elbows on body assault) creates the visual impression of self-preservation.
3. composure (Rank 3: Physical Reaction to Trauma & Danger)
Why it ranks third: The clearest tell of a soulless game animation is an avatar taking a flush $80\text{-power}$ right hook to the jaw and immediately resetting into an idle animation with zero emotional or physical recoil. composure dictates the flinch, the head-snap recovery duration, the desperate clinch reach, and whether the fighter’s eyes track the opponent when hurt. It injects human fragility into the sim.
4. hand_speed (Rank 4: Rhythmic Asymmetry & Striking Texture)
Why it ranks fourth: When every jab extends in exactly $0.20\text{ s}$ and retracts in $0.18\text{ s}$, the fight feels like a metronome. Real boxing is defined by broken rhythms: slow pawing range-finders punctuated by explosive combinations. Scaling punch extension/retraction across the $0.20\text{ s} \dots 0.28\text{ s}$ spectrum ensures that a blazing speedster looks unmistakably distinct from a thudding, methodical heavy hitter.
