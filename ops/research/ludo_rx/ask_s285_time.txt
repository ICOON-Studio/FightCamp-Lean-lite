TEXT ANSWER ONLY. FightCamp design question from the studio owner (he approves the final verdict): TIME PROGRESSION.

THE GAME TODAY (facts, not proposals): a boxing gym management sim (Steam PC, deep manager, Two Point Museum look + Mad Games Tycoon 2 build flow). The player owns a gym, builds it from an empty shell, signs boxers, trains them, books fights, watches bouts. Time model now:
- A gym DAY runs in real time on the 3D floor: 600 game minutes (08:00-18:00), 0.2 real seconds per game minute at 1x = about 2 real minutes per day, about 14 real minutes per 7-day week at 1x. Speed buttons: Pause, Play, Fast (multiplier on that).
- S276 owner ruling "nothing interrupts, everything that happens triggers an icon and the player can open it through the UI": the day rolls into the next by itself; the morning plan card, decision cards (fight offers, injuries, hires, debt) wait behind top-bar icons; nothing auto-pauses; only the player's own Pause stops the clock.
- The training plan is per weekday (a week plan repeats); fights are booked weeks ahead; a fight camp is the weeks before a bout; the fight itself is its own screen (watched live).
- Economy is weekly/monthly (wages + upkeep; members pay; purses).

THE OWNER'S PROPOSAL: use Mad Games Tycoon 2's time progression - time flows continuously in WEEKS (MGT2: a week ticks by in real time, the date advances day by day in the corner), and the game AUTO-PAUSES when something needs the player's decision.
