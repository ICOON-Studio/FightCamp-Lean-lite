PART A: TRAINING WEAR & READINESS ENTER THE BOUT (STEP 1b)

The design goal of carrying training wear into fight night is not to create a punishing death spiral, but to make the Fight Week Taper an active tactical trade-off. If a manager over-drills right up to Friday night, their boxer walks in flat, vulnerable, and sluggish. If they taper cleanly, they preserve full operational stats.

A1. Regional Body Condition Carryover
1. Daily Wear Accumulation & Decay

The gym already logs wear on each of the six body regions:
$$\text{Regions} = {\text{head_neck}, \text{torso_core}, \text{left_arm}, \text{right_arm}, \text{left_leg}, \text{right_leg}}$$

During camp, each daily training activity contributes raw wear points ($\Delta W_{\text{raw}}$) to targeted regions:

Heavy Sparring: $+18$ to $+24$ to head_neck, $+10$ to torso_core, $+6$ to arms.
Heavy Bag Work: $+14$ to $+18$ to active arms, $+6$ to torso_core.
Mitt / Pad Work: $+8$ to arms, $+6$ to torso_core.
Roadwork / Hill Sprints: $+16$ to $+22$ to left_leg and right_leg.
Conditioning / Calisthenics: $+10$ across all regions evenly.
2. Natural Daily Overnight Recovery (Baseline Taper)

Every midnight tick in the gym, before fight night:
$$W_{\text{day_end}} = \max\left(0.0,; W_{\text{day_start}} \times (1.0 - \text{DecayRate}) - R_{\text{flat}}\right)$$

Active Training Day: $\text{DecayRate} = 0.25$, $R_{\text{flat}} = 4.0$
Light Recovery Day (Walks, Foam Roll, Massage): $\text{DecayRate} = 0.45$, $R_{\text{flat}} = 10.0$
Full Rest Day (Bed rest, Hydration): $\text{DecayRate} = 0.65$, $R_{\text{flat}} = 18.0$
3. Fight Night Entry Formula (Converting $W_{\text{net}}$ to Ring Condition %)

On fight night at 00:00 (weigh-in / pre-fight lock), raw wear $W_{r}$ for each region $r$ is converted into starting ring structural health:
$$\text{StartingCondition}_r (%) = \text{clamp}\left(100.0 - (W_r \times 0.75),; \text{Floor}_r,; 100.0\right)$$

Absolute Hard Floor ($\text{Floor}_r$): 70.0%. Even an catastrophically mismanaged camp cannot send a licensed professional into an officially sanctioned ring below 70% structural integrity; commission doctors will intervene or natural biological resilience bottoms out here.
Soft Floor Tapering: A fighter entering with $W_r = 0$ enters at $100.0%$. A fighter with $W_r = 40$ (e.g., hard sparring on Thursday before a Saturday bout) enters at: $$100.0 - (40 \times 0.75) = 70.0%$$
4. Taper Recovery Math (Worked 3-Day Taper Example)

Suppose a fighter ends heavy training Wednesday evening with $W_{\text{head}} = 38.0$ and $W_{\text{legs}} = 34.0$:

Thursday (Light Recovery): $$W_{\text{head}} = \max(0, 38.0 \times (1 - 0.45) - 10.0) = \max(0, 20.9 - 10.0) = 10.9$$ $$W_{\text{legs}} = \max(0, 34.0 \times (1 - 0.45) - 10.0) = \max(0, 18.7 - 10.0) = 8.7$$
Friday (Full Rest / Weigh-In Day): $$W_{\text{head}} = \max(0, 10.9 \times (1 - 0.65) - 18.0) = \max(0, 3.815 - 18.0) = 0.0$$ $$W_{\text{legs}} = \max(0, 8.7 \times (1 - 0.65) - 18.0) = \max(0, 3.045 - 18.0) = 0.0$$
Saturday Night Ring Entry: Both regions enter at exactly $100.0%$. A standard 48-to-72 hour taper completely clears intense training wear.
A2. Starting Stamina & Max Stamina from Readiness

Readiness ($R$) is an aggregate 0–100 gym metric calculated from overall Central Nervous System (CNS) strain, sleep quality, and calorie deficit.

1. Max Stamina Cap ($S_{\max}$)

Standard base max stamina is $100.0$.
$$S_{\max} = \text{clamp}\left(80.0 + (0.20 \times R),; 80.0,; 100.0\right)$$

At $R = 100$: $S_{\max} = 100.0$.
At $R = 75$: $S_{\max} = 95.0$.
At $R = 50$: $S_{\max} = 90.0$.
At $R = 0$ (extreme overtraining exhaustion): $S_{\max} = 80.0$.
2. Starting Stamina Tank ($S_{\text{start}}$)

$$S_{\text{start}} = S_{\max} \times \text{clamp}\left(0.85 + (0.15 \times \frac{R}{100.0}),; 0.85,; 1.00\right)$$

At $R = 100$: $S_{\text{start}} = 100.0 \times 1.00 = 100.0$.
At $R = 80$: $S_{\max} = 96.0$, $S_{\text{start}} = 96.0 \times (0.85 + 0.12) = 96.0 \times 0.97 = 93.12$.
At $R = 50$: $S_{\max} = 90.0$, $S_{\text{start}} = 90.0 \times (0.85 + 0.075) = 90.0 \times 0.925 = 83.25$.
At $R \le 20$: $S_{\max} = 84.0$, $S_{\text{start}} = 84.0 \times 0.88 = 73.92$.
A3. In-Fight Consequences of Overtraining (Using Live Levers Only)

No new ad-hoc modifiers or artificial scripting are added. Wear and reduced readiness depress the exact combat levers deployed in step 1:

1. Evasion Penalty via Leg Condition ($e_{\text{eff}}$)

Evasion requires spring and lateral footwork. The live evasion stat $e$ is modulated by the average health of both legs:
$$\text{LegIntegrity} = \frac{\text{Condition}{\text{left_leg}} + \text{Condition}{\text{right_leg}}}{200.0} \quad \in [0.70,; 1.00]$$
$$e_{\text{effective}} = e \times (0.60 + 0.40 \times \text{LegIntegrity})$$

At 100% Legs: $e_{\text{eff}} = e \times 1.00$.
At 70% Legs (Worst-case heavy roadwork Friday night): $$e_{\text{eff}} = e \times (0.60 + 0.28) = e \times 0.88 \quad (-12% \text{ evasion})$$
Direct Engine Consequence: A fighter with base evasion 70 drops to $61.6$. In the live whiff formula: $$\text{whiff} = 0.22 \times \left(\frac{2e}{e+a}\right)^{1.5}$$ Against an attacker with accuracy 60, their baseline whiff rate drops from $27.9%$ to $23.1%$, immediately increasing their opponent's connect rate and reducing counter-strike opportunities.
2. Chin Vulnerability via Head/Neck Condition ($\text{chin}_{\text{eff}}$)

Neck and spinal fatigue reduce the boxer's ability to absorb rotational head acceleration:
$$\text{HeadIntegrity} = \frac{\text{Condition}{\text{head_neck}}}{100.0} \quad \in [0.70,; 1.00]$$
$$\text{chin}{\text{effective}} = \text{chin} \times (0.70 + 0.30 \times \text{HeadIntegrity})$$

At 100% Head: $\text{chin}_{\text{eff}} = \text{chin} \times 1.00$.
At 70% Head (Heavy sparring Thursday/Friday): $$\text{chin}_{\text{eff}} = \text{chin} \times (0.70 + 0.21) = \text{chin} \times 0.91 \quad (-9% \text{ chin})$$
Direct Engine Consequence: The live knockdown and shock multiplier is: $$\text{Mult}{\text{KD}} = \text{clamp}(1.6 - 1.2 \times \frac{\text{chin}}{100.0},; 0.5,; 1.4)$$ If base chin is 50, standard $\text{Mult}{\text{KD}} = 1.6 - 0.60 = 1.00$. Under 70% head wear, $\text{chin}{\text{eff}} = 45.5$, yielding $\text{Mult}{\text{KD}} = 1.6 - 0.546 = 1.054$ ($+5.4%$ shock received and wider knockdown check windows).
3. Guard / Accuracy Penalty via Arm Condition ($a_{\text{eff}}$)

Tired shoulders and forearms drop punch speed and snap:
$$\text{ArmIntegrity}{\text{arm}} = \frac{\text{Condition}{\text{arm}}}{100.0} \quad \in [0.70,; 1.00]$$
$$a_{\text{effective}} = a \times (0.75 + 0.25 \times \text{ArmIntegrity})$$

At 70% Arm Condition: $a_{\text{eff}} = a \times 0.925$ ($-7.5%$ accuracy).
Direct Engine Consequence: Lowers clean punch conversion: $$\text{clean_rate} = 0.85 + 0.30 \times \frac{a_{\text{eff}}}{100}$$ and directly feeds into the defender's whiff formula, causing the worn fighter to miss more often.
4. Accelerated Whiff Fatigue via Starting Stamina

Because an overtrained fighter enters with reduced $S_{\max}$ (down to 80.0) and lower current stamina, the existing live whiff penalty:
$$\text{Whiff Stamina Cost} = 1.5\times \text{Base Stamina Cost}$$
burns through their available pool roughly $25\text{–}35%$ faster in real round time, hitting the exhausted fatigue threshold (under $25%$ stamina) by round 4 instead of round 8.

A5. Pitfall Mitigation & Balance Targets
The Trap: "The Taper Trap" (Over-Punishing Honest Training)

In many sports sims, players hate readiness systems because any training at all feels like self-sabotage. If training Wednesday ruins Saturday's bout, the player stops using gym systems and defaults to mindless rest spam.

Mitigation Rules:
Fast Natural Clearance: A 2-day taper (Thursday light + Friday rest) must reliably clear up to $45$ points of wear back to $100.0%$.
No Wear Accumulation Below Thresholds: Base gym maintenance (mitts, tactical review, video study) generates $0.0$ wear.
Positive Camp Buffs Trump Small Wear: A fighter with high sharpness/tactical familiarity gains $+10%$ execution speed, which mathematically outweighs a $2\text{–}3%$ wear dip.
Target Distribution for Sanctioned Bouts

Across 1,000 simulated AI and player-managed fight camps:

Optimal Entry ($\ge 95%$ all regions, Readiness $\ge 92%$): $82.0%$ of all bouts. Sensible standard preparation will comfortably hit peak readiness.
Slightly Dinged ($90% - 94%$ in one region): $13.5%$ of bouts. Occurs when a manager pushes conditioning into late Wednesday.
Severely Compromised ($< 90%$ in any region or Readiness $< 80%$): $4.5%$ of bouts. Exclusively happens when the manager schedules full-contact sparring on Thursday or Friday, takes a short-notice replacement fight on 3 days' notice, or severely blows weight cut protocols.

B2 Selection Rules: The Three Coach Lines

The coach evaluates the round metrics in 3 distinct functional channels:

Slot 1 (Opponent Vulnerability): What did the opponent fail at or leave open in Round $N$?
Slot 2 (Player Risk / Structural Danger): Where did our fighter take critical damage or display technical breakdowns in Round $N$?
Slot 3 (Stamina / Tactical Efficiency): How is the stamina balance and punch economy trending?
Slot 1: Opponent Vulnerability Selection Logic (Priority Order)
Opponent Whiff Rate $\ge 40%$ (Min 10 thrown):
Data Check: $\text{OppWhiffed}{\text{round}} / \text{OppThrown}{\text{round}} \ge 0.40$.
Dialogue: "His {MOST_WHIFFED_PUNCH} is finding pure air — slipped {SLIPPED_N} of {THROWN_N}. Make him pay on the miss."
Action Button: [EXPLOIT WHIFF COUNTERS] (Adjusts tactical instruction: Counter_Frequency = Aggressive, $+25%$ counter check commitment).
Opponent Low Chin / Rocked State:
Data Check: Opponent Chin shock state was triggered $\ge 1$ time or Chin stat $\le 45$.
Dialogue: `"His
