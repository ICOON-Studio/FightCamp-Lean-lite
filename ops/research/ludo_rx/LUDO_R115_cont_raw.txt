Part 4: In-Game Help Document
HOW FIGHTS ARE DECIDED IN FIGHTCAMP
1. Every punch starts with an Evasion check: if your Accuracy and Footwork beat his Evasion, the punch tracks; otherwise, it Whiffs completely, burning heavy stamina.
2. If tracked, it hits the Guard: a high Guard absorbs 70% to 85% of the force onto the gloves and arms, preventing concussive damage and facial cuts.
3. Punches that pierce the Guard land Cleanly, transferring raw kinetic power directly into the head or ribs.
4. Power is scaled by Technique and Move Mastery: a poorly drilled Raw punch wastes over 35% of its kinetic energy through sloppy mechanics.
5. Head impacts test the Chin: clean force is divided by your Chin rating and current concussive wear to calculate Head Shock.
6. Shock above 1.0 stuns the fighter; Shock above 1.4 triggers a Knockdown check where low stamina makes falling more likely.
7. Knocked-down fighters use Conditioning, Recovery, and Ring IQ to clear Shock below 0.4 before the referee counts to ten.
8. Body shots do not knock fighters down easily, but permanently shrink their maximum Stamina pool for every round that follows.
9. Fast Hands shorten your punch windup frames; high Move Mastery cuts your recovery frames so you cannot be easily countered.
10. Low Stamina ruins everything: when exhausted, punching power drops by 20%, guard absorption drops by half, and evasion drops by 45%.
11. Mastered Pad Chains fire automatically when your fighter recognizes the opponent's telegraphed combat tell.
12. Fights are won in the gym: training stations increase the exact attributes and move masteries that drive every calculation above.
