FIGHTCAMP — LEAD DESIGN AUDIT: ROUND 103 LOOP 1 METERS
1. THE CONTRADICTION: CARD "FRESH" VS. METERS "OVERDONE"
Summary Verdict

CHANGE IT. Showing "FRESH" next to "OVERDONE" breaks simulation credibility within 10 seconds of play.

The Problem

The legacy top-left fighter card reports acute session stamina (he just woke up, ate breakfast, and walked into the gym at 08:50 with a full green stamina tank). The new Loop 1 load panel below it reports chronic systemic attrition (six consecutive hard days without variation, Foster monotony index $> 2.0$, weekly load 110/100). To an uninitiated player, this is an irreconcilable contradiction: "Is my guy fresh or is he overdone?"

The One Clear Rule



The upper card status word must reflect the same time horizon as its meters, or it must be explicitly prefixed by its time horizon.



The Fix

Rename the upper card status to:
$$\text{"SESSION: FRESH"}$$
Or, better yet, unify the hierarchy so that the card only shows live vitals and leaves all state-of-body descriptors to the load panel:

Top Card Line 4: Replace the naked word FRESH with:
STAMINA: [████████████████] 100% (READY)

Load Panel State Pill: Keep the diagnostic word here:
WEEK LOAD: OVERDONE (110/100)


Strict Rule: Never show an unqualified physiological state adjective (FRESH, TIRED, GROGGY) on the identity card when a systemic load panel is docked directly beneath it. If qualified, use TODAY: READY vs. WEEK: OVERDONE.

2. PANEL REVIEW: LAYOUT, VISUALS, READABILITY & THE 3 FIXES
Evaluation of Current State
Layout & Order: Logical top-down flow: Identity Card $\to$ Goal Tracker $\to$ Load Panel $\to$ Swap Action Button.
Colors: Green (#2ECC71), Amber (#F39C12), Red (#E74C3C) follow standard sports-science load conventions.
Wording Gradients:
Gains Slope: BUILDING $\to$ FLATTENING $\to$ DROPPING (Clear, actionable, intuitive).
Attrition Zone: OPTIMAL $\to$ PUSHING IT $\to$ OVERDONE (Hits the right gritty, blue-collar boxing tone without turning into an academic lab monitor).
What a First-Time Player Will Misread
The Double Name Header: The boxer's name (MARCO VELA) is printed twice within 150 vertical pixels (once at the very top of the identity card, once at the top of the load panel). The player will wonder if the bottom panel belongs to a different fighter or a sparring partner.
"DROPPING" vs. "+35.8 TODAY": The gains bar shows a bright green fill with text reading +35.8 today, but the right-hand label says DROPPING in red/amber. The player thinks: "It says plus 35, why is it dropping?" They will not understand that DROPPING refers to the rate of adaptation slope over 7 days, not today's absolute numerical yield.
The Attrition Hazard Stripes: The diagonal hazard stripes on the red bar look like a missing texture or an active clickable button rather than a status state.
The POWER +10.0 Sub-label: Having a specific attribute gain (POWER +10.0) floating above the Attrition label makes it look like the attrition bar is measuring power cost.

FIX 1: Kill the duplicate name and visually bind the two cards into ONE unified column. Remove MARCO VELA from the load panel. Connect the load panel directly to the base of the fighter card with a single $1\text{ px}$ dark border (#2A2A2A). It is one athlete; it must be one visual stack.
FIX 2: Disambiguate Rate vs. Value on the Gains Bar. Change +35.8 today to +35.8 pts and change the slope label from DROPPING to ADAPTATION: DROPPING (RATE -40%). This makes it immediately obvious that he is still learning, but the efficiency of his training has collapsed.
FIX 3: Visual separation between the Action Button and the Warning Pill. Right now, the red warning pill and the SWAP TO RECOVERY button are stacked as an awkward red-on-black sandwich. Turn the warning into a clean, single-line alert banner ([ ! ] Gains halved — monotony limit hit) with a wide, high-contrast, pulsating amber button directly underneath: [ SWAP TO RECOVERY (STRETCH MATS) ].

4. 3D FLOOR READABILITY: CHEAPEST VISUAL CUES FOR "OVERDONE"

The simulation should be readable from the high camera without looking at the 2D UI. When a fighter is OVERDONE (week load $> 100$, monotony $> 2.0$), the following low-cost visual cues must trigger on the 3D floor:

Visual Cues Checklist (Keep / Change)

| Cue Candidate | Decision | Implementation Details (Cheapest & Most Readable) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Punch Animation Speed Scale | KEEP | Scale animation playback speed to 0.75x. The punches visibly drag, have less snap, and hang on the bag longer. Zero asset cost: anim_player.speed_scale = 0.75. |
| Idle Stance Pose (Rest Cycle) | KEEP | Inject a breathing hunch. Instead of an active bounce on the balls of the feet, switch the station idle to a heavy-chested posture with shoulders slumped forward $8^\circ$. |
| Sweat / Steam Particle System | KEEP | Dense sweat droplet particle emitter. A lightweight particle burst from the forehead/chest (small translucent droplets flying off on bag impacts). High readability from a $46^\circ$ down-camera. |
| Facial Texture Swaps | CHANGE (DROP) | Too small to read from a $22\text{ m}$ camera distance. Do not waste texture memory or shader passes on grimaces or closed eyes that are $3\text{ px}$ wide on screen. |
| Dynamic Audio Snap Pitch | KEEP | Drop bag strike SFX pitch by $-3\text{ semitones}$ and volume by $-4\text{ dB}$. Instead of a crisp, sharp leather crack, the heavy bag makes a dull, flat thud. Immediate subconscious feedback that the fighter has no pop. |
| Ground Projection Contact Ring | KEEP | Tint the player's ground selection ring from neutral white/gold to a pulsing dark amber/crimson. Instant floor-level confirmation that this station is currently burning bad fuel. |

Summary of 3D Floor Scripting
# FighterController.gd (Simplified 3D Floor Hook)
func update_fighter_visual_load_state(attrition_state: int) -> void:
    match attrition_state:
        AttritionZone.OPTIMAL:
            anim_player.speed_scale = 1.0
            impact_audio.pitch_scale = 1.0
            sweat_particles.emitting = false
            selection_ring.modulate = Color(0.9, 0.7, 0.2, 0.4) # Warm Gold
        AttritionZone.PUSHING_IT:
            anim_player.speed_scale = 0.9
            impact_audio.pitch_scale = 0.95
            sweat_particles.emitting = true
            sweat_particles.amount = 8
            selection_ring.modulate = Color(0.9, 0.5, 0.1, 0.6) # Amber
        AttritionZone.OVERDONE:
            anim_player.speed_scale = 0.75                      # Sluggish hits
            impact_audio.pitch_scale = 0.82                     # Dull thud
            sweat_particles.emitting = true
            sweat_particles.amount = 24                         # Heavy dripping
            selection_ring.modulate = Color(0.85, 0.15, 0.15, 0.8)# Crimson Hazard
