FIGHTCAMP — STUDIO HEAD REVIEW & OFFICE ARCHITECTURE (ROUND 105.1)
1. HONEST VERDICT: DO WE AGREE WITH THE DESK PC HUB?
The Verdict: YES, WITH ONE STRICT GUARDRAIL.

We agree with the operator's shift. Separating The Floor (Execution & Observation) from The Office (Agency, Contracts & Macro Management) creates a visceral boundary that turns FightCamp from an arcade mini-game collection into a genuine management simulation.

However, it will fail if it creates navigation friction. In sports sims, forcing the player to click a door, watch an elevator/walk sequence, click a desk, and click a screen just to check a fight date destroys player momentum. The physical office must serve as an immersive, tactile space, while maintaining immediate fast-path access.

Industry Comparison: What Others Got Right & Wrong

The Arguments FOR the Office:
Thematic Authenticity: An actual boxing coach/manager does not book sanctioned 8-round professional bouts by shouting across a heavy bag rack. They sit down in a tobacco-stained, trophy-lined room, look at contracts, and check finances.
UI De-cluttering: Removing modal overlays and booking lists from the 3D training floor frees up the broadcast camera view, keeping attention focused on Danny Reyes, his technique, and his coach's pad work.
Natural Space for Narrative Confrontation: A fighter being cut or demanding a purse increase looks natural across an office mahogany desk. Doing that on a gym mat looks out of place.
The Arguments AGAINST (and how we solve them):
Friction risk: If booking requires navigating back and forth across rooms, players will resent it.
Solution: Single-click transitions between Floor and Office via an persistent tab switch on the primary HUD, paired with world-space notification badges on the office door and PC monitor.

Camera Transition Behavior
Rule: DO NOT stay at the top-down angle with a tiny centered window. It disconnects the player from the world.
Implementation: Smooth camera push-in.
Clicking the PC triggers a 0.35-second cubic ease-in-out camera transition (Godot Tween) from the overhead office vantage down to a high-contrast, straight-on view of the desk monitor.
The physical 3D frame of the monitor surrounds the UI edges, then cleanly blends into the fullscreen 1920×1080 management terminal interface.
Pressing [ESC] or [EXIT PC] tweens the camera back out to room view in 0.25 seconds.

7. GDSCRIPT: OFFICE INTERACTION & CAMERA SYSTEM
Below is the clean, self-contained controller handling the PC click, camera transitions, and tab management.
# OfficeController.gd
class_name OfficeController
extends Node3D
signal pc_entered
signal pc_exited
signal contract_signed(bout_id: String)
signal goal_resolved(chosen_next_goal: String)
@export var office_camera: Camera3D
@export var pc_screen_viewport: Control
@export var monitor_mesh: MeshInstance3D
@export var notification_billboard: Node3D
var is_at_terminal: bool = false
var default_cam_pos: Vector3
var default_cam_rot: Vector3
# Precise local desk monitor close-up target
const TERMINAL_CAM_POS: Vector3 = Vector3(0.68, 1.15, -0.22)
const TERMINAL_CAM_ROT: Vector3 = Vector3(-12.0, 15.0, 0.0)
func _ready() -> void:
assert(office_camera != null, "Office camera reference missing.")
default_cam_pos = office_camera.position
default_cam_rot = office_camera.rotation_degrees
set_contract_alert(true)
func _input(event: InputEvent) -> void:
if event.is_action_pressed("ui_cancel") and is_at_terminal:
exit_pc_terminal()
func interact_with_pc() -> void:
if is_at_terminal:
return
is_at_terminal = true
set_contract_alert(false)
var tween: Tween = create_tween().set_parallel(true).set_trans(Tween.TRANS_CUBIC).set_ease(Tween.EASE_IN_OUT)
tween.tween_property(office_camera, "position", TERMINAL_CAM_POS, 0.35)
tween.tween_property(office_camera, "rotation_degrees", TERMINAL_CAM_ROT, 0.35)
tween.chain().tween_callback(func():
pc_screen_viewport.visible = true
pc_entered.emit()
)
func exit_pc_terminal() -> void:
if not is_at_terminal:
return
pc_screen_viewport.visible = false
var tween: Tween = create_tween().set_parallel(true).set_trans(Tween.TRANS_CUBIC).set_ease(Tween.EASE_IN_OUT)
tween.tween_property(office_camera, "position", default_cam_pos, 0.28)
tween.tween_property(office_camera, "rotation_degrees", default_cam_rot, 0.28)
tween.chain().tween_callback(func():
is_at_terminal = false
pc_exited.emit()
)
func set_contract_alert(has_offers: bool) -> void:
if notification_billboard:
notification_billboard.visible = has_offers
# Update CRT dynamic monitor glow
var mat: StandardMaterial3D = monitor_mesh.get_active_material(0) as StandardMaterial3D
if mat:
mat.emission_enabled = has_offers
mat.emission = Color("38bdf8") if has_offers else Color("000000")
func on_contract_accepted(bout_id: String) -> void:
contract_signed.emit(bout_id)
exit_pc_terminal()
