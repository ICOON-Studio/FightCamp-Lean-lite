Visual Rules for the Chin Row
Lock Icon & Label:
Text label: "CHIN". Directly adjacent at $X = 54\text{ px}$, render a $10 \times 10\text{ px}$ padlock glyph locked.svg in #9CA3AF.
Tooltip suffix: "(Innate Genetic Trait)".
Ceiling Bar Suppression:
Chin has no future ceiling. It is biologically capped at its starting rolled value.
Do not render the #374151 ceiling lead track.
Pristine State Representation:
The bar fill uses a distinct structural bone/silver color: #94A3B8 (Matte Silver/Bone Slate).
Trend arrow column: Render a static dash — in #4B5563.
Post-Concussion / Knockdown Loss State:
When a fighter sustains a knockdown ($-1.5\text{ pts}$) or KO ($-4.0\text{ pts}$), the bar visually communicates the irreversible deficit:
Remaining Chin Bar: Rendered in #94A3B8 from $0 \dots \text{CurrentChin} \times 2.0\text{ px}$.
The Trauma Gap (Lost Chin): Rendered from $\text{CurrentChin} \times 2.0\text{ px} \dots \text{OriginalChin} \times 2.0\text{ px}$ using an oxblood crosshatch pattern (#7F1D1D background with $45^\circ$ diagonal stripes in #DC2626).
Trend Indicator: Flashes a red down-arrow ▼ (#EF4444) with the delta float text (e.g., "-1.5" or "-5.5") for 14 in-game calendar days following the bout.
5. SHAPE & FORM RADAR SPECIFICATION
The Architecture Decision: The 5 Derived Fight Dimensions

The radar must not attempt to display all 11 attributes. An 11-axis polygon produces an unreadable visual "starfish" where adjacent data points (such as technique and power) bleed together.

Instead, the central radar is repurposed to display the 5 Canonical Derived Combat Pillars. This bridges the gap between gym attributes and in-ring fight mechanics:

                         [ ACCURACY ] (Top: 90 deg)
                             / \
                            /   \
                           /     \
  [ EVASION ]             /       \             [ DAMAGE MITIGATION ]
  (Upper Left: 162 deg)   \       /             (Upper Right: 18 deg)
                           \     /
                            \   /
      [ TRAUMA THRESHOLD ]   \ /   [ KO LEVERAGE ]
      (Lower Left: 234 deg)   V    (Lower Right: 306 deg)

The 5 Axes & Underlying Engine Formulas

$$\begin{array}{|l|c|l|l|}
\hline
\textbf{Radar Axis Label} & \textbf{Angle} & \textbf{Exact Math Formulation (0--100)} & \textbf{Tactical Meaning in Fight} \
\hline
\textbf{ACCURACY} & 90^\circ\text{ (Top)} & \text{technique} \times 0.60 + \text{fight_iq} \times 0.25 + \text{hand_speed} \times 0.15 & \text{Hit probability on targeted zones} \
\textbf{DAMAGE MIT} & 18^\circ\text{ (Up-R)} & \text{guard} \times 0.65 + \text{composure} \times 0.20 + \text{technique} \times 0.15 & \text{Force reduction through arms/shell} \
\textbf{KO LEVERAGE} & 306^\circ\text{ (Dn-R)} & \text{power} \times 0.70 + \text{technique} \times 0.30 & \text{Tissue damage and knockdown shock} \
\textbf{TRAUMA THRESH} & 234^\circ\text{ (Dn-L)} & \text{chin} \times 0.60 + \text{composure} \times 0.25 + \text{conditioning} \times 0.15 & \text{Resistance to being rocked/stunned} \
\textbf{EVASION} & 162^\circ\text{ (Up-L)} & \text{head_movement} \times 0.50 + \text{footwork} \times 0.35 + \text{fight_iq} \times 0.15 & \text{Clean-miss slip and pull rate} \
\hline
\end{array}$$

Visual Styling of the Radar
Center Coordinate: $X = 210\text{ px}, Y = 160\text{ px}$ relative to Radar Card.
Radius: $R = 105\text{ px}$.
Web Rings: 4 concentric pentagons at $25, 50, 75, 100$ value thresholds. Color: #262F3D ($1\text{ px}$ stroke).
Polygon Fill: #E8B04B with alpha 0.22. Perimeter stroke: $2\text{ px}$ solid #E8B04B.
Opponent / Division Average Overlay (Toggle): Ghosted polygon in #4B5563 (alpha 0.10) showing division top-10 median.
