

Part 1: Skill Training $\rightarrow$ Move Quality Architecture

In real boxing, two fighters can throw identical biomechanical actions—such as a lead left hook—with drastically different outcomes. One throws a wide, looping arm punch that telegraphs early, lands on elbows, and leaves them exposed; the other throws a tight, compact strike that snaps off the hip, lands between the guard, and returns instantly to protect the jaw.

In FightCamp, a move is not simply an unlocked binary flag. Every certified boxing punch in a fighter’s repertoire possesses an independent Move Mastery Level ($M \in [0, 100]$) that scales its mechanical execution independently of, and synergistically with, the fighter's base attributes.

(a) What Move Quality Is Made Of & Exact Operational Formulas

A move's live execution quality is driven by:

Base Move Archetype Defaults: Static parameters inherent to the punch catalog (e.g., a Jab has a baseline faster windup than a Rear Overhand).
Move Mastery ($M \in [0, 100]$): The specific drill depth invested into that individual punch.
Underlying Fighter Attributes (0–100): Hand Speed ($HS$), Accuracy ($ACC$), Power ($POW$), Footwork ($FT$), Technique ($TECH$), and Ring IQ ($IQ$).

1. Execution Speed & Windup Frames

Windup frames represent the visual telegraph window before the punch reaches full extension. The game runs its combat engine at a fixed 60 ticks per second (TPS).

$$\text{WindupTicks} = \text{round}\left( \text{BaseTicks} \times \left(1.0 - \left(0.18 \times \frac{HS}{100}\right) - \left(0.22 \times \frac{M}{100}\right)\right) \right)$$

Example (Standard Lead Jab, $\text{BaseTicks} = 16$ frames / 266 ms):

Raw Jab ($M = 10, HS = 50$): $16 \times (1.0 - 0.09 - 0.022) = 14.2$ ticks ($\approx 14$ frames / 233 ms).
Signature Jab ($M = 95, HS = 85$): $16 \times (1.0 - 0.153 - 0.209) = 10.2$ ticks ($\approx 10$ frames / 166 ms). The opponent has $40%$ less time to react.
2. Dynamic Accuracy & Dispersion Cone

Accuracy dictates whether the punch lands on the intended target point or disperses into defensive buffers.

$$\text{EffectiveAccuracy} = \left(\text{MoveBaseAcc} \times 0.40\right) + \left(ACC \times 0.35\right) + \left(M \times 0.25\right)$$

Clean Landing Threshold ($CLT$): Punches that beat opponent evasion by $\ge 20$ points on a 100-scale land with 100% force.
Mastery scales the punch's ability to locate anatomical weak points: each 10 points of $M$ increases critical target tracking (ear-hole, chin tip, solar plexus, floating liver rib) by $+2.5%$.
3. Power Transfer Efficiency ($\eta_{p}$)

Punches thrown without mastery leak energy through poor foot placement, flared elbows, or incomplete hip rotation.

$$\eta_{p} = 0.50 + \left(0.25 \times \frac{TECH}{100}\right) + \left(0.25 \times \frac{M}{100}\right)$$
$$\text{OutputDamage} = \text{BaseMoveForce} \times \left(\frac{POW}{100}\right) \times \eta_{p}$$

Raw Move ($M = 10, TECH = 50$): $\eta_{p} = 0.50 + 0.125 + 0.025 = 0.65$ ($65%$ power transfer; $35%$ of punch force is lost to sloppy mechanics).
Signature Move ($M = 90, TECH = 80$): $\eta_{p} = 0.50 + 0.20 + 0.225 = 0.925$ ($92.5%$ mechanical efficiency).
4. Recovery Frames & Defensive Vulnerability Window

The time taken to retract the punch back into guard position. During recovery frames, the fighter is classified as Vulnerable (incoming strikes receive $+40%$ counter-damage and $+25%$ critical chance).

$$\text{RecoveryTicks} = \text{round}\left( \text{BaseRecovery} \times \left(1.0 - \left(0.15 \times \frac{TECH}{100}\right) - \left(0.30 \times \frac{M}{100}\right)\right) \right)$$

A mastered punch snaps back along the identical geometric line it was thrown, cutting the vulnerability window by nearly a third.
5. Stamina Expenditure

Poorly drilled punches burn excess energy through muscle tension and inefficient deceleration.

$$\text{StaminaCost} = \text{BaseCost} \times \left(1.30 - \left(0.20 \times \frac{TECH}{100}\right) - \left(0.40 \times \frac{M}{100}\right)\right)$$

A Raw Hook costs $1.25\times$ base stamina.
A Signature Hook costs $0.78\times$ base stamina ($37%$ lower physiological drain per punch thrown).
6. Telegraph Index ($\Theta \in [0.0, 1.0]$)

Measures the pre-punch body language (flaring the elbow, dropping the opposite hand, shifting weight prematurely) that tipped-off opponents exploit.

$$\Theta = \text{BaseMoveTelegraph} \times \left(1.0 - \left(0.30 \times \frac{TECH}{100}\right) - \left(0.60 \times \frac{M}{100}\right)\right)$$

$\Theta$ directly provides a flat bonus to the opponent's Evasion and Guard Read checks: $$\text{OpponentReadBonus} = \Theta \times 35.0$$
A Raw cross ($\Theta \approx 0.82$) gives the opponent a $+28.7$ point defensive read advantage. A Signature cross ($\Theta \approx 0.15$) reduces that bonus to just $+5.2$ points.
7. Contextual Selection Bias

Governs whether the AI fighter selects the move in the correct situation during autonomous combat.

$$P(\text{Select Correct Move}) = \left(\frac{IQ \times 0.60 + M \times 0.40}{100}\right) \times \text{DistanceFitModifier}$$

High $M$ allows a fighter to execute the punch instinctively under heavy pressure, preventing "freezing" or throwing ill-fitted punches (such as lunging rear uppercuts from outside distance).

Station Allocation & Mastery Gain Formula

Mastery is gained through dedicated gym floor drilling:

Focus Pads (Primary): Trains execution, snap, accuracy, and combination links ($+1.8\text{ to }+2.4\text{ M/hr}$).
Heavy Bag (Secondary): Trains power transfer and mechanical leverage ($+0.9\text{ to }+1.2\text{ M/hr}$).
Mirror / Shadowboxing: Cements recovery and telegraph reduction ($+0.7\text{ to }+1.0\text{ M/hr}$).
Sparring: Pressure-tests the move against live timing ($+1.5\text{ M/hr}$ directly to active tactical moves).

$$\Delta M = \text{BaseGainPerHour} \times \left(\frac{\text{StationTier}}{2.0}\right) \times \left(1.0 + \text{CoachPadSkill} \times 0.40\right) \times \left(1.0 - \frac{M}{\text{Cap}}\right) \times \text{FatigueEfficiency}$$

The Hard Attribute Ceiling ($\text{Cap}$)

A fighter cannot develop a world-class Signature check-hook if their underlying mechanical technique and balance are lacking.

$$\text{MasteryCeiling} = \text{clamp}\left(40.0 + \left(0.40 \times TECH\right) + \left(0.20 \times IQ\right), 50.0, 100.0\right)$$

If a fighter has $TECH = 50$ and $IQ = 60$, their maximum achievable Mastery for any punch is capped at $72.0$ (Sharp). To attain a Signature Level (85+), the player must first raise the athlete's foundational technique through dedicated coaching.
Mastery Decay

Skills are perishable:

If a move is not drilled during a calendar week, it loses $-0.75\text{ Mastery points per week}$, down to a floor of $30.0$ (Usable status is retained indefinitely once earned).
Dedicated Fight Week prep freezes decay across all active gameplan moves.
(c) Combinations & Pad Chains as Independent Mastery

Combinations are not simply sequential single punches; they require shifting weight between feet, changing target levels (head-to-body), and using the recoil of one punch to load the hips for the next.

       PAD CHAIN: 3-PIECE COUNTER ROUTE
  [ Trigger: Opponent Lead Jab ]
                │
                ▼
  [ Step 1: Slip Outside ] ────────► Evasion Check vs Opponent Accuracy
                │
                ▼
  [ Step 2: Rear Cross to Body ] ──► Level Change; loads front hip
                │
                ▼
  [ Step 3: Lead Hook to Head ] ───► Explodes off loaded front foot

Chain Mastery ($CM \in [0, 100]$): Evaluated separately from individual punch masteries.
Link Transition Window: $$\text{InterPunchDelay} = \text{max}\left(4\text{ ticks}, 12\text{ ticks} \times \left(1.0 - \frac{CM}{100}\right)\right)$$
At $CM \ge 75$, the transition delay drops to just 4 ticks ($66\text{ ms}$). The defender’s guard cannot reset between the first and second strike, granting the second strike an automatic $+30%$ penetration bonus against high guard.
Chain Execution Success Check: In live combat, a mastered chain triggers when the opponent presents the recognized trigger condition: $$P(\text{Chain Fires}) = \left(\frac{CM \times 0.50 + IQ \times 0.50}{100}\right) \times \text{ComposureModifier}$$
(d) Move Quality vs. Opponent Defensive Attributes

When Punch $A$ encounters Defender $B$, three sequential physical checks occur:

                     /                         \

                           - Clean Force vs Chin         Zero Concussive Shock

The Evasion Equation (Whiff vs Tracking):
$$\text{TrackingScore} = \text{EffectiveAccuracy} + (100.0 - \Theta \times 100.0) \times 0.30$$
$$\text{EvasionScore} = (B.\text{Evasion} \times 0.60) + (B.\text{Footwork} \times 0.40) \times \text{PositionModifier}$$
$$\Delta_{\text{evade}} = \text{EvasionScore} - \text{TrackingScore}$$

If $\Delta_{\text{evade}} > 15.0$: Clean Whiff. The punch hits empty air.
If $-15.0 \le \Delta_{\text{evade}} \le 15.0$: Glancing Contact or contested tracking.
If $\Delta_{\text{evade}} < -15.0$: Locked Tracking (Clean hit profile).

The Guard Equation (Block vs Penetration):
$$\text{PenetrationPower} = (\text{OutputDamage} \times 0.50) + (M \times 0.30) + (TECH \times 0.20)$$
$$\text{GuardAbsorb} = (B.\text{Guard} \times 0.70) + (B.\text{Power} \times 0.30) \times \text{StaminaRatio}$$

If $\text{PenetrationPower} > \text{GuardAbsorb} \times 1.25$: Guard Split. Punch penetrates directly to the face/ribs at $85%$ force.
Otherwise: Blocked. Damage reduced by $70\text{ to }90%$, zero head shock applied, zero cut risk.

Impact vs. Chin:
$$\text{ConcussiveShock} = \frac{\text{NetDamageDelivered}}{\text{Max}(20.0, B.\text{Chin} \times (1.0 - B.\text{AccumulatedConcussiveWear}))}$$

If $\text{ConcussiveShock} \ge 1.0$: Flash Stun (Fighter freezes, guard drops for 1.5 seconds).
If $\text{ConcussiveShock} \ge 1.6$: Knockdown Roll triggered.

The animation has an elongated windup; the fighter's non-punching glove dips down to chest level.
The punch lands with a dull, flat thud (sfx_punch_arm).

The punch snaps outward with a distinct whip motion; the non-punching hand remains glued to the cheekbone.
Clean impacts produce a crisp, resonant leather crack (sfx_punch_clean_crack).
If missed, the punch snaps back to guard without shifting the fighter's center of gravity.
Drilled Combination:
Fluid weight transfer: The hip pivot of the rear cross naturally winds the torso for an explosive follow-up lead hook. The strikes land with rapid, rhythmic impact cadence (crack-crack within 200 ms).

Formula 1: Distance & Initiative Gate

A punch can only be initiated if the fighters occupy the move's viable distance band ($D \in {\text{Outside}, \text{Pocket}, \text{Inside}}$):

$$\text{RangeScore} = 1.0 - \left(\frac{|\text{ActualDistance} - \text{OptimalDistance}|}{\text{Tolerance}}\right)$$
If $\text{RangeScore} < 0.20$, the strike suffers a $-60%$ accuracy penalty and cannot land cleanly.

Formula 2: Comprehensive Damage Equation

$$\text{RawForce} = \text{BaseMoveDamage} \times \left(0.35 + 0.65 \times \frac{POW}{100}\right) \times \left(0.50 + 0.50 \times \frac{TECH}{100}\right) \times \left(0.70 + 0.30 \times \frac{M}{100}\right)$$

$$\text{ImpactDamage} = \text{RawForce} \times \text{StanceModifier} \times \text{KinematicCounterMultiplier}$$

$\text{KinematicCounterMultiplier} = 1.45$ if defender is in punch windup/recovery ticks; $1.0$ otherwise.
Formula 3: Guard Penetration & Net Force

$$\text{AbsorbFactor} = \text{clamp}\left(\frac{\text{Guard} \times 0.75 + POW \times 0.25}{100.0} \times \left(\frac{\text{Stamina}}{100.0}\right), 0.10, 0.88\right)$$

$$\text{NetDamage} =
\begin{cases}
\text{ImpactDamage} & \text{if Evaded (Whiff) } \rightarrow 0.0 \
\text{ImpactDamage} & \text{if Clean Penetration} \
\text{ImpactDamage} \times (1.0 - \text{AbsorbFactor}) & \text{if Guard Held (Blocked)}
\end{cases}$$

Formula 4: Concussive Head Shock & Knockdown Mechanics

$$\text{Shock} = \frac{\text{NetDamage}}{\text{Chin} \times (1.0 - \text{AccumulatedWear})}$$
$$\text{AccumulatedWear} = 1.0 - \left(\frac{\text{CurrentHeadHP}}{\text{BASE_HEAD_HP}}\right) \times 0.40$$

If $\text{Shock} \ge 1.40$, trigger Knockdown Check ($KD_{roll}$): $$P(KD) = \text{clamp}\left((\text{Shock} - 1.40) \times 0.65 + \left(1.0 - \frac{\text{Stamina}}{100}\right) \times 0.30, 0.05, 0.95\right)$$
Formula 5: Beating the Count (Canvas Recovery)

Fighter has 10 seconds (600 ticks) to clear shock below $0.40$:

$$\Delta \text{ShockPerSecond} = \left(0.12 \times \frac{\text{Conditioning}}{100}\right) + \left(0.08 \times \frac{\text{Recovery}}{100}\right) + \left(0.05 \times \frac{\text{RingIQ}}{100}\right)$$
$$\text{TimeOnCanvas} = \frac{\text{Shock} - 0.40}{\Delta \text{ShockPerSecond}}$$
If $\text{TimeOnCanvas} > 10.0\text{ seconds}$, the result is ruled a Knockout (KO).

Formula 6: In-Round Stamina Depletion

$$\text{StaminaBurn} = \text{BasePunchCost} \times \left(1.0 - 0.35 \times \frac{M}{100}\right) \times \left(1.0 + \frac{100 - \text{Conditioning}}{200}\right)$$

Whiffed power punches apply a flat $1.60\times$ multiplier to $\text{StaminaBurn}$.
Body Damage Inflicted on Defender permanently lowers their maximum stamina ceiling for the remainder of the fight by $0.15\text{ points per damage unit}$.
