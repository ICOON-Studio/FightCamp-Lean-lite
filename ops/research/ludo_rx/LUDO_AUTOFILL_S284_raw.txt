1. WALL BAG SPACING RULE (30 x 22.5 m Hall)
Sane standard: 1 bag per 2.5 metres of wall.
Absolute physical minimum: 2.0 m (bags touch when swinging laterally).
Comfortable club standard: 2.5 m (allows clean footwork, pivoting, and a coach standing off-hip holding cues).
Luxury pro standard: 3.0 m.
On this 30 x 22.5 m warehouse shell:
A 30 m uninterrupted wall fits 11 wall-mount bags maximum (allowing 1.25 m clearance at each corner).
A 22.5 m end wall fits 8 wall-mount bags maximum.
Rule of thumb: Never fill more than 40% of total perimeter wall length with bags on Day 1, or room partitioning becomes impossible without tearing down mountings.
2. THE THREE PRESETS: DEFINITIONS & ROLES
PRESET 1: "CANVAS & IRON" (The True $25k Starter)
Identity: A raw, hungry contender's basement gym. Covers every functional training pillar on Day 1 (bag work, slip/speed timing, sparring, recovery, signups) while preserving a healthy cash float.
Budget: Target ≤ $18,000 spend ($7,000+ cash float out of $25,000).
PRESET 2: "FIGHT CLUB COMMERCIAL" (The $25k Member-Heavy Alternative)
Identity: Skips the heavy pro sparring ring to focus entirely on member cash flow and white-collar bag circuits. Maximum bag line, maximum lockers, higher upfront capacity.
Budget: Target ≤ $22,500 spend ($2,500 cash float out of $25,000).
PRESET 3: "CHAMPIONSHIP COMPOUND" (The $80k Dev / Full Club Preset)
Identity: The fully realized, turn-key regional contender facility with an elevated pro arena ring, private office, dedicated strength room, physio recovery clinic, and high-end member facilities.
Budget: Fits the $80,000 expanded tester budget (Target ~$70,000 spend, leaving $10,000 runway).

4. WHAT IS DELIBERATELY NOT PRE-PLACED IN $25K STARTERS

To keep the game a management simulation rather than an idle screensaver, the following pieces are intentionally omitted from both $25,000 starter presets:

The $18,000 Pro Ring ($2,500 permit + $3,840 floor + $15,500 ring):
Why omit: Placing this instantly bankrupts the player on Day 1. The player must earn this milestone by fighting away from home first, then building it when headlining.
The Physio Room ($750 permit + $1,400 table):
Why omit: Acute fatigue in the first 2 weeks can be managed by scheduled rest and light taper days. Makes Goal 9 ("FEEL THE STIFFNESS") a meaningful progression unlock.
The Dedicated Strength Room ($600 permit + $2,200 half rack):
Why omit: Heavy strength work is unnecessary for unranked 4-round novices. Bodyweight conditioning and agility ladders handle Week 1–4 physical prep.
Curved Manual Treadmill ($5,200) & Olympic Barbell Set ($2,400):
Why omit: Capital traps. Buying a curved treadmill on Day 1 eats 21% of your entire bank account for an item that only trains aerobic conditioning. The commercial bike ($1,450) or jump ropes fulfill the exact same requirement for a third of the price.
The Dedicated Management Office ($350 permit + $750 PC):
Why omit: In the starter presets, match contracts and administrative work are completed directly through the Reception Desk terminal. Building a private sanctuary office is a reward for when roster size grows to 2+ boxers.
5. EXECUTION & PLACEMENT SEQUENCE (Fallback Priority Order)

If cash runs short during placement (e.g., player customized room dimensions slightly larger than standard before clicking auto-fill), items and rooms must instantiate strictly in this priority order:

[STEP 01] Reception Room Shell + Desk ($1,515)
          * Hard Gate: Receptionist & signups impossible without this.

[STEP 02] Training Floor Shell + 1 Key Heavy Bag ($2,250)
          * Hard Gate: Boxer cannot execute any training without a designated floor.

[STEP 03] Locker Room Shell + 1 Key Locker Bank ($1,480)
          * Hard Gate: Member capacity remains 0 without this; walk-ins turn around.

[STEP 04] Sparring Pit Shell + Taped Ring ($1,840)
          * Hard Gate: Unlocks tactical sparring mechanics and safe infighting practice.

[STEP 05] Water Cooler & 1 Communal Bench ($530)
          * Operational: Halts rapid early hydration/fatigue spikes during drills.

[STEP 06] Wall-Arm Heavy Bag Line (Bags #2 and #3) ($1,100)
          * Operational: Allows simultaneous fighter + member training on the bags.

[STEP 07] Double-End Bag ($380) & Speed Bag ($450)
          * Technical: Unlocks rhythm, timing, and reflex training paths.

[STEP 08] Commercial Exercise Bike ($1,450)
          * Conditioning: Unlocks active aerobic recovery sessions.

[STEP 09] 1st Shower Stall ($1,100)
          * Economic: Adds +15 member places and eliminates the early "smelly gym" churn.

[STEP 10] Focus-Mitt Locker ($480) & Agility Ladder ($420)
          * Tactical: Unlocks coach pad work and footwork drills.

[STEP 11] 2nd Locker Bank ($620) & 2nd Shower Stall ($1,100)
          * Economic: Scales member cap from 27 to 54 for strong weekly subscription cash.

[STEP 12] Vending Machine ($850) & Trophy Cabinet ($600)
          * Secondary: Passive concession revenue and baseline gym prestige boost.


If the budget cuts off at any point along this chain, the gym remains 100% playable, operational, and financially viable.
