

1. START BUDGET & THE DAY 1 MINIMUM OPEN GYM
The Ring Dilemma: Does the 18,000 Ring Belong on Day 1?

No. An $18,000 ring out of a $25,000 starting wallet leaves exactly $7,000 for walls, reception, locker rooms, bags, weights, and initial operating payroll. That forces either a sterile void or an instant Day 14 bankruptcy before the first bout check clears.

In real-world club boxing (from Gleason's basement annexes to British small-hall gyms), an unbacked independent starter gym opens with a Taped Canvas / Floor Ring (Chalk & Tape Square).

The Taped Floor Ring costs $1,200 (canvas mat + perimeter tape + corner posts without elevated steel roping).
It delivers full technical footwork, technical ring craft, and light technical sparring at an innate penalty: Tactical Ring IQ gain rate is $0.75\times$, and heavy hard sparring carries a 15% higher slip/tumble risk.
Where are early bouts fought? Out-of-house! Early career four-rounders take place in civic halls, leisure centres, and regional licensed venues. The gym does not need an arena-certified elevated competition ring until the boxer headlines home shows or reaches regional title contention (typically Month 4–6).
The full Elevated Competition Ring ($18,000) becomes the mid-game milestone trophy that unlocks home fight hosting and elite sparring partners.
Minimum Viable Gym (MVG) on Day 1

The minimum open gym must consume 64% to 70% of starting capital, leaving a safe operational runway of ~$7,500–$8,000 for initial weekly burn before cash flows stabilize.

2. ROOM PRICING: HYBRID SYSTEM (FLAT PERMIT + TILE RATE + KEY ITEM)

Neither Two Point's pure flat fee (which ignores the physical constraints of an industrial warehouse) nor Mad Games Tycoon 2's pure per-tile rate (which makes larger aesthetic rooms feel like an active financial penalty) fits a spatial management sim.

We use a Hybrid Model:
$$\text{Total Room Cost} = \text{Room Permit Fee} + (\text{Tile Count} \times \text{Floor Surface Cost}) + \text{Key Activation Item}$$

Room Permit Fee (Flat): Covers drywall framing, foundational electrical drops, and zone licensing.
Tile Surface Cost ($25–$80/tile): Standard industrial screed vs slip-resistant rubber sports matting vs acoustic panelling.
Key Activation Item: The room is strictly non-functional (DORMANT) until this primary object is placed inside the bounds.
Selling / Remodelling Refund: 100% refund on walls and items within the first 5 in-game days of construction; 70% baseline depreciated refund thereafter.

5. THE TIGHTENING: FIRST-YEAR ESCALATION FORMULAS

The early game feels open and forgiving; by Month 3, the financial and operational screws begin to turn. This tightening does not stem from arbitrary penalties, but from four interconnected, scaling systems:

+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| THE TIGHTENING ESCALATION TIMELINE                                                                |
|                                                                                                    |
|  Month 1: The Honeymoon    Month 3: The Crunch       Month 6: The Regional Leap   Month 12: Elite  |
|  * Low Rent ($1.2k/mo)     * Base Rent Jump ($2.0k)  * Commercial Lease ($3.2k)   * City Tax       |
|  * High tolerance (20%)    * Standard Wear (60%)     * High Expectations (65%)    * Pro Standards  |
|  * Novice Opponents        * Hungry Gatekeepers      * Cut-Throat Contenders      * World Ranked   |
|  * Soft Wear on kit        * Kit starts breaking     * Breakdown downtime         * Major upkeep   |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

Formula 1: Commercial Lease & Municipal Rates ($C_{\text{rent}}$)

The starter lot owner provides a 60-day discounted grace period ($1,200/month). At Month 3, full municipal commercial rates kick in based on built square footage:
$$C_{\text{rent}}(m) = $1,200 + \left(\max(0, m - 2) \times $350\right) + \left(\text{Built Floorspace } m^2 \times $4.50\right)$$

Month 1: $1,200/mo
Month 3: $2,150/mo
Month 6: $3,450/mo
Month 12: $5,200/mo
Formula 2: Member Churn & Floor Standards ($E_{\text{member}}$)

In Month 1, community members tolerate raw concrete and basic facilities (Minimum Gym Score: 20/100). Every month, baseline member expectations increase by +4 points.
$$\text{Churn Rate} = \max\left(0.02, \frac{\text{Required Standard}(m) - \text{Actual Gym Score}}{100}\right)$$
If Actual Gym Score is below the required standard, members leave at up to 18% weekly turnover, devastating steady subscription revenue unless showers, lockers, and fresh gear are installed.

+-------+--------------------+------------------------+---------------------------------------+
| MONTH | REQUIRED GYM SCORE | COMMON CHURN REASON    | MITIGATION REQUIRED                   |
+-------+--------------------+------------------------+---------------------------------------+
| M 01  | 20 / 100           | None (Honeymoon)       | Keep floor clear of trash bags        |
| M 03  | 32 / 100           | "No showers / smells"  | Build Washroom / Shower bay           |
| M 06  | 48 / 100           | "Heavy bags worn down" | Service gear / Upgrade to leather bags|
| M 09  | 62 / 100           | "Overcrowded cardio"   | Add 2nd cardio unit or expand room    |
| M 12  | 75 / 100           | "Outdated facilities"  | Install recovery suite / sauna        |
+-------+--------------------+------------------------+---------------------------------------+

Formula 3: Progressive Equipment Degradation ($D_{\text{wear}}$)

Wear does not require manual maintenance clicks. Equipment condition degrades strictly based on Station Usage Minutes:
$$\Delta \text{Condition} = -(\text{Hours Used Today} \times \text{Wear Coefficient}) \times (1.0 - \text{Gym Cleaner Mod})$$

When condition drops below 40%, training yield drops to $0.70\times$ and injury chance triples.
When condition reaches 0%, the item breaks with an audible snap, blocking the station until serviced.
Wear Coefficient: Heavy bags = $0.4%/\text{hr}$; Cardio bikes = $0.7%/\text{hr}$; Canvas ring = $0.2%/\text{hr}$.
Formula 4: Matchmaking Difficulty & Opponent Step-Up

Early opponents are unranked tomato-cans ($ELO \approx 1000–1150$, record 2-8-1). By Bout 4, opponents actively counter your boxer's profile:
$$\text{Opponent Stat Target} = \text{Boxer Stat Average} + (N_{\text{wins}} \times 2.8) \pm 4$$
If you neglect defensive footwork, matchmaking scouts exploit it: opponents in Month 6 arrive with tailored game plans, turning routine wins into grueling, damage-heavy wars.

6. MONEY SYSTEMS AS POLICY, NEVER CHORES

To prevent the micromanagement traps that hurt games like Gym Empire (e.g., manually cancelling individual memberships, unclogging toilets one by one, micromanaging client injuries), FightCamp runs strictly on High-Level Macro Policies:

+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| MACRO MANAGEMENT POLICY DASHBOARD (NO CHORES, NO CLICK-HUNTING)                                   |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. MEMBERSHIP POLICY SLIDER                                                                        |
|    Monthly Dues: [$55 / mo] <---[o]------------------------->  Capacity Cap: [ 35 Members ]        |
|    Projected Demand: 42 Applicants/mo | Member Churn: 4.1%/mo | Weekly Income: +$481/wk            |
|                                                                                                    |
| 2. PAYROLL & STAFF RETENTION DIAL                                                                 |
|    Gym-Wide Wage Standard: [ COMPETITIVE (+10% above market) ]                                     |
|    * Staff Morale: 94% (Zero walkout risk)                                                        |
|    * Coaching Station Bonus: +8% efficiency across all stations                                    |
|                                                                                                    |
| 3. AUTO-MAINTENANCE CONTRACTS                                                                      |
|    Maintenance Tier: [ TIER 2 - LOCAL TRADES CONTRACT ($85/wk) ]                                  |
|    * Rule: Any equipment falling below 65% condition is auto-serviced overnight at 03:00.          |
|    * Player action: ZERO manual repair clicks. Fixes show on morning ledger receipt.               |
|                                                                                                    |
| 4. MEDICAL & REHABILITATION POLICY                                                                |
|    Health Cover: [ PRIVATE SPORTS CLINIC RETAINER ($120/wk) ]                                      |
|    * Acute injury duration reduced by 40%. Severe cuts healed automatically between fights.        |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

The Four Core Policy Rules
The Global Membership Slider: You adjust one gym-wide fee slider ($25 to $120/month). Lower fees boost volume and capacity pressures; higher fees increase income per head but raise minimum Gym Score expectations. Individual members never need to be managed manually.
One Staff Wage Tier Policy: Instead of negotiating individual salaries, staff compensation uses a global policy: Underpaid (-15%), Market (0%), Competitive (+10%), or Elite (+25%). This directly impacts staff morale, skill progression, and coaching performance.
Automated Maintenance Contracts: Equipment maintenance is handled via flat-rate service contracts (Basic DIY $20/wk, Local Trades $85/wk, Pro Service $210/wk). Technicians handle repairs overnight automatically.
Purse Splits by Contract: Professional fighters work on straightforward tier-based splits (e.g., Prospect: 70% Fighter / 30% Gym; Headliner: 85% Fighter / 15% Gym). Purses are split automatically upon bout completion.
7. DEBT, LOANS & SOFT BANKRUPTCY

Hard game-over screens on zero cash ruin long-term management sims. FightCamp implements a Lender & Promoter Bailout System that keeps the story moving while imposing meaningful gameplay consequences.

The Soft Bankruptcy Process (Triggered at -$5,000 Overdraft)

When the gym’s balance drops past -$5,000, normal operations freeze, and the game introduces an authentic narrative pivot rather than an abrupt game over:

                  [ CASH DROPS BELOW -$5,000 ]
                               |
                               v
            +--------------------------------------+
            |   SYNDICATE PROMOTER INTERVENTION    |
            |   "Frankie Vane steps in with cash"  |
            +--------------------------------------+
                               |
            +------------------+-------------------+
            |                                      |
            v                                      v
  [ COST CONCESSION ]                    [ CLEAR TARGET GOAL ]
  Promoter claims 50% of the gym's       "Deliver 2 knockout wins in
  cut on the next 3 bouts + takes        60 days, or forfeit the title
  title to 1 high-value station.         deed to the gym."

The Bailout Injection: A regional promoter steps in with an immediate $8,000 capital infusion, resetting the bank account to a stable baseline.
The Concession: The promoter takes a 50% cut of the gym's purse share for the next three fights, and claims the title to one high-value asset (e.g., your Olympic Half Rack or Pro Ring).
The Target Mandate: A clear, high-stakes recovery contract is issued:
"Win 2 bouts within 60 days to buy back the gym deed and restore normal purse splits."
The Consequence of Failure: Failing the recovery contract forces a move to a smaller, low-rent basement venue in a rougher neighborhood, resetting gym square footage while preserving your fighter's career, record, and stats.
