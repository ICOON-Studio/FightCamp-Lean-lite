2. HIT POWER BANDS & VISUAL CADENCE

To maintain a realistic broadcast feel—rather than looking like an arcade game where every jab snaps the head back—landed punches are mapped into three distinct power bands based on effective landed force:

$$\text{Landed Force} = \frac{\text{Attacker.power} \times \text{Technique.damage_multiplier} \times \text{Timing Factor}}{\text{Defender.defense} \times \text{Mitigation Factor}}$$

[0.00] ========= LIGHT (0.00 - 0.44) ========= [0.45] ===== MEDIUM (0.45 - 0.79) ===== [0.80] === BIG (0.80 - 1.00) === [1.00]
       - Upper-body bone blend only                   - Full-body recoil                             - Major displacement
       - Footwork continues uninterrupted             - Brief guard break (120-160ms)                - Heavy stun / knockdown check
       - 68% - 74% of clean head hits                 - 20% - 25% of clean head hits                 - 4% - 7% of clean head hits

Calibration & Visual Cadence
LIGHT POWER BAND (0.00 to 0.44):
Cadence: Represents 68% to 74% of all landed head punches in a typical 12-round fight (REAL).
Visual Behavior: Uses upper-body bone blending (spine and neck only). Footwork, stance width, and ring movement continue without interruption.
MEDIUM POWER BAND (0.45 to 0.79):
Cadence: Represents 20% to 25% of all landed head punches (REAL).
Visual Behavior: Full-body animation takes over. The fighter takes a short recoil step, breathing is briefly interrupted, and the guard splits open for 120ms to 160ms.
BIG POWER BAND (0.80 to 1.00):
Cadence: Represents 4% to 7% of all landed head punches (approx. 2 to 5 occurrences per round) (REAL).
Visual Behavior: Heavy visual impact. Feet freeze or buckle, head snaps violently, and the hit triggers an immediate knockdown roll based on remaining stamina and the defender's chin rating.
