# K86 (Kalli, game design) - the first hour of FightCamp with EVERY mechanic ON: play order, where it confuses, the "how" lines

Kalli, game design only (no research). Answer in TEXT, as long and complete as you need.

The operator (S222): "we need a playable version soon with all mechanics on". The demo is a PC boxing-gym management sim: you run a
small boxing club (Calder House, Nicosia, story start, one 21-year-old welterweight, Mara Solano, $25K cash, $367/wk upkeep), train
your boxer at stations on a 3D gym floor, scout and sign fighters, book bouts, run camps and watch the fight in 3D.

Every built feature is now ON together. Player-facing ones, in plain words:
- Gym: the gym IS the menu (click stations/rooms); training zones you can move before opening; stations and zones WEAR with use and
  train worse when worn (service / replace them); club capacity (members crowd the floor); routes fighters walk; open-spar night.
- Fighters: profile drawer (stats, stress, career wear), recovery rate, training strands, technique stages, pad chains, career wear.
- Week: the day timeline bar with a clock; skip drawer; last-spar screen + drawer; candidate cards to sign fighters.
- Money: sandbox promoter "quick money" offers and a hard DEBT decision when cash stays below zero (loan / emergency / insolvency
  ends the career); the story start has its own promoter debt.
- Fight: certified boxing moves only, feints, clinch, defence model, corner advice between rounds (doctor, cutman shouts, referee),
  limb damage, read-the-round, broadcast camera auto-cuts, fight VFX.
