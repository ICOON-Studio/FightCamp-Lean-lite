# TASK M37 - MARIA - DRESSING THE GENERATED FIGHTER, PER RULESET
Owner: maria (Grok Bot, Maria Art Director). Assigned 2026-09-27 (S98). Art direction only - no repo, no test.
Reply inline, <= 700 words, titled M37_DRESSING_THE_GENERATED_FIGHTER.

## Why
Our fighters are now made by a body generator (MakeHuman-style base mesh with shape sliders). The base is unclothed.
A coming stage dresses them. We need an art direction that makes a boxer, a kickboxer, a Muay Thai fighter, an MMA
fighter and a judoka read at a glance from a 3/4 camera, while staying legal for each ruleset and cheap to make
(a handful of garment meshes recoloured, not one model per man).

## What would a human do?
A sports-game art director builds a kit sheet: per sport, the garments that MUST be there (gloves and their size,
shorts cut, shin guards in amateur rules, rash guard or not, judogi), the parts that vary per fighter (colour, trim,
a name on the waistband), and the parts that never ship (real brand logos). Then a small palette system so two men in
the ring never wear the same colours.

## Questions
1. Per ruleset (boxing, kickboxing, Muay Thai, MMA, judo): the required garments and gear, their silhouette (e.g.
   boxing trunks long and high-waisted, MMA shorts short and split), and what differs between amateur and pro.
2. The minimum garment set to model once and recolour to cover all five (a list, with which rulesets share which).
3. The variation system: which parts take the fighter's colours, how to keep the two corners apart (red/blue corner
   rules where they exist), and what extra per-fighter touches read from a distance.
4. What must never ship (brand logos, real sponsor marks) and what can (plain patches, invented sponsors).
5. Your ONE recommendation for the first three garments to make.
