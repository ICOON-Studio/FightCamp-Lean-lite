# J60 - Free image-to-video generators, October 2026 (John, research only)

Model: John Research (Grok bot, browser). No repo access needed.

## Why
FightCamp's gym rooms are painted stills with a short looping motion area (a boxer seen through an office window,
a bag swinging). Today we make the motion in Dola (Seedance, free, ~4 credits/day = two 10 s clips). The operator
asked: "what other free platforms or AIs can do the generation?" We need more free capacity for ~8 rooms.

## Prior research
Our record has NO survey of video generators (searched: free, video, generator, text-to-video, image-to-video).
G58/J31 cover motion capture and 3D animation generators only, not video. We use Dola now and fal.ai pay-as-you-go.

## What would a human do?
A small studio artist would sign up for every free tier, test the same start image on each, and keep the two or
three that hold the image and loop cleanly - after checking the free tier's licence allows a commercial game.

## Prefab check
This IS the prefab check: we are not building a generator, we are picking a hosted one.
