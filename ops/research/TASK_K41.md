## Why this exists

Our game is **Godot 4.7.2**, sold commercially. Our fight animations are bought mocap clips from the Unity Asset Store
(standard EULA: usable in any electronic application; clause 2.2.1.1(g) forbids using them to train AI). We retarget
them onto our own Tripo-generated fighters with our own code, and the result looks bad. **Unreal Engine 5.8 is
already installed on our PC.** We want to use its IK Retargeter and Control Rig as an offline tool: import our FBX
clips and our fighter mesh, retarget, export FBX, and import that into Godot. The operator also asked about
Cascadeur, Rokoko Studio, DeepMotion Animate 3D and Move.ai. Before anything we export ships, we need the licence
answer.
