**Case B contradicts the prices.** STEP UP is "the nearest name ABOVE on the ladder." For a boxer in 4th, that is 3rd, and it is free. A NAME of the 3rd is the same bout for $450 less to the gym. The table's "likely best" is then never best. The line "worth the $450 because the plan can be written for that style" also applies to STEP UP, if that card already shows the name and style.

**STEP UP cannot both hide the style and show the name.** Section 5 prints name, club, record, place, chance, teach, stoppage on every card. Case E then says STEP UP "might hand over a counter-puncher." Once the name is on the card, it might not. Either hide the STEP UP identity until after the ask (then section 5 is wrong) or show it (then case E is a comparison of two known names, and "the host picks the style" is not a cost).

**A NAME can offer the same person as FAIR or STEP UP.** That row is then a strictly worse copy of a free button. For an unranked boxer (under 4 wins) the map never defines "4 places either side," so the third card has no legal pool, or it collapses to the same "nearest better record" as STEP UP, again for $450.

**The next show can be only 7 days away.** The pack only opens camp 3 or more weeks out, and the bout page always shows time for a camp, weight now and on the day, and a "Weight at risk" chip. This panel prints none of that, but pressing Ask is a booking. A boxer still over the limit, or a camp that cannot start, can be locked into a date the rest of the game treats as a bad booking. **"Place taken for certain" does not sit with the pack.** The pack's take-the-place rule is in the pro section. Amateur entry is at 4 wins, and how club-show places move is not fully spelled out. The map also says the ladder is wins minus losses. Under net wins, beating the boxer above is the surest pass, not a certain one (a lead of two or more nets is not closed by one win), and beating someone else can also draw level. Unranked boxers have no place to take, so a "what a win leaves: place" line is not actionable.

**The road on the card is the wrong one for some boxers.** "Wins toward the pro offer" is not the next act for a novice whose deadline is 5 bouts by nationals entry (a points loss still counts; a stoppage suspension can end the season). That number cannot be acted on in case A or C the way the pack's "only actionable information" rule requires.

Two boxers asking for the same next show is not specified. That is a gap, not a present-button contradiction.
