# FightCamp M43: boxing gym photo refs (real photos only)
Compiled 2026-10-08 (Asia/Nicosia). Every URL below was fetched with curl or WebFetch and the image was viewed. Local copies are in `refs/`.
BI = Business Insider, "We went to the world's most famous boxing gym…", Harrison Jacobs, published 2017-11-20.
Page: https://www.businessinsider.com/worlds-most-famous-boxing-gym-gleasons-brooklyn-new-york-photos (Gleason's Gym, 130 Water St, Brooklyn).
BI direct images all follow the pattern `https://i.insider.com/<id>?width=1200&format=jpeg`. I matched each image to its caption by its position on the page and then checked it by eye.
Measurements marked "est." are guesses from the photo. Nothing was measured. Hex values are median-pixel samples (PIL) from the named surface. Lighting and camera grading shift them, and the Commons Gleason's ring photo has an Instagram filter.

## 1. Ring
**Best: Gleason's Gym, Brooklyn (BI, ring corner and sparring).** File: refs/01_ring_gleasons_BI_corner.jpg. Direct image: https://i.insider.com/5a0e0875f914c359018b471d?width=1200&format=jpeg
- Raised ring with 4 red ropes. The corner post has 4 white padded turnbuckle covers marked "Everlast" and a black corner post pad. Steel turnbuckle hardware is visible.
- Canvas is blue, sampled **#3e67a1**. A second ring behind also has blue canvas and red/white ropes, and has a "GLEASON'S" corner pad.
- Black square columns carry a speaker box and framed photos. There is a big US flag hung from the ceiling, plus a "WBC" banner and a white "World Champion" banner.
- Also in shot: an exposed ductwork ceiling, fluorescent strip lights, and a red wall covered in about 25 framed photos.
- Foreground: a wooden bench with a red vinyl pad and a blue mat (a woman is stretching on it) and a black stepper/elliptical. The ring apron is about bench height or a bit higher, est. 0.9–1.0 m. Not verified.
- Same gym from inside the ring: https://i.insider.com/5a0e08763dbef482008b688f?width=1200&format=jpeg. Red ropes sampled **#bd163d**. Dark grey canvas (**#66757f**) with white "GLEASON'S GYM" and "EVERLAST" lettering, an "EVERLAST" rope spacer strap, a water bottle on the apron, and people leaning on the ropes.

**2nd: Box N Fit Gym, Auckland (Infusion Arch project page, completed 2009).** File: refs/01b_ring_boxnfit_auckland.jpg
- Page: https://www.infusionarch.co.nz/dt_projects/box-n-fit-gym/ . Image: https://www.infusionarch.co.nz/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/BOX-N-FIT-GYM-05.png
- Raised ring with blue canvas and apron (**#265594**), 4 black ropes (est. count), and black posts with yellow tape tags.
- A 3-step steel/wood stair leads up to it. Platform height est. 0.6–0.8 m.
- Spin bikes are lined up alongside the ring. Gloves and pads sit on the apron.
