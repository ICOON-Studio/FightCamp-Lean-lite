## Why this memo

J37 framed the standing gap: range and distance striking are relatively instrumented; the clinch is the least-understood standing position in the engine. `grapple_clinch_engage` is **3.8% of 2,477 events** (operator-stated fact). Without a rule-gated break clock, episode-duration distribution, ending-mode shares, or clinch-vs-distance work-rate, any clinch timer or break heuristic in FightCamp is folklore. This memo re-verifies Q1–Q4 against primary rule PDFs and published metrics. Gate (same as J37): only study-measured and rulebook-says gate a test. “Referees are trained to…” does **not** count. Every share needs a denominator. No commentator break-seconds.

## Q1 — THE REFEREE’S BREAK

### 1A. Unified Rules of MMA (ABC) — MEASURED (criteria); NOT FOUND (seconds)

**Source:** Association of Boxing Commissions and Combative Sports, *Unified Rules of Mixed Martial Arts*, amended with nonsubstantial changes **6 Aug 2025** (requested implementation of July 2024 changes Nov 2024).  
URL: https://www.abcboxing.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Unified-Rules-of-MMA-8.2025.pdf

**Verbatim — “Standing up or Breaking Fighters”:**

> The Referee shall either stand up or break the fighters when neither fighter is able to or fails to demonstrate real, significant and/or sustained effort to advance towards finishing the fight by any method. Simply maintaining what may be perceived as a superior position will not be considered effort to advance towards finishing the fight nor grounds for a guaranteed opportunity to maintain that position.

**Related foul (timidity / stall), verbatim excerpt:**

> Timidity is defined as any fighter who purposely avoids contact with his opponent or runs away from the action of the fight. Timidity can also be called by the referee for any attempt by a fighter to receive time by falsely claiming a foul, injury, or purposely dropping or spitting out their mouthpiece or other action designed to stall or delay the action of the fight.

**Numbered time for clinch / stall break in this document:** **NOT FOUND.**  
No “X seconds,” “after N seconds of inactivity,” or similar clock appears in the standing/break section or fouls list of the Aug 2025 Unified MMA PDF.

**Do not substitute:** commentator “refs break at 5–10 seconds,” training folklore, or kickboxing clocks (see §1D).

### 1B. Pro boxing — ABC regulatory guidelines — MEASURED (foul + break procedure); NOT FOUND (seconds)

**Source:** Association of Boxing Commissions, *Regulatory Guidelines and Rules for All World and Regional Championship Bouts* (modified **27 Jul 2005**).  
URL: https://www.abcboxing.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/08/Boxing-Handbook-ABC.pdf

**Fouls list — verbatim items relevant to clinch:**

> 3. Holding an opponent with one hand and hitting with the other;  
> 4. Holding or deliberately maintaining a clinch;  
> …  
> 15. Hitting on the break;

**Break procedure language (referee pre-bout / in-bout guidance in same handbook):** referees are directed to share how they will direct boxers to break (e.g. “punch” or “get out,” and if necessary step in and break). No countdown of seconds is written into the foul definitions.

**ABC Boxing Referee Manual (Sep 2025)** — mechanics, still no clock:  
URL: https://www.abcboxing.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/ABC-BOXING-REFEREE-MANUAL.pdf  

> When either or both boxers commence holding and/or fouling, the referee should immediately take the necessary action required to stop and prevent any such further tactics.  
> When physically breaking the boxers, it is recommended to use the word “Stop” as your first command, followed by “Break”…

**Numbered time for holding/clinch break in ABC boxing texts searched:** **NOT FOUND.**

### 1C. Pro boxing — BBBofC Rules & Regulations 2025 — MEASURED (holding foul + break duty); NOT FOUND (seconds)

**Source:** British Boxing Board of Control Limited, *Rules and Regulations 2025*.  
URL: https://bbbofc.com/build/documents/Rules_and_Regulations_2025.pdf  
(Also mirrored on https://bbbofc.com/boxing-rules)

**Verbatim 3.38(f) and 3.38(j):**

> 3.38 The following acts shall not be permitted during a contest:  
> …  
> (f) holding, butting, or careless use of the head, shouldering, wrestling or roughing;  
> …  
> (j) failing to break when so ordered, or striking or attempting to strike an opponent on the break;

**Verbatim 3.39:**

> When ordered by the Referee to ‘break’ both Boxers shall immediately take one step back before re-commencing to box. The parting of Boxers by force should be avoided if possible.

**Numbered time before “break” is ordered:** **NOT FOUND** in BBBofC 2025. Holding is a foul; when the ref says break, both must step back. Duration before that order is referee discretion, not a written second-count.

## Q2 — HOW LONG A CLINCH ACTUALLY LASTS

### 2A. Boxing — episode duration — MEASURED (mean ± SD); median/IQR NOT FOUND

**Martusciello et al. (2025).** *Time–Motion Analysis of the 2023 Women’s World Boxing Championships Finals.* *Sports* 13(6):187.  
DOI: https://doi.org/10.3390/sports13060187  
PDF: https://iris.uniroma4.it/retrieve/24d8283e-2c3e-4511-82f3-a6f5518aeeca/sports-13-00187-v2.pdf  

| Field | Value |
|-------|--------|
| Sample | **12** finals, IBA Women’s World Boxing Championships 2023 (New Delhi); all 12 weight categories; 3×3 min |
| Definition | Clinching (C) as fighting sub-phase: close-range lock / arm entanglement with strikes often exchanged |
| Episode duration (pooled) | **4.06 ± 2.27 s** (mean ± SD) |
| Median / IQR / percentiles | **NOT FOUND** in paper |
| Denominator note | Phase durations measured with Kinovea; intra-observer ICC = 1.0 |

**Caveat for gate:** gate asked for a **duration distribution (median and spread, not a single mean)**. Martusciello supplies **mean and SD** (spread yes; median no). Treat as **MEASURED mean±SD**; do **not** invent a median from ±2.27.

**Davis et al. (2016)** — *Performance analysis of elite female amateur boxers…* *Int J Sports Physiol Perform* 11:55–60.  
Cumulative clinch **time per round** (4×2 min London 2012 format), **not** episode length: R1 **9.4 ± 7.1 s**, R2 **14.2 ± 11.9 s**, R3 **16.4 ± 12.0 s**, R4 **17.8 ± 14.6 s** (as cited in Martusciello discussion / ResearchGate abstract of Davis). Useful as cumulative load; **does not** close episode distribution.

**Re-verify of J32 prior:** Martusciello **4.06 ± 2.27 s** — **CONFIRMED** MEASURED. The “~4–6 s” band as a rule of thumb remains INFERENCE if used as a design default beyond the study sample.

## Judgement

**Three of four priority questions are NOT FOUND or only half-closed.** Q1 gives a rulebook **effort** standard without a clock. Q2 gives a **boxing** episode mean±SD and MMA **TIP % / cumulative** seconds, but not an MMA episode distribution. Q3 and paired Q4 are empty for gating.

### INFERENCE candidates for the operator (not for gating tests without operator choice)

**Gap A — Break clock (Q1 time NOT FOUND)**  
1. **INFERENCE A1:** Model MMA standing-clinch break as **effort-gated, not time-gated** (match Unified Rules text); any numeric timeout is a product dial, not a rule cite.  
2. **INFERENCE A2:** If a numeric dial is still wanted for sparring UX, treat **kickboxing’s 5 s knee-clinch** and boxing **immediate-holding-foul** cultures as **separate sport modes**, never as Unified MMA defaults.

**Gap B — Episode length (MMA distribution NOT FOUND)**  
1. **INFERENCE B1:** Use Martusciello boxing **4.06 ± 2.27 s** as a **boxing-mode prior only**; do not transplant to UFC standing clinch without a new sample.  
2. **INFERENCE B2:** For MMA standing clinch, prefer TIP share (**~16–18%** fight time; ~⅓→~¼ of standing time) as **occupancy** prior until an episode tracker is built in-house on FightCamp video.

**Gap C — Ending modes (Q3 NOT FOUND)**  
1. **INFERENCE C1:** Until labelled, instrument endings as a **four-way taxonomy** (ref break / mutual or solo disengage / takedown / knockdown-from-clinch) and collect N≥200 episodes before any share becomes MEASURED.  
2. **INFERENCE C2:** Expect **takedown** and **disengage** to dominate MMA endings vs **ref break** (cage allows work; refs break on stall), and **ref break** to dominate boxing — label both as guesses until counted.

**Gap D — Work rate (Q4 paired NOT FOUND)**  
1. **INFERENCE D1:** Do **not** compare raw UFC “significant” SLpM across distance vs clinch without adjusting for FightMetric’s power-only clinch rule.  
2. **INFERENCE D2:** Fightnomics’ **~8 attempts/min in clinch** is a usable **attempt-rate** occupancy prior; pair it later with distance attempt-rate from the same TIP minutes for a true within-fighter contrast.

---

## One-liner judgement

**The rulebook tells you *why* a clinch breaks (no real effort); it never says *when* in seconds — and published science still has no MMA episode distribution, no ending-mode shares, and no same-fighter clinch-vs-distance SLpM.**
