# G94 - WHAT A CARD'S GATE AND ATTENDANCE ACTUALLY DO WITH HYPE (Grok research brief)

Owner: grok (Expert, Research mode - never Build). Assigned S73, 2026-09-25. Research only; no code.

## Why
S73 made the gate read hype: gate = house x attendance fill x ticket price. Every coefficient is INVENTED: a cold card
sells 35% of the house, a full-draw card sells 100% at twice the cold ticket price, a hometown fighter is worth 15
points of draw. The one hard contrast we have is UFC 229 vs UFC 226, same arena, $17.2M vs $5.67M (3.03x). We want
counted cards to replace the invented curve.

## Questions - numbers, a source link per number, and SOURCED / NOT FOUND / JUDGEMENT tags
1. **Per-card gate AND attendance from a commission.** The Nevada State Athletic Commission (and others: California,
   New Jersey, Texas) publish gate receipts and paid attendance per event. Give a table of 20+ combat cards (boxing and
   MMA, any level), each with: event, venue, capacity if known, paid attendance, total attendance, gate. Include
   small/regional cards, not only mega-fights.
2. **The fill floor.** From those cards, the lowest paid attendance as a share of capacity - what does a cold card in a
   real building actually sell?
3. **Price vs fill.** For cards in the SAME venue, how much of a gate gap comes from more seats vs a higher average
   ticket (gate / paid attendance)? Same-venue pairs are the whole point.
4. **Hometown draw.** Any counted comparison of the same fighter headlining at home vs away (attendance or gate).

## What would a human do? (operator rule 2026-09-25 - answer this first)
How does a real PROMOTER fill a building and set prices? Papering the house (free/comp tickets - what share of
"attendance" is paid vs comped?), scaling the house (curtaining off upper tiers for a cold card), tiered and dynamic
pricing, choosing a smaller venue for a cold card, a hometown hero headlining to sell locally. If the promoter's own
levers explain the fill floor, the game's gate should model THOSE choices, not a curve.
