## 1. The idea in five lines
- Four fighters stayed at the club you take over, in four weight classes: one PROFESSIONAL boxer, two AMATEUR boxers and one GRAPPLER.
- Every Monday ONE board, Club week, deals three short things between them: PAD PLACES, COACHED MORNINGS and PAID SEATS. Asked is always more than owned.
- A bout is not a separate game: an amateur's FIGHT WEEK (12 days' notice, no camp) and a professional's CAMP (6 weeks) sit on that same board.
- FAVOURS are the second currency: earned by helping other clubs, spent to bend the terms of a bout. Never a win.
- No Monday repeats the last: the jobs' rota moves, one club twist lands, a trial fighter comes every 4 weeks, the other corner has had a week too.

## 3. The week, as the player meets it
### 3.1 What is short (each has a counter on top of the board, all three in one unit: ASKED, COVERED, SHORT)
- PAD PLACES. Coach gives 3 evenings of 2 pad places = 6; You hold 2: 8. Always the two words, "pad place". Asked: a boxer's ordinary week 3; a fight week +2; a professional's camp 2 to 3, plus 1 to 2 sparring evenings; a trial fighter 1; film takes a whole Coach evening (2 pad places). A quiet week asks 10, a busy one 13. A
  fourth and fifth pad place for one boxer still pay, at 0.7 and 0.5. A boxer with 6 or more bouts can hold one extra pad place at 0.7 (the holder's own evening is gone; mood +3 for 4 weeks, then -2 a week with no bout booked). Coach has 3 extra evenings a quarter, drawn as three tokens on Coach's card. An evening with no pad
  place is a session alone at 0.6, unless the rota takes the evening.
- COACHED MORNINGS. You 4, Coach 2: 6. One coached morning covers ONE fighter in full, or TWO on the same session at 0.8 each (counted as 2 fighter-mornings covered). Alone trains at 0.6. Every slot shows what THIS coach adds to THIS session (Coach is a pad and bag coach, weaker on Cardio work). A fighter with discipline under
  45 skips 1 alone morning in 3 unless a team-mate trains in the room. A spare morning can WATCH: no lift, but 2 watched mornings close a trial fighter's number ranges.
- PAID SEATS. A seat is a 4-week block: $1,000, six mornings a week, no job rota. It ends by itself with no mood loss (given: mood +5; pulled early: -8). A professional cannot hold one: the purse is the pay. Estimate for this start (section 7): nobody seated, the bank grows about $150 a week; one seat runs it down about $100 a
  week. A fighter with potential 80 or more and no seat for 8 weeks asks for one: mood -3 a week until seated or given a dated promise (kept +5, broken -10).

### 3.2 What is dealt: each fighter's pulls
Coach reads each fighter in order: THE SCALE (over, with a bout inside 14 days), FIGHT (the last 7 days), REST (tiredness forecast 70 or more, or hurt), A HABIT the next opponent punishes (only your own pad places train it out: 6 pad places). Then the pulls side by side, each with a number, a date and whose voice it is (Coach's
star, the fighter's ask, the ladder):
- WEAK SPOT: the lowest of the 14 abilities on the fighter page, when it is 3 or more under the fighter's level. From the third bout the other corner goes for it: in every exchange that reads that ability the opponent gains +0.25 for each point under, from round 2. Shown as "Weak spot: Defence. Opponents gain +1.6 an exchange
  until Defence is 54". A plan line can cover it and uses one of the four lines brought. The level weighs each ability by what it is worth in a bout (a point of Defence counts 6 times a point of Flexibility), so the lowest number is not always the one to train: the chip prints the weak spot's cost beside what the next perk is
  worth, both in wins in 100.
- WEAPON: the number the next perk hangs on ("Finisher at Power 70: about 7 weeks; worth 3 wins in 100").
- GOAL: what the fighter asked for (met: mood +10; missed: -20). A bout goal is met when the bout is FOUGHT.
- AHEAD: the next name up the ladder and the answers owned ("Siobhan: answers 2 of 3").
A pull asks 2 slots a week: a weak spot, a weapon or a goal asks 2 coached mornings on its session; Ahead asks 2 pad places. A seat holder carries two pulls, a day-job fighter one. Coach has a taste (the weapon and the pads, 2 times in 3) and the Monday review keeps score ("Coach this month: 3 right, 1 wrong"). Three one-click
deals: by hand, SAME AS LAST WEEK (what the twist and the rota changed is marked red), COACH DEALS IT.

### 3.3 The rota that moves (each fighter's row names this week and the next; a seat removes it)
- SHIFTS: earlies and lates alternate by the week. Earlies: weekday mornings at work. Lates: mornings free, no weekday evenings, so no Coach pads.
- COLLEGE: term (Tuesday and Thursday mornings free), an exam fortnight twice a year (two sessions a week in all), holidays (every morning free).
- OFFICE: weekday mornings at work; about 1 week in 4 overtime is offered and the fighter asks you. CAFE (Ana): works every other Saturday.
- A professional under the top 10 keeps the job (in the code a professional trains full time on no pay: a change). Ranked in the top 10: full time.

### 3.4 One club twist a week (replaces the personal twist cards in the code: WeekTwist, TwistCard, TwistSwing)
Drawn on Monday from a deck of 12, never the same twice running, 1 week in 4 quiet (the code's own rules, kept). It hits something shared and has 2 or 3 answers, each with a price on named fighters, cash or a favour. Start week waits for it; unanswered, Coach picks. Examples: "Coach is off Wednesday: 2 pad places gone"; "A
visiting professional gives one sparring session: who gets it"; "The boiler is out Thursday: no mornings"; "Another coach asks you for a kilo"; "The local paper wants a picture: +2 members, 1 pad place"; "A club needs a boxer on Saturday: +2 favours, no fight week" (the 4-day call).

### 3.5 Favours, the second currency
0 to 3 held with each of the three clubs that run shows (Repton, West Ham, Fitzroy Lodge), drawn as dots beside the club's name.
- EARN 1: box on their show; take a late replacement on their card; a sparring trip there (one of your 2 pad places that week); say yes when their coach asks for a kilo; box their boxer who came in over the gap. EARN 2: send a boxer on a 4-day call to fill their card.
- LOSE 1: miss the weight on their show; pull your boxer out. Going home because THEIR boxer pulled out costs and earns nothing.
- SPEND 1: ask for a kilo; re-deal a bout sheet now; ask for a named opponent on their next show; move a bout by a week.
- CANNOT BUY: a win, a judge, a rank, training, a pad place, a seat. Favours bend terms, never bouts.

### 3.6 The squad changes
TRIAL MAT: every 4 weeks one prospect trains with the club for 2 weeks and takes 1 pad place a week from the four. At the end: Sign (a fifth fighter needs the second club coach, $280 a week, or bigger premises, or one of the four leaves) or Pass. SPARRING is any pair, by weight (a bout is never mixed): under 5 kg apart, full for
both; 5 to 10 kg, the lighter gets full work and the heavier half; over 10 kg, the lighter takes +10 tiredness. ANA: classes are free for Ana (the $34 in the code is what the club pays the members' class coach). On the cafe job 3 classes a week; on a seat 5 (drills 5/3 as fast, the stripe date printed for both); each class over
3 adds 6 tiredness. Ana can give one boxer a week a MAT MORNING (a coached Cardio morning at 0.8; it costs Ana one class). Ana's open is cornered by you, or by the mat coach for the day ($100), never by Coach.

## 4. The fight week inside the club week
An amateur bout has no camp. Its jobs are paid in the same pad places and mornings as everything else.
- THE SHEET. A READY boxer gets a sheet of up to three bouts every Monday, all for the Saturday 12 days out (a bout 5 days out only comes as a call). Three kinds, one of each: HOME (York Hall, the local hall: the $450 ticket cut, +8 members a win; never the best rank prize); A NAME ABOVE (away: a win takes that rank, at most 3
  up); THE UNKNOWN (a file of 1 of 4: the win chance band is 28 points wide, not 12, and the first look opens that file to 2 of 4 for the season). No card may beat another on all of cash, rank, win chance and file (it is redrawn). Away pays nothing, earns 1 favour with the host and +3 members a win; the top three names box on
  their own show 7 times in 10: the climb is away. Every win counts toward the 9-win offer, except over a name 6 or more points weaker (half the gate too). A loss to a name below drops 2 ranks. Two of yours on one home card: gate +$300. Each card prints the stoppage risk ("stopped 3 in 100: 30 days out") and what is given ("+0.7
  kg, +3 bouts"). HOME is a bout at York Hall on somebody's show. A show the club hosts itself (in the code: ClubShow, $3,500, every 16 weeks) is HOSTSHOW1, not this map.
- READY OR BUILDING. A pass keeps one name for 7 days, costs mood -2 (ready and held back), and a second pass running costs 1 favour with the club that stays. BUILDING to a date: no sheets, mood steady, and the weeks' training is kept at 1.1 (nothing to stay sharp for).
- THE SCALE. A club bout is made at the weight both boxers walk at, with a gap of 1.0 kg allowed. The answers appear only over that gap. Weigh-in: 4 pm on fight day. EAT LESS: tank -1 for each 0.1 kg (-2 past 0.5 kg). TRAIN IT OFF: each 0.25 kg is one session with no gain and +5 tiredness (free on rested legs, dear over 50). ASK
  A KILO: 1 favour, the other boxer's tank +5. HOLD: the club scale reads up to 0.2 kg either way, each 0.1 kg step as likely: 0.1 kg over is made 2 times in 5, 0.2 kg over 1 in 5, more never; a miss is no bout, $300 and a favour. Cuts to the class limit stay for professionals and the nationals.
- COACH'S EVENINGS. Prices in pad places: walk two plan lines 1; finish a nearly-learnt punch 1 for each 4.5 mastery short; southpaw rounds 1 (against a southpaw: the edge the code's "lefty week" gives today); film 2 (the whole evening, one a week for the club); stay sharp 1, inside the last five days. Lines and punches count up
  to 4 days before the bout; the last three days only keep the boxer sharp. The strip shows every pad place before the bout as a face and names who goes on the bag. Film asks one of three: the best punch, a habit, or THEIR WEEK. It can be spent on a name before the yes (choose better) or after (prepare better).
- THE PLAN IS A HAND. A plan holds as many lines as the coach can carry (6 with Coach; 4 to 9 in the code); a bout takes 4, each walked for it. A line left behind does not come out in round 1 and plays at 80% after. With no coach the plan holds 4: all come, the choice is which to walk. A corner that shared a show card with you
  has seen your winning line: it lands half as often, for two bouts. THE OTHER CORNER HAS A WEEK: their tank shows on the card (boxed 7 days ago -8, 14 days ago -4, back from a stoppage -5 and a slow start, fresh 0), or "?" unless one of yours shared a card with them.
- THE LEGS. One bar, THE TANK AT THE FIRST BELL (0 to 100). Tiredness over 50 costs 1 a point; eating weight off as above; your own last bout costs the same as theirs (7 days ago -8, 14 days ago -4); they ADD, at most 25 off. Three shapes: REST UP (tank full; SLOW START: round 1 at -1.0 points, the unit of the film and weapon
  edges; mood +4; a fifth of the week's training kept); TICK OVER (about 8 off; sharp if 1 pad place is spent in the last five days; half kept); TRAIN THROUGH (about 12 off; sharp; all kept; a sparring cut takes the boxer off the show 3 times in 100).
- ON THE NIGHT. The scale first, both weights. Then your corner, or COACH'S CORNER: the plan call and the water; no habit fix, no set-up, no talk. ROUTINE: "Coach runs it" fills Coach's standard week in one click, and the bill prints two win chances, Coach's week and yours.
- PULL-OUTS (15 in 100). Take the replacement (in the code: a tougher name, twice the reputation on a win), send another READY boxer, or go home.
- AFTER. A bout leaves tiredness (measured: 45 on a full tank, more when the tank was low, +10 after a stoppage loss, at most 95). No fixed wait: READY brings a sheet next Monday and the tank prices boxing again too soon. Stopped: 30 days out; again inside 90 days: 90.
