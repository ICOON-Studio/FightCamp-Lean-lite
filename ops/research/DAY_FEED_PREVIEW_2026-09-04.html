From the Motorsport Manager teardown · how it is actually built

White Primitives, Tinted And Stacked

Their entire interface is drawn from a few dozen white or grey sprites: a rounded box, a two-pixel outline, a soft vertical gradient, a box with a shadow, a feathered circle, a parallelogram, a chevron-pointed header, a hazard stripe, a half-ring dial. Colour is applied at runtime by tinting, and depth comes from stacking three or four of them. That is why every screen looks the same and every screen looks expensive. The kit is built the same way.

THE STACK
+ + = PANEL

Box + gradient + outline + shadow, each a white texture, each tinted from the ladder. One set of primitives, every surface in the game. UIKit primitives: box, gradient, outline, shadow, feather

TILT · THEIR IDENTITY SHAPE
SEND OUTCANCEL

The parallelogram is what makes it read as motorsport. Ours is not a copy: our identity shape is the corner-cut block, the shape of a ring apron and a fight poster. Used on the primary action and on fight-night chrome only. UIKit.cut_corner()

CHEVRON-TITLED HEADER
FIGHT WEEK

A title that points into its content. Their `ChevronTitled` header, adopted as our section header where a frame needs a direction. UIKit.chevron_header()

ENTITY LINK

Dmitri Vasa called out Kian Sterling ahead of Nicosia Fight Night, and Limassol Barbell are circling.

Every fighter, coach, gym, organization and event name is a link to its own screen, everywhere it appears, with back and forward in the shell. The Football Manager rule, and the thing that makes a game feel like a world. One treatment, never a web underline. UIKit.entity_link()

SCREEN INTRO

Panels enter from the left and settle in the middle, staggered. Their exact numbers live in compiled code I could not read, so this is designed from what you saw, not extracted: 0.38 s, ease-out quart, 60 px offset, 80 ms per panel, fade with the slide. UIKit.intro(panel, delay)

THE BODY · REFERENCE

Anatomical muscular figure, front view, the stock "muscular system" illustration style, with the six regions tinted over the musculature. Licensed from a stock library or commissioned in that style; nothing ripped ships.

Replaces the silhouette above. The regions stay the six the engine already simulates. assets/body/muscular_front.png + region masks

MOTORSPORT MANAGER · WHAT ELSE WAS IN THE FILES
Type is Mission Gothic (a condensed grotesque, three weights) for display and Cooper Hewitt (three weights) for text, plus two custom tabular numeral faces for timing. Round stat cells are one sprite, GraphCircleStats, tinted. Stars ship with a half state. Progress bars use a hazard stripe texture. The notification badge is CircleNotice over a feathered glow. Nothing in the atlas is coloured; the palette lives entirely in code.

What Changed And Why

✓Two mostly-empty bars → one header: crest, title, date, chips, roster, bell, date + Continue.
✓Gradient bars under the news → gone. The value cell on the right carries the figure.
✓Too dark → ground lifted to blue-grey, every panel top-lit, scrim at 92–96%, the gym colour on headers and selection.
✓Dead gap in the middle → the detail panel is a stack of full-width frames; the choices sit against the readout.
✓Bar beside a value cell → replaced by the paired label-and-value block. One instrument per figure.
✓Name not visible enough → names at 21–24px in the display face on every chip, card and speaker strip.
✓Marco Reyes buried → the speaker leads the detail panel in a highlighted strip with portrait and role.
✓Labels too small → labels are 16–17px display blocks or 11–12px bold caps. Nothing thinner than that anywhere.
✓Fire Pro cells → raised, top-lit, hard-edged; label and value in two colour families; uniform rows edge to edge; unmissable selection; framed groups with full-width header bars.
✓Stars and lock → in the kit and on the gym overview for reputation tier.
✓Football Manager → colour on the ground, round cells with lozenges, the round counter, date joined to Continue, chevron headers, alternating rows.
✓The body → a front-on fighter with hands up, six separately filled regions, leaders that touch, blocks beside their region, a legend.
✓Form first → form dot, then the stamina bar with its readout, then Open.
