## Your call (new mechanics, not in the approved loop)
At the goal's deadline today the floor says one line and picks his next goal itself. Ludo proposes:
1. **A result card** (green GOAL COMPLETED / red GOAL MISSED) with the trust and morale change, and **you choose his next goal
   from two** (e.g. POWER SURGE vs TANK CONDITIONING, each with its target, days and reward).
2. **A walk-out warning**: if his trust falls under 30 after a miss, a yellow box says he may leave the gym, and only one
   "damage control" goal is offered (3 days of rest and technique).

My recommendation: yes to both - choosing the next goal is the player's decision the loop is missing, and the walk-out warning
gives a missed deadline a real cost without a new system (trust already exists).
