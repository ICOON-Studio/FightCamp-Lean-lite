## What the screenshots show (a build labelled "QA PROMOTION", dated in-game 1 April 2026)
1. **Command Center**: left rail (Events / Promotions / World Boxing / Career), top bar reputation, capital, roster
   morale, days to fight night, Save, Simulate To Date, Advance Day. Photo-style backdrops (arena, belt and contract
   on a desk) behind stat tiles, Priority Desk ("Aaron Davis - unhappy with current deal"), timeline, news pulse,
   quick actions.
2. **Create New Show**: venue (The Back Bar Room, London, 120), show level, ticket price; itemised operating cost
   (venue hire, production, staffing, medical and officials, insurance) against a projected gate, sell-through and a
   red PROJECTED LOSS. "Projected loss does not block creation."
3. **Choose Venue**: UK venues from a 120-seat back room up to 80,000-seat stadiums, with estimated operating cost.
4. **Championships**: four world bodies (GBC, IBA, WCA, UBA) plus a European title - belts drawn as look-alikes of
   the real WBC/WBA/IBF/WBO belts - 8 divisions, champions, vacant titles, title pathways (continental, national).
5. **World Rankings**: a consensus board beside each body's ranking, top 10 per division.
6. **Talent Network**: scouting with potential as a RANGE and a confidence level ("47-85 - Moderate confidence").
7. **Roster & Contracts**: fighter dossier, contract terms (fights remaining, expiry, purse per fight, signing bonus),
   "Wants a better deal" status.
8. **News & Stories**: 263 stories, 219 bout reports, results, retirements, comebacks.

## Read against FightCamp
**Closest threat of the two surveyed, for three reasons**
- **It claims our edge: 3D fights with corner decisions between rounds.** Cage Legacy's fight is pixel art and a text
  log; this one says 3D. Until it shows footage we cannot judge its quality - that is the one thing to watch.
- **Its look is close to our direction A**: dark navy with gold accent chrome over moody photo backdrops (arena
  lights, a belt on a contract). Side by side on a store page, our brass-and-leather could read as the same school.
- **The promoter's economy is deeper than ours today**: an itemised show cost sheet, ticket pricing, sell-through,
  venue ladder, broadcast/streaming/PPV. That is the "run the business" half we have only started.

**Where we are ahead**
- **Disciplines**: boxing only vs our boxing, kickboxing, MMA and grappling.
- **Real organisation names** (operator ruling 2026-09-25) vs its made-up bodies with look-alike belts.
- **We can SHOW the fight.** Ours is simulated body-on-body with a camera director and a broadcast shot, and we have
  footage. Theirs is text on a store page. Whoever shows convincing 3D boxing footage first owns that promise.
- **A gym that is yours**: its gym side is a mode option; ours is the core.

**What it says for S81's hub-display decision**
- Both competitors use photo/art stills behind dashboards. **This one's stills are the same mood as ours**, which
  weakens option A as a way to stand out and strengthens showing our own fighters in 3D somewhere a player sees early.
- Its Create Show cost sheet (itemised costs vs projected revenue, loss in red, not blocking) is a strong pattern for
  our booking/finance pages - note, not copy.
