# FightCamp campaign pitch: THE HOUSE THAT HOLDS
**Writer:** Kalli  
**Structure used:** three-act career ladder (amateur → regional → national → world), rival heat arc, mentor-as-pressure (not mentor death), calendar-as-spine. No plot or characters taken from any named reference.

**Word target:** 2,200–3,200. Sections 1–8 and 10. Section 9 skipped (Dola only).

---

## 1. Logline + tone

You inherit a gym that still smells like someone else's work, and the sport will not wait for you to learn it. The campaign is about whether a house can hold: whether the people under your roof last long enough for the ladder to mean something. Tone is quiet and procedural, with the dry humour of people who count rounds and bills in the same voice; it must never become a sports-movie montage or a villain speech.

At the **10-hour mark** the player should feel competent and watched: they know the weekly loop, their first fighter has a name the city knows, and someone across town wants that name. At the **credits** they should feel the house is theirs: the gym still runs if the campaign flag turns off, the rival is a living number on a wall, and the next fighter walks into rooms that already remember the last one.

---

## 2. The spine

**Protagonist (player):** a new owner who was never a star. You were a corner second for twelve years under **Vince Calder**, who built Calder House Gym and walked away the night the lights stayed on and the bank account did not. You buy the lease with a thin loan and a promise you did not write down: keep the doors open. You are not the chosen coach. You are the person who still has a set of keys.

**First fighter: Mara "Wire" Solano.** Amateur welter, quiet, left hand that arrives late and honest. She walks in looking for a gym that will not sell her week to a promoter she does not trust. She becomes Chapter 1's body: if she lasts, the house lasts.

**Antagonistic force: the calendar wearing a human face.** The sport itself is the pressure (bouts on fixed dates, bills on fixed dates, ladders that do not care). The human face is **Rook Hale**, owner of Hale Yard, a rival gym that books the same weight, hunts the same regional belts, and treats heat as a business tool. Hale does not hate you. Hale needs your fighter to look small so his look big. Behind him sits **Northline Promotions**, which prefers gyms that hand over talent early and smile about it.

**Three acts on the ladder**

- **Act I — Amateur / early regional (Chapters 1–4).** Keep Solano fed, trained and booked. Learn the week. Survive the first bill crisis. Meet Hale when heat first ticks up.
- **Act II — Regional → national (Chapters 5–8).** Solano becomes a name. Heat with Hale's fighter **Jace Vellin** climbs past 50. Northline offers a bad contract that looks like help. A career injury or a towel decision tests the corner.
- **Act III — National → world gate (Chapters 9–12).** A title chase or a eliminator that the story frames; the sim still decides the result. Endings branch on money, heat, morale and whether the house still owns its fighters.

## 5. Choices that matter (5)

1. **Northline deal (Beat 6).** Sign: money now, purse cut, Orth stays in the inbox. Refuse: thinner cash, higher loyalty, harder early books. **If ignored:** Orth's offer expires; campaign continues on the "independent" branch; sandbox booking unchanged.
2. **Answer the callout (Beat 5).** Answer: heat spikes, rematch path opens. Ignore: heat cools slowly, Hale books someone else, story shifts to a different rival pair. **If ignored:** rivalry wall stays quiet; free play still has heat system.
3. **Shared spar (Beat 7).** Accept: better reads, injury risk, heat. Refuse: safer camp, Hale uses it in press. **If ignored:** no spar event; training week normal.
4. **Towel trust (Beat 8).** Trust corner: longer recovery, higher trust. Override: possible short-term booking, trust drop, Len Park may refuse hire. **If ignored:** default corner AI; story skips to Beat 9 with neutral trust.
5. **Lease buyout (Beat 11).** Keep: hard money, full control. Sell stake: cash injection, Hale gets a say in one booking per quarter. Walk: campaign ends early into free play with a "sold" flag. **If ignored:** timer ends; default Keep with a debt event.

Sandbox rule: every beat is a flag layer. Turning story off hides cards and keeps ladder, money, heat and fights fully playable.

## 6. Fight-night moments (4)

1. **Corner talk tied to a flag.** Between rounds, if heat ≥ 50 and opponent is Vellin, Len (or default coach) opens with one line about the last spar or the callout. Reads still come from the sim; the line is flavour on top.
2. **Rival entrance.** When Hale Yard is on the card, the walkout card shows Hale in the opposite corner for one beat. No scripted punches.
3. **Grudge round.** If heat ≥ 70, round start can show a short "this is the one they sold" press sting; judge scoring and engine unchanged.
4. **Injury callback.** If Solano is fighting on a shortened injury recovery, physio Ivy's pre-fight note sits on the corner tray: one risk line. The engine already models the body; the story only names it.

Never: forced knockdowns, scripted stoppages, or auto-wins.

---

## 7. Ending(s)

**A. The House Holds.** World gate opened with Solano still under contract, Northline deal refused or bought out cleanly, bank ≥ 0. Credits: Calder leaves a short note; free play inherits gym, cast, heat map.

**B. Sold Roof.** Player took Hale's stake or walked. Solano may stay or leave by loyalty. Free play continues in a renamed or co-owned gym; Hale remains a recurring booker.

**C. Wire's Choice.** Solano leaves for Northline or retires after injury path. Campaign ends on her exit card; next fighter tutorial is shorter because rooms already exist.

**Carry-over:** cash, facility unlocks, staff contracts, rivalry heat table, handbook chapters completed, and a "house memory" flag that changes locker ambient lines for the next fighter. Fight results are never rewritten.

## 10. The story shapes the menus, the UI and the loop

### (a) Title menu and new-career flow

**New Career asks, in order:** gym name (default Calder House / rename), weight-class focus for Chapter 1 (boxing welter locked for demo; other classes grey with "later"), story layer On/Off, difficulty. No origin slideshow.

**Returning player sees:** chapter number, next fight (name, date, heat pip), who is waiting (one line: Orth / Hale / Solano / bill), Continue (gold). Load Free Play sits under Continue when `story_on` is false or campaign complete.

### (b) Weekly loop

A **week** is: plan training/rest/spar → pay bills → optional book/scout → advance → week review.

**Interrupt:** at most one story card when a trigger fires, before week review. Never mid-training click.

**Always first on MY GYM:** NEXT UP card (top-left): next bout or next story promise, whichever is sooner.

**"One more week" pull:** NEXT UP always shows a visible promise: "Vellin in 3 weeks", "Orth calls Friday", "Rent due in 6 days". The player advances to shorten that number.

### (c) First hour (Chapter 1 demo)

| Minute | What happens |
|--------|----------------|
| 0–5 | Title → New Career → office card (Keys). Locker: meet Solano. Handbook page 1. |
| 5–15 | Gym floor hub. Assign first training week. Staff room empty except hire hint. |
| 15–25 | Advance week. Week review. NEXT UP: first bout in 3 weeks. |
| 25–40 | Two more weeks: light spar option, bill beat if cash thin, book first regional. |
| 40–55 | Fight week: camp summary, corner tray, fight sim. |
| 55–60 | Result screen ("why it went your way" from engine facts). Chapter 1 end card. Demo stop or continue. |

Raw boxer → first fight is the chapter end. No montage.

### (d) Each existing screen and what changes

- **Title menu:** chapter / next fight / who waits (above).
- **MY GYM:** NEXT UP; obligations row (bills, Orth, Hale); fighter strip with heat pip on Solano when relevant.
- **LADDER:** story gate markers (soft): "Chapter 2 opens at regional" as tooltip on the rung, not a hard lock beyond real level gates.
- **Schedule / booking:** Hale Yard entries tagged with a small rival icon; tooltip only.
- **Week review:** one story line if a beat fired; else clean.
- **Corner:** flag-gated one-liners; calls stay system-driven.
- **Scorebug:** no story text; icons only.
- **Result screen:** engine reasons first; optional "story note" under if heat or towel flag.
- **Handbook:** chapter pages unlock with beats; voice stays handbook-neutral (you / your gym).
- **Rooms hosting scenes:** Office = bills, Calder, lease, Orth paperwork. Locker = Solano, poach letter. Mat room = sparring war. Physio = injury callback. Staff = Len hire. Film room (if present) = Hale tape optional later.

### (e) One new screen: Rivalry Wall

Opened from MY GYM or LADDER. Text mock-up:

```
RIVALRY WALL
Solano  ████████░░  78   Vellin
Last: same card, regional, heat +12
Next: eliminator window in 21 days
[ANSWER CALLOUT]  [KEEP QUIET]  [OPEN BOOKING]
Hale Yard record vs you: 2–1–0 (nights)
```

Icons for heat, rematch, same-belt chase; tooltips carry the words.

### (f) UI rules the story implies

1. Icons over text; every icon has a tooltip.
2. The facility is a **gym**; never label the building "fight camp".
3. One clear next action (NEXT UP / Continue gold).
4. Story cards are dismissible; sandbox never blocks.
5. Rooms stay bright and alive; story scenes are short cards in rooms, not dark cutaways.
6. Men / women booking rules unchanged; story never asks for a cross-book.

### Loop diagram (plain text)

```
week plan → (optional story card if trigger) → advance
    → week review → decision (train / book / pay / answer)
        → fight week → corner (flag lines) → sim result
            → aftermath (money, injury, heat)
                → chapter beat card (if gate met)
                    → back to week plan
```

## Strongest ruling (argued against)

**Ruling:** the antagonist is the calendar with Hale as its face, not a cartoon villain, and Northline is a contract pressure, not a conspiracy.

**Against:** a clearer villain is easier to market and easier to write callouts for.

**Keep:** because the sim already makes results unfair-feeling when money and injuries stack; a cartoon villain would fight the design pillar that the fight is not scripted. Hale wanting the same oxygen as you is enough conflict for heat, booking and press without stealing the engine's job.

**NOT COMPUTED:** exact heat numbers for beat gates need a pass against the live rivalry curve once that curve is final.
