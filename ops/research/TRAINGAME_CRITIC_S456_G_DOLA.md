2. USABILITY & CLARITY SCORE: 7/10
What works:
Drag-and-drop + per-day Light/Normal/Hard toggles are immediately intuitive; the week layout matches real calendar logic, far clearer than list-only training screens in Boxing Manager or Football Manager.
Win probability range + fatigue curve with coach-plan baseline is excellent feedback — you see consequences before committing, unlike Punch Club where results are hidden until next day.
Ink-style icons align well with the Playbook visual language; colour coding by stat type keeps things scannable without clutter.
What holds it back:
Evening options (film/sparring/rest) lack at-a-glance fatigue cost — you must hover or drag to see impact, slowing decisions.
Mastery numbers on evening cards are small and easy to miss.
Comparison: Motorsport Manager training screens are cleaner on trade-offs; this has richer info but denser presentation.
3. DECISION DEPTH SCORE: 9/10
Excellent decision space — no single "correct" plan:
Every choice trades fatigue vs stat gain vs mastery vs injury risk vs fight-readiness goals.
Coach's plan is solid but not optimal; player changes always shift multiple metrics at once (+WIN here = -conditioning there, or -injury safety).
Hidden tells + move-answer mechanics reward scouting and planning, not just maximising numbers.
Fatigue curve, phase timing, and mastery caps mean what works this week fails next week.
