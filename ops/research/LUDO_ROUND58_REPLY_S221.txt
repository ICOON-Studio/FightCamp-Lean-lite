### Detailed Prop Specifications

#### 01 & 02. `Lockers/bank_west` & `Lockers/bank_north` (4-Tier Steel Lockers)
* **Function:** Left-clicking an individual locker door selects that assigned boxer, focusing their profile card and 9 attribute readouts (`POWER`, `TECHNIQUE`, `SPEED`, `STAMINA`, `CHIN`, `DEFENCE`, `FOOTWORK`, `RING IQ`, `COMPOSURE`).
* **Unscaled Dimensions:** $2.00\text{ m W} \times 0.50\text{ m D} \times 1.90\text{ m H}$ (Bank of 4 lockers, each $0.50\text{ m}$ wide).
* **Attachment & Front:** Floor-standing, backed flat against the wall. Front facing into the center room.
* **Materials:**
  * Locker Body & Frame: Dark Charcoal Structural Steel `#2b2f36` with edge chamfer highlights `#4a515c` (CC0 *Metal008* PBR set: Roughness 0.45, Metallic 0.85).
  * Nameplate Sliders: Antiqued Cast Brass / Gold `#f0c233`.
  * Name Cards: Cream Off-White `#f6f0e0` with printed fighter names.
* **Tri Budget:** $4,200$ tris per 4-locker bank ($1,050$ tris per locker column).
* **+25% Feature:** **The stamped brass nameplate holders and louvered ventilation slats** across the upper face of each door.
