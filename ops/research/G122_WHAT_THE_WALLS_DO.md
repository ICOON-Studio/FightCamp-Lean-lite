# G122_WHAT_THE_WALLS_DO (Grok, collected by Claude S89, 2026-09-26)
Source: grok.com chat "G122: Real Fight Gym Walls, Decor, and Trophy Impact" (Expert, 75 sources). Brief:

Headline: walls are identity, not kit. Small clubs pin paper; famous gyms build a museum you walk through. Evidence
that walls alone sell memberships is thin.

## 1. What hangs, by size
- **Back-room / ABA club:** old fight posters, newspaper clips, team photos, nailed gloves, cheap vinyl slogans.
  Repton (London) still shows a 1978 poster; Sunderland East End ABC paid for an exterior mural of its own kids. SOURCED (Alamy)
- **Established club:** dense poster/photo walls, framed results, a slogan or mural behind the ring, a small trophy
  shelf. Wild Card: hundreds of posters and photos, Roach as a young pro above the counter, a women's collage on the
  ladies' door. Gleason's: no bare wall, champion boards, Ali over the rope line. SOURCED (LA Times, Gear Patrol)
- **Academy / team HQ:** Wall of Fame portraits, belt cases, branded murals, neon, an entrance museum. Kronk (new
  building): memorabilia at the door, Steward overhead, an Icons Wall (Andy Lee added 2025). AKA's Red Room: ~40
  five-foot portraits, signed covers up the stairs, Wall of Fame. Tiger Muay Thai: athlete banners, slogans, fighter
  vs beginner zones, shop/cafe as the first walls. SOURCED. Jackson Wink: functional warehouse, tours show mats and
  cage more than trophies. PROXY. The old Kronk basement: cracked walls, faded clippings by the ring - character can
  be dirt or museum. SOURCED (ESPN)

## 3. Costs (USD, 2024-26) - SOURCED
- Painted mural ~$20-50 / sq ft pro, $2-5k for 100 sq ft; big names $50-100 / sq ft (HomeGuide).
- Printed wall graphic ~$18-25 / sq ft installed; accent wall $1.5-3k; whole facility $5-25k.
- LED neon $200-700 typical, custom large $400-700+.
- Framed posters: tens of dollars; a vintage gym poster ~$235.
- Belt display: acrylic ~$90-140; wood/UV cabinet ~$130-255; LED custom from ~$179.
- Startup-guide signage line $2-10k.
A wall is cheaper than a ring or a bag row - why owners do it when cash is short.

## 4. What it buys
**No fight-gym study isolates "trophy wall -> sign-ups." Said plainly.** Fitness servicescape research: ambient
conditions (light, noise, cleanliness, colour) predict loyalty more than decoration; signage a small path; design weak
(SOURCED Europe PMC). Owners treat vibe as retention, not a poster SKU (SOURCED Gymdesk). A whole-building refurb
(not walls alone) lifted one leisure centre's direct debits +109% (SOURCED Health Club Management). Wall-art vendors'
"+20% intensity" is marketing (PROXY). Morale/recruiting: owner testimony, not data. JUDGEMENT: walls buy reputation
and photo/video identity; they do not replace coaches, results or a clean floor.

## 5. JUDGEMENT - 8 game items
| Item | Cost | Hangs | Effect | Unlock |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Flyer / clip wall | $50 | office, locker hall | tiny morale | open gym |
| Framed poster rack | $200 | door sightline | walk-in reputation | 10 members or first show |
| Slogan paint | $400 | behind the ring | morale in sparring | wing, tier 3 |
| Neon wordmark | $600 | desk / street window | night reputation | cash + tier 3 |
| Fighter photo row | $300 | bags + locker corridor | fighter morale / recruiting | first regional title |
| Trophy cabinet | $500 | lobby | member pride | 3 amateur medals |
| Belt case | $250 + the belt | door, then ring wall | reputation spike | one of your fighters wins a belt |
| History mural | $3k | ring wall or entrance | brand / social share | tier 4 and a named champion from the gym |

**Rule: belts and named portraits are EARNED, not bought. Paper and neon can be bought.** That matches real rooms.

## Claude's reading (S89)
The rule in the last line is the design: decor is two kinds - bought (paper, neon, paint) and earned (belts,
portraits, medals, murals of your own champions) - and the earned kind is the career made visible. Effects stay
SMALL and land on reputation (which already moves members) and morale; section 4 forbids a big member multiplier.
