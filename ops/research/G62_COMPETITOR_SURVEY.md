## 1. Parity gap — what all five have that FightCamp does not

Nothing sits in all five columns. The cluster that 3–4 of them advertise and FightCamp's brief does not:

- You are the promoter, not the gym. Card, venue, gate, PPV/broadcast, sanctioning bodies. FC books bouts from a gym; they book the industry.
- AI rival organisations that sign, schedule, and poach in parallel (MSK, BPW, PROM, TFB).
- Shareable database / Workshop (MSK, BPW, PROM, BCM). FC brief has none.
- Start-at-the-top or generate-a-world sandbox as a first-class mode (MSK, BPW, PROM).
- Press-conference / narrative-selling layer (MSK pressers; BPW promos/angles; PROM "sell the narrative"). FC has a news feed, not a hype machine.

Do not chase BCM's 3D-in-the-career-loop as parity. It shipped and the reviews punished it. TFB's 3D is a trailer.

What FightCamp already has that this list does not: calibrated MMA statistics, a grappling model, weight-cut/weigh-in as systems, injury clearance, amateur federation, short-notice constraints, ~380 tests, combo/camp development. None of the five document a fight engine at that grain.

## 2. Why Boxing Club Manager is MIXED — ranked complaints

Shipped Oct 2022 at $39.99. Mixed, ~56–57% of ~87–93 reviews. Last content-bearing public build 14 June 2023 (UI perf). Concurrent players: 0–2 now, all-time peak 27 on EA launch day. Estimated ~2.7k units. Steambase +1

Recurring complaints, most-cited first:

1. Price vs meat. "$40 is a joke. It might be worth $10." "Should be £5." Completing a world-title run in a couple of hours. Most helpful negatives open on this.
2. Fight engine does not mean what it shows. Historical KO-not-registering (win-by-KO counted as points loss). Sim-skip described as coin-flip — "take a donkey, keep simulating, he will win." Only swarmer style viable. Tactics advertised, outcomes not trusted.
3. No boxing politics. "Does not meaningfully capture the drama of boxing, its tactical battles or the politics of prize fighting." (Most-helpful early negative, 32 helpful.)
4. Abandoned after 1.0. July 2024 review: "pointless, devs never fixed or added content… last reviews Jan 2023." News hub dies in June 2023. That is the review trajectory, not a vibe.
5. Opaque systems. No documentation of what stats do. Title history not updating. Can't move weight class after becoming champion.
6. 3D was the pitch and not the save. Positives like the fights "looking good / shiny." They did not hold the score. Presentation without a trusted sim is how you get Mixed at €35.

Failure mode to avoid: ship a dear 3D room on top of a thin gym loop, then go quiet. The operator already called FC's 3D "painful to watch." BCM is that sentence with a $40 sticker and a 2023 tombstone.
