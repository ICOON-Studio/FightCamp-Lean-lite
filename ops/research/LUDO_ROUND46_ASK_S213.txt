=== HOW REAL FIGHTERS IMPROVE (our sourced research - extend it, correct it if you know better) ===
- Motor skill follows a power law in practice hours (log-hours); everyday skills plateau after < 50 h once "good enough"; elite work fights the plateau with deliberate practice (Ericsson 2009). One night's sleep after a novel motor sequence gives ~18-20% faster execution (consolidation).
- Strength: untrained ~2.5%/week early, trained ~1.6%/week, slowing; detraining after 2-3 weeks off.
- Skills move through stages: shadow/technique -> pads/mitts (coach feeds) -> bag (power/volume) -> drilling with a partner -> situational sparring -> hard sparring -> fights; a move that works on pads often disappears under pressure until it has been sparred.
- Fight camps 6-10 weeks: build, peak, taper (last 7-10 days lighter); overtraining shows as flat performance, stress, injuries.
- Coaches matter by specialty and by bond; a fighter learns best from a coach who teaches HIS style; a "signature" move comes from thousands of reps of a few things, not breadth.
- Fights teach what the gym cannot (composure, ring IQ), and losses teach more than wins when reviewed (film study).
