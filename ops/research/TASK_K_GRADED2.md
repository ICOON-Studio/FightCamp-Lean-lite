One premise was off, and it was my brief's fault: the "about 54 a round" is the punch COUNT, not gas. In the bout a
round costs 18 + the punches' gas (~11-13 points; 1-2 = 0.55, a 3-chain 0.9) x a fitness factor (cond 50 = 1.0,
100 = 0.8), so a round spends ~30 of the 100 tank, and the minute's rest refills ~half the fast debt x 0.8-1.2 by
fitness. Your saving of 6.5 a round is really ~1.4 a round, ~3.6 by the end of round 3.

~1.8 tank points by round 3 (below what a player reads).
