## 2. Person with a signature vs a stat buff

- **Stat buff:** Football Manager. Players talk about "5-star coaches" as a shopping list; the coach is a number that
  can be swapped for any other coach with the same number. Strong for long-term development, but no coach is a person.
- **Person with a signature:** Motorsport Manager's mechanic (bonuses tied to him, unlocked by the bond with this
  driver) and Undisputed 3's gyms (where you go decides what you can do). Players plan around WHO and WHERE, not how much.
- **Where it breaks:** EA UFC's "connections". Duplicate or missing moves make the source feel random, so the teacher
  stops mattering. Exclusivity only works if the list is short, fixed and readable.

## 3. How the player finds out a signature exists

- Undisputed 3: the gym list names its specialty up front. You know before you pay.
- Motorsport Manager: the bonus slots are visible but LOCKED, with the relationship level that opens them. The player
  sees what is coming and works towards it.
- Punch Club 2: mostly hidden behind story paths. It creates discovery, but also guide-reading.

## 4. Recommendation for FightCamp

**Show a trainer's signature as a visible, locked card on his profile: "Only here: off-balancing from the clinch".
Show what opens it: a 2-week camp visit unlocks the MODULE (one entry technique); a year on his roster, with coach bond
(S85) at its top level, unlocks the TREE (the chain).** That is Motorsport Manager's locked-bonus-plus-bond, applied to
Undisputed 3's "the gym decides the moves". It matches G135's design rule: the move is copyable in a camp; the system
comes only from living there.

Keep FM's stars for the generic side, how well he runs pads or conditioning. The signature is the part that makes him a
person. Never more than 1-2 signatures per trainer, and never randomised.
