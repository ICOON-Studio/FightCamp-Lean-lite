## The model
An MMA fight has three parts; each art trains one or two:
- STRIKING: boxing (hands, footwork, head movement), kickboxing / Muay Thai (kicks, knees, clinch strikes)
- WRESTLING: takedowns, takedown defence, the clinch on the cage
- GROUND: no-gi BJJ (positions, escapes, submissions)
A fighter's MMA readiness = her coverage of the three; cross-training in the gym (a BJJ class, a wrestling class) closes a gap,
shown the way THIS CAMP shows a goal line.

| Wrestling | MMA update, before judo | the base that controls where the fight happens | WrestleChains, tie-up clips |
| Judo | later add-on, not now | a gi sport (operator: "we only use no gi on the game"); its throws come through wrestling/clinch | none |

## Why not now
The agreed release: Steam EA = boxing (+ no-gi BJJ), MMA = the first update. The lean restart's rule: a new system enters only
when the last build feels right. Kickboxing and wrestling join with the MMA update.
