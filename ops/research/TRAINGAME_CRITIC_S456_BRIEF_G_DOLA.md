# YOUR JOB (fresh critic): one fighter's Training screen, judged as a GAME screen, not as an information sheet
The owner wants: art where it matters, little text, and from the picture alone it is clear how you set the training, what your options
are and what each improves (by how much), with tiredness and intensity shown. The art must match the rest of the game's screens: the
camp plan's pictures, and session icons drawn like the Playbook's ink move pictures.

## What the picture shows (the player's one boxer, 12 days before a club-show bout)
Top bar: the club, the date, money, a Weekly card flag (unanswered: the coach picks if the player runs the week) and RUN THE WEEK.
Header: the fighter, the next bout and a countdown, a WIN dial (coach's plan 51%; the player's changes +1.5 to +4 = 53-55%, a range because Tuesday's film opens a still-hidden tell whose points count only if a Usable move answers it), the goal bar
(Stamina 60.4 by the bout: 60.1 now, 60.3 by the bout, 0.1 short, and the one change that closes it), the opponent's two habits.
THIS WEEK: a week goal (Peak / Build / Recover, each with the WIN % and tiredness peak it would give), Undo and Coach's plan, and what
next week's taper does. Seven days: a MORNING card (camp-plan picture, an ink icon ringed in its stat's colour, the stat and the number
one session adds, tiredness added, and a Light / Normal / Hard switch on every morning) and an EVENING card (pads - the move drilled and
its mastery before and after; film; sparring; a rest night). Where the player changed the coach's week, the day header names the coach's card and the card has a grey dashed outline. Only the player's own changes carry tags: green = WIN points added, red =
what the change gave up. ONE tiredness curve (Fresh / Working / Tired from 72 / Overreached from 80), with the coach's plan as a grey
dashed line for comparison. A sparring card is being dragged onto Saturday evening: an amber dashed line shows the tiredness it would
cause, and a strip shows what the drop buys and costs before the player lets go.
SESSIONS (bottom): every session as a round ink icon to drag, ringed in its stat's colour, with its number and tiredness at Normal;
mornings left of the divider, evenings right; a colour key.
Right column: ON FIGHT NIGHT (weight with a Hold / Cut switch and what Hold would do, the goal, the tiredness peak against the coach's,
the injury chance, the moves ready) with one line tying the film to the plan; the coach's pick as a handwritten note with how sure as a
number; the clicked card (Wednesday evening: one pad night split between two moves, the split bar, what it gives and gives up, and the
pad rule: a move gains less the nearer it is to its cap); USE COACH'S PICK with what the coach's plan gives.
The numbers come from the game's rules: a morning is a fifth of a focused week (x0.6 Light, x1.3 Hard), times the coach and the
headroom to potential, which is why one morning adds about 0.1 to a stat.
