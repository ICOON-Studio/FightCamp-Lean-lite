# YOUR JOB (fresh critic): one fighter's Training screen, judged as a GAME screen, not as an information sheet

The owner's words on the last version: "it lacks art the info is too text heavy and it is not clear from the picture how you set the
training, what are your options and what each improves, fatigue and intensity meters maybe would be nice, how much do you rate it for a
game not as an info sheet?"

What the picture shows (the player's one boxer, 12 days before a club-show bout; this is the last week whose pad nights count for it):
- Header: the fighter, the next bout and a countdown, a WIN dial (the coach's week against the player's week), the fighter's goal bar.
- SESSIONS tray (left): picture cards the player drags onto the week. Each card shows what it grows (arrows) and the fatigue it adds (dots);
  hover gives the numbers at Light / Normal / Hard.
- THIS WEEK (centre): seven days, a MORNING row (each card has its own Light / Normal / Hard buttons) and an EVENING row (pads with the
  coach - each pad card shows the punch or combination being drilled with a ring that fills to 30, the level at which the fighter can
  throw it in a fight - film of the opponent, sparring, free). Cards the player changed say YOURS. A card is being dragged onto
  Wednesday evening; its hover card shows what that change does to the WIN dial and what it gives up.
- FATIGUE (bottom): the day-by-day fatigue curve with the bands where gains slow and injury risk climbs. INTENSITY: the whole week's
  Light / Normal / Hard with a one-line consequence.
- WHAT THIS WEEK BUILDS (right): stat bars with this week's gain hatched, the goal marker; the moves as rings to 30; the weight line to
  the limit with the diet switch (Hold / Cut); one coach suggestion with Apply.
- CONTINUE (top right) runs the week.

Questions, in this order:
1. LOGIC CHECK: anything the game could not do at that moment, any number that contradicts another on the same screen, any name used
   outside a header.
2. AS A GAME, score 1 to 10: would you enjoy planning a week here? Is it clear in five seconds how you set the training, what your options
   are and what each improves? Is there art where it matters? Compare with screens you know (Football Manager training, Punch Club,
   Motorsport Manager, Two Point, Boxing Manager on Steam).
3. DEPTH, score 1 to 10: is the week a real decision (trade-offs that change), or is there one right plan?
4. Which options are missing, which are useless, what would each change?
5. END with "To reach 10/10", most important first. Then describe your 10/10 screen for this as a layout on 1920x1080, panel by panel.
Plain words. No praise. Score honestly: a 7 means you would defend it to the owner. Do not ask questions back.
