# Knowledge stakes — boxing design review

SCORE depth=6 general=6

**Depth — 6/10.** The best choice is taking a paying bout even though the opponent waits to counter and your boxer drops the right after the jab. A manager can afford that risk, decline it, or spend the one corner fix on it instead of another habit. The weakest choice is the supposed centrepiece: neither a test day nor an outside spar tells you whether that right hand drops. If both ends of the new attribute range would lead to the same contract and first opponent, keep the money. A better finish by a club that buys reads would not, by itself, show that learning habits mattered.

**General design — 6/10.** Keep the notebook, the opponent file and the single exchange cost. Each answers a different question: what your coach saw, what film showed, and what actually cost an edge. The map sometimes treats an attribute estimate as an observation, a style as a prediction, and a plan situation as a cure. Those distinctions matter more than another reveal button.

**Play — 7/10.** The satisfying return visit is the next bell: did the hand drop again, and did the instruction help? “It came up twice and cost less” rewards paying attention even in a lost round. A quiet week should stay one click. Bring back a note on Monday when it changes an offer or a training choice, not every time a range moves.

## What the numbers buy

Read `assets/data/fight_habits.json` and, in `scripts/rules/`, `HabitFix.gd`, `KnowReads.gd`, `Knowledge.gd`, `CoachNotes.gd`, `Recruit.gd`, `ModeTells.gd` and `TellWorth.gd`. Also checked the relevant tables and functions in `Exchanges.gd`, `PlanLines.gd` and `OppFile.gd`.

- **20 habits, 19 possible costs, 18 possible corner fixes.** The 17 situation entries *include* switching stance under pressure (press) and talking when ahead (counter), contrary to §1.3. Two more go by time: exchanges 1–2 of round one and 4–6 of the last round (index 3 onward). Only digging to the body when hurt never costs this penalty. A slow start is over before the first corner. If two match, only the first in list order costs anything.
- **6 power points, not six points on a scorecard.** With the 1.75 bout multiplier that is 10.5 before a fix. A matching fix saves the fraction held, not always half. It does not promise to win the exchange. A state such as being tired is not an opponent style. Even in the style table, 0.25 load-up is not the boxer-puncher's main game (range is 0.35); there is no free mode for that style.
- **Two kinds of evidence.** One habit is described at recruitment; that is not the same as a `habit:` entry seen by the corner. A fight notebook reveal takes the first unseen item, habits first, then notable traits (≥70 or ≤40). Sparring has a 35% chance per reveal call, not 35% for each habit. The notebook also has a film-source branch for one unseen habit; that function alone does not establish a prospect-film purchase. Opponent tape orders weapon, best available non-free mode, then habits before remaining modes. A half-tape night banks progress; two halves open one tell, not one tell each night.
- **Seven plan mappings, not seven cures.** These map opponent tells to situations. The move or drilled combination must answer the situation. “Tiring” for an opponent's slow start is not a clock trigger, and selecting a line there does not fix your boxer's slow start. The other 13 are unmapped, not unlockable by buying more tape.

## Options worth keeping, moving or cutting

| Option | What changes; when not to offer it |
|---|---|
| Sign now / pass | Spend the contract on this prospect or keep the place and cash. Neither decision needs a complete file. Keep both beside paid reads. |
| Look closer — $120 | Clears hype **or** targets the widest eligible group at half its starting width. Not a choice of habit or even a choice of group. Name the target before charging. |
| Trial — $500 | Four half-stipends at $125. Adds 24 exposure each to body and skills, 8 to under fire **only if open**. Mind does not narrow. Do not advertise four weeks of observation as a list of habits. |
| Test day — $60 | Adds 8 body exposure, 16 below five bouts. Width is curved: from a fresh half-width of 8, 16 exposure gives about 3.7, not zero. Show the resulting range, not “8 off each edge.” |
| Club / outside hard spar — $0 / $160 | Opens under fire; targets spar exposure of 7.25 / 10.875 rather than removing half / three quarters of displayed width. Both quote ×2 injury risk and +15 tiredness; the read helper reports risk, not proof of payment or injury. No reason to repeat at an already met target. |
| Recheck — $40 | Halves stale widening; 21-day gate. Put it with an eligible stale file, not on a fresh walk-in. It cannot discover a habit. |
| Starting focus | Four weeks (28 days) to remove the **first** habit rather than +10 signature mastery now or +5 potential. Preview the actual habit; removing a helpful one is not a reward. Show progress in the week, not a weekly re-purchase. |
| Film / keep the pad place | Learn about the opponent or develop a move. `TellWorth` can bound plan power from candidate situations, mastery and uses; it is not purse value or win probability. Do not quote a positive minimum for a hidden habit if an unmapped one could pay nothing. |
| Corner fix / leave unchanged | Reduce one seen, eligible cost next round, or make no habit instruction. No slow-start fix at the bell, no locked plan buttons for unsupported habits, no weekly percentage drill without a rule behind it. |

## To reach 10/10

1. **Give a read a decision to change.** At reception show the contract, available cash, first visible habit and the uncertainty that matters for the intended bout. Let the manager sign, pass or buy one eligible read and reconsider **before** committing. Separate “estimate narrowed” from “coach saw this.” Record the visible recruitment habit as known if the coach is meant to act on it. If pre-sign discovery is essential, add a new observed-spar event that can write one unseen habit and carry it into the signed notebook; label this new wiring, not a feature of the $60 test. Suppress reads with no possible change.
2. **Put evidence at the offer.** Join only a known habit to a free or observed mode. Show “Your boxer drops the right after the jab. The opponent waits to counter.” Offer accept (purse and risk), decline (forgo purse), and inspect file (no time spent). No warning from a hidden mode; no “safe” badge from an empty file. Give clock and hurt/tired habits separate conditional wording. Test the press/stance, counter/talking and no-free-mode cases as well as one non-match.
3. **Close the loop at the bell.** Link an opponent tell to the plan only if there is a playable answer; otherwise show the observation without a button. For your boxer, preview the eligible next-round fix and report actual occurrences and cost saved, including “did not come up.” Carry that evidence into a rematch. Use roles in copy: recruitment focus result strings currently insert the boxer's first name outside the header.
4. **Test decisions, not clairvoyance.** Pair 150+ seeded worlds over four seasons with the same offers, budgets and bot decision rules; score boxing contracts separately from the mixed club. Compare blind, mandatory read, selective read and free perfect habit knowledge. Hold attribute information fixed in a second comparison that varies only habit visibility. Log changed sign/pass, focus, offer, actual punished exchanges, net season cash and top-ten finishes. Report paired differences and uncertainty; define the proposed 4 and 8 gains as percentage points before testing. A selective manager should sometimes save the fee and sometimes change course; a policy that buys every time is not a depth win.
