What changed and why:
1. The film on you is written twice. SMALL stands today: READBACK1 found no way back in today's rules. FULL waits on READBACK2. Nothing else leans on it.
2. Every day now has a job (pads, tape, sparring, the change, pads, ringside, rest), so most days bring a decision or a landing he waited for.
3. Dead options are gone or greyed with the reason (sparring picks, half nights, class off, the second coach before a third boxer); Home holds one thing.
4. From the owner's outside proposal (PLAYBOOK_GAMBIT_IDEA_S425.md), TAKEN: rules for the boxer's own state as one more kind of line on the
   same plan; a line fires only as well as it is drilled; plan places are earned; a plate when a line fires and the Why naming what fired
   and what failed. NOT taken: the three sliders, the corner focus, sparring clones, rules on the scorecards (the player cannot see them: R29 is held).
5. Playing on now OPENS things, each seen coming: a plan place, the house line, the senior boxer, the regional draw, a named ask.

## 1. The idea in five lines
1. The measured heart of the game is "the answers the plan needs, in the boxer's hands before taper" (2 to 14 wins in 100 each).
2. The week is a race to that, on a fixed rhythm: the tape names what to answer, Reid's two pad evenings build it, sparring shows what is wrong.
3. On Thursday the week can be changed ONCE; there are usually more problems than one change, so he chooses which to carry.
4. On show Saturdays he goes ringside to bank a rival's weakness before any booking, or stays and holds pads himself.
5. What he builds stays and opens choices: rules the boxer runs alone when tired or hurt, a line that becomes the boxer's own, one more place on the plan.

## 2. The week as played
### Camp week (Lucy fights Kovacs in 19 days, taper in 12; Danny lost last Saturday, is Flat, has an offer; six newcomers)
- MONDAY, pads. Home shows the board, filled in by Reid. One question is open: Reid's Friday evening. Lucy's own line (Jab-Jab,
  23 points from a house line), Danny's first evening in three weeks, or the newcomers. He picks, presses Start week.
- TUESDAY, tape. Lucy's film night lands: the Tape Room (in the game today) has one new thing to spot on Kovacs, and the
  answer he picks becomes a plan line. If the boxer cannot do that answer yet, it now needs evenings. No stop if he skips it.
- WEDNESDAY, sparring. The club's sparring night; Reid watches. That night, in Reid's words, one sentence a boxer: "Lucy's
  right hand drops after the jab. Four times." "Danny looked like he did not want to be there."
- THURSDAY, the one change. Three problems are open (the new answer, the habit, the scale 0.4 kg off its line) and the week
  takes one change. Each shows what it buys, what it takes, and what waiting a week costs. The other two wait.
- FRIDAY, pads. Reid's second evening is spent as chosen. Danny's offer runs out today: take, pass, or ask for later.
- SATURDAY, ringside or the gym. Eastside's show is on. Go and watch one bout (a weakness on one of the two, banked before
  any booking), or stay and hold pads himself for whoever went without. SUNDAY, rest: nothing asks.
- NEXT MONDAY the board reads back as three lines at most: one that landed, one that was late, one surprise with its reason.
### Quiet week (no bout booked)
- MONDAY. Reid's two evenings have no bell to serve, so they go to things that stay: a habit (gone for good in four
  evenings), the best line toward a house line, the newcomers, the other boxer. He asks for a fight (level, weeks, a name if banked).
- TUESDAY. The tape from last Saturday's ringside is ready: the banked weakness opens on that rival's file.
- WEDNESDAY. Sparring night; Reid's sentence. Often "nothing new", and then Thursday does not stop.
- THURSDAY. The answer to the ask lands with its reason; if Reid named something, the one change can move Friday to it.
- SATURDAY. Every other week a show: ringside (the boxer he may ask for next) or the gym. Other Saturdays his hands go to
  whoever lost Friday, filled in for him. A player who does nothing gets Reid's week in one click, as today.

## 3. The four joined mechanics
### The plan and Reid's two evenings (R17, R55, R73, R74, R23, R15, R20; v1's pool cut down, joined with the outside proposal)
ONE plan, two kinds of line, the same few places (4 to 6, by the coach, as today):
- An ANSWER to what the opponent does (today's lines). Built on the pads. Worth 2 to 14 wins in 100 against this opponent; next camp may need others.
- A RULE OF THE BOXER'S OWN: when tired, when hurt, in the last round, then one of the corner's eight calls, made without the corner.
  It fires when the corner is silent or not heard (a Flat boxer hears 85%, a Low one 70%, a third instruction in a minute is lost,
  a simmed bout). Drilled on sparring night, where tired and hurt happen; every rule on the plan gets a share, so few rules drill faster.
  Right against an unscouted opponent (it works on anyone) and for a boxer who does not listen; wrong when a known weakness needs the place.
A line fires only as well as it is drilled: seldom when raw, often at usable, nearly always at sharp, always at Signature
(sizes to be measured). So pads and sparring have a job all camp and all year, and an own rule stays from fight to fight, fading if left.
Wednesday is sparring night and Reid watches everyone: no choice there. Monday and Friday his hands go to ONE claim each:
- An answer the plan needs: right when it lands before taper; pointless once it would not.
- A habit of the boxer's own (found by Reid on a Wednesday or by the Why): four evenings, gone for good. Right in a quiet
  week, or in camp when this opponent's file says it gets punished. Wrong when an answer would miss taper for it.
- The best line, toward sharp and a house line: right when no bell is near. Slow, and it stays.
- The other boxer: right at the third week without Reid ("feels second" is shown a week ahead), or when Flat before a booking.
- The newcomers (first 90 days): right after a win brings a batch in; wrong when few joined.
Decisions in order: the tape and the plan decide which answers are needed; Monday he lays the two evenings; Thursday's
change may move Friday; a senior boxer or his own Saturday hands can cover who lost; next week starts from what is now owned.
Greyed with the reason, not offered: a second coach and Hale ("pays from the third boxer"); a senior holding pads inside
his own last three weeks of camp. Cut: half nights, class off, the extra night, the watching eye as a claim.

### The week's rhythm and the one change (R92, R93, R07, R11, R19, R20, R05, R03; v1's board and call, rebuilt)
Each day has its job and the board shows it in plain words. Landings arrive on their day as the clock runs, without a stop.
The week is locked when it starts. Thursday is the one door: ONE change, picked from the problems the week has opened.
The twist card lands on the day it happens (a sore wrist on Tuesday, the heating on Friday), not on Monday.
The changes, and when each is right:
- Move Friday to the new answer: right when Friday plus next Monday still land it before taper. A week later it is too late.
- Move Friday to the habit: right when this opponent punishes it, or the bell is far.
- Mornings for the scale (roadwork, or cut 500): right when the margin is thin and the other two camp lines are green,
  because all three met is the peak camp the game already pays on every stat. Wrong at 74 tiredness: it breaks a line to save one.
- Change one plan line to something already owned: right when the new answer cannot land in time. Weaker, but free.
- No change: right when Reid said "nothing new" and the board is green; then Thursday does not stop at all.
Anticipated: Tuesday's tape, Wednesday's sentence, the three camp lights. Reward: peak camp on fight night.

### The next fight: ringside, the ask, the card (R37, R38, R39, R40, R42, R49 in small; v1's fourth, with a way to prepare)
1. Show Saturdays: he watches ONE bout. The bill is shown with each boxer's place beside his own boxers'. The weakness
   opens on Tuesday. Right when someone he may meet is on the bill; wrong when a boxer of his is short of pads before taper.
2. Monday: ask for a fight. By level and weeks, or BY NAME when he has that boxer's weakness banked or the place to call it.
3. Thursday: the answer with its reason as a list of what is missing; never a dice roll on yes or no.
4. The card, for offers and asks alike, in pictures: the win chance (narrow if banked, wide if not), four dots for answers
   owned by taper, how much the boxer listens (Fired up, Steady, Flat 85%, Low 70%), weeks of camp shared with another boxer.
5. A camp that starts with a banked weakness starts its answer on day one instead of day nine.
When each is right: tune-up for a Flat or Low boxer; step up when the last Why said the plan worked; pass or later when two
camps would share Reid for three weeks or more; short notice only for a boxer whose answers the quiet weeks already built.

### The film on you (R25, R17, R47) - TWO VERSIONS; READBACK1 is in, READBACK2 decides the second
MEASURED (docs/READBACK1_RESULT.md, 400 paired bouts a cell): the plain plan wins 41.3%; a corner that waits for the best
line from round 2 leaves 34.3% (7.0 points, 5 to 6 of them lost in round 2 itself, almost none in round 3). Dropping the
line wins back 0.7, swapping it for another right answer 0.0. In today's rules there is no way back.
SMALL (stands today). No corner waits: that rule stays switched off, because a rule that only takes points away is not worth
building. A line thrown in a public bout is "known" (an eye on it in the Playbook and on the card). A corner that studies
tape covers a known line from the first bell at the measured small cost (0.5 points halved, 2.5 erased). No hold, no swap,
no corner note. Two jobs: a reason over the years to keep adding lines, and a house line is the one line that cannot be
covered. The player never has to act on it.
FULL (CONDITIONAL on READBACK2: two new rules, in the game switched off, being measured now).
- SET-UP: the waited line is not waited for when it follows another planned line that just won an exchange in that round.
- WAIT COST: while the other corner waits for one line, the boxer's other planned lines earn more (x1.25, also tried at x1.5).
KEEPS IT: an arm within 2 points of the plain plan (39.3% or more) and at least 3 above the waited arm (37.3% or more).
KILLS IT: no arm gets there. WRONG, turn it down: any arm above the plain plan (being read must never beat not being read).
What the player does, if kept. The read costs round 2, so the answer has to be at hand in the corner minute after round 1:
the note names the waited line, and the one change is either "only behind the step back" (the set-up: worth having only if
that other line fires, so it must be drilled sharp) or "lean on the rest" (the wait cost: pays more the more of the plan is
owned and firing). Both are bought on Reid's evenings weeks before. Against a corner that already knows the line from an
earlier bout (waited for from round 1, READBACK2's last arm) the set-up is written into the plan before the bell. Offered
only against a corner the card says studies tape. Saving a line for a bigger night appears only once that night is drawn.

### What playing on opens (each earned, each shown coming on Home's "Opens next" and in its own menu)
- HOUSE LINE: a line at Signature (85) is named on the boxer's page. It stops fading, so its evenings come free; the boxer
  can teach it (holds pads for that one line for a clubmate); it cannot be covered. New choice: the freed evening.
- SENIOR: at 5 bouts (the nationals' own gate) a boxer may hold pads for a clubmate on a night without Reid. New choice: a third pair of hands, at the price of the senior's own evening.
- A PLAN PLACE: the plan holds 4 to 6 lines by the coach's skill (today). Reid's count grows with the peak camps he runs
  (a fifth place at two), and a boxer at Ring IQ 65 (the Ring general gate) holds one more. New choice: an answer or an own rule in it.
- THE DRAW: entries close in week 10; the day the regional draw is made the bracket shows names. New choice: which of
  them to watch from ringside, which answers to build for a semi-final that may not come.
- A BANKED WEAKNESS opens the ask by name. KEPT NEWCOMERS open a prospect card now and then, and a prospect a new claim.

## 6. Kill list
1. "With the small film the best idea is gone; what is left is a calendar." What is left is the one chain the game has
   measured (tape, answer, evenings, taper, 2 to 14 in 100), now started before the booking by ringside and forced through
   one change on Thursday against the three camp lines. If the Thursday measurement says the pick is obvious, I have no answer.
2. "Quiet-week claims are set and forget: eight weeks of the same line." Partly true. What breaks it: Reid naming a habit,
   an answer to an ask, a batch of newcomers after a win, a show Saturday every other week, the draw. A club with one boxer,
   no show and nothing asked still gets a one-click week. I let that stand.
3. "Own rules are written once and then the fight plays itself; and every firing number is made up." The answers change
   with every opponent; own rules stay but share the same few places and the same sparring nights, fade if left, and the
   corner still overrides them. Not answered: a plan of six sharp lines may need no corner at all, and all the sizes are
   invented until the four firing measurements are in. The senior and the fifth place are gates I made up on things in the game.
