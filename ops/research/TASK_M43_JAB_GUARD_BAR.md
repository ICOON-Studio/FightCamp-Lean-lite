# M43 - the visual bar for the jab and the live guard (Maria, S231)

FightCamp (Godot 4, MetaHuman bodies, broadcast camera ~3/4 high angle) shows boxers training and fighting. The CEO
rejected our jab stills with these words (verbatim):
- "the jab looks terrible, its the easiest thing to find and implement, i have never seen worse animation of a jab"
- "this looks terrible, it doesnt even look like a human"
- "that is extremely bad looking the fighter is too fat, it looks like human parts stitched together"
- on the guard: "ok but guard needs to be dynamic, not a still guard stance but fighter moves hands closer to head etc"
What he saw: in the profile viewer the jab pose had arms splayed wide, no gloves, the torso bulging; in the fight a hook
wrapped the arm around the fighter's own head.

Give the art bar our next stills must hit (a short checklist an animator can measure on a still):
1. JAB at three moments (chamber / mid-extension / contact): lead shoulder, chin tuck, rear glove position (cheek),
   elbow line, fist rotation, hip and lead foot, head offline - with rough angles.
2. GUARD as a living thing: what moves between punches (hand drift, glove bounce, elbow tuck, breathing, weight shift),
   amplitudes and rhythm, and what it does when the opponent feints.
3. BODY: proportions and silhouette that read as an athlete from this camera (shoulder/waist ratio, neck, no bulge),
   and the three most common retargeting faults that make "parts stitched together" - how to spot each on a still.
4. Three real reference sources (films/broadcast moments or coaching videos, named) that show the target jab and guard.
No new asset purchase.
