# ASK - ideas: training goals the player can FEEL (FightCamp, S370)

FightCamp is a PC boxing-gym management game (Godot, mostly 2D menus + a watched 3D fight). The operator asked: "lets focus
on the training and planning aspect, lets say a fighter doesnt want to compete just learn and get stronger, faster etc how
we make the player feel the goal and engage with it". Built so far (phase 1): the boxer's road is the real England Boxing
skills awards (six grades Preliminary->Platinum, each 8 coached sessions then the coach's assessment: the grade's moves
must be a weapon on the pads), and her engine shows as a bleep-test level (women's norms 6.6 average adult, ~9 national
team) with "in 4 weeks on this plan" that moves when you change her week. Proposal canvas: a goal is a test on a date
(projection bar, Sunday check-in, a test-day moment with a PASSED stamp, a record, an unlock), four grade roads
(Power / Speed / Engine / Skill). Combos are built as move cards with strength / stamina cost.

What we want from you: 5-8 IDEAS, each 2-3 lines, that would make training toward a goal FEEL rewarding and engaging for a
player in a menu-driven management game - especially a boxer who never competes. Name the game or real-world practice
each idea borrows from. Flag which ones you think are the strongest two, and one thing to AVOID (a chore, a trap).
No code. Plain words.
