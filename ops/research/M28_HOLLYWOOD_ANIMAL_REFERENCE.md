Fill with **evidence the sim already has**, presented in Cage Desk voice:

| List class | Filler that earns a place | Reject |

| Books / ledgers | Absent cells stay absent (M8 honesty) | Decorative zeros that pretend at data |

**Rule:** filler is **secondary read** under the primary name/state. If it needs a paragraph, it is content Kalli owns, not filler. If it needs a new sim field, say so — do not invent art-only fluff.

**Survive?** Yes if filler is marks/stamps from existing grammar. No if it becomes a third texture language or continuous animation on every row.
