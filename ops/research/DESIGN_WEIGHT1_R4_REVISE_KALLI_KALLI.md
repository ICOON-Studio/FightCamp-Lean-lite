## 1. The idea in six lines
1. Every fighter walks above the weight they box at. What that costs depends on the bout: a **club show** is matched between two declared weights, a **championship** uses the class limit, and a **pro** bout uses a contract (release 2).
2. At club shows your corner **declares** a weight to the matchmaker. Declare the walk and there's no cut, but fewer opponents are in reach. Declare lower and more or different opponents come into reach, at the price of a cut.
3. When there's weight to lose, three priced levers shape the cut. Which mornings to burn (a stat or the goal); how to answer the fighter's ask to eat (Energy, mood or cash); what runs if the Monday check reads over (Energy, tiredness, or pull out).
4. The scale is 1 hour before the first bell, so only the meal comes back. Energy at the bell, for both corners, sets **the window**: the exchanges where the opponent is tiring and your boxer isn't.
5. The plan's "when the opponent tires" line is priced against that window, counting how often it really fires, so weight and the plan are one budget.
6. What runs for months (the walk, a weight target, the class) lives in the fighter's BODY block.

### 4.3 Who is at fault at the scale (NEW)
1. Each boxer is checked against **their own declared weight + tolerance**.
2. **Outside it:** the coach's automatic re-weigh (inside the session, 30 minutes ESTIMATE, CHECK John for seniors): sweat up to 0.3 kg at −2 Energy a 0.1 kg.
3. **Still outside:** that boxer is at fault. If both are outside, the one further over is at fault.
4. **Both inside their own tolerance, but the pair is outside the gap:** the bout is off with **no fault**: no fee, no loss, next offer 6 days later (CODE path).

### 4.4 The matchmaker call (one per club every 8 weeks; NEW, stands in for AMATEURWEEK1's favours)
Spend it on one of two things:
- **"Ask the other corner for 0.5 kg"** (on an accepted club-show offer, until the Monday of the fight week). Your boxer's target rises by 0.5 kg. The other corner agrees about 1 time in 2 (ESTIMATE). A refusal prints its reason ("their boxer is at the limit") and **does not** spend the call.
- **"Find another name"** (in the weigh-in modal, when the bout falls through for any reason). A boxer within the gap is found about 1 time in 2 (ESTIMATE). If one is found, the bout goes ahead and the call is spent; if not, the call is kept.

**Why it's a choice:** spend it early to make the cut easier, or keep it as insurance at the scale. P21 checks each is used in 30–70 in 100 calls.

### 4.5 The fog
- **The opponent's declared weight is printed on the offer** and is the centre of the forecast.
- **What's uncertain** is whether the opponent comes in inside their own tolerance. It's printed as a band: "the opponent off their weight: 3–10 in 100".
- **Tape narrows the band** (tape 2, free).
- **With no tape** it stays wide and says why: "no tape: first bout".
- **Under 4.3 an opponent's miss never puts the fault on your boxer,** so the fog prices only the chance the bout falls through.

### 4.6 The size edge: out of release 1
- Reale 2017 found regain had no link to winning (41 to 40 bouts).
- At a 1-hour scale the two boxers are within the gap and get only the meal back.
- So P2 accepts about 0, and the edge isn't built in release 1.
- In release 2, P2 runs at the pro scale (30 hours out, bigger regain). The edge (+1 inside and clinch a kg past 1, at most +3) ships only if the bout model shows a clinch gain worth printing.
