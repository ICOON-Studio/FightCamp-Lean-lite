# John: how long does a boxing coach take to really know a fighter?

Kind: research

For a boxing gym management game: research only, real sources with links, say where a number is an estimate.

1. When a new amateur boxer joins a club, what can a coach judge in the first session, the first month, the first year? Which physical qualities can be measured quickly (strength, speed, conditioning, by what tests) and how long does a reliable read take?
2. Which qualities can only be known in competition or hard sparring: chin, composure, heart, fight IQ? How do trainers describe finding these out? Real examples of a boxer who looked one way in the gym and proved another in the ring, both directions (bust and late bloomer).
3. How accurate are coaches and scouts at judging potential in young boxers or comparable sports? Any studies on talent identification accuracy, relative age, or how often early judgments are wrong?
4. What does each staff role really measure in a boxing gym: strength and conditioning coach, physio, cutman, assistant coach?
5. How do trainers talk about these judgments, the words they actually use?

A table per question: value or finding, population, source.
