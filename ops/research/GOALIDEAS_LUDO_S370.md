1. The Audio Pad-Work Cadence - STRONGEST #1. During the coach's assessment, play a 10-second pad-work soundscape: early
   the leather is sluggish, muffled, off-beat; mastered, the crack is crisp and rhythmic. From: real pad-holding (the crack
   of clean timing), Punch Club / Taiko rhythm cues.
2. The Assessment Sparring Partner's Shift in Body Language - STRONGEST #2. She works the assessment rounds with a named
   senior member: at Preliminary the partner catches her shots and talks down; by Gold the partner takes the headguard off,
   breathing hard: "She almost caught me with that 2-slip-2." From: Football Manager (assistant/scout reactions), club
   sparring etiquette.
3. The "Breakthrough" Threshold Ghost. On the bleep/stamina curve a ghost runner marks her last assessment; tweaking the
   4-week plan shows the projected self overtaking it, with a "click" when the gate clears. From: TrackMania ghosts, Zwift.
4. Gym Relics & Wardrobe. Each grade changes her kit (bag gloves -> club wraps, runners -> boots) and her England Boxing
   certificate hangs in the office hallway with her photo. From: Motorsport Manager upgrades, belt ceremonies.
5. Card "Affinity & Snap". A move card at pad-weapon mastery changes: stamina cost -1, border grey -> bronze/silver/gold,
   a snappy sound and a coach's margin note ("Turns the hip over completely now"). From: Slay the Spire / Inscryption.
6. The Diary Event Check-In. Every 4 weeks a short dilemma tied to the goal (colleagues saw her run three flights without
   gasping; or dead legs on pad round 4); the player's call (reassure / push / lighten) buffs the test. From: Long Live the
   Queen, Game Dev Tycoon review beats.
7. The Heavy Bag Sound & Swing Weight. In the gym view the bag shows her strength/form: early it thumps dull and swings
   off-axis; later it stays tight on the chain and rocks with deep thuds. From: Punch Club, Fight Night Round 4 mini-games.

AVOID: a dice roll on the final assessment ("78% chance to pass") that fails her after 8 weeks of planning. If she hit the
bleep level and logged the coached rounds, she earned it. Pressure comes from mastery grades (Pass vs Distinction, clean
under fatigue), never from a random fail.
