# Claude on Claude v John (Claude wins by KO in round 2)

(1) WHY I WON: R1 E1, Pull Counter on his opening Lead Hook - a Pressure Boxer at Mid leads with a hook, so the read was
cheap, and the counter (6 with my reflexes) ROCKED him; rocked, he held no legal Counter or Defend and lost the next
exchange doing nothing. R2 E2-E3: Read Tendency showed his whole hand - at Long his only legal cards were Jab, Cut the Cage
and Read, and my Step-In Jab (9 speed at Long) beat all three. The KO was Slip & Cross on the Cross he had to throw at Mid.

(2) WHAT I COULD READ: his fighter type told me his opener; once I saw his hand, range legality told me everything. What I
could not read: whether he would shell up at Long (R2 E1 cost me a whiffed Check Hook).

(3) FUN: the counters are the best part - Pull Counter on a hook, Slip & Cross on a cross: real boxing reads, and they
pay. The Lead and the combo make winning an exchange feel like taking over. A human opponent (an agent) is far more fun
than a script: he did a pressure fighter's things, and punishing them was the game.

(4) TOO STRONG: Read Tendency - his whole hand + range legality = I knew his options exactly. The Out-Fighter's speed
(reach tie-break, +1 at Long) won every Attack v Attack: John landed NOTHING all bout (0 damage). NEVER WANTED: Circle Out
at cost 0 was fine, but Second Wind (block nothing, Long only) is a free gift when the other man does not attack.

(5) FUN 7, DEPTH 7, CLARITY 6, VARIETY 6

(6) TO REACH 10/10:
1. ROCKED needs an out: a rocked man with no Counter or Defend in hand must still be able to cover up (an always-legal
   "Cover Up": blocks the head, no stamina back). Today rocked + the wrong hand = a free exchange = a snowball.
2. Read Tendency shows TWO random cards of his hand, not all of it.
3. Speed: the Out-Fighter keeps +1 at Long but loses the reach tie-break (ties trade); the Pressure Boxer gets +1 speed at
   Close. Each style should win the speed race in its own range.
4. Both stepping in together: stop at Mid, not Close (a Step-In Jab that is jammed at Close does half).
5. A card's legal ranges shown as icons on the card and in the hand list, greyed when not legal - half my thinking was
   "what can he even play here".
6. Round score: show the damage + counters count during the round, not only at the bell.
