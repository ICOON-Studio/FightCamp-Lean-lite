# John on Claude v John (Claude wins by KO in round 2)

**(1) Why I lost.** It was a knockout in round 2, and two beats decided it. In R1 E1 I opened with a Lead Hook straight into Claude's Pull Counter. That cost me 6 head damage and left me rocked. Then in R1 E2 I had no legal card, so I lost a whole exchange and gave Claude the Lead for free. Round 1 went 10-8 against me, at 17 damage to 0. In R2 E5 I was on 6 head and threw a Cross, which Claude read with Slip & Cross for a 6-damage knockout. I never landed one punch on Claude's head.

**(2) What I could read and what I couldn't.** Claude threw straight punches whenever it held the Lead: Cross in R1 E5 and E6, and the Step-In Jab twice. It also kept its stamina up with resets and reads. What I couldn't read was when Claude would switch to a counter, as in R1 E1 and R2 E5. Claude also used Read Tendency in R2 E2 to see my hand, so it knew what I was holding while I guessed blind.

**(3) Was another agent more fun?** Yes, because Claude counter-read me twice, and I don't think a script would. But once I was rocked and behind on speed, the bout felt decided by R1 E2, which isn't much fun for the loser.

**(4) Too strong, and never wanted.** The plan that felt too strong was the speed gap. The Lead's +2 speed on top of the Out-Fighter's speed meant every straight punch beat my cards (10 v 7, 8 v 6, 7 v 5). Attack against attack was never a real choice for me. The switch I never wanted was Head Movement Feint. Its upside needs a counter, but Claude attacked into it in R1 E5. My Read Tendency in R2 E3 got punished the same way.

**(6) TO REACH 10/10.**
1. **Never deal a rocked fighter a dead hand (R1 E2).** A rocked fighter should always hold one legal Counter or Defend, or get a free basic "Cover Up" Defend. Losing a whole exchange to a deal is the worst moment in the bout.
2. **Make the Lead's speed bonus smaller (R1 E3, R1 E6, R2 E4).** Give +1 speed instead of +2, or let it fall away after one exchange. Right now the fighter who wins first wins every speed race after that, and the Pressure Boxer has no way back in.
3. **Make the hurt fighter's knockout threat show on the board (R2 E5).** At 6 head, any counter ends the fight. The screen should warn "one clean counter ends it," and give the Pressure Boxer's Iron Chin a once-a-fight survive at 1 head. That way the comeback has a turn left to happen, and the trait means something in a knockout fight.
