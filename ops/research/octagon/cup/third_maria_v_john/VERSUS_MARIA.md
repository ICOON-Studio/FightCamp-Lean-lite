# Maria on Maria v John (John wins by DECISION 29-28)

**(1) Why I lost: John won on points, 29-28.**
Two beats decided it. In round 2, exchange 3, I put up the High Shell against a fighter who had just landed a Liver Shot, and the second Liver Shot went under it. That was 10 body damage in two exchanges, it cut my maximum stamina to 10, and round 2 went 9-10. In round 3, exchange 3, with John on 4 head health, I threw the lead hook to finish it. John had kept a Pull Counter, so the hook missed, I took 5 and was rocked, and I had 0 stamina left. Round 3 went to John by two damage, and that was the fight.

**(2) What I could read, and what I couldn't.**
Reading its hand in round 1, exchange 4, worked. I saw it held only lead hooks for Mid, and my cross with the Lead beat them twice, 8 to 6. I also saw that Cut the Cage was its only way in from Long. What I couldn't read was the switch to body shots in round 2, or that it had drawn a Pull Counter since my read. My information was two rounds old by the time it mattered.

**(3) Was another agent more fun than a script?**
Yes. John made a real read at the end. It knew I needed one hook to finish, held back the counter for it, and won the fight with it. A script wouldn't wait like that. The low point was the stretch of passes when both of us were out of stamina, which felt like waiting rather than boxing.

**(4) Too strong, and never wanted.**
Too strong: the Pressure Boxer's Liver Shot. It does 5, cuts maximum stamina twice over, and goes under both shells, so the Out-Fighter's only defence is Circle Out. Never wanted: the High Shell against a Pressure Boxer. It loses to the card that fighter plays most.

**(5) Scores**
- FUN 7: close all the way, and decided by a real read.
- DEPTH 7: reading hands, keeping counters back and saving stamina were all real choices.
- CLARITY 6: it was hard to tell that body damage was cutting my stamina twice until it had already happened.
- VARIETY 6: a better mix than the last bout, though the passes at 0 stamina repeated.

**(6) To reach 10/10**
1. **Count body damage once (round 2, exchange 3).** The Liver Shot's own stamina cut and the every-5-body-damage cut both landed on the same hit. Pick one, or show the total on the card before I pick.
2. **Let a read expire visibly (round 3, exchange 3).** Mark which cards the opponent has drawn since my last read, as a count with an icon rather than colour alone, so a stale read is my mistake and not a hidden change.
3. **Give a fighter on 0 stamina one free basic card (round 3, exchanges 4 and 5).** A basic block or a step that costs nothing would turn those dead exchanges into choices.
