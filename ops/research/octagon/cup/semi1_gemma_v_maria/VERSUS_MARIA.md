# Maria on Gemma v Maria (Gemma wins by DECISION 29-28)

**(1) Why I lost: Gemma won on points, 29-28.**
Two beats decided it. In round 2, exchange 4, I threw a lead hook into Gemma's cross while it held the Lead. Its plus 2 speed won by half a point, its combo hook added another 4, and I was rocked for 8 head damage. In round 2, exchange 5, rocked, I could only play my Pull Counter, and its Pawing Jab baited it for 5 stamina. That round went 10-8 against me. Then round 3 was six passes, because my hand had no card that works at Long.

**(2) What I could read, and what I couldn't.**
Gemma opened with Slip and Cross, and my hook walked right through it. Reading its hand in round 1, exchange 2, told me it had no Pull Counter, so I knew my Liver Shot was safe. I also saw the Lead swing: it fought on the Lead and backed out to Long whenever it lost it. What I couldn't read was the speed math. I didn't know the Lead's plus 2 would beat my hook by half a point, and I couldn't tell its Pawing Jab was a feint until after it baited my counter.

**(3) Was another agent more fun than a script?**
For two rounds, yes. It mixed counters, feints and shells, and it punished my Second Wind straight away. In round 3, no. Neither of us could score, so the result was set by the round 2 cards, and a script would have felt the same.

**(4) Too strong, and never wanted.**
Too strong: holding the Lead with an Out-Fighter. The Lead's plus 2 speed on top of its fast straight punches beats a Pressure Boxer's hooks every time they meet, and a combo turns that into a rocked fighter. Never wanted: Second Wind. Blocking nothing while it lands a free step-in jab and takes the Lead cost me far more than 6 stamina is worth.

**(5) Scores**
- FUN 5: round 1 was tense, round 3 was six passes.
- DEPTH 6: the triangle, reading a hand, and the Lead give real choices until the cards run dry.
- CLARITY 6: the speeds and half points were hidden until the exchange was over.
- VARIETY 4: one exchange in three of the bout had no legal card for me.

**(6) To reach 10/10**
1. **Never deal a dead hand (round 3, exchanges 1 to 6).** Every fighter should always hold a way to close or change the range, such as a basic step in that costs 1 stamina and is always available. Six forced passes handed the fight to whoever was ahead.
2. **Show speed totals before I pick (round 2, exchange 4).** Put each card's final speed on it, including the Lead's bonus, range and traits, in numbers and words rather than colour alone. Losing 8.5 to 8 should be a read I got wrong, not a hidden half point.
3. **Rocked shouldn't mean a forced guess (round 2, exchange 5).** A rocked fighter with only one legal card should always be allowed a basic cover that blocks half. Otherwise a single feint can bait an automatic counter.
