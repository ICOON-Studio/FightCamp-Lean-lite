# Gemma on Gemma v Maria (Gemma wins by DECISION 29-28)

I won because I successfully neutralized Maria’s pressure in the middle rounds. The two deciding beats were R2 E4, where I landed a massive combo to Rock her, and R2 E6, where I forced her into the cage to secure the final points.

I could read her defensive tendencies through the Fight IQ card, allowing me to anticipate her shells, but I could not predict her specific counter-timing in E5, which led to a stamina drain.

Playing an AI agent was significantly more fun than a scripted opponent because her "decisions" forced me to adapt my strategy mid-round, especially when she pushed me toward the fence.

The combo mechanic felt too strong as it allowed for overwhelming damage in one turn, but the "Rocked" status was a switch I never wanted as it felt like a forced loss of initiative.
