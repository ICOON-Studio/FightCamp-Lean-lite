# Kalli on Claude v Kalli (Claude wins by DECISION 30-27)

**1. Why I lost.** I lost 30-27, and all of the margin came from R1, which went 10-7. The two deciding beats were R1 E2 and R1 E6. At R1 E2 I threw a cross with the Lead straight into his straight-punch counter, took 6 and got rocked. At R1 E6 I chose a slow body hook while he held the Lead, and his cross beat me to it for another 6. R2 was level, and R3 he guarded six times while I scored nothing, so I never got that deficit back.

**2. What I could read.** Once he was ahead in R3, he guarded every exchange, and my fakes beat that twice (R3 E2 and R3 E4). His tank was low all fight, so he punched rarely. What I couldn't read was the counter at R1 E2. I'd just countered his hook, so I expected him to swing again, and nothing told me a counter was coming. He also read two of my cards at R1 E5 and I saw nothing of his.

**3. Agent against script.** The early rounds were more fun because he adapted, baiting me into the counter and then punishing my fake. The last round was dull. He found the stall and stuck to it, which an agent does better than a script, but it isn't fun to play against.

**4. Too strong, never wanted.** Guarding with a lead was too strong again. The new rule charges a stall 1 stamina, but only on a reset, so a guard against my attack costs him nothing and still strips my Lead. He guarded five times in a row in R3 at no cost. The switch I never wanted was the Check Left Hook: it only answers a man stepping in, and from the fence he never steps in, so two copies sat dead in my hand.

**5. Scores**
FUN 6: one bad read in round one decided the fight, and round three was dead.
DEPTH 6: the fakes and the cage push worked, but they never turned into damage.
CLARITY 7: the stall rule shows in the log, but I couldn't see that a blocked jab still scores nothing.
VARIETY 4: he guarded six times in R3, and I threw the same jab five times.

**6. To reach 10/10**
1. Make a pinned man's guard leak. At cage space 0, a block should let 1 through, or the attacker's +1 at the fence should count against a guard (R3 E5 and E6). Right now pushing him onto the fence earns nothing.
2. Let a fake that beats a guard score for the judges, maybe 1 point, so that winning exchanges counts (R3 E2 and R3 E4). I won three exchanges in R3 and the round still scored 0-0.
3. Cap a rocking counter to stop one read swinging a whole round, for example by giving the Pull Counter at most 4 against a straight punch (R1 E2). A single 6-point counter with a rock attached decided a three-round fight.
