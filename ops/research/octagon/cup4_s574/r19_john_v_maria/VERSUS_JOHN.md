# John on John v Maria (John wins by DECISION 30-27)

**(1) Why I won, 30-27.** I took all three rounds, but two beats decided it.
- **R3 E4:** my Pull Counter Cross read his Lead Left Hook and landed 5 to the head. With the clean-counter bonus that put the last round out of reach at 9-0.
- **R1 E4:** my speed-8 Range Jab beat his speed-6 Liver Hook. That showed me the Truck's slow hands can't win a straight race, and it shaped the rest of the fight.

**(2) What I could read, and what I couldn't.** I could read that Maria uses the Stance Guard whenever she's behind: R1 E5, R2 E1, R2 E5, R3 E2 and R3 E5. That's five guards, and each one wiped out a committed punch of mine. I could also read that she swings big at Mid (R3 E4, R3 E6), which is exactly what a counter wants.

What I couldn't read was her feints. Cut the Cage at R1 E1, Read Tendencies at R1 E3 and the Shoulder Twitch at R1 E6 came with no warning. The Twitch broke my Circle Out and gave her a judge point for free.

**(3) Was another agent more fun?** Yes. She punished my Circle Out with a Feint right when I was protecting a lead, then used the tell it gave her. A script wouldn't time that. Her guard habit was also something I could learn and exploit, and finding that is the fun.

**(4) Too strong, and never wanted.**
- **Too strong:** the free-or-cheap Stance Guard for the man behind. She played it five times, and it never cost her more than 1 stamina while it ate my whole attack.
- **Never wanted:** Deep Breath & Pace (R2 E2). It blocks nothing, so against a man who jabs every exchange it's just a free 2 for him.

**(6) TO REACH 10/10**
1. **The Stance Guard repeat should cost +2, not +1, and should leak 1 on a blocked punch after the second use in a round (R2 E1, R2 E5, R3 E2, R3 E5).** Four of my punches went to nothing. The Pressure side ended up guarding, not pressing, which is backwards for "The Truck".
2. **Show the counter damage on the card exactly as it will land, with the clean-counter score next to it (R3 E4).** The card said 4 and the log said 5, and my round score jumped by 7. That's a big swing I could only guess at. The game should never hide what a beat scores.
3. **A Defend that meets a Defend or a whiffed Counter shouldn't charge the leader for "stalling" when he played a Counter (R3 E5).** My Slip & Counter was a real read, not a stall, yet I paid 1 stamina at 3 left. Charge the stall tax only for Defend against Defend, so counter-punchers aren't punished for reading.
