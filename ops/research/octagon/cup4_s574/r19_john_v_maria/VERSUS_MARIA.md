# Maria on John v Maria (John wins by DECISION 30-27)

**(1) Why I lost.** I lost 30-27, and I was never ahead after the first beat. In R1 E1, I faked my way in with Cut the Cage and his poke caught it for 2. That handed him the Lead and set the tone. In R3 E4, I threw a heavy hook into his Pull counter, it missed, and he landed 5. That took the last round outright and left my head at 6. Between those, his speed won every jab race: R1 E4, R2 E4 and R3 E1.

**(2) What I could read and what I couldn't.** I could read his habits. He jabs whenever he has the Lead, and he guards when he's low on stamina. Seeing his hand in R1 E3 told me he held a head block and a step-in counter, and my body hook in R1 E4 was the right read on paper, but he jabbed and beat it to the spot. I couldn't read when he'd switch from guarding to countering. R3 E3 was a guard and R3 E4 was a Pull counter, and nothing told them apart. I only saw his hand once all fight, because there are no cuts in this version.

**(3) Agent or script?** Playing an agent was more fun, but less than last bout. He played the counter-puncher properly. He made me come to him, then countered the one big punch I finally threw. A script wouldn't have saved that counter for the moment my hook came.

**(4) Too strong, and never wanted.** The plan that felt too strong was the always-there poke for a fast fighter. Truck's poke can never win a tie, and his often lands first, so he scores 2 a time with no risk. It was 7 of his 18 cards. The switch I never wanted was the Overhand at Long. At speed 4 it lost the race in R2 E4 before the cards were even shown.

**(6) To reach 10/10, most important first:**
1. Give slow power fighters a way to win a tie. Let a heavy punch land through a jab of 2 or less, so both punches land. In R2 E4 my Overhand lost to a 2-damage poke, and a heavy hitter should take one jab to land one bomb.
2. Make the counter-puncher's counter readable. A counter could show its colour after two Defends in a row, or a Pull could whiff more often against a man whose stamina is full. R3 E4 was a pure coin flip that decided the round, and I had no tell.
3. Bring back one way to read the other man's hand in this version, such as a weaker cut or a tell after a block. The only time I saw his cards was R1 E3. After that, every choice in R2 and R3 was a guess, and the fight was mostly a jab race.
