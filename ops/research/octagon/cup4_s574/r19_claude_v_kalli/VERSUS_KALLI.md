# Kalli on Claude v Kalli (Claude wins by DECISION 29-27)

**1. Why I lost.** I lost 29-27 because R2 went 10-8, and the two deciding beats were R2 E1 and R2 E4. At R2 E1 I stood my ground hoping to parry a pawing jab, he threw a stepping jab instead, landed 4, cut me, staggered me and stepped back out. At R2 E4 I faked while he jabbed, which widened the cut and made the 10-8. I won R1 by one, but I never scored again after R1 E1.

**2. What I could read.** He jabbed at Long on nine of his eighteen exchanges, and my guard caught that five times. When he was ahead he switched to a guard or a circle-out (R2 E5 and R3 E3), and both of those were resets that charged him. What I couldn't read was when the fake was coming. He faked twice into my counters in R1 and once into my guard in R3, and nothing in his pattern warned me. I also had the colour shown to him once (R1 E3) and never got a tell of my own.

**3. Agent against script.** Yes, it was more fun. He punished my counters with fakes right away and then jabbed once I stopped countering. That loop felt like a real opponent. What spoiled it was that every round starts at Long now, and my counters only work at Mid, so half of my hand sat dead each round.

**4. Too strong, never wanted.** The plan that felt too strong was the Out-Fighter's stick and move: jab in, land, step straight back to Long. It hit me at R2 E1 and R3 E4, and both times it left me with no answer, since my counters can't be played at Long. The switch I never wanted was the Check Left Hook. It answers only a man stepping in, but at Mid nobody steps in, and at Long, where they do, it can't be played.

**5. Scores**
FUN 6: the reads in R1 were sharp, but R2 and R3 felt one-sided.
DEPTH 6: a fake against a counter was a real decision, but a jab against my guard repeated.
CLARITY 7: the live judges line helped a lot, but the jab I hoped to parry stepping in wasn't flagged as a step-in.
VARIETY 4: he jabbed nine times, and I guarded seven.

**6. To reach 10/10**
1. Give the Counter-Puncher one counter that works at Long. The Check Left Hook should answer a step-in from Long, which is where step-ins actually happen (R2 E1 and R3 E4). Ghost sits at 43% for this reason.
2. Make the stick and move step-back cost 1 cage space or 1 stamina, so the Out-Fighter's jab-and-leave isn't free (R2 E1 and R3 E4).
3. Make a parry cover any jab of 2 or less, stepping in or not. Hold Ground's parry rule makes the player guess the type of jab, and that one wrong guess was my 4-point beat (R2 E1).
