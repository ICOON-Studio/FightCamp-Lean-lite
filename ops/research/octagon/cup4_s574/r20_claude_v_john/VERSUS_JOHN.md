# John on Claude v John (Claude wins by DECISION 29-28)

**1. Why I lost, 29-28.** Round 2 lost it, by 10-8. There were two deciding beats:
- **R2 E5:** my Liver Hook ran into his Pull Counter Cross. It cost me 5 to the head plus a stagger, and gave him the Lead and the round.
- **R1 E3 and R2 E2:** Cut the Cage twice into his Range Jab. Each time I bled for 2, and the cut showed him my hand for the rest of the fight.

Round 3 proved I could have won. The Lead Left Hook into his feint, then two blocked shots, got me a 10-9 but not the 10-8 I needed.

**2. What I could read.** He loops through counter, feint, guard at Mid. When I stepped in with a feint, he jabbed. After a feint he went to a guard (R3 E5 and E6). Both hooks into his feints came from reading that cycle.

What I couldn't read was which counter he'd pick. The cut showed him my Liver Hook, so he knew exactly when to throw Pull Counter Cross. The cut works as a permanent spy. In R3 E3 we both played Pull Counter, which was pure guessing.

**3. Agent or script.** The agent was more fun, clearly. He changed patterns between rounds (feint, then guard twice in a row to close out R3), and a script wouldn't have shut the door that calmly. The tension came from his adapting, not from the dice.

**4. Too strong and never wanted.**
- **Too strong:** a points lead plus Stance Guard at the end. R3 E5 and E6 cost him nothing, and the 10-8 I needed simply couldn't happen against a man happy to guard.
- **Never wanted:** Hold Ground. It costs the Lead, gives nothing back, and was never better than a free guard.

**6. TO REACH 10/10**
1. **Make the man ahead pay for guarding late (R3 E5–E6).** A guard by the man ahead in the last two exchanges of a round should score +1 to the attacker, or the judges dock 1 for running. Then a trailing fighter always has a way to a 10-8.
2. **Limit what the cut shows (R2 E5).** It should show one card once per round, not every exchange. Otherwise one cut decides the fight, and a reveal that gives away a counter is too much information.
3. **Give Cut the Cage a reason to exist against a jab (R1 E3 and R2 E2).** Make it beat a jab that doesn't step in, or make it immune to Range Jab. Right now a pressure fighter's signature step-in loses to the cheapest card in the game.
