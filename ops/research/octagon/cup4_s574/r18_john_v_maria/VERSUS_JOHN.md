# John on John v Maria (Maria wins by DECISION 30-29)

**(1) Why I lost, 30-29.** Rounds 1 and 2 were level, so round 3 decided it.
- **R3 E5:** I threw a Shoulder Twitch Feint into her Straight Cross, and the cut had shown me that Cross. That cost 4 points, the round and the fight.
- **R1 E4:** my Range Jab ran into her Pull Counter Cross for 5. It left me staggered and erased my good Cross from E3.

**(2) What I could read, and what I couldn't.** Her cut showed me the Cross at R2 E6, R3 E3 and R3 E5, and I still fed it a Feint. That's the clearest read I had all fight. Her Range Jab habit at Long was readable too, and it's how I won R2 E5 and R3 E3. What I couldn't read was when she'd switch to a Defend. She guarded at R2 E1, E2 and R3 E4, and each guard ate a punch I'd committed and stripped my Lead. The cut shows one card, but not which one she'll play.

**(3) Was another agent more fun?** Yes. Maria changed rhythm on purpose. She guarded twice, then jabbed my Read Tendencies, then used Read Tendencies on my Circle Out. A scripted opponent wouldn't play Read Tendencies against a guard it had just seen. It felt like being studied back.

**(4) Too strong, and never wanted.**
- **Too strong:** the cheap Range Jab at Long. Both of us paw for 1 or 2 at almost no risk, it beats every Feint, and Hold Ground only parries it at Long. Half of round 3 was jab trading.
- **Never wanted:** Elbows Down. Every punch of hers that mattered was a head shot, so a body-only guard sat dead in my hand all fight.

**(5) Scores**

**(6) TO REACH 10/10**
1. **The cut should show the card she's committed to, not a random card from her hand (R3 E5).** I saw "cross3" and still guessed wrong, because it might not be her play. Show it once she's sealed her card, at the cost of one less card drawn. Then a cut pays off for the man who opened it, and the read becomes real.
2. **A blocked attack should still score something for the attacker, or the guard should cost more after two blocks in a row (R2 E1, R2 E2, R3 E4).** Three guards wiped out three committed punches and cost her nothing extra. Give the attacker 1 judge point for clean pressure into a guard. That rewards the aggressor in a decision fight, the way real judges do.
3. **The Range Jab should cost 1 more after two jabs in a row, and lose the reach tiebreak to a step-in punch (R2 E5, E6, R3 E3, E4).** Four of the last eight exchanges were paw-jab trades. Make the paw a setup tool, not the main source of points, so the Out-Fighter has to step in with the Step-In Jab and Double Jab Feint to score.
