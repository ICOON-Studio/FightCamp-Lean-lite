# Maria on John v Maria (Maria wins by DECISION 30-29)

**(1) Why I won.** I won 30-29 on two beats. In R1 E4, he jabbed while holding the Lead at Mid and my Pull counter made it miss and landed 5. That evened a round he'd have taken otherwise. In R3 E5, I was 2 behind with two exchanges left, he faked, and my straight punch caught it for 4 and a stagger. That flipped the only round anyone won. The rest was mostly blocks cancelling blocks.

**(2) What I could read and what I couldn't.** I could read his habits. He jabs whenever he has the Lead, steps in at Long to open a round, and resets or guards right after he's hit. Seeing his hand through his cut paid off once. In R2 E4 it showed his step-out move, and my fake broke it. I couldn't read when he'd fake. He faked in R1 E1, R2 E3, R3 E1 and R3 E5 with no pattern I could spot, and the R3 E1 fake broke my guard and pushed me to the fence. His R3 E6 guard was a pure guess on my part, and I guessed wrong.

**(3) Agent or script?** Playing an agent was more fun. He changed things up after getting punished. After my counter in R1 E4 he stopped jabbing into me at Mid and backed out instead. A script would have kept feeding me jabs. It felt like a real argument.

**(4) Too strong, and never wanted.** The plan that felt too strong was the Out-Fighter's speed. Same-speed jabs go to him on reach, and with the Lead he gets +2 or +3, so R1 E6 and R2 E5 were lost before I picked a card. The switch I never wanted was Deep Breath. It blocks nothing against a man who jabs every exchange.

**(6) To reach 10/10, most important first:**
1. Make a tied jab a real trade, or cap his Lead bonus at +1. In R2 E5 we both jabbed and only he landed, on reach. That made poking back pointless for the slower fighter.
2. Make the cost of repeating Stance Guard grow more gently, maybe +1 per use. By R3 E5 it cost 4. Blocking was my only safe answer to a man jabbing at my cut, and the price pushed me into gambles that were close to coin flips.
3. Give a Pressure fighter a way to Close from Mid that a guard doesn't simply erase. Both my body hooks, in R1 E2 and R1 E5, hit a block or a step-out. My uppercuts sat dead in my hand all fight because we never reached Close. The "body puncher" never got to dig to the body once.
