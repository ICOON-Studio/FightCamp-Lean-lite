# John on Kalli v John (Kalli wins by DECISION 30-28)

**1. Why I lost, 30-28.** I lost because a Pressure fighter never got to fight at Mid. There were two deciding beats:
- **R1 E1:** my opening Overhand stepped straight into his Check Left Hook. That was 3 to the head plus the Lead, and he kept the round from there.
- **R2 E3 and E6:** Read Tendencies broke my Hold Ground twice. That was +2 with the judges and it walked me onto the fence, and it lost round 2. My parry habit became his target.

**2. What I could read.** I could read his Range Jab habit at Long: Hold Ground parried it twice. I could read that he'd retreat at Mid, with Circle Out, Clinch or a guard after every step-in. And when he got rattled, he went to a feint.

What I couldn't read was when he'd swap the jab for Read Tendencies against my Hold Ground. The cut showed him my hand, so he always knew. Hiding his Step-In Jab inside a run of Range Jabs (R3 E4) caught me for 4.

**3. Agent or script.** The agent was more fun, clearly. Kalli punished my habit, not my cards: he saw me parry twice and switched to a feint both times. A script wouldn't adapt like that, and the R1 E2–E3 tell-and-clinch sequence was a real duel.

**4. Too strong and never wanted.**
- **Too strong:** the Out-Fighter's Long-range loop. He jabs, steps back out with stick and move, and every round goes back to Long. In three rounds I played six exchanges at Mid. A Pressure fighter has almost no cards at Long, so I drew fresh hands of dead hooks.
- **Never wanted:** Deep Breath & Pace. It sat in my hand all fight, and against a jabber it's just a free 1 or 2 for him.
