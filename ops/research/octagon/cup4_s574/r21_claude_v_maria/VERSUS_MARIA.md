# Maria on Claude v Maria (Claude wins by DECISION 28-27)

**1. Why I lost, 27-28.** Two beats decided it. In R1 E4 I threw a cross into a man who had just baited two of my reads. He held the Lead, so his cross was faster, beat mine, and chained into a hook for 10. That rocked me and gave him a 10-7 round, the whole margin plus two. Before that, R1 E2 lost me a point and the Lead: I circled out at Mid and his free Read beat my guard. After that I won rounds 2 and 3, but 10-9 twice can't make up a 10-7.

**2. What I could read and what I couldn't.** Late in the fight he was easy to read. Once he got ahead he lived behind the Stance Guard: six guards in R3, and my fakes broke it twice. Seeing his overhand and Pull Counter in R2 E2 helped, because it told me not to throw hooks into the counter. I couldn't read his round 1 fakes. Read Tendencies, then Cut the Cage looked exactly like a pressure fighter about to step in, and my check hook bit on it. I also didn't see the speed swing from his Lead in R1 E4 until the punch landed.

**3. Agent or script?** The agent was more fun. Claude played like a real boxer: he baited my counters early, banked the big combo, then shelled up to protect the lead. A script wouldn't change gears with the scorecard. It also stung more, because his plan was clear once I saw the whole fight.

**4. Too strong and never wanted.** The plan that felt too strong was getting a 10-7 lead and then turtling behind the Stance Guard. In R3 he blocked five times and threw one jab, and that held the win. His guard even stopped my body shot in R3 E5, though I thought a body punch goes under a guard. The switch I never wanted was circling out at Mid, because a fake beats it and pushes me to the fence. It cost me in R1 E2.

**6. To reach 10/10**
1. Cap a combo's damage, or let the man being hit react once mid-combo. In R1 E4 one beat swung 10 points and settled the bout before round 2. A combo should win a round, not the fight.
2. Make the Stance Guard say what it covers, and let body shots get under it like the shells. R3 E5 blocked a Liver Hook, and I didn't expect that from reading the help. A clear zone rule makes turtling beatable by reading it.
3. Raise the Stance Guard cost faster when it's used again and again in a round, or score a fake that beats it as 2. In R3 Claude guarded five times. My two fakes scored only 1 each, while his guard saved him far more than that. The round went my way, but turtling still protected the lead.
