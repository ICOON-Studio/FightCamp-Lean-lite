# Maria on Kalli v Maria (Maria wins by DECISION 29-28)

**(1) Why I won.** I won the decision 29-28, and round 2 won it for me. Two beats decided it:
- **R2 E2:** Kalli threw an Overhand Right from Long, and my Check Hook caught it stepping in. That landed 5, rocked Kalli and handed me the Lead.
- **R2 E6:** Kalli had blocked my head hook with Tight High Guard the exchange before and went back to the same guard. My Ripping Liver Hook went under it for 5 and cut Kalli's stamina cap by 2. That made round 2 a 10-8 and decided the fight.

**(2) What I could read and what I couldn't.** Kalli's habit was easy to read: when low on stamina it hid behind the high guard, which it played six times in the bout. Its stamina number also told me when it couldn't afford to swing. What I couldn't read was R1 E4. I expected a hook, but Kalli threw a speed-9 Snap Jab that beat my body hook, and nothing warned me. Read Tendencies only showed me 2 of 5 cards, so the dangerous card was never one of them.

**(3) Agent or script?** Another agent was more fun. Kalli mirrored me, with counter against counter and read against read, and then set a trap with Cut the Cage in R3 E5. That felt like a real opponent. The downside is that the mirrors produced dead beats, like R1 E1, where both counters did nothing.

**(4) Too strong, and never wanted.** The plan that felt too strong was the free guard spam. Tight High Guard costs 0, gains 2 stamina and strips the Lead, so whoever falls behind can turtle almost for free. The switch I never wanted was Hold Ground. Blocking nothing for 1 stamina felt worse than doing nothing at all.

**(6) TO REACH 10/10**
1. **Give guards a cost when they're repeated (R2 E5, R2 E6, R3 E3, R3 E6).** For example, cost 1 on the second guard in a row, or a body shot getting +1 against a guard just played. Right now the guard is the safe answer far too often.
2. **Make stamina running dry a real decision (R3 E4 to E6).** By the end, both of us were on 0 stamina and could only reset. The fight ended in a 10-10 round of circling. Give back a little stamina every exchange so the final round has punches in it.
3. **Make counter against counter worth something (R1 E1).** Both counters at equal speed did nothing, which wasted an exchange. Let the faster one or the better reader take the Lead, or let a feint-free standoff cost both fighters 1 stamina so sitting back has a price.
