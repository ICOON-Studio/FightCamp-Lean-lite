# Kalli on Kalli v Maria (Maria wins by DECISION 29-28)

**1. Why I lost (29-28).** Round 2 lost the fight. In R2 E2 I threw an Overhand Right at Long into her Check Hook. It took 5 to the head, rocked me and handed her the Lead. Then in R2 E6 I put up a high guard against a Ripping Liver Hook. It went under the guard for 5 to the body and cost me 2 maximum stamina. That round went 0 v 13 and 8-10 on the cards, and it's the whole margin. After that I spent round 3 with no stamina: I played 0, 2, 1 and 0 stamina across the last exchanges and couldn't throw anything that scores.

**2. What I could read, and what I couldn't.** I could read her style. She steps out when I come in: she played Circle Out three times against my liver hook. She covers up against a cross, and she plays a feint when we're at Long. What I couldn't read was when she'd go for the counter and when for the feint. At Long, Check Hook and Read Tendencies look the same until they resolve. Her body shot under my guard in R2 E6 was a guess I couldn't make, because her last cards don't show where she's aiming.

**3. Another agent or a script?** The agent was more fun. Maria adapted: she stopped attacking once my stamina dropped and simply backed out of trouble. A script wouldn't have dragged me into a stamina trap. The cost is speed: every exchange waits on a reply.

**4. Too strong, and never wanted.** Too strong: Circle Out & Reset. It halves my hook, resets the Lead and costs the Out-Fighter nothing, so she banks a round just by leaving. Never wanted: Hold Ground. It gives up the Lead and blocks nothing, for 1 stamina. I'd always take the guard instead.

**5. Scores**
- FUN 7: the stamina squeeze at the end was tense, but it was also dead time.
- DEPTH 7: the triangle and the Lead give real reads. Body or head is the best choice in the game.
- CLARITY 6: the beat log is clear, but "half through the circle-out" and fresh hands appearing mid-round surprised me.
- VARIETY 6: about four cards decide most exchanges, and I'd seen most of them by round 1.

**6. To reach 10/10**
1. **Make the last exchanges count (R3 E3 to E6).** Both of us sat on 0 to 2 stamina and a 10-10 round was decided by guards. Give each round a stamina floor so the last two exchanges are always playable, or let the corner refill some stamina mid-round.
2. **Make stepping out cost something (R1 E3, R3 E4, R3 E6).** Circle Out ended three of my attacks for free. Make a fence that's closing take cage space, or let a pressure fighter who's cut the cage turn a step-out into a free hit.
3. **Give the counter a tell at Long (R2 E2).** I threw the Overhand blind into a Check Hook that I couldn't tell from a feint. Show one honest hint each exchange, such as her stance (stepping in or holding), so a big punch is a read and not a coin flip.
