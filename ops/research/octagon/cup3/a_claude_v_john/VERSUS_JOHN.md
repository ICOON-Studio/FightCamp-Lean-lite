# John on Claude v John (Claude wins by KO in round 3)

**1. Why I lost.** Two beats decided it. In R1 E1, my opening Snap Jab walked into Claude's Pull Counter Cross: 7 to the head, I was rocked, and I spent the rest of the round on Cover Up and lost it 10-8. In R3 E3, my High Guard blocked his jab, but the combo carried on through the block. The cross and then the hook finisher landed 12, and I went from 6 head to a KO. The setup came at R3 E2, where my stamina was so low that I had no legal card at Long. That free turn let him cut the cage, take the Lead and pin me near the fence.

**2. What I could read.** Claude opens at Mid with a counter, as he did in every bout. When he's rocked he covers up. After a feint he throws a straight, which my Pull Counter read in R2 E3. What I couldn't read was when he'd switch from feinting to defending. His guard in R2 E5 cost me the Lead, and his feint in R2 E6 fell into my liver hook by luck, not by a read. In R3 I saw "guard, snap" in his hand. I couldn't see the cross and hook behind the snap, so I covered against one punch and took three.

**3. Agent against script.** Yes, it was more fun. Claude changed after being punished: after I countered him in R2 he stopped leading with straights. A script wouldn't have tried a Read and then Cut the Cage to exploit my stamina. The tension was real.

**4. Too strong, never wanted.** Too strong: a combo that keeps going after its first card is blocked. My Defend did almost nothing, because the first card is a cheap jab and the finisher still gets +2. Never wanted: Read Tendencies in round 3 at low stamina. It cost the stamina that left me card-less next turn, and seeing 2 of his 7 cards didn't change my choice.

**6. TO REACH 10/10**
1. **A blocked first card ends the combo, or the rest is halved** (R3 E3). A guard that stops the jab should stop the chain, otherwise Defend is a trap against anyone holding the Lead. If chains must stay alive, the defender's +2 stamina should also give a free reset to Long.
2. **Always give one zero-cost legal card at every range** (R3 E2). A "Hold Ground" option would do it: 0 cost, keeps cage space, gives up the Lead. Being forced to pass is not a decision, and that pass handed Claude the fence and the KO setup.
3. **Make an opening counter cost something if nothing comes** (R1 E1). Right now the Out-Fighter can sit on Pull Counter at Mid for free, and the Pressure Boxer's only safe answer is a feint he may not hold. Either give the Pressure Boxer a free Cut the Cage in every opening hand, or make a counter that answers nothing cost 2 stamina, like Slip & Cross does. Then the first exchange is a real guess, not a tax on the man who has to come forward.
