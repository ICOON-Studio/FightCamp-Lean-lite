1. CORE COMBAT LOOP & VICTORY CONDITIONS
Match Format: 3 Rounds (5 Rounds for Boss Bouts) of 6 Simultaneous Exchanges each.
Win Conditions: KO/TKO (Head Damage reaches Max Head HP 10 + Chin), Submission (Stamina reduced to 0 by a
Submission card), or Judges' Decision (10-Point Must system based on total damage, knockdowns, and control).
Hand & Signature Stance Slot: Draw 4 cards each exchange. Every fighter also has SYS-01 (Signature Stance Guard)
permanently available outside their hand so you never brick on an all-Attack draw.
Desperation Surge: At 0 Stamina, you may play any Defend or Active Escape card for 0 STM by taking 2 Self-Body
Damage.

v2.7 Director's Cut Balance Rules:
Non-Stacking Modifier Cap: Only the single highest Speed bonus and single highest Speed penalty apply per exchange.
Counter-Initiative Bonus: Counter (Blue) cards gain +1 Speed when played against an opponent holding the Initiative
Token.
Referee Stand-Up: If 2 consecutive ground exchanges produce zero positional advancement and <2 total damage/drain,
the referee resets both fighters to Mid Range in Neutral.
Takedown Guard Break & Positional Gates: Takedowns bypass standard High Guards (requiring Sprawl or Anti-
Takedown Counters). Submissions from Clinch/Closed Guard suffer -2 Drain unless the defender is below 50% Stamina.
3. STAND-UP RANGE, GRAPPLING TRACK & LOCALIZED INJURIES
Stand-Up Range Track: Long Range (Kicks, Lunging Jabs) ↔ Mid Range (Boxing, Reactive Takedowns) ↔ Close Range
(Elbows, Knees, Clinch Entries). [Pre-Move In] shifts Range before strikes; [Post-Move Out] shifts after.
Grappling Position Track: Clinch → Closed Guard → Half Guard / Side Control → Mount / Back Control. Bottom fighters
must play [Active Escape] or [Active Sweep] cards to escape.
Localized Injuries: Head DMG triggers Rocked (stunned for 1 exchange) and Eye Cuts (reveals 1 hand card). Body DMG
reduces Max Stamina Cap. Leg DMG applies -1 Speed and increases movement costs.
