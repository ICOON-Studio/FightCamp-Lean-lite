# Kalli on Claude v Kalli (Claude wins by KO in round 3)

**1. Why I lost, KO in round 3**
I lost on two beats:
- **R1 E1:** I opened with the Lead Hook into his Pull Counter. That was 6 to the head, I was rocked, and it handed him a 10-8 round I never got back.
- **R3 E3:** I threw the Liver Shot (speed 5) into his Cross (speed 6). He landed 4, took the Lead, and left me on 3 head health with no guard in my hand. The hook that finished me in R3 E4 followed from that.

**2. What I could read, and what I couldn't**
I could read him at Long: the Step-In Jab, the paw, then Circle Out once he was hurt or tired. My Read Tendency baited his Slip & Cross (R1 E5), and my Cross beat his Cross to the punch, which set up the combo for 8 and rocked him (R2 E3).
I could not read when the Check Hook was coming. It answered my Cut the Cage in R3 E1, after it had sat unused in R2 E1. I also couldn't see that he would switch from countering to attacking just when I picked the feint (R3 E4).

**3. Agent against scripted**
Another agent was more fun. Claude saw my hand through Read Tendency and set a trap for Cut the Cage two exchanges later. Being out-thought by a real opponent beats losing to a script.

**4. Too strong, and never wanted**
Too strong: being rocked. One 6-damage counter in the first exchange took away my attacks, cost the Lead and swung the round 10-8. In practice it decided the bout.
Never wanted: Second Wind (R2 E5). It was the only card I could play at Long, and blocking nothing gave him the Lead for free.

**5. Scores**
- FUN: 7. The combo in R2 E3 felt great, and the swings were real.
- DEPTH: 7. The triangle plus the counters that only answer certain attacks give real reads.
- CLARITY: 5. Step In and Cover Up are "always in your hand" by the rules but went missing in most exchanges, and ranges hid three cards in five.
- VARIETY: 6. Most exchanges came down to one legal card at Long (R1 E5, R2 E2, R2 E5, R3 E1, R3 E2).

**6. To reach 10/10**
1. **Show Step In and Cover Up in every hand, as the V2 rules promise (R3 E4).** On 3 head health with his Lead, Cover Up would have halved the hook and kept me in the bout. Its absence turned a defence into a coin flip on a feint.
2. **Stop a first-exchange counter from rocking (R1 E1).** Rocking should need 5 or more head damage from counters too, or never trigger in a round's first exchange. One guess shouldn't decide two exchanges and a 10-8 round.
3. **Give every fighter a real card at Long (R2 E5, R3 E1).** At Long a Pressure Boxer should hold at least two playable choices, for example a cheap step in that a Check Hook doesn't hard-counter, or a guard. Otherwise he reads my one card and punishes it, as the Check Hook did.

VERDICT END
