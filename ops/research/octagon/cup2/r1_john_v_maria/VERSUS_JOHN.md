# John on John v Maria (John wins by KO in round 3)

**(1) Why I won.** I won by knockout in round 3, and two beats decided it. In R1 E3 my Lead Hook (7) beat Maria's Cross (6) for 6 to the head and rocked Maria. That made round 1 16-0 and took Maria from 17 to 11. In R2 E1 and again in R2 E5, Check Hook read Maria's Cut the Cage. That was 8 head damage from counters, and it left Maria on 1 head, so any punch in round 3 would end it.

**(2) What I could read and what I couldn't.** The big read was that a Pressure Boxer at Long has to step in. Maria walked into Check Hook twice with Cut the Cage. Read Tendency in R2 E3 showed me the Step-In Jab. What I couldn't read were the counter-for-counter beats. In R1 E2, Maria's Slip & Cross met my Pull Counter, and in R2 E4 we both played Slip & Cross. Those two cost me 6 stamina and did nothing.

**(3) Was another agent more fun?** Partly. Maria adapted by shelling and taking Second Wind once the Check Hook started landing, and a script wouldn't. But once Maria was on 1 head after round 2, round 3 was a formality.

**(4) Too strong, and never wanted.** The plan that felt too strong was Check Hook at Long against a Pressure Boxer. It cost 1 stamina, landed 4 and reset to Long, so the Pressure Boxer had no safe way in. The switch I never wanted was Circle Out & Reset. It was dead at Long, where every round starts, so I discarded it three times.

**(6) TO REACH 10/10.**
1. **Give the Pressure Boxer a safe way in (R2 E1, R2 E5).** Step In and Cut the Cage should beat a Check Hook when played after a feint. Or the Pressure Boxer could get a "Shell and Walk" card that steps in with a head guard. Right now the only way in is a guess that the Out-Fighter isn't holding the counter.
2. **Make counter against counter mean something (R1 E2, R2 E4).** When two counters meet, the faster one should take the Lead and the slower should lose 1 stamina. That turns a blank beat into a reading contest.
3. **Keep round 3 alive after a lopsided round 2 (R3 E1).** At 1 head, Maria lost to the first punch of round 3. Give back some head health between rounds, say 4, or make the judges' cards the only way to win unless someone is knocked down twice. Then the last round can still swing.
