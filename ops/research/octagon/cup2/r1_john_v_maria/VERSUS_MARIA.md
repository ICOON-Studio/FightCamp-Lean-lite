# Maria on John v Maria (John wins by KO in round 3)

**(1) Why I lost.** John won by KO in round 3 and didn't take a single point of damage all fight. Two beats decided it:
- **R1 E3:** John's Lead Hook at speed 7 beat my Cross at speed 6. That landed 6 and rocked me, and it showed the Lead's speed bonus winning every equal trade.
- **R2 E5:** I walked in with Cut the Cage a second time, straight into the same Check Hook that had already hit me in R2 E1. I took 4 more and dropped to 1 head. I read John's 1 stamina as "it can't afford the counter," but the Check Hook only needed 1. That was my mistake.

**(2) What I could read and what I couldn't.** I could see that John loved the Check Hook, which it played three times in round 2. I could see its stamina running dry, and Read Tendency in R2 E6 showed me its feint and paw. What I couldn't see was the speed totals until after each beat. In R3 E1 my jab lost 8.5 to 8, and the half point from the Lead decided the KO without me ever seeing it coming. I also didn't know which two cards Read Tendency would show, so the Step-In Jab John was holding stayed hidden.

**(3) Agent or script?** Another agent was more fun. John changed its plan around my habits: it stacked Check Hooks once I kept walking in, then took a Second Wind exactly when I couldn't punish it. A script wouldn't have adjusted like that. The flip side is that a 16-0 shutout felt like it was losing to the rules more than to John.

**(4) Too strong, and never wanted.** The plan that felt too strong was the Out-Fighter sitting at Long behind the Check Hook. Every Pressure Boxer way into range, whether Cut the Cage or Step-In Jab, counts as stepping in, so one counter shuts down the whole style. The switch I never wanted was Second Wind. Blocking nothing while sitting on low head felt like gambling with the fight just to keep breathing.
