# John on Claude v John (John wins by KO in round 3)

**(1) Why I won.** Two beats decided it. In R1 E2 my Lead Left Hook went over his Elbows Down for 8 and rocked him. That took him from 15 head to 7 in two exchanges, and his glass chin never got the room back. Then in R3 E2 he held the Lead and threw a Lead Left Hook, and my Slip & Cross read it for 6 and ended the fight. Truck's power did more than his speed could cover: every trade (R1 E1, R2 E3, R2 E6) went my way on damage, even with the jab taking 2 off.

**(2) What I could read, and what I couldn't.** I could read his range jab at Long. He threw it three times in a row (R1 E4, R1 E6, R2 E1), so Hold Ground parried it for free. I could also read his head-hunting: every punch he threw went upstairs, which made Slip & Cross a safe bet when the finish was there. What I couldn't read was when he'd step in. The R3 E1 Step-In Jab came right after I'd held twice, which is the same pattern Kalli punished, and it cost me 4 clean. His Pull Counter in R2 E5 was a guess I didn't see coming either, though it whiffed.

**(3) Agent versus script.** It was more fun. Claude switched plans between rounds: jab and reset in R1, step-in pressure in R3. He also punished my holding habit, which a script with fixed weights wouldn't do. The fun was in his adapting, not in the cards themselves.

**(4) Too strong and never wanted.** The plan that felt too strong was Hold Ground at Long against an Out-Fighter. It parries his main tool for nothing, so his best plan is to stop jabbing, and that empties the long range. The switch I never wanted was Feel Him Out. It sat dead in my hand again, because it does nothing a free Hold Ground doesn't already do better.

**(6) TO REACH 10/10:**
1. **Make Hold Ground at Long cost something (R1 E4, R2 E1).** After two holds in a row, give the opponent +1 speed on his next step-in, or make the third hold give -1 stamina. That way parrying jabs forever loses, and the long game becomes a real read instead of a free wall.
2. **Give the glass chin a way to survive one rock (R1 E2 into R1 E3).** One 8-point hook decided the whole bout. Let a fighter with the Lead spend 2 stamina to turn "rocked" into "hurt": -1 speed next exchange, but every card still playable. Speed fighters could then come back from one mistake, and that keeps R2 and R3 alive.
3. **Give Feel Him Out a real use (dead in every bout).** Let it reveal the opponent's last card type for next exchange and draw 1 extra card. A read card then pays off in a game built on reads, and it stops being a discard every hand.
