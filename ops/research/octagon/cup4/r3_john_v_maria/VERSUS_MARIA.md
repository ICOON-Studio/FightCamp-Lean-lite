# Maria on John v Maria (John wins by KO in round 3)

**(1) Why I lost.** John won by KO in round 3, and two beats decided it.
- **R1 E2:** his second Ripping Liver Hook went under my high guard for 8 body damage. My guard hand had nothing that stopped a body shot, so I spent the rest of the fight at 3 body health and couldn't step in.
- **R3 E2:** his Overhand Right went straight through my Range Jab, with mine landing first, for 7 and the KO. I'd seen the overhand in his hand when I read his cards in R2 E3 and still had no card that answered it at Long. The Check Hook would have, but I'd discarded it in R1 E3.

**(2) What I could and couldn't read.** He led with body hooks twice, so his pattern was clear, but I had nothing in hand that beat a body shot. His Pull Counter in R1 E3 I didn't read at all. I threw a cross to win the speed race, and he sat on a counter instead, which is a clever switch for a slow power puncher. My Pull Counter in R2 E2 read his jab perfectly. At Long he copied my pawing jab and lost every speed race, then switched to the overhand twice, and both times it hurt.

**(3) Agent or script?** The agent was more fun. He mixed body hooks, a counter, a jab, Cut the Cage and the overhand, and he saved the overhand for the moment it would finish me. A script would have kept throwing the hook into the guard.

**(4) Too strong and never wanted.** The overhand from Long felt too strong. Under the new trade rule, the jab still lands first but can't stop it, so at Long the only real answer is the Check Hook, and the hand rarely gives you one. The switch I never wanted was Cut the Cage. Walking my 3 body health into Mid was never a real option.

**(6) TO REACH 10/10**
1. **Make the Range Jab stop or slow a man stepping in (R2 E1, R3 E2).** Twice a speed 8 or 9 jab landed first and he still walked through it for 7. A jab that lands against a step-in punch should end the step or halve the punch, otherwise the outside fighter has no way to keep distance.
2. **Give every high guard hand a body answer (R1 E1, R1 E2).** Two body hooks took 14 body health and 4 max stamina before I could do anything. A cheap elbow-down defend, or a guard that covers the body, would turn round 1 into a decision instead of a lost round.
3. **Warn when a discard throws away the only answer to a card you've seen (R1 E3, R2 E3).** I saw the overhand in his hand but had thrown away the Check Hook two rounds earlier. A small hint like "this card answers his overhand" would turn the read into a plan.
