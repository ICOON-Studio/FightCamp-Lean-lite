# John on John v Maria (John wins by KO in round 3)

**1. Why I won.** Two beats decided it. In R1 E2, Maria put up the High Guard against my second Liver Hook. It went under for 8, took his body from 11 to 3 and cut his maximum stamina twice in the opening two exchanges. Every exchange after that, his body was one hook from a stoppage, so he had to fight me at Long. In R2 E1 and again in R3 E2, he threw the Range Jab while I threw the Overhand Right. Under the v4.2 rule his faster jab couldn't cancel my power punch, so I took 2 and landed 7 each time. The second one was the KO.

**2. What I could read.** Maria jabbed at Long nearly every exchange: six Range Jabs, plus a Snap Jab. Once I saw that, the overhand trade was a sure thing. What I couldn't read: the Pull Counter in R2 E2. I threw a 1-cost Snap Jab at a rocked man with 2 head left, and his counter caught it for 7 and nearly turned the fight. A rocked man who still counters is the danger, and I should have covered that with a feint.

**3. Agent against script.** Yes, it was more fun. Maria guarded at the right moment, just against the wrong zone, and saved the counter for the exchange I got greedy. A script wouldn't wait like that. But his loop of jabs at Long was easy to read, so the last round played itself.

**4. Too strong, never wanted.** Too strong: a power punch that steps in against a jab that can't stop it. The Overhand Right trades 7 for 2 every time, and with the jab rule there's no answer at Long except a counter. Never wanted: my own Range Jab. At speed 7 against his speed 9, it lost every one of four races (R1 E5, R2 E4, R3 E1, and the tie in R1 E5). For a slow Pressure man, it's a card that only pays the other guy.

**6. TO REACH 10/10**
1. **Give a power punch through a jab a real cost** (R2 E1, R3 E2). A jab that lands first should either push the power punch back to Long or cut its damage to half, not just 2 less. Right now an Out-Fighter's main weapon loses outright to the slowest punch in the game.
2. **Show which zone a Defend covers, and make the guard choice a real read** (R1 E2). The High Guard against a body hook cost Maria 8 and the fight. Give each style a body-cover card (Elbows Down) in the opening hand, so guarding is a head-or-body guess instead of a trap.
3. **Make the Range Jab a real option for every fighter** (R1 E5, R2 E4, R3 E1). Mine lost four speed races at 7 against 9. Give it a fixed speed 8 for everyone, or let a Pressure man's version push in on a trade. Then Long becomes a choice, not a tax the slow fighter pays.
