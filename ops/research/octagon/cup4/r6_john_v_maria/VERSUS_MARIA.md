# Maria on John v Maria (DRAW 28-28)

**(1) Why it was a draw.** I threw away a points win in round 3. Two beats decided it. In R3 E5 I put up a high guard against a fighter who'd already shown me a Ripping Liver Hook, and he dug 6 under it. That stripped my stamina and handed him the Lead. In R3 E6, his Lead made his jab 11 against my 10, and the round went 10-8 to him, which levelled the cards at 28-28. One body-guard choice would have won the bout. I had Elbows Down in hand and picked the wrong zone.

One more thing I couldn't follow: the log scores rounds 1 and 2 as 9-10 against me while my exchange screens showed me leading those rounds. If those totals are from his side, the summary line should say so.

**(2) What I could read and what I couldn't.** His counters in round 1 were easy to read. A fresh counter-puncher opens with a counter, so two cheap feints cost him 9 stamina by E3, and he was gasping all bout. His Hold Ground at Long was readable too, and I punished it twice with the Step-In Jab. What I couldn't read was his switch to the body when I was low. He'd covered high in his own low-stamina spots, so I assumed he'd poke at the head. He didn't, and nothing on screen hinted at it.

**(3) Was another agent more fun than a script?** Yes. He mirrored me. When I spent R2 E5 waiting on a Check Hook, he beat it with my own trick, Cut the Cage, and pinned me on the fence. A script doesn't learn your bait inside one bout.

**(4) Too strong, and never wanted.** Feints against a counter-puncher in the opening felt too strong. Two 1-stamina cards cost him 9 stamina, and he never recovered. I never wanted the Ripping Liver Hook, since it's slow and costs 3, so I discarded it twice, yet the same card drew the bout for him.

**(6) TO REACH 10/10**
1. **Make the judges readable (all three rounds).** Use one consistent scoreboard: always show my score first, and show the running tally after every exchange. Then a rounds-1-and-2 lead doesn't quietly become 18-20 on the cards.
2. **Make the wrong guard a visible gamble (R3 E5).** Show which zone a defend covers on the card's NOW line, and flag when the opponent has thrown a body shot this round. Picking high against a known body puncher should feel like a choice, not a slip.
3. **Soften the feint tax on counter cards (R1 E1 and E3).** A baited counter shouldn't cost 5 stamina in one exchange. Cap the loss at 3, so a counter-puncher who guesses wrong in the opener still has a fight left. In this bout he was spent by R1 E3 and spent most of the night waiting.
