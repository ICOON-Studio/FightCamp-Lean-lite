# John on John v Maria (DRAW 28-28)

**1. Why it was a draw.** The two beats that decided it were in R1. In R1 E1 I opened with Pull Counter and her Shoulder Roll baited it. That cost me 5 stamina in one card, and I spent the whole bout at 1 to 6 stamina, holding when I wanted to punch. In R3 E5 she sat behind a head guard on low stamina, and my liver hook went under it for 6. That turned R3 into a 10-8 and pulled me level. Round 2 was the round I gave away: three Hold Grounds while she scored 3 with one step-in jab.

**2. What I could read.** She guarded the head whenever she was low on stamina (R1 E5, R2 E6, R3 E5), and she opened rounds 2 and 3 with Read Tendencies, which my R3 E1 range jab punished. What I couldn't read was when she'd feint and when she'd counter. She baited my counter twice in R1 (E1 and E3), then mirrored my pull counter in R2 E3 and was quicker. A counter-puncher against a counter-puncher becomes a coin flip on which side of the triangle the other picks.

**3. Agent vs scripted.** It was more fun than a script. She adapted: when I sat on Hold Ground at Long, she stepped in with a jab that walks past it. A script would have kept pawing into my parry. But the mirror match made long stretches feel like waiting rather than fighting.

**4. Too strong, and never wanted.** The plan that felt too strong was the feint-first opener against a counter-puncher. It costs her 1 stamina and me 5, and that tax lasted all fight. The switch I never wanted was Hold Ground at Long. The step-in jab beats it outright, so it's only a stamina tool, and I played it four times.

**5. Scores**
- FUN: 6
- DEPTH: 6
- CLARITY: 8
- VARIETY: 5

**6. To reach 10/10**
1. **Cap the cost of a baited counter (R1 E1).** Losing 5 stamina to one wrong guess in the first exchange decided the next 17. Make a baited counter cost its own price plus 1, never more, so a misread stings without crippling.
2. **Let Hold Ground at Long answer a step-in too (R2 E2, R3 E3).** Hold Ground should give a free half-block against anything that steps in, or turn the jabber's step into a step back. Then waiting at Long is a real choice and not a free point for the other man.
3. **Break counter-v-counter ties with a third option (R2 E3).** Two pull counters meeting just hands the faster one the Lead. Have them clinch instead: both lose 1 stamina, range goes to Close, and nobody gets the Lead. That gives mirror matches a third range and a reason to bring Close cards.
