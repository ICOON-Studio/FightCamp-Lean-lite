# John on John v Kalli (John wins by DECISION 28-27)

**1. Why I won.** Two beats decided it. In R1 E2, Kalli threw a second Snap Jab after the first one won. My Slip & Cross read it for 7, he was rocked, and I took the Lead. Then in R1 E3, my Shoulder Roll & Feint baited the counter he reached for while rocked, which cost him 5 stamina. My hook in R1 E4 rocked him again, and that round went 10-8 to me, 14 v 5. In R2 E3, my Ripping Liver Hook caught his Cut the Cage for 6 to the body and cut his maximum stamina. That banked round 2 and left him too drained to finish me in round 3.

**2. What I could read.** Kalli repeats whatever just worked, which is how the second jab ran into my slip. When rocked, he reaches for a counter, so a fake makes it whiff. In round 3 he jabbed at Long six times in a row. What I couldn't read: the head guard in R2 E2, which made my counter whiff for 5 stamina, and the body hook trade in R2 E6.

**3. Agent against script.** Mostly yes. The first two rounds were real reading, with a counter for a repeat, a fake for a counter, and a body hook for a guard. Round 3 was not fun. Both of us saw that Long rewards one jab and nothing else, and it turned into six identical exchanges.

**4. Too strong, never wanted.** Too strong: the Out-Fighter's Range Jab at Long. Reach breaks the speed tie, so he won every jab race (R1 E6, R3 E1) and then jabbed five times into my Hold Ground. Nothing in my hand could stop it. Never wanted: Hold Ground. I played it five times in a row only because every other choice lost too, and I won by coasting, not by fighting.

**6. TO REACH 10/10**
1. **Give the hurt side an answer at Long** (R3 E2 to E6). Make the Check Hook or a new Slip the Jab card catch the Range Jab, or let Hold Ground at Long step you in to Mid for free. Then five free 2-point jabs can't decide a round.
2. **Stop a round that does nothing from scoring 10-8** (R3). Five jabs into a man standing still got a 10-8, and a 1-point margin of victory came from a round with no exchanges. Give a 10-8 only for a rocked fighter or a clean counter, not chip damage, so coasting isn't the best play.
3. **Make a counter that whiffs cost less when it reads the style right** (R2 E2). My Pull Counter cost 5 against a guard, and that put me at 2 stamina for R2 E4. A whiffed counter should cost only its card price, with +2 kept only for counters that misread the punch type. That keeps counter-punching a read instead of a gamble.
