# Maria on Kalli v Maria (Maria wins by DECISION 28-27)

**(1) Why I won.** I won on points, 28-27, and the whole margin came from round 1. Two beats decided it. In R1 E1 my cross landed on his faked shoulder roll for 5. In R1 E2 my pull counter read his liver hook, landed 7 and rocked him. That 18-0 round was the cushion. After it I lost rounds 2 and 3 by 10-9 each and survived by blocking. The near-miss was R1 E6, where my hook into his high guard was the punch that could have ended the fight, and he simply blocked it.

**(2) What I could read and what I couldn't.** Kalli's habits were easy to read. When rocked or low on health, he guards. At Long he paws or steps in with a jab. Once he held the Lead, he kept throwing quick jabs, and my high guard blocked each one. What I couldn't read was when he'd feint instead of punch. In R2 E2 his step-in feint baited my pull counter, cost me 2 stamina and pushed me to the fence. In R2 E6 he guarded, and my counter whiffed again. Even after my read in R2 E4 showed the counter in his hand, I couldn't tell whether he'd use it.

**(3) Another agent versus a script.** It was more fun. Kalli changed plans between rounds: at Long in round 3 he went from pawing to reading my hand to stepping in, and that broke my wait-and-parry plan. A script wouldn't have punished my standing still in R3 E4 with a stepping jab.

**(4) Too strong, and a switch I never wanted.** Too strong: the free high guard at Mid. It blocks every jab, strips the Lead and gives back 2 stamina. Rounds 2 and 3 were me blocking, at no cost, for the win. The switch I never wanted was stepping in from Long. My stepping jab cost 2 stamina I rarely had, so I never chose to close the distance.

**(6) TO REACH 10/10**
1. **Make blocking cost something.** In R3 E5 and E6, two free guards locked up a decision while I threw no punches. A guard should give no stamina back after the first one in a row, or keep giving the Lead to the opponent.
2. **Stop stamina from deciding every round.** I hit 0 stamina twice (R2 E3 and R3 E2) and could only stand still. Two whiffed counters shouldn't empty a counter-puncher. Either cap a whiff at 1 stamina, or let the between-rounds recovery scale with stamina used.
3. **Show what a read tells me about his next card.** In R2 E4 the read showed the counter in his hand, but in R2 E6 he guarded and I lost 2 stamina guessing. A read should hint at intent too, for example a marked card he plans to play, or his most likely card type next exchange.
