# Kalli on Kalli v Maria (Maria wins by DECISION 28-27)

**1. Why I lost, 28-27 on points.** Round 1 decided it. In R1 E2 my body hook ran into her Pull Counter, which took 7 off my head and rocked me. She won that round 18 to 0, which scored 10-7. After that I won rounds 2 and 3, but only 10-9 each, so I could never catch up. The second deciding beat was R3 E5 and E6. With the Lead and speed 10 to 11, I threw two fast jabs into her Tight High Guard. Even with two snap jabs and a cross chained behind them, nothing got through. I needed 12 points that round for a 10-7 and finished with 6.

**2. What I could read.** Her stamina told the whole story. On 0 to 4 she waited (Hold Ground three times in R3), guarded, or threw counters she couldn't afford. Cut the Cage baited her counter cleanly in R2 E2. What I couldn't read was the guard timing. She went to the high block exactly when I had the Lead at Mid (R2 E5, R3 E5, R3 E6), and nothing on screen told me guard was coming, apart from one card my R3 read showed.

**3. Agent against script.** Yes, more fun. She adapted, stopped attacking once she was drained and banked her round-1 lead. A script would have kept swinging into my hook. But that also showed the problem: once she was ahead, playing safe beat playing well.

**4. Too strong, and never wanted.** Too strong: winning one big round early and then guarding at no cost. Tight High Guard is free, gives +2 stamina and strips the Lead, so it blanked my best weapon three times. The switch I never wanted was Circle Out & Reset. At Close my whole hand was dead, so circling out was forced, not chosen.

**5. Scores**
- FUN: 6. The comeback felt real, but it ended on two blocked jabs.
- DEPTH: 6. The bait and the read worked, but guard answered everything at Mid.
- CLARITY: 8. The log was clear, and the 10-7 rule was the only thing I had to work out.
- VARIETY: 5. Rounds 2 and 3 were jab, guard, jab, guard.

**6. TO REACH 10/10**
1. Stop the free guard from being an answer to everything (R3 E5 and E6). A blocked jab with the Lead should still score a point, or a second guard in a row should cost stamina and stop giving the +2.
2. Make a lopsided round hurt the leader less, or reward a comeback (R1 against R2 and R3). 18-0 cost me 3 points, while 4-0 and 6-0 each earned only 1. Score 10-8 for winning by 6 or more, so steady pressure can close the gap.
3. Give every fighter one playable card at every range (R2 E3 and E4). Being at Close with nothing to throw made me waste two exchanges guarding and circling while she was empty on stamina.
