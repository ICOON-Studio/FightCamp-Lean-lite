**1. Why I lost, 29-28 on points.** First deciding beat: R1 E5. Both of us stepped in with a jab, his landed at 9.5 to my 9, and he took round 1. Second deciding beat: the round 3 score itself. By the log I landed 3, 3 and 5 for 11, and he landed 2, 3 and 3 for 8. The summary printed "8 v 11" and still gave the round to him 10-9. If that's right, round 3 was 10-9 to me and the bout was a 28-28 draw. I think the two names got swapped in the round tally. That needs checking before anyone trusts this result.

**2. What I could read.** Claude lived on the long-range paw: seven times in rounds 2 and 3. Hold Ground parried it every time I called it, four of four. What I couldn't read was when he'd switch. R2 E6 and R3 E4 he stood his ground instead, and in R3 E1 he played Read Tendencies. Nothing showed those coming.

**3. Agent against script.** More fun, yes. He found my weakness (I'm slow at long range) and pawed there all fight, which a script wouldn't plan. But round 2 was nine points of nothing: paw, hold, paw, hold.

**4. Too strong, and never wanted.** Too strong: the long-range paw for an out-fighter. It's 1 stamina, wins every trade at 8.5 against my 8, and gives him the Lead. A pressure fighter has no good answer except waiting. The switch I never wanted was Read Tendencies in R2 E3. It walked straight into a paw for 2, because at long range a feint is just a free target.
