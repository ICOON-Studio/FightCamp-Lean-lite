# Kalli on Claude v Kalli (Claude wins by KO in round 3)

**1. Why I lost (KO in R3 E3).** Two beats decided it. In R2 E2 I countered expecting a head punch, he went to the body, and my counter whiffed while his liver hook landed for 7. That left me on 6 body health, and one more body trade put me on 1. In R3 E1 his overhand right stepped in, my long-range jab went out of range at Mid, and the full 8 landed and rocked me. From then on any punch to either target finished me, and I could only guard one.

**2. What I could read, and what I couldn't.** I could read his straight punch twice. In R1 E5 he guarded high, my body hook went under it, and his maximum stamina dropped by 2. In R2 E4 my straight punch won the speed tie on reach against his hook. What I couldn't read were his switches between targets. He guarded his body first (R1 E1), then crossed to my head, hooked my body twice, then went back to my head with the overhand and the finishing cross. He always hit the zone I wasn't covering.

**3. Another agent or a script?** More fun. Claude used Read Tendencies in R1 E3 to see my hand and then hit me with a faster body hook. In round 3 he faked once to get speed and finished me while my body was on 1. A script wouldn't have pressed the body and then switched back to the head for the kill.

**4. Too strong, and never wanted.** Too strong: the overhand right stepping in from long range. It hit me twice (R2 E1, R3 E1), and my long-range jab, supposedly its answer, only halved it once and missed entirely the second time. Never wanted: Elbows Down at the end, a guard that leaves the head open when the head is the other way to finish me.

**5. Scores**
- FUN 7: real reads and a scramble when both of us were low on stamina.
- DEPTH 7: choosing a target between head and body mattered every exchange.
- CLARITY 5: the long-range jab "lands first" but is "out of range at mid," which reads as a contradiction.
- VARIETY 6: both fighters went to the body, with different timing.

**6. To reach 10/10**
1. **Make the long-range jab work against a step-in (R3 E1).** If it's faster than a man stepping in, it should land before the range changes and halve the punch every time, not miss because the range moved. Right now the counter to the step-in only sometimes works.
2. **Give a fighter low on both health bars one guard that covers both (R3 E3).** At 3 head and 1 body I had only one-zone guards, so the last exchange was a coin flip. A Cover Up that halves damage to both targets while badly hurt would make it a read instead of a guess.
3. **Make a whiffed counter cost less when the attack went to the other target (R2 E2).** Losing 7 body and 2 stamina for guessing head versus body is a double punishment. Cap the whiff penalty at 1 stamina, or let the slip counter halve a body punch.
