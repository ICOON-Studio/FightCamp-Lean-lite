# Maria on Claude v Maria (Maria wins by KO in round 2)

**(1) Why I won.** I won by KO in round 2, and two beats decided it.
- **R1 E3:** he'd read my hand and had +2 speed, so I sat on Pull Counter Cross. His second Ripping Liver Hook missed and I landed 7, which rocked him and took the Lead.
- **R2 E1:** my Step-In Jab caught his Cut the Cage mid-fake for 4, which left him on 4 head health. From there, every trade with a jab that lands first was a losing trade for him.

**(2) What I could and couldn't read.** He opened with the same body hook as the last two pressure fighters, so after that one beat I could read it. Reading two of his cards showed me Cut the Cage and the cross, and both came out exactly when I expected. I didn't read the opening hook in R1 E1. I stood still, and it cost me 7 body health and 2 max stamina. I also didn't expect his guard in R2 E4, which took my jab and the Lead.

**(3) Agent or script?** The agent was more fun. Claude read my hand before attacking and switched to a guard once he was low. He traded crosses into my jabs on purpose, knowing the jab only takes 2 off his punch. A script wouldn't make that call. Still, he threw the body hook into my counter on the one exchange it was obvious.

**(4) Too strong and never wanted.** Read Tendencies against a rocked opponent felt too strong. He can't attack, so the feint is free, and it shows two cards, draws two and adds +2 speed. Both Claude and I used it the same way, which says it's the one right answer. The switch I never wanted was standing still at Mid. Against a pressure fighter it just hands over 7 body damage.

**(6) TO REACH 10/10**
1. **Take the free read away from a rocked fighter's opponent (R1 E4).** Being rocked already costs the attack. If a feint against a rocked man only draws one card and skips the speed bonus, the rocked beat becomes a choice instead of the same play every time.
2. **Give the outside fighter a body answer at Mid (R1 E1, R2 E3).** The body hook still lands for 7 against standing still, and still takes 3 through the circle-out. A cheap defend that covers the body would stop the pressure fighter's opening hook from being the best play in every match.
3. **Make the trade rule depend on punch size, not always 2 (R1 E6, R2 E5, R2 E6).** Three exchanges ended with both of us landing a jab and a cross. A jab that lands first should take a bigger bite out of a power punch, or only a straight punch should trade, so the trades feel like a skill rather than coin flips.
