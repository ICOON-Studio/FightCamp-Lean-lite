# John on John v Kalli (Kalli wins by KO in round 2)

**1. Why I lost.** Two beats decided it. In R1 E5 I threw a slow liver hook (speed 6) into his Lead, and his left hook got there first for 6 to the head. In R2 E6 I threw the same slow hook again with 5 head left, and his cross landed first at speed 10 and knocked me out. Both times my own pattern from R1 E1 (the body hook trading through a jab) made me think a hook was safe. It only trades with a jab. Against a power punch with the Lead behind it, a speed-6 card just loses.

**2. What I could read.** He pawed at Long whenever he had nothing better (R1 E3, R2 E1, R2 E2), and Hold Ground blanked that twice. He punched with the Lead, which let my pull counter catch his cross for 7 in R1 E6. What I couldn't read was when he'd switch from the jab to a step-in. He did it twice right after I held (R1 E4 with the step-in jab, R2 E3 with Cut the Cage), and both times holding left me hit or pushed to the fence.

**3. Agent vs scripted.** Yes, more fun. He punished my habit: once I held twice at Long, he stopped pawing and walked in. A script wouldn't have noticed. Losing to a read felt fair. Losing to slow-hook speed math felt less so.

**4. Too strong, and never wanted.** The plan that felt too strong was jab at Long until the other man holds, then step in. It costs the out-fighter almost nothing, and the slow fighter has no card that beats both halves. The switch I never wanted was Elbows Down in R2 E4. With no head card in my hand, it was a free stamina point that a jab simply went over.

**6. To reach 10/10**
1. **Let power punches trade with power punches when the speed gap is small (R1 E5, R2 E6).** A 6-speed hook against an 8-speed hook shouldn't simply vanish. Make it "both land when they're within 2 speed, the faster first", so the Pressure fighter's one-punch power is a real threat in an exchange and not just a jab-trade trick.
2. **Give Hold Ground a read against a step-in (R1 E4, R2 E3).** At Long, holding should also meet a man stepping in with a free half-block, or push his Cut the Cage back. Without that, holding is just a jab parry that gets punished by the obvious switch.
3. **Make sure a hand at Mid always has a head defend option for a low-stamina fighter (R2 E4 to E5).** I sat at 5 head with no guard for two exchanges and could only discard and hope. Make Tight High Guard always available at Mid like Hold Ground, or let a free discard draw from the defends, so a hurt man has a real choice instead of waiting for the finish.
