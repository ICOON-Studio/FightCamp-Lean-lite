# Kalli on John v Kalli (Kalli wins by KO in round 2)

**1. Why I won (KO in R2 E6).** Two beats decided it. In R1 E2 I circled out to long range just as he threw a straight-punch counter. It whiffed, cost him 2 stamina, and took the fight to my range. In R2 E3 my Cut the Cage stepped in past his stand-still defence for free. It pushed him to 1 cage space and gave me the Lead, so from there my jab and straight punch came in faster than any punch he had. He dropped from 23 stamina-worth of fight to 2, and my boosted straight punch at speed 10 finished him over a slow body hook.

**2. What I could read, and what I couldn't.** I could read his standing still. He played Hold Ground five times. Once I saw it parry my range jab (R1 E3), I switched to step-in cards it can't stop (R1 E4, R2 E3) and scored every time. I also read his empty stamina tank: after he fell under 4, every punch I threw was faster. What I couldn't read was R1 E6. His pull counter caught my straight punch for 7, rocked me, and turned a round I was winning 13 to 6 into a 14-13 loss. I was greedy for the 10-8 round and walked into his one good read.

**3. Another agent or a script?** More fun, because he had a real plan: trade body shots early, then wait for a counter. A script wouldn't have saved the pull counter for the last exchange of round 1. It got dull once his stamina ran out, though. A drained agent and a script look the same.

**4. Too strong, and never wanted.** Too strong: stepping in against a man standing his ground. Hold Ground parries only a jab that doesn't step in, so every step-in jab or feint walks straight past it for free. Never wanted: Elbows Down for me against a fighter on 5 head health. There was nothing on my body worth guarding.

**5. Scores**
- FUN 7: the round 1 swing was dramatic, and round 2 was a clean squeeze.
- DEPTH 7: my answer to standing still was real, and so was his counter trap.
- CLARITY 8: the long-range jab and step-in rules from v4.6 read cleanly. The extra speed from Read Tendencies showed +2, not +1, which looks like a bug.
- VARIETY 6: four standing-still or long-range jab exchanges in round 2.

**6. To reach 10/10**
1. **Make running out of stamina a comeback threat, not a slow death (R2 E2 to E5).** At 2 to 4 stamina he could only wait while I picked him apart. Let a spent fighter have one cheap desperation punch, like a free clinch at Mid, so the stamina drain doesn't turn him into a punching bag.
2. **Let Hold Ground stop a step-in feint too, or give it a penalty (R2 E3).** Right now standing still is either a free parry or a free gift. Make it beat the long-range jab and Cut the Cage, but lose to a step-in punch.
3. **Fix the speed boost text (R2 E5).** The help says Read Tendencies gives +1 speed, but the log showed +2, and my straight punch went 7 to 10 with the Lead. Make the number on screen and the rule agree, so I know whether a speed tie was earned or a bug.
