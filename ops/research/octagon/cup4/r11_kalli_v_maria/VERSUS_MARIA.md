# Maria on Kalli v Maria (DRAW 29-29)

**(1) Why it was a draw.** I threw away a round I was winning. Two beats decided it.
- **R1 E1:** I opened with a jab straight into his big counter. That took 7 off my head, left me rocked, and cost me round 1 by 8 to 2.
- **R3 E5:** I was 4 to 0 up in the last round. Against a man with 3 stamina, I chose a body guard that a head punch goes straight over. His cross landed 4, the round finished level, and so did the fight.

R2 E3 is the beat I'm proud of. He had thrown three punches in a row, so I sat on the power counter and caught him for 6.

**(2) What I could and couldn't read.** I could read his stamina. Once he ran low, he alternated a counter, a shell and a fake, so the free read in R2 E4 and the shoulder fake in R3 E2 both baited his slip.

I couldn't read whether he'd punch on almost no stamina. Twice he threw when I was sure he'd sit back: once in R2 E2, and again in R3 E5 on 3 stamina.

His read in R3 E3 cracked my circle-out. After that he knew my hand, and I had no tell that he knew it.

**(3) Was another agent more fun?** Yes. A script wouldn't have opened with the counter, and wouldn't have punched out of exhaustion with the fight on the line. The mind games felt mutual.
