# Kalli on Kalli v Maria (DRAW 29-29)

**1. Why it was a draw.** It finished 29-29. Two beats decided it.
- R2 E3: I threw a straight into Maria's Pull Counter, took 6, got rocked, lost the Lead and lost the round.
- R3 E5: my straight went over her elbow guard for 4 and levelled round 3 at 4-4. That saved the draw.

What cost me the win was stamina. I hit 0 in R3 E6, holding the Lead with her on 4 head, and had no punch left to throw.

**2. What I could read.** Maria mirrored defence with defence early (R1 E3 and E4, guard against guard and circle against circle). Once I'd seen her hand (R3 E3) she couldn't surprise me. What I couldn't read was her feints. Read Tendencies (R2 E4) and the Shoulder Feint (R3 E2) both baited my slip counter while I was hurt, and twice I gave up a turn I couldn't afford.

**3. Agent or script.** The agent was more fun. Maria answered my power counter with a power counter of her own a round later (R2 E3). That's a lesson learned mid-fight, which no script does.

**4. Too strong, and never wanted.**
- Too strong: the Pull Counter opener. It landed 7 and rocked her in R1 E1, and that single card won round 1. Both fighters opened with it across two bouts, and both times it worked.
- Never wanted: Hold Ground. It was in my hand all fight, I never played it, and Maria's one use of it (R2 E2) let my step-in jab land clean for 3.

**5. Scores**
- FUN: 7. Counter-against-counter reads were tense, and the end was tense too.
- DEPTH: 7. The feints really do punish a counter-puncher, which is right.
- CLARITY: 8. Scores now carry names, which fixed my earlier misread.
- VARIETY: 5. Four of the six exchanges in round 1 were defence or counter versus defence or counter.

**6. TO REACH 10/10**
1. **Let the Counter-Puncher regain stamina when a counter or feint works, not only when one lands.** In R3 E6 I held the Lead with Maria on 4 head and 0 stamina to use it. A tired fighter who can't take a finishing chance makes the ending flat, not tense.
2. **Make a guard against a guard cost the Lead or a little cage space.** In R1 E3 and E4 we both blocked and nothing happened, twice. Two dead exchanges out of six is a third of a round lost to waiting.
3. **Tone down the Pull Counter opener**, for example by stopping it from rocking in the first exchange of a round. In R1 E1 it decided the round before either fighter had read anything.
