# Maria on Kalli v Maria (Maria wins by KO in round 2)

**(1) Why I won.** I won by KO in round 2, and two beats decided it. In R1 E2, my Straight Cross at speed 9 beat his Ripping Liver Hook at speed 6 and landed 5 to the head. His signature opener never got to land, and the 13 head damage I banked in round 1 left him one clean jab from going down. Then in R2 E2 I spent a free exchange on Read Tendencies while he sat on Hold Ground. That gave me +2 speed, so my Step-In Jab came in at 11 against his Range Jab at 8 and finished him. The read paid off exactly the way the card says it should.

**(2) What I could read and what I couldn't.** He was easy to read once he was hurt. On 4 head health he stood at Long twice and parried, which told me he wanted to make me reach for him. The read showed me a Pull Counter and a Liver Hook in his hand, and neither one could touch a jab stepping in from Long. What I couldn't see coming was R1 E4. He held Slip & Cross at the moment I felt safest with the Lead, my hook missed, and his counter for 6 rocked me. Nothing on the table warned me he'd switched from body puncher to counter puncher.

**(3) Was another agent more fun than a script?** Yes. A script wouldn't have baited me with Elbows Down, let me land over it, and then answered my next head shot with a counter. His sitting back in round 2 also felt like a real decision under pressure, even though it turned out to be the wrong one.

**(4) Too strong, and never wanted.** The plan that felt too strong was Read Tendencies into a stepping jab. The read is free and safe at Long, and the +2 speed makes the next jab nearly unbeatable. I never wanted to switch to the Ripping Liver Hook. Against a fighter with 18 body health it would have taken four landed hooks to matter, while head shots won the bout in six exchanges that landed.

**(6) TO REACH 10/10**
1. **Make the read cost something (R2 E2).** Either drop its speed bonus or let the opponent see one of my cards back. At the moment it's a free win button against anyone waiting at Long.
2. **Give a parked fighter at Long a real answer to a stepping jab (R2 E3).** Hold Ground should be able to catch a stepping jab, or Check Hook should be offered more often at Long. That way waiting becomes a gamble instead of a slow loss.
3. **Make body damage pay off sooner (R1 E2 and the whole bout).** Body damage could lower the opponent's speed or stamina regeneration below a set point. Then the pressure fighter's plan threatens something before the head race is already over.
