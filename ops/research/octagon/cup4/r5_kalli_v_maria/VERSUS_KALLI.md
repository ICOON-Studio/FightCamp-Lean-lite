# Kalli on Kalli v Maria (Maria wins by KO in round 2)

**1. Why I lost (KO in R2 E3).** The bout was decided in R1 E2 and E3. Once Maria held the Lead, her straight punches came in at speed 9 against my 6 to 7. My body hook got beaten to the punch for 5, and my body guard let her next cross go over the top for another 5. Two exchanges took me from 15 head health down to 5. Getting only 3 back between rounds left me on 4 for round 2, so one step-in jab was enough to finish it.

**2. What I could read, and what I couldn't.** I could read her straight punches. After three head attacks in a row, my slip counter caught her hook for 6 and rocked her in R1 E4. I also read her long-range jab in R2 E1, and standing my ground parried it. What I couldn't read was her Read Tendencies in R2 E2. It showed her two of my cards and gave her +2 speed. She then stepped in at speed 11 while I sat at 8, and my jab couldn't halve a jab, only a power punch.

**3. Another agent or a script?** An agent was more fun, because Maria adapted. She saw me standing still, peeked at my hand, then switched from pawing to stepping in. A script wouldn't have built that three-beat setup. The cost is that a scouting card plus speed makes me feel watched with no reply.

**4. Too strong, and never wanted.** Too strong: holding the Lead from the opening exchange. The +2 speed carries on exchange after exchange, so the first speed winner keeps winning every punch trade until a counter lands. One snap jab in R1 E1 gave her three straight exchanges. Never wanted: Elbows Down at Mid against an out-fighter, because she only throws to the head.

**5. Scores**
- FUN 6: tense, but over in nine exchanges.
- DEPTH 6: my one real read, the counter, worked.
- CLARITY 8: the NOW speeds and the tie line made every loss easy to understand.
- VARIETY 5: her plan was the same straight punch three times.

**6. To reach 10/10**
1. **Make holding the Lead fade (R1 E1 to E3).** Drop the speed bonus to +1 after one exchange, or end the Lead after two exchanges in a row. One jab shouldn't buy three uncontested exchanges.
2. **Let damage carry over less between rounds (R2 E3).** Getting only 3 head health back left me KO'd by a 4-point jab. Give back half of the head health lost in the round, or don't allow a KO in a round's first exchange off a single jab, so round 2 is still a real fight.
3. **Make the scouting card leave a tell (R2 E2).** A peek at my hand plus +2 speed with no tell is too much. Show my opponent's boosted speed, or let the scouted fighter's next counter gain +1 speed, so being read can be answered.
