# John on John v Maria (John wins by KO in round 3)

**(1) Why I won.** Speed won it. Ghost's punches came out at 7 to 10 and Body Shop's liver hook at 6, so every straight punch against her hook landed first and cancelled it (R1 E1, R1 E3, R3 E4). Two beats decided it. In R3 E3 she held at Long, and my Step-In Jab beat the hold, landed 3 and took the Lead. In R3 E4 the cross landed at speed 9 before her hook for 5, which left her on 4 head. The finish in R3 E6 was a read: she had just jabbed my head with the Lead, so I slipped the next head shot and her cross gave me the 6.

**(2) What I could read, and what I couldn't.** Her rhythm in round 1 read as hook, guard, hook. Cut the Cage at Long read too: the range jab caught it in R2 E1. I couldn't read when she'd switch from feinting to countering. Her Slip & Cross in R2 E4 caught my second Snap Jab in a row, and I'd made it predictable by repeating it. I also misread R2 E5: I baited a counter with a feint and she simply threw the liver hook for 6 and a stamina cut. That one round flipped to her because I got cute.

**(3) Agent versus script.** It was more fun. Maria mixed all four card types, so I had to guess, and she punished the one habit I showed (two snap jabs running). A script wouldn't have read that repeat.

**(4) Too strong and never wanted.** The plan that felt too strong was simply being faster. A speed 8 or 9 straight against a speed 6 hook isn't a read, it's a sure win, and the Pressure fighter's main card loses every straight race. The switch I never wanted was Pull Counter Cross. It whiffed twice in R1 E4 and E5, cost 3 each time, and got baited onto the fence. Whiffing feels the same as getting hit.

**(6) TO REACH 10/10:**
1. **Let a slow power punch beat a fast straight sometimes (R1 E1, R1 E3, R3 E4).** Give hooks "through": when a hook is slower but has at least 3 more damage, both land, and the straight does 1 less. Then the Pressure fighter's plan works, and a speed edge stops winning every race.
2. **Make the Pull Counter cheaper to miss, or give it a tell (R1 E4–E5).** Either it costs 2, or a whiff still steps me back to Long, so I lose stamina but keep my spacing. That way two misses don't put me on the fence.
3. **Show the last two cards' zone, head or body (R2 E5).** Her body hook beat my feint because I couldn't see whether she'd been going high or low. Add "HEAD" or "BODY" next to each of her last cards, so a body puncher's pattern can be read and answered with Elbows Down, the read the game is built on.
