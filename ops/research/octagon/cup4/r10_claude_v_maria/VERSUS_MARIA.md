# Maria on Claude v Maria (Maria wins by KO in round 2)

**(1) Why I won.** I knocked Claude out in round 2, and two beats decided it. In R1 E5 my body hook caught his shoulder feint for 7 and cut his maximum stamina. That left him short of stamina for the rest of the fight. In R2 E3 he tried to read my hand at Long, and my Overhand Right stepped in and caught the fake for 8. That rocked him and left him on 5 head health. After that, the read in R2 E4 was free, because a rocked fighter can only counter or defend, and his Clinch & Hold couldn't stop a feint. The straight cross in E5 finished it.

**(2) What I could read and what I couldn't.** Claude's habits were easy to read. When hurt or low on stamina, he guarded or stood still. At Long he pawed or held his ground, and after my parried paw in R2 E2 I knew he'd sit still again. What I couldn't read was his first counter. In R1 E3 his slip caught my fast poke for 4, and I had no clue it was coming. His R1 E5 feint was also a surprise, but my hook happened to beat it.

**(3) Another agent versus a script.** It was more fun. Claude used everything a counter-puncher has: slips, a combo, a feint, a read. Because he was trying to outthink me, he kept faking, and that's why my real punches landed. A script wouldn't have tried a read at exactly the wrong moment.

**(4) Too strong, and a switch I never wanted.** Too strong: a feint at a rocked fighter. In R2 E4 a rocked fighter can only counter or defend, and my feint beat both under the new rules, so his rocked turn handed me a free read and +2 speed. The switch I never wanted was the Second Wind block. I kept it as my combo answer three times and never used it.

**(6) TO REACH 10/10**
1. **Give a rocked fighter one option a feint can't beat.** In R2 E4 Claude's only choices both lost to my feint, so being rocked meant a free turn for me. Clinch & Hold should beat a feint, or a rocked fighter should get a safe "take a knee" card that only a real punch beats.
2. **Make a Long-range read risky only for a reason the player can see.** In R2 E3 the read lost to my stepping overhand for 8, a rock that decided the fight. The card should show "loses to any step-in" right on it, so the risk is part of the choice rather than a hidden trap.
3. **Let a counter-puncher earn stamina back when the round goes well.** Claude led round 1 on his slip in R1 E3, but my feint-catching body hook in E5 drained his stamina and he never recovered. A clean counter should give 1 stamina back, so the counter-puncher's best moment isn't followed by running out of gas.
