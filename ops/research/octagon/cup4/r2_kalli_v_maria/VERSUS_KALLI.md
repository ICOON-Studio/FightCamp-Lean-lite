# Kalli on Kalli v Maria (Maria wins by KO in round 2)

**1. Why I lost (KO in round 2).** Two beats decided it. In R1 E3 my body hook ran into her pull counter: 8 to the head, and I was rocked at 4 head health with 14 damage taken in three exchanges. In R2 E4, when I had to keep the Lead, I threw a cross into her snap jab, lost 10 to 8 on speed, and dropped to 3 head health. After that, the second jab in R2 E5 was a formality.

**2. What I could read, and what I couldn't.** I could read her shell. After I hit her in R2 E2, she went defend, defend, cover, and I won that stretch by stepping in safely and pinning her near the fence. Reading her hand in R2 E1 set up my overhand cleanly. What I couldn't read was when she'd switch from counter to attack. She hooked in R1 E1, faked, countered, and then jabbed exactly when I committed. Her snap jab at speed 9, plus her higher hand speed and the Lead, beat everything I threw at mid range.

**3. Another agent or a script?** The agent was more fun and harder. Maria mixed attack, fake, counter and read in her first four cards, so there was no pattern to farm. A script wouldn't have held back a jab through three defends to fire it the moment I took a risk.

**4. Too strong, and never wanted.** Too strong: the snap jab. For 1 stamina it's the fastest card at mid, and it closed the fight twice (R2 E4, R2 E5). Never wanted: Hold Ground. At 3 to 6 head health it blocks nothing, so every time it came up it was a dead card. She took 7 standing on it in R2 E2.

**5. Scores**
- FUN 7: the comeback in round 2 felt real, but the KO came quickly once I was low.
- DEPTH 7: the read, the step-in fake and the fence all mattered.
- CLARITY 6: it wasn't clear before I played that her pull counter answers hooks at mid but not up close, or that my cover card can't be used as a reaction.
- VARIETY 6: about four cards decided most exchanges, and the snap jab dominated mid range.

**6. To reach 10/10**
1. **Price the snap jab (R2 E4, R2 E5).** Speed 9 for 1 stamina, plus her speed and the Lead, beats every hook and cross. Make it cost 2 stamina, or cap a 1-stamina punch below the fastest counter. I flagged the same issue against John, so it's a pattern across fights.
2. **Make getting rocked survivable (R1 E3, R1 E4).** After losing 14 head health in three exchanges, I had no way back into the round. Let the 3 head health between rounds scale with how far behind you are, or let Cover Up also give back 1 stamina.
3. **Let any defend card work as a reaction, or say clearly which ones can (R1 E2, R1 E4).** I was refused twice for choosing Cover Up and Hold Ground as reactions. Either allow them or mark the allowed cards in the hand.
