# Maria on John v Maria (Maria wins by KO in round 3)

**(1) Why I won.** I won by knockout in round 3. Two beats decided it.
- **R1 E1:** My opening fake caught his guard and pinned him on the fence. That set up round 1, where I landed 15 and he landed nothing.
- **R2 E6:** The big counter I'd seen in his hand only works at Mid. I'd faked my way up close, so I threw the uppercut. His step-in counter missed, I landed 6, and that left him on 4 head going into round 3.

**(2) What I could and couldn't read.**
- **Could read:** In round 1 he was a counter-puncher stuck in his shell, playing defence six times running, so heavy shots and body hooks were free. My read in R2 E4 showed me his big counter, which is why I faked at Mid and punched only once we were up close.
- **Couldn't read:** whether he'd punch at Mid. In R2 E1 his paw caught my fake, and in R2 E2 his cross traded through my jab. Both times I expected him to sit back.

**(3) Was another agent more fun?** Yes. He adapted. After round 1 he stopped turtling and started jabbing from Long, and he matched my read with his own. A script wouldn't change its whole approach between rounds.

**(4) Too strong, and never wanted.**
- **Too strong:** fake in, then hit up close. Against a man who defends, the fake both wins the exchange and pins him, so the next heavy punch on the fence is close to free.
- **Never wanted:** Overhand Right. It only works at Long, and at speed 4 it was never safe there. It just sat in my hand.
