# John on John v Maria (Maria wins by KO in round 3)

**(1) Why I lost.** I lost by KO in R3. Round one was the deciding beat. In R1 E1 her Cut Off the Cage broke my opening guard and put me on the fence at Close. Then in R1 E3 her Short Uppercut went over my Elbows Down for 7 and rocked me. After that my hand was nearly dead at Close: two exchanges of forced Hold Ground (R1 E5 and E6) let her land 7 to the body and cut my maximum stamina. The second deciding beat was R2 E6: my check hook whiffed because her uppercut didn't step in, and it left me on 4 head for the last round.

**(2) What I could read.** I could read her opening habit. She steps in with a feint, Cut Off the Cage, from Long, and my Range Jab caught it in R2 E1. I could also tell she goes to the body after a guard, and alternates hooks and uppercuts at Close. What I couldn't read was when she would stop punching and defend: her Tight High Guard in R3 E4 and Hold Ground in R3 E2 both blanked my plays. Her Step-In Jab against my feint in R3 E3 was a clean read on her part.

**(3) Another agent or a script?** Playing Maria was more fun, even in a loss. She varied her answers to my Range Jab: a Range Jab back, then a parry with Hold Ground, then a Step-In Jab through my feint. A script would have repeated one answer, and I'd have solved it.

**(4) Too strong, and never wanted.** The plan that felt too strong was Cut Off the Cage into Close hooks. Once I'm on the fence, my Counter-Puncher hand has almost nothing playable at Close (slip3, pull3, cross3 and jab are all Mid or Long only). The switch I never wanted was Elbows Down: it read the body right in R1 E3, she went to the head, and I lost 7.
