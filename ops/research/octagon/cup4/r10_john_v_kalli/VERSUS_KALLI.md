# Kalli on John v Kalli (Kalli wins by DECISION 29-28)

**1. Why I won.** I won 29-28 by taking rounds 1 and 2 and then surviving round 3. Two beats decided it. In R1 E1 my Pull Counter read his flicker jab, landed 6 and rocked him, and that one punch won the round on its own. In R3 E6 I was on 5 head with the round already lost, so I blocked instead of swinging and banked the decision.

**2. What I could read.** In round 3 John attacked three exchanges in a row, and after I hit him (R3 E4) he went into defence twice. Both were easy to see. What I couldn't read: in R3 E3 he threw a cross that doesn't step in, so my Lead Check Hook had nothing to answer and I took 5. His Read Tendencies in R2 E5 also caught my guard cold.

**3. Agent or script.** The agent was more fun. John changed gear when I hurt him, which a script doesn't do. He went from attacking to guarding the moment R3 E4 landed, so I had to guess again.

**4. Too strong, and never wanted.**
- Too strong: blocking. Guard, Circle Out and Second Wind took 5 of my attacks across the bout. Each block also stripped my Lead, and Circle Out reset the range as well (R3 E5). Defence was cheap and it stopped every combo plan.
- Never wanted: Hold Ground. It blocks nothing, and in R3 E2 his step-in jab walked straight through it for 4.

**5. Scores**
- FUN: 7. The counter opener and the comeback cross both felt great.
- DEPTH: 7. Reading his pattern changes really mattered.
- CLARITY: 6. Lowered by the score-order problem below and the hidden step-in rule.
- VARIETY: 6. Most exchanges came down to an attack against a block.
