# John on John v Kalli (Kalli wins by DECISION 29-28)

**(1) Why I lost.** I lost 29-28, and the first exchange cost me the fight. In R1 E1 I opened with my fastest jab, and Kalli read it with a Pull Counter Right for 6, which rocked my glass chin. That handed her round 1 and the clinch, the fence and three exchanges of defending that followed. The other deciding beat was R2 E3: my Slip & Counter Cross got baited by her Shoulder Feint, which pushed me to the fence and gave her the Lead. I scored 1 in that whole round. I won R3 by 11 to 4, but it was only 10-9, so the deficit stood.

**(2) What I could read, and what I couldn't.** I could read her shell-up pattern when she was low on stamina. Read Tendencies broke her guard in R2 E5, and in R3 my step-in jab and cross landed 9 while she held and threw a check hook at nothing. I couldn't read her opening, because a power counter on exchange one, with no history, is a pure guess. I also misread R3 E4: I expected a body puncher to dig to the body, so I played Elbows Down, and she threw a straight cross for 4 over it. Her name and trait told me "body", and she knew I'd believe it.

**(3) Agent versus script.** It was more fun, and more painful. Kalli played against her own label, switching to straights to the head when I guarded low, and she baited my counters twice with feints. A script built around "digs to the body" would have walked into my elbows.

**(4) Too strong and never wanted.** The plan that felt too strong was the power counter on a glass chin in the opening exchange. One correct guess was worth 6 head, a rock, the Lead and the round. The switch I never wanted was the slip counter under v5. It's free to miss, but a feint still drags me to the fence (R2 E3), so "costs nothing" isn't true.

**(6) TO REACH 10/10:**
1. **Soften the first exchange (R1 E1).** Make a counter on exchange 1 of a fight unable to rock, so it does damage only. Then the opening becomes a read with stakes instead of a coin flip that decides the round.
2. **Make a round's winning margin count when it's close and late (R3 against R1).** I won R3 by 7 and got 10-9, the same as her 5-point R1. Score 10-8 when the margin is 6 or more in the final round, or award a point for the knockdown-like "rocked" state. A comeback round should be able to swing a close fight.
3. **Show the bait risk on reactive counters (R2 E3).** The card text says "a whiff costs you nothing but the Lead", but the feint also pushed me toward the cage. Add "a feint pushes you 1 toward the fence" to the card, so the player can weigh it and the text stays honest.
