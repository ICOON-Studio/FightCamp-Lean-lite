# Maria on Claude v Maria (Claude wins by DECISION 30-27)

**(1) Why I lost.** Claude won 30-27, every round 10-9, and two beats decided it. In R1 E1 my Slip & Cross whiffed against his opening read. I fell behind on stamina right away, and he saw my hand on top of it. My answers kept missing his fakes in E3 and E6, and I was gasping for the rest of the fight. In R3 E3 I threw a slow body hook up close, and his Lead Left Hook got there first by half a speed point. It landed 6 to the head and rocked me, which ended any comeback until my last-second hook in E6.

**(2) What I could read and what I couldn't.** His waiting was readable. He stood at Long three times in round 2, and Hold Ground parried his paws. What I couldn't read was the pattern underneath, which was simply that he faked whenever I had an answer ready. His read in E1 showed him my counters, and he baited them twice more. I kept guessing he'd attack when he was really taking stamina off me.

**(3) Was another agent more fun than a script?** Yes, and that's what hurt. He used my own counter-puncher plan against me: read first, fake second, punch only when I'd gassed. A script wouldn't have adapted to my hand after one look.

**(4) Too strong, and never wanted.** The plan that felt too strong was opening with a read and then faking. Three baited answers cost me 11 stamina in round 1, and I never got above 5 again. The switch I never wanted was Cut the Cage at Long in R3 E1. His stepping jab caught it for 4, and the new rule that lets a jab meet a fighter stepping in makes that fake a trap there.

**(6) TO REACH 10/10**
1. **Let a reader's opponent hide something (R1 E1).** A read shouldn't show the counter you're holding for free. Have it show one card picked at random, or let the read player reveal one card back. Seeing my counters set up all three of his baits.
2. **Show half-point speeds before they decide a bout (R3 E3).** He won 7.5 to 7 off a speed bonus my hand never showed in that form. The NOW line should show the opponent's speed for each card zone, or the top line should say "he wins ties and is 0.5 ahead".
3. **Make waiting at Long cost something (R2 E4 to E6).** Three exchanges of Hold Ground against Hold Ground scored nothing and let the leader run out the clock. A judges' penalty for passivity, or a small stamina drain after the second straight wait, would force someone to act.
