# Kalli on Claude v Kalli (Claude wins by KO in round 3)

**1. Why I lost (KO in round 3).** Two beats decided it. In R2 E3 we both threw the step-in jab, he won on speed 10 to 9 and hit me for 4, and from then on I was fighting from 9 head health. In R3 E3 we both threw the snap jab, he won 11 to 10, and his combo cross landed for 6 and ended it. Every jab-against-jab trade went his way: R1 E1, R2 E3, R3 E1 and R3 E3. With higher hand speed and reach than me, he had no reason to throw anything else.

**2. What I could read, and what I couldn't.** I could read his straight punches at mid range. My pull counter in R1 E2 rocked him for 7, and my step-in fakes beat his counters twice (R1 E3, R3 E2). What I couldn't read was his switch to the body in R2 E5. His liver hook beat my head hook by half a speed point and took 2 off my maximum stamina for the fight. I also misread his long-range game: I expected him to step in and set a check hook (R2 E2), but he just pawed with the jab.

**3. Another agent or a script?** More fun, and harder. Claude started out mixing his cards, then went to pure jabs once he saw he won every jab trade. That's what a real out-fighter does, and a script wouldn't have adapted that way. The downside: once he found that edge, there was little I could do about it.

**4. Too strong, and never wanted.** Too strong: the faster fighter's jab. Speed decides every jab trade outright, so the fighter with the higher hand speed simply wins them. In this bout that was four for four, including the knockout. Never wanted: Hold Ground. I discarded it in eight of my turns, and it never once beat another card.

**5. Scores**
- FUN 7: the round-1 counter and the step-in fakes felt sharp, but the ending was a speed race I couldn't win.
- DEPTH 6: once he had the edge in speed, jab against jab took the choice out of most exchanges.
- CLARITY 7: the log was clear, but half-point speeds like 10.5 and 7.5 can't be read before you play.
- VARIETY 6: jabs made up 9 of his 17 cards.

**6. To reach 10/10**
1. **Let an even jab trade go both ways (R1 E1, R2 E3, R3 E1, R3 E3).** When two jabs meet, either both land, or the speed gap has to be 2 or more to cancel the slower one. A 1-point edge in hand speed shouldn't win every trade.
2. **Show the final speed numbers before the cards are sealed (R2 E5, R3 E3).** I lost by 0.5 and 1 point to modifiers I couldn't see. Put each card's real speed in the hand, including the bonuses from the Lead, reach and hand speed.
3. **Replace Hold Ground with something useful (it was in my hand all bout).** Give it 1 stamina back, or let it cancel a jab, so a defensive choice exists at long range, where I had no block (R2 E1, R2 E2).
