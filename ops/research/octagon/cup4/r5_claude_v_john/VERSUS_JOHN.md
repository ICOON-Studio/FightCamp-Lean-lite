# John on Claude v John (John wins by KO in round 1)

**1. Why I won.** Two beats decided it. In R1 E1 my Lead Left Hook traded straight through his Snap Jab. The jab rule took 2 off, but 6 still landed on a 15-head glass chin and rocked him from the first exchange. In R1 E4 he jabbed with the Lead, my Slip & Cross read it, and the counter landed 8 and rocked him again with 1 head left. After that the finish was a formality: Cover Up only halves damage, and half of a cross is still more than 1.

**2. What I could read.** He threw head punches only: jab, cross, jab, all straight and all upstairs. That made my head counter a near-certainty. When rocked, he defended instead of countering, both times. What I couldn't read was R1 E3. I expected him to guard again, threw a slow liver hook, and his 8.5 cross beat me to it for 5. Speed differences under 1 point aren't something I can predict from the screen.

**3. Agent vs scripted.** It was more fun than a script for the first three exchanges, because he mixed guard, cross and jab. It went flat after that. An agent with a glass chin should know to stay at Long and paw, but he stood at Mid and traded. A script would have made the same mistake, so this time the difference was small.

**4. Too strong, and never wanted.** The plan that felt too strong was pressure fighter versus a glass chin. One trade through the jab rule rocks him, and every rock leaves him only Counter or Defend, so I'm guessing from 3 or 4 cards instead of 8. The bout lasted five exchanges. The switch I never wanted was Read Tendencies. It sat in my hand all bout, because with him rocked twice there was nothing left to read.

**5. Scores**
- FUN: 6
- DEPTH: 5

- VARIETY: 5

**6. To reach 10/10**
1. **Cap what a single trade can do to a glass chin (R1 E1).** A jab-for-power trade in the very first exchange shouldn't rock an Out-Fighter. Let the first rock of a round need 6+ head damage after the jab reduction, or give Out-Fighters a "stick and move" bonus: a jab that lands first pushes the trade back a range instead of both landing. That way a fast fighter's speed actually protects his chin.
2. **Give a rocked fighter one real escape (R1 E5).** Cover Up halving damage can't save someone at 1 head. Add a rocked-only Clinch, a Defend that stops all damage this exchange, costs 3 stamina and hands the Lead over, so the finisher has to beat a choice and isn't just collecting.
3. **Make a fast fighter want to stay at Long (R1 E3 to E4).** Silk won his only clean beat on speed at Mid, then stayed there and got countered. Give Out-Fighters a free Long-range step-back card in hand every round, and let Range Jab score a clean point for the judges at Long. Then the duel becomes pressure cutting the cage versus a man circling, which is the fight these two characters should have.
