# Dola on Dola v Ziva (Ziva WINS on the cards 30-26)

(1) Why lost & deciding beats: Lost on cumulative damage/points — I traded 1-for-1 too often while Ziva stole two big 2-point cross counters. Deciding beats: R1B3 & R2B3 — both times I feinted, Ziva stepped in→feinted→cross, landing clean for +2 points each; feinting left me defenseless to his double-feint rhythm, and I never punished his step-in reliably. R3B2 sealed it: I jabbed into his lead hook, got dropped for 4 health, fell too far behind to catch up.
(2) Read vs hidden: Read perfectly — Ziva’s body jab at Long was his default 11/18 beats; his step-in→feint→cross/hook was his Mid-range finisher. What I missed: his double-feint beat structure — I guarded against one feint, not two; and he switched from cross → hook on step-in (R3B2) without warning, my jab met a guard I didn't see coming.
(3) Agent vs scripted: More fun — Ziva adapted within patterns rather than just looping lines; seeing him escalate from body jabs → feint crosses → knockdown hook felt like a real opponent learning me, not just reading a script. Even predictable patterns felt alive because I knew he might pivot, and when he did it hurt.
(4) Too strong & unwanted switch: Too strong: Feint→big-punch — the feint guaranteeing "cannot be covered" makes any follow-up nearly free; Ziva landed 3/3 crosses after feints. Unwanted switch: R3B2 rear=low — I'd guarded body so often I got caught expecting body jab; habit punished hard in one moment.
(5) Ratings: FUN 8/10, DEPTH 7/10, CLARITY 9/10, VARIETY 6/10
(6) Three changes, tied to beats:
#1 — Limit feint stacking: A feint only guarantees the next single moment, not an entire beat. R1B3/R2B3: Ziva stacked two feints then landed cross with zero risk — one feint should still leave a small opening to block.
#2 — Show opponent's range intention: When someone chooses step-in/back, show it before punch resolution. R3B2: I'd have seen Ziva stepping in and chosen a hook or clinch instead of a Long-range jab that got blocked.
#3 — Body-jab stamina cost or range cap: Body jab is identical to jab at every range for same cost — too safe. Cap bjab at Mid max or raise cost to 2; R1–R3 almost every beat was just 1-for-1 body jab trades at Long with no reason to vary.
