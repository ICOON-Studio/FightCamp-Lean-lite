# Maria on Maria v Dola (Dola WINS on the cards 29-27)

**(1) Why I lost.** I lost on the cards, 29-27, because I gave away round 1 at Mid. Two beats decided it.
- **Round 1, beat 3.** I threw a medium body cross into a fast lead hook. The hook beat me to the punch for 3 health and 2 points, and set the tone for a 10-8 round.
- **Round 1, beat 5 (and again in round 2, beat 3).** I dropped my lead hand low and threw a cross, and Dola's cross, thrown with its lead hand high, blocked mine and landed on me. That was the same mistake twice: 6 health and 4 points gone.

I won round 3 by staying at Long and countering, but the hole from round 1 was too deep.

**(2) What I could read, and what I couldn't.** One habit was clear. Every time I was at Mid, Dola threw the lead uppercut, which only reaches Inside. Backing off with a body jab caught it three times for 3 health each (round 2, beat 5; round 3, beats 2 and 5). At Long it jabbed or stepped in and jabbed, so holding both hands high blocked every one. What I couldn't read was its choice at Mid between the hook, the body hook and the cross. It kept its rear hand high and its lead hand free, so all three looked the same until they landed.

**(3) Another agent against a script.** It was more fun in round 3, because I found a real habit and beat it. But Dola never adjusted to my backing off, and it threw a lead uppercut at Mid, a punch that couldn't reach, three times. A tuned script would have been sharper. Against another agent the mistakes are odd rather than readable.

**(4) Too strong, and never wanted.**
- **Too strong:** backing off with a body jab. It dodges every Mid punch, adds 1 health as a pull counter, and won me round 3 on its own.
- **Never wanted:** the clinch. We were never Inside, and every big exchange happened at Mid.
