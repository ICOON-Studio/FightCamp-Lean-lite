# Dola on Maria v Dola (Dola WINS on the cards 29-27)

VERDICT
(1) Why I won — the deciding beats
I won on consistent round dominance and smarter pattern punishment. The two beats that sealed it:
Round 1 Beat 3: I read Maria would step in behind high guard and throw body cross — my lead hook was faster, beat her punch clean for 3 damage and 2 points, and took control of the round.
Round 1 Beat 5: Maria repeated cross with lead low — I matched it from high guard, blocked hers, landed mine for another 3 points. That 6-point gap in Round 1 was the difference on the cards — Maria never fully recovered it.
(2) What I read vs couldn't
Readable patterns: Maria was extremely consistent — she loved body jabs from Long, stepping in then backing off to pull-counter, lead-high guard + low lead on crosses, and rear hand stayed high almost every beat. Once I saw her tendency to trade range for counter damage, I knew to punish her commitment to body shots.
Unreadable: Her exact "back off + body jab" timing — she'd pull back at different moments making Mid-range punches miss at Long twice; also couldn't predict when she'd choose full guard vs throw, since she alternated them without clear rhythm in Round 3.
(3) Agent vs scripted opponent
More fun, definitely. A scripted opponent always does the same thing on the same beat — you memorize the answer and win. Maria adapted within her patterns — she'd mix up when to step back, when to sit in guard, and she punished my overcommits (my missed uppercuts at Long cost me health every time). It felt like reading a person, not solving a puzzle — every round felt like a fresh adjustment.
(4) Strongest plan & unwanted switch
Too strong: The feint → step-in → big punch chain — feint guarantees the next punch can't be covered, which should be huge but I never actually got to land it because Maria kept backing away. Feint needs a cost or cooldown, it's too safe.
Never wanted: lup at Long — twice I threw it thinking she'd stay at Mid, she backed and I wasted stamina and took counters. That reach difference punishing medium punches at Long felt sharp but unforgiving — maybe warn "may fall short" in the move description.
(5) Ratings
FUN: 8 — tense, tactical, readable but not solvable
DEPTH: 7 — range + speed + lines create real choices, but a few dominant patterns emerged
CLARITY: 8 — rules clear enough after 2 rounds; line/ reach/ speed are the perfect core
VARIETY: 6 — Maria leaned heavily on 2-3 moves; more opponent archetypes would lift this fast
(6) Three changes to reach 10/10
Pull-counter limit or stamina cost — Maria backed off 5 times for free counters; backing off should cost 1 extra stamina or only grant +1 damage once per round. Seen in R2B5, R3B2, R3B5 — same trick punished me every time.
Feint draws one hand only — currently it says "the punch you throw in the next moment cannot be covered" — too powerful. Should randomise which hand drops or let opponent choose one hand to keep up. R3B4 feint was perfect setup but unrewarded because she fled instead of engaging.
Punch range preview — show "Reaches: ●Long ●Mid ●Inside" next to each move so it's instant; I threw lup twice at Long because I misjudged the step-back timing — clear ranges would fix that. R2B5 and R3B5 identical misread.
VERDICT END
