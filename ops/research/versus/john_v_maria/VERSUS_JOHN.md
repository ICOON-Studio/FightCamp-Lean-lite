# John on John v Maria (John WINS on the cards 30-28)

**(1) Why I won.** Two beats decided it. In R2 B4 my feint then cross landed for 3 while her body cross only took 2, and that put me 3-2 up in a round where every other beat was blocked or level, so it won me the round 10-9. In R3 B6 I held my ground while Maria stepped in. The points ended level, and the rule that gives a level round to the man who backed off less handed me round 3, because she'd backed off in R3 B4 and I never did.

**(2) What I could read and what I couldn't.** At Long she body-jabbed almost every beat (R1 B1, R2 B1, R3 B1, R3 B5), so my body jabs traded evenly with hers, and holding my lead low in R2 B5 blocked one. Inside, she leads with uppercuts and keeps her rear hand high, so my hook was wasted (R1 B4), but feint then cross landed every one of the five times I threw it. What I couldn't read was when she'd back off and counter with a body jab off the pull (R2 B5, R3 B4). That counter cost me 3 health with my jab blocked, and my R3 B6 body jab hit her lead held low.

**(3) Was another agent more fun?** Yes, though less than against Claude. Maria mixed up stepping in, waiting and pulling back, so Long range was a real guessing game. But we kept mirroring each other, with four even body-jab trades, and a scripted opponent would have produced the same stalemate.

**(4) Too strong, and never wanted.** Feint then cross is still too strong. It landed 5 times out of 5 in this bout and 5 out of 5 in the one against Claude. The switch I never wanted was the lead hook. Every opponent so far keeps the rear hand high, so it only ever gets blocked.
