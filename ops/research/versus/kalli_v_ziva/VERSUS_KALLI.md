# Kalli on Kalli v Ziva (Ziva WINS on the cards 29-27)

**1. Why I lost, 29-27**
I lost to one combination, the feint then the cross, which landed three times for 12 health. The two beats that decided it:
- **Round 2, beat 3:** I threw the hook and the uppercut into her feint. The hook was blocked, the cross landed as a counter, and her faster cross stopped my uppercut. That cost me the round 8-10.
- **Round 3, beat 3:** I read her 3 stamina as "no feint" and threw the hook. She spent all her stamina on the feint and cross, and the knockdown handed her the bout. I was right that her stamina was low but wrong about what she would do with it.

**2. What I could read, and what I couldn't**
I could read her opening at Long: a body jab with her rear hand high, every time. My lead hand held low blocked it three times. I could also read that any rear punch from her opens her side to a fast hook, which landed twice for 3.
I could not read when the feint was coming. It came after a step in, at Inside, and at Mid on low stamina, and nothing on the screen told it apart from a plain cross. Her push off against my clinch in round 3, beat 2 also came out of nowhere.

**3. Agent against scripted**
Another agent was more fun. Ziva changed her plan once I solved the body jab, and the matching steps in (round 2, beat 2 and round 3, beat 1) felt like two people reading each other. A script would have kept throwing body jabs.

**4. Too strong, and never wanted**
Too strong: the feint then the cross. It won both rounds Ziva took and still has no visible cost.
Never wanted: the overhand. Her rear hand was always high, so a slow punch at 3 stamina into a covered line never made sense.

**5. Scores**
- FUN: 7. The tight finish was gripping, with 4 health against 5 on the last beat.
- DEPTH: 6. Reading the opening was real, but one combination decides it.
- CLARITY: 8. The moment-by-moment log explains every land and every block.
- VARIETY: 5. Of her 18 beats, 8 were body jabs or jabs and 6 were crosses.

**6. To reach 10/10**
1. **Give the feint a tell or a price (round 2, beat 3).** Either her lead hand stays out of its guard for the next moment, or a feint puts her rear hand one moment later. Then the hook into a feint could still land.
2. **Make a clinch that gets pushed off cost the pusher (round 3, beat 2).** The push should cost her the punch in that same moment. As it is, she shoved me off and still threw a cross, so the clinch was a risk with no reward.
3. **Let a lead hook that beats a cross to the punch also count as a counter (round 3, beat 5).** My hook stopped her cross, yet my body cross after it got no counter and was blocked. A stopped punch should open him up the way a missed one does, so the patient read pays off.
