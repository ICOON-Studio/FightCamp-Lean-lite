# Claude v Ziva - VERDICT (Claude WINS by referee stoppage in round 3; Ziva led that round 5-1)

**1. Decided by two beats.** R2 beat 2: his feint and overhand for 4 damage - my first knockdown of the night -
and R2 finished 10-8 to him, which was the whole bout once R1 had gone 9-10. R3 beat 4: at 1 health I loaded both
hands, and his body jab killed me at moment one - a one-damage punch deciding a fight in which I had won the round
5-1 and floored him inside it.

**2. Readable, and not.** His rear hand's position was his game and I read it late: home high shut his side (my
hook died on it twice) and meant no dig was coming, while a LOW lead gave away the straight - my clean hits came
through that line, including the R3 beat 1 cross that floored him. The best tell was his feet: three times he
stepped at moment one and dug at moment two. What I could not read is his moment one: a feint and a hook look
identical until the beat resolves, and the feint is what uncorked the overhand that took me down.

**3. Agent versus script.** Yes, more clearly than in any bout but Maria's. No script times a clinch for the exact
beat I have paid two stamina into (R3 beat 2: my lead uppercut, erased, +2 to him), and no script cuts me off at
the ropes the beat after my back-off worked. Which answers the feedback question directly: I repeated my winning
beat (R2 beat 4's back-off-into-body-jab) at R2 beat 5 and was punished for it immediately, the range lost and the
dig landed. What he lacked was range: three moment-one options and three punches.

**4. Too strong, never wanted.** Too strong: the feint. One stamina makes a four-damage punch uncoverable, which
is how a man on 6 health beats one on 4. Never wanted: the clinch, which deletes the other man's whole paid beat
and pays the clincher +2 stamina for doing it.

**5. Scores.** FUN 9, DEPTH 9, CLARITY 8, VARIETY 8 - the best bout of my five: four knockdowns, every round
swinging both ways, and me the one carried out.

**6. TO REACH 10/10.** (a) Bound the feint - R2 beat 2, an uncoverable 4-damage overhand against a guard with both
hands home. (b) Stop the knockdown healing - R2 beat 2, down at 4 health and up on 6, more than I had before the
beat. (c) Charge the clinch - R3 beat 2, it erased my paid beat and paid him.

VERDICT END
