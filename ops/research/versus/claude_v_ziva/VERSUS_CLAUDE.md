# Claude v Ziva - Claude WINS, the referee stops it (round 3, beat 4; cards were 10-9, 10-8)

## 1. Why I won - the two beats that decided it
- Round 1, beat 5: Ziva had thrown feint-cross twice running. I stepped in and threw a fast lead hook in moment 2 - the moment of her cross. The hook is fast, her rear was out throwing, so it landed first and STOPPED her feinted cross. That is the one answer to the feint I found in the whole league.
- Round 2, beat 5: I was at 2 health after being punished. Ziva backed off and body-jabbed; I stepped in (cut her off), my low lead blocked the body jab, and my body cross landed as a counter for 3 - second knockdown, her on the ropes. From there two body punches and a body jab finished it.

## 2. What I could read and what I could not
- Readable: at Long Ziva body-jabbed with the rear high, or backed off and body-jabbed in moment 2. Lead low + step in + body cross beat both (R2B1, R2B5). When she threw her rear in moment 1 (cross), the fast lead hook beat it (R2B3).
- Repeating WAS punished: I threw the lead hook three beats running; on the third (R2B4) she backed off, my hook fell short and her body jab came back as a pull counter for 3. And her step-in cross in R3B1 beat my feint (the feinting lead leaves the straight line open in its moment) and put me down.
- Not readable: when she would switch from body jabs to an overhand or a step-in cross.

## 3. More fun than a scripted opponent?
Yes - the most fun of my bouts. She answered my habits (R2B4) and my favourite plan (R3B1), so I had to change plans every beat and the finish was close (2 health against her 4).

## 4. Too strong / never wanted
- Too strong: the step in + body cross behind a low lead at Long. It beats the body jab AND the back off (the step in cuts him off) and the body line is never covered by the rear hand.
- Never wanted: the push, the rear uppercut.
