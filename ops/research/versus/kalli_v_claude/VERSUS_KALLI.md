# Kalli on Kalli v Claude (Claude WINS - the referee stops it)

**(1) Why I lost.** The referee stopped it in round 3, beat 2. I got cut off, and the third knockdown ended the bout. Two beats decided it. In round 1, beat 6, I held both hands at Long and he stepped in, feinted and landed the overhand for 4, which took my health from 7 to 3. Everything after that was survival. In round 2, beat 2, I backed off while already at Long, which did nothing, and his body jab, step in and body cross put me down under my high lead hand. I misread the body line three times all bout.

**(2) What I could read.** Claude lives on the body cross under a high lead hand (four times), and on the step in with a feint from Long. He rests both hands right after spending stamina (round 1, beat 5 and round 2, beats 3 and 6). What I couldn't read was whether a step in from Long brings a feint and a head punch or a body jab and a body cross. Both start the same way, and they need opposite hands.

**(3) Is another agent more fun than a script?** Yes. Claude worked out that I back off and punished it: in round 2, beat 2 and round 3, beat 2, he cut me off. A script wouldn't notice my habit. But losing to a feint I can't block, for the third bout running, stops being fun.

**(4) Too strong, and never wanted**
- **Too strong:** step in, feint, then a rear punch. It works from Long because the step in carries it to Mid, so backing off is no longer an answer. It landed for 4, 4, and missed only once.
- **Never wanted:** backing off when already at Long. It cost a whole beat in round 2, beat 2 and gave nothing.

**(5) Scores**
- FUN: 6
- DEPTH: 5
- CLARITY: 6
- VARIETY: 6

**(6) TO REACH 10/10**
1. **The feint can't follow a step in on the same beat.** In round 1, beat 6 and round 3, beat 2, the step in, feint and overhand from Long had no answer: holding lost to the feint, and backing off was cut off. The feinter should have to arrive first, so the man at Long gets one beat to react.
2. **A man already at Long can't choose back off.** If he does, the game should say so before the beat is played. In round 2, beat 2, I lost a beat and took a knockdown to a rule I didn't know.
3. **A slow punch after a feint lands for 2 less.** The overhand did 4 in both deciding beats. The feint should open fast punches, not make a slow one a finisher.
