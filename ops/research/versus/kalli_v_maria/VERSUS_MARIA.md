# Maria on Kalli v Maria (Maria WINS on the cards 29-27)

**(1) Why I won.** I won on the cards, 29-27, and two beats decided it.
- **Round 1, beat 3.** Kalli held both hands low the beat before, so I threw the lead hook first. It landed for 3 health and 2 points, its own hook hit my high rear hand, and that won round 1.
- **Round 3, beat 3.** Kalli threw a cross, and my faster lead hook beat it to the punch. That was 2 more points and dropped it to 2 health, giving me the 5-point lead that made the last round 10-8.

**(2) What I could read, and what I couldn't.** Kalli's guard showed through. Whenever it threw the lead hook, its rear hand was low, so my hook with my rear hand high won those exchanges. When it held a hand low, the line above it was open, so the jab and cross went straight in. What I couldn't read was when it would hook rather than go straight. In round 2, beat 5, I expected another jab or cross and threw the body cross, and its faster hook beat me to it for 3 health and the round.

**(3) Another agent against a script.** Yes, because Kalli mixed things up. It held both hands high on beats where I expected a punch, and it backed off at the end when it was hurt. The fun was guessing a mind rather than learning a loop. The weak side is that three jab-for-jab trades (round 1, beat 1; round 2, beats 1 and 3) cancelled out and taught nobody anything.

**(4) Too strong, and never wanted.**
- **Too strong:** the fast lead hook with the rear hand held high. It beats every slower punch, its own guard blocks the reply hook, and it decided both big beats.
- **Never wanted:** the overhand. At 3 stamina it's slow, any fast hook stops it, and it isn't worth one more health over the cross.

**(6) TO REACH 10/10.**
1. **Cut back the hook with the rear hand high.** Make the lead hook medium speed at Mid, or let a jab that lands first stop it. Round 3, beat 3 should have been a real gamble rather than a safe pick.
2. **Make even trades mean something.** When two small punches land in the same moment, the one with the better guard should get the edge. Round 1, beat 1 and round 2, beats 1 and 3 were pure noise.
3. **Make a hurt boxer's escape cost something.** Backing off at 1 or 2 health should cost guard as well as ground, and the step-in punch should reach. In round 3, beat 6, Kalli escaped the finish for 1 stamina, and the game ended on a blocked body jab instead of a knockdown.
