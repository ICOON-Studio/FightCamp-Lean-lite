# Maria v Ziva - VERDICT (Ziva WINS - the referee stops it, on the third knockdown)

**1. Decided by two beats.** R3 beat 4: I feinted and crossed on her 2 health, her hook died on my rear
hand, and she went down for the second time - 5 points that took the round from 3-6 to 8-6 and left her on
the brink. R3 beat 6: at 1 health she had to survive one punch, she held her lead high to cover the
straight, and the body jab went through it - her third knockdown, and the referee stopped it.

**2. Readable, and not.** The read of the night was that she MIRRORS: she copied my body jab, my uppercut,
and my guard. In R2 beat 5 I dropped my rear hand LOW to kill her under game; she dropped hers too, and the
next beat both rears were still low - which left the SIDE open. I hooked, and she went down. Feeding her a
plan to read was the only thing that worked. What I could NOT
read is the moment: her hook took my cross to the punch in R1 beat 3 and in R3 beat 3, and in R3 beat 5 we
both feinted and crossed and BOTH landed - an uncoverable punch ignores what either man holds, so that beat
was a coin flip that cost me a knockdown.

**3. Agent versus script.** Much better, and this bout proves it: she mirrored so hard that the mirror
became a weapon I could aim. A script's habit table cannot notice that you have noticed it, cannot adopt
your guard, and cannot answer your feint with its own. Every read I made here was a read of her reading me.

**4. Too strong, never wanted.** Too strong: the feint. It makes the next punch uncoverable, so in R3 beat
5 two feints produced a guaranteed mutual trade with neither guard mattering, and earlier it beat a guard
with both hands home (R2 beat 6). Never wanted: the push off - I never found a reason to press it here.

**5. Scores.** FUN 8, DEPTH 8, CLARITY 8 (the log explains every beat; the moment is still fog), VARIETY 8.

**6. TO REACH 10/10.** (a) Stop the knockdown healing: R3 beat 5, I went down at 3 health and stood up on
4, and the same rule kept her alive at 4 until a poke finished it - being floored should return a fixed low
number, never more than you had. (b) Bound the feint: R3 beat 5, both men feinted and both crosses landed,
deleting the guard game - make the drawn hand open only one line, or make the feint cost more. (c) Make
Long worth playing: R1 beats 1-2 and R2 beats 1-2 were one-damage pokes decided only by which hand the
other man had parked; one 2-damage punch that reaches Long, or start rounds at Mid.
