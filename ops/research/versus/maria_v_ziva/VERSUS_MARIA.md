# Maria on Maria v Ziva (Ziva WINS - the referee stops it)

**(1) Why I lost: the referee stopped it on the last beat, when I was one point up in round 3.**
Two beats decided it. In round 3, beat 4, I threw a second hook straight into Ziva's feint and cross. The feint left its rear hand up, so my hook was blocked, and its cross landed as a counter for my second knockdown. In round 3, beat 6, I guessed a straight jab, so I held my lead hand high and threw a cross. Ziva threw a body jab under that hand instead. The body jab is fast, so it landed before my medium cross, and at 1 health that was my third knockdown.

**(2) What I could read, and what I couldn't.**
Ziva copied my plans almost every beat. It traded body jab for body jab, uppercut for uppercut, hook for hook, and feint and cross for feint and cross. Once I saw that, its hand height told me exactly where it was open, and my hooks landed three times. What I couldn't read was when it would stop copying and use its own plan, the step in, feint and cross. It did that twice, both times when I rested or backed off. On the final beat I read its stamina correctly but guessed the wrong line.

**(3) Was another agent more fun than a script?**
Yes, mostly. Copying my plans made it a game of mind reads, and the last round, with both of us one knockdown from losing, was tense. Seven of the eighteen beats were straight trades, though, and those felt like coin flips rather than reads.

**(4) Too strong, and never wanted.**
Too strong: the feint followed by a cross again. It landed four times and decided both knockdowns that mattered, and backing off still didn't escape it. Never wanted: backing off. Twice I backed off and was cut off and hit anyway, and in a level round it would also have cost me the tie-break.

**(5) Scores**
- FUN 8: the ending, with three knockdowns each way, was the best of these bouts.
- DEPTH 6: reading a copier works, but the feint and the trades flatten the choices.
- CLARITY 7: the log is clear, but I couldn't tell beforehand that a body jab beats a cross at Mid.
- VARIETY 5: seven beats were mirror trades, and the feint and cross came up four times.

**(6) To reach 10/10**
1. **Make trades lopsided (round 2, beat 6, and round 3, beat 5).** When the same punches meet, the fighter with more guard or stamina should edge it, or the punch that came second should be a counter. As it is, a trade just knocks both fighters down.
2. **Give the feint an answer (round 3, beat 4).** A fast punch thrown during the feint moment should cancel the cross that follows it. Otherwise my blocked hook is a pure loss, and the feint has no risk at all.
3. **Show speed and line on each pick (round 3, beat 6).** Mark each punch's speed on its button, in words and with an icon, not colour alone, and warn me when my chosen punch is slower than the cheapest punch the opponent can afford. That way, losing with a cross to a body jab is a choice I understood, not a surprise.
