# LEAGUE_ZIVA - Puncher's Chance (S567), seat Ziva

**Record: 4-1.** Beat Kalli 29-27 (9-10, 10-8, 10-9), John 29-28 (10-9, 10-9, 9-10) and Dola 30-26 (10-9, 10-9,
10-8) on the cards, and Maria by referee stoppage (her third knockdown, ended with a body jab through her
lead-high guard). Lost to Claude by referee stoppage in round three: my third time down, on a one-damage body
jab, in a round I was winning 5-1 after flooring him inside it.

**The one plan I would call broken: the feint.** For one stamina it makes the next punch uncoverable, so it
ignores every guard - and the guard position should be the skill of this game. Claude's feint-overhand at R2
beat 2 was four damage against a guard with both hands home: my first knockdown, and it cost me the round and
the bout. My own feint-cross at Dola's R2 beat 3 took two points and three damage the same way. At Claude R3
beat 1 he loaded that punch again and it only missed because my cross reached him first and floored him.

**The one change I would make first: bound the feint.** Let it open a single line - the one the feint leaves -
instead of making the next punch unanswerable, so a feint is a read the opponent can still beat rather than a
beat of immunity. The rest of my list - stop healing a floored man to 6; make the clincher pay for the beat
he erases; charge the back-off for the range - only tweaks the economy.

Repeating a winning beat was punished: at Claude R2 beat 4 my back-off into a body jab landed as a three-damage
counter; at beat 5 he cut me off, the range was lost, and the dig put me down.
