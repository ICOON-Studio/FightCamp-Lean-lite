# John on Kalli v John (John WINS on the cards 30-26)

**(1) Why I won.** I won 30–26 on the cards, and two beats decided it.
- **R2 B3, feint then cross:** Kalli threw a lead hook into my rear hand, which was still up because my cross came in the second moment. The cross then landed as an uncoverable counter for 4 health. That took him from 10 to 6 and taught me the pattern.
- **R3 B2, lead hook then rear uppercut:** his rear hand was held high to block my hook, which left the under line open. The uppercut landed as a counter for 5 and floored him. R3 B5 repeated the R2 B3 trick for the second knockdown.

**(2) What I could read, and what I couldn't.**
- **What I could read:** Kalli almost always threw exactly one punch a beat and held the other hand. When the punch was a cross, his lead was held high or low. When it was a lead hook, his rear was held. So the line he left open was always the one his held hand didn't cover, and a two-punch plan with my second hand still up (it blocks until its moment comes) beat his single punch most beats.
- **What I couldn't read:** his feet. The back-offs at R2 B4 and R2 B6 wasted 5 of my stamina on punches that fell short. His step in at R3 B1 smothered my body jab, and his cross landed for 4. The shown history gives no tell for movement, so feet stayed a coin flip.

**(3) Was another agent more fun?** Yes. Kalli adapted: his lead went low twice to block my body cross (R1 B3, R2 B2), so I switched to the feint. And the clinch at the final bell (R3 B6) was a human-feeling survival move. A script repeats; Kalli made me change plans twice.

**(4) Too strong, and never wanted.**
- **Too strong:** feint, then cross. It landed for 4 every time the range allowed it, at a cost of 3 stamina, and nothing in the rules answers it except backing off.
- **Never wanted:** push. I was Inside for five beats and it never had a job.

**(5) Scores.**
- **FUN 8:** two knockdowns, and every beat was a read.
- **DEPTH 7:** the held-hand logic is rich, but the feint flattens it.
- **CLARITY 8:** "blocked: still up, its punch comes in moment 2" explained every beat. Only the counter maths took re-reading.
- **VARIETY 6:** Kalli's whole kit was jab, cross, lead hook and back off, and mine was feint and cross from R2 on.

**(6) TO REACH 10/10.**
1. **Give the feint an answer (R2 B3, R3 B5):** a defender who holds BOTH hands that beat is not drawn by the feint, and the feinter's next punch counts as blocked. That turns the feint into a real guess.
2. **Show a tell for footwork (R2 B4, R2 B6, R3 B1):** add a line under his history, "his feet this round: in 1, hold 2, back 2", the same way the hands are counted. That makes reading the range a read instead of a coin flip.
3. **Give push a job Inside (R3 B2–B5):** a landed push also cancels his punch in that moment and costs him 1 stamina. Then it becomes a defensive switch worth picking when he's loaded up on an uppercut.
