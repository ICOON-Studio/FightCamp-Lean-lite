# Claude v Maria - Claude WINS, the referee stops it (round 3, beat 3; cards were 10-9, 10-8)

## 1. Why I won - the two beats that decided it
- Round 2, beat 1: step in, feint, overhand while Maria waited at Long with lead low / rear high - 4 health she could not cover, 8 down to 4. Two beats later feint-cross dropped her (R2B4).
- Round 3, beat 3: Inside, Maria at 1 health threw a lead hook with the rear high, as she had all bout. My rear high blocked the hook and my lead uppercut came up the under line her high rear never covers: a counter for 4, third knockdown, stopped.

## 2. What I could read and what I could not
- Readable: Maria's rear hand was high on almost every beat, and when it punched (cross, body cross) it went in moment 1 - so my fast lead hook beat her rear punches to the punch (R1B3). At Long she backed off and body-jabbed in moment 2 (R2B5, R3B1); stepping in in moment 1 cut her off.
- Readable after the fact: she sometimes copied my last beat (lead hook after my lead hook, lead hook + cross after my jab + cross, the same low/high wait after mine).
- Not readable: R3B2, where she switched from back-off-body-jab to hook + cross and took me from 5 to 2 health; I had guarded low for a body jab that never came.

## 3. More fun than a scripted opponent?
Yes - the copying and the switch in R3B2 were things a script would not do, and that beat scared me. But a single guard habit (rear always high) decided the bout: once seen, the uppercut and the hook were free.

## 4. Too strong / never wanted
- Too strong: STEP IN + FEINT + OVERHAND / CROSS again (R2B1, R2B6, R3B1 all landed; the step in also "cut off" her back off in R3B1). In my four finished bouts it was never once answered.
- Never wanted: the push, and the clinch (I never needed to stop a clinch or to clinch at Inside).

## 6. To reach 10/10
1. Answer the feint: make a feint read by a held, unmoving guard (or a back off in the feint's moment) cost the feinter - e.g. his next punch is blocked and he loses 1 guard. Tied to R2B1/R2B4/R3B1: three feinted punches, three landings, three of my five knockdowns across the league.
2. Make a guard habit cost the man who keeps it less absolutely: a hand held high all beat could cover two lines at half strength (a big punch through it does half). Tied to R3B3: one rear-always-high habit gave away the under line for a 4-health finish.
3. Show health after each punch in the beat text as "Maria 4 -> 1" and the round margin to 10-8. Tied to R3B2, where I had to add up three numbers to know she was at 1 and I at 2 - the most tense beat should be the easiest to read.
