# Maria on Maria v Claude (Claude WINS - the referee stops it)

**(1) Why I lost: the referee stopped it in round 3, beat 3.**
Two beats decided it. In round 2, beat 4, I jabbed into Claude's feint and its cross, which couldn't be covered, knocked me down. That set the pattern for the rest of the bout. In round 3, beat 1, I backed off to dodge the same plan, but Claude stepped in and cut me off. The feint and cross landed anyway for my second knockdown. After that I was hunting a knockout at 4 health and walked into a lead uppercut with my rear hand held high.

**(2) What I could read, and what I couldn't.**
I read that Claude rests with its lead hand low, and my cross landed through that gap three times. I also read its feint followed by a big punch, which it used four times in nine beats. Reading it didn't help, because nothing I own beats it once it starts. What I couldn't read was the switch to Inside. It stepped in with a body cross, then threw a lead uppercut, a punch it hadn't shown all bout, right into the one hand I always keep high.

**(3) Was another agent more fun than a script?**
Yes. Claude adapted. Once I started backing off, it began opening with a step in, and when I kept my rear hand high, it went under it. A script would have kept throwing whatever worked. That still felt like a person punishing my habit.

**(4) Too strong, and never wanted.**
Too strong: stepping in followed by a feint and a big punch. It closes the distance, makes my block useless, and still costs less than my whole beat, and backing off didn't escape it. Never wanted: the overhand. At 3 stamina with a slow speed, it only works behind a feint, and Claude used it better than I ever could.

**(5) Scores**
- FUN 7: close to the end, and both of us were one punch from going down in round 3.
- DEPTH 6: the reads were real, but the feint wipes most of them out.
- CLARITY 7: the log explains every result, though "cut off" while backing off came as a surprise.
- VARIETY 5: the same feint and big punch decided five beats.

**(6) To reach 10/10**
1. **Give the feint an answer (round 2, beat 4, and round 3, beat 1).** A punch that lands during the feint moment should cancel the follow-up. My jab and my body jab both landed in that moment and changed nothing.
2. **Spell out the cut-off rule (round 3, beat 1).** If stepping in beats backing off, the beat screen should say so before I pick. Better still, backing off could win when the other fighter also throws a feint, because that costs them a moment.
3. **Show uppercut danger at Inside (round 3, beat 3).** When the range changes to Inside, flag that a rear hand held high leaves the uppercut open, with words and an icon rather than colour alone. That way the knockout comes from a read, not a hidden rule.
