**(1) Why I lost.** I lost 30-27, and her feint won every round. Two beats decided it. In round 1, beat 4, I held both hands at Mid and her feint and cross went straight through for 3. In round 3, beat 2, I threw a hook and a cross into her feint and overhand, both were blocked, and the overhand landed as a counter for 5, leaving me on 1 health with nothing left but survival. I never knocked her down, and I won no round.

**(2) What I could read.** Dola opens almost every beat with the jab, her lead hand sits high, and at Long she throws a rear punch that falls short, which I punished for counters and for her stamina (she fell to 1 in round 2, beat 3). What I couldn't read was which beat at Mid would be the feint. She threw it four times, and it looked identical to her plain jab-and-cross beats, so I guessed wrong each time.

**(3) Is another agent more fun than a script?** Yes, but less than against John. Dola mixed the jab-cross, the feint and the overhand, and she rested both hands when tired. That felt human. But at Mid the only answer to her feint was to leave Mid, so the bout became a dance of me backing off and her stepping in, which a script could do too.

**(4) Too strong, and never wanted**

- **Never wanted:** the lead hook. Every time I threw it, her rear hand was still up or held, and it was blocked all three times.
