# John on John v Ziva (Ziva WINS on the cards 29-28)

**(1) Why I lost.** Two beats decided it. In R1 B6 I was a point down and threw a hook then a cross. Ziva's feint then cross left Ziva's rear hand up through moment 1 to block my hook, Ziva's lead hand was back up in moment 2 to block my cross, and Ziva's cross landed as a counter. That swung round 1 from level to 9-10. In R2 B5 I body-jabbed at Long while Ziva stepped in, feinted and crossed. That put me down, made it 3 knockdown points to Ziva, and lost me round 2. I knocked Ziva down in R3 B2 but was a round short.

**(2) What I could read and what I couldn't.** At Long, Ziva lived on the jab and the body jab, always with the rear hand high. Inside or at Mid, Ziva threw the feint then cross, three times in four beats. What I couldn't read was which line was coming at Long. Every time I blocked one, Ziva switched to the other: R1 B3, R2 B2, R3 B4, and R3 B6, where I held high and the body jab came. That guessing game cost me 4 health for nothing. I also missed the one step-in from Long in R2 B5.

**(3) Was another agent more fun?** Yes, because Ziva adapted beat by beat. Ziva switched lines the moment I blocked, which a scripted opponent wouldn't do. But eight of the eighteen beats were jab or body jab trades at Long, which is the dullest stretch I've played in these bouts.

**(4) Too strong, and never wanted.** Feint then cross again. It landed for both of us every time it was thrown: R1 B4, R1 B5, R1 B6, R2 B5 and R2 B6. Across four bouts it has never missed. The switch I never wanted was the lead hook again. At Mid, Ziva's rear hand was always high, and in R1 B6 the hook was blocked.

**(6) TO REACH 10/10.**
1. **Give the feint an answer (R1 B6, R2 B5).** A man who steps back or holds both hands in the moment of a feint should make it miss, so the feinter's next punch can be blocked as normal and the feinter loses 1 guard. A feint that always works is a single best move.
2. **Make Long range more than a coin toss (R2 B2, R3 B4, R3 B6).** Holding one hand to guess the line just loses to the other line, so make a hand held "middle" block both jab lines for half damage, or let a blocked jab cost the jabber 1 guard. Then waiting at Long becomes a real choice instead of a guess.
3. **Reward closing the gap (R1 B3, R2 B5).** Stepping in to Mid should take the stepper's lead hand out of the guard for that moment, so a jab or body jab thrown into the step-in lands as a counter. Ziva's step-in from Long took my knockdown for free, and my own step in ate a jab.
