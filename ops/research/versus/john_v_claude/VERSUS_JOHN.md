# John on John v Claude (A DRAW 28-28)

**(1) Why it was a draw.** Two beats decided it. In R1 B4 I held my lead high and my rear low at Inside, and his feint then rear uppercut landed for 4. That beat lost me round 1 and left me on 7 health for the rest of the bout. In R3 B6 I stepped in, feinted and crossed, and it put him down for the 10-8 that levelled the cards. The beat that cost me the outright win was R3 B5: I jabbed into his back-off, and his body jab countered me off the pull and put me down.

**(2) What I could read and what I couldn't.** He kept his lead hand high almost every beat, so the body jab and body cross under it landed six times. He goes Inside to throw uppercuts, and he likes a feint before his big rear hand. What I couldn't read was when he'd stay put and when he'd come in. In R2 B1 and R2 B5 he stepped in with a body cross both times I stood at Long jabbing, and I never held my lead low there to block it. I also didn't see his back off and body jab coming in R3 B5.

**(3) Was another agent more fun?** Yes. He changed plans when I did. When I backed off he started stepping in, and when I countered off the pull he did it straight back to me. A scripted opponent repeats a pattern I'd learn by round 2. This one made me guess every beat, and the stamina swings felt like a real fight.

**(4) Too strong, and never wanted.** Feint then a big rear punch is too strong. It landed every time, for both of us: R1 B4, R2 B2, R3 B1, R3 B3 and R3 B6. Five feints, no answer. The switch I never wanted was lup, the lead uppercut. It only works Inside, the rear uppercut does the same job harder, and at Inside I'd rather clinch or throw a hook.

**(6) TO REACH 10/10.**
1. **Give the feint an answer (R3 B6, R1 B4).** If a man is holding his feet back or throwing a fast jab in the same moment as the feint, the feint should come up empty and cost the feinter 1 extra stamina. Right now a feint is a free, guaranteed landing.
2. **Show stepping-in risk before the beat (R2 B1, R2 B5).** Each time I stood at Long, his step in plus body cross landed through my high lead. A line on the beat screen, "he has stepped in to the body on 2 of his last 3 beats," would turn that pattern into a read instead of a surprise. It's honest information that the history line already holds.
3. **Make the counter off the pull a named switch (R3 B4, R3 B5).** Both pull counters landed for 3 and dropped someone, but they happened by accident from back off plus a punch. A "pull and counter" feet option costing 2 stamina, which only pays off if his punch falls short, would make that skill a choice. It would also give a trailing man a planned way back besides feint then cross.
