**J_CARDLADDER_S538 is done.** About 60 combat-sport card games are out or in development, and none has become a big hit. Three of them came out in the last six weeks and are very close to our idea: **Cagebound**, **Underdog: Corner Deck** and **Neon Knucklez**. In nearby games, players praise three things most: **a climb they can see, with floors they can't fall below; a few training choices that each have a visible cost; and rivals**. **What makes a climb feel good (INF):**
- a number or belt you can see;
- floors you can't drop below;
- a bonus for winning streaks;
- a title shot that feels like an event, followed by defences;
- a named rival;
- seasons with a partial reset;
- a stake you choose yourself.

**What gets boring:**
- the same few best decks;
- a flat grind;
- a "harder" mode that only means fewer options;
- progress that drains away;
- a climb cleared on the first try.

## 4. Clair Obscur: Expedition 33
**Standing:**
- Game of the Year at The Game Awards 2025 ([BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c1kpwmezkpdo)).
- Metacritic 92.
- Steam: 96% of 104,326 English reviews.
- The director added parrying after playing Sekiro ([GamesRadar, 10 Nov 2024](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/rpg/clair-obscur-expedition-33-creative-director-put-parrying-in-his-turn-based-rpg-after-playing-sekiro-and-thinking-why-cant-i-parry-in-my-game/)).

**Your turn is a choice with no time limit** ([wiki](https://clair-obscur.fandom.com/wiki/Battles)):
- A base attack gains **+1 AP**.
- Skills cost AP, with up to 6 equipped.
- Free-aim shots cost 1 AP each.
- An item uses up the turn.
- Attacks and skills have **timed presses for a bonus**, which you can set to automatic.
- From Act II, AP you spend fills a **3-level Gradient gauge**. A Gradient attack doesn't use up your turn.

**Their turn is a timing test** ([Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/en/tips/clair-obscur-expedition-33-how-to-get-the-hang-of-dodges-parries-jumps-and-gradient-counters/)):
| Defence | Window | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Dodge | The widest | No damage, no AP |
| Parry | Tighter (Perfect Dodge timing) | No damage and **+1 AP**. **Parry the whole combo for an automatic counter.** Enemies **feint** |
| Jump | For sweeps you can't dodge or parry, flagged with a **gold flare** | A counter |
| Gradient Counter | For the biggest attacks, flagged when **the screen drains of colour** | No damage plus a counter |

**Windows in milliseconds:** NOT FOUND. Patch 1.3.0 made **Story-mode windows 40% wider** and cut damage taken to 50% ([patch notes, 12 Jun 2025](https://www.expedition33.com/post/patch-1-3-0-is-now-live)).

**Accessibility** ([Can I Play That](https://caniplaythat.com/2025/12/04/clair-obscur-expedition-33-accessibility-review/)):
- Three difficulties you can change at any time.
- **Automatic presses cover your attacks only; defence can't be automated.**
- Challenge modifiers, colour-blind filters, remapping, and a HUD scale from 80 to 120%.
- Missed defences "nearly wipe out" the party even on easy. A fan mod widens the windows up to 450%.

**Praised:** IGN 9/10 and Eurogamer 4/5, according to an [OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/18026/clair-obscur-expedition-33/reviews) summary I didn't open. Reviewers liked that defence feels active and turns stay tense.

**Criticised:**
- **PC Gamer 70/100:** "dodge or parry … or get melted"; the timing felt like "mandatory distractions" ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/clair-obscur-expedition-33-review/)).
- Players complain about memorising parry timings ([r/truegaming](https://www.reddit.com/r/truegaming/comments/1pv3q2m/something_about_memorizing_parry_timings_in/)).
- Late-game one-shot builds (NV).

**Other games where your turn is a choice and theirs is a timing test:**
- **Martial Arts Brutality:** 85% of 720; swiping with a mouse is a complaint.
- **Witch the Showdown** (EA 18 Nov 2026): **parries earn mana for cards**, with a turn-based option and a Tournament mode. 90% of 70 ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3103620)); Stadion Gaming gave it 7/10 ([review](https://www.stadiongaming.eu/en/news/witch-the-showdown-review-a-sharp-parry-deckbuilder-half-built)).
- **WrestleQuest:** timing on everything, which GameSpot found "tedious".
- **Submission Saga:** one timed gauge, used only on the finish.
- **Sea of Stars** ([PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2022/07/07/a-closer-look-at-the-turn-based-combat-in-sea-of-stars)).

**The case for it in boxing:**
- Boxing defence really is timing. Block, slip, parry and roll map neatly onto dodge, parry, jump and gradient.
- "A parry gives AP" is counter-punching in one rule.
- It fits our open table: you see the hook, and timing lets you beat it. The gold and colour-drain flags work as tells.
- It keeps 5-minute bouts loud and physical.
- It's proven, both by E33 and by small card games.

**The case against:**
- It breaks "every number shown", because reflexes start to decide the result.
- It risks being a "mandatory distraction", as PC Gamer and the WrestleQuest reviews say.
- Lag makes online ranked play unfair.
- Grappling is about position, not reflexes.
- It's the biggest animation bill, since every move in 7 arts needs a readable wind-up, a feint and a hit frame.
- Players who can't time presses get stuck.
- Timing on touch screens is imprecise.

**Verdict (INF):**
- Keep "your turn is a choice" as the core.
- Add **an optional timed bonus on the guard you already picked**:
  - the card works even if you miss;
  - a perfect press adds +1 stamina or a free counter;
  - each opponent has one feint tell.
- Add an **auto-timing toggle**, switch timing **off in ranked PvP**, and leave grappling untimed.

This is an ESTIMATE that needs a player test.

## 5. What our design is missing (most important first)
1. **A career ladder with rungs you can see.** Club, area, national, title shot, then **title defences 1–5**, with a rank number, **floors**, and a season where you can lose a game but must qualify.
2. **Position states for the grappling arts.** Standing, clinch and ground, each with its own cards. Four of our seven arts are grappling, and "one guard per turn" only covers striking.
3. **A training turn with a visible cost.** Pick 1 or 2 of 4 or 5 options, with an energy bar, a **failure % on show** and **no stat decay**. Training adds or upgrades a card.
4. **Rivals who come back.** Each has one visible "Edge" to solve, and rematches carry history.
5. **Skills learned along belt paths across the arts,** so MMA is a reward rather than a mode.
6. **A corner between rounds,** with one fix: a cut, the plan, or a breather.
7. **Reasons to come back:** a daily seeded fight card, ghost-deck ladders and partial-reset seasons.
8. **A difficulty ladder after the title,** each rung adding one rule.
9. **A stake you choose:** double the purse, or take the dive.
10. **Optional E33-style timing on guards,** as in Q4.
