0. What changed from v8 and why
v8 probe verdict (S526): As a pure gain multiplier, fit had no stakes — TOP fit moved bout wins by +0.08 in 100, invisible to the player. The fit formula and words stay, but fit now works through four felt channels instead of just output. Every pass line restates to numbers the code can actually reach.
The four big changes in v9
Fit works through 4 felt channels, not just speed: Learning speed, breakdown risk, plan reliability, corner trust. No multiplier cap raised — effect comes from where fit applies, not how big the number is.
Restated pass lines to measured numbers from the probe: FOCUS gain, mastery timing, win impact — all anchored to what the code actually delivered.
Bond effect moved out of fit formula and into the plan/corner directly: Bond is what the boxer listens to; fit is what the coach teaches well — separate systems, clearer feedback.
Modes rebalanced so every mode is the best pick in 30–70% of weeks: No universal best mode; each answers a different problem.

1. Core idea revised — what fit actually does
Fit = how well this coach teaches this boxer this week. It does not just make numbers go up faster. A good fit shows in four places you can see during a fight:
Table
Channel	What good fit gives	Where you see it	Effect size (ESTIMATE)
📚 LEARN	Mastery threshold reached sooner	Mastery bar: "Sharp in 6.2 weeks (good fit)"	−1.5 weeks to Sharp; moves usable 1–2 weeks sooner
💪 LAST	Fatigue rises slower; less drift from overtraining	Round exchanges: "Tiredness drift this round: −8 (good fit)"	3–5% less energy loss per 30s exchange; stays fresh 1–2 rounds longer
🧠 TRUST PLAN	Planned lines hold pressure longer	Fight flow: "Plan pressure retained: 92% (Trusted coach)"	+4–6% pressure retention per use; plan worth +2–3 wins/100 over a camp
📣 CORNER LISTENS	Shouts and corrections take fully	Corner minute: "Corner trust: 95%"	+5–8% of the call's actual effect; wrong call costs less trust
Fit formula kept from v8 (no change):
Fit = 50 + temper/mode + coach time + bond + taught out + focus, clamped 10–90
Bond separated from fit: Bond no longer modifies fit. Bond directly modifies TRUST PLAN and CORNER LISTENS. Fit modifies LEARN and LAST. Two levers, two different effects — no hidden interaction.

3. The four channels in detail
3.1 📚 LEARN — mastery speed (fit only, evenings/mornings)
Moves: Fit above 50 → sooner to Usable (30) and Sharp (60). Below 50 → slower.
Steps (S463): The study slot inherits the pads coach's fit. Fit 75+ → pivot out at 30 in ~4 weeks instead of 5.5.
Mornings: FOCUS morning +1.2–1.8 gain/week above baseline; fit modulates that ± up to 20%.
Ceiling rule kept: Within 5 of coach ceiling → −6 fit and LEARN slows to ½ speed. Tag: "APPROACHING LIMIT: pads 69/74, about 5 weeks remaining"
3.2 💪 LAST — fatigue and stability (fit only, all sessions)
Fatigue drift = 1.05 − (fit ÷ 100), clamped 0.92–1.08
Every exchange in every round applies this multiplier to how much energy the boxer loses.
TOP FIT = 8% less energy lost per exchange → lasts 1–2 rounds longer at the same intensity.
RISKY = 8% more energy lost → fades visibly in rounds 3–4.
Visible on the round summary card: "Energy used this round: 22 · −2 from fit · −5 from opponent pressure"
Injury risk: Fit below 40 → overreached flag → acute:chronic load spike risk ×1.5 (was ×2–4; softened because fit is a modifier, not the cause)
3.3 🧠 TRUST PLAN — plan effectiveness (bond only)
Plan retention = 70 + bond_bonus + fit_bonus (mode only), clamped 55–95%
Table
Bond step	Bonus	What it means
Stranger	+0%	Plan lines lose 45% pressure per use (55% retained)
Working	+5%	Plan lines lose 35% pressure per use (65% retained)
Trusted	+10%	Plan lines lose 25% pressure per use (75% retained) + +1 extra plan line (max 7)
Corner	+15%	Plan lines lose 15% pressure per use (85% retained) + lines keep full value for first 3 uses in a round instead of 2
Mode bonus (fit contribution here): POLISH with mostly Sharp cards → +5% retention. TEACH with mostly Raw cards → +5% retention. Wrong mode → −5%.
Visible on Game plan: "Lines: 6 of 6 · Retention: 75% (Trusted coach)" → changes to "7 lines · 80% retention" when Trusted
3.4 📣 CORNER LISTENS — trust in instructions (bond × morale)
Word taken in (kept from v8) = morale trust × coachability step × corner bond step, capped 1.10
Correction absorption = 0.75 + (corner bond step × 0.25)
When you make a wrong call: Stranger boxer absorbs only 75% of the correction next round. Trusted absorbs 90%.
Visible in corner minute: "Last call: late. Correction absorbed: 90% (Trusted)"
Bond growth kept from v8: +1 per coached session together; +4 post-bout; −1 apart per week; +0.5 Hard morning coached
