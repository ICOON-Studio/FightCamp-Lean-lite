## 1. The idea
- An amateur bout comes 1 to 2 weeks out and has no camp. Camps stay for pro fights, title fights and the nationals.
- The player finds the bout on THE FIGHTER'S OWN PAGE (Fights tab): up to three bouts that pull different ways.
- The days before the bell use the SAME screen as a camp, cut to one week: where she trains, how hard, film, and who pays.
- Every choice lands on one box the player can read before saying yes, ON FIGHT NIGHT:
  - the tank at the first bell (0 to 100)
  - sharp or flat in round 1
  - plan lines ready
  - the scale margin
  - the win band
- Reid's evenings are the shared money of the week. Each one spent on one boxer is shown against the boxer who loses it, in roles, never names (the names rule).
- A later bout date now buys something: a second week means a trip away, a finished punch, or a full tank. That is the base Ask for a fight (ASKFIGHT1) stands on.

## 2. Find a fight (the fighter page, Fights tab)
- When she is READY, the tab shows up to three bouts from the coming club shows, side by side as versus cards (the tale-of-the-tape look already used everywhere).
- Taking one closes the others. Each card shows:
  - WHEN: this Saturday week, or the one after. The second gives a full week to work with (section 4).
  - HOME OR AWAY: the trade, section 3.
  - RANK: what a win and a loss do to her place. The rule is in section 3; RankLine shows it.
  - WHAT YOU KNOW: the opponent's file, out of five habit groups (section 5).
  - THE RISK: the chance of being stopped, and the days out if it happens (30).
  - THE WEEK IT BUYS: the ON FIGHT NIGHT box at the coach's default week, before the yes.
- READY / BUILDING until a date / RESTING sits on the same tab.
- The next sheet comes 21 days after a points bout and 28 after a stoppage: 9 to 11 bouts a year.

## 3. The bout sheet's trades (each choice now costs something)
| Choice | Gives | Costs |
|---|---|---|
| Home show | the club keeps the $450 ticket cut; a win brings +8 members, +5 reputation; close rounds lean your way 6 in 10 | home shows draw names BELOW her: a win adds a win and no climb |
| Away show | the host club puts its ranked names on its own card, so away is where she climbs: a win over a name above takes that place; free facts on two boxers you watch that night | no ticket cut, $40 van; close rounds lean their way 6 in 10 |
| Pass | keeps her fresh; the next sheet in 7 days | mood -1 (boxers want to box); 6 weeks without a bout = FLAT in round 1 of the next one (ring rust); two passes on the same club = it stops calling for 8 weeks |
| Loss to a name above | no place lost | the loss counts in her season record; the nationals selection reads the season's win share (one more loss can drop her below the line, shown on the card) |
| Loss to a name below | drops two places | the same record line |

The money line:
- Four boxers at 9 bouts a year is 36 bouts.
- Each home bout is $450, so the year's ticket money runs from $0 (all away) to $16,200 (all home).
- The player's mix of home and away IS the choice between money and climbing. The card shows the season so far: "home 5, away 3: $2,250 earned, 2 places climbed".

## 4. The fight week screen (the camp plan, one week long)
Opened from the bout card ("Plan the week") or by the coach's default. The same layout as camp plan v3, scaled down:
- WHERE TO TRAIN. Each location card shows its buffs as numbers the fight reads, its drills with tooltips, and its cost.
  - Home gym: free; normal pad nights.
  - City club trip, 1 or 2 nights: new sparring styles (+1 plan test a night), Fight IQ gains x1.3, injury x1.3; about $400 a week (J).
  - Hill cottage and seaside: only when the bout is two weeks out (a week away needs a week back at home before the bell); cottage $875 a week (J), seaside $1,000 a week (J); buffs as in the camp plan.
- HOW HARD. Three sliders with pictures, each with stops and what each stop does on fight night:
  - THE WEEK'S LOAD: Rest up / Tick over / Train through.

    | Stop | Tank at the bell | Round 1 | Banked this week |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | Rest up | full | FLAT | a fifth of a week |
    | Tick over | 8 off | sharp | half |
    | Train through | 12 off | sharp | a full week |

    There is no cap on heavy legs any more: 1 off the tank for every point of tiredness over 50.
  - SPARRING: 0 / 1 / 2 nights. Each night is one plan test against a live partner, and tiredness rises.
  - THE WEIGHT: shown only when she walks outside the allowed gap (section 6). Stops: Hold, Eat less (1 off the tank per 0.1 kg, up to 10), Train it off (tiredness).
- WHAT THE WEEK BUILDS: Technique, Power and Cardio shares (always 100%). They apply only to the BANKED part of the week, so Rest up builds little, whatever the shares.
- WHO PAYS (any trip):
  - The club: cash, then mood +1 and bond +1.
  - Half each: mood stays.
  - The boxer: her savings only; greyed out with "can't afford it" when she has too little. Paying herself and losing costs mood -2.
  - Savings: a new number on the boxer, filled by the stipend's leftover and amateur expenses; John checks the real amounts (J).
- THE COACH'S PICK: the pinned note with the coach's face, a one-line reason and how sure he is (it grows with his skill). "Use coach's pick" fills the sliders.
- ON FIGHT NIGHT: tank, sharp or flat, lines ready, scale margin, win band. It moves live with every slider.

## 5. The film evening, and tells confirmed in the fight
- Film takes Reid's whole evening (both pad places that night). The board says, by role, which boxer goes to the bag instead.
- One evening answers ONE of five habit groups (from the Fight IQ article he sent; LINKS_PLAN_TRAINING_S449.md):
  - entries
  - preferred range
  - reactions under pressure
  - defensive habits
  - when she starts slow or fades
- The opponent's file shows the five groups, each SEEN, CONFIRMED or UNKNOWN.
- A tell seen on tape is a guess until the fight shows it:
  - A plan line built on a SEEN tell runs at 90% in round 1.
  - At the first corner minute the corner says whether it showed (CONFIRMED: full value from round 2) or not (the line drops to Works).
  - A CONFIRMED tell is kept for the rematch at half value: the other corner has seen your answer.
- Free facts come from shows you are on: up to two boxers at your weights, one group each.
- The pool refreshes: two new names a weight each season and one leaves, so film does not run out (the critics' "film is empty by the fifth week").

## 6. The weight, at walking weight
Real club shows match boxers at their walking weight inside an allowed gap. Nobody cuts to the class limit for a club show; John checks the exact gaps (J).
- Each bout card names the agreed weight: the heavier boxer's walking weight, the other inside the gap.
- She is inside the gap: there is no weight question at all, and the slider is hidden.
- She is just outside: Eat less or Train it off, as in section 4.
- ASK FOR A KILO costs one FAVOUR (section 7). The opponent then starts with 5 more in the tank.
- A miss at the scale means no bout, and a favour is lost with the host.
- The fighter card: "can take a bout in 7 days: yes / no".

## 7. Favours (defined)
- A favour is a count of 0 to 3 per club, shown on the club's row of the region.
- Earned:
  - taking a late replacement for their show
  - saying yes to their kilo ask
  - (later) putting their boxer on your hosted show (HOSTSHOW1)
- Spent:
  - asking for a kilo
  - pulling out of their show late without losing their calls
- At 0, a club stops offering its ranked names for 8 weeks.
- Favours are the second currency between clubs. They never buy rank or wins.

## 8. Things that happen, and routine bouts
- PULL-OUT: 15 in 100 between Monday and Thursday (J). The matchmaker offers ONE late replacement: less known, up to a kilo heavier, or three bouts more experienced (never across the novice line).
  - Take it: +1 favour with the host.
  - Go home: no bout, mood -3, the week spent.
  - The Continue button stops for it.
- TWO SHOWS ON ONE NIGHT: pick your corner. Reid takes the other, and his calls are right about 6 times in 10.
- ROUTINE: "The coach runs this week" fills the default week in one click. The clock stops only for a pull-out, a weigh-in margin under 0.2 kg, or two shows on one night.

## 11. Build order
1. Probe first (Qwen): the tank price, plus the flat round 1. The best week against the laziest should be worth 10 to 12 wins in 100, and no single job more than half of that. The probe sets the prices to hit it.
2. Pure rules with tests:
   - FightWeek (tank, sharp, lines ready, weight inside the gap)
   - BoutSheet (three bouts, home/away draw, pass memory, ring rust)
   - CampPlace (the four locations' buffs as multipliers on stats the fight reads; shared with the pro camp)
   - Favours
   - TellConfirm (seen, confirmed, half on a rematch)
   - the boxer's savings
3. Clear the camp rules for amateurs: the 1.5-point "no camp" loss, the Set camp flag, the peak bonus, the coach's Peak week.
4. Screens, one a task: the Fights tab with bout cards, the fight week screen (from the camp plan look), the week board's phase, the corner's confirm line, the result's sizes.
5. Golden path: Find a fight, pick, plan the week (one trip, one film), weigh-in, fight with a confirm call, the result with sizes and the rank earned.
