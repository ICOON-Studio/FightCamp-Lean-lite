The One Critical Production Risk

THE RISK: LIGHT BLEED & AMBIENT PROBE CLASH ACROSS THE GLASS PARTITION WALLS.

In Godot 4.7 Forward+, the Office window ($Z = 0.0\text{ m}, X: 0.4 - 5.2\text{ m}$) looks directly into the main gym hall, and all four rooms feature open doorways cut into the $Z = 0$ wall.

If ambient light probes (VoxelGI or LightmapGI) are baked across the boundary without strict per-room culling or dedicated localized ReflectionProbe boundaries, the cool $4000\text{ K}$ medical fluorescent light from the Physio and the bright $3500\text{ K}$ halogen light from the Equipment Store will bleed across the hall floor, washing out the moody $2700\text{ K}$ warm tungsten aesthetic of the Reception and Main Gym.
The Mitigation: Place an explicit AABB boundary box on every interior room's ReflectionProbe and ensure interior Omni/Spot lights use negative shadow-contact offsets to prevent light leaks through the $Z=0$ cut-plane.
