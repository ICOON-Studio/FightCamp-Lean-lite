## 1. The one idea
What is true about a boxer never changes because you looked. What changes is how much you can see, and **you choose what to find out and pay for it**: in money, in a session, in risk, or in a bout.

**The range never lies.** The true number is always inside it. The bout always reads the truth, so the fight's balance doesn't move (CODE: the fight reads Attrs).

## 2. One word for each act
- **Read** is the general word for anything that narrows a range, on your own boxer or a prospect.
- **Scouting** keeps its pack meaning: opponents only. Amateur tape study in camp and the pro $250 scout stay as they are (CODE).
- **Find a fighter** keeps prospects. Its new read is called **Look closer**, so the word "Scout" never means a prospect (answers Maria 1 and the pack wording).

## 5. Hype
- A prospect or walk-in may carry the tag **Hyped**. Its shown centres sit 3 to 8 points high (ESTIMATE). Every other prospect's centre is off by 2 or less.
- Look closer or a trial clears the tag: the centre jumps to the truth, inside the old band, so the range never lied.
- A flop is a read you could have made and didn't, never a rigged roll.

## 7. The moment you find out
- **It stops the game only when** a range's centre moves 5+ points, or Under fire opens for the first time (answers Maria 7). Smaller moves wait for Monday.
- What it shows: a top-bar flag, never a pop-up. Its card holds the old band ghosted beside the new one, one sentence, and one button.
  - "Rebuild the defence" opens the Week with a Defence morning added.
  - "Book the step up" opens Fights.
  - "Write it" opens the Game plan.
- The card stays on the fighter page's bottom strip until it's acted on or the week ends.
- **Monday report:** at most two knowledge lines per boxer, each with what narrowed it.

## 8. Closing the leaks (answers Maria 2 and 6)
