# J32 — What a Real Clinch Is, in Numbers
Research date: 2026-09-21 (Asia/Nicosia). Claims labeled DOCUMENTED / INFERENCE / NOT FOUND. No animation advice. No invented numbers.

## DECISIONS
1. UFC clinch share of **total fight time**: ~**18%** (2013); was **16%** in 2001 — DOCUMENTED (Fightnomics/FightMetric TIP).
2. Clinch as share of **standing-only** time: historically **>1/3**, later **~1/4** — DOCUMENTED (same).
3. Boxing (elite amateur): total clinch time **per round** rises with round; **~5–6 s mean per clinch** (INFERENCE = total clinch time ÷ clinch frequency from Davis). Women’s finals 2023: clinch mean **4.06±2.27 s** (Martusciello et al.) if that is the parent’s C metric — treat as DOCUMENTED only if paper defines C as per-clinch duration.
4. MMA **per-clinch episode duration (median/spread)**: **NOT FOUND**. Miarka “clinch without attack” **11.4–12.6 s** is **cumulative phase time per round**, not episode length.
5. How clinches end (% break / ref / TD / KO): **NOT FOUND** as percentages of clinch episodes.
6. MT Interaction medians (**5 s**, IQR 3–10) are **not clinch-specific** — DOCUMENTED that Interaction ≠ clinch.
7. Strike tradeoff: clinch more accurate, lower knockdown power; clinch KD rate **<½** distance; clinch KD count **<1/5** distance — DOCUMENTED (Fightnomics 2007–Apr 2013).
8. Torso/hip separation (m/cm): **NO STUDY**.
9. Grip changes / position switches per clinch: **NOT FOUND**.
10. Boxing vs MT contrast: boxing clinches are short, frequent, mostly interrupted (ref stops); MT allows prolonged scoring clinch — **qualitative rules contrast DOCUMENTED; MT episode-duration numbers NOT FOUND**.

## 4 INSIDE (strike rate / grips)

### Clinch vs distance striking — DOCUMENTED (Fightnomics; FightMetric UFC **2007–April 2013**)
- Chart N = **43,269** clinch strike attempts.
- Target mix: clinch strikes to **head 53%** vs distance **~80%** head; clinch **more body-oriented**.
- Accuracy (clinch): head jab **~89%**, body jab **~97%**, head power **~51%**, body power **~83%**, leg jab/power **~94%/92%** (OCR of book charts; treat as published book figures).
- Tradeoff stated: easier to land, less damage; clinch knockdown rate **less than half** of distance per landed power head strike.
- Clinch striking pace rose over years but less than distance pace; distance striking attempts/min roughly doubled vs early-2000s averages in their pace chart narrative.

### Miarka et al. 2017 — DOCUMENTED clinch strike **counts per round** (by division; mean±SD)
- Heavyweight clinch: head landed **3±7**, attempted **4±9**.
- Half-middleweight clinch: body landed **2±5**, attempted **3±5**.
- (Distance-phase head volumes are higher — e.g. half-middleweight keeping-distance head landed **7±8**, attempted **24±22** — same paper; different phase, not a paired rate per minute.)

### Muay Thai technique volume — DOCUMENTED (Myers 2013; not rate-while-clinched)
- Knees: mean **14.09±10.96** per fighter per bout; **68.35%** of knees rated effective.
- Clinching for attack: **15.97±11.22** per bout.
- Thai winners used **~11 more knees/bout** than UK winners (model simulation).

### Grip changes / position switches per clinch
- **NOT FOUND**. Trial & Wu 2014 compares double collar-tie vs double underhook kinematics (lab knees; n=10 amateurs) — joint angles, **not** switch counts.
