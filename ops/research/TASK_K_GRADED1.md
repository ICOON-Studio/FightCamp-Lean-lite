# TASK K_GRADED1 - Kalli (design review) - how much a passed skills grade should save in the ring (S373)
Kind: research

Thanks for K_CALIB1 - all three fixes are in (women's club anchor 8.0, punches to 0.1, force in 25 N steps, the norm
words on the records wall).

New mechanic, built and waiting on your numbers. A boxer climbs six skills grades (Preliminary, Standard, Bronze,
Silver, Gold, Platinum; England Boxing's awards). Each grade's assessment checks a few punches on the pads: Standard
= jab + cross, Bronze = lead hook + body hook, Silver = uppercut + check hook, Gold = jab, cross, lead hook, body hook
again at a sharper level. Once a grade is passed, its punches are "graded": the combo card wears the grade's colour
and the punch costs less gas (clean technique wastes less), in the Combos tab and in the bout.

The gas model in the bout: a 100-point tank; a round costs 18 points before punches, plus the punches (about 54 a
round; a 1-2 costs about 0.55, a 3-punch chain about 0.9); a minute's rest refills only part. From 70 down she
loses power points (0 at 70, -6 when empty); from 40 her combinations shorten. Punch mix in a round: jab 45 %,
cross 27 %, lead hook 14 %, body hook 8 %, uppercut 6 %, check hook 4 %.

Q1. How much less gas should a graded punch cost so the player FEELS the grade in round 3 of a 3 x 3 min bout, but a
    graded boxer does not beat a fitter ungraded one on gas alone? A % per grade, and one for Gold's re-grade.
Q2. Should the saving be the same for every grade, or grow (Standard smallest, Gold biggest)? Why, in one line.
Q3. One thing on the combo card that would show the saving better than a "Bronze grade" chip and a smaller gas %.
Short verdict per question; the exact numbers you would use.
