## 2. Owner / fit-out rules
- Ring/cage clearance >= 1 m from wall (England Boxing checklist, Esprit-Boxe); commercial 1.5-2.0 m all sides (Compound Fitness). SOURCED
- Bag spacing >= 1.5 m; hang bags, never freestanding in the walk. SOURCED England Boxing, Ligum.
- Zoning: core = ring + bags; S&C adjacent, not across boxing traffic; lockers/office/retail off the core; high intensity away from the door. SOURCED Monster Rings.
- Sightlines: the coach sees bags, open floor and ring from one stance; a dead-centre ring that kills perimeter bag rows is a waste. SOURCED Monster Rings.
- S&C on rubber, weights to the back; recovery quiet and separate; bags as the strip between mats and iron. SOURCED Zebra, VERVE.
- Fire: exits <= 18 m apart, routes clear of kit. SOURCED England Boxing.
- Kids vs live cage: own zone or room; parents watch from seats, not through the sparring apron. SOURCED Zebra, UFC Gym plates, Mejiro.

## 3. First-visit read
- Works: door hits reception with a sightline to the ring, not through a live bag or spar zone; bags on a long wall with an aisle behind; changing off the lobby; coach corner sees the hall; kids/recovery behind a door.
- Fails: path to lockers crosses the bag line; ring jammed to a wall; showers opening onto mats; plates dropping into the walkway; kids class against a live cage; dead-centre ring.

## 4. JUDGEMENT - four presets (1 m cells)
- T1 Club (~16x14 = 224 m²): Reception (0-4,0-3). Changing (4-8,0-3). Office (8-11,0-3). Walk y=3. Ring (4-11,5-12). Bags x=0-2, y=4-13 (6 stations). Open/cardio (12-16,4-13). Coach (11-12,4-5).
- T2 Two-mat (~22x18 = 396 m²): Reception+shop (0-6,0-4). Changing/showers (6-12,0-4). Office (12-16,0-4). Walk y=4. Ring (3-11,6-14). Bags x=0-2, y=5-17. Adult mats (13-22,5-14). Kids/second mat (13-22,14-18). Cardio (16-22,0-4). Coach (11-13,5-6).
- T3 Two halls (~28x25 = 700 m²): Spine x=12-16: reception (0-4), office (4-7), physio (7-11), S&C (11-25). Hall A x=0-12: cage/ring (2-10,8-16), bags x=0-2 and x=10-12. Hall B x=16-28: adult mats (8-20), kids hall (20-25), showers (16-22,0-4). Coach dais (12-14,8).
- T4 Flagship (~36x32 = 1,152 m²): front y=0-6: desk+shop (0-12), cafe (12-20), office (20-28). Lockers/showers (28-36,0-10). Cage (4-14,10-20), ring (16-24,10-18), bag room (0-4,10-28). Mat hall (24-36,12-24). Kids hall (24-36,24-32). S&C (4-16,22-32). Physio (16-24,22-32). Coach (14-16,10-12).

## Hard rules to enforce
1. Ring/cage cells keep a 2-cell (2 m) empty apron on all sides; no station, door or walk inside it.
2. Bag cells never on a required walk or fire path; bags >= 2 cells apart centre-to-centre.
3. Kids mat / showers never share an edge with a live cage/ring cell; office or reception sees the main combat floor.

## Claude's read (S88)
Usable as delivered. Note the presets as written do not all satisfy rule 1 by themselves (T1's bags at x=0-2 next to a ring
starting x=4 leave exactly a 2-cell apron, fine; T3's bags at x=10-12 touch the cage at x=2-10 - violates the apron). The
game's presets are therefore authored FROM these, and the S88 test checks every preset against the three rules rather than
trusting the numbers.
