## 1. THE TITLE BUMP

### 1.1 What the question needs

When a gym’s fighter wins a major title (UFC / Bellator / PFL / world boxing belt / national title), what happens to that gym’s **enquiries, trials, sign-ups**, and for **how long**?

Ideal evidence = named gym + named fighter + named event + **figure** + **decay window**, distinguishing enquiry spike vs paid conversion.

## 2. KIT AS A DRAW

### 2.1 Question

Do members choose a combat gym for its **ring / cage / mat area / strength block / showers** versus **coaches** and **price**?

### 2.2 Martial-arts-specific preference study (closest primary)

**Indonesian martial artists’ preferences — conjoint analysis (PLOS One, published 5 Apr 2024)**  
Link: [https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0301229](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0301229)

- **N = 55** Indonesian martial artists (purposive sample; data collected **1 Aug – 1 Dec 2022**).
- **Attribute importance shares** (relative importance of the 7 tested attributes; denominator = total importance mass of those attributes):

| Attribute | Relative importance | Rank |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Type of martial arts offered | **30.449%** | 1 |
| Distance / location | **27.970%** | 2 |
| Price range | **22.706%** | 3 |
| Social environment | **11.097%** | 4 |
| Class preference (open vs private) | **5.080%** | 5 |
| Goal (hobby vs competition) | **1.562%** | 6 |
| Schedule frequency | **1.135%** | 7 |

- Preferred price band in that sample: monthly **IDR 150,000–300,000** (~**USD 10–20** at authors’ conversion in **2024** paper), then **IDR 300,000–450,000** (~**USD 21–30**).
- Preferred distance: **0–8 km**.
- **What was *not* tested:** ring vs cage vs mat square footage, strength block, showers, locker quality, or named coach pedigree. The paper notes equipment needs *qualitatively* when discussing martial-art type (e.g. MMA needing more equipment than kickboxing) but does **not** score “kit” as a conjoint attribute.
- **Tag:** art-type / distance / price ranking — **SOURCED**; kit-vs-coach head-to-head — **NOT FOUND** in this study.

### 2.3 Broader fitness “why join / why choose” surveys

#### HFA / ABC Fitness — Latin America Fitness Consumer Survey (2024)

- Link: [Latin Americans Embrace Fitness Facilities…](https://www.healthandfitness.org/latin-americans-embrace-fitness-facilities-as-key-to-an-active-lifestyle/)

### 2.5 Section 2 synthesis

**SOURCED pattern:** in both martial-arts conjoint and general fitness surveys, **location/convenience**, **price**, and **facility hygiene / equipment access** dominate published rankings. **Art / program type** dominates when it is tested as an attribute.

**NOT FOUND:** a survey that asks combat members to rank **“our octagon” vs “our head coach” vs “monthly dues”** with N, year, and percentages.

**JUDGEMENT (design only):** for FightCamp, treat **kit** as a *visible credibility / differentiation* lever (especially cage/ring presence for MMA/boxing identity) that sits **below** coach reputation and price/location in most consumers’ revealed preferences — but do not invent a “cage = +X% attractiveness vs coach” coefficient as if surveyed. That coefficient must stay **game-invented** and labelled as such in the design gate (§4).

### Honesty paragraph

FightCamp **must invent** (and should label as fiction in design docs):

1. A precise **“+% sign-ups in the week after a title”** and its **decay curve** — primary sources only support *that phones ring* and *dormant members wake*, not a surveyable percentage.  
2. A precise **kit-type attractiveness coefficient relative to a named coach** — no combat-sports survey publishes that head-to-head share.  
3. A combat-gym-only **refit interval in years** — only general health-club capex guidance is public.  
4. **Purse-share → gym renovation** accounting — owners describe small coach cuts and dues economics, not published build ledgers financed by title purses.

What **should not** be invented when real numbers exist: retail kit price bands (**2026 USD/EUR** lists), named mega-gym timelines (ATT, Tiger, Jackson Wink, Wild Card, AKA), and the qualitative existence of fight-night membership interest (Elorde, Wink’s).
