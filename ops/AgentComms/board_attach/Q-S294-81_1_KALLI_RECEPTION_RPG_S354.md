**1. RPG elements for the screen**
1. **Style as a class.** The player sees a class badge ("Out-boxer", "Pressure") with a tooltip naming one strength and one weakness. The decision: does this fighter suit the gym and the local scene?
2. **Potential as stars.** The player sees 2 to 5 stars, blurred until scouting is done, with a "coach's guess" tooltip. The decision: sign a ready fighter now, or a raw prospect for later?
3. **Two visible traits with effects.** The player sees two of the six, as words with effects, for example "Big heart: comes back after losing a round, +10% next round" and "Short fuse: loads up when hurt". The rest stay hidden until sparring reveals them. The decision: which flaw can the gym live with?
4. **One habit, named.** The player sees "Slow starter" with a tooltip showing what it costs in round 1. The decision: fix it in camp, or plan around it?
5. **A signature move.** The player sees one move that's already a weapon, at mastery 30+. The decision: build fight plans around it, or round the fighter out?
6. **An ambition, as a goal.** The player sees one line, like "Wants a title within a year". Meeting it gives a morale boost, and missing it drops loyalty. The decision: can the gym deliver it?

**2. Replace the week cards: one starting focus**
The player picks one of three:
- **Sharpen the signature:** the weapon gets +10 mastery now.
- **Fix the habit:** the habit is gone in 4 weeks instead of 10.
- **Raise the ceiling:** +5 potential, but nothing shows for a while.

The trade-off is win now, stop losing, or be great later. Each matches a different player plan, and none is free.

**3. Perk tree (unlocks as training builds)**
1. **Stiff jab:** technique 60+. Jabs stop a step_in 20% more often.
2. **Iron chin:** chin 70+. The first knockdown each fight becomes a stagger instead.
3. **Body snatcher:** body hook mastery 30+. Body shots drain 15% more of the opponent's stamina.
4. **Ring general:** ring IQ 65+. Corner calls work 25% better.
5. **Second wind:** conditioning 70+. Recovers 10% stamina at the start of round 3.
6. **Counter sense:** defence 65+ and slip mastery 30+. A slip is followed by a free counter 30% of the time.
7. **Finisher:** power 70+ and a combo mastered. A stoppage is 1.5x more likely when the opponent is hurt.
8. **Cool head:** temper below 40, or a camp spent on it. The loads_up_when_hurt habit can't fire.

**NOT COMPUTED:** every number above needs a playtest.

**4. What others do**
- **Football Manager:** the coach's star rating with uncertainty, and a scout report written in words. Borrow the blurred potential.
- **XCOM 2:** a class with a visible build path, and a promotion choice between two perks. Borrow the either/or perk at each unlock.
- **Darkest Dungeon:** quirks that are mixed blessings, removed at a cost. Borrow habits as quirks with a price to fix.

**5. Don't add**
Random dice-rolled stats on signing. The fighter should feel like a person the scouting found, not a loot drop.
