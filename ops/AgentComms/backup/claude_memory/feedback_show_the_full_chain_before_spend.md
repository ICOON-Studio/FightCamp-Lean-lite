Operator, 2026-09-25, on learning the colour-suit video route (Higgsfield Seedance + segment suits, C180/C181)
still needs an unbuilt multi-view 3D solve onto our rig: "we spent so much money and time on this and this is
the first time you mention this ... you are the role of the game designer you should be aware of these
techniques". The gap WAS in the record (Codex's C180 review: "useful as constraints not a solve") but was never
put in front of the operator as the gating step.

**Why:** the operator funds routes by eye on a promise; a hidden unbuilt link turns every credit spent upstream
into a bet they never knowingly made.

**How to apply:** before proposing or continuing any paid or multi-session route, write the chain
(source -> capture/track -> 3D -> retarget -> in game) and mark each link PROVEN / UNBUILT / FAILED. Prove the
riskiest UNBUILT link first, on data already owned, before new spend. Decision taken the same day: stay 3D,
fixed high broadcast angle (~60-70 deg) on the ground (Cursor U171 next pass); no Higgsfield credits on the suit
route until a solve is proven on the clips on disk. Related: [[project_higgsfield_mma_study]],
[[project-watch-loop-2026-09-24]].
