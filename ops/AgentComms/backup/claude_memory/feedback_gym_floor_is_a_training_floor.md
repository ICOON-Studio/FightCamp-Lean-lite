S177 operator on the GymHall3D stills: "it doesn't look like a gym at all. I see a board in the middle, a chair [...] You
have a bathtub in the gym floor [...] Do gym floor have bathtubs? The whole design is [...] very bad."

**Why:** the hall placed every room's furniture (physio table + ice bath, film TV + bench, whiteboard, chairs) on the
training floor to satisfy "one big gym" (D38) - a data-driven layout nobody checked against a real boxing gym.
**How to apply:** a training floor holds only what a real boxing gym floor has (ring, bags, speed bags, pads space,
mirrors, skipping, a few machines); recovery/office/film stay behind doors. Ask the operator about layout before
building, show a paper plan and one still first. Related: [[feedback_design_first_first_hour]].
