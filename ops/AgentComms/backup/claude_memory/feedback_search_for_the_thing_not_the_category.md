On 2026-09-11 the operator refused the conclusion "exactly ONE clip is grappling"
and said **"we have to find a ton more."** They were right, and a ten-euro
listing disproved a carefully reasoned survey.

C75 had read six SOURCE CLASSES — Mixamo, CMU, a Unity MMA pack, a Fab pack,
itch.io, open text-to-motion — quoted the licence clauses, and concluded from
**architecture** that paired, contact-matched two-body animation has zero
verified instances **at any source at any price**, so a ground fight needed
custom two-performer capture. That drove M19, "the ground is PRESENTED not
animated", and a whole stage's scope.

**It never searched the store for the word "paired".** RamsterZ
`Full Mount Attacks` (Unity, EUR 10.11) publishes 32 clips as explicit
ATTACKER/VICTIM pairs, tagged `PAIRED ANIMATION` and `SYNC ANIMATION`.

**Why:** a survey organised by CATEGORY ("free mocap libraries", "paid MMA
packs") inherits the categories' blind spots. The thing we needed was a
property — two synchronised bodies — that cuts across every category and is a
searchable keyword in its own right.

**How to apply:** before writing "does not exist at any price", search the
marketplaces for the PROPERTY by name, not just the genre. And when a store page
publishes no manifest, that is `NV`, never `0` — the same survey wrote `NV` in
three cells and the vendor's own site listed every clip. See
[[feedback_check_research_record_first]] (check the record first — still right,
but a stale record can be wrong) and [[feedback_deadend_means_assign_research]].
