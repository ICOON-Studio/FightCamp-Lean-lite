**User decision 2026-09-07: the game will model recovery and healing per BODY
PART, realistically** — not one readiness clock with an age multiplier bolted
on. The six regions `BodyReadiness` already tracks heal on their own terms; a
torn hamstring, a broken hand and a concussion are not the same injury slowed
by the same factor. Queued as a FUTURE stage with its own research pass, behind
R53/R54. Not assigned yet, and deliberately not date-gated.

**Why:** it settles the operator question J12 left open — author a
labelled-UNKNOWN `recovery_scale()`, or keep healing age-blind and ship only
the extra-clearance flags? Answer: the second. A whole-body age multiplier
would be the wrong shape twice — unsourced (J12 D1), and about to be replaced
by the per-region system.

**How to apply:** R54 ships the DIRECT commission evidence (extra clearance at
age/mileage thresholds, never longer hold days) plus the price half, and leaves
the healing clocks alone. The future stage inherits J12's findings rather than
re-deriving them: separate clocks for training-load vs injury (D2), no 30/33
cliff (D4), years and mileage both real and not interchangeable (D5),
return-to-sport odds ≠ weeks-to-heal (D8), concussion hold days are not
age-scaled in any rulebook (D9).
