Operator 2026-09-25 (S80), verbatim: "i dont like any of these pirtures they look like abandonned gyms , why they are all so dark ?"

Four dusk/night, no-people gym backgrounds (following M34 direction A's "dim gym, warm brass light") were all rejected. The round-2 prompts that fixed it: bright daytime, busy with fictional fighters sparring/on bags/skipping, a successful and well-kept gym, with brass only as accents.

Round 2 (bright, busy with generated people) was ALSO rejected: "all terrible again , better with no people". So the target is: NO people, but bright, clean, well-kept, premium, the gym ready before opening (round 3 prompts, S80).

Final S80 outcome: the operator generated his own picture (normal light, full frame, no people) and it shipped. Later rulings: no dominating empty wall; no brand names; no composition seen in another game (hooded walkout from behind in a tunnel was rejected); every pin must sit on an object that means that page.

**Why:** the gym is the player's pride and the hub; dark and empty reads as failure or dereliction, not mood.
**How to apply:** any gym/hub/title art: normal/bright light, full frame, NO generated people, well kept - and check every draft at full size before showing it. Never dim-and-empty. Treat the M34 direction-A word "dim" as wrong for backgrounds; keep the brass/leather UI chrome. Related: [[feedback-enhance-proposals-aaa-presentation]]
