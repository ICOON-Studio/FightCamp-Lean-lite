## Bound failure

**test 5 `loser_hurt`**: measured **100%**, bound 40–90%.

Root cause: RoundScript's round winner lands more punches (`need[w]-1` + final big) than the loser (`need[l]`). The bout winner (more round wins) always absorbs more total damage, so their minimum place value is always ≤ the bout loser's minimum. When the loser is hurt (≤55), the winner is also hurt. The bounds `loser_hurt ∈ [40,90]%` AND `winner_fine ≥ 50%` are mutually exclusive under this punch distribution — no values of the four tunable constants can satisfy both simultaneously.
