Q4 Recommendation: 3-tier rule (all odds and durations JUDGEMENT)
Tier	Look	Duration	Fresh leg	Hurt leg
Light	shift, lifted foot, flinch	0.3 s	72%	25%
Medium	stumble step or knee dip, hands drop	0.9 s	24%	45%

These are anchored on about 16% knockdowns in the most extreme calf fight, 0 leg knockdowns in UFC 257, and Poirier's "after the second kick".
