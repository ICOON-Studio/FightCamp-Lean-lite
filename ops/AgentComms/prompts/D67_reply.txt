## Notes — What Games Skip vs Player Notices

1. **Skipped — Suspension granularity**: Most games treat "KO = X days" as a flat timer and ignore the 2-in-3-months / 3-in-12-months escalation rules. Players rarely notice until a long ban triggers unexpectedly.
2. **Skipped — Medical depth**: Bloodwork, MRI, and eye-exam renewal windows are almost never modelled; games usually reduce medicals to a single "health %" stat. Players never see the difference.
3. **Skipped — Weight allowance tiers**: The pro 1-lb grace, 25% purse deduction, and re-weigh rules simplify down to "made weight / missed weight". Players notice missing weight but not the graduated penalty structure.
4. **Enforced & noticed — Age/sex**: Minimum age and no cross-gender matches are nearly always gated; players see these as basic eligibility and notice violations immediately.
5. **Skipped — Experience matching**: Amateur bout-count limits and age-gap matrices are dropped entirely; games match by rating/record regardless of inexperience. Players notice when mismatches feel unfair.
