PUNCHER'S CHANCE

How to play
  • Each exchange you both pick one card at the same time; then both turn over and the exchange plays in the ring.
  • Attack beats a Feint. Counter beats the attacks it names. Feint beats a Counter or a Defend. Defend blocks an Attack.
  • Two attacks: the faster lands first. 2 or more faster stops the other punch. DAMAGE on a card includes your power.
  • The Lead: win an exchange and you hold it: +1 speed, and a CHAIN card can be followed by up to two more attacks (click them).
  • Range: Long, Mid, Close. A card works only at the ranges lit on it. Room to the ropes: at 0 your back is on the ropes.
  • Judges score each round on damage plus clean counters. 3 rounds of 6 exchanges.
  • Point at a card to read all of it. Enter throws, Escape closes this.
