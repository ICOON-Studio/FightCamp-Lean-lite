Puncher's chance · card fight

Pick a card, then Throw (or press Enter).

How to play
  • Each exchange you both pick one card at the same time. Then both show.
  • Attack beats a Feint. Counter beats an Attack. Feint beats a Counter or a Defend. Defend blocks an Attack.
  • Each card shows two big numbers: DAMAGE (with your fighter's power) and SPEED. Two attacks: the faster one lands first and stops the other.
  • Range: Long, Mid, Close. A card only works at the ranges lit on it. Some cards step you in.
  • The Lead: +1 speed on your attacks (+2 on your v2.7 rules and lite), and a Chain card can be followed by a combo. Speed bonuses do not add up: only the biggest one counts.
  • Cards cost stamina. A guard gives a little back.
  • Judges score each round on damage plus clean counters. 3 rounds of 6 exchanges.
  • A tell shows you the colour of his card before you pick.
  • Point at a card to read all of it.
