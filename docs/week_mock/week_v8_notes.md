# Week page v8 - what the stills cannot show (interaction notes)

week_v8.png is the page at 1920x1080 on a Saturday, planning next week for the boxer Lucy Jackson. week_v8_pop.png is
the same page with Wednesday morning's card clicked open.

- Effort uses one scale, TIREDNESS 0-100 (a night's sleep keeps 87 % of it; 70-80 heavy, 80+ overreached: a morning
  that starts there trains at 60 % and its card says so in red). Each day ends with "tired N" and a bar in the zone colour.
- ONE way to edit a slot: click its card. A popover opens beside it: on top the segment row (mornings: Light | Normal |
  Hard; evenings: Rest | Pads | Sparring), under it the list (mornings: every session with its gain and its tiredness,
  Rest last; a Pads evening: the drill cards). One click sets; nothing cycles; Esc or a click outside closes it.
  Hovering a segment or a row ghosts that day's tired bar, the week meter and the goal bar to the new values first.
- Each card shows its effort chip and its cost ("+6 tired"); the icon colour is the attribute the session trains (those colours are used for nothing else). "Do it" (its effect written on the button) applies the coach's one suggestion, its effect is printed beside it; "Back to the coach's week" resets every slot.
- Drill cards in the LEARN tray: drag one onto any evening (or click it, then the evening). While one is held, every
  evening lights blue; dropping on a Rest or Sparring evening makes it a Pads night. The x on a pads card sends the drill
  back to the tray. Anything that removes something shows a 6 s "Undo" toast; Ctrl+Z also undoes.
- Gold = the goal only (goal bar, dot on mornings that train it; the dashed gold tick = where this pace ends). Blue =
  what you can set. Green = a gain or a tier reached. The ladder New > Raw > Usable 30 > Sharp 60 > Signature 85 is the
  one word set the whole game uses for moves.
- "Coach's week" resets every slot to the coach's plan; "Do it" (its effect written on the button) applies the coach's one suggestion (undoable).
  "Learn the Cross first" puts the Cross (a single punch) in that tray slot. THIS CAMP sits below, unchanged.
