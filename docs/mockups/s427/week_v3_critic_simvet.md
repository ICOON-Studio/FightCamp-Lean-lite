# The week, v3 - review from the sim veteran's seat (S427)

Read: the pack, the access page, the v3 design, its two pictures, the two look pictures. No earlier version, no other critic. My 10/10: `week_v3_10_simvet.png` (same folder).

Short verdict: the spine is right and measured (answers drilled before taper, lines that fire by level, a plate when they
fire, the Why that names the week). The choices hung on that spine are mostly a queue sorted by clock. It is a well-built
calendar with one good bet a week, not yet a week of decisions.

## 1. Two weeks played in my head
CAMP WEEK (one boxer in camp, one Flat clubmate)
- Mon: I lay Reid's Monday evening. Friday I leave open: Tuesday and Wednesday bring news and holding it costs nothing.
  One decision, and only when two claims can both still land before taper. AUTO from week 3 (Reid's lay).
- Tue: the tape lands, the band narrows, a new problem appears. A good small reveal. NOTHING TO DO.
- Wed: Reid's sentence, one tap. "Note it" is always right: Thursday offers the same choice with more known. DEAD TAP.
- Thu: the squeeze. As drawn it is no squeeze: the hook is the only claim on Friday that can land (the habit needs 4
  evenings with 2 left) and the scale is the only claim on the body. Two forced picks and one leftover. AUTO.
- Fri: NOTHING TO DO for the boxer in camp. Sat: ringside, one of two, or my own hands. The one real bet. I keep it.
- Next Mon: three lines, the leftovers back with shorter clocks. I am waiting for the bell, not for Monday.
QUIET WEEK
- Mon: pick a block (one Monday in three; the others have no question), maybe ask for a fight.
- Tue, Wed, Thu: NOTHING TO DO ("Wednesday's sentence can change a block's order" is not defined). Fri: test day prints ON TRACK or AT RISK with no action offered. A readout.
- Sat: open spar (the same three options every fortnight) or the show (one of two, new names each time).
Four of six days are empty; two weeks in three the whole quiet week is the Saturday pick.

## 2. Is each decision real?
| Decision or option | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Evening to a missing answer | Right when it lands before taper, and then nearly always first: LOOKUP by clock |
| Evening to drill an owned line to sharp | REAL and under-sold: measured +2.8, more than the last missing answer (2, and it fires 1 in 2 when only usable) |
| Evening to the habit, in camp | DEAD in camp (cannot finish, no cost shown for this fight). Right only in quiet weeks |
| Evening to the other boxer | LOOKUP: pick it when the "feels second" flag shows. Its worth is not measured |
| Wednesday: start on it / note it | DEAD while Thursday exists |
| Thursday: roadwork for the scale | CHORE: missing weight voids the bout, so it is a bill. The body has no second claim |
| Change a plan line to one already owned | REAL (short notice, a clock that cannot be met). Keep |
| Senior holds pads; bag night | Senior REAL only if the senior's evening has its own claim. Bag ALWAYS RIGHT: free, no limit |
| Saturday: ringside one of two, or own hands | REAL, if a banked weakness is worth 3 or more (not measured) |
| Open spar: rival / visitor / nobody | Rival ALWAYS RIGHT until a bigger night is drawn (the design says so); visitor DEAD; nobody a LOOKUP |
| Block picker (four tiles) | LOOKUP, and the screen prints the rule ("RIGHT WHEN answers are owned"). If a tile can state when it is right, the game can check it, and Reid's default does |
| Rest a line before a bigger night | REAL, the best late-career choice here. It arrives last in the build order |
THE CHAIN. Truly linked: week to next week (the hook takes next Monday too; clocks shorten), week to fight (answers and
firing, measured), fight to the Why (plates, fired and failed). Only stated: habit off rival files, house line, mood into
the other boxer's bout, a banked weakness, the Why quoted on the next camp card (a sentence, not a rule).
THE STAKE. Thursday's choice moves about 2 to 3 wins in 100; the card's own band is 8 wide; a boxer fights every 4 to 8
weeks. No player can see in results that a Thursday mattered. Only the plates in the fight can show it.
MISSING FROM THE WEEK: the diet switch (Hold or Cut, in the game) fixes the scale without the body and its price is never
named; the built pad night (75 minutes split over drill cards, the part the owner calls fun) becomes "one evening, one
claim"; the mornings (his first core mechanic) appear only as roadwork; two boxers in camp at once, the richest case for
two evenings, is neither written nor drawn; the head coach holds pads only on a Saturday.

## 3. Anticipation and reward
- Tuesday: yes, the tape and the band. Fight night: yes, will the line fire. That is the strongest pull in the design.
- Thursday: nothing (the problems were known on Wednesday). Next Monday: weak. Friday test: none, a forecast has no surprise.
- Next season: nothing on screen. A quiet week shows no dates ahead, so a block is never a bet against a date.
- Rewards are the right kind (an empty tile, an empty slot, a face to drag) and "Opens next" shows them coming. But the
  house line removes an upkeep cost the design itself adds, the senior and the freed evening loosen the scarcity the week
  runs on, and the fifth plan place changes what Ring general does today. Three of the six are invented.

## 4. What rests on invented numbers
- THE ONE: the price of hands. Two evenings a week, one claim each, an answer in 2, a habit in 4, sharpen in 7, senior at
  half, bag at a quarter and free. None is measured, and "two evenings" is not even listed as invented (the game has three
  pad nights). If seniors, bags and Saturday let everything fit, there is no squeeze in camp or in quiet weeks.
  Left standing: the fight card, firing by level, the plates and the Why (build steps 1 and 2). Those I would ship.
- Second: the band. Wide enough to stop sorting, it also hides that a choice mattered; narrow enough to show it, it is an
  exact payoff again. In the picture the ghost widens the band after a pick (8 becomes 13), which reads as "I know less".
- Third: habit and mood under 2 points each. Then "career" and "club" are labels and Thursday is the fight against nothing.
- IF THE THURSDAY GATE FOLDS IT INTO MONDAY: little is lost. Named problems and clocks stay. The week becomes Monday lay,
  mid-week news with one tap, Saturday. That is the honest shape; put the stop on Wednesday, where the news lands.

## 5. Wired and shown (the menu table)
- Home: right place. The same fact shows three times (answers as dots, as a light, as a card) and the dots have no names.
- Training "one editor, two doors": right. Not said: how a claim sits on the built 75-minute night and its drill cards.
- Playbook "fires 1 in 2, evenings to the next level": right place. Fights: the card on every offer is good; "ask by name" for amateurs is agreed, not built: a dependency, say so.
- Club > People "Reid's evenings (read-only rota)", "hires greyed": shown, cannot be acted on. Cut, or make it the editor.
- World rival file "habits of yours she lists": acted on only through a 4-evening block. Fine if the habit measures 2+.
- Live fight plates: the most important row in the table. Build it with the card, as step 1.
- Not in the table: where the season is seen, where two fights at once are seen, where the diet sits beside the scale.

## 8. To reach 10/10 (most important first)
1. CUT THE EVENING INTO THIRDS AND LET PROBLEMS BE PART-PAID. An evening is the built 75-minute pad night: three thirds of
   25 minutes, six a week from Reid. Every problem shows the thirds it needs, the thirds laid and its last day. Levels are
   steps (usable, sharp), so six thirds against 15 to 20 asked for is a small packing puzzle, different every camp. The
   player drags thirds; Reid's default still fills the week in one click.
2. NAME AND WEIGH THE ANSWERS. Four named tiles, not dots: situation, line, level, "fires 1 in 2", and what it is worth
   against THIS opponent (the measured 2, 4, 9, 14). Mark the key one. Then "sharpen the 9" against "start the 2" is visible.
3. ONE MID-WEEK STOP, ON WEDNESDAY, AND IT IS A TEST YOU SET. On Monday the player picks what Wednesday's sparring tests
   (one line, or the plan against a partner of the opponent's kind: club mate free, outside partner $160). Wednesday shows
   it fire n of 6 with the fight's own plates: something to do, something to wait for, and news about which line is not
   what its level says. Tuesday's tape lands on the same stop. Thursday and the Wednesday tap go.
4. GIVE THE BODY RIVALS AND MAKE IT CARRY. Each week at least three things ask for the room to 80: roadwork for the scale,
   a bag night (a third of hands, +3: never free), a hard Wednesday (a truer test, +6), the open spar (+5). Put the diet
   beside them (Cut: no body cost, the week's gains down a fifth). Next week starts at this week's level less 30.
5. PRICE THE HABIT AND THE CLUBMATE IN THIS FIGHT AND THE NEXT. A habit on the opponent's file shows its cost on the card
   now ("-2 to -5"); a clubmate left a third week shows the band that boxer drops to and what Low does in the next bout.
   Build neither until the 300-bout gates say 2 or more; if they say less, cut the kind rather than keep a label.
6. TWO FIGHT CARDS WHEN TWO ARE IN CAMP, side by side on Home; every third laid moves one card and ghosts the other.
7. BLOCKS AGAINST DATES. A strip of the next 8 to 12 weeks on Home (bells, shows, nationals entries, the draw, rivals'
   bouts, block ends, each "opens next" on its forecast week). The player sets a block's length (2, 3 or 4 weeks) and an
   asked fight must fit it. Friday's test is a sparring test with a spread; AT RISK offers three actions: Saturday's
   hands, one more week at a mood cost, or bank what is done.
8. THE OPEN SPAR COSTS FROM DAY ONE AND YOU CHOOSE THE LINE SHOWN: the better the line shown, the better the tell given.
   The unranked visitor is chosen by stance or style and gives the game's existing first-meeting rule (no round-1 penalty).
9. THE HEAD COACH HAS HANDS: three thirds a week of the player's own on any evening; ringside costs them.
10. SCREEN: one headline question per screen ("Friday: 3 thirds to give, 17 asked for"), a drawn picked state, the ghost
   on the tile that changes, late problems greyed with the word, faces at 90 px or more.
Needs things the game does not have yet: amateur ask by name, a sparring test that returns counts, the weeks-ahead strip.
