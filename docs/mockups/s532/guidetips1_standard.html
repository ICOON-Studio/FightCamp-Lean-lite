A tooltip must never

  • repeat its label ("Feint and draw" / "Feint and draw")
  • run as one long line: 330 px wide, wrapped, 45 words at most
  • pile numbers in a sentence: three or more go into rows
  • name a person: your boxer, the opponent, your coach
  • use a word the game has not taught; game words are underlined and open a short note
  • be missing: every number on a screen has a card

A plain value gets a SHORT card (two lines). A test walks every main screen and fails the build on any of these. Today 216 of 289 tooltips fail. Move names show the move playing on a loop.
