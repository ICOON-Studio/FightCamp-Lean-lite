# The Floor - pitch

**The one idea.** The gym is the game. One painted cutaway of the club (ring, bag row, weights, reception, office mezzanine) fills the screen, lit by the real hour of the day. Everyone you coach is a small person on it, working. There are no menus: you play by walking the camera, picking people up and putting them to work.

**Minute to minute.** Press CONTINUE and the day runs: boxers arrive, members drift in, light moves from morning to dusk. Things that need you glow over a head or a door (newcomer waiting, scale warning, offer envelope). Click a boxer: a big round card with face and rings (freshness, mood, weight). Drag her to a station: that is her session. Drag a combo card onto the coach's pads: that is the evening drill, played live. The week is one slow lap of the sun; Monday dawns on the Week board.

**Reached from the floor.**
- Training: drag people to stations, drag cards onto pads. Station heat shows who is drilling what.
- Booking a fight: the office door on the mezzanine; the envelope there is the offer. The calendar poster on the wall circles the fight date.
- Fight plan: the whiteboard in the office, lines drawn with a marker; the ring is where it is rehearsed.
- Money: the cash tin on reception and the bills spike by the door; upgrades are placed on the floor.
- News: the radio and the noticeboard by the entrance.

**Year three.** The building grows with you: new rooms, a banner for every title, faces that age, rivals' posters on the wall. The floor shows history, so a glance tells the story. Nothing is a menu you leave.

**Hard to build.** Readable crowds (12 people with clear tasks); drag-to-station on a painted diorama; keeping real stats reachable by hover/zoom; layered art for every state of every room; the light cycle.

**To reach 10/10**
1. Camera that zooms from whole building to one bag with the same painting quality (layered, not one image).
2. A tuned set of 8 station art kits and a pool of 30+ distinct, readable people.
3. Hover/zoom stat cards that keep true numbers without clutter.
4. Fight night as the same room going dark, the floor emptying to the ring.
5. Room-upgrade art states, season and time-of-day lighting.
6. A single proven drag flow (person to station, card to pads) playtested for speed.
