# Fight display: three critics and Claude's verdict (S422, Q-S294-170)

The operator: "has these reviewed and jusdged by critics ? i want their 10/10 designs". Three critics, each with the whole game
(CRITIC_PACK) and a different lens, judged the S421 drawing and drew a 10/10.

| Critic | Look | 5-second clarity | Decision depth | Excitement | 10/10 drawing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Sports-management veteran (FM, Motorsport Manager) | 4 | 5 | 3 | 4 | fight_10_simvet.png |
| First-time player | 4 | 3 | 6 | 5 | fight_10_newplayer.png |

All three capped the look at 4 for the same fault: numbers with no visible meaning (the big 1 and 2, the 41 and 49 on the
face, "+0.5").

## What all three agree on
1. Cut the bare numbers: the 1 / 2 by the clock become "THIS ROUND" with the six exchange dots; the 41 / 49 become a word and a
   picture (EYE CLOSING, BODY dots), the number on hover.
2. The bars get their word: LEGS, ENERGY, GUARD, horizontal, with the number; the opponent's show the range a corner can see
   ("ENERGY TIRING 20-40", legs and guard "?").
3. Readable round cards (R1 R2 R3 in the round winner's colour).
4. The last call shows its record (won / lost dots), not a bare X that reads as "close".
5. Cut the dotted line across the ring and the "FITS THE MOMENT" stamp.
6. One clock for the call: "NEXT CALL 14 s".

## Two of three
- Pace (Save / Steady / Push) and Aim (Own / Head / Body) on the call bar: both are built and stay in force, but not on screen.
- The NOW! set-up plate, dim until the gap opens: built, the most exciting call a corner has.
- Whether the boxer hears you: two shouts a minute get through.
- A trouble plate: EYE HURT, TIRING, ROCKED, and the same for the opponent (the moment that wins amateur fights).

## Where they split, and the decision
- Grading the calls ("Beats it / Works / Wrong", or "BEST NOW / OK") vs. only a hint: the coach says a grade plays the game for
  the player; the other two want a grade. DECISION: the hint, not the verdict. The eye icon that marks the spotted habit
  ("Waits to counter", "CAUGHT YOU x x x") sits on the call that answers it; no word grades the calls. Your S419 words were
  "hint things to player by him looking numbers graphics visual effects".
- Landed / thrown during the round (the coach) vs. in the corner minute (S419): DECISION: in the round, as the coach drew it,
  one centre-out bar in the scorebug ("being out-worked" is shouted during the round, not after).

## What I would build: the coach's drawing as the base
It already has Pace / Aim, NOW!, CAUGHT YOU, HEARS YOU, landed / thrown, labelled bars and the eye stages. Taken from the
others: the first-time player's "ROUND 2 of 3" and names on the THIS ROUND row, the 1x / 2x / Skip round buttons, the SAY
NOTHING button; the veteran's energy forecast to round 3 (marked not built yet). Fixed from the coach's: blue's "19 / 9"
(landed above thrown is impossible; real numbers come from the round's record).
