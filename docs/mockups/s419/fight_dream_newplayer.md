# Fight screen, my 10/10 without limits (new player lens, S419)

Drawing: fight_dream_newplayer.png / .html. Gold NEW tag = needs something the game lacks today. Untagged = exists today (pack: opponent mode clue, last-call result, exchange blocks, damage places, tanks, habit chip, tiring, call and effects).
Words on screen about 33 counting every NEW tag; smallest type 22 px; covered about 42%, clear about 58%.

Each NEW element: what / rule or data needed / why a new player expects it / what it changes / size.

1. Coach voice line (speaker icon + "Cover up!", also spoken). Needs: audio lines per call (a voice per head-coach edge) and a text echo. Expected: every boxing game and every telecast has the corner's voice; a first-timer learns the words by hearing them. Changes: the player hears the corner follow the call, so they know it went through (ties to hint "call is working"). Size: medium (audio via the sound engineer; text echo small).
2. Between-round and live REPLAY of the big shot (picture in picture). Needs: the 3D fight to record the settled exchange and replay it from a second camera; a trigger on big shots. Expected: replays are the single most teaching thing on a boxing telecast. Changes: shows which place was hit and why the guard failed, so the player picks Hands up or Elbows in. Size: large (camera and clip playback). UNSURE if the 3D fight can replay a past exchange; the pack says it only shows the settled round.
3. Listening meter (ear + 3 bars on the call card). Needs: morale-as-trust already exists (Fired up 1.05 to Low 0.7); the screen would show it live. Expected: a boxer who ignores the corner is a known drama; a new player thinks the shout is always obeyed. Changes: with a low bar, a different call (calm) or the corner minute TALK instead of shouting. Size: small (value exists, display only; wording of Low is in rules).
4. Judges' lean (three judge icons with a colour bar), hidden by default. Needs: per-round three-judge scoring shown live, not only the result; the owner HELD BACK scorecards after each round, so this would need his call; the pack has an estimate on the cards. Expected: new players do not know the fight can be lost on points though nobody is hurt. Changes: pace, Go for the finish, Save energy when ahead. Size: small (estimate exists) to medium (judge noise).
5. Crowd meter (speaker + bar). Needs: a crowd value (momentum, reputation, who landed last) and a crowd sound bed; none exists. Expected: a crowd that swings is a big part of a fight's feel and tells momentum. Changes: little by itself unless crowd gives an edge (hometown); mostly teaching and mood. Size: medium; value only if a rule uses it.
6. Range ruler in the ring (bracket with word MID, long and inside). Needs: live distance state; plan places (outside, long, mid, inside, clinch, ropes) exist in the plan; live range display is UNSURE (the pack names ropes and clinch as states only). Expected: "keep him on the end of the jab" means nothing without seeing the distance. Changes: Jab and move versus Tie up, plan lines "At long range". Size: small to medium.
7. More damage places (temple, jaw, solar plexus beside head, eyes, nose, ribs, liver). Needs: damage model with 9 places and a knockout rule weighted to the jaw and temple. Expected: a body punch to the solar plexus and a jaw shot are the famous knockouts; the owner asked "do we only have 2 hit points". Changes: the call (Hands up for jaw), the corner's chin note, the plan (body to the plexus). Size: large (damage tables, tests, balance).
8. Per-place hit pips (small dots: how many shots took each place this fight). Needs: landed punches stored by place, today by kind and head/body only. Expected: "where am I being hit" is the first question. Changes: Hands up or Gloves high, aim calls. Size: small to medium. Honest unscouted for the opponent's places.
9. Tank ghost (dashed level at the bell). Needs: a snapshot at round start. Expected: a fall is seen faster than a number. Changes: when to Breathe. Size: small.
10. Call forecast (gold dashed rise on the tank the call changes). Needs: the call's effect values before shouting (they are in the call chips today as numbers). Expected: a choice shows its result. Changes: picks between three calls without reading. Size: small to medium.
11. Opponent's corner calls (blue megaphone chip with its effect arrows). Needs: an AI corner that shouts calls with effects. Expected: the other side has a coach too; a first fight feels two-sided. Changes: counter the opponent's call (they raise guard, so go body). Size: large (new rule). UNSURE: nothing in the pack gives the opponent a corner.
12. ADAPTED chip on the opponent. Needs: the agreed opponent adjusts by round 2 (AGREED, NOT BUILT). Expected: opponents learn. Changes: write a new plan line, change the one thing in the corner. Size: medium.

Ranked by how much a new player misses it: 1 per-place pips and the full body (8, 7), 2 coach voice (1), 3 call forecast (10), 4 replay (2), 5 range ruler (6), 6 listening meter (3), 7 judges' lean (4), 8 opponent's corner (11), 9 tank ghost (9), 10 adapted (12), 11 crowd (5).

Build first: the call forecast (10) with the per-place pips (8): small, honest, and they turn the screen from numbers into a lesson ("this call raises that tank"), with no new art.
