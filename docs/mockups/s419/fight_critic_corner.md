# Fight display review: the cornerman's lens (S419), on fight_b.png

Lens: someone who has worked hundreds of amateur and pro corners. A corner watches six things: breathing, legs, where the hands are, the eye that is closing, body shots adding up, the opponent's habits and tells; and the clock. Parts taken from A: the scorebug idea (clock, round, exchange blocks). C's call wheel is good for a quick pick but B's three-card row is better for a corner who names the call.

## 1. The look: 6/10
No hard fail by size (all type 22 px or more). Hard-fail check: the damage rings on the bodies are numbers on a picture, which passes; "ROUND 2 OF 3" and the 4/12, 3, 1 row are words with bare icon+number pairs a new viewer cannot decode (what is the egg, what is the cup?). Cap not applied, but the bottom stat row comes close to a "bare number" fail. The left and right panels are 24% of the screen each side with top and bottom bars: about 38% covered, under the 45% cap. Look reads as a game, not a spreadsheet. The face with the closing eye is the best thing on it.

## 2. Element by element
- Six damage places and numbers: KEEP the face and body with rings. CHANGE: the rings cover the very eye they report (the eye ring sits on the eye). Put the ring beside the place with a short leader. Add a ring state per band: green fine, amber hurt, red critical, and a pulse under 35. Learn: where Lucy is being hit. Do: pick Hands up, or Work the head.
- Face close-up, cut, closing eye: KEEP, it is the corner's job. CHANGE: eye swelling should grow with damage (open, half, slit, shut) and the cut should show as a stripe with blood running as it opens. Learn: the eye is going. Do: cutman job in the minute, or stop the shots to that side with a guard call.
- Three tanks (energy, legs, guard) with band ticks: KEEP. CHANGE: the ticks at 55 and 35 are right; add the drain arrow as a sliding chevron on the energy tank itself, not a number under it. Learn: how long Lucy lasts. Do: Breathe, Tie up, or Save pace.
- Drain arrow: KEEP, change to a chevron that speeds up with the drain.
- Punch counts "4/12, 3, 1" row: CUT in the live view. No corner shouts about counts; he sees landed vs thrown as output and accuracy only in the minute. Put it in the corner minute card.
- Exchange blocks: KEEP, they are the only honest sense of who is winning the round. Blue blue red here means Erin took two of three.
- Clock: KEEP, big. Add the last-10-seconds pulse.
- Fight bar: CHANGE or CUT. A corner thinks in rounds on the cards, not a single bar. Replace with three round boxes (10-9 marks) once the judges would have read, and an estimate marker, labelled estimate.
- Plan chip "Work the body": KEEP. Learn: what the plan says. Do: obey it or change the arrow in the minute.
- Scouted habit chip "Drops the right hand": KEEP and make it the best chip on the screen: show the sequence as two glove pictures (jab, then right hand low) with a counter. Learn the tell. Do: pick the call that answers it.
- Unknowns: KEEP the dashed rings and hatched tanks; they look unknown, which is honest. Add a range wedge on Erin's energy once scouted.
- Corner's call with three effect chips: KEEP, strong. The effect chips (+12 guard, -2, -1) are bare numbers; give each its icon plus the word it changes.
- Two other calls: KEEP as quiet buttons; number them 1-2-3 for keys.
- Timer ring (0:14): KEEP, it is the time to the next shout.
- Big-shot burst: KEEP but short (1.2 s), tied to the place it landed, and it must name the place ("liver").

## 3. The hints (ranked by how much they change a decision)
1. TRUE: an eye is closing and the next clean shot may bring the doctor. SHOWN: the eye ring goes red and breathes (1 s pulse), the swollen lid narrows to a slit, a red cross badge appears beside it; a soft heartbeat tick sound. DO: call Hands up, or turn the head away from that side in the minute; cutman job (enswell the eye) between rounds; pull the fighter if the badge pulses fast. Honest always (own fighter).
2. TRUE: the opponent's guard drops when tired. SHOWN: Erin's guard tank has a blue ghost zone at the level it falls to, and her right glove on the portrait sags a few pixels once energy goes under the tell line; the ghost glove lights when the line is crossed. DO: Let your hands go, or Go for the finish. Honest only when scouted; unscouted it is a plain hatched tank with a question mark and no ghost.
3. TRUE: the liver side has taken shots that add up. SHOWN: small dots stacked under the ring, one per body hit this fight, the ring pulses when the stack reaches three, the ribs flush red. DO: Hands up; Elbows in at the minute; ice on the ribs. Honest always.
4. TRUE: the last call is not working. SHOWN: the call card has a ring that fills as the next exchange resolves; a flat or falling edge turns the ring grey with a small break mark, a good one fills gold with a star. DO: change the call, or leave it and trust it. Honest always.
5. TRUE: the opponent always answers the jab with a right hand. SHOWN: the habit chip as glove pictures; when Erin throws a jab, the right glove icon glows for one beat on her side, then the counter option lights on the call row (Slip and counter). DO: a Draw the lead call or the plan line "When she jabs, slip right". Scouted only; unscouted, the chip is a locked eye with three dots.
6. TRUE: Erin is breathing hard. SHOWN: sweat drops fall from Erin's portrait, the mouth opens, and the energy tank shows a range band (~30 to 45) not a number. DO: Push pace and Go to the body. Honest when scouted; otherwise the drops show but the band is a smear.
7. TRUE: Lucy's legs are going (the legs tank under the 55 tick). SHOWN: the legs tank turns amber, the figure's stance in the ring widens slightly, a flash on the tick. DO: Jab and move stops working; call Tie up or Breathe; ask for the stool.
8. TRUE: the round is nearly gone and it is close. SHOWN: the last 10 seconds, the clock goes amber then red, exchange blocks flash. DO: spend the last call on Let your hands go, because the round may be won in the closing flurry.
9. TRUE: Lucy is hurt (rocked). SHOWN: the ring edge gets a thin red vignette, the portrait goes grey for a beat, the guard tank drops sharply. DO: Tie up now; Breathe.
10. TRUE: the opponent's best weapon is public tape. SHOWN: one weapon icon on Erin's chip with its target (head or body arrow). DO: choose Hands up when the arrow points at the head, or Go to the body to move the fight.
11. TRUE: Lucy's trust is low and the shouting is not being taken in (Low morale). SHOWN: the call buttons carry a dim fill showing how much gets through. DO: use the minute to talk (calm, fire up), not shout.

## 4. Missing and useless
Missing: the opponent's weapon icon (on the chip); a body-hit stack count; the cut's progress (open or closing, how many shots to the doctor); the breathing sign on both; "how much of my call gets through" for a low-trust fighter; the current Pace and Aim (Push / Steady / Save, Head / Body) as two small icons by the call.
Useless: the 4/12 thrown and landed row in the live view (move to the minute); the "FIGHT" label on the bar (a corner thinks in rounds); the long wording on the call card ("Hands up, move your head" fits as "HANDS UP").

## 5. The moments
- Knockdown and the count: the panels fade to a third, a big number counts in the middle of the ring, the downed boxer's guard and legs tanks flash empty-to-refill, the call row becomes "Stay down / Get up" for the corner only if there is a choice, otherwise silent.
- Cut: a red stripe grows on the portrait, a doctor cross badge appears, the place ring flashes once.
- Hurt (rocked): red vignette, portrait grey, guard tank dips, Tie up auto-glows as the best call.
- Last 10 seconds: clock red and pulsing, exchange blocks flash, the call row shows only Let your hands go and Hold.
- Corner minute opening: the ring blurs, panels slide in to full size, the timer ring becomes the 60-second ring, the corner's three fixes (RECOVER, ONE CHANGE, TALK) appear.

## 6. Scores
Look 6. Read in two seconds 6 (who is winning is clear from the exchange blocks and the bar, but where Lucy is hurt takes longer than two seconds). Depth 7 (all the corner's information is there). Teaches 5 (the tells are on the screen but not linked to the calls).

## 7. To reach 10/10, most important first
1. Make the habit chip a pair of glove pictures and tie it to the call that answers it with a gold line (the teaching link).
2. Eye: swelling in four stages, a pulse, a doctor-cross badge, and the shots left before the doctor.
3. Move damage rings to the side of each place so none sit on the eye or the cut.
4. Stack dots for body shots so the liver is not one number.
5. The call card ring that shows whether the call is working.
6. Breathing as pictures (open mouth, sweat drops) and the drain as a chevron on the tank.
7. Opponent unknowns as ranges and ghosts once scouted, plain question marks if not.
8. Cut the thrown/landed row and the FIGHT bar; show round cards instead.
9. Every effect chip gets an icon and a word.
10. All moments (knockdown, hurt, last 10 seconds) wired as above, with sound.
