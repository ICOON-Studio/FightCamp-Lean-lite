HOSTED CLUB SHOW: the map

About three times a year the club puts on its own amateur night. You pick the date, the hall and the card. Critics: John 7 depth / 7 play, Dola 8 / 8. Builds after the fight week.

One show, start to finish

8+ weeks out
Book it
  • Pick 1 of 4 Saturdays (a region show the same day = fewer seats)
  • Pick the hall: gym floor 88 seats / community 250 / town 400
  • Hold each boxer for the show, or let away offers in
24 days out
Be the matchmaker
  • Each boxer: ask a name above, a name below, an outside novice, a skills bout, wait, or leave off
  • How to ask: as it is, spend a favour, or promise a return bout
  • Each choice shows: yes chance, win chance, rank, pro-offer chance, seats
Same weeks
Fill the card
  • The coach fills visiting pairs
  • Ladder pairs: swap a pair so a rival above can lose (costs a favour with each club)
  • Book 0-2 standby reserves at $40 each
12 days out
Ticket read
  • See seats sold so far
  • Keep / cheaper door / boxers sell tickets (lose a pad night) / move to a smaller hall / cancel
Final week
Pull-outs and weight
  • 1 in 4 bouts falls over: use a reserve, a tougher short-notice name, a novice, or run short
  • Close on weight: keep the cut, ask the other corner, harder week, or stand down
Show day
The night
  • Weigh-in the same day
  • Visiting bouts, then ours, the main event last
  • Watch or skip each bout
After
The sheet
  • Cash vs boxing away ($450 a boxer)
  • Reputation, new members, favours
  • Buttons: add places, review a pro offer, book the next show

THE DECISION THAT BINDS

A bigger hall pays more and lifts the asks, but a half-empty night costs reputation. Favours are scarce (0-2 a club, about 4-6 earned a year), so each ask, swap or weight plea spends the same small purse.

ALREADY IN THE GAME

The show itself (every 112 days), win chance, rank, pro offer, region shows, members. NEW: halls, favours, ladder swaps, reserves, ticket read, same-day weigh-in, the sheet.

BUILD (after the fight week)

13 small tasks, each under 500 lines with a test first; a probe checks the cash and seat numbers before any screen shows them; one golden-path test plays a whole show.
