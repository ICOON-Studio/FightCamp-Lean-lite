PUSH SWITCH + TANK: the map

Your two bars beside the fighter, race-manager style: you choose which tank to spend tonight, and against whom. Critics: Dola 8 depth / 7 play, Maria 7 / 6. Builds after the fight week.
The fight screen (drawn S442): two panels on top, the rings under the feet, the corner minute on the right.
HANDS spends WINDCoast / Steady / Push

Push lands more and harder but drains wind fast; under 40 wind pushing is worth nothing, under 35 she is gassed. Coast saves wind for a round already won. Wind comes back in the minute.

FEET spends LEGSPress / Hold / Move

Picks where the fight happens: in close, in the pocket, or long. Press costs both sides legs; under 30 legs she cannot step in or off the ropes. Legs barely come back in the minute.

HEAD: no switchthe wear both protect

Rises with every exchange lost. At 60 one more clear round can end it in a count. It never comes back during the fight, and after it decides injuries and days off sparring.

SpendRight when
Wind, save legs (Push + Hold)against a mover; a pocket war with the better chin
Legs, save wind (Steady + Move)against a body puncher; ahead late
Both (Push + Press)the round that decides it; a hurt opponent
Neither (Coast + Hold)a round already settled, head low

One fight, in the order you touch it

Before: the game plan
HANDS and FEET for each round, plus what to do in the last round if ahead or behind.
The round
Bars drain live. Changing HANDS or FEET is a shout: it uses 1 of the 2 shouts a round that land in full, and a fighter who stopped listening keeps the minute's setting.
The opponent
Has a plan too, seen only as tells ("her corner has her pushing") and, once filmed, tighter ranges on her bars.
The corner minute (pit stop)
Set HANDS and FEET for free; water gives wind back, a little legs; nothing gives head back.
After
Wind, legs, head at the last bell on the result; the tiredness card at home turns them into rest days.
