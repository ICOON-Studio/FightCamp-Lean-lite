# R55 Pads for two - coach's review (lens: 20 years sharing one pair of pad hands; plays management games)

Scores: look 6, clarity in five seconds 5, depth 4, realism 5.

## 1. Is each decision real?
1. **Booking (overlap line).** Real, and the best of the six: I do say "not that date, I have two in camp". But amateurs get one offer at a time and answer by a date, so "ask for a later one" does not exist for them (no Ask for a fight for amateurs). Today it is Accept/Pass: a pass costs the fight, so the line informs and rarely decides. It bites only once amateurs can ask for a window, or when the line also names the cost in money/mood. Half-real.
2. **Hiring.** One option is DEAD: Marcus Hale ($1,090, 58). The map's own numbers say Hale every night and Reid every night land the same week, and the $280 second coach (45, Tue/Thu/Sat) covers the days Reid is off for $810 less. With Reid plus the second coach, Hale is never right. Reid alone is right with one boxer, Reid + second coach from the second boxer. That is a threshold, not a choice. Fix: give Hale a reason (full-time means he also runs the member classes, so the +$350 evening class and your own night are free; or his hands are worth more for a long-camp boxer) or drop him from this mechanic.
3. **Monday lay (who gets each evening).** The only real weekly decision, but the screen offers two answers ("Share evenly" / "Keep it") against the coach's pick. Coach's pick is almost always right (nearer fight first), so "Keep it" is the dead button; the player only ever overrides to hurt himself. It becomes real only if the override can be right: the boxer with the long camp has a weak spot the coach cannot see, a boxer on the edge of a bad mood, a card already Usable that needs Sharp. Give it three or more different lays with a visible price each, not "his or yours".
4. **Moving a pad night.** Near-dead. It swaps one tired evening for another; with a coach working three fixed evenings the player will always just match the rota. Make it bite: moving costs the coach his club night (class income), or the boxer's sparring day.
5. **Fight night and after.** Not a decision, it is a report. Good report, but it changes nothing the player can do. Keep it as the teacher of decisions 1 and 3.
6. **Across the career (feels second, promise).** Morale -3 is INVENTED and tiny next to win +8 / loss -12; a boxer never leaves over it. It is flavour until R58 exists. Decide it with a threshold: three weeks with no evenings and a boxer asks to be heard.

The core finding in the map is honest and bad for depth: the coach's hands are worth one week, Hale and Reid are equal, and sharper-than-usable pays +1 point. A decision worth one week is a decision most players will never feel. Until sharp/signature pays more (the map's own open question - answer yes), the whole mechanic is a bookkeeping rota.

## 2. What is missing (the real choices of a small club)
- **Rounds, not evenings.** I never give one boxer the whole night. A camp pad night is 6 rounds with A, 6 with B, or three each. The rule "one boxer an evening" is the wrong grain and makes sharing look free-or-nothing. Split the night (half and half, 2:1) and you get the real choices, and the map's own 50/50 split already exists for cards. Screen: Week and the rota cell.
- **Fight night corner.** Two boxers on one show (club shows every other Saturday) and one head coach: who is in whose corner, who gets the second coach, who gets a stranger. This is the strongest missing link: corner trust, round-one calls, cuts. Screen: Fights > offer ("both on the same card").
- **Pads vs the club.** The coach holding pads is not running the evening class (+$350). Evening pads cost income, which is why a real coach says "I cannot do five nights". This puts money in the decision. Screen: rota row and Money.
- **Boxers hold for each other.** The boxer without the coach holds pads for the clubmate (a real fallback, weaker, and tiring). The map calls it "no coach: no lift" - too harsh and too plain.
- **Sparring is the other sharer.** Two boxers in camp are each other's sparring partner, which is free but hurts when one is Taper week. Link to Camp partner choice.
- **The injured or hurt boxer** frees the coach: a boxer on a layoff gives his evenings to the other. Screen: rota, automatically.
- **Style fit.** A coach holding pads for a southpaw or a pressure fighter: the card he drills best is in his hands. Optional depth.

## 3. What is useless
- Hale's 58 vs Reid's 74 in this mechanic: no difference to the week (measured). The rating number on the rota changes no decision.
- "no Reid: week 5" / "all Reid: week 2" chips: the second one is a best case nobody can reach beside a clubmate. Replace with what to do (needs two evenings, one is free).
- The tiny sparring icon in the No coach row (a spar is not a pad night and clutters the row).
- "Sat" column: Reid never works it and nobody hired is Saturday-free; one empty column all week.
- Staff cards for strength, physio, cutman: unrelated to pads, fine as small cards, but they take a whole row of the screen (230 px) for four one-line effects.

## 4. The screen, element by element
- **Rota grid (rows x six evenings): KEEP the idea, CHANGE the cells.** The face alone says who, not what. Show the card name under the face (Jab-Jab, Uppercut), and split a cell in halves when the night is shared. Six columns, three of them mostly hatched, wastes the screen.
- **Hatched cells: KEEP.** Clear: the coach is not there.
- **Ghost rows (Second coach, Hale) with + cells: CHANGE.** Fine as hiring, but nothing says what hiring would change. Hover/preview: "Danny's card a week earlier" in the row.
- **No coach row: CUT** as a row. Put "a clubmate holds" as the cell look of an uncovered night, not a fourth row.
- **Warning chip "5 pad nights - 3 with a coach": CHANGE.** It is a count. Say what is lost: "Lucy misses the bell by a week".
- **Boxer cards: CHANGE.** The bar with a hatched block and a white tick needs a manual; "Raw 26" is fine to me, but "Reid 2 / 2" and "Reid 1 / 3" are jargon. Say "Needs Mon + Wed" / "Needs Fri". The "By the bell - week 4" green chip is the best element on the screen: KEEP and make it the biggest thing.
- **Coach's line + two buttons: CHANGE.** Two buttons make a dead choice ("Keep it" is always right). Three lays with their price.
- **Staff cards: KEEP**, small, move to a bottom strip. Faces are good.
- Type is 26 px and up everywhere: no hard fail. The look is on the game's wood and slate and reads as one family. Held at 6 for: a table of six mostly-empty columns, chips that need explaining, and no fight-date on the timeline.

## 5. Scores
Look 6. Clarity 5 (I could read who is with whom, not what it costs). Depth 4 (one binary choice, and the numbers say it is worth one week). Realism 5 (evenings are the wrong grain; no corner, no club class, no pads-for-each-other).

## 6. How to reach 10/10 (most important first)
1. Change the grain to rounds: a pad night is split between boxers (all / half / 2:1), and the screen cells show the split. Real sharing, real pay-offs.
2. Make sharper than usable pay (answer the map's own question: yes). Otherwise the stake is one week and no decision matters.
3. Add the fight-night corner to the mechanic: two boxers on one show means one corner man; Reid or the second coach, and what each costs in trust.
4. Add the club cost: pads evenings take the coach from the evening class (+$350). Hiring vs income becomes a decision.
5. Replace "Keep it / Share" by three lays with a visible price each (Lucy first: Danny a week late; Share: both a week late; Danny first: Lucy misses the bell), and show the answer on the same card before the click.
6. Fix hiring: give Hale a reason to exist, or stop pretending he is a choice.
7. Put the timeline under the rota: a bar per boxer, a bell on the fight date, a mark for "Usable", and a ghost bar for "if you change it". It is the only picture that shows the stake in two seconds. (See my drawing.)
8. Cut the No coach row and the Sat column; say "needs Mon + Wed", never "Reid 2 / 2".
9. Replace morale -3 with a threshold ("third week without pads") and tie it to R58 or drop it.
10. Amateur Ask-for-a-window, so the overlap line has a "later date" to offer.
