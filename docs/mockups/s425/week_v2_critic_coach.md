Real decisions: 4 (Fri evening, tape answer, Thursday, Danny's offer). Auto-button candidates from week 3: Monday (Reid's pick), Thursday (Reid's pick, and the design itself says "if one change is best 7 times in 10 it is a lookup"), Saturday.

QUIET WEEK: Mon one claim among habit / best line / newcomers / other boxer; Tue the banked tape (if I went ringside); Wed "often nothing new"; Thu "does not stop"; Sat a show only every other week. Mon plus the ask for a fight are the only decisions; three to four days with nothing. A real coach would use this week to build, so it is fine that it is calm, but "one click" is what it will be from week 3. The design admits it (kill list 2) and lets it stand.

## 2. Is each decision real?
- Friday evening claims. ANSWER: right before taper, pointless after: REAL, and the best link in the design (tape to answer to evenings to taper, measured 2 to 14 in 100). HABIT: right in a quiet week or when the file punishes it: real. BEST LINE: right when no bell is near: real but the reward (62 to 65 of 85, three points an evening) is a long grind. OTHER BOXER: the feels-second warning makes it real for Danny. NEWCOMERS: +$70 a month against a camp evening for a boxer with a fight is ALWAYS wrong in camp; it is only a choice in a quiet week. CHORE-ish.
- Thursday, as drawn: option 1 (+6 in 100 v Kovacs, too late if it waits) is plainly the best and is tagged Reid's pick: obvious. Option 2 (+3 in 100 every fight) is a real counter-claim only if more fights are in sight. Option 3 (roadwork) at 74 tired going to 79 of 80 is DEAD in its own picture: the design's rule says it is wrong at 74, and 0.4 kg at 16 days is a small cut. "Use a line she owns" is right only when option 1 cannot land: a fallback, not a pick. "No change" is dead while three problems are open. So one live fork out of five buttons.
- Options 1 and 2 both take Friday: that conflict is the good part. Keep it and widen it.
- Ringside: right when a likely opponent is on the bill, wrong when a boxer is short; both costs are invisible on the board. Needs the stake shown on the tile.
- Danny's offer: strongest link (Flat boxer, tune-up answers, listens 85%). Not enough weight on screen.
- Chain check: one week's choice reaching the fight is shown for the answer (taper lights) and stated for the rest. House line, fifth place, senior: stated, numbers invented. Newcomers to club money: $70, trivial next to $18,700.
- SMALL film: the player never acts on it. A thing on screen he cannot act on breaks the owner's rule. It is one eye icon and a cost. I score it as nothing; it does not carry weight either way.

## 3. Anticipation and reward
- Forward: Tue's tape, Wed's sentence, Thu's phone, the three camp lights, the bell countdown. Good. Next Monday's three lines: good. Fight night: not on the board except as a chip. Next season: the draw bar.
- Reward: peak camp (a number on every stat), house line (stops fading, frees an evening: a real new choice), senior and fifth place (new pair of hands, a slot: new choices), the draw (new names to watch). Mixed: half open choices, half bigger numbers. The best reward in the design, a house line, takes about 8 evenings at three points each: too slow to feel in a quiet week.

## 4. What rests on invented numbers
The one that collapses it: how often a line fires by how well it is drilled. Without it pads and sparring stop mattering after "usable" and house line, fifth place, own rules and the plate all go with it. Second: the Thursday change (7 in 10 obvious kills it). What is left if firing measures flat: tape, answer, evenings, taper (the measured chain) plus a calendar. Three of the four Opens-next rows (senior, fifth place, house line freeze) are gates made up on existing numbers.

## 8. To reach 10/10 (most important first)
1. Make the Thursday fork honest. Remove "Reid's pick" everywhere (a pick he can press without thinking is an auto-button). Offer only options that are live today; let each cost the same scarce thing (Friday evening, the mornings, tiredness) so picking one visibly drops another; show the unpicked ones on the board with their own "too late" clocks so a problem carries to next Thursday. Measure and publish how often "no change" wins.
2. Put every decision on the one question block, with the stake in pictures: a card per claim with an evenings count, one big number (+6 in 100) and one clock. Danny's offer gets a card. Ringside gets a stake (a name on the bill, a weakness it would open). Cut "newcomers" from camp weeks (offer it in quiet weeks only).
3. Give the dead days jobs or take them off the board. Wednesday: Reid's sentence turns into one tap (start fixing it now / note it) or the day goes. Sunday: gone. Danny: his own row only when he has something to decide.
4. Replace the label-value rows and unlabelled dots with pictures: arrows, clocks, one legend. One row of three camp lights on Home all week; the bell countdown beside it.
5. Make the reward a choice, not a bar: the house line frees an evening you can see, the fifth place appears as an empty slot on the plan the day it opens, the senior as a face on a Friday tile.
6. SMALL film: either give it an action (a known line is one you may drop, swap or protect on the plan screen) or drop it from the pictures.
7. FULL film, if READBACK2 keeps it: only a gain if the note names a line, the player has one change, and the answer was bought weeks before. Today it adds nothing; do not count it.
8. Run the Thursday lookup measurement before building anything: if the best change is obvious 7 times in 10, fold it into Monday.
