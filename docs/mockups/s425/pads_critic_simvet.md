# R55 pads - critic: management-sim veteran (S425)

Scores: look 6, clarity 5, depth 4, realism 7.

## 1. Is each decision real?
Measured stake: the coach's hands are worth ONE WEEK on a card, and a card is worth +3.3 points only once usable (+1.0 more when sharp). So the whole mechanic is one question: "is the card usable by the bell?" That is a real question, but only for the one card that is near the line. Every other pad night is irrelevant to the result.
1. Booking (overlap line). Real, and the best of the six: it links booking, money and the plan. Dead only when one boxer is in camp (no overlap). Case for each option exists (overlap / stagger / pass). KEEP.
2. Hiring. Hale is a DEAD option. Map's own number: Hale 58 every night = Reid 74 every night on every card, and costs $640 more. Reid plus the $280 club coach beats Hale on every cell and costs $730 less than Hale alone. Nobody ever hires Hale. The second coach is ALWAYS right from the second boxer. Reid alone is always right with one. That is a lookup, not a choice.
3. Monday magnets. A CHORE. Reid lays them, Reid's answer is "nearer fight first", the player presses Keep it every Monday for a career. The only live case is "the boxer not fighting wins when her cards are owned", which is rare and which the coach could also lay. The auto-button wins within two weeks.
4. Moving a pad night. Near-dead. It costs a rest evening to buy back one coach evening. With 3 coach evenings and 5 pad nights it almost never beats "take the clubmate-held night". Never the right pick except one edge case.
5. Fight night / after. Not a decision: a report line. Good for learning, but it changes nothing the player chose.
6. Career (morale -3). A flat tax, not a choice. Only bites if the player can answer it. "Pads are yours next camp" (a promise) is the one live idea here, and it is parked in R58.

## 2. What is missing
- THE PLAYER IS A PAIR OF HANDS. Sam Carter is a head coach. A boxing club owner holds pads himself. Put "You" as a row: weaker hands (about 50), free, but each night you hold is a night not on classes, film or the books. This kills the Hale problem and makes the owner's time the scarce thing. Screen: People rota.
- CLASSES VS PADS. Reid's evening class pays $350 a week with a coach. A pad evening taken from a class costs real money. This is the sim-veteran link: money against a fight. Screen: rota (a class row) and Money.
- COACH FIT. Reid is a jab-and-combination man; Hale is better on defence. Pads of 74 are one number. Give each coach a lean, so which coach holds which boxer's card matters, not only whose evening is free.
- SPARRING IS THE OTHER SHARING. In a real small club the clubmate holds the pads, and the clubmates spar. Danny sparring Lucy in week 3 is "pads" for her and a bruise for him. A "who partners whom" link to the Week and to injuries is the real two-boxer trade.
- THE CARD THAT MATTERS. Rank cards by which one wins a round vs this opponent's tape (tape study already opens tells). Spend the scarce evenings on the card the plan needs, not on the one with the lowest mastery.
- Boxer leaving / loyalty: a boxer who loses his evenings twice looks at the bigger gym. Needs the same screen to show it before it happens.
- Fight night weight: coach hands in the corner (cutman, second) cost evenings too: a corner man is not at the gym that night.

## 3. What is useless

## 4. The screen, element by element
- Rota rows and cells: KEEP the idea (rows are hands, columns are evenings, a face in a cell). CHANGE: put the card name on the cell (Jab-Jab / Uppercut) and let the player drag a face across; today a cell says only who.
- Warning chip "5 pad nights, 3 with a coach": CHANGE. It is a count. Say the loss: "Danny's Uppercut needs 2 more nights".
- Boxer cards: KEEP faces, date chip and the bar. CUT "Raw", CUT the second chip pair. CHANGE the green "By the bell" into one line that reads "Fight night: ready" or "Misses the bell by a week".
- "Reid 2 / 2", "Reid 1 / 3": CHANGE to the evenings themselves (it already says it on the rota). CUT.
- Coach's line + Share evenly / Keep it: CHANGE. Two buttons where one is always pressed. Make it a real swap question ("Take Wed from Lucy?") that shows what each boxer loses and gains before you answer.
- Staff cards (strength, physio, cutman): KEEP as small cards, but they do not belong on a pads screen; move to a strip and give each a number that matters.
- Look: type is 26 px or more throughout, wood and slate matches; no wall of text. Hard-fail clear. But the screen is still a table of faces with no cost on it: no money, no tiredness, so it looks right and reads flat. Cap on look held at 6 (nothing is wrong with pixels).

## 6. How to reach 10/10
1. Add "You" as a row of hands and add the Wednesday class (+$350) as the thing the evening competes with. Money against the fight is the link that makes it bite.
2. Kill Hale as a dominated buy: give him a lean (defence) and a reason to hire (more evenings AND better on a specific card type), or drop him.
3. Turn the Monday lay into a swap question: drag a face; the screen shows, before the click, both boxers' ready week, the win chance, and the morale.
4. Give each card a value in the plan (the move the tape says wins), so the one card at the line is named; the other nights drop out of the screen.
5. Make "the pads are yours next camp" a promise on this screen, kept or broken, with a visible cost, so the career link is a choice and not a -3.
6. Couple the second boxer to sparring: who partners whom changes who is hurt and who learns.
7. Show the one edge case where giving up the nearer fight's night is right (her card is already in her hands), as a green tick the player can find.
8. Cut Raw, the double chips and the staff cards off this page.
