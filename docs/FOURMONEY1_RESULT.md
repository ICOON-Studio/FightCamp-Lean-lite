## Three sentences

A fresh gym with four fighters and today's start cash ($17,200) lasts 9 weeks before the bank runs out — not enough to reach the first show.

To keep three months of runway with four fighters the start cash needs to rise to $26,900 (fresh), $9,000 (debt), or $59,200 (backed).

Breaking even with four fighters requires 257 members (fresh and debt) or 283 members (backed, with the bigger rooms) — far above the 30-40 the starts walk in with.
