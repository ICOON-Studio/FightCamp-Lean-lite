# More to do during the round - plan for approval (S320, 2026-10-31)

Operator S320: "need more features for player to do/select during the round", "key stats shown during the fight would be
nice so the player can push his strategy according to those".

What the player can do today in a round: the shout bar every 30 s (8 calls), 2x speed, skip round, skip bout. Between rounds:
the corner minute (call, one habit fix, cutman, water/ice, set-up combination, talk).

Evidence: old repo research G44 (what a real chief second can do mid-fight: commissions, Halperin et al. 2016 on corner
talk, Football Manager / Motorsport Manager / Title Bout cadence) and TV boxing's live punch stats (CompuBox, on every
major broadcast since the 1980s: thrown/landed for jabs and power punches, per round and in total). G44's rules kept:
the player never becomes the fighter (no button for a punch), nothing rewrites an exchange already settled, every
choice costs something.

## Build now (1-3)

1. **Live punch stats (CompuBox style)** - a small panel under each boxer's body map: jabs landed/thrown, power punches
   landed/thrown, to the body, accuracy %, this round and the fight. When the numbers show an opening it lights a short
   line, e.g. "Maria's jab: 4 of 22 - she can't find Lucy" or "Lucy is taking 3 to the body for every 1 she lands". The
   shout bar then points at the call those numbers favour. Cost: none - it is information, the TV numbers.
2. **Point the shout on the body map** - when the shout bar opens, tap a place on the opponent's figure (head, eyes,
   nose, ribs) and the shout becomes "Go to the ribs!" / "Close that left eye!". Her next 30 s aims there (more punches
   to that place, BodyMap takes them). Cost: it is the shout for that 30 s (one voice, one instruction).
3. **Pace: Push / Steady / Save** - three buttons always on screen. Push = more punches, energy falls faster (our
   69/48/31 energy numbers); Save = fewer punches, energy back for the late rounds. A real corner yells "pick it up" /
   "breathe". Cost: energy now or later.

## Next (4)

4. **Throw in the towel** - stop the fight when she is badly hurt (G44's first pick: the one real mid-round power a
   corner has). Costs the fight (a loss by stoppage), saves her health for the next bout (fewer weeks out injured).

## Later (5)

5. **Mark it for the gym** - tap when you see a habit; the coach writes it down and it becomes a training card after
   the bout. Three marks a fight.

Also fixed now (a defect, no approval needed): after a corner fix the next corner card says whether it paid off
("FIXED: she stopped chasing the stoppage - it would have cost her 2 exchanges").
