## The numbers

Bag ($5,530): +0.7 speed over 156 weeks, $7,900 per point. Mirror ($800): +1.2 technique, $667 per point. Mat ($4,290): +0.5 defense, $8,580 per point. Rack and bench ($15,600): +0.6 power and +0.7 chin, $12,000 per power point and $22,286 per chin point. Cardio ($12,400): +0.7 conditioning, $17,714 per point. All together ($38,620): +0.7 on power/speed/chin/conditioning, +1.2 on technique, +0.5 on defense. The mirror is the best buy per point; cardio and mat are mid-range; bag is cheap but the gain is small because speed bag is only one day of the week; rack is the most expensive and the gain is split between two stats.
