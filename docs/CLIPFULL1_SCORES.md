The judge's pattern (the hand drops to or behind the hip before the punch, and the guard sags afterwards) is a thing that can be measured: REARHAND1.

## To reach 10/10, what the judge asked of most clips
1. Keep the hand that is not punching at the chin, all the way through.
2. No drop or chamber behind the hip before the punch: the glove leaves from the guard.
3. Come back to a high guard with the spine straight.
4. Plant the feet, or make any foot move a clear step.
5. Hips turn first, then the shoulders.
