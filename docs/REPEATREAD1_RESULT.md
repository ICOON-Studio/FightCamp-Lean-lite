## What this means (written by hand after the run, S538)

Owner (Q-S294-301): "maybe add a penalty if some combos are repeated too often the have a bigger chance to be read by the
opponent for a counter punch". Probe: `tools/repeatread_probe.gd` (nothing under `scripts/` changed). Run:
`godot --headless --script res://tools/repeatread_probe.gd -- nights=3 boxers=60`, 1,080 bouts a plan, 6,480 in all.

**Verdict: FAIL. Not built, no design map, no critic queued.**

| bar (fixed before the run) | plan's own lines counted | every thrown move counted |
|---|---|---|
| (a) one-line plan loses 5+ in 100 against the counter-puncher | 1.1 - FAIL | 2.8 - FAIL |
| (a) and 2+ overall | 0.4 - FAIL | 0.9 - FAIL |
| (b) a main weapon still beats an even spread somewhere | yes, all six styles - PASS | yes, all six styles - PASS |
| (c) the effect is over the cell's error | no - FAIL | yes (2.8 v 2.4, 0.9 v 0.6) - PASS |

Why it fails, from the tables:

1. **A plan line cannot be spammed in today's game.** A one-line plan fires 2.9 times in a bout of 18 exchanges (4.6
   against the counter-puncher); a four-line plan fires 7.6 times across all its lines. A line belongs to one range, the
   range changes with every exchange, and a line under Sharp comes off half the time (FireLevel). So "the same line more
   than twice in six exchanges" almost never happens: 0.06 reads a bout. There is no spam to punish.
2. **What repeats is her instinct, not the plan.** Count every move thrown and the top move is 39% of the bout - but most
   of those are the exchanges where NO plan line fired and she threw the textbook move for that range. A penalty there
   lands hardest on the boxer with the thinnest plan and gives the player nothing to choose: he never picked that move.
3. **One countered exchange rarely turns a bout.** Even at 1.2 reads a bout against the counter-puncher, a read costs one
   exchange of six in a round and a round is one of three: 2.8 wins in 100.
4. **The game already has this rule, and it is silent.** `scripts/rules/PlanRead.gd` ADJUST (S396 "opponents adjust",
   wired at `scripts/state/LiveRound.gd:255`): a right answer loses 30% of its edge for every use after the first two in a
   round - "leaning on one line stops paying". No screen says so. A second penalty on top would be a second layer on the
   same idea (also near it: `ReadBack` - the opponent waiting for a line; `HabitFix` - a counter-puncher punishing a habit;
   `Adaptation.REPEAT_HOLD` - the same corner change twice).

What would have to change for the owner's idea to pass:

- **Show the rule that exists first** (no new mechanic, cheap): where PlanRead.adjust bites, the fight feed and the result
  page's Plan tab say "she has seen your one-two: it paid less the third time", and the chart warns on a plan that leans
  on one answer. That alone delivers "repeat it and get read" in words the player can act on.
- **Give the player the lever**: a line needs a way to be repeated or varied on purpose - a second line on the same range
  and situation (a mix), so the choice is "best answer every time, and get read" against "second-best sometimes, and stay
  fresh". Without it the repetition is made by the range flow, not by him.
- **Make the read a counter, not a tax**: for the bar's 5 in 100 the read must do what a counter does (a clean shot: hurt,
  a knockdown chance through `Knockdowns`), on the PlanRead uses count, for counter-punchers and high-rated opponents
  only - then probe that against the same bars. Raising this greybox's chance or cost until the bar passes would only be
  tuning a number to a target.

Greybox limits (said in the probe's header): the penalty moved the exchange's margin only (not energy, hurt or ring
position after it); a bout the game stopped kept its result in both arms; the rule was laid over recorded bouts, ON
against OFF on the same bouts.
