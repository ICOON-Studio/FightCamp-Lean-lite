## Numbers

At reputation 10 the floor never binds for any bot — natural members (57) sit below the 66-member starting cap, so WHOLE, BUYER and NEVER all end with 57 members in both tiers. At reputation 30 the floor binds for NEVER (66 members, equipment-limited from week 9 onward in 148 of 156 weeks) but the BUYER's single $400 purchase (plyo boxes, 4 places = 12 members) lifts the cap to 70 and the BUYER carries 69 — a 3-member gain over NEVER. The WHOLE gym, with every piece standing from day one, also reaches 69. At reputation 50 and tier 0 the pattern holds: NEVER caps at 66, BUYER at 70 with one $400 piece, WHOLE at 70. The floor changes members by 5 or more only at tier 1 with reputation 50 and above: at rep 50 tier 1 the WHOLE gym carries 82 vs NEVER's 66 (+16), the BUYER 81 (+15); at rep 80 tier 1 the WHOLE gym carries 101 vs NEVER's 66 (+35), the BUYER 81 (+15). The BUYER's $555 spend at rep 50 tier 1 ends the run $16,924 better off than NEVER (cash -55,511 against -72,435, the price already paid): 15 extra members from week 9, about 147 weeks x 15 x $8.08 a week = $17,800 in dues. (Corrected by Claude, S416: the seat wrote ~$178,000, a slip of ten times; the table is right.) Every career closes: without fighters there is no bout income, and the coach salary ($450/wk) plus rent exceed member dues at every population the floor allows.

S416 reading (Claude): BUYER stops at 81 at rep 80 tier 1 because its 8-week reserve runs out while the career closes, not because the floor is full, so the money columns describe a club with no fighters and no classes. The members columns are the result.
