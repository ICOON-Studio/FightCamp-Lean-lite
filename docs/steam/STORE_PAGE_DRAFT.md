## Name

## Short description (Steam limit 300 characters; this is 263)
Run a small boxing gym. Scout an unknown, plan every week of training, set the plan for fight night and watch it play out in the
ring. When she wins or loses, the game shows you why. A management game for people who know what a jab is for.

## About this game

**You are the coach.** A small club, a handful of fighters, a calendar and not enough money. Every Monday you choose what each
fighter trains, how hard, and what it costs. Every fight night you choose the plan.

**The plan is the star.** Before the bell you pick how she boxes: stay long and jab, go to the body, draw the lead, tie him up when
hurt. In the corner between rounds you read the numbers and change it. After the fight the game shows you which calls worked, round
by round. A good plan is worth real wins; a bad one costs them.

**Fighters are people.** Every boxer has habits you discover by watching her fight, a weight class to make, a body that tires and
gets hurt, a goal of her own. Scout, interview and sign them; raise an unknown through the amateurs toward a title.

**Every week costs something.** Training load against fatigue, weight cuts against strength, rent and fees against the purse.

**Watch it happen.** Fights play out in 3D with real-motion boxers, a corner team between rounds and a ringside broadcast, or
skip straight to the result.

**Also in the gym:** a no-gi grappling mat, with its own belt road and match plan.

### Early Access
FightCamp is in development by a very small studio. Early Access is planned with boxing, one gym, a small roster and the full
week-to-fight-night loop. The road map (longer careers, more fighters, more of the world) will be published with the release date.
