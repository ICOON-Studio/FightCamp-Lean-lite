In the table, opening counts are fighter-plans (two per started round), including the raw counts and shares; the most common **exact** plan includes guard and order, while the action share ignores guard. Mirror rows also show the share of rounds where either man chose the most common exact plan or action (a shared choice counts only once); those per-round unions were not collected for styled opponents. Both-hold and opening range use rounds. Opening damage and stamina are summed across both men per started round, before recovery (the measured stamina losses match the sum of the selected action costs in every row); health/round likewise includes both men and partially completed stoppage rounds. Lone-step wins are scored rounds with exactly one opening step-in; level rounds count in the denominator, and stopped rounds are excluded and shown separately. Styled opponents differ in strength, so their lone-step results are descriptive, not causal. Long is the share of all beats ending Long. Jab includes head and body jab among executed punches. Stoppages are reconstructed as integer counts out of 84; opponent knockdowns marked ≈ sum the instrument's separately rounded per-fighter rates. The nine bars are assessed only for mirror. The instrument rounds displayed percentages, so a displayed 3% can still fail the 3% threshold.
